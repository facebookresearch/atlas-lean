# The fraction of cases is the prevalence

Let `μ` be a probability measure on `Ω` and let `L` be a real function on `Ω`, `μ`-almost everywhere
measurable, with the standard normal law under `μ`. Then for every real threshold `t` the cases
`{t ≤ L}` have probability

`μ{t ≤ L} = K(t) = Φ(-t)`,

the [prevalence](prevalence.md).
