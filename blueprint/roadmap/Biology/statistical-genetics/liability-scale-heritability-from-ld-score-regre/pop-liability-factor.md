# Population conversion factor of Dempster and Lerner

For a real threshold `t`, the population conversion factor of Dempster and Lerner (1950) is

`K (1 - K) / z²`,

with the [prevalence](prevalence.md) `K = K(t)` and the
[standard normal density at the threshold](threshold-density.md) `z = z(t)`. Its denominator is
positive for every `t`. It converts the observed-scale heritability of a population sample to the
liability scale ([Dempster–Lerner conversion](h2-eq-pop-liability-factor-mul.md)).
