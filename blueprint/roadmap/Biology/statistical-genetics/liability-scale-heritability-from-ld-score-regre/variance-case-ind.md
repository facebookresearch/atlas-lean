# Variance of the case indicator

Let `μ` be a probability measure on `Ω` and let `L` be a measurable real function on `Ω` with the
standard normal law under `μ`. Then for every real threshold `t`, the [case indicator](case-ind.md)
`Y = 1{L ≥ t}` has variance

`Var Y = K (1 - K)`,

with the [prevalence](prevalence.md) `K = K(t)`.
