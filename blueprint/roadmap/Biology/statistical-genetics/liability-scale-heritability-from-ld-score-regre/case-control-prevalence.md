# Sampling cases at their population fraction recovers the population

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` (not necessarily a probability measure), `L` a measurable real function
on `Ω` and `t` a real threshold, and suppose that the cases `C = {t ≤ L}` have mass `0 < μ(C) < 1`.
Then the [case-control law](case-control.md) `μ_cc` with case fraction `P = μ(C)` is `μ` itself.
