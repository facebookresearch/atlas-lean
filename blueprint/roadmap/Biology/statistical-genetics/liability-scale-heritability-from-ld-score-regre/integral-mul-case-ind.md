# Partial first moment of the liability above the threshold

Let `μ` be a probability measure on `Ω` and let `L` be a measurable real function on `Ω` with the
standard normal law under `μ`. Then for every real threshold `t`, with the
[case indicator](case-ind.md) `Y = 1{L ≥ t}`,

`E[L Y] = ∫_{t ≤ L} L dμ = z(t)`,

the [standard normal density at the threshold](threshold-density.md).
