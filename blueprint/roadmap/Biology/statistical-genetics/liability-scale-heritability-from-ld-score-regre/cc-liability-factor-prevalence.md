# With case fraction equal to the prevalence, the two factors agree

For every real threshold `t`, the [case-control conversion factor](cc-liability-factor.md) at the
case fraction `P = K(t)` equals the [population conversion factor](pop-liability-factor.md):

`K² (1 - K)² / (K (1 - K) z²) = K (1 - K) / z²`,

with the [prevalence](prevalence.md) `K = K(t)` and the
[standard normal density at the threshold](threshold-density.md) `z = z(t)`.
