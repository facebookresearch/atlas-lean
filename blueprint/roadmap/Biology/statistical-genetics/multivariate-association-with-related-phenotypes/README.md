# Multivariate association with related phenotypes

Testing a genetic variant against several correlated traits at once. Partitioning traits into unassociated, directly associated and indirectly associated sets turns every classical univariate, conditional and multivariate test into a Bayes factor for one partition against the global null, and Bayesian model averaging over partitions combines them.
