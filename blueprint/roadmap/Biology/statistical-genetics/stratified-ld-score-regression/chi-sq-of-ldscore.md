# Stratified statistic agrees with the LD-score statistic

For every [LD-score model](../ld-score-regression/model.md) and every real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $X$, the [association statistics](chi-sq.md) of the [corresponding stratified model](of-ldscore.md) coincide, as functions on $\Omega$, with the [association statistics of the LD-score model](../ld-score-regression/chi-sq.md).
