# Heritability from local ancestry

Narrow-sense heritability estimated from unrelated individuals of a recently admixed population: when global ancestry is the same for every individual, the phenotypic variance explained by local ancestry is a fixed multiple of the total additive heritability, and otherwise approximately so, the multiplier depending on the admixture proportion and on allele-frequency differentiation of the ancestral populations at causal loci.
