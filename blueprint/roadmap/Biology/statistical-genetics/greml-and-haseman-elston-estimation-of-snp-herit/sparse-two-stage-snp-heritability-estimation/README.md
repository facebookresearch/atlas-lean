# Sparse two-stage SNP heritability estimation

SNP heritability can be defined with SNP effects treated as fixed (the quantitative-genetics view) or random (the mixed-model view); the two definitions coincide in expectation when the nonzero effects are i.i.d. and independent of genotypes. Variance-component estimates in the ultrahigh-dimensional regime have large standard errors; selecting a sparse model on one half of the sample and estimating variance components on the other half reduces them while keeping the estimate nearly unbiased.
