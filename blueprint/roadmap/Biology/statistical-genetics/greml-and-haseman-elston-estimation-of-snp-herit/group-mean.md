# Mean squared rotated contrast with a given eigenvalue

For a finite index set $r$, real vectors $d, w \in \mathbb{R}^r$ (contrast eigenvalues and rotated
contrasts) and a real $a$, the class mean of $w_i^2$ is

$$m_a = \frac{1}{n_a} \sum_{i \in r,\; d_i = a} w_i^2, \qquad n_a = \#\{i \in r : d_i = a\},$$

with the convention $x / 0 = 0$: $m_a = 0$ when no $d_i$ equals $a$.
