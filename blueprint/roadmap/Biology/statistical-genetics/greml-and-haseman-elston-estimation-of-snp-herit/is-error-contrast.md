# A diagonalizing orthonormal contrast is an error contrast

Let $m$, $k$ and $r$ be finite index sets, $X$ a real $m \times k$ matrix, $K$ a real $m \times m$
matrix, $B$ a real $r \times m$ matrix and $d \in \mathbb{R}^r$ with $BX = 0$, $BB^\top = I$,
$|r| + |k| = |m|$ and $BKB^\top = \operatorname{diag}(d)$
([diagonalizing orthonormal error contrast](is-spectral-contrast.md)). Then $B$ is an error
contrast for $X$: $BX = 0$, $BB^\top$ is invertible (so $B$ has full row rank) and
$|r| + |k| = |m|$.
