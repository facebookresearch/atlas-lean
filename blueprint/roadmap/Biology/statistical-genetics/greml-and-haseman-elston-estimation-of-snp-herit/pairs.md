# Pairs of individuals with $i < j$

For $N$ individuals labelled $1, \dots, N$, the set of pairs is

$$\{(i, j) : 1 \le i < j \le N\}.$$

Each unordered pair of distinct individuals appears exactly once, and the set is empty when
$N \le 1$. These pairs are the observations of [Haseman–Elston regression](he-estimate.md).
