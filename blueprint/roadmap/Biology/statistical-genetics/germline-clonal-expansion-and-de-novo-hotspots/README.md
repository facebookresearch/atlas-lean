# Germline clonal expansion and de novo mutation hotspots

Mutations that give spermatogonial stem cells a proliferative advantage expand clonally in the testis and are transmitted at inflated rates. Two quantitative handles detect this: an ascertainment bound that de novo enrichment for a disease gene cannot exceed without such inflation, and the mean and variance of population allele counts at constrained sites when the mutation rate is multiplied by an expansion factor.
