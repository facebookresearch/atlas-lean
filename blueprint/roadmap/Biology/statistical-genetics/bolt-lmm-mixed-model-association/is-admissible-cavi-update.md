# Coordinate-ascent update is an admissible factor

Let $X$ be a real $n \times m$ matrix, let $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$, let $\sigma_e^2 > 0$, let $\pi$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ such that $b \mapsto b^2$ is $\pi$-integrable, let $q = (q_1, \dots, q_m)$ be measures on $\mathbb{R}$, and let $j \in \{1, \dots, m\}$. Then the [coordinate-ascent update](cavi-update.md) $q_j^\ast$ is an [admissible factor](is-admissible.md) for $\pi$.
