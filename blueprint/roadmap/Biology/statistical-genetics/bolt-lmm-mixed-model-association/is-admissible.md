# Admissible variational factor

Let $\pi$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$. A measure $q$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is an admissible variational factor for $\pi$ if

- $q$ is a probability measure;
- $b \mapsto b^2$ is $q$-integrable;
- $q$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $\pi$;
- the log-likelihood ratio $\log \frac{dq}{d\pi}$ is $q$-integrable.

The last two conditions make the Kullback–Leibler divergence of $q$ from $\pi$ finite.
