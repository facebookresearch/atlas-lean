# Information of a SNP outside the column space of $X$ is positive

Let $(K, \sigma_g^2, \sigma_e^2)$ be [variance components](model.md) on $n$ individuals, let $X$ be a real $n \times p$ matrix of full column rank with [residual-forming matrix](proj.md) $P$, and let $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ not lie in the column space of $X$. Then

$$x^\top P x > 0.$$
