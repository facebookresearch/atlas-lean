# One coordinate-ascent step

Let $X$, $y$, $\sigma_e^2$, $\pi$ be as for the [coordinate-ascent update](cavi-update.md), let $q = (q_1, \dots, q_m)$ be measures on $\mathbb{R}$ and $j \in \{1, \dots, m\}$. One coordinate-ascent step at $j$ replaces the factor $q_j$ by its update $q_j^\ast$ and leaves the other factors unchanged.
