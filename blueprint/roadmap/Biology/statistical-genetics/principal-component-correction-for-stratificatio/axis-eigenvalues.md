# Eigenvalues of the axes of variation

For a real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $C$, the eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{N-1}$ of [Patterson's relatedness matrix](relatedness-matrix.md) $\Psi$, listed with multiplicity in nonincreasing order; $\lambda_i$ is the eigenvalue of the [axis](axes.md) $a_i$.
