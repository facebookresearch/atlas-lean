# All-ones vector lies in the kernel of the relatedness matrix

For every real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $C$ with $N > 0$, [Patterson's relatedness matrix](relatedness-matrix.md) satisfies $\Psi \mathbf{1} = 0$, where $\mathbf{1} \in \mathbb{R}^N$ is the all-ones vector. Hence at most $N - 1$ eigenvalues of $\Psi$ are nonzero.
