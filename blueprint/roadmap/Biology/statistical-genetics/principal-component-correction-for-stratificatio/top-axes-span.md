# Span of the top axes of variation

For a real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $C$ and a natural number $K$, the span of the top $K$ axes is the linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^N$ spanned by the [axes of variation](axes.md) $a_i$ with $i < K$ (all axes when $K \ge N$). EIGENSTRAT adjusts phenotypes and genotypes for this subspace.
