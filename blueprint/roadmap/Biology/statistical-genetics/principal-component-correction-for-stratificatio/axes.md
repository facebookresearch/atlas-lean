# Axes of variation

For a real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $C$, the axes of variation $a_0, \dots, a_{N-1}$ are a fixed orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^N$ consisting of eigenvectors of [Patterson's relatedness matrix](relatedness-matrix.md) $\Psi$, ordered so that the corresponding [eigenvalues](axis-eigenvalues.md) are nonincreasing.
