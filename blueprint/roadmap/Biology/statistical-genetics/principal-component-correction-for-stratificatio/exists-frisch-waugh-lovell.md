# Frisch–Waugh–Lovell theorem, existence

For every linear subspace $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^N$ and all $y, g \in \mathbb{R}^N$, there is $c \in V$ such that

$$y - c - \frac{\langle r_V(g), r_V(y) \rangle}{\|r_V(g)\|^2}\, g$$

is orthogonal to $V + \operatorname{span}\{g\}$, where $r_V$ is the [residual](adjusted.md) and $x/0 = 0$.
