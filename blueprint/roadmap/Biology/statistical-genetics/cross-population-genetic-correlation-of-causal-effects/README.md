# Cross-population genetic correlation of causal effects

The correlation of SNP effects between two populations measures how much of a trait's genetic architecture they share. Estimates from genotyped SNPs mix two things: the similarity of the causal effects and the similarity of the linkage disequilibrium that makes genotyped SNPs tag them. This topic states the exact LD correction that converts one into the other.
