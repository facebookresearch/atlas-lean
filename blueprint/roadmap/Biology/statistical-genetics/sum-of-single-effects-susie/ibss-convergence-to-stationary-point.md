# Convergence of IBSS to a stationary point of the ELBO

In the [SuSiE model](sum-of-single-effects-model.md), suppose $0 < \sigma < \infty$,
$0 < \sigma_{0l} < \infty$ for all $l = 1, \dots, L$, and $\pi_j > 0$ for all $j = 1, \dots, p$.
Parameterize each factor $q_l$ by its posterior inclusion probabilities $\alpha_l \in \Delta_{p-1}$
and conditional Gaussian means and variances $(\mu_{1l}, \sigma_{1l}^2)$.
Then the sequence of iterates generated by IBSS converges to a limit point that is a stationary point
of the ELBO $F$.

## Expansion

Under the finiteness and positivity assumptions on $\sigma, \sigma_{0l}, \pi_j$, the ELBO is
bounded above by $\log p(y \mid X, \sigma^2, \sigma_0^2)$, each block subproblem has a unique
maximizer (the SER posterior), and the iterates remain in a compact parameter set. Standard
block coordinate-ascent convergence then yields convergence to a stationary point of $F$.
