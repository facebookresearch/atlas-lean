# Functionally informed eQTL fine-mapping

Statistical fine-mapping with a uniform prior often cannot separate variants in tight LD. A variant-level probability of regulatory function, learned from confidently fine-mapped eQTLs and genomic annotations, can serve as a prior that reweights the posterior. This article gives the reweighting of single-effect posteriors and when it is exact.
