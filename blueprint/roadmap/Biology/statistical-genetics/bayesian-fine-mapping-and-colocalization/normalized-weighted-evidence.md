# Normalized weighted evidence

Let $\iota$ be a finite set. For arbitrary real functions $w, \mathrm{BF} : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ and $j \in \iota$, the normalized weighted evidence is

$$\alpha_j(w, \mathrm{BF}) = \frac{w_j \,\mathrm{BF}_j}{\sum_{k \in \iota} w_k \,\mathrm{BF}_k},$$

with the convention $x / 0 = 0$, so that $\alpha_j(w, \mathrm{BF}) = 0$ for every $j$ when the denominator vanishes. No sign or normalization condition is imposed on $w$ or $\mathrm{BF}$.
