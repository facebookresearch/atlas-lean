# Colocalization hypothesis of a causal configuration

Let $\iota$ be a set of variants and let $\iota_\ast = \iota \cup \{\ast\}$, where $\ast$ means "no causal variant". A configuration is a pair $S = (a, b) \in \iota_\ast \times \iota_\ast$, with $a$ the causal variant of trait 1 and $b$ that of trait 2. Its [colocalization hypothesis](coloc-hypothesis.md) is

$$H(S) = \begin{cases} H_0 & a = \ast,\ b = \ast,\\ H_1 & a \in \iota,\ b = \ast,\\ H_2 & a = \ast,\ b \in \iota,\\ H_3 & a, b \in \iota,\ a \ne b,\\ H_4 & a, b \in \iota,\ a = b. \end{cases}$$
