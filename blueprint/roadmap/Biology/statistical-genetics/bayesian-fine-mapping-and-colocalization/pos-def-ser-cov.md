# Single-effect regression covariance is positive definite

Let $I$ be a finite index set, let $\sigma^2$ and $\sigma_0^2$ be nonnegative reals with $\sigma^2 \ne 0$, and let $x \in \mathbb{R}^I$. Then the [single-effect regression covariance](ser-cov.md) $\Sigma(\sigma^2, \sigma_0^2, x) = \sigma_0^2 x x^\top + \sigma^2 \mathrm{I}$ is positive definite.
