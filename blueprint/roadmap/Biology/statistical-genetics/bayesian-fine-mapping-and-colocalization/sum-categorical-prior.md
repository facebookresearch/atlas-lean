# Categorical colocalization priors sum to one

Let $p_1, p_2, p_{12}$ be [colocalization prior parameters](coloc-prior.md) on a finite set $\iota$ of variants. Then the [categorical prior](categorical-prior.md) sums to one over all configurations:

$$\sum_{S \in \iota_\ast \times \iota_\ast} \pi(S) = 1.$$
