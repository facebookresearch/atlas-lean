# Posterior inclusion probabilities sum to one

Let $w$ be [prior weights](prior-weights.md) on a finite set $\iota$, and let $\mathrm{BF} : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathrm{BF}_k > 0$ for every $k$. Then the [posterior inclusion probabilities](pip.md) sum to one:

$$\sum_{j \in \iota} \mathrm{PIP}_j(w, \mathrm{BF}) = 1.$$
