# Nonnegativity of configuration Bayes factors

Let $\iota$ be a set of variants and let $\mathrm{BF}^{(1)}, \mathrm{BF}^{(2)} : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathrm{BF}^{(1)}_j \ge 0$ and $\mathrm{BF}^{(2)}_j \ge 0$ for every $j$. Then the [configuration Bayes factor](coloc-bf.md) satisfies $\mathrm{BF}(S) \ge 0$ for every configuration $S \in \iota_\ast \times \iota_\ast$.
