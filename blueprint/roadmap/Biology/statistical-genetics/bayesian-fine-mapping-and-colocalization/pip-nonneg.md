# Nonnegativity of posterior inclusion probabilities

Let $w$ be [prior weights](prior-weights.md) on a finite set $\iota$, and let $\mathrm{BF} : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathrm{BF}_k > 0$ for every $k$. Then the [posterior inclusion probability](pip.md) satisfies $\mathrm{PIP}_j(w, \mathrm{BF}) \ge 0$ for every $j \in \iota$.
