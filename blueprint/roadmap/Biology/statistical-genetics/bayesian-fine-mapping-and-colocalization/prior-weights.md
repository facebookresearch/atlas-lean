# Prior weights over the causal variant

Let $\iota$ be a finite set (the variants). Prior weights on $\iota$ are a function $w : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $w_j \ge 0$ for every $j$ and $\sum_{j \in \iota} w_j = 1$, that is, a probability distribution on $\iota$, used as the prior over the single causal variant.
