# Null mass of the categorical colocalization prior

For [colocalization prior parameters](coloc-prior.md) $p_1, p_2, p_{12}$ on a finite set of $m$ variants, the null mass is

$$p_0 = 1 - \bigl(m\, p_1 + m\, p_2 + m (m - 1)\, p_1 p_2 + m\, p_{12}\bigr),$$

which is nonnegative by the defining bound of the parameters.
