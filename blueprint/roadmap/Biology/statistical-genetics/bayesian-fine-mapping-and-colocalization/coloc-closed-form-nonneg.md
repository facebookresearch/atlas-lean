# Nonnegativity of closed-form colocalization scores

Let $p_1, p_2, p_{12}$ be [colocalization prior parameters](coloc-prior.md) on a finite set $\iota$ of variants, and let $\mathrm{BF}^{(1)}, \mathrm{BF}^{(2)} : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathrm{BF}^{(1)}_j \ge 0$ and $\mathrm{BF}^{(2)}_j \ge 0$ for every $j$. Then the [closed-form score](coloc-closed-form.md) satisfies $C(h) \ge 0$ for each of the five hypotheses $h$.
