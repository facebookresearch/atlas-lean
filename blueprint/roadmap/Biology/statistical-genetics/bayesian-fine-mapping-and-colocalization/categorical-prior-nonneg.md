# Nonnegativity of categorical configuration priors

Let $p_1, p_2, p_{12}$ be [colocalization prior parameters](coloc-prior.md) on a finite set $\iota$ of variants. Then the [categorical prior](categorical-prior.md) satisfies $\pi(S) \ge 0$ for every configuration $S \in \iota_\ast \times \iota_\ast$.
