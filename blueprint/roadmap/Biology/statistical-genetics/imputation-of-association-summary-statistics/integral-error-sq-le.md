# ImpG is the best linear imputation

In a [second-moment imputation model](model.md) on a measure $P$ with a finite set $\iota$ of typed markers, let $w \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ satisfy $\Sigma_{oo} w = \Sigma_{ot}$. Then for all weights $w' \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$,

$$\int (z_t - w^\top z_o)^2 \, dP \le \int (z_t - w'^\top z_o)^2 \, dP.$$
