# Marginal scores are square integrable

Let `(h², β, ε)` be an [LD-score model](../ld-score-regression/model.md) on a measure `μ` with `N`
individuals and `M` markers, and let `X` be a real `N × M` genotype matrix. Then every
[normalized marginal score](../ld-score-regression/beta-hat.md) `β̂_j` is square integrable with
respect to `μ`.
