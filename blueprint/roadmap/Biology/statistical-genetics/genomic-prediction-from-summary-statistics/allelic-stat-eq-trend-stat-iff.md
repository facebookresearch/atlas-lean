# The allelic test equals the trend test iff the pooled sample is in Hardy–Weinberg proportions

Let $r, s$ be a [case-control genotype table](total.md) with $\sum_i r_i > 0$ and $\sum_i s_i > 0$, [pooled allele frequency](allele-freq.md) $0 < p < 1$, [pooled score variance](score-var.md) $\mathrm{Var} > 0$ and nonzero [trend numerator](numer.md) $U$, and let $n_i = r_i + s_i$. Then (Sasieni 1997)

$$A = T \iff n_1^2 = 4 n_0 n_2,$$

where $A$ is the [allelic test statistic](allelic-stat.md) and $T$ the [trend statistic](trend-stat.md).

## Sources

- P. D. Sasieni, *From genotypes to genes: doubling the sample size*, Biometrics 53 (1997), 1253–1261.
