# Scaled marginal score for the first trait

In a [bivariate LD-score model](cross-trait-model.md) with sample $S_1$, effects $\beta^{(1)}$ and noise $\varepsilon^{(1)}$, and for a real $n \times M$ genotype matrix $X$, the scaled marginal score of marker $j$ for trait 1 is the function

$$z^{(1)}_j = \sqrt{N_1}\, \frac{1}{N_1} \sum_{i \in S_1} X_{ij}\, y^{(1)}_i, \qquad y^{(1)}_i = \sum_{k=1}^{M} X_{ik} \beta^{(1)}_k + \varepsilon^{(1)}_i,$$

on $\Omega$, where $N_1 = |S_1|$ and $x/0 = 0$. The model does not ensure the normalization of a z-score.
