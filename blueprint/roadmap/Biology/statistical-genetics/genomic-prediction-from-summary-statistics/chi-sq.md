# Association statistic in the stratified LD-score model

In a [stratified LD-score model](stratified-model.md) with $N$ individuals, and for a real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $X$, the association statistic of marker $j$ is the function $\chi^2_j = N \hat\beta_j^2$ on $\Omega$, where $\hat\beta_j$ is the [normalized score](beta-hat-2.md).
