# Local and global ancestry interaction models

Models of gene-by-ancestry interaction in admixed individuals in which a variant effect depends either on the local ancestry of the haplotype carrying it or on the individual global ancestry. The two models predict nearly identical average effects but different accuracy of partial polygenic scores.
