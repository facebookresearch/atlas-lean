# The trend statistic is $N$ times a squared correlation

For every [case-control genotype table](total.md) $r, s$ with total $N$ (Armitage 1955),

$$T = N \rho^2,$$

where $T$ is the [Cochran–Armitage trend statistic](trend-stat.md) and $\rho^2$ the [squared correlation of case status and genotype score](corr-sq.md). In degenerate tables, where a denominator vanishes, both sides are $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$.

## Sources

- P. Armitage, *Tests for linear trends in proportions and frequencies*, Biometrics 11 (1955), 375–386.
