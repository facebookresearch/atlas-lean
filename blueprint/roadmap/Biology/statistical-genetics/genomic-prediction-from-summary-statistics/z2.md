# Scaled marginal score for the second trait

In a [bivariate LD-score model](cross-trait-model.md) with sample $S_2$, effects $\beta^{(2)}$ and noise $\varepsilon^{(2)}$, and for a real $n \times M$ genotype matrix $X$, the scaled marginal score of marker $j$ for trait 2 is the function

$$z^{(2)}_j = \sqrt{N_2}\, \frac{1}{N_2} \sum_{i \in S_2} X_{ij}\, y^{(2)}_i, \qquad y^{(2)}_i = \sum_{k=1}^{M} X_{ik} \beta^{(2)}_k + \varepsilon^{(2)}_i,$$

on $\Omega$, where $N_2 = |S_2|$ and $x/0 = 0$.
