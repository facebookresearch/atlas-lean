# Genotype-phenotype cross products from marginal estimates

For every real $n \times \iota$ matrix $X$ ($\iota$ a finite set) and every $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$ (Yang et al. 2012),

$$X^\top y = D \hat\beta,$$

where $D = \operatorname{diag}(X^\top X)$ is the [diagonal of the Gram matrix](gram-diag.md) and $\hat\beta$ the vector of [marginal estimates](marginal-estimate.md). At a zero column both sides vanish.

## Sources

- J. Yang et al., *Conditional and joint multiple-SNP analysis of GWAS summary statistics identifies additional variants influencing complex traits*, Nat. Genet. 44 (2012), 369–375.
