# Departure of the genotype-score variance from Hardy–Weinberg

For every [case-control genotype table](total.md) $r, s$ with total $N \ne 0$ and pooled counts $n_i = r_i + s_i$,

$$\mathrm{Var} - 2p(1 - p) = \frac{4 n_0 n_2 - n_1^2}{2N^2},$$

where $\mathrm{Var}$ is the [pooled score variance](score-var.md) and $p$ the [pooled allele frequency](allele-freq.md). For nonnegative counts, $\mathrm{Var}$ is the empirical score variance and $2p(1-p)$ the score variance under Hardy–Weinberg proportions with allele frequency $p$.
