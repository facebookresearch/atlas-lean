# Integral of a product of two finite linear combinations

Let `μ` be a measure on a measurable space `Ω`, let `I` and `K` be finite index sets, let `f_k`
(`k ∈ I`) and `g_l` (`l ∈ K`) be real functions on `Ω` that are square integrable with respect to
`μ`, and let `a_k` and `b_l` be real numbers. Then

`∫ (∑_k a_k f_k) (∑_l b_l g_l) dμ = ∑_k ∑_l a_k b_l ∫ f_k g_l dμ`.
