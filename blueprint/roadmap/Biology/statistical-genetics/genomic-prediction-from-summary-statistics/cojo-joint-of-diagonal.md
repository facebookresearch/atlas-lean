# Joint estimate equals marginal estimates for uncorrelated markers

Let $X$ be a real $n \times \iota$ matrix ($\iota$ a finite set) such that $X^\top X$ is diagonal and $(X^\top X)_{jj} \ne 0$ for every $j$, and let $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$. Then

$$(X^\top X)^{-1} X^\top y = \hat\beta,$$

the vector of [marginal estimates](marginal-estimate.md).
