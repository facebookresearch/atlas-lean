# The training integral expands into effect cross moments

Let `μ` be a measure on a measurable space `Ω`, let `S` be a real `M × M` matrix, and let
`a = (a_1, …, a_M)` and `b = (b_1, …, b_M)` be real functions on `Ω` such that every `a_j` and every
`b_k` is square integrable with respect to `μ`. Then the
[training integral of the target cross moment](quad-cov.md) is

`C_S(a, b) = ∑_j ∑_k S_jk ∫ a_j b_k dμ`.
