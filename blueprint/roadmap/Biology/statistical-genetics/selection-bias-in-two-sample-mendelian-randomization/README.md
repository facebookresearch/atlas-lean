# Selection bias in two-sample Mendelian randomization

Nonrandom selection into the exposure and outcome samples of a two-sample Mendelian randomization study conditions on variables in the instrument-exposure-outcome causal graph. Conditional-independence criteria characterize when the Wald ratio is still the population causal effect.
