# First-moment identity, exact in-sample form

For every [first-moment SLDP model](model.md) on a measure $P$ with mean effects $m$, every real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $X$ and every marker $j$,

$$\int \hat\alpha_j \, dP = (\hat R\, m)_j,$$

where $\hat\alpha$ is the vector of [normalized marginal scores](alpha-hat.md) and $\hat R$ the [in-sample LD matrix](ld-matrix.md).
