# Phenotype in the SLDP regression model

In a [first-moment SLDP model](model.md) with effects $\beta_k$ and noise $\varepsilon_i$, and for a real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $X$, the phenotype of individual $i$ is the function

$$y_i = \sum_{k=1}^{M} X_{ik} \beta_k + \varepsilon_i$$

on $\Omega$.
