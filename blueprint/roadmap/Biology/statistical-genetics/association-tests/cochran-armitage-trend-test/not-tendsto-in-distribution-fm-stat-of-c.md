# Singular fixed-margin design: no χ²₁ limit

Let `P` be a probability measure, `R_N ≤ N` for every `N`, and `G_{N,k}` random genotype classes
with `G_{N,k} = c` almost surely for every `N` and every `k < N`, for one class `c`. Then the
[fixed-margin statistic](fm-stat.md) `T(R_N, N, G_{N,·})` does not converge in distribution to the
[χ² law with one degree of freedom](../../../../Probability/distributions/continuous/chi-square-distribution/chi-square-measure.md):
it is almost surely `0` for every `N`,
[since all subjects are in one class](fm-stat-eq-zero-of-const.md). No independence is assumed. The
hypothesis of positive score variance in the
[asymptotic theorem](tendsto-in-distribution-fm-stat.md) cannot be dropped.
