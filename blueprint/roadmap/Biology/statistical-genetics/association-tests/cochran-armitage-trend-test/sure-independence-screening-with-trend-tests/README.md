# Sure independence screening with trend tests

Marginal screening of SNPs in ultra-high-dimensional case–control studies by ranking sample-size-normalized Cochran–Armitage trend statistics, for a fixed genetic model or maximized over the recessive, additive and dominant score vectors. Under bounded genotype frequencies and a signal-strength gap that may shrink polynomially in $n$, the procedures keep every associated SNP with probability tending to one even when the number of SNPs grows exponentially in a power of $n$.
