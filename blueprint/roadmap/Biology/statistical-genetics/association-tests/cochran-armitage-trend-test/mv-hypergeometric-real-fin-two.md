# With two colours the multivariate hypergeometric law is hypergeometric

For natural numbers `N, K, R, k` with `K ≤ N` and `k ≤ R`, the
[multivariate hypergeometric mass](mv-hypergeometric-real.md) with two colours of sizes `K` and
`N − K`, `R` draws and colour counts `k` and `R − k` equals the
[hypergeometric mass function](../../../../Probability/distributions/discrete/hypergeometric-distribution/hypergeometric-real.md)
`C(K, k) C(N − K, R − k) / C(N, R)` of `k` marked items in `R` draws from `N` items of which `K` are
marked.
