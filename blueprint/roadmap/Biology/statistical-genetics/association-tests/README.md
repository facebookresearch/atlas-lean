# Association tests

Tests used in genetic association studies, and their calibration: tests of association between
genotypes and a trait (the Cochran–Armitage trend test of genotype counts, the SKAT
variance-component test with relatedness, genomic control) and the exact and asymptotic tests of
Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium of genotype counts that accompany them. Fisher's exact test, the burden
test and SKAT-O, multiple testing across genes, and genomic control with an estimated inflation
factor are under [Hypothesis testing](../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/README.md) in
Probability. The chapter also collects tests and models for gene–gene interaction (epistasis),
joint location–scale and multivariate association tests, family-based randomization tests, and
association models for cell-type-specific methylation, pathways and sparse sets of markers.
