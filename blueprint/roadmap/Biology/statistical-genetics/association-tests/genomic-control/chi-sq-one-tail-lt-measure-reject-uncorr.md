# With λ > 1 the uncorrected test is anti-conservative

Consider a [common-inflation model](common-inflation-model.md) on a measure `P`, with inflation
factor `λ > 1` and null scores `Z_j`, each with law `𝒩(0, λ)` under `P`. For every marker `j` and
every threshold `c > 0`,

`P(χ²₁ > c) < P(Z_j² > c)`:

without the correction, the test that rejects marker `j` when `χ²_j = Z_j²` exceeds `c` rejects
strictly more often than its nominal level, the [`χ²₁` tail](chi-sq-one-tail.md) at `c`.
