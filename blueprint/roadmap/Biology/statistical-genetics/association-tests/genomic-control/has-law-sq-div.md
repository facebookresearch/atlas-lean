# The corrected statistic has law χ²₁ in the common-inflation model

Consider a [common-inflation model](common-inflation-model.md) on a measure `P`, with inflation
factor `λ > 0` and null scores `Z_j`, each with law `𝒩(0, λ)` under `P`. For every marker `j`,
the corrected statistic `Z_j² / λ` has law `χ²₁` under `P`: the image of `P` under it is the
[chi-square law](../../../../Probability/distributions/continuous/chi-square-distribution/chi-square-measure.md)
with one degree of freedom.
