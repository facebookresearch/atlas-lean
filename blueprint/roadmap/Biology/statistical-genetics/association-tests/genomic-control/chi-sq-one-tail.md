# Tail of the chi-square law with one degree of freedom

For a real threshold `c`, the `χ²₁` tail is `P(χ²₁ > c)`: the mass that the
[chi-square law](../../../../Probability/distributions/continuous/chi-square-distribution/chi-square-measure.md)
with one degree of freedom gives to the half-line `(c, ∞)`, as an extended nonnegative real
number. It is defined for every real `c` and lies in `[0, 1]`; for `c ≤ 0` it equals `1`, since
the law is carried by the positive reals and has no atom at `0`.
