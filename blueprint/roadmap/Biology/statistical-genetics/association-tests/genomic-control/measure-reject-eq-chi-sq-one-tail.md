# Tail of a centred Gaussian square divided by a positive constant

Let `P` be a measure on a measurable space `Ω` and let `Z` be a real function on `Ω` with law
`𝒩(0, v)` under `P`, for a variance `v > 0` (so `P` is a probability measure). For every real
`k > 0` and every real threshold `c`,

`P(Z² / k > c) = P(χ²₁ > c k / v)`:

the test that rejects when `Z² / k > c` has rejection probability equal to the
[`χ²₁` tail](chi-sq-one-tail.md) at `c k / v`, which is the tail at `c` when `k = v`.
