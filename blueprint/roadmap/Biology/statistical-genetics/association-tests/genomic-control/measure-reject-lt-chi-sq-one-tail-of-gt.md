# Dividing by λ above the null variance is conservative

Let `P` be a measure, let `Z` be a real random variable with law `𝒩(0, v)` under `P` for a
variance `v > 0`, and let `λ` be a real number with `λ > v`. For every threshold `c > 0`,

`P(Z² / λ > c) < P(χ²₁ > c)`:

a marker whose null variance `v` is below the factor `λ` used is strictly conservative after
genomic control. Here `P(χ²₁ > c)` is the [`χ²₁` tail](chi-sq-one-tail.md).
