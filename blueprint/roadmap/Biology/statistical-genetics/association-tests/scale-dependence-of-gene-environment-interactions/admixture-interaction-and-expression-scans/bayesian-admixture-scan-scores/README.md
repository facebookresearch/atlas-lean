# Bayesian admixture scan scores

An admixture scan asks, locus by locus, whether local ancestry in a recently admixed sample deviates from the genome-wide average in a way the phenotype predicts. Averaging likelihood ratios over a prespecified family of risk models gives a locus score, averaging again over loci gives a genome-wide Bayes factor and a credible interval for the position, and contrasting cases with controls gives a score that survives systematic error in the inferred ancestry.
