# Additive relationship matrix of a pedigree

For a [pedigree](../../../quantitative-genetics/pedigree-kinship-and-inbreeding/pedigree.md) on `N`
individuals, the additive relationship matrix is the `N × N` matrix `2 φ` with entries `2 φᵢⱼ`,
where `φ` is the tabular
[kinship coefficient by Malécot's recursion](../../../quantitative-genetics/pedigree-kinship-and-inbreeding/kinship.md).
