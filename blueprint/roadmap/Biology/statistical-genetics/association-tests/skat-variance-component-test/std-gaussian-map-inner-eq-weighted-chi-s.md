# A standard Gaussian quadratic form has a weighted chi-square law

Let `A` be a real `n × n` matrix, `n` a finite index set, and let `b₁, …, bₙ` be an orthonormal
basis of the Euclidean space `ℝⁿ` made of eigenvectors of `A`, with `A bⱼ = λⱼ bⱼ`. If `Z` has the
standard Gaussian law on `ℝⁿ`, then the quadratic form `⟪Z, A Z⟫` has the weighted chi-square law
`∑ⱼ λⱼ χ²₁`, the law of `∑ⱼ λⱼ ξⱼ²` for independent standard normal `ξⱼ`.
