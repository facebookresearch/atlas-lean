# A singular covariance confines the phenotype to a hyperplane

Let `S` be a positive semidefinite real `n × n` matrix and `v ∈ ℝⁿ` with `S v = 0`. For every
`μ ∈ ℝⁿ`, if `y` has the Gaussian law `N(μ, S)` with mean `μ` and covariance `S`, then almost surely
`vᵀ (y - μ) = 0`. When `v ≠ 0`, and such a `v` exists when `S` is singular, `y` lies almost surely
on the hyperplane through `μ` orthogonal to `v`.
