# Diagonal entries of Gᵀ N G are quadratic forms in the columns of G

For a real `n × m` matrix `G` with columns `g₁, …, gₘ`, every real `n × n` matrix `N` and every `j`,

`(Gᵀ N G)ⱼⱼ = gⱼᵀ N gⱼ`.

With `N = P` the [null projection](proj-p.md), `wⱼ gⱼᵀ P gⱼ` is the term of variant `j` in the
[null mean of the SKAT statistic](integral-skat-q-null-eq-sum.md).
