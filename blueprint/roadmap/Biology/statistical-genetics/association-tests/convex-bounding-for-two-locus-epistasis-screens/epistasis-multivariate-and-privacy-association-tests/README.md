# Epistasis, multivariate and privacy association tests

Association methods beyond single-SNP tests of one trait: screens for epistatic SNP interactions,
joint tests of several traits or of trait mean and variance, structured and sparse models of
causal markers, randomization tests in trio studies, cell-type-specific methylation association,
and the genotype data and privacy guarantees that association studies rest on. Reading order does
not assert a mathematical dependency.
