# The sparse table violates Cochran's rule

The table of $N = 10$ individuals with genotype counts $(9, 0, 1)$ (two copies of $A$, carried by
one homozygote) is not in the [chi-square regime](in-chi-sq-regime.md) with $c = 5$: its sample
allele frequency is $\hat p = 1/10$ and its expected $AA$ count is $10 \cdot (1/10)^2 = 0.1 < 5$.
