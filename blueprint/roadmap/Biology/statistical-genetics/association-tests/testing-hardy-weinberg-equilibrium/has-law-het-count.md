# The uniform-placement model realizes Levene's law

For all natural numbers $N$ and $n_A$, under the [uniform placement model](uniform-arrangements.md)
$U_{N, n_A}$, the uniform law on the sets of $n_A$ of the $2N$ gamete slots, the
[number of heterozygous individuals](../../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/exact-test-for-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium/het-count.md)
has [Levene's law](levene-measure.md) $\lambda_{N, n_A}$: its image measure is $\lambda_{N, n_A}$.
For $n_A > 2N$ both measures are zero (Levene 1949; Wigginton, Cutler and Abecasis 2005).
