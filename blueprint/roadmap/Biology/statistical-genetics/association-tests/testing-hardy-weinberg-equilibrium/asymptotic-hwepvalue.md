# Asymptotic chi-square p-value for Hardy–Weinberg

For natural numbers $N$, $n_A$ and $h$, the asymptotic p-value is the
[chi-square tail](../genomic-control/chi-sq-one-tail.md) with one degree of freedom at the
[heterozygote-count statistic](hwe-chi-sq-of-het.md) $X^2$:

$$P\bigl(\chi^2_1 > X^2\bigr),$$

as a real number in $[0, 1]$.
