# Hardy–Weinberg genotype proportions

A biallelic locus has alleles $A$ and $B$; the genotype class $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ of an individual
is its number of copies of $A$. For a real number $p$, the Hardy–Weinberg proportions at allele
frequency $p$ of $A$ are

$$\pi(p) = \bigl((1 - p)^2,\ 2p(1 - p),\ p^2\bigr),$$

indexed by the genotype classes $0, 1, 2$. They [sum to one](sum-hwe-prob.md) for every real $p$;
they are probabilities, the genotype probabilities of a population in Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium,
when $0 \le p \le 1$. For $p$ outside $[0, 1]$ the middle entry $2p(1 - p)$ is negative.
