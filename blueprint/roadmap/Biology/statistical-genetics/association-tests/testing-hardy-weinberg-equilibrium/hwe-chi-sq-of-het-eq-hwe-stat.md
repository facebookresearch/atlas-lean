# The heterozygote-count statistic is the genotype-table statistic

For natural numbers $n_0$, $n_1$ and $n_2$, the numbers of individuals carrying $0$, $1$ and $2$
copies of $A$, the [heterozygote-count statistic](hwe-chi-sq-of-het.md) at the sample size
$N = n_0 + n_1 + n_2$, the allele count $n_A = n_1 + 2 n_2$ and the heterozygote count $n_1$ is the
[fitted Hardy–Weinberg statistic](hwe-stat.md) of the table: $X^2(N, (n_0, n_1, n_2))$. The floor
divisions and truncated subtractions are exact on every realizable table.
