# Uniform placement of the A alleles in the gamete slots

For natural numbers $N$ and $n_A$, the uniform placement model $U_{N, n_A}$ is the uniform measure
on the sets of $n_A$ of the $2N$ gamete slots $(j, c)$, $j \in \{0, \dots, N - 1\}$,
$c \in \{0, 1\}$, two per individual: each such set, the slots occupied by the copies of $A$, has
measure $\binom{2N}{n_A}^{-1}$ and the other sets of slots have measure $0$. It is the
Hardy–Weinberg null model conditional on the allele count, a probability measure when $n_A \le 2N$;
for $n_A > 2N$ there is no such set and it is the zero measure.
