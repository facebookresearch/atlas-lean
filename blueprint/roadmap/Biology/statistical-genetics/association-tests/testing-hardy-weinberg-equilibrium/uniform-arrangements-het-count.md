# Uniform placement gives the heterozygote count Levene's probabilities

For all natural numbers $N$, $n_A$ and $h$, the [uniform placement model](uniform-arrangements.md)
$U_{N, n_A}$, the uniform law on the sets of $n_A$ of the $2N$ gamete slots, gives the event that
the
[number of heterozygous individuals](../../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/exact-test-for-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium/het-count.md)
equals $h$ the
[Levene probability](../../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/exact-test-for-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium/levene-prob.md):

$$U_{N, n_A}\bigl(\{s : \mathrm{het}(s) = h\}\bigr) = L(N, n_A, h).$$

For $n_A > 2N$ both sides are $0$.
