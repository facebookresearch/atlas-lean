# Hardy–Weinberg proportions sum to one

For every real $p$ the [Hardy–Weinberg proportions](hwe-prob.md)
$\pi(p) = ((1 - p)^2, 2p(1 - p), p^2)$ sum to one:

$$(1 - p)^2 + 2p(1 - p) + p^2 = 1.$$

No condition on $p$ is needed; for $p$ outside $[0, 1]$ the middle term is negative, so the
proportions are then not probabilities.
