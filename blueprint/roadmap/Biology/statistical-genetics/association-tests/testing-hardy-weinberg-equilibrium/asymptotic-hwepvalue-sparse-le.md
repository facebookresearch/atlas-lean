# The asymptotic p-value of the sparse observation is at most 0.05

For $N = 10$ individuals carrying $n_A = 2$ copies of $A$ and no heterozygote, the
[heterozygote-count statistic](hwe-chi-sq-of-het.md) is $10$ and the
[asymptotic p-value](asymptotic-hwepvalue.md) $P(\chi^2_1 > 10)$ is at most $1/20$.
