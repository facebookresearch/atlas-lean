# Levene probabilities are nonnegative

For all natural numbers $N$, $n_A$ and $h$,
[Levene's probability](../../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/exact-test-for-hardy-weinberg-equilibrium/levene-prob.md)
$L(N, n_A, h)$ of $h$ heterozygotes among $N$ individuals carrying $n_A$ copies of $A$, is
nonnegative: $L(N, n_A, h) \ge 0$.
