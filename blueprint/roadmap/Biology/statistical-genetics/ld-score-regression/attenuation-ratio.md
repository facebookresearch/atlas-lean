# Attenuation ratio

For real numbers $b_0$ (an LD-score regression intercept) and $\bar\chi^2$ (a mean association statistic), the attenuation ratio is

$$R = \frac{b_0 - 1}{\bar\chi^2 - 1},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$. Loh et al. (2018) read it as the share of the mean inflation not explained by polygenicity.

## Sources

- P.-R. Loh, G. Kichaev, S. Gazal, A. P. Schoech, A. L. Price, *Mixed-model association for biobank-scale datasets*, Nat. Genet. 50 (2018), 906–908.
