# Mean association statistic with constant stratification

Under the hypotheses of [LD-score regression with constant stratification](integral-chi-sq-strat-of-const.md), and if moreover $M \ne 0$,

$$\frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} \int \chi^2_j \, d\mu = \frac{N h^2}{M}\, \bar\ell + (1 - h^2 + N a),$$

where $\chi^2_j$ is the [association statistic under stratification](chi-sq-strat.md) and $\bar\ell$ the [mean LD score](mean-ldscore.md).
