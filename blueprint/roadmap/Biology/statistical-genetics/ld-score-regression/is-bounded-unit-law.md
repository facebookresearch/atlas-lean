# Centred law with unit variance and bounded support

A measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a real number $C$ satisfy the bounded unit law condition if
$\int x \, d\nu(x) = 0$, $\int x^2 \, d\nu(x) = 1$ and $|x| \leq C$ for $\nu$-almost every $x$. For
a probability measure $\nu$ this says that $\nu$ is centred, has unit variance and is supported in
$[-C, C]$; the Rademacher law with $C = 1$ is an example.
