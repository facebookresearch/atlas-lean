# The unweighted LD-score regression estimator is integrable

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $D$ an [LD-score model](model.md) under $\mu$ with $N$
individuals and $M$ markers. For every real $N \times M$ matrix $X$, every $s \in \mathbb{R}$ and
$c \in \mathbb{R}^N$, the [unweighted estimator](ols-h2-hat.md) $\hat h^2$ computed from the
[LD scores](ld-score.md) of $X$ and the
[association statistics under stratification](chi-sq-strat.md) $\chi^2_1, \dots, \chi^2_M$ is
integrable.
