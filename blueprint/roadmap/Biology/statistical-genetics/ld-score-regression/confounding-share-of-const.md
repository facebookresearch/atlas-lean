# Confounding share of inflation under constant stratification

Under the hypotheses of the [attenuation ratio under constant stratification](attenuation-ratio-of-const.md), with $\bar\chi^2 = \frac1M \sum_j \int \chi^2_j \, d\mu$,

$$\frac{(1 - h^2 + N a) - (1 - h^2)}{\bar\chi^2 - (1 - h^2)} = \frac{N a}{N a + N h^2 \bar\ell / M},$$

where $\bar\ell$ is the [mean LD score](mean-ldscore.md), with the convention $x/0 = 0$. The left side is the share of the mean inflation above the null level $1 - h^2$ of the model that is due to stratification.
