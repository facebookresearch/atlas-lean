# The association statistic under stratification is integrable

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $D$ an [LD-score model](model.md) under $\mu$ with $N$
individuals and $M$ markers. For every real $N \times M$ matrix $X$, every $s \in \mathbb{R}$,
$c \in \mathbb{R}^N$ and every marker $j$, the
[association statistic under stratification](chi-sq-strat.md) $\chi^2_j$ is integrable.
