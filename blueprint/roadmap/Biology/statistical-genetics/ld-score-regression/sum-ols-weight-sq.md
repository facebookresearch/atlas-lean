# The squared least-squares weights sum to the reciprocal spread

For a real vector $x$ indexed by a finite set, with mean $\bar x$ and spread
$S_{xx} = \sum_j (x_j - \bar x)^2$, $\sum_j \bigl((x_j - \bar x) / S_{xx}\bigr)^2 = 1 / S_{xx}$,
with the convention $x/0 = 0$: both sides are $0$ when $S_{xx} = 0$.
