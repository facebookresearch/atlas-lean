# Statistics of different blocks are independent

For a [block design](block-design.md), a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$ and $h^2 \in [0, 1]$, the
statistics $\chi^2_j$ and $\chi^2_k$ of the [model on the product space](model-2.md), without
stratification, are independent under the [product measure](coord-measure.md) whenever the markers
$j$ and $k$ lie in different blocks.
