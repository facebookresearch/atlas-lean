# The LD-score spread of the replicated design

For a real $g \times k$ matrix $T$ and a natural number $b \neq 0$, the spread
$S_{\ell\ell} = \sum_j (\ell_j - \bar\ell)^2$ of the [LD scores](ld-score.md) of the
[replicated matrix](replicate.md) with $b$ copies is $b$ times the spread of the LD scores of $T$.
