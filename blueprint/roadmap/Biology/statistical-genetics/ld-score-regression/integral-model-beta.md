# The effects of the product-space model have mean zero

For a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$, $h^2 \in [0, 1]$ and all
natural numbers $N$ and $M$, every effect $\beta_k$ of the [model on the product space](model-2.md)
satisfies $\int \beta_k \, d\nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}} = 0$.
