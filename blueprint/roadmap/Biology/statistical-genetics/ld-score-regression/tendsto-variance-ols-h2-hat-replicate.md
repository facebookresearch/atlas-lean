# Vanishing estimator variance along the replicated designs

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$, $h^2 \in [0, 1]$, $T$ a
[standardized](is-standardized.md) real $g \times k$ template matrix whose LD scores have positive
spread $S_{\ell\ell}(T) > 0$, and, along a filter on an index set, $b_n \neq 0$ numbers of copies
with $b_n \to \infty$. Then the variance of the [unweighted estimator](ols-h2-hat.md) $\hat h^2_n$
computed from the LD scores of the [replicated matrix](replicate.md) with $b_n$ copies and the
statistics of the [model on the product space](model-2.md) with $b_n g$ individuals and $b_n k$
markers, without stratification, tends to $0$.
