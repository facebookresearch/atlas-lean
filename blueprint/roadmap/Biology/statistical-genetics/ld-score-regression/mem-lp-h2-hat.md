# The LD-score regression estimator is square integrable

For a measure $\mu$, natural numbers $N$ and $M$, real numbers $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_M$ and
square-integrable statistics $\chi_1, \dots, \chi_M$, the estimator $\hat h^2 = \hat b\, M / N$,
with $\hat b$ the least-squares slope $S_{\ell\chi} / S_{\ell\ell}$ of the statistics on $\ell$,
where $S_{xy} = \sum_j (x_j - \bar x)(y_j - \bar y)$, is square integrable.
