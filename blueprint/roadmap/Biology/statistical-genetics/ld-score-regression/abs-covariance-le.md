# Covariances under an LD-block model are bounded by the variance bound

For a finite measure $\mu$ and statistics $\chi_1, \dots, \chi_M$ satisfying an
[LD-block model](ldblock-model.md) with variance bound $V$,
$|\operatorname{Cov}(\chi_j, \chi_k)| \leq V$ for all markers $j$ and $k$, the covariance being
taken with respect to $\mu$.
