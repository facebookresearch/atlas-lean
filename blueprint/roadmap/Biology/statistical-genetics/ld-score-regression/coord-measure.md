# Product measure with independent coordinates of law ν

For a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the product measure $\nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}$ on
the space $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}}$ of real sequences $\omega = (\omega_0, \omega_1, \dots)$: under
it the coordinates $\omega_s$ are independent with law $\nu$.
