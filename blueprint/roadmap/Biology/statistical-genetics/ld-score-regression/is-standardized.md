# Standardized genotype matrix

A real $N \times M$ matrix $X$ is standardized if every column has sum $0$ and sum of squares $N$:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{N} X_{ik} = 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \sum_{i=1}^{N} X_{ik}^2 = N \qquad \text{for every } k,$$

for $N > 0$, that is, every marker has sample mean $0$ and unit sample variance.
