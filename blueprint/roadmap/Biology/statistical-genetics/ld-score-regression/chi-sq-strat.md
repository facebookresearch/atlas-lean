# Association statistic under stratification

In an [LD-score model](model.md) with $N$ individuals, for a real $N \times M$ genotype matrix $X$, a real number $s$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}^N$, the association statistic of marker $j$ under stratification is the function $\chi^2_j = N \hat\beta_j^2$ on $\Omega$, where $\hat\beta_j$ is the [normalized score under stratification](beta-hat-strat.md).
