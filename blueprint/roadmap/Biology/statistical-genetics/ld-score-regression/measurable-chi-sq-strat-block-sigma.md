# The statistic of a marker depends only on the coordinates of its block

For a [block design](block-design.md), a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$, $h^2 \in [0, 1]$ and a marker
$j$, the statistic $\chi^2_j$ of the [model on the product space](model-2.md), without
stratification, is measurable with respect to the [σ-algebra of the coordinates](block-sigma.md) of
the block of $j$.
