# The LD-block model holds along a block design

For a [block design](block-design.md) with $N$ individuals, $M$ markers, block size $K$, cohort size
$G$ and genotype matrix $X$, a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$, $h^2 \in [0, 1]$ and
$\lambda \geq 0$ with $N \leq \lambda M$, the statistics $\chi^2_1, \dots, \chi^2_M$ of the
[model on the product space](model-2.md), without stratification, satisfy an
[LD-block model](ldblock-model.md) under the [product measure](coord-measure.md) with the blocks of
the design, block size $K$ and variance bound $(B/2)^2$, where $B = G \cdot 2 C^2 (K^2 \lambda + 1)$
is the [bound constant](chi-sq-bound.md).
