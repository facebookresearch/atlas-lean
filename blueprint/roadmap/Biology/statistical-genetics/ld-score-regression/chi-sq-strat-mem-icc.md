# Bound on the statistics of a block design at bounded coordinates

For a [block design](block-design.md) with $N$ individuals, $M$ markers, block size $K$, cohort size
$G$ and genotype matrix $X$, a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$, $h^2 \in [0, 1]$ and
$\lambda \geq 0$ with $N \leq \lambda M$, and $\omega \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}}$ with
$|\omega_s| \leq C$ for every $s$, the statistic $\chi^2_j(\omega)$ of the
[model on the product space](model-2.md), without stratification, lies in $[0, B]$ for every marker
$j$, where $B = G \cdot 2 C^2 (K^2 \lambda + 1)$ is the [bound constant](chi-sq-bound.md). Since
[almost surely](ae-abs-coord-le.md) every coordinate is bounded by $C$ in absolute value, the bound
holds almost surely.
