# Bound constant for the statistics of a block design

For natural numbers $K$ and $G$ and real numbers $C$ and $\lambda$, the bound constant is
$B(K, G, C, \lambda) = G \cdot 2 C^2 (K^2 \lambda + 1)$, the
[bound on the association statistics](chi-sq-strat-mem-icc.md) along a block design with block size
$K$ and cohort size $G$.
