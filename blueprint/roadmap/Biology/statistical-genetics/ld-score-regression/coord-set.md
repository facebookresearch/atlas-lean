# Coordinates used by an LD block

For a [block design](block-design.md) with blocks $b(j)$ and cohorts $g(i)$ and a block label
$u \in \mathbb{N}$, the set of coordinates used by block $u$ is
$\{2k : b(k) = u\} \cup \{2i + 1 : g(i) = u\}$: the coordinates of the effects of its markers and of
the noise of its cohort in the [model on the product space](model-2.md).
