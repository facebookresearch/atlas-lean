# The noise of the product-space model has mean zero

For a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the
[bounded unit law](is-bounded-unit-law.md) condition with bound $C$, $h^2 \in [0, 1]$ and all
natural numbers $N$ and $M$, every noise term $\varepsilon_i$ of the
[model on the product space](model-2.md) satisfies
$\int \varepsilon_i \, d\nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}} = 0$.
