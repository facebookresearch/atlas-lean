# Normalized cross products of the replicated matrix

For a real $g \times k$ matrix $T$ and a natural number $b \neq 0$, the
[normalized cross product](ld-corr.md) in the [replicated matrix](replicate.md) with $b$ copies
between marker $a$ of copy $u$ and marker $a'$ of copy $u'$ is the normalized cross product
$r_{a a'}(T)$ of $T$ if $u = u'$, and $0$ otherwise.
