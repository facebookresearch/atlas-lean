# De novo variant burden and the number of risk genes

Comparing the rate of damaging de novo variants in affected probands with the rate in unaffected controls estimates what fraction of the case variants actually carry risk. The pattern of recurrence, how many genes are hit more than once, then constrains how many genes those risk-carrying variants are spread over.
