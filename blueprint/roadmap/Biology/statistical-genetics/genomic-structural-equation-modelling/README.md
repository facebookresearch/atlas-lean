# Genomic structural equation modelling

Genomic structural equation modelling (genomic SEM) fits latent-factor models to the genetic covariance matrix of several traits estimated from GWAS summary statistics by multivariable LD-score regression, weighting by the sampling covariance of those estimates so that samples may overlap to an unknown degree. These articles state the genome-wide factor model with SNP-level extensions and the annotation-stratified enrichment of factor variances.
