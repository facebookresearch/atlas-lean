# Genetic correlation in the latent causal variable model

In every [latent causal variable model](model.md) on a probability measure $P$, the [marginal effects](alpha1.md) satisfy (O'Connor and Price 2018, after Equation 1)

$$\int \alpha_1 \alpha_2 \, dP = q_1 q_2.$$

This common value is the genetic correlation $\rho_g$.

## Sources

- L. J. O'Connor, A. L. Price, *Distinguishing genetic correlation from causation across 52 diseases and complex traits*, Nat. Genet. 50 (2018), 1728–1734.
