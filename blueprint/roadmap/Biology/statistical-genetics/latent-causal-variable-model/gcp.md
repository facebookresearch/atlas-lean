# Genetic causality proportion

For real numbers $q_1$ and $q_2$, the genetic causality proportion is (O'Connor and Price 2018)

$$\mathrm{gcp}(q_1, q_2) = \frac{\log|q_2| - \log|q_1|}{\log|q_2| + \log|q_1|},$$

with the conventions $\log 0 = 0$ and $x/0 = 0$. It is meaningful for $0 < |q_1|, |q_2| \le 1$ with $(q_1 q_2)^2 < 1$; for $|q_1| = |q_2| = 1$ the denominator vanishes and the value is $0$.

## Sources

- L. J. O'Connor, A. L. Price, *Distinguishing genetic correlation from causation across 52 diseases and complex traits*, Nat. Genet. 50 (2018), 1728–1734.
