# Fourth-moment criterion for a nonnegative genetic causality proportion

In a [latent causal variable model](model.md) on a probability measure $P$, suppose that the [excess kurtosis](kurtosis.md) satisfies $\kappa_\pi > 0$, that $q_1 q_2 \ne 0$ and $(q_1 q_2)^2 < 1$, and that (O'Connor and Price 2018, after Equation 2)

$$\Bigl|\int \alpha_1 \alpha_2^3 \, dP\Bigr| \le \Bigl|\int \alpha_1^3 \alpha_2 \, dP\Bigr|,$$

where $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ are the [marginal effects](alpha1.md). Then the [genetic causality proportion](gcp.md) satisfies $\mathrm{gcp}(q_1, q_2) \ge 0$.

## Sources

- L. J. O'Connor, A. L. Price, *Distinguishing genetic correlation from causation across 52 diseases and complex traits*, Nat. Genet. 50 (2018), 1728–1734.
