# Mixed fourth moment $E(\alpha_1 \alpha_2^3)$

In every [latent causal variable model](model.md) on a probability measure $P$, the [marginal effects](alpha1.md) satisfy

$$\int \alpha_1 \alpha_2^3 \, dP = \kappa_\pi\, q_1 q_2^3 + 3\, q_1 q_2,$$

where $\kappa_\pi$ is the [excess kurtosis](kurtosis.md) of $\pi$.
