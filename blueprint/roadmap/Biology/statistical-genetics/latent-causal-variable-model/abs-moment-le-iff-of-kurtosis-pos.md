# Uncentred moment comparison for positive excess kurtosis

In a [latent causal variable model](model.md) on a probability measure $P$, suppose that the [excess kurtosis](kurtosis.md) satisfies $\kappa_\pi > 0$ and that $q_1 q_2 \ne 0$. Then

$$\Bigl|\int \alpha_1 \alpha_2^3 \, dP\Bigr| \le \Bigl|\int \alpha_1^3 \alpha_2 \, dP\Bigr| \iff q_2^2 \le q_1^2,$$

where $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ are the [marginal effects](alpha1.md). O'Connor and Price (2018) state the forward implication without the sign condition on $\kappa_\pi$.

## Sources

- L. J. O'Connor, A. L. Price, *Distinguishing genetic correlation from causation across 52 diseases and complex traits*, Nat. Genet. 50 (2018), 1728–1734.
