# Mixed fourth moment

In every [latent causal variable model](model.md) on a probability measure $P$, the [marginal effects](alpha1.md) satisfy (O'Connor and Price 2018)

$$\int \alpha_1^3 \alpha_2 \, dP = \kappa_\pi\, q_1^3 q_2 + 3\, q_1 q_2,$$

where $\kappa_\pi$ is the [excess kurtosis](kurtosis.md) of $\pi$.

## Sources

- L. J. O'Connor, A. L. Price, *Distinguishing genetic correlation from causation across 52 diseases and complex traits*, Nat. Genet. 50 (2018), 1728–1734.
