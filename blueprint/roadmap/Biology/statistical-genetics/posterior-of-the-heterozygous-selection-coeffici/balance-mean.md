# Mean PTV count at mutation-selection balance

For real numbers $n$ (the number of sampled chromosomes), $U$ (the PTV mutation rate of a gene) and $s$ (the heterozygous selection coefficient), the mean protein-truncating-variant count at deterministic mutation–selection balance is

$$\lambda(s) = \frac{n U}{s},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (so $\lambda(0) = 0$); it is meaningful for $s > 0$.
