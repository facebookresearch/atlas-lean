# DENTIST-S is chi-squared with one degree of freedom under correct LD

Under the hypotheses of the [exact law of the standardized difference](map-standardized-eq-gaussian-real.md) with reference correlation $r = \rho$ and $\rho^2 < 1$, the law of the [DENTIST-S statistic](dentist-s.md) $(z_i - \rho z_c)^2 / (1 - \rho^2)$ under $P$ is the law of $X^2$ for $X$ standard normal.
