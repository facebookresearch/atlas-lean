# Variance of the standardized DENTIST difference

For real numbers $\rho$ (the true correlation) and $r$ (the reference correlation), put

$$v(\rho, r) = \frac{1 - 2 r \rho + r^2}{1 - r^2},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$. When $r^2 < 1$ and $(z_i, z_c)$ is Gaussian with unit variances and covariance $\rho$, it is the variance of the standardized difference $(z_i - r z_c)/\sqrt{1 - r^2}$ ([exact law](map-standardized-eq-gaussian-real.md)).
