# State-space reduction of inheritance HMMs

Multipoint linkage likelihoods are computed with a hidden Markov model whose hidden state is the inheritance vector of a pedigree, with $2^{k}$ states for $k$ meioses. When the states can be lumped into equivalence classes that preserve emission probabilities and class-to-class transition probabilities, the forward recursion runs on the classes instead; counting partitions of selector clusters and chains of non-informative meioses give exponential reductions.
