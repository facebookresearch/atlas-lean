# Validity of the one-sided TDT

If the transmission count $B$ has the fair-coin law $\mathrm{Bin}(d, 1/2)$, then the exact one-sided TDT
p-value $P$ satisfies $\Pr[P \le s] \le s$ for every $s \in [0, 1]$.

The null law is an explicit hypothesis. Its intended reading is one informative transmission from each
of $d$ independent families (heterozygous parent, no linkage). The number $d$ of informative
transmissions is fixed, not random. Transmissions to several siblings of one family are not given a
joint law here.

## Sources

- Spielman, McGinnis and Ewens (1993); Lehmann and Romano (2005), Lemma 3.3.1.
