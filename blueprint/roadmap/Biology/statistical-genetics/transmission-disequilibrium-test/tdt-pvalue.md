# Exact one-sided TDT p-value

For a natural number $d$ and a natural-valued statistic $B$, the exact one-sided p-value of the transmission
disequilibrium test (TDT) at $\omega$ is the binomial upper tail $\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(d, 1/2) \ge B(\omega)]$,
which is $0$ when $B(\omega) > d$. In the test, $B$ is the number of transmissions of the allele of interest
among $d$ transmissions from heterozygous parents, and the p-value is the null probability of at least as
many transmissions.
