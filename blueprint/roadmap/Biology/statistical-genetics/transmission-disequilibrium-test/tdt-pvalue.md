# Exact one-sided TDT p-value

Let $B$ be the number of transmissions of the allele of interest among $d$ transmissions from
heterozygous parents. The exact one-sided p-value of the transmission disequilibrium test (TDT) is
$\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(d, 1/2) \ge B]$, the null probability of at least as many transmissions.
