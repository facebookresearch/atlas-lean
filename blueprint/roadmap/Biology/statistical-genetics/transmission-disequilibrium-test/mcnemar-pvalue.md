# Exact one-sided McNemar p-value

For a natural number $N$ and a natural-valued statistic $X$, the exact one-sided McNemar p-value at
$\omega$ is the binomial upper tail $\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(N, 1/2) \ge X(\omega)]$, which is $0$ when
$X(\omega) > N$. In the test, $X$ is the number of discordant pairs of the first type among $N$ discordant
pairs.
