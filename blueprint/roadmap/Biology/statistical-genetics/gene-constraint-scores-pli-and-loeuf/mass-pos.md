# Positivity of the class masses

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set, let $\lambda : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\lambda_c > 0$ for every $c$, let $x_1, \dots, x_N$ be [gene data](gene-data.md) with $N > 0$, and let $w$ be [strictly positive weights](pos-weights.md) on $\iota$. Then the [class mass](mass.md) satisfies $M_w(z) > 0$ for every $z \in \iota$.
