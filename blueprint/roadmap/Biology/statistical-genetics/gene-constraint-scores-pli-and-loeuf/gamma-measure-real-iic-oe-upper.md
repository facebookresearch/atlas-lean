# The flat-prior posterior quantile is Garwood's bound

Let $0 < \alpha < 1$, $E > 0$ and let $o$ be a natural number. Then the Gamma distribution with shape $o + 1$ and rate $E$ assigns probability exactly $1 - \alpha$ to the half-line $(-\infty, U_\alpha(E, o)]$, where $U_\alpha(E, o)$ is the [upper bound for the observed/expected ratio](oe-upper.md).
