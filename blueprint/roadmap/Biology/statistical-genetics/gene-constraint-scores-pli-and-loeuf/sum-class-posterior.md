# Class posteriors sum to one

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set, let $w, \lambda : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $w_c > 0$ and $\lambda_c > 0$ for every $c$, let $E > 0$ and let $o$ be a natural number. Then the [class posteriors](class-posterior.md) sum to one:

$$\sum_{c \in \iota} P(c \mid o) = 1.$$
