# LOEUF

For a real number $E$ (the expected number of loss-of-function variants) and a natural number $o$ (the observed number), the loss-of-function observed/expected upper bound fraction is

$$\mathrm{LOEUF}(E, o) = U_{1/20}(E, o),$$

the [upper bound for the observed/expected ratio](oe-upper.md) at level $\alpha = 1/20$. For $E > 0$ it is the upper end of a $90\%$ confidence interval for the observed/expected ratio.
