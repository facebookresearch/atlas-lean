# Class posterior is strictly antitone in the observed count

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, let $w, \lambda : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $w_c > 0$ and $\lambda_c > 0$ for every $c$, let $E > 0$, and let $c_0 \in \iota$ satisfy $\lambda_{c_0} \le \lambda_c$ for every $c$, with $\lambda_{c_0} < \lambda_c$ for some $c$. Then the [class posterior](class-posterior.md) $o \mapsto P(c_0 \mid o)$ is strictly decreasing on the natural numbers.
