# Positivity of the Poisson mixture joint likelihood

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, let $\lambda : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\lambda_c > 0$ for every $c$, and let $w$ be [strictly positive weights](pos-weights.md) on $\iota$. Then for all [gene data](gene-data.md) $x$ and classes $z$, the [joint likelihood](joint.md) is positive: $p_w(x, z) > 0$.
