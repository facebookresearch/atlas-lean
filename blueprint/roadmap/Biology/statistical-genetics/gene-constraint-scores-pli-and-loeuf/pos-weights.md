# Strictly positive class weights summing to one

Let $Z$ be a finite set. Strictly positive weights on $Z$ are functions $w : Z \to \mathbb{R}$ with $w_z > 0$ for every $z$ and $\sum_{z \in Z} w_z = 1$. They form the parameter space of the class weights in the EM algorithm for pLI, of the mixture weights in the general EM algorithm for finite latent variables, and of the latent weights in the weights-only EM step for item response models.
