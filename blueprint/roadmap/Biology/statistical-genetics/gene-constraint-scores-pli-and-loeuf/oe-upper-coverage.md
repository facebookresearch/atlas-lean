# Exact coverage of the observed/expected upper bound

Let $0 < \alpha < 1$, $E > 0$ and $\gamma \ge 0$. If $o$ is Poisson distributed with mean $\gamma E$, then

$$\Pr\bigl(\gamma \le U_\alpha(E, o)\bigr) \ge 1 - \alpha,$$

where $U_\alpha(E, o)$ is the [upper bound for the observed/expected ratio](oe-upper.md).
