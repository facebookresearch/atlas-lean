# Poisson likelihood of an observed count

For a real number $\mu$ and a natural number $o$, the Poisson likelihood of the count $o$ at mean $\mu$ is

$$L(\mu, o) = \frac{e^{-\mu} \mu^{o}}{o!},$$

defined by this formula for every real $\mu$ (with $\mu^0 = 1$); for $\mu \ge 0$ it is the probability that a Poisson variable with mean $\mu$ equals $o$.
