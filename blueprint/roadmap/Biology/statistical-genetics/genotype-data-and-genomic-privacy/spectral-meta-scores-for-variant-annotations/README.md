# Spectral meta-scores for variant annotations

Unsupervised combination of many correlated functional annotations of genetic variants (conservation, protein-function and regulatory scores) into one meta-score. Treating each standardized annotation as a two-component mixture over the unknown functional status of a variant, conditional independence makes the off-diagonal covariance rank one, and its leading eigenvector ranks and weights the annotations.
