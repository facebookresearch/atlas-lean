# Genetic susceptibility from twin concordance

Monozygotic co-twins share a genotype, so the disease risk of the co-twin of an affected twin reflects the average and spread of penetrance across genotypes. Combining population prevalence with that recurrence risk bounds the fraction of the population that is genetically able to develop the disease at all.
