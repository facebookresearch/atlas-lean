# Winner's-curse-free Mendelian randomization

Selecting instruments by thresholding their exposure z-scores and then reusing the same estimates biases summary-data Mendelian randomization. Randomizing the selection and Rao–Blackwellizing the effect estimates removes the winner's curse; combining this with a measurement-error-corrected loss, invalid-instrument screening and bagging gives an asymptotically normal causal estimator.
