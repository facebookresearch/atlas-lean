# First-ladder decomposition of a high walk maximum

For a list of real scores and a level `t > 0`, the [walk maximum](walk-max.md) is at least `t` if
and only if some prefix of length `k`, at most the length of the list,
[ends at the first strict ascending ladder epoch](is-first-ladder.md), with ladder height `H`, and
the walk maximum of the remaining scores is at least `t - H`. Such a `k` is
[unique](eq-of-is-first-ladder-take.md).
