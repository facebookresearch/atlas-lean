# Repeating one letter bounds the probability of a high score sum

Let `q` be nonnegative weights and `f` real scores on a finite alphabet, `b₀` a letter, `k` a
natural number and `t ≤ k f(b₀)` real. Then the [score probability](score-prob.md) that the sum of
`k` scores is at least `t` is at least `q_{b₀}^k`, the weight of the word made of `k` copies of
`b₀`.
