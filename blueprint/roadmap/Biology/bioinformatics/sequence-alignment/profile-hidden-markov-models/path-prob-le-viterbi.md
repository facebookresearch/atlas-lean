# Every path weighs at most the Viterbi value

For a profile HMM with weights in a linearly ordered semiring and [nonnegative weights](nonneg.md), every [alignment](../pair-hidden-markov-models/alignments-of.md) of $x$ to the columns has [path weight](path-prob.md) at most the [Viterbi value](viterbi.md) of $x$.
