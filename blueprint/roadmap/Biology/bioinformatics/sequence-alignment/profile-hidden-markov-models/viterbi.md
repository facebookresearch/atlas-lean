# Viterbi value of a sequence under a profile HMM

For weights in a linearly ordered semiring, the Viterbi value of $x$ is the [max-recursion value](../pair-hidden-markov-models/max-dp.md) of $x$ and the [columns](columns.md) from $(M, 0)$ for the [associated automaton](to-automaton.md). Under [nonnegative weights](nonneg.md) it is the largest weight of a path emitting $x$.
