# Alignments of two nonempty sequences by first column

For letters $a, b$ and sequences $x, y$, the [list of alignments](alignments-of.md) of $a x$ and $b y$ is the list of alignments of $x$ and $y$ with $(a, b)$ in front of each, followed by those of $x$ and $b y$ with $(a, -)$ in front, followed by those of $a x$ and $y$ with $(-, b)$ in front.
