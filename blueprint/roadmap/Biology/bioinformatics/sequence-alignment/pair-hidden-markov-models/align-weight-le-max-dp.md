# Every alignment weighs at most the max recursion

For an alignment automaton with weights in a linearly ordered semiring and [nonnegative column weights](nonneg.md), every control state $q$ and every [alignment](alignments-of.md) $C$ of $x$ and $y$, the [weight](align-weight.md) of $C$ read from $q$ is at most the [max-recursion value](max-dp.md) of $x$ and $y$ from $q$.
