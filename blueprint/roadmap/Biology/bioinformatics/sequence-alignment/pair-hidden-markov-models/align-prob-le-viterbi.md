# Every alignment weighs at most the Viterbi value

For a pair HMM with weights in a linearly ordered semiring and [nonnegative weights](nonneg-2.md), every [valid alignment](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/is-alignment.md) $A$ of $x$ and $y$ has [joint weight](align-prob.md) at most the [Viterbi value](viterbi.md) of $x$ and $y$.
