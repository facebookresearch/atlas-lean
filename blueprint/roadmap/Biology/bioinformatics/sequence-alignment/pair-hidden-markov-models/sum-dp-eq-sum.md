# The sum recursion is the sum over alignments

For every alignment automaton with weights in a semiring, every control state $q$ and sequences $x, y$, the [total weight](sum-dp.md) equals the sum, over the [alignments](alignments-of.md) $C$ of $x$ and $y$, of the [weight](align-weight.md) of $C$ read from $q$.
