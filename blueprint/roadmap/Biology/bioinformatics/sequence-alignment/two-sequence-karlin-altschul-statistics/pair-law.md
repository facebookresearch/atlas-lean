# Product law of an aligned letter pair

For real weights `p` and `q` on a finite alphabet, the pair law is the weight `(a, b) ↦ p_a q_b`
on pairs of letters. When `p` and `q` are
[probability vectors](../../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/bayesian-inference-and-sampling/expectation-maximization-algorithm/is-dist.md)
it is a probability vector on pairs: the law of an aligned pair made of a letter of law `p` from
the first word and an independent letter of law `q` from the second.
