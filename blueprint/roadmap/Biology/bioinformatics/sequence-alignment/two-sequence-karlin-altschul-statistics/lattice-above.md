# Lattice point one step past a threshold in the direction of the span

For real numbers `h` and `t`, put `t⁺ = (⌊t / h⌋ + 1) h`. For a positive span `h > 0`, the intended
case, `t⁺` is the smallest point of the lattice `hℤ` strictly greater than `t`, the next lattice
point above `t`. For `h < 0` it is the largest point of `hℤ` strictly smaller than `t` (it lies in
`[t + h, t)`; for example `t⁺ = -1` for `h = -1` and `t = 0`), and for `h = 0` it is `0`, by the
convention `x / 0 = 0`.
