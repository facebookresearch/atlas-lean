# Poisson limit of elementary symmetric sums of uniformly small terms

For each natural number `N` let `D_N` be a finite set and `f_N(i) ≥ 0` real values for
`i ∈ D_N`. Suppose that eventually `f_N(i) ≤ μ_N` for every `i ∈ D_N`, where `μ_N → 0`, and that
`∑_{i ∈ D_N} f_N(i) → c`. Then for every natural number `k` the
[elementary symmetric sum](elem-symm.md) `e_k(f_N)` tends to `c^k / k!` as `N → ∞`. These limits
are the factorial moments `E[C(W, k)]` of a Poisson variable `W` with mean `c`.
