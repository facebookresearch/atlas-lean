# The maximal segment score of lattice scores is on the lattice

For every real number `h` and every list of real scores each of the form `k h` with `k` an integer,
the [maximal segment score](../maximal-segment-score-of-a-random-sequence/max-seg-score.md) of the
list is also of the form `k h` with `k` an integer (the empty segment gives `0 = 0 · h`).
