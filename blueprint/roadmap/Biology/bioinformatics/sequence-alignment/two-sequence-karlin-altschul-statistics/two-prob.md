# Weighted probability of an event on pairs of words

For real weights `p` and `q` on a finite alphabet, natural numbers `m` and `n` and a property `P`
of two words, the two-sequence probability of `P` is the [two-sequence average](two-expect.md) of
the indicator of `P`: the total weight of the pairs of words `u` of length `m` and `v` of length
`n` with `P(u, v)`. When `p` and `q` are probability vectors it is the probability of `P(u, v)`
for independent words of independent letters with laws `p` and `q`.
