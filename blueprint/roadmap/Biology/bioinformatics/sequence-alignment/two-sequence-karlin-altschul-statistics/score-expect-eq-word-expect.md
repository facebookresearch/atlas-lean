# The score-sequence average is a word average

For real weights `p` and real scores `f` on a finite alphabet, a natural number `n` and a real
functional `F` of score lists, the
[average over score sequences](../../../../Probability/random-walks/maximal-segment-score-of-a-random-sequence/score-expect.md) of
`F` equals the [word average](word-expect.md) over words `w` of length `n` of
`F(f(w₀), …, f(w_{n-1}))`.
