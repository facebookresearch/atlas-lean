# Elementary symmetric sum

For a finite set `D`, real values `f(i)` and a natural number `k`, the elementary symmetric sum is
`e_k(f) = ∑_{T ⊆ D, |T| = k} ∏_{i ∈ T} f(i)`. In particular `e_0(f) = 1` (the empty product) and
`e_k(f) = 0` for `k > |D|`. When the `f(i)` are the probabilities of independent events `i ∈ D`,
`e_k(f)` is the `k`-th factorial moment `E[C(W, k)]` of the number `W` of events that occur.
