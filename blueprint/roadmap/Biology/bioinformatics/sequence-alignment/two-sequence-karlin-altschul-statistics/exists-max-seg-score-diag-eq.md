# The maximal ungapped alignment score is attained on a diagonal

For a real score `s` on pairs of letters and words `u` and `v`, there is an integer `d` such that
the [maximal segment score](../../../../Probability/random-walks/maximal-segment-score-of-a-random-sequence/max-seg-score.md) of the
[diagonal of offset](diag.md) `d` equals the
[maximal ungapped alignment score](max-align-score.md) `M(u, v)`.
