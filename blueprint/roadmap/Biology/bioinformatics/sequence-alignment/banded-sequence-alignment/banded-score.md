# Banded alignment score

For integer substitution scores $\sigma$, an integer gap score $g$, a band width $w$ and sequences $x, y$, the banded score is the largest [alignment score](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/alignment-score.md) of a [$w$-banded](is-banded.md) alignment among the [alignments of $x$ and $y$](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/alignments.md), and is undefined when there is none.
