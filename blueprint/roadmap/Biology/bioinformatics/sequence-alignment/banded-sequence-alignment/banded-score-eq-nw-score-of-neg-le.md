# The banded optimum is exact

If $\sigma(a, b) \le 0$ for all letters, $g \le -1$ and the [Needleman–Wunsch score](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/nw-score.md) of $x$ and $y$ is at least $-w$, then the [banded score](banded-score.md) with band width $w$ is defined and equals the Needleman–Wunsch score.
