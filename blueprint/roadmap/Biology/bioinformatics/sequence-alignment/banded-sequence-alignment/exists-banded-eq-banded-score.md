# A defined banded score is attained by a banded alignment

Let $\sigma$ be integer substitution scores, $g$ an integer gap score and $w$ a band width. If the [banded score](banded-score.md) of sequences $x$ and $y$ is defined and equals $s$, then some [valid alignment](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/is-alignment.md) $A$ of $x$ and $y$ is [$w$-banded](is-banded.md) and has [alignment score](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/alignment-score.md) $s$ with respect to $\sigma$ and $g$.
