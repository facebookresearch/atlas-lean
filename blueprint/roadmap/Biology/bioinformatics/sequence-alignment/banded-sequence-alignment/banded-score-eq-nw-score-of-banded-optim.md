# Banded alignment is exact when some optimal alignment is banded

If some [valid alignment](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/is-alignment.md) of $x$ and $y$ is [$w$-banded](is-banded.md) and has score equal to the [Needleman–Wunsch score](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/nw-score.md), then the [banded score](banded-score.md) is defined and equals the Needleman–Wunsch score.
