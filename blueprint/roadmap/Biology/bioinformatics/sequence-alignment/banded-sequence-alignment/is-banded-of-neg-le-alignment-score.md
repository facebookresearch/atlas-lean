# The band lemma

If $\sigma(a, b) \le 0$ for all letters and $g \le -1$, then every alignment with [score](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/alignment-score.md) at least $-w$ is [$w$-banded](is-banded.md) (Ukkonen 1985).

## Sources

- E. Ukkonen, *Algorithms for approximate string matching*, Information and Control 64 (1985), 100–118.
