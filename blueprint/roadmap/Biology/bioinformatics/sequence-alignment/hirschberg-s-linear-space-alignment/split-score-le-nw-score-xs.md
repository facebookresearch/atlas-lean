# A split of the first sequence scores at most the Needleman–Wunsch optimum

For all sequences $x, y$ and natural numbers $m, k$, $\mathrm{NW}(x_{<m}, y_{<k}) + \mathrm{NW}(x_{\ge m}, y_{\ge k}) \le \mathrm{NW}(x, y)$, with the notation of [the split of the second sequence](split-score-le-nw-score-ys.md).
