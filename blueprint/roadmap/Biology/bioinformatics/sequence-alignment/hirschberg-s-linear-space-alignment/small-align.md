# Base-case alignment for Hirschberg

For sequences of length at most one, the base-case alignment is: the empty alignment for two empty sequences; the single column $(-, b)$ for empty and $b$; the single column $(a, -)$ for $a$ and empty; for $a$ and $b$, the column $(a, b)$ if $\sigma(a, b) \ge 2g$ and the columns $(a, -), (-, b)$ otherwise. For longer sequences it is the empty alignment.
