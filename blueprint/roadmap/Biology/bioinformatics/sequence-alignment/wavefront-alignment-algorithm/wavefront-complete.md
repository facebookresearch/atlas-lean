# Completeness of the wavefront algorithm

For $v \le |x|$, $h \le |y|$ and every [valid alignment](../pairwise-sequence-alignment/is-alignment.md) $A$ of the first $v$ letters of $x$ and the first $h$ letters of $y$, the [wavefront offset](wavefront.md) $W(c, h - v)$ at the [cost](cost.md) $c$ of $A$ is reachable and at least $h$.
