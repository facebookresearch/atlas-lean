# The table computes the optimal global alignment score

For all integer substitution scores $\sigma$, gap scores $g$ and sequences $x, y$, the [table value](nw-tab.md) equals $\mathrm{NW}(x, y)$, the [Needleman–Wunsch score](nw-score.md) (Needleman–Wunsch 1970).

## Sources

- S. B. Needleman and C. D. Wunsch, *A general method applicable to the search for similarities in the amino acid sequence of two proteins*, J. Mol. Biol. 48 (1970), 443–453.
