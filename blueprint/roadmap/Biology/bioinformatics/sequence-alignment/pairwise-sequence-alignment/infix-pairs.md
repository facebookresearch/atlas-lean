# Infix pairs

The infix pairs of $x$ and $y$ are the pairs $(x', y')$ such that, for some [prefix pair](prefix-pairs.md) $(p, q)$ of $x$ and $y$, $x'$ is a suffix of $p$ and $y'$ is a suffix of $q$: $x'$ and $y'$ are contiguous segments of $x$ and $y$. Each pair is listed once for every prefix pair $(p, q)$ it arises from, so a pair may occur more than once.
