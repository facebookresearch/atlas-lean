# Lattice renewal input built from ladder renewal data

Let `P` be a probability measure, `h > 0`, and `X 0, X 1, …` real scores satisfying the
[Cramér conditions](cramer-scores.md) with root `θ`, each almost surely on the lattice `hℤ`. Given
a [ladder input](ladder-input.md) for their [maximal segment scores](max-segment-score-rv.md)
whose excursion heights lie almost surely on `hℤ`, and
[lattice ladder renewal data](lattice-ladder-renewal-data.md) with span `h` for its first
excursion height `Y 0`, this construction gives a [lattice renewal input](lattice-renewal-input.md)
for the scores whose tail condition is derived, with renewal constant
`C = e^{θ h} C_∞ (1 - E e^{θ D})`. No renewal-theorem hypothesis is used.
