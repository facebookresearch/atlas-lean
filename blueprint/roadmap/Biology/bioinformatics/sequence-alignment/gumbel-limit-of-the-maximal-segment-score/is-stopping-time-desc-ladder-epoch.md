# The first descending ladder epoch is a stopping time

On the space of paths `ℕ → ℝ` with the natural filtration `σ(x 0, …, x t)` of the coordinate
process, the time equal to the [first descending ladder epoch](desc-ladder-epoch.md) when the walk
[drops below `0`](has-desc-ladder.md), and to `∞` otherwise, is a stopping time.
