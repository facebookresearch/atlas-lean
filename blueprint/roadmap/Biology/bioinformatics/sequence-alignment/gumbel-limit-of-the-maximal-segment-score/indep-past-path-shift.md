# The first t scores are independent of the path after time t

For a probability measure `ν` on `ℝ` and every natural number `t`, under the product measure `ν^ℕ`
on paths `ℕ → ℝ`, the [σ-algebra of the first `t` scores](past-measurable-space.md) is independent
of the σ-algebra generated by the [path seen from time `t`](path-shift.md).
