# Height of the i-th descending ladder excursion

For a path of real scores and a natural number `i`, the height `Y_i` of the `i`-th descending
ladder excursion is the [maximal excursion height](excursion-max.md)
[evaluated on the `i`-th excursion](iter-ladder.md): the maximal rise of the walk inside that
excursion, measured from its value at the start of the excursion.
