# Gotoh cell recurrence from diagonal, upper and left cells

For letters $a, b$ and integer triples $d = (d_M, d_X, d_Y)$, $u = (u_M, u_X, u_Y)$ and $l = (l_M, l_X, l_Y)$, the step gives the triple

$$\bigl(\max(d_M + \sigma(a, b), u_X + o, l_Y + o),\ \max(d_M + \sigma(a, b), u_X + e, l_Y + o),\ \max(d_M + \sigma(a, b), u_X + o, l_Y + e)\bigr).$$
