# Information content of a position weight matrix column

For real functions $p, q$ on $A$, the information content of the column $p$ against the background $q$ is

$$\mathrm{IC}(p \,\|\, q) = \frac{1}{\log 2} \sum_b p(b) \log \frac{p(b)}{q(b)},$$

in bits, with the conventions $x/0 = 0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$; letters with $p(b) = 0$ contribute $0$ (Schneider et al. 1986).

## Sources

- T. D. Schneider, G. D. Stormo, L. Gold and A. Ehrenfeucht, *Information content of binding sites on nucleotide sequences*, J. Mol. Biol. 188 (1986), 415–431.
