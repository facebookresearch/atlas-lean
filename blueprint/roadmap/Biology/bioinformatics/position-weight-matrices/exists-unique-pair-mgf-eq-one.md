# Existence and uniqueness of the Karlin–Altschul parameter

Let $p$ be a distribution on $A$ with $p_a > 0$ for every $a$, and let $s$ satisfy $\sum_a \sum_b p_a p_b s_{ab} < 0$ and $s_{ab} > 0$ for some $a, b$. Then there is exactly one $\lambda > 0$ with $\Phi(\lambda) = 1$, where $\Phi$ is the [moment generating function](pair-mgf.md) (Karlin and Altschul 1990).

## Sources

- S. Karlin and S. F. Altschul, *Methods for assessing the statistical significance of molecular sequence features by using general scoring schemes*, PNAS 87 (1990), 2264–2268.
