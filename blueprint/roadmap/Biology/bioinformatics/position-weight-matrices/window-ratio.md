# Likelihood ratio of a motif window

For a background $q$, a site model $\theta$ and a sequence $x$, the window ratio is $1$ for "no occurrence" and $\lambda_\theta(x_{[j]}) / \beta(x_{[j]})$ for an occurrence at $j$, where $x_{[j]}$ is the [window](window.md), $\lambda_\theta$ the [site likelihood](site-lik.md), $\beta$ the [background likelihood](bg-lik.md) and $x/0 = 0$.
