# The column recursion computes the exact score distribution

For every real $q$, integer matrix $M$ and integer $t$, the [score distribution](score-dist.md) at $t$ is $\sum_{s \,:\, \mathrm{sc}_M(s) = t} \prod_i q(s_i)$. When $q$ is a distribution it is the background probability of the score $t$.
