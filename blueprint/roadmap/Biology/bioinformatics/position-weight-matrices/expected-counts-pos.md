# Positivity of expected motif counts

Let $Z$ be nonempty, $q(b) > 0$ for every $b$, $\pi(z) > 0$ for every $z$, and let the sequences satisfy the [coverage condition](covers.md). Then for every positive matrix $\theta$, column $i$ and letter $b$, the [expected count](expected-counts.md) satisfies $c_i(b) > 0$.
