# The reverse-complement filter scans the other strand

For every complement map, filter $W$, sequence $x$ and $i \le m$, the [response](conv.md) of the [reverse-complement filter](rc-filter.md) at $i$ equals the response of $W$ at $m - i$ on the [reverse complement](rc-seq.md) of the prefix of length $m + w$ of $x$.
