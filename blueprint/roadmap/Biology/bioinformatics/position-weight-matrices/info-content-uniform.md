# Information content against the uniform background

For a nonempty finite alphabet $A$ and every probability law $P$ on $A$, the [information content](info-content.md) of $P$ against the uniform background $1/|A|$ is $\log_2 |A| - H(P)$, where $H(P) = -\sum_b P(b) \log_2 P(b)$ is the Shannon entropy in bits (Schneider et al. 1986).

## Sources

- T. D. Schneider, G. D. Stormo, L. Gold and A. Ehrenfeucht, *Information content of binding sites on nucleotide sequences*, J. Mol. Biol. 188 (1986), 415–431.
