# Log-odds score of a selected site is its scaled energy up to a constant

For a nonempty alphabet and $q(b) > 0$ for every $b$, the log-odds [score](score.md) of every site $s$ under the [Boltzmann matrix](boltzmann-pwm.md) is $-\lambda E(s) - \sum_i \log Z_i$, where $E(s) = \sum_i \varepsilon_i(s_i)$.
