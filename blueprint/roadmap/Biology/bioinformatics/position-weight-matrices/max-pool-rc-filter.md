# Max pooling the reverse-complement filter equals max pooling the filter on the reverse complement

For every complement map, filter $W$, sequence $x$ and $m$, the [max-pooled output](max-pool.md) of the [reverse-complement filter](rc-filter.md) on $x$ equals that of $W$ on the [reverse complement](rc-seq.md) of the prefix of length $m + w$ of $x$.
