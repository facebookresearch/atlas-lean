# Error of scaling and rounding a weight matrix

For every real matrix $W$ of width $w$, every $c > 0$ and every site $s$,

$$\Bigl|\mathrm{sc}_W(s) - \frac{1}{c}\, \mathrm{sc}_{[cW]}(s)\Bigr| \le \frac{w}{2c},$$

where $[cW]$ is the matrix of the nearest integers to $c W_i(b)$ and $\mathrm{sc}$ the [score](score.md).
