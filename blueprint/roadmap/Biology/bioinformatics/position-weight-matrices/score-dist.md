# Background score distribution of an integer matrix

For a background $q$ and an integer matrix $M$ of width $w$, the score distribution is obtained from the point mass at $0$ by applying the [column step](score-step.md) with the columns $M_0, M_1, \dots, M_{w-1}$ in turn (Staden 1989; Touzet and Varré 2007).

## Sources

- R. Staden, *Methods for calculating the probabilities of finding patterns in sequences*, CABIOS 5 (1989), 89–96.
- H. Touzet and J.-S. Varré, *Efficient and accurate P-value computation for Position Weight Matrices*, Algorithms Mol. Biol. 2 (2007), 15.
