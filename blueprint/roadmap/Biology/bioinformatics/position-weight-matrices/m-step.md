# M-step for position weight matrices

Under the hypotheses of [positivity of expected counts](expected-counts-pos.md), with a nonempty alphabet, the M-step maps a positive matrix $\theta$ to the positive matrix $\theta'_i(b) = c_i(b) / \sum_{b'} c_i(b')$, where $c$ are the [expected counts](expected-counts.md).
