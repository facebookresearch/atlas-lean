# Clusters of orthologous groups from symmetric best hits

Orthology clustering across many genomes from a graph whose edges join genes in different genomes
that are each other's best match: clusters of orthologous groups (COGs) are the maximal subgraphs
in which any two edges are linked by a chain of triangles sharing sides, and they can be found by
growing clusters edge by edge instead of comparing all pairs of triangles.
