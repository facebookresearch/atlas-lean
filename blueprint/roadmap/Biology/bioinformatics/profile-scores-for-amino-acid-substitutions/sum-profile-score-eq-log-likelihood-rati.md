# The profile score is a log-likelihood ratio

Let $J$ be a finite set of positions with profiles $p_j$, $q$ a background and $s = (s_j)_{j \in J}$ a sequence, with $p_j(a) > 0$ and $q(a) > 0$ for all $j, a$. Then

$$\sum_{j \in J} \log \frac{p_j(s_j)}{q(s_j)} = \log \frac{\prod_j p_j(s_j)}{\prod_j q(s_j)},$$

the sum of the [profile scores](profile-score.md).
