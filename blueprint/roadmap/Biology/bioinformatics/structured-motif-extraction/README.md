# Structured motif extraction

Discovery of DNA motifs made of several short components separated by gaps of variable but bounded length, as in prokaryotic promoters and composite transcription-factor binding sites. Because a structured motif can occur more often than its parts, the usual downward closure of frequent-pattern mining fails, and pruning instead relies on gap-span bounds.
