# Hannenhalli–Pevzner duality

**Hannenhalli–Pevzner duality** (1999, Theorem 5). For every signed permutation `π` of
`1, …, n` (`SValid n π`), the reversal distance `sdist n π` equals `b(π) - c(π) + h(π) + 1` if `π`
is a fortress and `b(π) - c(π) + h(π)` otherwise, where `b(π) = sbreaks n π`.

## Sources

- S. Hannenhalli and P. A. Pevzner, J. ACM 46 (1999) 1-27, Theorem 5.
- R. Shamir and I. Pe'er, *Algorithms for Molecular Biology*, lecture 11 scribe notes, Tel Aviv University, 1998, Theorem 11.12, <https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~rshamir/algmb/98/scribe/html/lec11/node13.html>.
