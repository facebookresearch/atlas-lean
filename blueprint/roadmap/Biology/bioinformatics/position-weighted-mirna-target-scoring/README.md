# Position-weighted miRNA target scoring

Animal microRNAs pair imperfectly with their target messages, and the pairing is asymmetric: complementarity near the 5' end of the microRNA counts for more than complementarity near its 3' end. Target prediction therefore scores a local complementarity alignment with position-dependent weights, filters by duplex free energy, and keeps only sites whose sequence and position are conserved in orthologous untranslated regions.
