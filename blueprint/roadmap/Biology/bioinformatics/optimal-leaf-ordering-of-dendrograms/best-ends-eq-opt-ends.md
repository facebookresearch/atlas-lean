# Correctness of the optimal leaf ordering recursion

For every similarity $S$, dendrogram $t$ and labels $i, j$, the [dynamic program](best-ends.md) value $M_t(i, j)$ equals the [optimal score with ends $i$ and $j$](opt-ends.md) (Bar-Joseph, Gifford and Jaakkola 2001).

## Sources

- Z. Bar-Joseph, D. K. Gifford and T. S. Jaakkola, *Fast optimal leaf ordering for hierarchical clustering*, Bioinformatics 17 (Suppl. 1) (2001), S22–S29.
