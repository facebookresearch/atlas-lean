# Leaf orderings of a dendrogram

The leaf orders of a [dendrogram](dendrogram.md) form the list defined by: $[[a]]$ for a leaf $a$; for a merge of $l$ and $r$, all concatenations $u\,v$ with $u$ a leaf order of $l$ and $v$ of $r$, followed by all concatenations $v\,u$ with $v$ a leaf order of $r$ and $u$ of $l$: one entry for each choice, at every internal node, of the child drawn first.
