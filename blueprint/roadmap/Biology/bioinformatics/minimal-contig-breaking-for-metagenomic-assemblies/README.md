# Minimal contig breaking for metagenomic assemblies

Post-processing of long-read metagenomic assemblies breaks contigs at junctions that the assembly graph cannot resolve. A combinatorial formulation over source-to-sink trail decompositions characterizes the breaking sets that make the broken contigs agree with every plausible genome and gives a minimum-size construction.
