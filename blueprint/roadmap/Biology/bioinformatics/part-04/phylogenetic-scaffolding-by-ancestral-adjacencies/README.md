# Phylogenetic scaffolding by ancestral adjacencies

Gene adjacencies in extant genomes descend from adjacencies in ancestral genomes, gained and broken by rearrangements along reconciled gene trees. Parsimony over pairs of gene trees reconstructs ancestral adjacencies and, when extant assemblies are fragmented, also proposes missing extant adjacencies, using a prior probability derived from counting the ways contigs can be joined into chromosomes.
