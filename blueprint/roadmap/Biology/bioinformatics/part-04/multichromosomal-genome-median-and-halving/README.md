# Multichromosomal genome median and halving

Ancestral gene-order reconstruction posed as optimization over genomes that are sets of adjacencies between gene extremities. The median problem seeks a genome minimizing the total distance to three given genomes; genome halving seeks an ordinary genome whose doubling is closest to an all-duplicates genome; guided halving combines the two. Their complexity depends on the distance (breakpoint or double-cut-and-join) and on the allowed karyotype (linear, circular or mixed chromosomes).
