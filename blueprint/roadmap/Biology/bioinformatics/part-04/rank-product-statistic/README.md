# Rank product statistic

A non-parametric statistic for differential expression across replicate experiments: each of $n$ molecules is ranked in each of $k$ experiments and the ranks are multiplied. Its exact null distribution obeys a recursion over the number of experiments; replacing sums by integrals yields guaranteed upper and lower bounds that can be solved in closed form piece by piece, giving fast conservative $p$-values far into the tail.
