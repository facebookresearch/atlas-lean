# Combinatorial-entropy mutation impact scores

Scores that predict the functional impact of an amino-acid substitution from the residue distribution in a column of a multiple sequence alignment of homologs, using the change of combinatorial entropy caused by the substitution, at the level of the whole protein family and of the subfamily the mutated protein belongs to.

- [Combinatorial-entropy conservation score of a substitution](combinatorial-entropy-conservation-score.md)
- [Functional impact score from family and subfamily conservation](functional-impact-score-from-family-and-subfamily-conservation.md)
