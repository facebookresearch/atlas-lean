# Global network alignment

Alignment of two biological networks (for example protein–protein interaction networks of two species) by a partial injective map between their vertex sets that rewards similar nodes and conserved interactions. In the bipartite alignment-graph view, alignments are matchings and conserved interactions are pairs of matching edges, which leads to integer programs and Lagrangian bounds solved with bipartite matching.
