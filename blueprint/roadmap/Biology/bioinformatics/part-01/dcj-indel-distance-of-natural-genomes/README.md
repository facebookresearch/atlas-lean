# DCJ-indel distance of natural genomes

The DCJ-indel distance counts double-cut-and-join operations plus insertions and deletions of contiguous marker blocks needed to transform one genome into another. For singular genomes it has a closed form in terms of the cycles, paths and runs of the relational diagram; for natural genomes with duplicated markers it is the minimum over all matchings of marker occurrences, which reduces to a maximum-weight consistent cycle decomposition of a capped multi-relational diagram.
