# Signed breakpoints are at most twice the signed reversal distance

For every natural number $n$ and every [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$,
the [signed breakpoint count](sbreaks.md) and the [signed reversal distance](sdist.md) satisfy
$b^{\pm}_n(\pi) \le 2\,d^{\pm}(\pi)$, that is, $d^{\pm}(\pi) \ge b^{\pm}_n(\pi)/2$: the signed
analogue of Theorem 11.2 of Shamir and Peer (Lecture 11).
