# Unsigned breakpoints are at most twice the unsigned reversal distance

For every natural number $n$ and every [permutation](uvalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$, written
as a list of integers, the [unsigned breakpoint count](ubreaks.md) and the
[unsigned reversal distance](udist.md) satisfy $b_n(\pi) \le 2\,d(\pi)$, that is,
$d(\pi) \ge b_n(\pi)/2$ (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, Theorem 11.2).
