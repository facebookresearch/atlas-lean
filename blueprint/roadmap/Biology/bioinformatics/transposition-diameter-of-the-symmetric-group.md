# Transposition diameter of the symmetric group

Determine the diameter TD(n) of permutations under block transpositions—moving one contiguous block past an adjacent block. This calibrates a basic genome-rearrangement model. It is not the easy problem of swapping two individual entries.

## What would settle it

Define the block-transposition transition relation and shortest-path distance on permutations. Prove an exact formula for $\max_\pi d(\pi, \mathrm{id})$, or settle a remaining finite n using a verified exhaustive certificate. An all-n theorem requires more than finitely many computed diameters.

## Status

The cited sources, in the versions cited, record the partial results below; they do not pose the question in this form, and this page does not certify its present status.

Silva, Kowada, Rocco and Walter (preprint, §1) record that TD(n) was known for n ≤ 15, determine TD(16), and improve the general upper bound by one for n ≡ 1 (mod 3) with n ≥ 16; they give no exact formula for all n.

This page records the research target above and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
