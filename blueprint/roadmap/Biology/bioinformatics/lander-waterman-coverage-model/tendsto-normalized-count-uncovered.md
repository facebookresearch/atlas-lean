# Lander–Waterman limit for the uncovered fraction

Let $L$ be a natural number, $G_N \ge L$ genome lengths for $N = 0, 1, \dots$ and $c$ real with $N L / G_N \to c$. Then for any choice of bases $x_N$, the [normalized count](normalized-count.md) of the start tuples of $N$ reads leaving $x_N$ uncovered on the genome of length $G_N$ tends to $e^{-c}$ as $N \to \infty$.
