# Interior bases of a linear genome are covered by exactly $L$ starts

On a linear genome with bases $\{0, \dots, G-1\}$ and reads of length $L$ with start positions
$s \in \{0, \dots, G - L\}$, the read starting at $s$ covering $x$ when $s \le x < s + L$: every
base $x$ with $L - 1 \le x$ and $x + L \le G$ is covered by exactly $L$ starts. Here $L - 1$ is
natural subtraction; for $L = 0$ every base satisfies the conditions and the count is $0 = L$.
