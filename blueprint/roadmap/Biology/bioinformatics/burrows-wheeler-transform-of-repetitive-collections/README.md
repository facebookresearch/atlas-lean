# Burrows–Wheeler transform of repetitive collections

The Burrows–Wheeler transform (BWT) and FM-index underlie read aligners and pangenome indexes; building the BWT of very large, highly repetitive genome collections needs workspace proportional to their distinct content rather than their length.
