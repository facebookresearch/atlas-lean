# Tensor sketching of sequences

Embedding a sequence by its counts of gapped length-$t$ subsequences, and sketching that embedding to low dimension, yields Euclidean distances that grow linearly with the mutation rate between related sequences. This makes long inexact seeds usable for alignment of divergent sequences to graphs.
