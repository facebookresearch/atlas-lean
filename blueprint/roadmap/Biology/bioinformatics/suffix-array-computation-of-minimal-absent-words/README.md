# Suffix-array computation of minimal absent words

A minimal absent word of a sequence is an absent word all of whose proper factors occur. Characterizing minimal absent words by the letters that precede occurrences of their longest proper suffix, and showing that those suffixes are always spelled by suffix-array and LCP windows, gives a linear-time and linear-space enumeration that scales to whole genomes.
