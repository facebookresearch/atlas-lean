# Safe sequences of walk covers via dominators

In multi-assembly (transcript, metagenomic and viral strain assembly), a weighted assembly graph with cycles is decomposed into weighted source-to-sink walks. Sequences of vertices or edges that occur in some walk of every walk cover are characterized through the two dominator trees of the graph, enumerated in linear time, and used to fix variables of integer programs for flow decomposition.
