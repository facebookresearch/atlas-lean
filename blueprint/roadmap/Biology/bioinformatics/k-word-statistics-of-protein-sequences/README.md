# k-word statistics of protein sequences

Alignment-free comparison of protein sequences through the frequencies of their overlapping words of length $k$. Divergences between $k$-word frequency profiles, including a relative entropy against the mixture profile that stays finite when words are absent, give distances usable for classification and phylogeny.
