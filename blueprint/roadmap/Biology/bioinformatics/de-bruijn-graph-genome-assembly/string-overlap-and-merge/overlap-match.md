# Matching overlap length

For lists $a$ and $b$ over a type with decidable equality, a natural number $k$ is a matching overlap length when $k \le \min(|a|, |b|)$ and the suffix of $a$ of length $k$ equals the prefix of $b$ of length $k$. The bound matters: on two empty lists, the suffix and prefix of length $1$ are both empty, but $1$ is not a matching length.
