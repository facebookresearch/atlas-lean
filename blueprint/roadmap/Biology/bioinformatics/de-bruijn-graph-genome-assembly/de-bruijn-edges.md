# De Bruijn multigraph of a string

The de Bruijn multigraph of a string $s$ for $k$ is the list of [de Bruijn edges](kmer-edge.md) of its [$k$-mers](kmers.md), one edge per occurrence, in order of position (Pevzner–Tang–Waterman 2001).

## Sources

- P. A. Pevzner, H. Tang and M. S. Waterman, *An Eulerian path approach to DNA fragment assembly*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 98 (2001), 9748–9753.
