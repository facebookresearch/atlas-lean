# Reference-assisted scaffolding with breakpoint graphs

Ordering and orienting the contigs of a fragmented assembly using related complete genomes and their phylogeny. Synteny-block adjacencies of the assembly and of the references form a multi-colour breakpoint graph; the missing assembly adjacencies are chosen by phylogenetic parsimony on half-breakpoint states followed by a minimum-cost perfect matching.
