# Metric filtering of chromosome conformation graphs

Selecting a high-confidence subset of chromosome conformation capture (3C, 4C, Hi-C) interactions whose implied spatial distances are mutually consistent with a metric, before embedding the genome in three dimensions. Consistency is required along all short paths (triangles in the most lenient version), and the resulting maximum-reward subgraph problems are NP-hard but admit set-cover approximations.
