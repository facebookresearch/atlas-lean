# Branching-process models of PCR amplification

Polymerase chain reaction modelled as a population-size-dependent Galton–Watson process in which each molecule duplicates with a Michaelis–Menten probability that falls as molecules accumulate. Explains why amplification is invisible for many cycles, when it becomes observable, and why the early random phase limits inference of the initial copy number in quantitative PCR.
