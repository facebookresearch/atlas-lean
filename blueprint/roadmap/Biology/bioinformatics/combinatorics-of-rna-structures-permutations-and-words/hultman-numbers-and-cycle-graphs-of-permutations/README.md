# Hultman numbers and cycle graphs of permutations

The cycle graph of a permutation (Bafna–Pevzner) decomposes uniquely into alternating cycles, whose number controls transposition and block-interchange distances in genome rearrangement. The Hultman numbers count permutations by this cycle number; they have topological (polygon gluing) and random-matrix interpretations and explicit generating functions.
