# Irreducible components of RNA secondary structures

An RNA secondary structure (a noncrossing arc diagram with arcs of length at least 4) decomposes uniquely into a sequence of unpaired vertices and irreducible blocks, each closed by an arc joining its first and last vertex. Under Boltzmann-type energy weighting the number of irreducible blocks undergoes a phase transition governed by the relative position of the singularities of the block and structure generating functions.
