# Information limits of shotgun assembly

When can a genome be reconstructed exactly from $N$ error-free reads of length $L$ sampled uniformly at random? Beyond Lander–Waterman coverage, the answer is governed by repeats: interleaved repeat pairs and triple repeats whose copies are not bridged by any read make two genomes indistinguishable, while assemblers succeed under bridging conditions of increasing generality.
