# k-shortest-path inference of regulatory pathways

Candidate regulatory pathways through a gene of interest found as the $k$ shortest simple paths from it to every other gene of a weighted interaction network, stored in a pseudo tree; size bounds, a Dijkstra-like construction and its complexity.
