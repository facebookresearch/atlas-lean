# Graph-regularized tensor completion of spatial transcriptomes

Imputation of missing entries of a gene × spatial-row × spatial-column expression tensor by a nonnegative low-rank CP factorization fitted to observed entries and smoothed over the Cartesian product of the spatial grid graphs and a protein interaction network.
