# Outside algorithm

For weights in any commutative semiring and positions $i, j$ with $i + m < j$ that [can pair](can-pair.md),

$$\bar Z(i, j)\, w(i, j)\, Z(i + 1, j - i - 1) = \sum_{P \ni (i, j)} \prod_{(a, b) \in P} w(a, b),$$

the sum over the [secondary structures](structures.md) containing the pair $(i, j)$, where $\bar Z$ is the [outside value](outside.md) and $Z$ the [inside value](inside.md) (McCaskill 1990).

## Sources

- J. S. McCaskill, *The equilibrium partition function and base pair binding probabilities for RNA secondary structure*, Biopolymers 29 (1990), 1105–1119.
