# Omnitigs are safe walks

Every [omnitig](is-omnitig.md) of a directed multigraph is [safe](is-safe.md) (Tomescu and Medvedev 2017, Theorem 1, (2) ⇒ (1)).

## Sources

- A. I. Tomescu and P. Medvedev, *Safe and complete contig assembly through omnitigs*, J. Comput. Biol. 24 (2017), 590–602; arXiv:1601.02932, Theorem 1.
