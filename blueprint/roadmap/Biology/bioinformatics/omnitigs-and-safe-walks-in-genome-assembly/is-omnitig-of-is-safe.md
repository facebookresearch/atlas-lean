# Safe walks are omnitigs

Let $w$ be a nonempty [walk](is-walk.md) in a directed multigraph that has at least one [circular edge-covering walk](is-circular-covering.md). If $w$ is [safe](is-safe.md), then $w$ is an [omnitig](is-omnitig.md) (Tomescu and Medvedev 2017, Theorem 1, (1) ⇒ (2)). The nonemptiness is needed: the empty sequence is safe but is not an omnitig.

## Sources

- A. I. Tomescu and P. Medvedev, *Safe and complete contig assembly through omnitigs*, J. Comput. Biol. 24 (2017), 590–602; arXiv:1601.02932, Theorem 1.
