# Zuker's algorithm computes the minimum free energy

For every [loop energy model](loop-energy.md) with real energies of either sign, the [Zuker minimum free energy](zuker-mfe.md) of $s$ equals the minimum of the [free energy](free-energy.md) over all [secondary structures](../mccaskill-partition-function-for-rna-folding/structures.md) on $s$ with minimum hairpin $m$ (Zuker and Stiegler 1981; Zuker and Sankoff 1984; affine multiloop energy of Jaeger, Turner and Zuker 1989). The minimum is over a finite set containing the empty structure, so it is a real number.

## Sources

- M. Zuker and P. Stiegler, *Optimal computer folding of large RNA sequences using thermodynamics and auxiliary information*, Nucleic Acids Res. 9 (1981), 133–148.
- M. Zuker and D. Sankoff, *RNA secondary structures and their prediction*, Bull. Math. Biol. 46 (1984), 591–621.
- J. A. Jaeger, D. H. Turner and M. Zuker, *Improved predictions of secondary structures for RNA*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 86 (1989), 7706–7710.
