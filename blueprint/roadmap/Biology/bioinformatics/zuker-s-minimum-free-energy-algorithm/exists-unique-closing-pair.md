# Unique loop decomposition

Let $P$ be a [secondary structure](../nussinov-algorithm-for-rna-secondary-structure/is-structure.md) on a sequence and $q \in P$. Then exactly one of the following holds:

- $q$ is an [exterior branch](exterior-branches.md) of $P$ and a [branch](branches.md) of no pair of $P$;
- $q$ is not an exterior branch, and there is exactly one $p \in P$ of which $q$ is a branch.
