# Nearest-neighbor loop energy model

A loop energy model consists of

- real hairpin energies $H(i, j)$ for the hairpin loop closed by the pair $(i, j)$;
- real energies $I(i, j, k, l)$ for the stack, bulge or interior loop closed by $(i, j)$ with inner pair $(k, l)$;
- real multiloop parameters $a$ (per multiloop), $b$ (per branch) and $c$ (per unpaired base).

The exterior loop has energy $0$.
