# Digital cytometry: deconvolution and expression purification

Digital cytometry treats a bulk tissue expression profile as a nonnegative combination of cell-type reference profiles. Three linear-algebraic problems follow: recovering the cell-type fractions from a signature matrix, recovering one representative expression profile per cell type once the fractions are known, and recovering sample-specific, cell-type-specific expression. The first two are overdetermined nonnegative systems; the third is underdetermined and needs extra constraints.
