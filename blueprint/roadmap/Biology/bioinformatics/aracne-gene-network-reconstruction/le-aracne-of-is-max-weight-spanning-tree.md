# Chow–Liu trees survive ARACNE

Let $V$ be finite, $I$ symmetric and $I_0$ real, and let $T$ be a spanning tree on $V$ of maximal total weight $\sum_{ik \in T} I(i, k)$ among all spanning trees, with $I_0 < I(i, k)$ for every edge $ik$ of $T$. Then every edge of $T$ is an edge of the [ARACNE network](aracne.md) (Margolin et al. 2006, Theorem 2).

## Sources

- A. A. Margolin, I. Nemenman, K. Basso, C. Wiggins, G. Stolovitzky, R. Dalla Favera and A. Califano, *ARACNE: an algorithm for the reconstruction of gene regulatory networks in a mammalian cellular context*, BMC Bioinformatics 7 (Suppl. 1) (2006), S7, Theorem 2.
