# ARACNE keeps every edge of a forest network

Let $T$ be a forest on $V$, $I$ symmetric and $I_0$ real, such that

- $I_0 < I(i, k)$ for every edge $ik$ of $T$, and
- $I(a, b) \le \min(I(a, c), I(c, b))$ whenever $c \notin \{a, b\}$ and every walk in $T$ from $a$ to $b$ visits $c$.

Then every edge of $T$ is an edge of the [ARACNE network](aracne.md).
