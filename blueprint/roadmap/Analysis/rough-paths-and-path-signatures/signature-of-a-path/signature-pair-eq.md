# Second level of the signature: increments and Lévy area

Let every component of the velocity of $X$ be continuous. For all reals $s, t$ and letters
$i, j$,

$$S(X)^{ij}_{s,t} = \tfrac12 \big(X^i_t - X^i_s\big)\big(X^j_t - X^j_s\big) + A^{ij}_{s,t},$$

where $A^{ij}$ is the [Lévy area](levy-area.md). The symmetric part of the second level is
fixed by the increment, by the shuffle identity $S^i S^j = S^{ij} + S^{ji}$; only the
antisymmetric part, the area, carries new information. This is why a rough path with
$2 \le p < 3$ consists of a path and its area.
