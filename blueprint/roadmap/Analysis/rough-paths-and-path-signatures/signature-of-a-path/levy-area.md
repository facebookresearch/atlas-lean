# Lévy area

For letters $i, j$ and reals $s, t$, the Lévy area of the plane path $(X^i, X^j)$ over $[s, t]$ is

$$A^{ij}_{s,t} = \tfrac12 \big( S(X)^{ij}_{s,t} - S(X)^{ji}_{s,t} \big),$$

the signed area enclosed by the path and the chord joining its endpoints. It is antisymmetric
in $(i, j)$, and together with the increments it determines the second level of the signature
(see [the second level of the signature](signature-pair-eq.md)). For Brownian motion it is the
extra datum a rough path adds to the path.
