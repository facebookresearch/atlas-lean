# Zeroth term of the signature

Let $X$ be a path in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with velocity $v = (v^i)_{i \in \iota}$ and [signature](signature.md) $S(X)$. For all reals $s, t$, the coefficient of the empty word is $S(X)^{\varnothing}_{s,t} = 1$ (Chevyrev and Kormilitzin, arXiv:1603.03788v2, Definition 1.4).
