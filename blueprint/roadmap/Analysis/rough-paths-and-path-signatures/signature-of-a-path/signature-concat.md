# Recursive definition of the signature

Let $X$ be a path in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with velocity $v = (v^i)_{i \in \iota}$ and [signature](signature.md) $S(X)$. Appending a letter $i$ to a word $w$ integrates against $dX^i$: for all reals $s, t$,

$$
S(X)^{w i}_{s,t} = \int_s^t S(X)^{w}_{s,u}\, v^i(u)\, du
$$

(Chevyrev and Kormilitzin, arXiv:1603.03788v2, §1.2.1, the recursion before equation (3)).
