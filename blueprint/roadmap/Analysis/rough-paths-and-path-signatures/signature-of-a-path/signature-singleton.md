# First level of the signature is the increment

Let $X$ be a path in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with velocity $v = (v^i)_{i \in \iota}$ and [signature](signature.md) $S(X)$. For every letter $i$ and reals $s, t$, $S(X)^{i}_{s,t} = \int_s^t v^i(u)\, du$, an oriented integral, with no hypothesis on $v$. When $X^i$ is absolutely continuous with derivative $v^i$ almost everywhere, this is the increment $X^i_t - X^i_s$ (Chevyrev and Kormilitzin, arXiv:1603.03788v2, equation (1), for piecewise $C^1$ paths).
