# Product of tensor series, coefficientwise

For tensor series $F = \sum_w F(w)\, e_w$ and $G = \sum_w G(w)\, e_w$, given by their coefficients on words over an alphabet $\iota$, the concatenation product has coefficients

$$
(F \otimes G)(i_1 \cdots i_k) = \sum_{m=0}^{k} F(i_1 \cdots i_m)\, G(i_{m+1} \cdots i_k)
$$

(Chevyrev and Kormilitzin, arXiv:1603.03788v2, Definition 1.19 and equation (20)).
