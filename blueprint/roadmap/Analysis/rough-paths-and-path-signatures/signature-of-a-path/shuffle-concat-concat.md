# Last-letter recursion of the shuffle product

For words $I, J$ and letters $i, j$, the [shuffle product](shuffle.md) satisfies

$$
(I\,i) ⧢ (J\,j) = (I ⧢ J\,j)\,i \;+\; (I\,i ⧢ J)\,j,
$$

the shuffles ending in $i$ followed by those ending in $j$ (Chevyrev and Kormilitzin, arXiv:1603.03788v2, the "combination identity" in the proof of Theorem 1.14).
