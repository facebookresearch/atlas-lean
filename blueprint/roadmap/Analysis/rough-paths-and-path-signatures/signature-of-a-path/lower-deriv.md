# Lower-endpoint derivative of an iterated integral (its value)

Let $X$ be a path in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with velocity $v = (v^i)_{i \in \iota}$ and [signature](signature.md) $S(X)$. For reals $b, c_0$, define the coefficients $D(\varnothing) = 0$ and $D(i\, w) = -v^i(c_0)\, S(X)^w_{c_0,b}$ for a letter $i$ and a word $w$. For continuous velocity, $D(u)$ is the derivative of $c \mapsto S(X)^u_{c,b}$ at $c_0$ ([derivative of the signature in its lower endpoint](has-deriv-at-signature-lower.md)).
