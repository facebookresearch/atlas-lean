# Rough paths and path signatures

Signatures of paths and controlled differential equations: iterated integrals of a path, Chen's identity, signatures of piecewise-linear paths, the shuffle identity, time reversal and reparametrisation, the Lévy area, signature series of linear controlled equations, uniqueness of the signature up to tree-like equivalence, universal approximation by linear functionals of the signature, and multiplicative functionals of finite p-variation with Lyons's extension theorem. Brownian motion and stochastic integrals are under Probability.

## Related topics

- [Brownian motion and stochastic calculus](../../Probability/stochastic-calculus/README.md): Brownian
  paths have finite $p$-variation only for $p > 2$; lifted with their Lévy area they are the basic
  rough paths, and Stratonovich equations are rough differential equations.
- [Quadratic variation of Brownian motion](../../Probability/stochastic-calculus/quadratic-variation-of-brownian-motion/README.md):
  why Brownian paths have unbounded variation, so that their iterated integrals are not
  Riemann–Stieltjes integrals.
- [Ordinary differential equations](../ordinary-differential-equations/README.md): controlled
  equations driven by $X_t = t$ are ordinary differential equations.
- [Residual networks as discretised flows](../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/supervised-learning/residual-networks-as-discretised-flows/README.md):
  a residual network is an Euler scheme for a differential equation in depth; driving that
  equation by a data stream instead of by time gives a controlled differential equation, whose
  response is a function of the stream's signature.
