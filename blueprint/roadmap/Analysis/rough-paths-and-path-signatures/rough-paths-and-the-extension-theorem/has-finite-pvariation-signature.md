# Signatures of continuously differentiable paths have finite $1$-variation in every degree

Let $\iota$ be a finite alphabet and let $X$ be a path in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with continuous velocity $v = (v^j)_{j \in \iota}$; write $\omega(s, t) = \int_s^t \sum_j |v^j(u)|\, du$ for its $\ell^1$ length between $s$ and $t$, and $S(X)_{s,t}$ for its [signature](../signature-of-a-path/signature.md). Then for every $n$ and every $J \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, the signature has [finite $1$-variation](has-finite-pvariation.md) of degree $n$ on $J$, controlled by $\omega$ with weight $1$.
