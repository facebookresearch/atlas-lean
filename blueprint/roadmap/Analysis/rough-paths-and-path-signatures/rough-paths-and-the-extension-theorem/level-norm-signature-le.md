# Factorial decay of the signature

Let the velocity of a path $X$ in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ ($\iota$ finite) be continuous, and let
$s \le t$. Then for every $i$,

$$\sum_{|w| = i} \big| S(X)^w_{s,t} \big| \le \frac{\omega(s,t)^i}{i!}, \qquad \omega(s,t) = \int_s^t \sum_j |v^j(u)|\, du ,$$

where $\omega(s,t)$ is the $\ell^1$ length of the path between $s$ and $t$. So the levels of the
signature decay factorially; this is the case $p = 1$, $\beta = 1$ of Lyons's control
$\omega^{i/p}/(\beta\,(i/p)!)$, and the reason the signature series of a linear controlled
equation converges.

## Sources

- T. Lyons, *Differential equations driven by rough signals*, Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 14 (1998),
  inequalities (2.20)–(2.21), here for paths with continuous velocity.
