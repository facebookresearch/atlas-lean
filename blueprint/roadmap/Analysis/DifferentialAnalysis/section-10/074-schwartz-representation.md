# Theorem 10.5 (Schwartz representation)

Any tempered distribution can be written in the form of a finite sum $u = \sum_{|\alpha| \leq m,\, |\beta| \leq m} x^{\alpha} D_{x}^{\beta} u_{\alpha\beta}$, $u_{\alpha\beta} \in \mathcal{C}_0^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, or in the form $u = \sum_{|\alpha| \leq m,\, |\beta| \leq m} D_{x}^{\beta}(x^{\alpha} v_{\alpha\beta})$, $v_{\alpha\beta} \in \mathcal{C}_0^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$. Thus every tempered distribution is a finite sum of derivatives of continuous functions of polynomial growth.
