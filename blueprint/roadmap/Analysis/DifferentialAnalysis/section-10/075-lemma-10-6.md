# Lemma 10.6

For any $\gamma \in \mathbb{N}_{0}^{n}$ there are polynomials $p_{\alpha,\gamma}(x)$ of degrees at most $|\gamma - \alpha|$ such that $\langle x \rangle^{k} D^{\gamma} v = \sum_{\alpha \leq \gamma} D^{\gamma - \alpha}(p_{\alpha,\gamma} \langle x \rangle^{k - 2|\gamma - \alpha|} v)$.
