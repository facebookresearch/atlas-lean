# Proposition 10.2

If $u \in H^m(\mathbb{R}^n)$, $m \in \mathbb{R}$, then $D^\alpha u \in H^{m-|\alpha|}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $D^\alpha \colon H^m(\mathbb{R}^n) \to H^{m-|\alpha|}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is continuous.
