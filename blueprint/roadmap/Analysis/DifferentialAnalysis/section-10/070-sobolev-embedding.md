# Theorem 10.1 (Sobolev embedding)

If $u \in H^m(\mathbb{R}^n)$ where $m > n/2$ then $u \in \mathcal{C}_0^0(\mathbb{R}^n)$, i.e., $H^m(\mathbb{R}^n) \subset \mathcal{C}_0^0(\mathbb{R}^n)$, $m > n/2$.
