# Definition 9.5

If $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ we define its Fourier transform by $\hat{u}(\varphi) = u(\hat{\varphi})$ for all $\varphi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$.
