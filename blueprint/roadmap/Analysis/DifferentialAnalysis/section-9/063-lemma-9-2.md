# Lemma 9.2

For all $\varphi, \psi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$, Parseval's identity holds: $\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \varphi \bar{\psi}\, dx = (2\pi)^{-n} \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \hat{\varphi} \overline{\hat{\psi}}\, d\xi$.
