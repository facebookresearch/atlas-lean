# Lemma 9.4

If $m \in \mathbb{N}$ is an integer, then $u \in H^m(\mathbb{R}^n) \Leftrightarrow D^\alpha u \in L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$ for all $|\alpha| \leq m$.
