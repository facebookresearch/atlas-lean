# Proposition 9.6

The definition (9.7) gives an isomorphism $\mathcal{F} \colon \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n) \to \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$, $\mathcal{F}u = \hat{u}$, satisfying the identities $\widehat{D^\alpha u} = \xi^\alpha u$ and $\widehat{x^\alpha u} = (-1)^{|\alpha|} D^\alpha \hat{u}$.
