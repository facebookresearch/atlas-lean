# Lemma 11.7

If $v \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ has compact support and $\varphi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ then $v * \varphi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
