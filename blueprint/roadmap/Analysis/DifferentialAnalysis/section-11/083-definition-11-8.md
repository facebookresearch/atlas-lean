# Definition 11.8

A parametrix for a constant coefficient differential operator $P(D)$ is a distribution $F \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ such that $P(D) F = \delta + \psi$, $\psi \in \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$. An operator $P(D)$ is said to be hypoelliptic if it has a parametrix satisfying $\operatorname{sing\,supp}(F) \subset \{0\}$, where for any $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $(\operatorname{sing\,supp}(u))^{\complement} = \{\bar{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \mid \exists \varphi \in \mathcal{C}_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),\, \varphi(\bar{x}) \neq 0,\, \varphi u \in \mathcal{C}_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\}$.
