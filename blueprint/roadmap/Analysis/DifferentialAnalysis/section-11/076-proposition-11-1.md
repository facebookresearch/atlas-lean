# Proposition 11.1

The subspace $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n}) \subset \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ is weakly dense, i.e., each $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ is the weak limit of a sequence $u_{j} \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
