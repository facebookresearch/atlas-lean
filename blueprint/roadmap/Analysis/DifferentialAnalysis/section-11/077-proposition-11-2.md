# Proposition 11.2

If $u_{j} \to u$ and $u_{j}' \to u'$ weakly in $\mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ then $c u_{j} \to c u$, $u_{j} + u_{j}' \to u + u'$, $D^{\alpha} u_{j} \to D^{\alpha} u$ and $\langle x \rangle^{m} u_{j} \to \langle x \rangle^{m} u$ weakly in $\mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
