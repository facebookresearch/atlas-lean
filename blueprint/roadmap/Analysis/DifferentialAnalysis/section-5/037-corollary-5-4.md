# Corollary 5.4

For any positive measure $\mu$, any continuous linear functional $L \colon L^{2}(X, \mu) \to \mathbb{C}$ is of the form $Lf = \int_{X} f\bar{g}\, d\mu$, for some $g \in L^{2}(X, \mu)$.
