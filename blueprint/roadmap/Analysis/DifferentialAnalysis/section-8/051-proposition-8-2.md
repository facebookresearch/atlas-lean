# Proposition 8.2

If $v \in \mathcal{C}_0^{0}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ then as $t \to 0$, $v_t = v * \varphi_t \to v$ in $\mathcal{C}_0^{0}(\mathbb{R}^n)$.
