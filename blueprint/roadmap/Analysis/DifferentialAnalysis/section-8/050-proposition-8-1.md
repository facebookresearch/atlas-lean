# Proposition 8.1

If $v \in \mathcal{C}_0^{0}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $\psi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ then $v * \psi \in \mathcal{C}_0^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^n) = \bigcap_{k \geq 0} \mathcal{C}_0^{k}(\mathbb{R}^n)$.
