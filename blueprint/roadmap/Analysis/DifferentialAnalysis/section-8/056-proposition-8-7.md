# Proposition 8.7

If $U_a \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ are open for $a \in A$ and $K \Subset \bigcup_{a \in A} U_a$ then there exist finitely many $\varphi_i \in \mathcal{C}_c^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^n)$, with $0 \leq \varphi_i \leq 1$, $\operatorname{supp}(\varphi_i) \subset U_{a_i}$ such that $\sum_i \varphi_i = 1$ in a neighbourhood of $K$.
