# Definition 6.6

Schwartz' space of test functions on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n}) = \{u \colon \mathbb{R}^{n} \to \mathbb{C} \mid u \in \langle x\rangle^{-l} \mathcal{C}_0^{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n}) \text{ for all } k \text{ and } l \in \mathbb{N}\}$.
