# Definition 6.2

Let $\mathcal{C}_0^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ be the subspace of $\mathcal{C}_0(\mathbb{R}^{n}) = \mathcal{C}_0^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ such that each element $u \in \mathcal{C}_0^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ is continuously differentiable and $\frac{\partial u}{\partial x_{j}} \in \mathcal{C}_0(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $j = 1, \ldots, n$.
