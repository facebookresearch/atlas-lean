# Corollary 7.2

For any $k \in \mathbb{N}$ the norms $\|\langle x\rangle^{k} \varphi\|_{\mathcal{C}^{k}}$ and $\sum_{|\alpha| \leq k,\, |\beta| \leq k} \|x^{\alpha} D_{x}^{\beta} \varphi\|_{\infty}$ are equivalent.
