# Lemma 11.10

If $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ then for any polynomial $\operatorname{sing\,supp}(P(D) u) \subset \operatorname{sing\,supp}(u)$ for all $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$.
