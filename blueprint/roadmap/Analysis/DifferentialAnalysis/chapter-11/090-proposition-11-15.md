# Proposition 11.15

If $f \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ has compact support then $\operatorname{sing\,supp}(u * f) \subset \operatorname{sing\,supp}(u) + \operatorname{sing\,supp}(f)$.
