# Proposition 11.16

If $f \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ has compact support then there exists a unique $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with $\operatorname{supp}(u) \subset \{t \geq -T\}$ for some $T$ and $(\partial_t + \Delta) u = f$ in $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$.
