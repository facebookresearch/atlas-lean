# Lemma 11.13

If $P_m(\xi)$ is homogeneous of degree $m$ and elliptic then $Q(\xi) = \frac{(1-\varphi(\xi))}{P_m(\xi)} \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is the Fourier transform of a parametrix for $P_m(D)$, satisfying (11.27).
