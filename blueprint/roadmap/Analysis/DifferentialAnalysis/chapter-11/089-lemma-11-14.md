# Lemma 11.14

Let $P(\xi)$ be a polynomial of degree $m$ satisfying $|P(\xi)| \geq C|\xi|^m$ in $|\xi| > 1/C$ for some $C > 0$, then for some constants $C_\alpha$, $\left|D^\alpha \frac{1}{P(\xi)}\right| \leq C_\alpha |\xi|^{-m-|\alpha|}$ in $|\xi| > 1/C$.
