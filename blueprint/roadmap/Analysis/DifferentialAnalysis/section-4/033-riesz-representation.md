# Theorem 4.12 (Riesz representation.)

If $X$ is a locally compact metric space then every continuous linear functional on $\mathcal{C}_0(X)$ is given by a unique finite Radon measure on $X$ through the map $\mathcal{C}_0(X) \ni f \longmapsto \int_{X} f \, d\mu$.
