# Definition 4.4

A measurable extended function $f \colon X \to [-\infty, \infty]$ is said to be integrable on $E$ if its positive and negative parts both have finite integrals over $E$, and then $$\int_{E} f\, d\mu = \int_{E} f_{+}\, d\mu - \int_{E} f_{-}\, d\mu.$$
