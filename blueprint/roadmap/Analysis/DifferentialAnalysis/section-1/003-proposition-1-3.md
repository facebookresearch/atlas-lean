# Proposition 1.3

The following are equivalent conditions on a linear functional $u \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ on a normed space $V$. (1) $u$ is continuous. (2) $u$ is continuous at $0$. (3) $\{u(f) \in \mathbb{R} \mid f \in V,\, \|f\| \leq 1\}$ is bounded. (4) $\exists C$ s.t. $|u(f)| \leq C\|f\|$ $\forall f \in V$.
