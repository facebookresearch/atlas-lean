# Lemma 1.4

Each $f \in \mathcal{C}_0(X)$ can be decomposed uniquely as the difference of its positive and negative parts $f = f_+ - f_-$, $f_{\pm} \in \mathcal{C}_0(X)$, $f_{\pm}(x) \leq |f(x)|$ $\forall x \in X$.
