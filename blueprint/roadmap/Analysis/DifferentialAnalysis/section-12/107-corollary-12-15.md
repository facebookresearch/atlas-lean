# Corollary 12.15

For $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $\mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u) = \emptyset \Longleftrightarrow u \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
