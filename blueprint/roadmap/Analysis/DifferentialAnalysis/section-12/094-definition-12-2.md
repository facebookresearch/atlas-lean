# Definition 12.2

The cone-support and cone-singular-support of a tempered distribution are the subsets $\operatorname{Csp}(u) \subset \mathbb{R}^{n} \cup \mathbb{S}^{n-1}$ and $\operatorname{Css}(u) \subset \mathbb{R}^{n} \cup \mathbb{S}^{n-1}$ defined by the conditions: $\operatorname{Csp}(u) \cap \mathbb{R}^{n} = \operatorname{supp}(u)$; $(\operatorname{Csp}(u))^{\complement} \cap \mathbb{S}^{n-1} = \{\omega \in \mathbb{S}^{n-1} \mid \exists R>0,\, \psi \in \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{n-1}),\, \psi(\omega) \neq 0,\, \psi_{R} u = 0\}$; $\operatorname{Css}(u) \cap \mathbb{R}^{n} = \operatorname{sing\,supp}(u)$; $(\operatorname{Css}(u))^{\complement} \cap \mathbb{S}^{n-1} = \{\omega \in \mathbb{S}^{n-1} \mid \exists R>0,\, \psi \in \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{n-1}),\, \psi(\omega) \neq 0,\, \psi_{R} u \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\}$.
