# Lemma 12.3

For any $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $\operatorname{Csp}(u)$ and $\operatorname{Css}(u)$ are closed subsets of $\mathbb{B}^{n}$ and if $\tilde{\psi} \in \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{n})$ has $\operatorname{supp}(\tilde{\psi}) \cap \operatorname{Css}(u) = \emptyset$ then for $R$ sufficiently large $\tilde{\psi}_{R} u \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
