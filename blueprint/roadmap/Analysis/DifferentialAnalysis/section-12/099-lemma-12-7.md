# Lemma 12.7

If $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ and $\operatorname{Css}(u) \cap \Gamma = \emptyset$ where $\Gamma \subset \mathbb{S}^{n-1}$ is a closed set, then $u = u_{1} + u_{2}$ where $\operatorname{Csp}(u_{1}) \cap \Gamma = \emptyset$ and $u_{2} \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$; in fact $u = u_{1}' + u_{1}'' + u_{2}$ where $u_{1}' \in \mathcal{C}_{c}^{-\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ and $0 \notin \operatorname{supp}(u_{1}'')$, $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \setminus \{0\}$, $x/|x| \in \Gamma \Longrightarrow x \notin \operatorname{supp}(u_{1}'')$.
