# Corollary 12.17

For any $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$, $(p, q) \in \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(u) \Longleftrightarrow (q, -p) \in \mathrm{WF}_{\mathrm{sc}}(\hat{u})$.
