# Lemma 12.10

If $u, v \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ and $\omega \in \operatorname{Css}(u) \cap \mathbb{S}^{n-1} \Longrightarrow -\omega \notin \operatorname{Css}(v)$ then their convolution is defined unambiguously, using the pairing in Lemma 12.5, by $u * v(\phi) = u(\check{v} * \phi)$ for all $\phi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$.
