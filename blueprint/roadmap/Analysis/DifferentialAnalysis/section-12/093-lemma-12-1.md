# Lemma 12.1

If $\psi \in \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n} \setminus \{0\})$ is homogeneous of degree $0$ then $|D^{\alpha} \psi| \leq C_{\alpha}|x|^{-|\alpha|}$.
