# Corollary 12.9

Under the conditions of Lemma 12.6, $\operatorname{Css}(u * v) \subset (\operatorname{sing\,supp}(u) + \operatorname{sing\,supp}(v)) \cup (\operatorname{Css}(v) \cap \mathbb{S}^{n-1})$.
