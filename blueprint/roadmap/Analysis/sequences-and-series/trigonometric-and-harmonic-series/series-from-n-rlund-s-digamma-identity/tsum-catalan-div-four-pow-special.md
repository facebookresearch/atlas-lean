# Catalan series summing to $4(1 - \log 2)$ and $\frac{10}{3} - 4 \log 2$

With $C_n$ the
$n$th Catalan number,

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{C_n}{4^n (n + 1)} = 4 (1 - \log 2)$$

and

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{C_n}{4^n (n + 1)(n + 2)} = \frac{10}{3} - 4 \log 2.$$

## Sources

- Kunle Adegoke and Robert Frontczak, *Series Associated with a Forgotten Identity of Nörlund*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A18, Theorem 3, first two identities (`integers-2025-A18-thm-3`).
