# A product series for $\pi \cot(\pi/k)$

For every integer $k \ge 2$,

$$(k - 2)\Bigl(1 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n + 1}
\prod_{j=1}^{n} \frac{kj - (k - 2)}{kj + 1}\Bigr) = \pi \cot\frac{\pi}{k}.$$
