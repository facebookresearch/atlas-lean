# Catalan series over powers of $4$ equal to $4\ln 2$ and $4\ln 2 - 2$

Let $C_n$ be the
$n$-th Catalan number. Then

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{2n+1}{n+1} \cdot \frac{C_n}{4^n} = 4\ln 2$$

and

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{2n+1}{(n+1)(n+2)} \cdot \frac{C_n}{4^n} = 4\ln 2 - 2.$$
