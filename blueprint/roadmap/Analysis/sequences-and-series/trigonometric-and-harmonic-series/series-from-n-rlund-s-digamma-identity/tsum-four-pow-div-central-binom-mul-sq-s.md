# Central binomial series for $\pi^2/8$ and a series for $2\ln 2$

We have

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{4^n}{\binom{2(n+1)}{n+1} (n+1)^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$$

and

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2n+1)(n+1)} = 2\ln 2.$$
