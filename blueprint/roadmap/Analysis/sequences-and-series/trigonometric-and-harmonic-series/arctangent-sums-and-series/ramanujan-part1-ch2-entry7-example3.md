# Arctangent series with halved squares (Entry 7, Example 3)

For every real number
$n > 0$, the series

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \arctan \frac{1}{2(n + k + 1)^2}$$

converges absolutely and its sum is $\arctan \frac{1}{2n + 1}$.
