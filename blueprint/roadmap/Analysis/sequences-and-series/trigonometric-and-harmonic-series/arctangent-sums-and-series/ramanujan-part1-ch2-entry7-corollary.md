# Ramanujan's infinite arctangent series (corollary to Entry 7)

For every real number
$n > 0$, the series

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \arctan \frac{2}{(n + 2k + 1)^2}$$

converges absolutely and its sum is $\arctan(1/n)$.
