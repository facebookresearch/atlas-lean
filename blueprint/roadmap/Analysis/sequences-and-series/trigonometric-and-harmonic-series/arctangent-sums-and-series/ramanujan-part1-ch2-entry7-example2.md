# Alternating arctangent series (Entry 7, Example 2)

For every real number $n > 0$, the
series

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (-1)^k \arctan \frac{2}{(n + k + 1)^2}$$

converges absolutely and its sum is $\arctan \frac{1}{n^2 + n + 1}$.
