# Arctangent series at the exceptional point (Entry 7, Example 1)

Let
$c = (\sqrt{5} - 1)/2$. The series

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \arctan \frac{2}{(c + k + 1)^2}$$

converges absolutely and its sum is $\pi/2$.
