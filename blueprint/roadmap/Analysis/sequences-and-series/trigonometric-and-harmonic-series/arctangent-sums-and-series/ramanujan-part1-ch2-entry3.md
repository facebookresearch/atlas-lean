# Ramanujan's arctangent identity for $\sum_{k=1}^{2n+1} \arctan(1/(n+k))$

For every
positive integer $n$,

$$\sum_{k=1}^{2n+1} \arctan \frac{1}{n+k} = \frac{\pi}{4} +
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \arctan \frac{10k}{(3k^2 + 2)(9k^2 - 1)}.$$
