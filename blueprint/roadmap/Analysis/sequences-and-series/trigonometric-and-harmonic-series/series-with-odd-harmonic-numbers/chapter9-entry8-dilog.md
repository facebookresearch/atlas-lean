# The dilogarithm as a power series

For real $x$, the value is $\sum_{n \ge 1} x^n / n^2$, the dilogarithm $\operatorname{Li}_2(x)$, when $|x| \le 1$, and $0$ when $|x| > 1$, where the series diverges. It is the dilogarithm power series $\sum_{n \ge 0} x^{n+1}/(n+1)^2$, assigned $0$ where it is not summable, with no change of domain.
