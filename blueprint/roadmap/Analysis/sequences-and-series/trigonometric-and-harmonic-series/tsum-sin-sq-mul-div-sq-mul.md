# A closed form for $\sum \sin^2(n\delta)/(n^2\delta)$

For every real $\delta$ with
$0 < \delta < \pi$,

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sin^2(n\delta)}{n^2\delta} = \frac{\pi - \delta}{2}.$$
