# Terms of the cosine-minus-sine central binomial series

For a real number $x$ and a
natural number $k$, the $k$-th term is
$\binom{2k}{k} \frac{\cos^{2k+1} x - \sin^{2k+1} x}{2^{2k} (2k+1)^2}$.
