# The geometric series of a real ratio r with |r| < 1 sums to 1/(1 − r)

Let $r$ be a real number with $|r| < 1$. Then the series $\sum_{n \ge 0} r^n$ converges, and $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} r^n = \frac{1}{1 - r}$.
