# The alternating harmonic series sums to $\log 2$

The partial sums
$\sum_{i=0}^{k-1} (-1)^i / (i + 1)$ of real numbers converge to $\log 2$ as $k \to \infty$.

Doubled, the partial sums $2 \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} (-1)^i / (i + 1)$ converge to $2 \log 2$: this is
the Madelung constant of the one-dimensional ionic chain of alternating charges, taken as the limit
of the partial sums in this order, since the series converges only conditionally.
