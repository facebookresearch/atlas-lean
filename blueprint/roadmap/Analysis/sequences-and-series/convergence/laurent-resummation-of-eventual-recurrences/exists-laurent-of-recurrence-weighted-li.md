# Laurent numerators of weighted lattice-line series with eventual recurrences

Let $w : \mathbb{Z}^2 \to \mathbb{C}$ be arbitrary complex weights with $w(n) = 0$ whenever $n_1 < N_1$ or $n_2 < N_1$, let $e_1, e_2$ be positive integers, and let $c(t) = \sum_{e_1 n_1 + e_2 n_2 = t} w(n)$, a finite sum. Suppose there are $N \in \mathbb{Z}$, a complex polynomial $E = \sum_{i \le d} E_i X^i$ with $E(0) \ne 0$ and $M \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $c(m) = 0$ for every $m < N$ and $\sum_{i=0}^{d} E_i\, c(N + m - i) = 0$ for every $m \ge M$. Then there are complex polynomials $P$ and $Q \ne 0$ and $m_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that for every $X \ne 0$ at which $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}^2} w(n)\, X^{e_1 n_1 + e_2 n_2}$ is summable,

$$\Bigl(\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}^2} w(n)\, X^{e_1 n_1 + e_2 n_2}\Bigr)\, Q(X) = X^{m_0} P(X).$$

The polynomials $P$, $Q$ and the shift $m_0$ do not depend on $X$.
