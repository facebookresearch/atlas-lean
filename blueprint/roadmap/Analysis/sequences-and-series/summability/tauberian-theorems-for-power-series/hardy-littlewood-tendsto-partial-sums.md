# Hardy–Littlewood Tauberian theorem

Hardy and Littlewood (1914). Let $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be real with $a_n \ge 0$. If the power series $\sum_n a_n r^n$
converges for every $r \in (0, 1)$ and its sum tends to $A$ as $r \to 1^-$ (Abel limit $A$), then the partial sums
$\sum_{n < N} a_n$ converge to $A$.
