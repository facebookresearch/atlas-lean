# Agreement of two definitions of the harmonic numbers

For every natural number $n$, the rational number $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \frac{1}{k+1}$ equals the [harmonic number](../../../../Biology/coalescent-theory/fu-s-branch-length-covariances-for-every-sample/harm.md)
$H_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (k+1)^{-1} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{i}$: the two expressions of the $n$-th harmonic number
agree.
