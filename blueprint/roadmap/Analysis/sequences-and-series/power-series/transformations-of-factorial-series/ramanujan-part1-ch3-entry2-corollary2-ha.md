# Ramanujan's series for the harmonic numbers, restated

Let $H_k = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} (i+1)^{-1}$ be the $k$-th harmonic number. For every complex number $x$, the series
$\sum_{k \ge 1} \frac{H_k x^k}{k!}$ and $\sum_{k \ge 1} \frac{(-1)^{k-1} x^k}{k!\, k}$ converge absolutely, and
$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{H_k x^k}{k!} = e^x \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{k-1} x^k}{k!\, k}$. This is
[Chapter 3, Entry 2, Corollary 2](ramanujan-part1-ch3-entry2-corollary2-ha-2.md) with the harmonic numbers written
as sums of reciprocals $(i + 1)^{-1}$; the [two expressions agree](harmonic-eq-mathlib-harmonic.md).
