# Convergence of the left side of Ramanujan's Chapter 3, Entry 2

For all complex numbers
$x$ and $z$ such that $z + r \ne 0$ for every natural number $r$, the series
$\sum_{j \ge 0} \frac{x^{j+1}}{(z+j)\, j!}$ converges absolutely.
