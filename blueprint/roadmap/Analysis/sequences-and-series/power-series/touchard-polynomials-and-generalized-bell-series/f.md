# Generalized Bell function extended to $n = -1$

For an integer $n \ge -1$ and a complex
number $x$, $f(n, x) = 1$ if $n = -1$, and otherwise $f(n, x)$ is the generalized Bell function
$F(0, 1, x; n) = e^{-x} \sum_{k \ge 0} (k + 1)^n x^{k + 1} / k!$.
