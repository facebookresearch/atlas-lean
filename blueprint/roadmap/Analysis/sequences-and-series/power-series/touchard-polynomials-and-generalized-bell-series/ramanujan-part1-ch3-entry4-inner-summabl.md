# Absolute convergence of Ramanujan's Bell-type series (Entry 4)

For every complex
number $x$ and every natural number $n$, the series
$\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{(j+1)^n x^{j+1}}{j!}$ converges absolutely.
