# Ramanujan's tree-function series (Chapter 3, Entry 15)

Let $x$ and $u$ be real numbers
with $0 < u < 1$ and $u - \log u = 1 + x^2/2$. Then the series

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(k+1)^{k-1}}{k!}\, e^{-k(1 + x^2/2)}$$

converges to $u\, e^{1 + x^2/2}$.
