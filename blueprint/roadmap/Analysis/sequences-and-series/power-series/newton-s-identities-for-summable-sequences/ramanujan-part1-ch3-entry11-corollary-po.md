# Powers of an absolutely summable sequence are absolutely summable

Let $(a_j)_{j \ge 0}$
be a sequence of complex numbers with $\sum_j |a_j| < \infty$, and let $n \ge 1$ be an integer.
Then $\sum_j |a_j^n| < \infty$.
