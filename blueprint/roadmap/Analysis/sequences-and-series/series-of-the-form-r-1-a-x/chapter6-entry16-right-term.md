# Right-hand terms of Entry 16 of Chapter 6

For complex $a, r, x$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, with $k = j + 1$, the $j$-th term of the second series on the right-hand side of Entry 16 is

$$\frac{a^{k-1}\, r^k\, x^{(k-1)k}}{1 - r x^{k-1}}.$$

Complex division is total: the term is $0$ when its denominator vanishes, for example at $j = 0$ and $a = r = x = 1$. The term is defined for all complex $a, r, x$; no convergence hypothesis enters the definition.
