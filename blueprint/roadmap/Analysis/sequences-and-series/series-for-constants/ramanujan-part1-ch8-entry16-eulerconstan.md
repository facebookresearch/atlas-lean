# Ramanujan's series for Euler's constant

For $k \ge 0$ let $A_k = (3^k - 1)/2$. Then the
series

$$S = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (k+1) \sum_{j=A_k+1}^{A_{k+1}} \frac{1}{(3j)^3 - 3j}$$

converges, and $\gamma = \log 2 - 2S$, where $\gamma$ is Euler's constant.
