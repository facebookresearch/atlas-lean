# Potential-heat restart and spatial regularity

The actual Volterra field restarts from its own time slices. Positive viscosity yields first spatial smoothing from continuous exponentially growing data, with uniform bounds away from initial time. C1 data with exponentially growing derivative give joint derivative continuity and recovery at initial time.
