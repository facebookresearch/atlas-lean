# The time-scaled heat solution is smooth at positive times

Let $\Phi(t, x) = (4\pi t)^{-1/2} e^{-x^2/(4t)}$ be the heat kernel on $\mathbb{R}$, and for $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ let $u(t, x) = \int \Phi(t, x - y) f(y)\, dy$ for $t > 0$ (and $u(t, x) = f(x)$ for $t \le 0$). Let $f$ be almost everywhere strongly measurable and bounded, and $\nu > 0$. Then $(t, x) \mapsto u(\nu t, x)$ is jointly $C^\infty$ on $(0, \infty) \times \mathbb{R}$.
