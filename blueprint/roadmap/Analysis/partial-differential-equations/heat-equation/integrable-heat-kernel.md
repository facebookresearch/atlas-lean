# The heat kernel is integrable

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, with its Lebesgue measure, and let $\Phi$ be the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) on $E$. For $t > 0$, the function $x \mapsto \Phi(t, x)$ is integrable on $E$.

## Informally uses

- [The heat kernel has total mass one](integral-heat-kernel.md): the Gaussian integral computed there
  is finite.
