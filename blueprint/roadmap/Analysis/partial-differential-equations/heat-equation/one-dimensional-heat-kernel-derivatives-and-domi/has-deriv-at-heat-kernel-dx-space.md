# Second spatial derivative of the one-dimensional heat kernel

For $t > 0$, with $\Phi(t, x) = (4\pi t)^{-1/2} e^{-x^2/(4t)}$ and $\partial_x \Phi(t, x) = -\frac{x}{2t}\Phi(t, x)$, the function $x \mapsto \partial_x \Phi(t, x)$ is differentiable at every $x$ with derivative $\Big(\frac{x^2}{4t^2} - \frac{1}{2t}\Big) \Phi(t, x)$.
