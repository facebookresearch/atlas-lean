# Quittner's conjecture on positive entire solutions of u_t − Δu = uᵖ

Quittner's conjecture (Oberwolfach Report 29/2005, Conjecture 1): for `N ≥ 1` and `p > 1`, the
equation `u_t - Δu = u^p` on `ℝ^N × ℝ` has a positive classical solution if and only if
`p (N - 2) ≥ N + 2`.

Its resolution: [Liouville theorem for the semilinear heat equation](positive-solution-iff-supercritical-hold.md).
