# The Gaussian heat solution has exponential growth on every strip

For an initial datum $f$, the heat evolution is $u(t,x) = \int G_t(x-y) f(y)\,dy$ for $t > 0$ ($G_t$ the one-dimensional heat kernel) and $u(t,x) = f(x)$ for $t \le 0$; $f$ has exponential growth when $|f(x)| \le M e^{c|x|}$, and a field $u(t,x)$ has exponential growth on the strip $[0,T]$ when $|u(t,x)| \le M e^{c|x|}$ for all $t \in [0,T]$ and all $x$, with constants $M, c \ge 0$. The Gaussian solution has spatial exponential growth uniformly on any finite strip.
