# Single-mode solution of the Dirichlet heat equation

On the nondimensional interval $[0, 1]$ with diffusivity $\kappa > 0$, the function $u(t, x) = e^{-\kappa\pi^2 t}\sin(\pi x)$, $t \ge 0$,
satisfies $u_t = \kappa\,u_{xx}$, the boundary conditions $u(t, 0) = u(t, 1) = 0$ and the initial condition $u(0, x) = \sin(\pi x)$. On a
physical interval of length $\ell > 0$ the same mode is $e^{-\kappa\pi^2 t/\ell^2}\sin(\pi x/\ell)$. This is one exact solution, not a
well-posedness result for arbitrary initial data.
