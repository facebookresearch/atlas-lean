# Time derivative of the heat kernel

Let $E$ be a real inner product space, let $n = \dim E$ when $E$ is finite-dimensional and $n = 0$ otherwise, and let $\Phi$ be the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) on $E$. For $t > 0$ and $x \in E$,

$$\partial_t \Phi(t, x) = \Phi(t, x) \left( \frac{\|x\|^2}{4 t^2} - \frac{n}{2 t} \right).$$
