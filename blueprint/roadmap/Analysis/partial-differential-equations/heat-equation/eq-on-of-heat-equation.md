# Uniqueness for the heat equation on a bounded domain

Let $U$ be a bounded open subset of a finite-dimensional real inner product space and $T \ge 0$.
Two functions that are continuous on $[0, T] \times \overline U$, of class $C^2$ in space and
left-differentiable in time on $(0, T] \times U$, solve $u_t - \Delta u = f$ there and agree on
the parabolic boundary agree on all of $[0, T] \times \overline U$.
