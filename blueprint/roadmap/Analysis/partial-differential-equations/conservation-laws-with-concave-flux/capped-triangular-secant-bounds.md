# Secant slopes of a capped triangular flux lie between -w and v

Let `v > 0`, `w > 0`, `kj > 0` and `Qmax ≥ 0`, and let `q(k) = min(v k, w (kj - k), Qmax)` be the
capped triangular flux. For distinct densities `kL ≠ kR` in `[0, kj]`, the secant slope satisfies
`-w ≤ (q(kR) - q(kL)) / (kR - kL) ≤ v`: the rising branch has slope `v`, the falling branch slope
`-w` and the plateau slope `0`, and taking pointwise minima produces no steeper secant, including
secants that cross a corner or the plateau.
