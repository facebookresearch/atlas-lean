# Waves for a triangular flux

This node records an open direction: shocks and rarefactions for a scalar conservation law with a concave, piecewise-smooth flux such as
a triangular or capped traffic fundamental diagram, which needs a one-sided or entropy treatment separate from the strictly concave
smooth case. Finite counts of fronts are a separate accounting, and a cumulative-count (Newell) formulation is a further extension.
