# Burgers field from heat evolution at time zero

For all $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $x$, the Burgers field obtained from $f$ by heat evolution with heat time $\nu t$ equals $-2\nu\, f'(x)/f(x)$ at $t = 0$, with real division ($0$ where $f(x) = 0$).

Here $f'(x)$ denotes the derivative of $f$ at $x$ when $f$ is differentiable there, and $0$ otherwise (for instance $f(x) = 1 + |x|$ at $x = 0$); real division is total, with $a/0 = 0$.
