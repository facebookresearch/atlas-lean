# Potential-heat spatial bootstrap

The actual Volterra solution has finite-order spatial regularity under bounded continuous spatial potential derivatives. Its actual derivatives are jointly continuous at positive times and have uniform exponential bounds on every closed strip separated from initial time. Differentiating the restarted mild equation gives the corresponding heat/Duhamel identity. Continuous exponentially growing initial data suffice; potential time derivatives are not assumed.
