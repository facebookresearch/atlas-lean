# Normalized inverse Cole–Hopf potential

For $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, $u : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ and a base point $(t_0, x_0)$, the normalized inverse potential is $\psi(t, x) = \int_{t_0}^{t} r(s)\,ds - \frac{1}{2\nu}\int_{x_0}^{x} u(t, y)\,dy$ with $r(s) = \frac{u(s, x_0)^2}{4\nu} - \frac{\partial_x u(s, x_0)}{2}$ (oriented integrals, real division).
