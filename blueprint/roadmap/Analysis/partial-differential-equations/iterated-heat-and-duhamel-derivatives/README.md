# Iterated heat and Duhamel derivatives

Finite-order spatial derivatives commute with Gaussian heat evolution and Duhamel integration under exponential growth bounds. Positive viscosity gains one further spatial derivative from the last continuous forcing derivative. Two further spatial forcing derivatives justify the time derivative of each differentiated Duhamel field at interior times, without assuming a forcing time derivative.
