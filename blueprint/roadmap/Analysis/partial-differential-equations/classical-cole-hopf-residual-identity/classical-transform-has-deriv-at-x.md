# Spatial derivative of the Cole–Hopf transform

Let $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be $C^3$. Let $u(t,x) = -2\nu\, \partial_x \varphi(t,x) / \varphi(t,x)$ be the classical Cole–Hopf transform. At every $(t, x)$ with $\varphi(t,x) \ne 0$, the function $u(t, \cdot)$ is differentiable at $x$ with

$$\partial_x u(t,x) = -2\nu\, \frac{\partial_x^2 \varphi(t,x)\, \varphi(t,x) - (\partial_x \varphi(t,x))^2}{\varphi(t,x)^2}.$$
