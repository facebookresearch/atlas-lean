# Second spatial derivative of the Cole–Hopf transform

Let $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $\varphi : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ be $C^3$. Let $u(t,x) = -2\nu\, \partial_x \varphi(t,x) / \varphi(t,x)$ be the classical Cole–Hopf transform. At every $(t, x)$ with $\varphi(t,x) \ne 0$,

$$\partial_x^2 u(t,x) = -2\nu \Big(\frac{\partial_x^3 \varphi}{\varphi} - \frac{3\, \partial_x \varphi\, \partial_x^2 \varphi}{\varphi^2} + \frac{2 (\partial_x \varphi)^3}{\varphi^3}\Big)(t, x).$$
