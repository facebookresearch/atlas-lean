# Differentiation under a spatial integral on an open product

Let $I, J \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be open with $J$ an interval, and let $u : \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be $C^2$ on $I \times J$. For $a, b \in J$ and $t \in I$, the function $s \mapsto \int_a^b u(s, y)\, dy$ is differentiable at $t$ with derivative $\int_a^b \partial_t u(t, y)\, dy$ (oriented integrals).
