# Uniqueness for the Dirichlet problem for $\gamma u + H(Du) = f(x)$

On a bounded open subset $\Omega$ of a finite-dimensional real inner product space, two viscosity
solutions of $\gamma u + H(Du) = f(x)$ ($\gamma > 0$, $f$ continuous on $\overline\Omega$) that are
continuous on $\overline\Omega$ and agree on $\partial\Omega$ agree on $\Omega$. Apply the
[comparison principle](comparison-principle-model-case.md) in both directions.

## Sources

- M. G. Crandall, H. Ishii and P.-L. Lions, "User's guide to viscosity solutions of second order partial differential equations", Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 27 (1992), 1–67, arXiv:math/9207212, Theorem 3.3, first-order model case of Example 3.6.
