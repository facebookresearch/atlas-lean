# Stability of viscosity supersolutions under locally uniform limits

Let $F$ be jointly continuous and $\Omega$ open in a finite-dimensional real inner product space.
If $u_n$ are continuous viscosity supersolutions of $F(x, u, Du) = 0$ on $\Omega$ and $u_n \to U$ locally
uniformly on $\Omega$, then $U$ is a viscosity supersolution on $\Omega$.

The proof applies the [subsolution case](subsolution-of-tendsto-locally-uniformly-on.md) to
$-u_n$ and the equation $-F(x, -r, -p) = 0$.

## Sources
