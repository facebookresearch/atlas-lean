# Classical supersolutions are viscosity supersolutions

Let $\Omega$ be open. If $u$ is differentiable at every point of $\Omega$ and
$F(x, u(x), Du(x)) \ge 0$ for all $x \in \Omega$, then $u$ is a viscosity supersolution of $F = 0$
on $\Omega$. The argument is that of the [subsolution case](is-viscosity-subsolution-of-classical.md),
at a local minimum of $u - \varphi$.
