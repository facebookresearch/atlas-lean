# A $C^1$ viscosity supersolution is a classical supersolution

If $u$ is $C^1$ and a viscosity supersolution of $F = 0$ on $\Omega$, then
$F(x, u(x), Du(x)) \ge 0$ at every $x \in \Omega$, by testing with $\varphi = u$.

## Sources

- J. Calder, *Lecture notes on viscosity solutions*, University of Minnesota, version of 30 July 2024, Remark 2.7.
