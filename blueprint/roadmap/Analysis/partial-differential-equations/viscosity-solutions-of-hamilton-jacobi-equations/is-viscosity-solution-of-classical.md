# Classical solutions are viscosity solutions

Let $\Omega$ be open. A function differentiable at every point of $\Omega$ with
$F(x, u(x), Du(x)) = 0$ there is a viscosity solution of $F = 0$ on $\Omega$: combine the
[subsolution](is-viscosity-subsolution-of-classical.md) and
[supersolution](is-viscosity-supersolution-of-classical.md) cases. Pointwise differentiability
suffices; no continuity of $Du$ is assumed.
