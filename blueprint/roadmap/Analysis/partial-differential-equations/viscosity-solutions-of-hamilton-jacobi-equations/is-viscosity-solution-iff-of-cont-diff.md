# Consistency of viscosity and classical solutions

On an open set $\Omega$, a $C^1$ function is a viscosity solution of $F(x, u, Du) = 0$ if and only if
$F(x, u(x), Du(x)) = 0$ for every $x \in \Omega$. One direction is
[classical solutions are viscosity solutions](is-viscosity-solution-of-classical.md); the other tests
with $\varphi = u$ ([subsolution](subsolution-le-of-cont-diff.md),
[supersolution](supersolution-le-of-cont-diff.md)). So the viscosity notion extends, and does not
change, the classical one.
