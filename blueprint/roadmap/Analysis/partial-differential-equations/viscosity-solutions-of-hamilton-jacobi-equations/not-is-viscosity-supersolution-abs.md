# $|x|$ is not a viscosity supersolution of $(u')^2 - 1 = 0$

The constant test function $0$ touches $|x|$ from below at $0$, and there $0^2 - 1 < 0$. So $|x|$ is
not a viscosity supersolution, hence not a viscosity solution, of $(u')^2 - 1 = 0$ on $\mathbb{R}$,
although it solves the equation classically on $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$. Compare
[$-|x|$](is-viscosity-solution-neg-abs.md), which is a viscosity solution.

## Sources

- M. G. Crandall, H. Ishii and P.-L. Lions, "User's guide to viscosity solutions of second order partial differential equations", Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 27 (1992), 1–67, arXiv:math/9207212, Remark 2.5.
