# A $C^1$ viscosity subsolution is a classical subsolution

If $u$ is $C^1$ and a viscosity subsolution of $F = 0$ on $\Omega$, then
$F(x, u(x), Du(x)) \le 0$ at every $x \in \Omega$: take $\varphi = u$ itself as test function,
since $u - u = 0$ has a local maximum everywhere. No openness of $\Omega$ is needed.

## Sources

- J. Calder, *Lecture notes on viscosity solutions*, University of Minnesota, version of 30 July 2024, Remark 2.7.
