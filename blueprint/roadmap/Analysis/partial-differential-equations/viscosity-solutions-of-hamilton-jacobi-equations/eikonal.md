# The one-dimensional eikonal Hamiltonian

The Hamiltonian $F(x, r, p) = p(1)^2 - 1$ on $\mathbb{R}$, that is the equation $(u')^2 - 1 = 0$. Its
classical solutions away from a point include both $|x|$ and $-|x|$; the viscosity notion selects
one of them (see [the eikonal example](is-viscosity-solution-neg-abs.md)).
