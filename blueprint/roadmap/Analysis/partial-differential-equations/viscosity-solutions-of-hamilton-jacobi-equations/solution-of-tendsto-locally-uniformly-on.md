# Stability of viscosity solutions under locally uniform limits

Let $F$ be jointly continuous and $\Omega$ open in a finite-dimensional real inner product space.
If $u_n$ are continuous viscosity solutions of $F(x, u, Du) = 0$ on $\Omega$ and $u_n \to U$ locally
uniformly on $\Omega$, then $U$ is a viscosity solution on $\Omega$.

Together with the [comparison principle](comparison-principle-model-case.md), this is the basis of
convergence proofs for approximations (vanishing viscosity, numerical schemes): limits are
solutions, and comparison identifies them (Remark 6.4).
