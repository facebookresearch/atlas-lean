# Mooney–Savin non-quadratic entire solutions of u₁₁u₂₂ = 1

This settles [Are entire coordinate-convex solutions of u₁₁u₂₂ = 1 quadratic?](entire-solutions-quadratic.md) in the negative: its statement is false.

The growth condition cannot be removed. Mooney and Savin construct non-quadratic
entire solutions of `u₁₁u₂₂ = 1` that are convex along coordinate lines (Theorem 1.7; smooth by
their Corollary 1.5, and by their Corollary 1.2 they grow faster than quadratically, like `|x|² log
|x|` along the diagonals).

## Sources

- AimPL: Nonlinear PDEs in real and complex geometry, Section 1 (Monge-Ampère equations), https://aimpl.org/nonlinpdegeom/1/, Problem 1.45 (Xiangwen Zhang); resolution: C. Mooney and O. Savin, Regularity results for the equation u11u22 = 1, Discrete Contin. Dyn. Syst. 39 (2019), no. 12, arXiv:1806.05532, Theorem 1.7: there exist non-quadratic entire solutions to (1), the equation (u11u22)^\{1/2\} = 1 for coordinate-convex u; Corollary 1.5: viscosity solutions of (1) are smooth; Corollary 1.2: a smooth entire solution with |u| < C(1 + |x|²) is a quadratic polynomial; the solution constructed grows like |x|² log |x| along the diagonals, https://doi.org/10.3934/dcds.2019235.
