# Uniform bounds for every derivative of the sine potential

For every real $\nu,T$ and every integer $n\ge0$, there exists $M\in\mathbb R$ such that

$$\forall(t,x)\in[0,T]\times\mathbb R,\qquad \|D^nq_\nu(t,x)\|\le M,$$

where $q_\nu(t,x)=\sin x+\nu t\sin x-\nu t^2\cos^2x$ and $D^n$ is the actual iterated Fréchet derivative on $\mathbb R^2$ with its product norm. The bound is uniform in time and space and may depend on $\nu,T,n$.

The bound is proved from compactness of the time interval, bounded derivatives of sine and cosine, and the Leibniz estimate for iterated derivatives. It is not an assumed property of the potential. Order zero is included, as are every higher and mixed derivative order. For $T<0$ the strip is empty; no positive viscosity hypothesis is required for this estimate.
