# Joint smoothness of the sine potential

For every $\nu\in\mathbb R$, the function

$$(t,x)\longmapsto q_\nu(t,x)=\sin x+\nu t\sin x-\nu t^2\cos^2x$$

is $C^\infty$ on $\mathbb R^2$. This follows from smoothness of the polynomial time factors and the trigonometric spatial factors. No sign condition on $\nu$ and no restriction of the domain is needed.
