# The Duhamel integrand is integrable

Let $S$ be a strongly continuous semigroup on a real normed space $X$ (completeness is not needed). Here strong continuity includes local boundedness: for every $T \ge 0$ there is $M$ with $\|S(s)\| \le M$ for $0 \le s \le T$. On a Banach space this bound follows from orbitwise continuity by uniform boundedness; without completeness it is part of the hypothesis. Let $g : \mathbb{R} \to X$ be continuous. For all $t, a, b$, the integrand $s \mapsto S(\max(t - s, 0))\, g(s)$ is integrable on the interval between $a$ and $b$.
