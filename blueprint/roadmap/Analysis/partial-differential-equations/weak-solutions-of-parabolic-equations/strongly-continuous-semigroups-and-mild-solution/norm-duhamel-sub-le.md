# Difference estimate for Duhamel terms

Let $S$ be a strongly continuous semigroup of operators on a real Banach space $X$ and let $g, h : \mathbb{R} \to X$ be continuous. Let $t \ge 0$, $M \ge 0$ with $\|S(\tau)\| \le M$ for $\tau \le t$, and $\|g(s) - h(s)\| \le K$ for $s \in [0, t]$. Then the Duhamel terms $\int_0^t S(t - s) g(s)\, ds$ and $\int_0^t S(t - s) h(s)\, ds$ differ in norm by at most $M K t$.
