# Coordinate of the kernel component along φ

Let $E$ and $Y$ be real normed spaces, let $L : E \to Y$ be a bounded linear map, and fix a Fredholm
package of $L$: decompositions $E = X_1 \oplus X_0$ and $Y = Y_1 \oplus Y_0$ into topologically
complementary subspaces with $X_0$ and $Y_0$ finite-dimensional, and an isomorphism
$A : X_1 \to Y_1$ of normed spaces, such that $L x = A(\pi_1 x)$ for every $x$, where $\pi_1$ is the
projection onto $X_1$ along $X_0$. Then $\ker L = X_0$ and $\operatorname{range} L = Y_1$. Let
$\varphi \in E$ be such that every $z \in X_0$ is a real multiple of $\varphi$.

For $x \in E$, the kernel coordinate of $x$ is a real number $a$ with $\pi_0 x = a\varphi$, where
$\pi_0$ is the projection onto $X_0$ along $X_1$. It is unique when $\varphi \neq 0$; for
$\varphi = 0$ every real number qualifies and one is chosen.
