# A field of Neumann modes has zero derivative at x = ℓ

Let $\ell \neq 0$, let $\iota$ be a finite set, and let each mode $i$ have the
[Neumann wavenumber](../../../Biology/pattern-formation/turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/neumann-wavenumber.md)
$\kappa_i = m_i \pi/\ell$ for some $m_i \in \mathbb{N}$. Then for all coefficients
$w_i \in \mathbb{R}^2$ the [mode field](mode-field.md) $u(x) = \sum_i \cos(\kappa_i x)\, w_i$
satisfies $u'(\ell) = 0$: the zero-flux condition at the right end of $[0, \ell]$ (for $\ell > 0$).
