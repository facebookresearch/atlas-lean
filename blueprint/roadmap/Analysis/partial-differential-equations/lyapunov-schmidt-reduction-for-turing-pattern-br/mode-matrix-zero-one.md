# The off-diagonal entry m₀₁ of every mode matrix is f_v

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the
Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady state), and let $d_u$, $d$ and $k$ be real. The
entry in row $0$, column $1$ of the
[mode matrix](../../../Biology/pattern-formation/turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/mode-matrix.md)
$J - k^2 \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d)$ is $f_v$, since the diffusion matrix is diagonal.
