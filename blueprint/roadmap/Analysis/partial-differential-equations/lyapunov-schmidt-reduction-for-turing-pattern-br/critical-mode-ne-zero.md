# For f_v ≠ 0, the critical mode is nonzero

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the
Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady state) with $f_v \neq 0$, let $\iota$ be a set of
modes with real wavenumbers $\kappa_i$, let $d_u, d$ be real and $i_0 \in \iota$. Then the
[critical mode](critical-mode.md), the coefficient field equal to $(f_v,\ \kappa_{i_0}^2 d_u - f_u)$
in mode $i_0$ and to $0$ elsewhere, is nonzero.
