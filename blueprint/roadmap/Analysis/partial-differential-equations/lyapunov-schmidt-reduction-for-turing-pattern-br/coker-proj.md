# Projection onto the cokernel part of a Fredholm package

Let $E$ and $Y$ be real normed spaces, let $L : E \to Y$ be a bounded linear map, and fix a Fredholm
package of $L$: decompositions $E = X_1 \oplus X_0$ and $Y = Y_1 \oplus Y_0$ into topologically
complementary subspaces with $X_0$ and $Y_0$ finite-dimensional, and an isomorphism
$A : X_1 \to Y_1$ of normed spaces, such that $L x = A(\pi_1 x)$ for every $x$, where $\pi_1$ is the
projection onto $X_1$ along $X_0$. Then $\ker L = X_0$ and $\operatorname{range} L = Y_1$.

The cokernel projection is the bounded linear projection $1 - Q : Y \to Y_0$ onto $Y_0$ along
$Y_1 = \operatorname{range} L$, where $Q$ is the projection onto $Y_1$ along $Y_0$.
