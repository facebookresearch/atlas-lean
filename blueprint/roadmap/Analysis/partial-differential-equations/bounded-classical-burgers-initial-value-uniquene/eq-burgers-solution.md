# Identification of stripwise bounded Burgers solutions

Let $\nu>0$. Suppose $u$ is jointly continuous for nonnegative times, jointly $C^2$ for positive times, and satisfies $u_t+uu_x=\nu u_{xx}$ at every positive time and spatial point. Assume that for every $T\ge0$ there is a finite constant $M_T$ with $|u(t,x)|\le M_T$ for $0\le t\le T$ and every $x$. Then, for every spatial anchor, $u$ equals the Cole–Hopf construction from $u(0,\cdot)$ at every nonnegative time. No bound uniform in all time is required.
