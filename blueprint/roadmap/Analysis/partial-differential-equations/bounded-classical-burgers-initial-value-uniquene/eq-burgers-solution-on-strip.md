# Identification of bounded classical Burgers solutions on a strip

Let $\nu>0$ and $T\in\mathbb R$. Suppose $u$ is jointly continuous on $[0,\infty)\times\mathbb R$, jointly $C^2$ on $(0,\infty)\times\mathbb R$, satisfies $u_t+uu_x=\nu u_{xx}$ there, and is uniformly bounded on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$. For every anchor $a$ and $t\in[0,T]$, $u(t,x)$ equals the Cole–Hopf construction from initial data $u(0,\cdot)$, for every $x$. The conclusion is empty when $T<0$. No initial spatial derivative or derivative bound near time zero is assumed.
