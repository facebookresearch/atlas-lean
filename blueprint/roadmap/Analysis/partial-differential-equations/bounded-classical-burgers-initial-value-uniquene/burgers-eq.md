# Uniqueness for stripwise bounded classical Burgers solutions

Let $\nu>0$. Suppose $u,v$ are jointly continuous on the nonnegative time half-plane, jointly $C^2$ on the positive time half-plane, solve $w_t+ww_x=\nu w_{xx}$ at positive times, and are each uniformly bounded on every finite closed time strip. If their initial values agree everywhere, then they agree for every nonnegative time and every spatial point. Bounds may depend on the strip and on the solution; values at negative times are unconstrained.
