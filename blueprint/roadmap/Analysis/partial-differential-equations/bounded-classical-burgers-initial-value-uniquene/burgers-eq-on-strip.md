# Uniqueness of bounded classical Burgers solutions on a strip

Let $\nu>0$ and $T\in\mathbb R$. Suppose $u,v$ are jointly continuous on the nonnegative time half-plane and jointly $C^2$ on the positive time half-plane, both solve $w_t+ww_x=\nu w_{xx}$ for all positive times, and each is uniformly bounded on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$. If $u(0,x)=v(0,x)$ for every $x$, then $u(t,x)=v(t,x)$ for all $t\in[0,T]$ and $x\in\mathbb R$. The bounds may differ; regularity and the PDE are assumed on the whole positive half-line.
