# Second spatial derivative of the heat kernel

Let $\Phi(t, x) = (4\pi t)^{-1/2} e^{-x^2/(4t)}$ be the one-dimensional heat kernel. For all real $t$ and $x$, this function is $\Big(\frac{x^2}{4t^2} - \frac{1}{2t}\Big)\Phi(t, x)$, with real division ($a/0 = 0$). It is the second spatial derivative of $\Phi$, and for $t > 0$ it is also the time derivative $\partial_t \Phi(t, x)$.
