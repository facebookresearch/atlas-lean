# The spatial derivative loses one order of smoothness

Let $I, J \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be open and let $\varphi : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ be $C^n$ on $I \times J$, where $n \ne 0$ (possibly $n = \infty$ or analytic). For every $m$ with $m + 1 \le n$, the spatial derivative $(t, x) \mapsto \partial_x \varphi(t, x)$ is $C^m$ on $I \times J$.
