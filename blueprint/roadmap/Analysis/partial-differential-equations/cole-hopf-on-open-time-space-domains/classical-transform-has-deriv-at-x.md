# Spatial derivative of the Cole–Hopf transform on an open domain

Let $I, J \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be open, $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be $C^3$ on $I \times J$. Let $u(t,x) = -2\nu\, \partial_x \varphi(t,x) / \varphi(t,x)$ be the classical Cole–Hopf transform. For $t \in I$ and $x \in J$ with $\varphi(t,x) \ne 0$, the function $x \mapsto u(t,x)$ is differentiable at $x$ with

$$\partial_x u(t,x) = -2\nu\, \frac{\partial_x^2 \varphi(t,x)\, \varphi(t,x) - (\partial_x \varphi(t,x))^2}{\varphi(t,x)^2}.$$
