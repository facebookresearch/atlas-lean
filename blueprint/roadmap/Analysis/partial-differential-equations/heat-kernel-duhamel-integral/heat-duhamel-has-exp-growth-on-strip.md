# Heat Duhamel has Exp Growth On Strip

Let $T,\nu\ge0$. If $f$ is jointly continuous on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$ and has uniform exponential spatial growth there, then the [Duhamel integral](heat-duhamel.md) also has uniform exponential spatial growth on the same strip: there are constants $A,c\ge0$ such that $|D_\nu f(t,x)|\le A e^{c|x|}$ for every $t\in[0,T]$ and $x\in\mathbb R$.
