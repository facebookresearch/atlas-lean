# Abs heat Duhamel le

Suppose $f$ is jointly continuous on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$ and $|f(s,y)|\le M e^{c|y|}$ there, with $M,c\ge0$. Let $\nu\ge0$ and $t\in[0,T]$. Then, for every $x$,

$$|D_\nu f(t,x)|\le tM\left(\int_{\mathbb R}K_1(y)e^{c\sqrt{\nu T}\,|y|}\,dy\right)e^{c|x|}.$$

Here $D_\nu$ is the [Duhamel integral](heat-duhamel.md). The displayed Gaussian moment is finite and nonnegative.
