# Heat Duhamel mono

Suppose $f,g$ are jointly continuous on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$, each has a uniform exponential spatial bound, and $f(s,y)\le g(s,y)$ throughout the strip. Then for every real $\nu,x$ and $t\in[0,T]$,

$$D_\nu f(t,x)\le D_\nu g(t,x).$$

For arbitrary viscosity this uses the [stationary extension](heat-duhamel.md); it asserts no backward heat equation.
