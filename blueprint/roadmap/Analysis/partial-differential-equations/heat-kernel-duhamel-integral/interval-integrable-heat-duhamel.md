# Interval Integrable heat Duhamel

Suppose $f$ is jointly continuous on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$ and there are constants $M,c\ge0$ such that $|f(s,y)|\le M e^{c|y|}$ throughout this strip. For every $t\in[0,T]$ and real $\nu,x$, the time integrand

$$s\mapsto P_{\nu(t-s)}(f(s,\cdot))(x)$$

is integrable on $[0,t]$. The heat operator uses the [stationary extension](heat-duhamel.md) at nonpositive heat times. No continuity outside the strip is assumed.
