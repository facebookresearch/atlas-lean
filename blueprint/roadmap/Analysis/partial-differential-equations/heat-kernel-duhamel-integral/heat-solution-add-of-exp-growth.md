# Heat Solution add of exp growth

Let $f,g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous, and suppose each has exponential
growth: $|f(x)|\le M_f e^{c_f|x|}$ and $|g(x)|\le M_g e^{c_g|x|}$ for nonnegative
constants and every $x$. For all real $t,x$,
\[
P_t(f+g)(x)=P_tf(x)+P_tg(x).
\]
Here $P_t$ is Gaussian heat evolution for $t>0$ and its stationary extension
$P_th=h$ for $t\le0$. Exponential growth proves the integrability needed for
addition; no separate integrability hypothesis or boundedness is required.
