# Heat Duhamel nonneg

Let $t\ge0$ and suppose $f(s,y)\ge0$ for every $s\in[0,t]$ and $y\in\mathbb R$. Then the [Duhamel integral](heat-duhamel.md) satisfies $D_\nu f(t,x)\ge0$ for every real $\nu,x$. The statement is valid for the stationary extension at nonpositive heat times; it does not assert a backward heat equation or integrability for arbitrary $f$.
