# Heat-kernel Duhamel integral

The Gaussian Duhamel integral describes the mild contribution of a forcing term with zero initial data. Uniform exponential spatial growth permits integrability, joint initial continuity, quantitative estimates and comparison on finite time strips. Classical differentiated formulas require additional regularity.

Reference: Stanford Math 220B, [Heat Equation, §2.7.2](https://web.stanford.edu/class/math220b/handouts/heateqn.pdf). The results here use explicit Gaussian domination to extend the compact-support setting.
