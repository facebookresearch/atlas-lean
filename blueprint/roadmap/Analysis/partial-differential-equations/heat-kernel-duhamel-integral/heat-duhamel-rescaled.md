# Heat Duhamel rescaled

For any real $\nu,t,x$ and any forcing $f$, the [Duhamel integral](heat-duhamel.md) has the fixed-domain representation

$$D_\nu f(t,x)=t\int_0^1\int_{\mathbb R}K_1(y)f(tr,x-\sqrt{\nu(t-tr)}\,y)\,dy\,dr.$$

Here $K_1$ is the time-one heat kernel and the real square root is zero on nonpositive arguments. The identity includes $t=0$ and does not assert differentiability there.
