# Heat Duhamel continuous On

Let $T\ge0$. Suppose $f$ is jointly continuous on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$ and $|f(s,y)|\le M e^{c|y|}$ there for some constants $M,c\ge0$. For every real viscosity $\nu$, the [Duhamel integral](heat-duhamel.md) is jointly continuous on the closed strip, including its zero initial trace. No continuity outside the strip or classical PDE differentiability is assumed or concluded.
