# Abs heat Solution le exp bound

Let $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and satisfy $|g(y)|\le M e^{c|y|}$ with $M,c\ge0$. For real heat times $s\le H$ and every $x$,

$$|P_sg(x)|\le M\left(\int_{\mathbb R}K_1(y)e^{c\sqrt H\,|y|}\,dy\right)e^{c|x|}.$$

The Gaussian moment is finite. The [stationary extension](heat-duhamel.md) and real square root convention also cover nonpositive $s$ and $H$.
