# Heat Duhamel

For real viscosity $\nu$ and forcing $f:\mathbb R\times\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, define

$$D_\nu f(t,x)=\int_0^t P_{\nu(t-s)}(f(s,\cdot))(x)\,ds.$$

Here $P_h$ is Gaussian heat convolution for $h>0$ and the stationary extension $P_hg=g$ for $h\le0$. The time integral is oriented. Integrability and classical differentiability are separate assertions; the definition alone supplies neither.
