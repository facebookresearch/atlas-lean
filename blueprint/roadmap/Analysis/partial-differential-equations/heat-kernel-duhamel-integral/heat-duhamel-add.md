# Heat Duhamel add

Suppose $f,g$ are jointly continuous on $[0,T]\times\mathbb R$, each with a uniform exponential spatial bound (the constants and growth rates may differ). Then for every real $\nu,x$ and $t\in[0,T]$,

$$D_\nu(f+g)(t,x)=D_\nu f(t,x)+D_\nu g(t,x).$$

The [Duhamel integrals](heat-duhamel.md) and heat convolutions are integrable under these assumptions; no separate integrability hypotheses are needed.
