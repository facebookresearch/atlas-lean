# The heat solution at nonpositive times

Let $\Phi(t, x) = (4\pi t)^{-1/2} e^{-x^2/(4t)}$ be the heat kernel on $\mathbb{R}$, and for $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ let $u(t, x) = \int \Phi(t, x - y) f(y)\, dy$ for $t > 0$ (and $u(t, x) = f(x)$ for $t \le 0$). For every $f$, $t \le 0$ and $x$, $u(t, x) = f(x)$.
