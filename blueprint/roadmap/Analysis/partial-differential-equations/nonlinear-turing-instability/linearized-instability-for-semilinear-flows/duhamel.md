# Discrete Duhamel formula

Let $X$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $L$ a continuous linear endomorphism of $X$, and $(y_n)_{n \ge 0}$ any sequence in $X$. Then for every $n$, $y_n - L^n y_0 = \sum_{j < n} L^j\big(y_{n-j} - L\, y_{n-1-j}\big)$. Linearity of $L$ matters: for the nonlinear map $x \mapsto x^2$ the identity fails.
