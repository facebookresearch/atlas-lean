# Pointwise action of a 2 × 2 matrix on fields with values in ℝ²

For a compact topological space $K$ and a real $2 \times 2$ matrix $J$, the pointwise action of $J$
is the bounded linear operator on $C(K, \mathbb{R}^2)$ that sends a field $y$ to
$x \mapsto J\, y(x)$. At a homogeneous steady state $a$ with kinetics Jacobian $J$ it is the linear
part of the reaction term $w \mapsto f \circ w$ at the constant field $a$
([remainder bound](norm-nemytskii-sub-le.md)).
