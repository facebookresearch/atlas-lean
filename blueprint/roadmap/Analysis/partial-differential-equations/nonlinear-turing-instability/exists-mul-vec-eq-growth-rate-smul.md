# If δ < 0, the growth rate is an eigenvalue with a real eigenvector

Let $J$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state), let
$d_u, d_v$ and $k$ be real, let $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$, and let $\tau$ and $\delta$ be
the trace and the determinant of the
[mode matrix](../../../Biology/pattern-formation/turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/mode-matrix.md)
$J - k^2 D$. If $\delta < 0$, then the [growth rate](growth-rate.md)
$\lambda = (\tau + \sqrt{\tau^2 - 4\delta})/2$ of the mode is a real eigenvalue of $J - k^2 D$ with
a real eigenvector: there is a nonzero $v \in \mathbb{R}^2$ with $(J - k^2 D)\, v = \lambda v$.

In the model $d_u, d_v > 0$; the statement holds for all real values.
