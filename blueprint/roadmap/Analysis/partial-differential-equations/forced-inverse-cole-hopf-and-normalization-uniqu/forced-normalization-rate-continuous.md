# Continuity of the forced normalization rate

Let $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, $u : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ $C^2$, $F : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ continuous, and $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$. The forced normalization rate $r(t) = \frac{u(t, x_0)^2}{4\nu} - \frac{\partial_x u(t, x_0)}{2} + F(t, x_0)$ is continuous.
