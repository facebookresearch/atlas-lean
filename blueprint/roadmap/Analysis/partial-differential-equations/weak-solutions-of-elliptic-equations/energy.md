# Dirichlet energy of a bilinear form and a load

For a bounded bilinear form $B$ and a bounded linear functional $L$ on $H$, the quadratic functional

$$I[w] = \tfrac12 B[w, w] - L(w).$$

For $B[u, v] = \int_U Du \cdot Dv$ and $L(v) = \int_U f v$ it is
$I[w] = \int_U \tfrac12 |Dw|^2 - f w$, whose Euler–Lagrange equation is Poisson's equation
$-\Delta u = f$.
