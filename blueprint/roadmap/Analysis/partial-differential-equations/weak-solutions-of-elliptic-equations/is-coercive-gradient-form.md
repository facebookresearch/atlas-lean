# Poincaré-type bound gives coercivity

If $\beta > 0$ and $\beta \|u\|^2 \le \|D u\|^2$ for all $u \in H$, then the Dirichlet form
$\langle D u, D v \rangle$ is coercive. On $H^1_0(U)$ with the $H^1$ norm, Poincaré's inequality
$\|u\|_{L^2} \le C \|Du\|_{L^2}$ gives such a bound with $\beta = \min(1/(2C^2), 1/2)$.
