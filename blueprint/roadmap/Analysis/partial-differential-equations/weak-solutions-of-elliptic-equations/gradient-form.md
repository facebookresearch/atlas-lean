# Dirichlet form of a gradient operator

For a bounded linear map $D : H \to G$ between real inner product spaces, the bounded bilinear form

$$B[u, v] = \langle D u, D v \rangle_G .$$

With $H = H^1_0(U)$ and $D$ the gradient into $L^2(U; \mathbb{R}^n)$ this is
$B[u, v] = \int_U Du \cdot Dv$, the form of the weak formulation of $-\Delta u = f$.
