# Coefficients of the Laplacian

The principal coefficients $a^{ij} = \delta^{ij}$. With $b = 0$ and $c = 0$ the divergence-form
operator is $L u = -\Delta u$ and $B[u, v] = \int_U Du \cdot Dv$.
