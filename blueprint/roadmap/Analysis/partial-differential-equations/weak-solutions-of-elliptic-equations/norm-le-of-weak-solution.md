# Energy estimate for the abstract Poisson problem

Under the hypotheses of the existence theorem, the weak solution satisfies

$$\|u\| \le \frac{\|L\|}{\beta},$$

from $\beta \|u\|^2 \le \langle D u, D u \rangle = L(u) \le \|L\| \|u\|$.

- [Stability bound for the variational solution](../../numerical-analysis/c-a-s-lemma/norm-variational-solution-le.md): the same
