# Weak partial derivatives on the whole space

On $U = \mathbb{R}^n$, a function $g$ is the weak partial derivative $\partial_i u$ on $U$ if and
only if it is the whole-space weak derivative $D^m u$ for the multi-index $m = e_i$, in the sense of
the [weak derivative](../../function-spaces/sobolev-embedding-theorem/is-weak-deriv.md) used by the Sobolev embedding theorem. So the domain
notion extends the whole-space one, and the two encodings agree where both apply.

## Background

- [Weak partial derivative on an open set](is-weak-partial-deriv-on.md) and
  [weak derivative](../../function-spaces/sobolev-embedding-theorem/is-weak-deriv.md): the two definitions compared; the mixed derivative for
  $m = e_i$ is $\partial_i$ and $(-1)^{|m|} = -1$.
