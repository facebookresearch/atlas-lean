# The hypotheses of interior $H^2$ regularity are satisfiable

For the coefficients of the Laplacian ($a^{ij} = \delta^{ij}$, $b = 0$, $c = 0$), the balls
$U = B(0, 2)$ and $V = B(0, 1)$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ and $\theta = 1$, every hypothesis of interior
$H^2$ regularity holds: $U$ is open and bounded; the coefficients are symmetric, $C^1$ and bounded
on $U$; $b, c \in L^\infty(U)$; $\theta > 0$ and uniform ellipticity holds; $V$ is open and
$\overline V$ is compact and contained in $U$. So the [interior $H^2$ regularity theorem](interior-h2-regularity.md) is not vacuous.
