# Integration by parts of a primitive against a function of H¹₀(0, 1)

Let `f` be square integrable on `[0,1]`, with primitive `F(x) = ∫₀ˣ f(s) ds`, and let `v` be an
[element of `H¹₀(0,1)`](weak-h10.md), with weak derivative `v'`. Then
`∫₀¹ F(x) v'(x) dx = -∫₀¹ f(x) v(x) dx`. Both `F` and `v` are absolutely continuous on `[0,1]`,
with derivatives `f` and `v'` almost everywhere, and the boundary terms of the integration by
parts vanish because `v(0) = v(1) = 0`.
