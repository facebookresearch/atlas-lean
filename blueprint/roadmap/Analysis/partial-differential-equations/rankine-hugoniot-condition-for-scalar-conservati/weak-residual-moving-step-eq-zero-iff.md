# Weak solutions of a moving step satisfy exactly the jump condition

For a flux `f` and real numbers `u_L`, `u_R` and `c`, the [weak residual](weak-residual.md) of the
[moving step](moving-step.md) is `0` against every admissible test (a smooth `φ` vanishing outside
some bounded rectangle, with its time and space partial derivatives) if and only if
`c (u_R - u_L) = f(u_R) - f(u_L)`.
