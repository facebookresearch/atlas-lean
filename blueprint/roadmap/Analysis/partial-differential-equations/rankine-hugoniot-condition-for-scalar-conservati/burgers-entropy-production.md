# Entropy production of a Burgers jump

For real numbers `c` (speed), `u_L` and `u_R` (left and right states), the entropy production of the
jump is `[q] - c [η] = (q(u_R) - q(u_L)) - c (η(u_R) - η(u_L))`, with the
[entropy](burgers-entropy.md) `η` and the [entropy flux](burgers-entropy-flux.md) `q`. A jump is
called entropy-admissible when this quantity is nonpositive; at the Rankine–Hugoniot speed a
[decreasing jump is admissible](burgers-decreasing-jump-admissible.md).
