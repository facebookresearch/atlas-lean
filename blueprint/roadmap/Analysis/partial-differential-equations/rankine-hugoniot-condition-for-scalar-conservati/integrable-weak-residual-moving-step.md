# The weak residual of a moving step is a genuine integral

For a flux `f`, real numbers `u_L`, `u_R` and `c`, and a smooth test function `φ` with time and
space partial derivatives `φ_t` and `φ_x` that vanishes outside a bounded rectangle
`[T₀, T₁] × [X₀, X₁]`, the integrand `u φ_t + f(u) φ_x` of the [weak residual](weak-residual.md) of
the [moving step](moving-step.md) `u` is integrable on the plane, and `t ↦ φ(t, c t)` is integrable
on `ℝ`.
