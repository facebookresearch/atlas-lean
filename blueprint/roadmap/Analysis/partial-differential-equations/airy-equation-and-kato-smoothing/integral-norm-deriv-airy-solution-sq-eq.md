# Sharp Kato smoothing identity for Schwartz data

For a Schwartz initial datum $u_0$,
the spatial derivative of the Airy solution belongs to $L^2$ in time at every spatial point.
Its squared $L^2$ norm is one third of the squared $L^2$ norm of $u_0$. The factor $1/3$ follows
from Plancherel's identity in time after the substitution $\tau = \xi^3$.

## Sources

- J. E. Colliander and C. E. Kenig, *The generalized Korteweg-de Vries equation on the half line*, Lemma 4.1, proof of (4.6), p. 16, with $s = -1$ and $\phi = \partial_x u_0$ ([arXiv:math/0111294v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0111294v3#page=16)).
