# Sharp Kato smoothing identity for Schwartz data

At each spatial point, the spatial
derivative of the Airy solution belongs to $L^2$ in time. Its squared $L^2$ norm is one third
of the squared $L^2$ norm of the initial datum.

## Sources

- Plancherel's identity (`MeasureTheory.Lp.inner_fourier_eq`) applied after the cubic change of time frequency.
