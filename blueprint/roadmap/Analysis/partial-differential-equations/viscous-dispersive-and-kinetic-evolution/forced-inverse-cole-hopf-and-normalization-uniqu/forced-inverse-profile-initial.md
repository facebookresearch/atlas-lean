# Forced inverse Cole–Hopf profile at the initial time

For every potential $F$, at the initial time, $\varphi(t_0, x) = \exp\big(-\frac{1}{2\nu}\int_{x_0}^{x} u(t_0, y)\,dy\big)$ for every $x$.

Here $u$, $\nu$, $t_0$ and $x_0$ are arbitrary: division is total, with $a/0 = 0$ (so for $\nu = 0$ the profile is $1$), and the integral is a Bochner integral, taken as $0$ when $y \mapsto u(t_0, y)$ is not integrable on the interval.
