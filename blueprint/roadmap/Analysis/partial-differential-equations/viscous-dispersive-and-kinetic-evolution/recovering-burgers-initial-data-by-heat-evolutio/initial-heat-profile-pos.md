# The initial heat profile is positive

For all $\nu$, $u_0 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $x_0$ and $x$, the initial heat profile $\exp\big(-\frac{1}{2\nu}\int_{x_0}^{x} u_0(y)\,dy\big)$ is strictly positive.

Division is total, with $a/0 = 0$; so for $\nu = 0$ the exponent is $0$ and the profile is identically $1$. The integral is an oriented integral, taken as $0$ when $u_0$ is not integrable on the interval between $x_0$ and $x$; for example, for $u_0(y) = 1/y$, $\nu = 1$, $x_0 = 0$ and $x = 1$ the profile is $\exp(0) = 1$. In every case it is the exponential of a real number, hence positive.
