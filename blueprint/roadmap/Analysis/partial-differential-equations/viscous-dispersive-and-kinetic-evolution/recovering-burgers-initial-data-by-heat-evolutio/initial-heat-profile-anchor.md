# The initial heat profile equals 1 at the anchor

For all $\nu$, $u_0 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $x_0$, the initial heat profile $\varphi_0$ anchored at $x_0$ satisfies $\varphi_0(x_0) = 1$: its exponent is $-\frac{1}{2\nu}$ times the oriented integral of $u_0$ from $x_0$ to $x_0$, which is $0$.

The profile is $\varphi_0(x) = \exp\big(-\frac{1}{2\nu}\int_{x_0}^{x} u_0(y)\,dy\big)$ with total division, $a/0 = 0$, and the oriented integral taken as $0$ when $u_0$ is not integrable on the interval; so away from the anchor it need not be the exponential of a finite classical integral (for $u_0(y) = 1/y$, $\nu = 1$, from $0$ to $1$ the profile is $1$).
