# Burgers solution at time zero

Let $u_0 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous, $\nu \ne 0$ and $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$. The explicit Burgers solution built from the heat equation with the anchored initial profile $x \mapsto \exp\big(-\frac{1}{2\nu}\int_{x_0}^{x} u_0(y)\, dy\big)$ satisfies $u(0, x) = u_0(x)$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
