# Second spatial derivative of the exponential-sum Cole–Hopf solution

Let $s$ be a finite index set, $c, k : s \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, let $M_n(t, x) = \sum_{i \in s} c_i k_i^n e^{\nu k_i^2 t + k_i x}$ be the exponential heat moments, and let $u = -2\nu\, M_1 / M_0$ be the Cole–Hopf solution of the exponential-sum heat profile $M_0$ (real division). At every $(t, x)$ with $M_0(t, x) \ne 0$,

$$\partial_x^2 u = -2\nu \Big(\frac{M_3}{M_0} - \frac{3 M_1 M_2}{M_0^2} + \frac{2 M_1^3}{M_0^3}\Big).$$
