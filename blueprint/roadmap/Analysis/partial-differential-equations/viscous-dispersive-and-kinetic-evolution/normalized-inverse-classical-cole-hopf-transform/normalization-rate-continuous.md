# Continuity of the normalization rate

Let $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, $u : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ $C^2$ and $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$. The normalization rate $t \mapsto \frac{u(t, x_0)^2}{4\nu} - \frac{\partial_x u(t, x_0)}{2}$ is continuous.

Real division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, since $\nu$ may be zero; for $\nu = 0$ the first term vanishes.
