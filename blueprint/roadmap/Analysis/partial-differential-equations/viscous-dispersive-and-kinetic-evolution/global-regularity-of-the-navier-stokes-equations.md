# Global regularity of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations

Do smooth divergence-free initial velocities always yield global smooth solutions to the three-dimensional incompressible equations with zero forcing?

## What would settle it

Use exactly one source formulation: Euclidean space with the specified decay and bounded-energy conditions, or periodic data on the torus. Prove global regularity for every eligible datum, or exhibit a genuine unforced singularity. A theorem about smooth nonzero forcing is a different statement.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

This is the unforced problem as stated by Fefferman for the Clay Mathematics Institute (statements A and B). Results about flows driven by a nonzero force are different statements.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
