# Continuity of the forced normalization rate on an open domain

Let $I, J \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be open, $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, $u, F : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ with $u$ $C^2$ on $I \times J$ and $F$ continuous on $I \times J$, and $x_0 \in J$. Then the forced normalization rate $r(t) = \frac{u(t, x_0)^2}{4\nu} - \frac{\partial_x u(t, x_0)}{2} + F(t, x_0)$ is continuous on $I$.

Real division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, since $\nu$ may be zero; for $\nu = 0$ the first term vanishes.
