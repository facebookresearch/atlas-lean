# Potential-heat time evolution and smoothness

The actual spatial derivatives of the Volterra solution satisfy their differentiated potential-heat equations at interior times. Finite spatial potential regularity suffices for these time derivatives. A jointly smooth potential with separately bounded Fréchet derivatives gives joint positive-time smoothness of the field and all its spatial derivatives, while initial data remain continuous with exponential growth.
