# Fick's first law

Fick's first law in one dimension with constant diffusivity `D`: the flux of
the concentration field `c` (position, then time) at `(x, t)` is `J = -D
∂ₓc(x, t)`. The derivative is taken to be `0` where `c (·) t` is not
differentiable at `x`, so results about the flux assume differentiability
separately.
