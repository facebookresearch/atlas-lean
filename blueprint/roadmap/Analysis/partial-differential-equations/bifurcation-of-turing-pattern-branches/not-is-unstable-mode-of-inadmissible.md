# For tr J < 0 < det J, g_v ≤ 0, inadmissible modes are never unstable

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the
Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady state) with $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $\det J > 0$
and $g_v \le 0$, and let $d_u \ge 0$ and $d \ge 0$. If the wavenumber $k$ is not admissible,
$f_u k^2 - d_u k^4 \le 0$, then the Fourier mode of wavenumber $k$ is not
[linearly unstable](../../../Biology/pattern-formation/turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/is-unstable-mode.md)
for the diffusion matrix $\operatorname{diag}(d_u, d)$.
