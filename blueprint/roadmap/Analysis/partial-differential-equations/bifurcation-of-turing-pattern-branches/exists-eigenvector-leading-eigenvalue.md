# With a nonnegative discriminant, the leading real eigenvalue is one

For a real $2 \times 2$ matrix $M$ with $(\operatorname{tr} M)^2 - 4 \det M \ge 0$, the
[leading real eigenvalue](leading-eigenvalue.md)
$\lambda_+(M) = (\operatorname{tr} M + \sqrt{(\operatorname{tr} M)^2 - 4 \det M})/2$ is an
eigenvalue of $M$: there is a nonzero $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$ with $M v = \lambda_+(M)\, v$.
