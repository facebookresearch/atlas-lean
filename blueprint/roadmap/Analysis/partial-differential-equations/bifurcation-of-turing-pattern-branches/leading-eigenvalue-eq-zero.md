# A singular matrix with negative trace has leading real eigenvalue 0

For a real $2 \times 2$ matrix $M$ with $\operatorname{tr} M < 0$ and $\det M = 0$, the
[leading real eigenvalue](leading-eigenvalue.md)
$\lambda_+(M) = (\operatorname{tr} M + \sqrt{(\operatorname{tr} M)^2 - 4 \det M})/2$ is $0$.
