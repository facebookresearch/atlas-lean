# Numerator of a thermal trace token

The numerator of a token is the integer $-N$ when the token is negative and $N$ otherwise, where $N$ is the positional decimal value of its numerator digits. A minus sign on numerator digits denoting zero (such as $-0$ or $-00$) yields the numerator $0$. The numerator depends only on the numerator field: the raw token $-1/0$ has rational value $0$ (division by zero gives $0$) but numerator $-1$.
