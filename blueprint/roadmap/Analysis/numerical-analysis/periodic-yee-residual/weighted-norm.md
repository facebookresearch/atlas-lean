# Weighted grid norm

For $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $w \in \mathbb{R}^N$, the weighted grid norm is $\|w\|_h = \sqrt{h \sum_j w_j^2}$, with $\sqrt{\cdot} = 0$ on negative numbers. For $h \ge 0$ its square is the weighted sum of squares $h \sum_j w_j^2$; for $h < 0$ that sum can be negative while the norm is $0$.
