# Bound on the second time derivative of the traveling sine wave

For all real $c_0, a, v, L, t, x$, the second derivative in $t$ of the traveling wave $c(t,x) = c_0 + a \sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi (x - v t)}{L}\right)$ satisfies

$$\left|\partial_t^2 c(t,x)\right| \le v^2 M, \qquad M = |a|\left(\frac{2\pi}{L}\right)^{2}.$$
