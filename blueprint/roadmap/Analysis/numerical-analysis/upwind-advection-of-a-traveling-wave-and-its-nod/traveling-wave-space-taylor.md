# Upstream spatial Taylor defect of the traveling sine wave

For all real $c_0, a, v, L, t, x, h$, with the traveling wave $c(t,x) = c_0 + a \sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi (x - v t)}{L}\right)$ and $M = |a|(2\pi/L)^2$,

$$\left|c(t, x-h) - c(t,x) + h\,\partial_x c(t,x)\right| \le \frac{M h^2}{2}.$$

Real division by zero is $0$: when $L = 0$ the quotients $2\pi(x - vt)/L$ and $2\pi/L$ are $0$, so the [traveling wave](traveling-wave.md) is the constant $c_0$ and the statement holds with $2\pi/L = 0$.
