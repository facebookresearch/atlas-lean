# Traveling sine wave solves the advection equation

For all real $c_0, a, v, L$ and every point $(t, x)$, the traveling wave $c(t,x) = c_0 + a \sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi (x - v t)}{L}\right)$ satisfies

$$\partial_t c(t,x) + v\, \partial_x c(t,x) = 0.$$

Real division by zero is $0$: when $L = 0$ the quotients $2\pi(x - vt)/L$ and $2\pi/L$ are $0$, so the [traveling wave](traveling-wave.md) is the constant $c_0$ and the statement holds with $2\pi/L = 0$.
