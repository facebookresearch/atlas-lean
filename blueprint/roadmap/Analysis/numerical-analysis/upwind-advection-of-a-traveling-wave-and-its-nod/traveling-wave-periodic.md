# Traveling wave is spatially periodic

Let $c(t, x) = c_0 + a \sin \theta$ with phase $\theta = 2\pi (x - v t)/L$ be the traveling sine wave with mean $c_0$, amplitude $a$, speed $v$ and spatial period $L$ (all real). If $L \ne 0$, then for all real $c_0, a, v, t$ the map $x \mapsto c(t, x)$ is periodic with period $L$: $c(t, x + L) = c(t, x)$ for every $x$.
