# Finite-volume schemes for transport

Four results on explicit schemes for one-dimensional transport, each in its own setting: the
inventory identity of a conservative finite-volume step on cells of arbitrary positive widths, with
inflow and outflow boundary fluxes and reaction terms; the positivity of an upwind finite-volume scheme
with diffusion in a pipe of equal cells, with an inlet concentration and a zero diffusive gradient at
the outlet; a bound on the maximum error of the nodal upwind scheme for pure advection in a finite pipe
with exact inflow values; and the positivity, bounds and conservation of a nodal upwind–diffusion
scheme on a periodic grid, with a bound on its maximum error. For $D = 0$ the update of the periodic
scheme is the periodic upwind update of the enclosing topic, upwind scheme.
