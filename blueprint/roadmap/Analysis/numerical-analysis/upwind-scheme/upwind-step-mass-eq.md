# The conservative upwind step preserves the total mass

For a natural number `n ≥ 1`, real numbers `A > 0`, `Δx > 0`, `Δt` and `v ≥ 0`, and cell values `c`,
`c'` on `ℤ/nℤ` related by the [conservative step](is-conservative-step.md) with the
[upwind face flux](upwind-face-flux.md) of `c`, `∑_i A Δx c'(i) = ∑_i A Δx c(i)`, for every Courant
number.
