# Under the CFL condition the face-flux upwind step stays nonnegative

For a natural number `n ≥ 1`, real numbers `A > 0`, `Δx > 0`, `Δt ≥ 0` and `v ≥ 0` with
`v Δt / Δx ≤ 1`, and cell values `c ≥ 0` and `c'` on `ℤ/nℤ` related by the
[conservative step](is-conservative-step.md) with the [upwind face flux](upwind-face-flux.md) of
`c`, every `c'(i)` is nonnegative.
