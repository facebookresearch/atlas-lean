# The symplectic Euler step does not preserve the energy exactly

For `m = k = h = 1`, the [symplectic Euler step](symplectic-euler-matrix.md) maps the state `(0, 1)` to
`(1, 1)`, whose [oscillator energy](oscillator-energy.md) `1` differs from the initial energy `1/2`: a
symplectic step need not preserve the energy.
