# Adjoint stability

Let $H$ and $L$ be real inner product spaces, $B$ a bounded bilinear form on $H$, $J : H \to L$ a
bounded linear map and $\alpha, C_{\mathrm{emb}} \in \mathbb{R}$ with
$\alpha \|v\|^2 \le B(v, v)$ for every $v \in H$ and $\|J\| \le C_{\mathrm{emb}}$. If $e, z \in H$
satisfy the adjoint problem $B(v, z) = \langle Je, Jv\rangle$ for every $v \in H$, then
$\alpha \|z\| \le C_{\mathrm{emb}}\, \|Je\|$.
