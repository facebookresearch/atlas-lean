# Existence for the adjoint problem

Let $H$ and $L$ be real Hilbert spaces, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $H$ (there is
$C > 0$ with $C\|u\|^2 \le B(u, u)$ for all $u$) and $J : H \to L$ a bounded linear map. Then for
every $e \in H$ there is $z \in H$ with $B(v, z) = \langle Je, Jv\rangle$ for every $v \in H$.
