# Aubin–Nitsche lemma, infimum form

Let $H$ and $L$ be real inner product spaces, $B$ a bounded bilinear form on $H$, $J : H \to L$ a
bounded linear map and $V_h \subseteq H$ a linear subspace. Let $e, z \in H$ satisfy Galerkin
orthogonality, $B(e, w_h) = 0$ for every $w_h \in V_h$, and the adjoint problem
$B(v, z) = \langle Je, Jv\rangle$ for every $v \in H$. Then

$$\|Je\|^2 \le \|B\|\, \|e\|\, \operatorname{dist}(z, V_h) .$$
