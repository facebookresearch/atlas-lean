# Stored heat of the bath-cooling trajectory

Let $C > 0$, $G > 0$, real $T_{\mathrm{amb}}$ and $\Delta$, and put $\tau = C/G$ and $Q(T) = -G(T - T_{\mathrm{amb}})$. For every $t$, the stored heat $s \mapsto C\, T(s)$ of the trajectory $T(t) = T_{\mathrm{amb}} + \Delta\, e^{-t/\tau}$ has derivative $Q(T(t))$ at $t$.
