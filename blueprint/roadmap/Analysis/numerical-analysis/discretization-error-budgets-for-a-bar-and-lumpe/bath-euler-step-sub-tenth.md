# Bath Euler step of a tenth of the time constant contracts differences by 9/10

Let $C > 0$, $G > 0$, real $T_{\mathrm{amb}}$, and put $\tau = C/G$ and $Q(T) = -G(T - T_{\mathrm{amb}})$. For all real $T$ and $X$, the Euler step $S(T) = T + (\tau/10)\,Q(T)/C$ satisfies

$$S(T) - S(X) = \tfrac{9}{10}\,(T - X).$$
