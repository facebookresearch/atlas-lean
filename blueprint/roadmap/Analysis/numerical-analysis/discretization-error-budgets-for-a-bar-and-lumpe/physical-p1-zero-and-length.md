# Dirichlet values of the P1 bar reconstruction

Let $P$ be a partition of $[0,1]$, $I_P$ the continuous piecewise-linear nodal interpolant on $P$, and $\hat u(\xi) = \xi(1-\xi)$. For $EA, q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $L \ne 0$ let $\alpha = \dfrac{q L^2}{2\,EA}$ and $u_h(x) = \alpha\,(I_P \hat u)(x/L)$. Then

$$u_h(0) = 0 \quad\text{and}\quad u_h(L) = 0.$$

For $EA = 0$ the amplitude takes the convention $x/0 = 0$, so $\alpha = 0$.
