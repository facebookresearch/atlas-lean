# Mismatched time units in the bath Euler step

Let $C > 0$, $G > 0$, real $T_{\mathrm{amb}}$, and put $\tau = C/G$ and $Q(T) = -G(T - T_{\mathrm{amb}})$. For every real $\theta$, the Euler step $T \mapsto T + \Delta t\, Q(T)/C$ with $\Delta t = 60 \cdot \tau/10$ maps $T_{\mathrm{amb}} + \theta$ to $T_{\mathrm{amb}} - 5\theta$.
