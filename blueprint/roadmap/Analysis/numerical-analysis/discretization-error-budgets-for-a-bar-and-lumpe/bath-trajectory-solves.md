# Bath-cooling trajectory solves the bath equation

Let $C > 0$, $G > 0$, real $T_{\mathrm{amb}}$ and $\Delta$, and put $\tau = C/G$ and $Q(T) = -G(T - T_{\mathrm{amb}})$. The trajectory $T(t) = T_{\mathrm{amb}} + \Delta\, e^{-t/\tau}$ satisfies $T(0) = T_{\mathrm{amb}} + \Delta$, and at every time $t$ it has derivative

$$T'(t) = \frac{Q(T(t))}{C},$$

that is, $C\,T' = Q(T)$.
