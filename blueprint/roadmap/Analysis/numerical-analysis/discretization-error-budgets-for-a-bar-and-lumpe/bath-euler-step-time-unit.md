# Bath Euler step is invariant under a common time unit

Let $k > 0$ and real $C$, $G$, $T_{\mathrm{amb}}$, $\Delta t$, $T$. Replacing $G$ by $G/k$ multiplies the time constant by $k$, $C/(G/k) = k\,(C/G)$, and the Euler step $T \mapsto T + \Delta t\, Q(T)/C$, $Q(T) = -G(T - T_{\mathrm{amb}})$, with conductance $G/k$ and step $k\,\Delta t$ equals the step with conductance $G$ and step $\Delta t$.

Real division by zero is $0$: $\tau = C/G$ is $0$ when $G = 0$, and $Q(T)/C$ is $0$ when $C = 0$, so then the Euler step is the identity.
