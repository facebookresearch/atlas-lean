# Energy form of the equation $-u'' = f$

For functions $u, v : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the energy form is

$$a(u, v) = \int_0^1 u'(x)\, v'(x)\, dx,$$

with the derivative taken to be $0$ at points of non-differentiability and the integral taken to be
$0$ when $u' v'$ is not integrable on $(0, 1)$ (for instance $u = v = \sqrt{|x|}$, where
$u'(x) v'(x) = 1/(4x)$). For P1 functions the points of non-differentiability are mesh nodes, a null
set, and the integrand is integrable.
