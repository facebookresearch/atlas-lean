# Stiffness matrix is the Gram matrix of the hat functions

Let $0 = x_0 < \dots < x_n = 1$ be a partition of $[0, 1]$ with [hat functions](hat.md) $\varphi_j$
and [stiffness matrix](stiffness.md) $K$. Then $a(\varphi_j, \varphi_k) = K_{jk}$ for all nodes
$j, k$, where $a$ is the [energy form](energy.md).
