# Weak form of the classical solution for P1 test functions

Let $0 = x_0 < \dots < x_n = 1$ be a partition of $[0, 1]$, let $u$ be a [classical
solution](is-classical-solution.md) of $-u'' = f$ with $u(0) = u(1) = 0$, and let $v$ belong to
the [P1 space](p1-space.md) $V_h$. Then $a(u, v) = \int_0^1 u' v' = \int_0^1 f v$.
