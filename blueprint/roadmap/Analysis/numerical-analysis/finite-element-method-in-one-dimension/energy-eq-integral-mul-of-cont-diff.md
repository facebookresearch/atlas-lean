# Weak form of the classical solution for $C^1$ test functions

Let $u$ be a [classical solution](is-classical-solution.md) of $-u'' = f$ on $(0, 1)$ with
$u(0) = u(1) = 0$, and let $v$ be $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}$ with $v(0) = v(1) = 0$. Then

$$a(u, v) = \int_0^1 u'(x)\, v'(x)\, dx = \int_0^1 f(x)\, v(x)\, dx .$$
