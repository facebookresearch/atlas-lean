# Energy form of two P1 functions

Let $0 = x_0 < \dots < x_n = 1$ be a partition of $[0, 1]$ with element lengths $h_i$, and let
$p_c$ and $p_d$ be the [P1 functions](p1.md) with nodal values $c, d \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$. Then
the [energy form](energy.md) is

$$a(p_c, p_d) = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \frac{(c_{i+1} - c_i)(d_{i+1} - d_i)}{h_i} .$$
