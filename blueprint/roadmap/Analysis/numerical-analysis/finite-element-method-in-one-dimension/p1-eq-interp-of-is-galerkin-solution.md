# In one dimension the Galerkin solution is the nodal interpolant

Let $0 = x_0 < \dots < x_n = 1$ be a partition of $[0, 1]$, let $u$ be a [classical
solution](is-classical-solution.md) of $-u'' = f$ with $u(0) = u(1) = 0$, and let $c$ be a
[solution of the Galerkin system](is-galerkin-solution.md). Then the Galerkin solution $p_c$ equals
the [nodal interpolant](interp.md) $I_h u$.
