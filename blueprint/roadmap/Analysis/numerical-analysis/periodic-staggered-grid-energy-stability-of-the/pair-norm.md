# Pair norm

For $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^N$, let $S_h(w) = h \sum_j w_j^2$ and let the pair norm be $\|(u, v)\|_h = \sqrt{S_h(u) + S_h(v)}$, with $\sqrt{\cdot} = 0$ on negative numbers. For $h \ge 0$, $S_h$ is the square of the weighted grid norm and the pair norm is the Euclidean norm of the pair; for $h < 0$, $S_h$ can be negative.
