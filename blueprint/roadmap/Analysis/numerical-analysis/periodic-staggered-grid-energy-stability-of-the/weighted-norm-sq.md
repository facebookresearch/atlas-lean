# Weighted sum of squares on the grid

For $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $w \in \mathbb{R}^N$, let $S_h(w) = h \sum_j w_j^2$, a quadratic form defined for every real $h$; for $h \ge 0$ it is the square of the weighted grid norm $\|w\|_h = \sqrt{h \sum_j w_j^2}$, while for $h < 0$ it can be negative (for $h = -1$, $N = 1$, $w = (1)$ it is $-1$, whereas the square root, taken as $0$ on negative numbers, is $0$).
