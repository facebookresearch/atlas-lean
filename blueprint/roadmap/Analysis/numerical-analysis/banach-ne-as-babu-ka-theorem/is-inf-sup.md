# Inf-sup condition

Let $E$ and $F$ be real normed spaces. A bounded bilinear form $B$ on $E \times F$ satisfies the
inf–sup condition if there is $\beta > 0$ such that $\beta \|x\| \le \|B(x, \cdot)\|_{F^*}$ for
every $x \in E$. The right side is the dual norm $\sup_{\|y\| \le 1} |B(x, y)|$
([dual norm](norm-apply-eq-i-sup.md)).
