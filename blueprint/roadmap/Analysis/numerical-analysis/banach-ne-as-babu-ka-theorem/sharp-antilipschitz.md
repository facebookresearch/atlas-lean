# Inf-sup condition makes the associated operator antilipschitz

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$ satisfying the [inf–sup condition](is-inf-sup.md). Then the [associated
operator](bilinear-form-sharp.md) $B^\sharp$ is antilipschitz: there is $C > 0$ with
$\|x - x'\| \le C \|B^\sharp x - B^\sharp x'\|$ for all $x, x' \in E$.
