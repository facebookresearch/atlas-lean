# Inf-sup condition makes the associated operator's range closed

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$ satisfying the [inf–sup condition](is-inf-sup.md). Then the range of the [associated
operator](bilinear-form-sharp.md) $B^\sharp$ is closed in $F$.
