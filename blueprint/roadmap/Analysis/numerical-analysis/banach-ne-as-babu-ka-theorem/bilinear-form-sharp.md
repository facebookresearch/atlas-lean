# Operator associated to a bounded bilinear form

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B : E \times F \to \mathbb{R}$ a
bounded bilinear form. The associated operator $B^\sharp : E \to F$ is the bounded linear map
characterized by $\langle B^\sharp x, y\rangle = B(x, y)$ for all $x \in E$, $y \in F$ (by the Riesz
representation of $B(x, \cdot)$).
