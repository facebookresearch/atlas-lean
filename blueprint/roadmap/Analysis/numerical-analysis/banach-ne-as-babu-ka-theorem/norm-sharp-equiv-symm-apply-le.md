# Pointwise inverse bound for the Banach–Nečas–Babuška isomorphism

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$, nondegenerate in the second variable, and let $\beta > 0$ with
$\beta \|x\| \le \|B(x, \cdot)\|_{F^*}$ for every $x$. Then the inverse of the
[isomorphism](sharp-equiv.md) $B^\sharp$ satisfies $\|(B^\sharp)^{-1} y\| \le \beta^{-1} \|y\|$ for
every $y \in F$.
