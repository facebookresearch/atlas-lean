# Banach–Nečas–Babuška isomorphism is characterized by the form

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$ satisfying the inf–sup condition and nondegeneracy. Then the
[isomorphism](sharp-equiv.md) $B^\sharp$ satisfies $\langle B^\sharp x, y\rangle = B(x, y)$ for all
$x \in E$ and $y \in F$.
