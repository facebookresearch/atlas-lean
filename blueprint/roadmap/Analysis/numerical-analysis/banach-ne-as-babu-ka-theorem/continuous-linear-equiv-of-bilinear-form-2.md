# Banach–Nečas–Babuška equivalence acts as the bilinear form

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$ satisfying the [inf–sup condition](is-inf-sup.md) and [nondegeneracy](is-nondegenerate.md).
Then the [Banach–Nečas–Babuška isomorphism](continuous-linear-equiv-of-bilinear-form.md)
$E \to F^*$ sends $x$ to the functional $B(x, \cdot)$.
