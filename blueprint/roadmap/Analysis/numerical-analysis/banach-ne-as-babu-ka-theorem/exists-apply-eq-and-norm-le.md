# Stability bound for the inf-sup variational problem

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$. Let $\beta > 0$ with $\beta \|x\| \le \|B(x, \cdot)\|_{F^*}$ for every $x \in E$, and
suppose that $B$ is [nondegenerate](is-nondegenerate.md) in the second variable. Then for every
$\varphi \in F^*$ there is $x \in E$ with $B(x, y) = \varphi(y)$ for every $y \in F$ and
$\|x\| \le \beta^{-1} \|\varphi\|$.
