# Norm of the Banach–Nečas–Babuška isomorphism applied to a vector

Let $E$ be a real Banach space, $F$ a real Hilbert space and $B$ a bounded bilinear form on
$E \times F$ satisfying the inf–sup condition and nondegeneracy. Then for every $x \in E$ the
[isomorphism](sharp-equiv.md) $B^\sharp : E \to F$ satisfies
$\|B^\sharp x\| = \|B(x, \cdot)\|_{F^*}$.
