# The second Barzilai–Borwein step is the shorter one

If $s^\top y > 0$, then

$$\frac{s^\top y}{y^\top y} \le \frac{s^\top s}{s^\top y},$$

that is, the [second Barzilai–Borwein step](bb-step-2.md) is at most the
[first](bb-step.md). This is the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality $(s^\top y)^2 \le \|s\|^2 \|y\|^2$.
