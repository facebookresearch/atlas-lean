# Certified sign of a difference of enclosed sums

Let $S_1, S_2, L_1, L_2, w_1, w_2 \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|S_1 - L_1| \le w_1$, $|S_2 - L_2| \le w_2$ and $w_1 + w_2 < |S_1 - S_2|$. Then $L_1 - L_2$ and $S_1 - S_2$ have the same sign: $\operatorname{sign}(L_1 - L_2) = \operatorname{sign}(S_1 - S_2)$.
