# Increasing a Z-matrix preserves the nonsingular M-matrix property

Let $A$ and $B$ be real $n \times n$ matrices with $A \le B$ entrywise and $b_{ij} \le 0$ for $i \ne j$. If $A$ is a [nonsingular M-matrix](../../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-nonsingular-mmatrix.md), then so is $B$.
