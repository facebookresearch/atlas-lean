# Increasing a Z-matrix preserves the M-matrix property

Let $A$ and $B$ be real $n \times n$ matrices with $A \le B$ entrywise and $b_{ij} \le 0$ for $i \ne j$. If $A$ is an [M-matrix](m-matrix.md), then so is $B$.
