# Monotonicity of the spectral radius on nonnegative matrices

Let $A$ and $B$ be real $n \times n$ matrices with $0 \le A \le B$ entrywise. Then $A^k \le B^k$ entrywise for every $k \ge 0$, and

$$\rho(A) \le \rho(B).$$

## Proof depends on

- [Spectral radius formula in a complex Banach algebra](../../../operator-algebras/spectral-radius-formula.md)

## Sources

- Y. Saad, *Iterative Methods for Sparse Linear Systems*, 2nd edition, SIAM, 2003, Section 1.10, Proposition 1.26, Corollary 1.27 and Theorem 1.28 with its proof through $\rho(X) = \lim_k \|X^k\|^{1/k}$. <https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~saad/IterMethBook_2ndEd.pdf>
