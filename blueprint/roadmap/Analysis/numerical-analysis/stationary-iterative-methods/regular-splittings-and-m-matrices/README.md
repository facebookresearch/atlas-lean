# Regular splittings and M-matrices

For matrices with a sign structure, convergence of a splitting iteration is decided by nonnegativity: if $B \ge 0$ entrywise, then $\rho(B) < 1$ exactly when $(I - B)^{-1}$ exists and is nonnegative. This characterizes the nonsingular M-matrices among the Z-matrices by a nonnegative inverse and through their Jacobi iteration matrices, and gives Varga's theorem that a regular splitting $A = M - N$, with $M^{-1} \ge 0$ and $N \ge 0$, converges exactly when $A^{-1} \ge 0$.
