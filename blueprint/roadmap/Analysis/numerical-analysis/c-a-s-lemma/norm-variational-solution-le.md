# Stability bound for the variational solution

Let $V$ be a real Hilbert space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $L \in V^*$, and
$\alpha > 0$ with $\alpha \|v\|^2 \le B(v, v)$ for every $v \in V$. Then the [variational
solution](variational-solution.md) satisfies $\|u\| \le \|L\| / \alpha$.
