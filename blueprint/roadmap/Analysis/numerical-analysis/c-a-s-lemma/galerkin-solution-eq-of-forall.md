# Galerkin solution is the unique solution of the Galerkin equations

Let $V$ be a real inner product space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $W \subseteq V$
a complete linear subspace and $L \in V^*$. If $x \in W$ satisfies $B(x, w) = L(w)$ for every
$w \in W$, then $x$ is the [Galerkin solution](galerkin-solution.md).
