# Galerkin solution satisfies the Galerkin equations

Let $V$ be a real inner product space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $W \subseteq V$
a complete linear subspace and $L \in V^*$. Then the [Galerkin solution](galerkin-solution.md)
$u_h \in W$ satisfies $B(u_h, w) = L(w)$ for every $w \in W$.
