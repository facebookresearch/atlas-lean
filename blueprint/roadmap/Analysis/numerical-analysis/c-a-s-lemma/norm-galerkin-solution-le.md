# Stability bound for the Galerkin solution

Let $V$ be a real inner product space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $W \subseteq V$ a
complete linear subspace, $L \in V^*$, and $\alpha > 0$ with $\alpha \|v\|^2 \le B(v, v)$ for every
$v \in V$. Then the [Galerkin solution](galerkin-solution.md) satisfies
$\|u_h\| \le \|L\| / \alpha$.
