# Céa's lemma

Let $V$ be a real Hilbert space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $W \subseteq V$ a
complete linear subspace, $L \in V^*$, and $\alpha > 0$ with $\alpha \|v\|^2 \le B(v, v)$ for every
$v \in V$. Then the [variational solution](variational-solution.md) $u$ and the [Galerkin
solution](galerkin-solution.md) $u_h$ satisfy, for every $v \in W$,

$$\|u - u_h\| \le \frac{\|B\|}{\alpha}\, \|u - v\| .$$
