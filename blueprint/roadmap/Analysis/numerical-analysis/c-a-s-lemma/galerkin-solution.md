# Galerkin solution

Let $V$ be a real inner product space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $W \subseteq V$
a complete linear subspace and $L \in V^*$. The Galerkin solution is the element $u_h \in W$ solving
the variational problem $B(u_h, w) = L(w)$ for all $w \in W$, the [variational
solution](variational-solution.md) for the restrictions of $B$ and $L$ to $W$.
