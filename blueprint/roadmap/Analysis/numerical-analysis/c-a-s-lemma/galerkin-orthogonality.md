# Galerkin orthogonality

Let $V$ be a real Hilbert space, $B$ a coercive bounded bilinear form on $V$, $W \subseteq V$ a
complete linear subspace and $L \in V^*$, with [variational solution](variational-solution.md) $u$
and [Galerkin solution](galerkin-solution.md) $u_h \in W$. Then $B(u - u_h, w) = 0$ for every
$w \in W$: the error is $B$-orthogonal to $W$.
