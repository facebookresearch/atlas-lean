# Adjoint of the forward difference

On the periodic grid $\mathbb{Z}/N$ with spacing $h$, let $(D_+ v)_j = (v_{j+1} - v_j)/h$. The adjoint $D_+^*$ is the transpose of the matrix of $D_+$, applied to $u$; for the weighted inner product $h \sum_j u_j v_j$ with constant weight $h$ this is the adjoint.
