# The residual of the central second difference on x⁴ is exactly 2h²

For the manufactured solution `u(x) = x⁴`, whose second derivative is `u''(x) = 12 x²`, every real
`x` and every step `h ≠ 0`, the central second difference
`D²u(x) = (u(x + h) - 2 u(x) + u(x - h)) / h²` has residual `D²u(x) - u''(x) = 2 h²` exactly:
second order in `h`.
