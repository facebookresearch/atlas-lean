# Second-order bound on the central-difference residual for x⁴

For the manufactured solution `u(x) = x⁴`, whose second derivative is `u''(x) = 12 x²`, every real
`x` and every step `h ≠ 0`, the central second difference
`D²u(x) = (u(x + h) - 2 u(x) + u(x - h)) / h²` satisfies `|D²u(x) - u''(x)| ≤ 2 h²`.
