# Gram polynomials of degree-one monomials are nonnegative

Let $Q$ be a positive semidefinite rational matrix indexed by $\{\ast\} \cup \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, and let $z = (1, X_0, \dots, X_{n-1})$ be the vector of monomials of degree at most $1$. The Gram polynomial $z^{\mathsf T} Q z = \sum_{i, j} Q_{ij}\, z_i z_j$ is nonnegative at every real point $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
