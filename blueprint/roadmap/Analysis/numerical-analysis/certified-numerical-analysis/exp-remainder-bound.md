# Rational remainder bound for the exponential

For a rational $q$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the rational remainder bound $|q|^n \cdot \frac{n + 1}{n!\, n}$ for the Taylor polynomial of $e^q$ of degree below $n$; it bounds the remainder when $|q| \le 1$ and $n > 0$. For $n = 0$ the division by zero makes the value $0$.
