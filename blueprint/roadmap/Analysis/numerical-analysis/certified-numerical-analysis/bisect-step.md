# One bisection step

One bisection step on a rational bracket $I = [\ell, u]$ with midpoint $m$: the decision true keeps $[\ell, m]$ and the decision false keeps $[m, u]$. The step chooses a half regardless of any function; a separate sign certificate records the evidence for each decision, $0 \le f(m)$ for true and $f(m) \le 0$ for false.
