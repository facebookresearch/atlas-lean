# The bar loads are Q L times the unit loads

For a real `L > 0` and a real `Q`, the [exact interior load](quad-load.md) is `Q L` times the unit
[load](../finite-element-method-in-one-dimension/load.md) of `ξ²`, and the
[bar midpoint load](phys-midpoint-load.md) of `Q (x / L)²` is `Q L` times the unit
[midpoint load](midpoint-load.md) of `ξ²`, both at the interior node of the
[two-cell partition](two-cells.md).
