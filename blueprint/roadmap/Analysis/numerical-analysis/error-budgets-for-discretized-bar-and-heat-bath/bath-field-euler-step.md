# The one-stage Runge–Kutta step of the bath field is the Euler step

For all real `C`, `G`, `T_amb`, `t`, `T` and `dt`, one [step](../runge-kutta-methods/rk-step.md) of
the [one-stage (Euler) Runge–Kutta method](../runge-kutta-methods/euler-rk.md) on the
[bath field](bath-field.md) from `(t, T)` with step `dt` equals the [Euler step](bath-euler-step.md)
of size `dt` from `T`.
