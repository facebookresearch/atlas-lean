# Energy of the reconstructed quadrature error

For real numbers `EA > 0`, `L > 0` and `Q`, the difference `w` of the
[hat reconstructions](two-cell-reconstruction.md) of the [exact-load](quad-coeff.md) and
[midpoint](quad-midpoint-coeff.md) coefficients satisfies
`∫_0^L EA w'(x)² dx = (4 EA / L) (β / 384)²`, with the [amplitude](quad-amplitude.md) `β`;
derivatives are taken to be `0` where a function is not differentiable.
