# The reconstruction error is the coefficient error times the hat

For a real `L ≠ 0` and all real `EA`, `q`, `c̃` and `x`, the [reconstruction](physical-p1.md) `u_h`
on the [two-cell partition](two-cells.md) and the [hat reconstruction](two-cell-reconstruction.md)
`ũ_h` of `c̃` satisfy `u_h(x) - ũ_h(x) = (c - c̃) φ(x / L)`, with the
[interior coefficient](two-cell-coeff.md) `c` and the interior
[hat function](../finite-element-method-in-one-dimension/hat.md) `φ`.
