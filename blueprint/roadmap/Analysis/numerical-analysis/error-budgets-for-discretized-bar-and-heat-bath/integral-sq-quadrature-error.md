# Squared L² norm of the reconstructed quadrature error

For real numbers `EA ≠ 0`, `L > 0` and `Q`, the difference `w` of the
[hat reconstructions](two-cell-reconstruction.md) of the [exact-load](quad-coeff.md) and
[midpoint](quad-midpoint-coeff.md) coefficients satisfies `∫_0^L w(x)² dx = (L / 3) (β / 384)²`,
with the [amplitude](quad-amplitude.md) `β`.
