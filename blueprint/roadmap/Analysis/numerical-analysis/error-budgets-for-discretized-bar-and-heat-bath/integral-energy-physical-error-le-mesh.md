# Mesh bound on the energy of the bar error

For a [partition](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/partition.md) `P` of `[0, 1]`
with [mesh](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/mesh.md) `h`, real numbers `L > 0` and
`EA > 0`, and every real `q`, `∫_0^L EA e'(x)² dx ≤ (EA / L) α² · 4 (h / π)²`, with the
[physical error](physical-error.md) `e` and the [amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) `α`. Derivatives are
taken to be `0` where a function is not differentiable.
