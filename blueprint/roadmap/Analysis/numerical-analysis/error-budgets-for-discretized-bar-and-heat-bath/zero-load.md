# At zero load the displacement, reconstruction and error vanish

For every [partition](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/partition.md) `P` of
`[0, 1]` and all real `EA` and `L`, at the load `q = 0` the [amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) is `0`,
and the
[fixed-fixed displacement](../../../Physics/elasticity-and-structures/prismatic-bars-shafts-and-beams/axial-displacement.md),
the [reconstruction](physical-p1.md) and the [physical error](physical-error.md) are `0` at every
`x`.
