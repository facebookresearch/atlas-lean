# A change of temperature unit scales the residuals and the budget

For a real `k > 0` and all real `c`, `C`, `G`, `T_amb`, `Δ` and every real sequence `Y`, under the
change of unit `T ↦ k T + c` (bath `k T_amb + c`, sequence `k Y_j + c`, excess `k Δ`, heat capacity
`C / k`, conductance `G / k`), every [residual](bath-euler-residual.md) and the
[ten-step error budget](ten-step-error-budget.md) are multiplied by `k`.
