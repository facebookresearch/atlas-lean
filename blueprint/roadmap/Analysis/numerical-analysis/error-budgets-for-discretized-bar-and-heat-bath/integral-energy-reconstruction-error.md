# Energy of the reconstruction error from the residual

For real numbers `EA > 0`, `L > 0`, `q` and `c̃`, with `u_h` and `ũ_h` as in the
[pointwise bound](abs-physical-p1-sub-two-cell-reconstruct.md),
`∫_0^L EA (u_h - ũ_h)'(x)² dx = K (c - c̃)² = L r² / (4 EA)`, with the
[stiffness](two-cell-stiffness.md) `K`, the [interior coefficient](two-cell-coeff.md) `c` and the
[residual](two-cell-residual.md) `r` of `c̃`; derivatives are taken to be `0` where a function is
not differentiable.
