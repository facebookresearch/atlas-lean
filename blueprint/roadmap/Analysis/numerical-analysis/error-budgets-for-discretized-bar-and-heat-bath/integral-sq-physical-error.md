# Squared L² norm of the bar error in terms of the unit error

For a [partition](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/partition.md) `P` of `[0, 1]`, a
real `L > 0` and all real `EA` and `q`, `∫_0^L e(x)² dx = L α² ∫_0^1 ê(ξ)² dξ`, with the
[physical error](physical-error.md) `e`, the [unit error](unit-error.md) `ê` and the
[amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) `α`.
