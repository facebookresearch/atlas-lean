# The stored heat of the cooling body changes at the bath power

For real numbers `C > 0`, `G > 0`, `T_amb`, `Δ` and `t`, the stored heat `s ↦ C T(s)` of the
[trajectory](bath-trajectory.md) has derivative `Q(T(t))` at `t`, with the
[bath power](bath-power.md) `Q`: the
[storage law](../../../Physics/heat-and-mass-transport/energy-identity-for-the-heat-equation-with-bound/has-deriv-at-thermal-storage.md)
of a one-node network with capacity `C`, no internal conductance and the bath power as its source.
