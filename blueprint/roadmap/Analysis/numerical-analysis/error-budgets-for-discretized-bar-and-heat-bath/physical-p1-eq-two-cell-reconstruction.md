# The two-cell reconstruction is the hat times its midpoint value

For a real `L ≠ 0` and all real `EA`, `q` and `x`, the [reconstruction](physical-p1.md) on the
[two-cell partition](two-cells.md) equals the [hat reconstruction](two-cell-reconstruction.md) of
the [interior coefficient](two-cell-coeff.md) `u_h(L / 2)`.
