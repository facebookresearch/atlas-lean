# Nodal exactness leaves the quadrature and off-node errors

For real numbers `EA > 0`, `L > 0` and `Q ≠ 0`: the [exact-load coefficient](quad-coeff.md) equals
the [displacement](quad-displacement.md) `u(L / 2)`; the
[midpoint coefficient](quad-midpoint-coeff.md) does not; and at `x = L / 4`, `u` minus the
[hat reconstruction](two-cell-reconstruction.md) of the exact-load coefficient is `7 β / 3072 ≠ 0`,
with the [amplitude](quad-amplitude.md) `β`.
