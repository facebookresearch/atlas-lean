# The defect of a computed sequence obeys a damped recurrence

For real numbers `C > 0`, `G > 0` and `T_amb`, a real sequence `X` whose terms follow
[Euler steps](bath-euler-step.md) of size `τ / 10`, every real sequence `Y` and every `j`,
`Y_{j+1} - X_{j+1} = (9/10) (Y_j - X_j) + η_j`, with the [residual](bath-euler-residual.md) `η_j` of
`Y`.
