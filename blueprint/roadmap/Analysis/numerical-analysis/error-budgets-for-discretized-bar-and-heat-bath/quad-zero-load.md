# At zero quadratic load the displacement and both loads vanish

For all real `EA` and `L`, at `Q = 0` the [amplitude](quad-amplitude.md) is `0`, the
[displacement](quad-displacement.md) is `0` at every `x`, and the [exact load](quad-load.md) and the
[midpoint load](phys-midpoint-load.md) of the interior node are `0`.
