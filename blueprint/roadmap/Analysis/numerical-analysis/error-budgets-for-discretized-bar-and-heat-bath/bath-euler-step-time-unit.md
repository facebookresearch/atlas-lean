# A common change of time unit leaves the Euler step unchanged

For a real `k > 0` and all real `C`, `G`, `T_amb`, `dt` and `T`, the
[time constant](thermal-time-constant.md) with conductance `G / k` is `k` times the time constant
with `G`, and the [Euler step](bath-euler-step.md) of size `k dt` with conductance `G / k` equals
the step of size `dt` with conductance `G`.
