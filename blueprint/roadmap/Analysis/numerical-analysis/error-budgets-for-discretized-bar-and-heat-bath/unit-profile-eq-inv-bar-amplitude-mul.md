# For nonzero amplitude the unit profile is recovered from the bar

For real numbers `L ≠ 0`, `EA` and `q` with [amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) `α ≠ 0`, and every real
`ξ`, the [unit profile](unit-profile.md) satisfies `û(ξ) = α⁻¹ u(L ξ)`, where `u` is the
[fixed-fixed displacement](../../../Physics/elasticity-and-structures/prismatic-bars-shafts-and-beams/axial-displacement.md).
