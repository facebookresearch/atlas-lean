# A Runge–Kutta step of the bath field does not depend on the time

For all real `C`, `G`, `T_amb`, `t`, `T` and `dt`, one [step](../runge-kutta-methods/rk-step.md) of
the classical [fourth-order Runge–Kutta method](../runge-kutta-methods/rk4.md) on the
[bath field](bath-field.md) from time `t` equals the step from time `0`.
