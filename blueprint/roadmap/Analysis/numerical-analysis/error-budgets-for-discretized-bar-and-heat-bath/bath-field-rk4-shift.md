# A Runge–Kutta step of the bath field acts on the excess temperature

For all real `C`, `G`, `T_amb`, `t`, `T` and `dt`, one
[classical Runge–Kutta step](../runge-kutta-methods/rk4.md) of the [bath field](bath-field.md) from
`T` equals `T_amb` plus the step of the linear decay `θ' = (-1/τ) θ` from the excess `T - T_amb` at
time `0`, with `τ = C / G`. With the convention `a / 0 = 0`, `-1/τ = 0` when `τ = 0`.
