# The interior stiffness is the energy of the rescaled hat

For a real `EA` and a real `L > 0`, `∫_0^L EA (d/dx φ(x / L))² dx = 4 EA / L`, the
[interior stiffness](two-cell-stiffness.md), where `φ` is the
[hat function](../finite-element-method-in-one-dimension/hat.md) of the interior node of the
[two-cell partition](two-cells.md); the derivative is taken to be `0` where the function is not
differentiable.
