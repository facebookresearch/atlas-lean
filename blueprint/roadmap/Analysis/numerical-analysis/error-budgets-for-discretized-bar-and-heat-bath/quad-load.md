# Exact consistent load of the quadratic load at the interior node

For real numbers `L` and `Q`, the exact consistent load is `∫_0^L Q (x / L)² φ(x / L) dx`, where `φ`
is the [hat function](../finite-element-method-in-one-dimension/hat.md) of the interior node of the
[two-cell partition](two-cells.md).
