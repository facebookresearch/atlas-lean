# On the bar the reconstruction error is bounded by the residual

For real numbers `EA > 0`, `L > 0`, `q` and `c̃`, and every `x ∈ [0, L]`, the
[reconstruction](physical-p1.md) `u_h` on the [two-cell partition](two-cells.md) and the
[hat reconstruction](two-cell-reconstruction.md) `ũ_h` of `c̃` satisfy
`|u_h(x) - ũ_h(x)| ≤ L |r| / (4 EA)`, where `r` is the [residual](two-cell-residual.md) of `c̃`.
