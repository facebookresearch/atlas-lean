# Load and coefficient defects of the midpoint rule on two cells

On the [two-cell partition](two-cells.md), for `f(ξ) = ξ²` at the interior node, the
[midpoint load](midpoint-load.md) exceeds the exact
[load](../finite-element-method-in-one-dimension/load.md) by `1/96`; after division by the unit
[stiffness](../finite-element-method-in-one-dimension/stiffness.md) entry `4` the coefficients
differ by `1/384`; and the exact-load coefficient `7/192` equals `û(1/2)` for the
[quadratic-load profile](quad-profile.md) `û`.
