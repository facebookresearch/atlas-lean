# Energy of the bar error in terms of the unit error

For a [partition](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/partition.md) `P` of `[0, 1]`, a
real `L > 0` and all real `EA` and `q`, `∫_0^L EA e'(x)² dx = (EA / L) α² ∫_0^1 ê'(ξ)² dξ`, with the
[physical error](physical-error.md) `e`, the [unit error](unit-error.md) `ê` and the
[amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) `α`. Derivatives are taken to be `0` where a function is not
differentiable (at finitely many nodes, which no integral sees).
