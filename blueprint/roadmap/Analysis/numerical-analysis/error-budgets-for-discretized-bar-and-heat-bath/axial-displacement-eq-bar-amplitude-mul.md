# The bar displacement is the amplitude times the rescaled unit profile

For real numbers `L ≠ 0`, `EA`, `q` and `x`, the
[fixed-fixed displacement](../../../Physics/elasticity-and-structures/prismatic-bars-shafts-and-beams/axial-displacement.md)
satisfies `u(x) = α û(x / L)`, with the [amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) `α` and the
[unit profile](unit-profile.md) `û`.
