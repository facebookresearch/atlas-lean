# Mesh bound on the squared L² norm of the bar error

For a [partition](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/partition.md) `P` of `[0, 1]`
with [mesh](../../real-functions/integration/riemann-integral/mesh.md) `h`, a real `L > 0` and all
real `EA` and `q`, `∫_0^L e(x)² dx ≤ L α² · 4 (h / π)⁴`, with the
[physical error](physical-error.md) `e` and the [amplitude](bar-amplitude.md) `α`.
