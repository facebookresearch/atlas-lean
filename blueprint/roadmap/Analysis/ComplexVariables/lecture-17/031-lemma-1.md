# Lemma 1

If $(A_{n})$ is bounded, $v_{n} \to 0$, and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |v_{n} - v_{n+1}| < \infty$, then $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_{n} v_{n}$ converges.
