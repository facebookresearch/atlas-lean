# Theorem 1 (Analytic Theorem)

Let $f(t)$ ($t \geq 0$) be bounded and locally integrable and assume the function $g(z) = \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-zt} f(t)\, dt$ for $\operatorname{Re}(z) > 0$ extends to a holomorphic function on $\operatorname{Re}(z) \geq 0$, then $\lim_{T \to \infty} \int_{0}^{T} f(t)\, dt$ exists and equals $g(0)$.
