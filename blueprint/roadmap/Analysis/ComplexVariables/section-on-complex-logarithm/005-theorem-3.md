# Theorem 3

In the slit plane, $\operatorname{Log}(z_{1} z_{2}) = \operatorname{Log}(z_{1}) + \operatorname{Log}(z_{2}) + n \cdot 2\pi i$, where $n = 0$ or $\pm 1$, and $n = 0$ if $-\pi < \operatorname{Arg}(z_{1}) + \operatorname{Arg}(z_{2}) < \pi$.
