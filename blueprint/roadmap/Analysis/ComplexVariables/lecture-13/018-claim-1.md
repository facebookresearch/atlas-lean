# Claim 1

$g$ is continuous on $\Omega \times \Omega$ and holomorphic in each variable and $g(z, \zeta) = g(\zeta, z)$, where $g(z, \zeta) = \frac{f(\zeta) - f(z)}{\zeta - z}$ for $z \neq \zeta$ and $g(z, \zeta) = f'(z)$ for $z = \zeta$.
