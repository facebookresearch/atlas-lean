# Proposition (iii)

For $x > 0$, $y > 0$, $$\int_{0}^{1} t^{x-1}(1-t)^{y-1}\, dt = \frac{\Gamma(x)\,\Gamma(y)}{\Gamma(x+y)}.$$ This extends to $\operatorname{Re} z > 0$, $\operatorname{Re} w > 0$: $$\int_{0}^{1} t^{z-1}(1-t)^{w-1}\, dt = \frac{\Gamma(z)\,\Gamma(w)}{\Gamma(z+w)}.$$
