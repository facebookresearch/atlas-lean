# Claim 2

$h$ is holomorphic, where $h$ is defined on $\mathbb{C}$ by $h(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_{\gamma} g(z, \zeta)\, d\zeta$ for $z \in \Omega$ and $h(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_{\gamma} \frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\, d\zeta$ for $z \in \Omega'$.
