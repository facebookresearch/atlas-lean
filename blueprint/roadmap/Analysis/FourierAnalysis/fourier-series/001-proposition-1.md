# Proposition 1 (Fourier Series, Continued (part 3))

If $f$ and $g$ belong to $L^1(\mathbb{T})$, then $f * g \in L^1(\mathbb{T})$ and $\|f * g\|_p \leq \|f\|_p \|g\|_1$.
