# Theorem 4 (Approximate identity)

If $K \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$, $K_{\epsilon}(x) = (1/\epsilon) K(x/\epsilon)$, and $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} K(x) \, dx = 1$, then $\|K_{\epsilon} * f - f\|_1 \to 0$ for all $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
