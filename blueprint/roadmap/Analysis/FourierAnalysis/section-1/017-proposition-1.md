# Proposition 1 (Section 1, Fourier Integrals of finite measures)

For $\mu \in M_+(\mathbb{R})$, we define the Fourier transform by $\mathcal{F}(\mu) = \hat{\mu}(\xi) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} e^{-ix\xi} \, d\mu(x)$. Then $\mathcal{F} \colon M_+(\mathbb{R}) \to C_b(\mathbb{R})$ where $C_b(\mathbb{R})$ is the space of bounded continuous functions.
