# Proposition 1 (Continuity)

If $\|u_n - u\| \to 0$ and $\|v_n - v\| \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$, then $\|u_n\| \to \|u\|$ and $\langle u_n, v_n \rangle \to \langle u, v \rangle$.
