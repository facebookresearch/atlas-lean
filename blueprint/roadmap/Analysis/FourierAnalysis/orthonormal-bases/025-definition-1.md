# Definition 1

$\varphi_n$ is called an orthonormal sequence, $n = 1, 2, \ldots$, if $\langle \varphi_n, \varphi_m \rangle = 0$ for $n \neq m$ and $\langle \varphi_n, \varphi_n \rangle = \|\varphi_n\|^2 = 1$.
