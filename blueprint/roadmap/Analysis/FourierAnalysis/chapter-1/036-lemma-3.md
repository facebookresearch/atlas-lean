# Lemma 3

For $m \geq 1$ and $0 \leq \beta \leq 2$, $$R_{\beta}(m) = \int_{0}^{1} r^{m} (1 - r)^{\beta} \, dr \leq 100 \, m^{-1-\beta}.$$
