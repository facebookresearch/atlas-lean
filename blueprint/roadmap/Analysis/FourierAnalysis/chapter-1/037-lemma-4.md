# Lemma 4

For any $\beta > 1$, $$\mathbb{E} \int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{1} |\nabla F(r e^{it})|^{4} (1 - r)^{\beta} r \, dr \, dt < \infty,$$ and, consequently $$\int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^1 |\nabla F(r e^{it})|^4 (1 - r)^\beta r \, dr \, dt < \infty,$$ almost surely.
