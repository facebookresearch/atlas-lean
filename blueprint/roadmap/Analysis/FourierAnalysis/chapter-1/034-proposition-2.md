# Proposition 2 (Chapter 1 (Brownian Motion))

Suppose that $B(t)$ is such that $B(0) = 0$. Then $B$ satisfies property (2) if and only if (a) $\sum_{j=1}^{m} \xi_j B(t_j)$ is a Gaussian random variable with mean $0$, and (b) $\mathbb{E}(B(s) B(t)) = s \wedge t$ ($s \wedge t = \min(s, t)$).
