# Browder's demiclosedness principle

Let $H$ be a real or complex Hilbert space and $T : H \to H$ nonexpansive, that is
$\|Tx - Ty\| \le \|x - y\|$ for all $x, y$. If a sequence $(u_n)$ converges weakly to $x$, meaning
$f(u_n) \to f(x)$ for every bounded linear functional $f$, and $u_n - T u_n \to 0$ in norm, then
$Tx = x$.
