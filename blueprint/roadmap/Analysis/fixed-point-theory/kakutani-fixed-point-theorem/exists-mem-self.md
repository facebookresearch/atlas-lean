# Kakutani fixed-point theorem

Let $K$ be a nonempty compact convex subset of a finite-dimensional real normed space $E$, and let $\Phi$ assign
to each $x \in K$ a set $\Phi(x) \subseteq K$ that is nonempty and convex. Suppose the graph
$\{(x, y) \in E \times E : x \in K,\ y \in \Phi(x)\}$ is closed. Then $\Phi$ has a fixed point: some $x \in K$
with $x \in \Phi(x)$.

Since $K$ is compact, the closed graph says the same as: every value $\Phi(x)$ is closed and $\Phi$ is upper
hemicontinuous.
