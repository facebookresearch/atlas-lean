# Fejér means of the Fourier integral converge in L¹(ℝ)

For complex-valued $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$ with Fourier transform $\hat f(\xi) = \int f(y) e^{-iy\xi}\,dy$, the Fejér means

$$\sigma_N(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-N}^{N} \Bigl(1 - \frac{|\xi|}{N}\Bigr) \hat f(\xi) e^{ix\xi}\,d\xi$$

converge to $f$ in $L^1(\mathbb{R})$ as $N \to \infty$.
