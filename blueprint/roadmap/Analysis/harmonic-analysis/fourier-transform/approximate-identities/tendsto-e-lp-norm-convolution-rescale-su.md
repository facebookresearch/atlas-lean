# Approximate identities in L¹(ℝ)

Let $K : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a real-valued integrable kernel with $\int K = 1$ and put $K_\varepsilon(x) = \varepsilon^{-1} K(x/\varepsilon)$. Then for every complex-valued $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$, the convolution $(K_\varepsilon * f)(x) = \int K_\varepsilon(y) f(x - y)\,dy$ satisfies $\|K_\varepsilon * f - f\|_1 \to 0$ as $\varepsilon \to 0^+$.
