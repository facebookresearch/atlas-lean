# Heisenberg's uncertainty principle about an arbitrary center

Let $f$ be a complex-valued Schwartz function on $\mathbb{R}$. For every $a \in \mathbb{R}$, with the position second moment measured about $a$,

$$
\Big( \int_{\mathbb{R}} |f(x)|^2\, dx \Big)^2 \le 4 \Big( \int_{\mathbb{R}} (x - a)^2 |f(x)|^2\, dx \Big) \Big( \int_{\mathbb{R}} |f'(x)|^2\, dx \Big).
$$
