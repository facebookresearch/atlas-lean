# Gressman–He–Kovač–Street–Thiele–Yung bound for the determinantal form

This settles [Lᵖ bounds for the determinantal trilinear form](determinantal-trilinear-bound.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Gressman, He, Kovač, Street, Thiele and Yung proved that the principal value of their
determinantal form exists for Schwartz functions (Section 2.1) and is bounded by $C \|f\|_p \|g\|_q \|h\|_r$,
with $C$ depending only on $p, q, r$, exactly in the range $2 < p, q, r < \infty$, $1/p + 1/q + 1/r = 1$ (Theorem 1, which also shows
failure outside it); the AIM list cites their paper as the answer.

## Sources

- AIM workshop Carleson theorems and multilinear operators (2015), open problems compiled by S. Guo, https://aimath.org/pastworkshops/multilinopsproblems.pdf, Question 6 (B. Street); resolution: P. Gressman, D. He, V. Kovač, B. Street, C. Thiele and P.-L. Yung, On a trilinear singular integral form with determinantal kernel, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 144 (2016), 3465-3477, arXiv:1510.01069, Theorem 1 for the form (1.1): |Λ(f, g, h)| ≲_\{p,q,r\} ‖f‖_p ‖g‖_q ‖h‖_r whenever 2 < p, q, r < ∞ and 1/p + 1/q + 1/r = 1, and the a priori estimate fails for every other 1 ≤ p, q, r ≤ ∞; Section 2.1: the principal value (limit of the integrals over |det[1 1 1; x y z]| > ε) exists for Schwartz f, g, h; Section 3.2: det[1 1 1; x y z] = 3 det(x y) when x + y + z = 0; the AIM list itself cites Gressman et al. as the answer, https://doi.org/10.1090/proc/13007.
