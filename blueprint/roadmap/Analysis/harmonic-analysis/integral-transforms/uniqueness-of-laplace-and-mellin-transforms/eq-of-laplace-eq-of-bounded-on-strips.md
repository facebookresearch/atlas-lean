# Uniqueness of the Laplace transform

Let $g_1, g_2 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be continuous, let $c \in \mathbb{R}$, and let
$\Lambda : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be entire and bounded on vertical strips. Suppose that for every $s$ with
$\operatorname{Re} s > c$ the function $t \mapsto g_1(t)\, e^{s t}$ is integrable on $\mathbb{R}$ with
$\int_{\mathbb{R}} g_1(t)\, e^{s t}\, dt = \Lambda(s)$, and that for every $s$ with $\operatorname{Re} s < -c$
the function $t \mapsto g_2(t)\, e^{s t}$ is integrable with $\int_{\mathbb{R}} g_2(t)\, e^{s t}\, dt = \Lambda(s)$.
Then $g_1 = g_2$.
