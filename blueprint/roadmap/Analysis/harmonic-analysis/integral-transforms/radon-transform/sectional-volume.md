# Sectional volumes of a set

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, a direction $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the sectional volume $A_K(\xi, t) \in [0, +\infty]$ is the Euclidean Hausdorff measure, of the natural-number dimension $n - 1$ (dimension $0$ when $n = 0$), of $K \cap \{x : \langle x, \xi\rangle = t\}$. For $\xi \ne 0$ the level set is a hyperplane, and for a unit $\xi$ this is the $(n-1)$-dimensional volume of the section of $K$ by the hyperplane at signed distance $t$ from the origin.
