# The normalized transform is the inverse transform at negated frequency

For $N \ge 1$, $\Phi : \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$, the $1/N$-normalized discrete Fourier transform of $\Phi$ at $k$ equals the inverse discrete Fourier transform of $\Phi$ at $-k$: normalization and inversion are consistent.
