# Markov bound for high-degree Fourier weight

For every $f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and every real $t > 0$,

$$\mathbf{W}^{>t}[f] = \sum_{|S| > t} \hat f(S)^2 \le \frac{\mathbf{I}[f]}{t},$$

because $\mathbf{I}[f] = \sum_S |S|\, \hat f(S)^2$ and $|S| > t$ on the left.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), proof of Proposition 3.2 (from Theorem 2.38).
