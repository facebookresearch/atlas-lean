# Total influence is at most the degree

If $f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \{-1, 1\}$ has degree at most $d$, then $\mathbf{I}[f] \le d$: in
$\mathbf{I}[f] = \sum_S |S|\, \hat f(S)^2$ every nonzero term has $|S| \le d$, and
$\sum_S \hat f(S)^2 = 1$ by Parseval.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Fact 3.7.
