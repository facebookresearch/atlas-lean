# Symmetrization evaluated on the Boolean cube

For a real polynomial $p$ in $n$ variables, let $\operatorname{sym}(p) = \frac{1}{n!}\sum_{\sigma \in S_n} p(X_{\sigma(1)}, \dots, X_{\sigma(n)})$. For every $x \in \{0, 1\}^n$ (true read as $1$, false as $0$), $\operatorname{sym}(p)(x) = \frac{1}{n!}\sum_{\sigma \in S_n} p(x_{\sigma(1)}, \dots, x_{\sigma(n)})$.
