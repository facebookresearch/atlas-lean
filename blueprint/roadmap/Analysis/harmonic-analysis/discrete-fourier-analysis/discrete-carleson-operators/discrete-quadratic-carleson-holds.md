# Krause–Roos theorem on the discrete quadratic Carleson operator

Krause and Roos proved `ℓ²(ℤⁿ)` bounds for `sup_λ |∑_\{y ≠ 0\} f(x - y) e(λ|y|^\{2d\})
K(y)|`; the case `n = d = 1`, `K(y) = 1/y` (`Ω = sign`, mean zero on `S⁰`) is this question, which
they credit to Lillian Pierce at the 2015 AIM workshop.

## Sources

- AIM workshop Carleson theorems and multilinear operators (2015), open problems compiled by S. Guo, https://aimath.org/pastworkshops/multilinopsproblems.pdf, Question 8 (T. Anderson, L. Pierce); resolution: B. Krause and J. Roos, Discrete analogues of maximally modulated singular integrals of Stein-Wainger type, J. Eur. Math. Soc. 24 (2022), 3183-3213, arXiv:1907.00405, Theorem 1.1: sup_\{λ∈R\} |Σ_\{y∈Z^n∖\{0\}\} f(x - y) e(λ|y|^\{2d\}) K(y)| is bounded on ℓ^2(Z^n) for a homogeneous Calderón-Zygmund kernel K(x) = Ω(x)/|x|^n (Ω smooth, degree 0, mean zero on the sphere); n = d = 1, K(y) = 1/y is the case Lillian Pierce asked at the 2015 AIM workshop, https://doi.org/10.4171/jems/1160.
