# ℓ² boundedness of the discrete quadratic Carleson operator

The AIM question (Carleson theorems and multilinear operators, Question 8, T. Anderson and L. Pierce): the
discrete quadratic Carleson operator $f \mapsto \sup_t \bigl|\sum_{m \ne 0} f(n - m)\, e^{i t m^2} / m\bigr|$ is bounded
on $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$, stated on finitely supported $f$ with the supremum linearized (an arbitrary
$t = t(n)$ for each $n$) and over the finite windows $|n| \le N$.

Its resolution: [Krause–Roos theorem on the discrete quadratic Carleson operator](discrete-quadratic-carleson-holds.md).
