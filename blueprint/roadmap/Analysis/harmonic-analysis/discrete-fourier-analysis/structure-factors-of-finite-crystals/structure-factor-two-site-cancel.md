# Destructive interference of a two-site motif

For finitely many sites with complex amplitudes $A_i$ at positions $p_i \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ ($N \ge 1$), the structure factor at the wavevector index $k \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ is $S(k) = \sum_i A_i\, e^{-2\pi i\, k p_i / N}$. For two equal amplitudes $a$ at positions $0$ and $1$ in $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, $S(1) = 0$.
