# Finite structure factor

For finitely many sites with complex amplitudes $A_i$ at positions $p_i \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$
($N \ge 1$), the structure factor at the wavevector index $k \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ is
$S(k) = \sum_i A_i\, e^{-2\pi i\, k p_i / N}$. Here the product $k p_i$ is taken in
$\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$, and any integer representative of it may be used in the exponential. The
kernel has the negative exponent, as in the discrete Fourier transform on $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$
and in Kittel's structure factor $\sum_j f_j e^{-i G \cdot r_j}$; some texts use the opposite sign.
