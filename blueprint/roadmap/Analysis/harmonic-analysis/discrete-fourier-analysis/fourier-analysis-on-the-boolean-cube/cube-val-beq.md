# Signs multiply under the cube product

For Boolean values $a, b$, with signs $\sigma(\mathrm{true}) = 1$ and $\sigma(\mathrm{false}) = -1$, the
sign of "$a$ equals $b$" is $\sigma(a)\,\sigma(b)$. This is the coordinate case of the cube
product: $(x y)_i = x_i y_i$.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Section 1.2.
