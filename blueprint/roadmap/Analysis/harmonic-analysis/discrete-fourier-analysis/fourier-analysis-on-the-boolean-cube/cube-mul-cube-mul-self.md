# Every point is its own inverse

For all $x, y \in \{-1, 1\}^n$, $(x y)\, y = x$: under the coordinatewise product every point
$y$ satisfies $y y = (1, \dots, 1)$, as $y + y = 0$ in $\mathbb{F}_2^n$.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Sections 1.2 and 1.5.
