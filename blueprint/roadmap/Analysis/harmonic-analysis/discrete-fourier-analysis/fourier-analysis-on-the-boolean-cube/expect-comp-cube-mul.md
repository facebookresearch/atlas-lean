# Translation-invariance of averages

For every $y \in \{-1, 1\}^n$ and every $F : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$,
$\mathbb{E}_x[F(x y)] = \mathbb{E}_x[F(x)]$ for uniform $x$: multiplication by $y$ is a bijection of
the cube, so $x y$ is again uniform.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Section 1.5 (used in the proof of Theorem 1.27).
