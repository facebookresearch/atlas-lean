# Product on the Boolean cube

For points $x, y$ of the Boolean cube $\{-1, 1\}^n$, the product $x y$ is taken coordinatewise:
$(x y)_i = x_i y_i$. With Boolean coordinates read as signs ($\mathrm{true} = 1$,
$\mathrm{false} = -1$), the product of two coordinates is the Boolean value "$x_i$ equals $y_i$".
Under the identification of $\{-1, 1\}$ with $\mathbb{F}_2$, this is addition in
$\mathbb{F}_2^n$ written multiplicatively; every point is its own inverse.
