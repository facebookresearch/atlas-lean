# The cube product is commutative

For all $x, y \in \{-1, 1\}^n$, $x y = y x$, where $(x y)_i = x_i y_i$ is the coordinatewise
product ($\mathbb{F}_2^n$ written multiplicatively).

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Sections 1.2 and 1.5.
