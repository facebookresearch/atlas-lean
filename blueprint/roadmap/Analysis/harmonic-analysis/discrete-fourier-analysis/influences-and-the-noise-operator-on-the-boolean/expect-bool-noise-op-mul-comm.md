# The noise operator is self-adjoint

For all $f, g : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and every real $\rho$,
$\mathbb{E}[T_\rho f \cdot g] = \mathbb{E}[f \cdot T_\rho g]$: in the Fourier basis both sides
equal $\sum_S \rho^{|S|} \hat f(S) \hat g(S)$.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Section 2.4.
