# Noise stability at $\rho = 1$

For every $f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $\mathrm{Stab}_1[f] = \mathbb{E}[f^2]$: $T_1$ is the
identity, so a $1$-correlated copy of $x$ is $x$ itself.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Section 2.4.
