# Plancherel formula for the noise operator

For all $f, g : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and every real $\rho$,

$$\mathbb{E}[f \cdot T_\rho g] = \sum_S \rho^{|S|}\, \hat f(S)\, \hat g(S),$$

from $\widehat{T_\rho g}(S) = \rho^{|S|} \hat g(S)$ and Plancherel's Theorem.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Proposition 2.47 and Plancherel's Theorem (Section 1.4).
