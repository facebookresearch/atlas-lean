# Sums over paths split along the first index

For any $g \ge 0$ on the paths of length $m + 1$, $\sum_{p} g(p) = \sum_{\lambda \in \Lambda} \sum_{q} g(\lambda, q)$, where $q$ ranges over the paths of length $m$ and $(\lambda, q)$ prepends $\lambda$. Sums are in $[0, \infty]$, so no summability condition is needed even when $\Lambda$ is infinite.
