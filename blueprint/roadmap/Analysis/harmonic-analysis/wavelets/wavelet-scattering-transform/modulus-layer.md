# Wavelet modulus operator

$U[2^j] h = |h \star \psi_{2^j}|$, the wavelet modulus operator of S. Mallat, *Group invariant scattering*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 65 (2012), 1331–1398 (arXiv:1101.2286), Definition 2.2, in dimension $d = 1$, with convolution $(h \star g)(x) = \int h(u)\, g(x - u)\, du$ (equation (6); the integral is read as $0$ when the integrand is not integrable) and values viewed in $\mathbb{C}$. The modulus removes the phase of the wavelet coefficient and maps its energy to lower frequencies.
