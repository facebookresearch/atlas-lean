# Energy conservation of the windowed scattering transform

If $\psi$ is an [admissible scattering wavelet](is-admissible.md) with low-pass $\varphi$, then for every $J \in \mathbb{Z}$ and every real-valued $f \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$,

$$\sum_{p \in P_J} \|S_J[p] f\|^2 = \|f\|^2,$$

where $P_J$ is the set of all finite paths of indices $2^j > 2^{-J}$ (S. Mallat, *Group invariant scattering*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 65 (2012), 1331–1398 (arXiv:1101.2286), Theorem 2.6, equation (32), in dimension $d = 1$). Squared norms are written as lower Lebesgue integrals $\int |g|^2$ in $[0, \infty]$. The paper's statement is for $L^2(\mathbb{R}^d)$ and a finite rotation group; this is the one-dimensional case.
