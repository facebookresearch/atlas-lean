# Fourier transform in angular frequency

The Fourier transform in the convention of S. Mallat, *Group invariant scattering*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 65 (2012), 1331–1398 (arXiv:1101.2286), Notations: for $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$, $\hat f(\omega) = \int f(x)\, e^{-i x \omega}\, dx$, read as $0$ when the integrand is not integrable. In terms of the Fourier transform $\mathcal{F} f(\xi) = \int f(x)\, e^{-2\pi i x \xi}\, dx$ it is $\hat f(\omega) = \mathcal{F} f(\omega / 2\pi)$; using it lets the conditions on wavelets be stated exactly as in the paper.
