# The function $\hat\Psi$ of the admissibility condition

For $\rho : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\eta \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\hat\Psi(\omega) = |\hat\rho(\omega - \eta)|^2 - \sum_{k \ge 1} k\,\big(1 - |\hat\rho(2^{-k}(\omega - \eta))|^2\big),$$

the function of equation (29) of S. Mallat, *Group invariant scattering*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 65 (2012), 1331–1398 (arXiv:1101.2286), Theorem 2.6. Here $\hat\rho$ is the [Fourier transform in angular frequency](angular-fourier.md) of $\rho$, and the series is read as $0$ when it is not absolutely convergent (a conditionally convergent series included).
