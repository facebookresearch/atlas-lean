# Low-pass window at scale $2^J$

For $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ and $J \in \mathbb{Z}$, $\varphi_{2^J}(x) = 2^{-J} \varphi(2^{-J} x)$, the averaging window of equation (7) of S. Mallat, *Group invariant scattering*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 65 (2012), 1331–1398 (arXiv:1101.2286) in dimension $d = 1$. When $\varphi$ is an averaging window (for instance integrable, nonnegative, with integral $1$ and concentrated near $0$), $A_J f = f \star \varphi_{2^J}$ averages $f$ over a domain of size proportional to $2^J$.
