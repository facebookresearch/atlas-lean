# Scaling function of a multiresolution approximation

If $(V_j)$ is a [multiresolution approximation](is-multiresolution-approximation.md) of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$, there is a scaling function $\varphi \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ whose integer translates $\varphi(x - k)$, $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, form an orthonormal basis of $V_0$ (S. Mallat, *Multiresolution approximations and wavelet orthonormal bases of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 315 (1989), Section 2). By property (3), $\{2^{j/2}\varphi(2^j x - k)\}_k$ is then an orthonormal basis of $V_j$. Mallat obtains $\varphi$ by orthonormalizing, in the Fourier domain, the Riesz basis that property (5) provides; orthonormality of the translates is equivalent to $\sum_k |\hat\varphi(\omega + 2k\pi)|^2 = 1$. The statement asserts a Hilbert basis of $V_0$ indexed by $\mathbb{Z}$ whose elements are almost everywhere equal to the translates of some $\varphi$; it does not include the paper's uniqueness claim or normalization.
