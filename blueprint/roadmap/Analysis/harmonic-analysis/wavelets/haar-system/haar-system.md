# Haar system

For $j, k \in \mathbb{Z}$, the Haar functions are

$$H_{j,k}(x) = 2^{j/2} H(2^j x - k),$$

supported on the dyadic interval $2^{-j}[k, k+1)$ and normalized in $L^2(\mathbb{R})$ (S. Mallat, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 315 (1989), the family $\sqrt{2^j}\,\psi(2^j x - k)$ of Section 4 (after (70)) with $\psi = H$; R. A. DeVore, *Nonlinear approximation*, Acta Numerica 7 (1998), (3.41), for $j \ge 0$ and $0 \le k < 2^j$, where his indices are written $H_{k,j}$).
