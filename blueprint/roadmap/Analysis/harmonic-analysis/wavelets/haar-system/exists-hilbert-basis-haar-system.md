# The Haar wavelet basis of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$

The Haar functions $H_{j,k}(x) = 2^{j/2} H(2^j x - k)$, $(j, k) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, form an orthonormal basis of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$ (S. Mallat, *Multiresolution approximations and wavelet orthonormal bases of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 315 (1989), Section 4, after (70): the Haar basis is the wavelet orthonormal basis of the multiresolution approximation with scaling function $\mathbf{1}_{[0,1]}$). Unlike the basis of $L^2[0,1)$, no constant function is needed: the constant is not in $L^2(\mathbb{R})$, and the coarse scales $j \to -\infty$ take its place. The statement asserts a Hilbert basis of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$ indexed by $\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ whose elements are almost everywhere equal to the $H_{j,k}$.

Orthonormality is [Orthonormality of the Haar system](integral-haar-system-mul-haar-system.md); completeness is the other half.
