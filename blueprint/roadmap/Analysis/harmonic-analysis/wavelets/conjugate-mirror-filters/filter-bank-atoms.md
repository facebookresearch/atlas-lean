# Atoms of the two-channel filter bank

The atoms of the two-channel filter bank with filters $h, g$ on $\mathbb{Z}/2N$ are the $2N$ even translates $\tau_{2n} h$ (low-pass) and $\tau_{2n} g$ (high-pass), $0 \le n < N$, indexed by two copies of $\{0, \dots, N-1\}$. The analysis coefficients of $x$ are its inner products with these atoms.
