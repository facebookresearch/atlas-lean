# Conjugate mirror filters give an orthonormal two-channel filter bank

If the even translates $\tau_{2n} h$ ($0 \le n < N$) of a filter $h$ on $\mathbb{Z}/2N$ are orthonormal, then with the [mirror filter](mirror-filter.md) $g[k] = (-1)^{1-k}\overline{h[1-k]}$ the whole family $\tau_{2n} h, \tau_{2n} g$ is orthonormal, so [perfect reconstruction](perfect-reconstruction.md) applies. The even translates of $g$ have the same correlations as those of $h$ (reindex $k \mapsto 1 - 2e - k$), and each $\tau_{2m} h$ is orthogonal to each $\tau_{2n} g$ because the involution $k \mapsto c - k$ with $c$ odd changes the sign of every term of their inner product. This is the finite periodic, time-domain form of the fact that (69) makes the matrix (68) unitary in S. Mallat, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 315 (1989), Section 4.
