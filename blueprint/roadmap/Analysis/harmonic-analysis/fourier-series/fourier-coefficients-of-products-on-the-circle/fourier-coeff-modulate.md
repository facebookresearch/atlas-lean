# Multiplication by a character shifts Fourier coefficients

Let $T > 0$, let $F$ be a continuous complex function on the circle $\mathbb{R}/T\mathbb{Z}$, and let
$e_k(t) = e^{2\pi i k t / T}$. For all integers $n$ and $m$, the $m$-th Fourier coefficient of $e_{-n} F$ is the
$(m + n)$-th Fourier coefficient of $F$: $\widehat{e_{-n} F}(m) = \hat F(m + n)$.
