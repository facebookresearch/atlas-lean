# Fourier correlation series of a continuous function on the circle

Let $T > 0$ and let $F$ be a continuous complex function on the circle $\mathbb{R}/T\mathbb{Z}$. For every integer $n$,
the series $\sum_{m \in \mathbb{Z}} \overline{\hat F(m)}\, \hat F(m + n)$ converges, and its sum is the $n$-th Fourier
coefficient of $|F|^2 = \overline{F} F$.
