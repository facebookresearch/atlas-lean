# Complex kinetic-energy cancellation after projection

Let $S$ be finite and symmetric, and let $p$ be Hermitian with $\operatorname{supp}(p)\subseteq S$. Then

$$\sum_{k\in S}\frac{\overline{p_k}(P_SC(p))_k}{|k|^2}=0.$$

Division by zero is zero. The cancellation itself does not require zero mean; recovering all of $p$ as the curl of the reconstructed velocity separately requires $p_0=0$. No additive closure of $S$ is assumed.
