# Transport as an actual physical Jacobian

Let $L_p(x)=\sum_k p_ke^{ik\cdot x}$ be the physical periodic lift of a finite polynomial. For every $x\in\mathbb R^2$,

$$L_{C(p)}(x)=\partial_0L_{\psi(p)}(x)\,\partial_1L_p(x)-\partial_1L_{\psi(p)}(x)\,\partial_0L_p(x).$$

These are actual real-variable coordinate derivatives of complex-valued functions, formed by varying one coordinate at a time. Their multiplier is $ik_j$, appropriate to period $2\pi$.
