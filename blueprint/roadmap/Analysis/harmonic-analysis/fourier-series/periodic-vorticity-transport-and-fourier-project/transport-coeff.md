# Exact vorticity convection coefficient formula

For any finite complex polynomial $p$ and frequency $k$,

$$C(p)_k=\sum_{l\in\operatorname{supp}(p)}\frac{\det(l,k)}{|l|^2}\,p_l p_{k-l}.$$

Division by zero is zero. Both ordered interactions are included by the finite sum; the convective evolution uses its negative. No reality or zero-mean assumption is needed for this coefficient identity.
