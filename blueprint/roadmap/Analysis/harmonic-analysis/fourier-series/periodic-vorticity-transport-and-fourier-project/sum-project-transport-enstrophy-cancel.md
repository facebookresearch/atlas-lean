# Complex enstrophy cancellation after projection

Let $S$ be a finite symmetric frequency set, and let $p$ have Hermitian coefficients with $\operatorname{supp}(p)\subseteq S$. Then

$$\sum_{k\in S}\overline{p_k}(P_SC(p))_k=0.$$

This is an exact complex identity. Neither zero mean nor additive closure of $S$ is required.
