# Erdős Problem 225 on the L¹ norm of real-rooted trigonometric polynomials

Erdős Problem 225: if the trigonometric polynomial `f(θ) = ∑_{k=0}^n c_k e^{ikθ}` has roots and
all of them are real, and `max_{θ ∈ [0, 2π]} |f(θ)| = 1`, then `∫_0^{2π} |f(θ)| dθ ≤ 4`.

Its resolution: [Saff–Sheil-Small theorem on real-rooted trigonometric polynomials](real-rooted-trig-polynomial-integral-le-2.md).
