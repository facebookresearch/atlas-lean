# Symmetric partial sums of a periodic function are the partial sums of its lift

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be continuous and $2\pi$-periodic, and let $F$ be its [lift to the circle](../absolutely-convergent-fourier-series/lift-periodic-continuous-map.md) $\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$. For every natural number $N$ and every real $x$, the [symmetric Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md) of $f$ at $x$ equals the [partial Fourier sum](../fej-r-kernel-and-fej-r-s-theorem/partial-fourier-sum.md) of $F$ at the class of $x$:

$$S_N(f)(x) = S_N F(x).$$
