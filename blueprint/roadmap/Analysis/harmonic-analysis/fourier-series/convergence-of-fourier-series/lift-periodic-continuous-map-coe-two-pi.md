# A periodic function agrees with its lift to the circle at every real point

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be continuous and $2\pi$-periodic, and let $F$ be its [lift to the circle](../absolutely-convergent-fourier-series/lift-periodic-continuous-map.md) $\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$. Then $F$ takes the value $f(x)$ at the class of every real number $x$, not only at the classes of $x \in (0, 2\pi]$.
