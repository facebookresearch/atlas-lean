# Cesàro means of a periodic function are the Cesàro–Fourier means of its lift

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be continuous and $2\pi$-periodic, and let $F$ be its [lift to the circle](../absolutely-convergent-fourier-series/lift-periodic-continuous-map.md) $\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$. For every natural number $N$ and every real $x$, the [Cesàro mean](fourier-cesaro-mean.md) of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x$ equals the [Cesàro–Fourier mean](../fej-r-kernel-and-fej-r-s-theorem/cesaro-fourier-mean.md) of $F$ at the class of $x$:

$$\sigma_N(f)(x) = \sigma_N F(x).$$

With [the agreement of $f$ and $F$ at real points](lift-periodic-continuous-map-coe-two-pi.md), this reduces [Fejér's theorem](fejer.md) on the real line to its version on the circle.
