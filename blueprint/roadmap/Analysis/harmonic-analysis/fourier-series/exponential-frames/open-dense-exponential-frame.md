# Führ's question on exponential frames for open dense sets

Führ's question (Oberwolfach Report 10/2004, p. 532), in its exponential-frame form: for every
open dense set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ of finite measure, $L^2(A)$ admits a frame of exponentials.

Its resolution: [Nitzan–Olevskii–Ulanovskii theorem on exponential frames](open-dense-exponential-frame-holds.md).
