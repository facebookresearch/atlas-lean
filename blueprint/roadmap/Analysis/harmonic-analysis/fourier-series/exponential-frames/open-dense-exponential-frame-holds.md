# Nitzan–Olevskii–Ulanovskii theorem on exponential frames

Nitzan, Olevskii and Ulanovskii prove that `L²(S)` has a frame of
exponentials `{e^{iλt} : λ ∈ Λ}`, `Λ` discrete, for every set `S ⊆ ℝ` of finite measure, in
particular for every open dense `A` of finite measure.

## Sources

- H. Führ, A reproducing kernel without (?) discretization, in Mini-Workshop: Wavelets and Frames, Oberwolfach Reports 1 (2004), no. 10, p. 532, Question, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2004/10; resolution: S. Nitzan, A. Olevskii and A. Ulanovskii, Exponential frames on unbounded sets, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 144 (2016), no. 1, 109-118, DOI 10.1090/proc/12868, arXiv:1410.5693, Theorem 1: there are positive constants c, C such that for every set S ⊂ ℝ of finite measure there is a discrete set Λ ⊂ ℝ such that \{exp(iλt), λ ∈ Λ\} is a frame in L²(S) with frame bounds c|S| and C|S|, https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5693.
