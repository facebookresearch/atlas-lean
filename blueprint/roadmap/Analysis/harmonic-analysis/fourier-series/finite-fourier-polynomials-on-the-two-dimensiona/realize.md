# Realization by torus characters

For a finite coefficient family $p$, define $R(p)(y)=\sum_k p_k e^{2\pi i k\cdot y}$ on $(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^2$. This is a continuous complex function. The realization map is a complex algebra homomorphism: it preserves constants, sums, exact convolution products and complex scalar multiplication.
