# Recovery of finite Fourier coefficients

For every finite Fourier polynomial $p$ and every $k\in\mathbb Z^2$, its realized function has normalized Haar coefficient $\int_{(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^2}e^{-2\pi i k\cdot y}R(p)(y)\,dy=p_k$. This includes frequencies outside the support, where the coefficient is zero.
