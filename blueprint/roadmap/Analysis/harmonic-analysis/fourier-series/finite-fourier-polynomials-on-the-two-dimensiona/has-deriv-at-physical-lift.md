# Actual coordinate derivatives of Fourier polynomials

Let $p$ be a finite complex Fourier polynomial, $x\in\mathbb R^2$ and $j\in\{0,1\}$. Vary only the $j$-th coordinate, writing $x[j\leftarrow t]$. The real-variable complex-valued function $t\mapsto L_p(x[j\leftarrow t])$ is differentiable at $t=x_j$, with derivative $L_{D_jp}(x)$. Thus the actual physical derivative has multiplier $i k_j$, with no extra $2\pi$ factor.
