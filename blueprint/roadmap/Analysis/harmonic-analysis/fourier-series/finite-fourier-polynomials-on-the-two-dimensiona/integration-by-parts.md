# Integration by parts for finite Fourier polynomials

For finite complex polynomials $p,q$ and a coordinate $j\in\{0,1\}$,

$$((D_jp)q)_0=-(p(D_jq))_0.$$

Products are exact convolution and zero coefficients are normalized integrals of the realized products. This is the bilinear integration-by-parts identity; it has no implicit complex conjugation and needs no Hermitian assumption.
