# Normalized Parseval for finite Fourier polynomials

For every finite complex Fourier polynomial $p$,

$$\int_{(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^2}|R(p)(y)|^2\,dy=\sum_{k\in\operatorname{supp}(p)}|p_k|^2.$$

The measure is normalized Haar probability measure. The unnormalized physical integral over a period-$2\pi$ cell has the additional area factor $(2\pi)^2$.
