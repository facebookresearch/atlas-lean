# Conversion to TorusSobolev coefficient normalization

If $p$ is Hermitian and $u=\operatorname{Re}P(p)$, then the TorusSobolev Fourier coefficient of $u$ at $k$ is $a_k=2\pi p_k$. The coefficient $p_k$ uses normalized mean measure, whereas $a_k=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{[0,2\pi]^2}u(x)e^{-i k\cdot x}\,dx$.
