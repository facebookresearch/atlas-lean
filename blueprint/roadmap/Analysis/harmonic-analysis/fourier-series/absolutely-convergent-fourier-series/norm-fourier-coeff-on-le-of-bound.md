# Fourier coefficient bounded by the sup norm on a period

Let $a < b$, let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ and $C \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|f(x)| \le C$ for every $x \in [a, b]$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, the Fourier coefficient of $f$ on $(a, b]$,

$$
\frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b f(x)\, e^{-2\pi i n x/(b - a)}\, dx,
$$

has modulus at most $\max(C, 0)$. The integral is read as $0$ when the integrand is not integrable, for instance when $f$ is not measurable; the bound then holds trivially.
