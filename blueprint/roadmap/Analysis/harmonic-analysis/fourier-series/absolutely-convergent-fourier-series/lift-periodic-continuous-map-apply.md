# Lifted periodic function agrees with the original on a period

Let $T > 0$ and let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be continuous and $T$-periodic. For every $x \in (0, T]$, the [descended function](lift-periodic-continuous-map.md) on $\mathbb{R}/T\mathbb{Z}$ takes the value $f(x)$ at the class of $x$.
