# Fourier coefficients of the lifted periodic function

For $T > 0$ and $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$, write $\hat g(n) = \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T g(x)\, e^{-2\pi i n x/T}\, dx$, $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, for the Fourier coefficients of $g$ on the period $(0, T]$ (an integral whose integrand is not integrable being read as $0$). If $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ is continuous and $T$-periodic, then for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ the $n$-th Fourier coefficient of the [descended function](lift-periodic-continuous-map.md) on $\mathbb{R}/T\mathbb{Z}$ is $\hat f(n)$.
