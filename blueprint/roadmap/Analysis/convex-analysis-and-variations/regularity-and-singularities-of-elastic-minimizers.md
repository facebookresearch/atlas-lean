# Regularity and singularities of elastic energy minimizers

This node records a research direction: when is an energy-minimizing deformation in nonlinear elasticity smooth, and when must it be singular (for example by cavitation)? Existence of minimizers does not imply regularity.

## What would settle it

Fix a class of stored-energy functions (for example smooth and strictly polyconvex), the domain and boundary regime, and prove a regularity theorem for minimizers under explicit hypotheses, or a precise description of their singular sets. A statement that every minimizer is smooth is false, since singular minimizers are known for some energies.

## Status

No cited source poses this question in the form above; it is a research direction motivated by the cited results. This page does not certify the status or novelty of any particular formulation. The status evidence for this direction is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Ball poses, as Problem 3, when the minimizer given by the polyconvex existence theorem is smooth, notes that additional hypotheses on the stored-energy function are needed, and records that the problem is unsolved even in the simplest special cases; the only situation in which smoothness seems to have been proved is the pure displacement problem with small boundary displacements from a stress-free state.

This page records a research direction and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
