# Successor equation of varying-step projected gradient descent

For a real inner product space $E$, a map $\operatorname{proj} : E \to E$, gradients $g_t : E \to E$, step sizes $\eta_t \in \mathbb{R}$ and a start $x_0$, the varying-step iterates satisfy $x_{t+1} = \operatorname{proj}\big(x_t - \eta_t\, g_t(x_t)\big)$ for every $t$.
