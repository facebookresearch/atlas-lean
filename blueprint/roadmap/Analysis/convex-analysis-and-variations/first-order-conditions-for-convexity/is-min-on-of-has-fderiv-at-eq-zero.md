# Stationary points of convex functions are global minimizers

Let $E$ be a real normed space, let $f \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$ be convex on $s \subseteq E$, and let $a \in s$ with $Df(a) = 0$. Then $f(a) \le f(x)$ for every $x \in s$.

## Sources

- S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, Cambridge University Press, 2004 (https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/), §4.2.3, eq. (4.22) (unconstrained problems).
