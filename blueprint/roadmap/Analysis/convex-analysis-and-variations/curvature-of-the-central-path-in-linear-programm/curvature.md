# Sonnevend–Stoer–Zhao curvature of the central path

The Sonnevend–Stoer–Zhao curvature $\kappa(\nu) = \|\nu \, \dot x(\nu) \circ \dot s(\nu)\|^{1/2}$ of a path
$\nu \mapsto (x(\nu), s(\nu))$ in $\mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^n$, with the Euclidean norm (as in the report
and Monteiro–Tsuchiya §1 and (7)) and $\circ$ the componentwise product. The dots are Mathlib's total
derivative in $\nu$: the derivative where the path is differentiable at $\nu$ and $0$ where it is not. So the
formula is defined for arbitrary paths, also off the smooth central path, and has its classical meaning
where the path is differentiable, as the central path is under the standing assumptions.
