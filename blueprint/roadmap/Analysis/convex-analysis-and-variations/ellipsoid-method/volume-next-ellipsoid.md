# Exact volume factor of a central-cut step

For $n \ge 2$, a positive definite $A$, a centre $a$ and $c \ne 0$,

$$
\mathrm{vol}\, E(a', A') = \frac{n}{n+1}\Bigl(\frac{n^2}{n^2-1}\Bigr)^{(n-1)/2}\, \mathrm{vol}\, E(a, A).
$$

By the [volume formula](volume-ellipsoid.md) both volumes are $\sqrt{\det}$ times the volume of the unit ball; [the determinant after a step](det-next-matrix.md) and [the square root of the determinant ratio](sqrt-det-ratio-eq-volume-ratio.md) give the factor. $A'$ is positive definite by [the lemma on the central-cut matrix](next-matrix-pos-def.md).
