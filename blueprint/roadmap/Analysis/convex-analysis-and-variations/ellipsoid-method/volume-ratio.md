# Volume factor of a central-cut ellipsoid step

In dimension $n$, one central-cut step multiplies the volume of the ellipsoid by

$$
\rho_n = \frac{n}{n+1}\Bigl(\frac{n^2}{n^2-1}\Bigr)^{(n-1)/2} .
$$

The first factor is the shrinking along the cut direction, the second the stretching in the $n - 1$ orthogonal directions.
