# Admissible densities of given mass

For $m \ge 0$, an admissible density of mass $m$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$ is a measurable $\rho$ with
$0 \le \rho \le 1$ everywhere and $\int \rho = m$. The definition is stated for every real $m$, comparing the
lower integral of $\rho$ with the nonnegative part $\max(m, 0)$ of $m$: for $m < 0$ it requires $\int \rho = 0$,
that is $\rho = 0$ almost everywhere, rather than the unsatisfiable $\int \rho = m$.
