# Minimizers of the interaction energy

$\rho$ is an [admissible density](is-admissible.md) of mass $m$ and $E_\alpha[\rho] \le E_\alpha[\sigma]$ for
every admissible density $\sigma$ of mass $m$, with $E_\alpha$ the [interaction energy](energy.md). For
$m \ge 0$ this is the classical constrained minimization; for $m < 0$ the admissible densities are the
measurable functions with values in $[0, 1]$ everywhere that vanish almost everywhere; all of them have energy $0$, so they are
exactly the minimizers.
