# Frank–Lieb theorem: large-mass minimizers are balls

This settles [Frank–Lieb question on minimizers of large mass](large-mass-minimizers-are-balls.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Frank and Lieb proved that for $0 < \lambda < N - 1$ and $\alpha > 0$, for all large masses $m$ the only
minimizers of $\frac12 \iint \rho(x)(|x - y|^\alpha + |x - y|^{-\lambda}) \rho(y) \, dx \, dy$ under
$0 \le \rho \le 1$, $\int \rho = m$ on $\mathbb{R}^N$ are characteristic functions of balls, up to null sets;
here $N = 3$, $\lambda = 1$. Before, only $\alpha = 2$ and $2 < \alpha \le 4$ were known.

## Sources

- R. L. Frank (with E. H. Lieb), in Many-Body Quantum Systems and Effective Theories, Oberwolfach Reports 13 (2016), no. 3, Report 43/2016, p. 2506, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2016/43; resolution: R. L. Frank and E. H. Lieb, Proof of spherical flocking based on quantitative rearrangement inequalities, Ann. Sc. Norm. Super. Pisa Cl. Sci. (5) 22 (2021), 1241-1263, Theorem 1.1 (Theorem 1 in arXiv:1909.04595): for $0 < \lambda < N - 1$ and $\alpha > 0$ there is $m_{N,\alpha,\lambda} < \infty$ such that for all $m > m_{N,\alpha,\lambda}$ the only minimizers for $E_{\alpha,\lambda}(m)$ are characteristic functions of balls (up to sets of measure zero); here $N = 3$, $\lambda = 1$, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04595.
