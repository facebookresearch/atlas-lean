# Frank–Lieb theorem: large-mass minimizers are balls

Frank and Lieb proved that for `0 < λ < N - 1` and `α > 0`, for all large
masses `m` the only minimizers of `½ ∬ ρ(x)(|x - y|^α + |x - y|^{-λ}) ρ(y) dx dy` under `0 ≤ ρ ≤ 1`,
`∫ ρ = m` on `ℝ^N` are characteristic functions of balls, up to null sets; here `N = 3`, `λ = 1`. Before, only `α = 2` and `2 < α ≤ 4` were known.

## Sources

- R. L. Frank (with E. H. Lieb), in Many-Body Quantum Systems and Effective Theories, Oberwolfach Reports 13 (2016), no. 43, p. 2506, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2016/43; resolution: R. L. Frank and E. H. Lieb, Proof of spherical flocking based on quantitative rearrangement inequalities, Ann. Sc. Norm. Super. Pisa Cl. Sci. (2021), arXiv:1909.04595, Theorem 1: for 0 < λ < N - 1 and α > 0 there is m_\{N,α,λ\} < ∞ such that for all m > m_\{N,α,λ\} the only minimizers for E_\{α,λ\}(m) are characteristic functions of balls (up to sets of measure zero); here N = 3, λ = 1, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04595.
