# Subdifferential of a convex function

Let $X$ be a real normed space with dual $X^*$ and $f : X \to (-\infty, \infty]$ a convex function, finite at $x$. The *subdifferential* of $f$ at $x$ is

$$\partial f(x) = \{x^* \in X^* : f(y) \ge f(x) + \langle x^*, y - x \rangle \text{ for all } y \in X\}.$$

Its elements are the *subgradients* of $f$ at $x$.
