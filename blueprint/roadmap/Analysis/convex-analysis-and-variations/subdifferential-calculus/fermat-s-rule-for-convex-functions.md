# Fermat's rule for convex functions

Let $X$ be a real normed space and $f : X \to (-\infty, \infty]$ a convex function, finite at $x$. Then $x$ minimizes $f$ over $X$ if and only if $0 \in \partial f(x)$: taking $x^* = 0$ in the definition of the subdifferential says exactly that $f(y) \ge f(x)$ for all $y$.
