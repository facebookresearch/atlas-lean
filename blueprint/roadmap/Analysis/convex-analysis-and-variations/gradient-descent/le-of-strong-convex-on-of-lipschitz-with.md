# Strong convexity modulus is at most the smoothness constant

Let $E$ be a nontrivial real Hilbert space and let $f \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, $m$-strongly convex, with $L$-Lipschitz gradient ($L \ge 0$). Then $m \le L$.

## Sources

- S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, Cambridge University Press, 2004 (https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/), §9.1.2 (the bounds $mI \preceq \nabla^2 f \preceq MI$, whose ratio $M/m$ bounds the condition number).
