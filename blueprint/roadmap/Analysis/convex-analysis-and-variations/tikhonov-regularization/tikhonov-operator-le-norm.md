# Tikhonov normal operator is bounded below by the regularization parameter

Let $H$ and $K$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $A : H \to K$ a
bounded linear operator and $\Lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. Then
$\Lambda \|x\| \le \|(A^* A + \Lambda I) x\|$ for every $x \in H$.
