# Inner product of the Tikhonov normal operator with its argument

Let $H$ and $K$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $A : H \to K$ a
bounded linear operator and $\Lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. Then for every $x \in H$,
$\langle (A^* A + \Lambda I) x, x\rangle = \|Ax\|^2 + \Lambda \|x\|^2$ (a real number).
