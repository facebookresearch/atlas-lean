# Tikhonov solutions tend to zero as the parameter grows

Let $H$ and $K$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $A : H \to K$ a
bounded linear operator and $b \in K$. Let $\Lambda_n > 0$ with $\Lambda_n \to \infty$, and let
$x_n \in H$ satisfy $(A^* A + \Lambda_n I) x_n = A^* b$. Then $x_n \to 0$.
