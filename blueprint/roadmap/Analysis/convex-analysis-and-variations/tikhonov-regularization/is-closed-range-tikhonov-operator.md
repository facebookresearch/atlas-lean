# Tikhonov normal operator has closed range

Let $H$ and $K$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $A : H \to K$ a
bounded linear operator and $\Lambda > 0$. Then the range of the [normal
operator](tikhonov-operator.md) $A^* A + \Lambda I$ is closed, since the operator is bounded below.
