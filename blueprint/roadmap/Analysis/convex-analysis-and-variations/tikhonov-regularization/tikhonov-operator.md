# Normal operator of the Tikhonov problem

Let $H$ and $K$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $A : H \to K$ a
bounded linear operator and $\Lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. The normal operator of the Tikhonov problem
is $A^* A + \Lambda I$ on $H$, with $A^*$ the adjoint of $A$.
