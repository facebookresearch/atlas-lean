# Weak duality for linear programs

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix, $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}^n$. If $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ satisfies
$x \ge 0$ and $A x \le b$, and $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$ satisfies $y \ge 0$ and $A^{\top} y \ge c$ (all componentwise),
then $c \cdot x \le b \cdot y$.
