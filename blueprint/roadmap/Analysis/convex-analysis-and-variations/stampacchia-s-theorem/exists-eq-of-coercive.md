# Coercive bounded operators on a real Hilbert space are surjective

Let $E$ be a real Hilbert space, $A : E \to E$ a bounded linear operator and $\alpha > 0$ with
$\alpha \|u\|^2 \le \langle Au, u\rangle$ for every $u \in E$. Then for every $f \in E$ there is
$u \in E$ with $Au = f$. This is the whole-space case of Stampacchia's theorem; the Lax–Milgram
theorem is the analogous statement for bilinear forms.
