# Stampacchia's theorem, operator form

Let $E$ be a real Hilbert space, $K \subseteq E$ a nonempty closed convex set, $A : E \to E$ a
bounded linear operator and $\alpha > 0$ with $\alpha \|u\|^2 \le \langle Au, u\rangle$ for every
$u$, and $f \in E$. Then there is exactly one $u \in K$ with $\langle Au - f, v - u\rangle \ge 0$ for
every $v \in K$.
