# Time dilation preserves the ideal scaling

Let $\tau$ have derivative $\dot\tau(t) > 0$ at $t$, and let $\beta$ have derivative $\dot\beta$ at $\tau(t)$ with $\dot\beta \le e^{\alpha_{\tau(t)}}$ (the ideal scaling (2.2a)). Then $\beta \circ \tau$ has derivative $\dot\beta\,\dot\tau(t)$ at $t$ and

$$
\dot\beta\,\dot\tau(t) \le e^{\alpha_{\tau(t)} + \log\dot\tau(t)} = e^{\tilde\alpha_t},
$$

so the reparametrized parameters of [time dilation](time-dilation.md) again satisfy (2.2a). This is one direction, for (2.2a), of the equivalence that the source states for both conditions (2.2a)–(2.2b).
