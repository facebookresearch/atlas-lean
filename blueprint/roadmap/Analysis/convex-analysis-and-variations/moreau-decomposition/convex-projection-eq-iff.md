# Variational characterization of projection onto a convex cone

Let $E$ be a real inner product space and $C \subseteq E$ a convex cone containing $0$ that is
complete, and let $x \in E$ and $p \in C$. Then $p$ is the metric projection of $x$ onto $C$ if and
only if $\langle x - p, y\rangle \le 0$ for every $y \in C$ and $\langle x - p, p\rangle = 0$. The
ambient space need not be complete.
