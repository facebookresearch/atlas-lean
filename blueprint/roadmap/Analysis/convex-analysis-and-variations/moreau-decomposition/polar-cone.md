# Negative polar cone

Let $E$ be a real inner product space and $K \subseteq E$. The negative polar cone of $K$ is
$K^\circ = \{y \in E : \langle x, y\rangle \le 0 \text{ for all } x \in K\}$, the dual cone of $-K$.
It is a closed convex cone containing $0$, without completeness of the ambient space.
