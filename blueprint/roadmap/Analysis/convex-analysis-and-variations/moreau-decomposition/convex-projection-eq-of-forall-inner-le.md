# Variational characterization determines the cone projection

Let $E$ be a real inner product space and $C \subseteq E$ a complete convex cone containing $0$, and
let $x \in E$ and $p \in C$ with $\langle x - p, y\rangle \le 0$ for every $y \in C$ and
$\langle x - p, p\rangle = 0$. Then $p$ is the metric projection of $x$ onto $C$.
