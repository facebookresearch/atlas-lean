# Moreau decomposition

Let $E$ be a real Hilbert space and $C \subseteq E$ a closed convex cone containing $0$, with
[negative polar cone](polar-cone.md) $C^\circ$ and metric projections $P_C$, $P_{C^\circ}$. Then for
every $x \in E$,

$$x = P_C x + P_{C^\circ} x \qquad \text{and} \qquad \langle P_C x, P_{C^\circ} x\rangle = 0 .$$
