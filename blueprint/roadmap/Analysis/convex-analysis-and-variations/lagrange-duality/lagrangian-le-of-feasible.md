# Lagrangian lower bound

Throughout, $E$ is a real vector space, $D \subseteq E$, $\iota$ is a finite index set, and the problem is to minimize $f_0(x)$ subject to $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for $i \in \iota$, with $f_0, f_i \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$. If $\mu \ge 0$ and $x$ satisfies $f_i(x) \le 0$ for all $i$, then $L(x, \mu) \le f_0(x)$, since every term $\mu_i f_i(x)$ is nonpositive.
