# Convexity of the Lagrangian

Throughout, $E$ is a real vector space, $D \subseteq E$, $\iota$ is a finite index set, and the problem is to minimize $f_0(x)$ subject to $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for $i \in \iota$, with $f_0, f_i \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$. Here $E$ is a real normed space. If $f_0$ and every $f_i$ are convex on $D$ and $\mu \ge 0$, then $x \mapsto L(x, \mu)$ is convex on $D$.
