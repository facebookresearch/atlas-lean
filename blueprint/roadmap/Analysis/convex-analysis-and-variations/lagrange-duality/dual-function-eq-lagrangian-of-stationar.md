# The dual function at a stationary point of the Lagrangian

Throughout, $E$ is a real vector space, $D \subseteq E$, $\iota$ is a finite index set, and the problem is to minimize $f_0(x)$ subject to $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for $i \in \iota$, with $f_0, f_i \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$. Here $E$ is a real normed space. Suppose $f_0$ and every $f_i$ are convex on $D$ and differentiable at $x \in D$, and $\mu \ge 0$ satisfies $Df_0(x) + \sum_i \mu_i\, Df_i(x) = 0$. Then $x$ minimizes $L(\cdot, \mu)$ over $D$, so $g(\mu) = L(x, \mu)$.
