# Lower bound property of the dual function

Throughout, $E$ is a real vector space, $D \subseteq E$, $\iota$ is a finite index set, and the problem is to minimize $f_0(x)$ subject to $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for $i \in \iota$, with $f_0, f_i \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$. For every $\mu \ge 0$ and every feasible $x$ (that is, $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for all $i$), $g(\mu) \le f_0(x)$. No convexity is assumed.
