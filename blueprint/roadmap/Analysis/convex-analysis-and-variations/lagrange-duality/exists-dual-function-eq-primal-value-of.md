# Dual attainment under Slater's condition

Throughout, $E$ is a real vector space, $D \subseteq E$, $\iota$ is a finite index set, and the problem is to minimize $f_0(x)$ subject to $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for $i \in \iota$, with $f_0, f_i \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$. If $f_0$ and every $f_i$ are convex on $D$, Slater's condition holds, and $p^\star > -\infty$, then some $\mu \ge 0$ satisfies $g(\mu) = p^\star$: the dual optimum is attained.

## Sources

- S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, Cambridge University Press, 2004 (https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/), §5.2.3 (Slater's condition "also implies that the dual optimal value is attained when $d^\star > -\infty$").
