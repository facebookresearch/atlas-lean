# Strong duality under Slater's condition

Throughout, $E$ is a real vector space, $D \subseteq E$, $\iota$ is a finite index set, and the problem is to minimize $f_0(x)$ subject to $x \in D$ and $f_i(x) \le 0$ for $i \in \iota$, with $f_0, f_i \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$. If $f_0$ and every $f_i$ are convex on $D$ and some $\tilde x \in D$ has $f_i(\tilde x) < 0$ for all $i$ (Slater's condition), then $d^\star = p^\star$ in $[-\infty, \infty]$.

## Sources

- S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, Cambridge University Press, 2004 (https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/), §5.2.3, with proof in §5.3.2 (problems without equality constraints).
