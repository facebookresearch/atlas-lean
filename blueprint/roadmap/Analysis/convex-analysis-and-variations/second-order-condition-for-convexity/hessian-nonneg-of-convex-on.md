# Convexity implies a positive semidefinite Hessian

Let $E$ be a real normed space, let $s \subseteq E$ be open, and let $f \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$ be convex on $s$ with a Fréchet derivative $Df(z)$ at every $z \in s$. If $Df$ is differentiable at $y \in s$ with derivative $D^2 f(y)$, then $D^2 f(y)(v, v) \ge 0$ for every $v \in E$.

## Sources

- S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, Cambridge University Press, 2004 (https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/), §3.1.4 (proof left as exercise 3.8).
