# Lower bound for an example semidefinite program

Let $C = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -i \\ i & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and $E = I$. For every positive semidefinite complex $2 \times 2$ matrix $W$ with $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(A W) \le 1/2$ and $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(E W) = 1$ (that is, $W_{11} \le 1/2$ and $\operatorname{tr} W = 1$), $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(C W) \ge 1/2$.
