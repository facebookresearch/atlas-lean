# The space $\mathbb{R}^n$ with the $\ell_1$ norm

The space $\mathbb{R}^n$ with the norm $\|x\|_1 = \sum_i |x_i|$. Its dual norm is
$\|\cdot\|_\infty$; this is the norm of the $\ell_1$-setup of mirror descent on the simplex.

## Sources

- A. Nemirovski, A. Juditsky, G. Lan and A. Shapiro, *Robust stochastic approximation approach to stochastic programming*, SIAM J. Optim. 19(4) (2009), 1574–1609; PDF at <https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~brecht/cs294docs/week1/09.Nemirovski.pdf>, Section 2.3, Example ($\ell_1$-setup, $\|\cdot\|_* = \|\cdot\|_\infty$).
