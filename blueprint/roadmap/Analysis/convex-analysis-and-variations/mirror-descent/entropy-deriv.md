# Derivative of the entropy

$\omega'(x) = \sum_i (\log x_i + 1)\, e_i^*$, the gradient of the [entropy](entropy.md) $\sum_i x_i \log x_i$ at points with all $x_i > 0$, as a linear form on the [$\ell_1$ space](l1.md).

## Sources

- A. Juditsky and A. Nemirovski, *First order methods for nonsmooth convex large-scale optimization, I: general purpose methods*, in S. Sra, S. Nowozin and S. J. Wright (eds.), *Optimization for Machine Learning*, MIT Press (2011); author PDF at <https://www2.isye.gatech.edu/~nemirovs/MLOptChapterI.pdf>, Section 5.7.1, setup 2(c) (the derivative of (5.38) on the interior of the orthant).
