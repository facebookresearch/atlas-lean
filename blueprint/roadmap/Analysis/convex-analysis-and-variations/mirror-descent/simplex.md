# The probability simplex

$\Delta_n = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : x_i \ge 0,\ \sum_i x_i = 1\}$, as a subset of the [$\ell_1$ space](l1.md).

## Sources

- A. Nemirovski, A. Juditsky, G. Lan and A. Shapiro, *Robust stochastic approximation approach to stochastic programming*, SIAM J. Optim. 19(4) (2009), 1574–1609; PDF at <https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~brecht/cs294docs/week1/09.Nemirovski.pdf>, Section 2.3, Example (the standard simplex).
