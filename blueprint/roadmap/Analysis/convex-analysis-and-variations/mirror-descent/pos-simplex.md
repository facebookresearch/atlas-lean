# The relative interior of the simplex

$\Delta_n^\circ = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : x_i > 0,\ \sum_i x_i = 1\}$, the set $X^o$ of the entropy setup, where the [entropy](entropy.md) is differentiable along the simplex.

## Sources

- A. Nemirovski, A. Juditsky, G. Lan and A. Shapiro, *Robust stochastic approximation approach to stochastic programming*, SIAM J. Optim. 19(4) (2009), 1574–1609; PDF at <https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~brecht/cs294docs/week1/09.Nemirovski.pdf>, Section 2.3, Example: $X^o = \{x \in X : x > 0\}$.
