# The uniform point of the simplex

$(1/n, \ldots, 1/n)$, the minimizer of the [entropy](entropy.md) on the simplex and the starting point of entropic mirror descent.

## Sources

- A. Nemirovski, A. Juditsky, G. Lan and A. Shapiro, *Robust stochastic approximation approach to stochastic programming*, SIAM J. Optim. 19(4) (2009), 1574–1609; PDF at <https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~brecht/cs294docs/week1/09.Nemirovski.pdf>, Section 2.3, Example: $x_1 = \operatorname{argmin}_X \omega = n^{-1}(1, \ldots, 1)^T$.
