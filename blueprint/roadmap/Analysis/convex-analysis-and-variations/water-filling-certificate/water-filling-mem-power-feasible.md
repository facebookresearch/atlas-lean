# The water-filling allocation is admissible

For a finite set $\iota$ of modes with gains $\lambda$, if $\nu$ solves $S(\nu) = P$ for the water-level sum $S(\nu) = \sum_{i : \lambda_i > 0} \max(\nu - 1/\lambda_i, 0)$, then the water-filling allocation at level $\nu$ (power $\max(\nu - 1/\lambda_i, 0)$ on modes with $\lambda_i > 0$ and $0$ elsewhere) is admissible for the budget $P$.
