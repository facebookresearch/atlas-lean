# Total water-filling power

For a finite set $\iota$ of modes, gains $\lambda$ and a level $\nu$, the total power $\sum_i p_i(\nu)$ of the water-filling allocation $p_i(\nu) = \max(\nu - 1/\lambda_i, 0)$ for $\lambda_i > 0$ and $p_i(\nu) = 0$ otherwise equals the water-level sum $S(\nu) = \sum_{i : \lambda_i > 0} \max(\nu - 1/\lambda_i, 0)$.
