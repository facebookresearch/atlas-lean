# Zero power is optimal when every gain vanishes

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of modes with gains $\lambda_i = 0$ for all $i$, and let $P \ge 0$. Then the zero allocation is admissible for the budget $P$ and maximizes the sum rate $R(p) = \sum_i \frac{\log(1 + \lambda_i p_i)}{2 \log 2}$ over all admissible allocations.
