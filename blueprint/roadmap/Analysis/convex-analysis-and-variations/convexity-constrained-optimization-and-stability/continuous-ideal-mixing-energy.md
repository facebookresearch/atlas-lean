# The ideal mixing energy is continuous

The ideal mixing energy $E(x) = x \log x + (1 - x) \log(1 - x)$ of a binary mixture with composition $x$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$.

Here $\log$ is the real logarithm extended to all of $\mathbb{R}$ by $\log t = \log|t|$ for $t \ne 0$ and $\log 0 = 0$; so $0 \log 0 = 0$, and outside $[0, 1]$ the expression differs from the classical one (for example $E(-1) = 2\log 2$).
