# The ideal mixing energy is strictly convex

The ideal mixing energy $E(x) = x \log x + (1 - x) \log(1 - x)$ of a binary mixture with composition $x$ is strictly convex on $[0, 1]$, since the binary entropy is strictly concave there.

At the endpoints of $[0, 1]$ the convention $0 \log 0 = 0$ applies.
