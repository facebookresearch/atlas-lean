# Supporting line of the ideal mixing energy

The ideal mixing energy $E(x) = x \log x + (1 - x) \log(1 - x)$ of a binary mixture with composition $x$ satisfies the supporting-line inequality at its minimizer $1/2$: $E(1/2) + E'(1/2)\,(y - 1/2) \le E(y)$ for every $y \in [0, 1]$.

At the endpoints of $[0, 1]$ the convention $0 \log 0 = 0$ applies.
