# Characterization of the rational LP gap check

For a rational $m \times n$ matrix $A$, vectors $b \in \mathbb{Q}^m$, $c, x \in \mathbb{Q}^n$, $y \in \mathbb{Q}^m$ and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{Q}$, the rational LP gap check accepts exactly when $x \ge 0$, $A x \le b$, $y \ge 0$, $A^{\mathsf T} y \ge c$ (all componentwise) and $0 \le b \cdot y - c \cdot x \le \varepsilon$.
