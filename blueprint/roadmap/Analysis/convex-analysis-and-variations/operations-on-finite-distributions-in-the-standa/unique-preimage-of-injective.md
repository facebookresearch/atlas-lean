# Pushforward along an injective face map is injective

Let $f : \{0, \dots, k-1\} \to \{0, \dots, n\}$ be injective and $p$ a point of the standard simplex on $\{0, \dots, n\}$. If two points $q_1, q_2$ of the standard simplex on $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ both push forward along $f$ to $p$, then $q_1 = q_2$.
