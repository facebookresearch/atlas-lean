# A strictly positive face point has the face as support

Let $f : \{0, \dots, k-1\} \to \{0, \dots, n\}$ be injective, let $q$ be a point of the standard simplex on $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ with all weights strictly positive, and let $p$ be the point of the standard simplex on $\{0, \dots, n\}$ obtained by pushing $q$ forward along $f$. Then the image of $f$ is the support $\{i : p_i \ne 0\}$ of $p$.
