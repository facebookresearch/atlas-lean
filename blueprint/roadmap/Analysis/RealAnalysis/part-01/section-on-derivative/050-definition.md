# Definition (Section on Derivative)

Let $I$ be an interval, let $f:I \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $c\in I$. We say that $f$ is differentiable at $c$ if the limit $\lim_{x\to c} \frac{f(x)-f(c)}{x-c}$ exists.
