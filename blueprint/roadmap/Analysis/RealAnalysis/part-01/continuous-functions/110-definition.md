# Definition (Continuous Functions, Limits of Functions) (2)

Let $S\subset \mathbb{R}$ and suppose $c$ is a cluster point of $S\cap (c,\infty)$. Then, we say $f(x)$ converges to $L$ as $x\to c^{+}$ if $\forall \epsilon>0$ $\exists \delta >0$ such that if $x\in S$ and $c < x< c+\delta$ then $|f(x) -L|\lt \epsilon$.
