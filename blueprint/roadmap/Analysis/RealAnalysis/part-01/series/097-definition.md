# Definition (Series section)

Given $\{x_n\}$, the symbol $\sum_{n=1}^\infty x_n$ or $\sum x_n$ is the series associated to $\{x_n\}$. We say $\sum x_n$ converges if the sequence $\left\{s_m = \sum_{n=1}^m x_n\right\}_{m=1}^\infty$ converges. We call the terms of $\{s_m\}$ the partial sums. If $\lim_{m\to \infty} s_m = s$, we write $s = \sum x_n$ and treat $\sum x_n$ as a number.
