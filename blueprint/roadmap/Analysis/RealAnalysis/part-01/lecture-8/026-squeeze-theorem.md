# Theorem (Squeeze Theorem) (Lecture 8, Facts About Limits)

Let $\{a_n\}$, $\{b_n\}$, and $\{x_n\}$ be sequences such that $\forall n\in \mathbb{N}$, $a_n \leq x_n \leq b_k$. Suppose that $\{a_n\}$ and $\{b_n\}$ converge and $\lim_{n\to \infty}a_n = x = \lim_{n\to \infty}b_n.$ Therefore, $\{x_n\}$ converges and $\lim_{n\to \infty}x_n = x$.
