# Theorem (Real Numbers section, Lecture 4)

$\exists! r\in \mathbb{R}$ such that $r>0$ and $r^2 =2$. In other words, $\sqrt 2 \in \mathbb{R}$ but $\sqrt 2 \notin \mathbb{Q}$.
