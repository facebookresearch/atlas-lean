# Definition (Relative Maximum/Minimum) (Section on Mean Value Theorem)

Let $S \subset \mathbb{R}$ and $f:S\to \mathbb{R}$. Then, $f$ has a relative maximum at $c\in S$ if $\exists \delta>0$ such that for all $x\in S$, $|x-c|\lt \delta \implies f(x) \leq f(c).$ The definition for relative minimum is analogous.
