# Theorem (Theorem I)

We will show the following: (1) $\forall x,y\in \mathbb{R}$, $|\cos x - \cos y| \leq |x-y|$. (2) Let $c\in \mathbb{R}$. Then, for all $K\in \mathbb{N}$, $\exists y\in (c+\pi /K, c+3\pi/K)$ such that $|\cos(Kc) - \cos(Ky)| \geq 1$.
