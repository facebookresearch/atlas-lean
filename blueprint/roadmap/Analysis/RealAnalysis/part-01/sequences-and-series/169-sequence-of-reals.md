# Definition (Sequence of Reals) (Sequences and Series, Sequences and Limits) (2)

A sequence of real numbers is a function $x: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$. We denote $x(n) = x_n$ and we denote the sequence by $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$, $\{x_n\}$, or $x_1,x_2,\dots$.
