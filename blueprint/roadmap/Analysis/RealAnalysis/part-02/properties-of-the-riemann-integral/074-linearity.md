# Theorem (Linearity) (Properties of the Riemann Integral section)

If $f,g\in C([a,b])$ and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, then $\int_a^b (\alpha f + g) = \alpha \int_a^b f + \int_a^b g.$
