# Theorem II (Section on the Riemann Integral)

If $(\underline{x}, \underline{\xi})$ and $(\underline{x}', \underline{\xi}')$ are tagged partitions of $[a,b]$ such that $\underline{x}\subset \underline{x}'$, then if $f\in C([a,b])$ then $|S_f(\underline{x},\underline{\xi}) - S_f(\underline{x}',\underline{\xi}')| \leq w_f(\lVert \underline{x}\rVert) (b-a).$
