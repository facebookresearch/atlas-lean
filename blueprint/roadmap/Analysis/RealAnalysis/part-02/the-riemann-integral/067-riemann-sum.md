# Definition (Riemann sum) (The Riemann Integral section)

The Riemann sum of $f$ corresponding to $(\underline{x},\underline{\xi})$ is the number $S_f(\underline{x}, \underline{\xi}) \coloneqq \sum_{k=1}^n f(\xi_k) (x_k-x_{k-1}).$
