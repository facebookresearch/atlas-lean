# Theorem I (The Riemann Integral section)

For all $f\in C([a,b])$, $\lim_{\eta\to 0} w_f(\eta) = 0$. In other words, for all $\epsilon>0$, there exists a $\delta>0$ such that $\forall \eta < \delta$, $w_f(\eta) < \epsilon$.
