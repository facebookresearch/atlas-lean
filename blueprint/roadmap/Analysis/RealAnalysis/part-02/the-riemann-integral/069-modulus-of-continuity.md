# Definition (Modulus of Continuity) (The Riemann Integral section)

For $f\in C([a,b])$, $\eta >0$, we define the modulus of continuity $w_f(\eta) = \sup \{|f(x) - f(y)| \mid |x-y| \leq \eta\}.$
