# Theorem (Integration by Parts) (Fundamental Theorem of Calculus section) (2)

Suppose $f,g\in C([a,b])$ and $f',g'\in C([a,b])$. Then, $\int_a^b f'g = (f(b)g(b)-f(a)g(a)) - \int_a^b fg'$.
