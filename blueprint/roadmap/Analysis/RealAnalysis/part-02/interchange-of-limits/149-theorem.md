# Theorem (Interchange of Limits)

Let $\sum_{j=0}^\infty a_j(x-x_0)^j$ be a power series of radius of convergence $p\in (0,\infty]$. Then, $\forall r\in (0,p)$, $\sum_{j=0}^\infty a_j(x-x_0)^j$ converges uniformly on $[x_0-r,x_0+r]$.
