# Mountain Range

`a, b` are separated by a **mountain range** at height `ε`, radius
`r`: `f a = 0`, `f ≥ ε > 0` on the sphere `S_r(a)`, and `f b ≤ 0` for
some `b` strictly outside that sphere.
