# Directional Deriv

Given functions `v u : ℝ³ → ℝ → ℝ³`, we define the (spatial) directional derivative `u · ∇ₓ v`
of `v` in the direction of `u` by the formula: `Dₓv(x,t)[u(x,t)]`.
