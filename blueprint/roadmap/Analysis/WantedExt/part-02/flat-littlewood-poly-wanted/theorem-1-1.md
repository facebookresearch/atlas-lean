# Theorem 1 1

Statement of Theorem 1.1:

There exist constants `∆ > δ > 0` such that, for all `n ≥ 2`, there exists a Littlewood
polynomial `P(z)` of degree `n` with `δ√n ≤ |P (z)| ≤ ∆√n` for all `z ∈ ℂ` with `|z| = 1`.
