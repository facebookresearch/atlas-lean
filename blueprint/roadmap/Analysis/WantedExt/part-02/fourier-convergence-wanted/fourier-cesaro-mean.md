# Fourier Cesaro Mean

The Cesàro mean of the first `N + 1` Fourier partial sums:
`σ_N(f)(x) = (S_0(f)(x) + S_1(f)(x) + ⋯ + S_N(f)(x)) / (N + 1)`.
