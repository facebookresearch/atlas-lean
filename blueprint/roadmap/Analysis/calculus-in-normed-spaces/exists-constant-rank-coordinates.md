# Constant rank theorem in finite-dimensional normed spaces

Let $E$ and $F$ be finite-dimensional real normed spaces, $U \subseteq E$ open, $x \in U$, $n \ge 1$ (possibly $\infty$ or analytic), and $f : E \to F$ of class $C^n$ on $U$. Suppose the derivative of $f$ has rank $r$ at every point of some neighbourhood of $x$. Then there are open partial homeomorphisms
$$\varphi : E \rightharpoonup \mathbb{R}^r \times \mathbb{R}^{\dim E - r}, \qquad \psi : F \rightharpoonup \mathbb{R}^r \times \mathbb{R}^{\dim F - r},$$
with $\varphi$, $\varphi^{-1}$, $\psi$, $\psi^{-1}$ of class $C^n$ on their domains, such that

- $x$ lies in the source of $\varphi$, the source of $\varphi$ lies in $U$, $\varphi(x) = 0$ and $\psi(f(x)) = 0$;
- $f$ maps the source of $\varphi$ into the source of $\psi$;
- in these coordinates $f$ reads $\psi(f(\varphi^{-1}(p))) = (p_1, 0)$ for every $p = (p_1, p_2)$ in the target of $\varphi$;
- for every $y$ in the source of $\varphi$, $\varphi$ maps the local fibre $\{z \in \operatorname{src}\varphi : f(z) = f(y)\}$ onto the slice $\{p \in \operatorname{tgt}\varphi : p_1 = \varphi(y)_1\}$.
