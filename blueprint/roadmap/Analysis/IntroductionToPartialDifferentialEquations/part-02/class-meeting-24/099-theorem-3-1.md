# Theorem 3.1 (Class Meeting #24, Section 3 ("Solving" Burger's equation))

Let $u$ be a $C^{1}$ solution to (3.0.15), and let $(t,x)$ be a spacetime point. With $(t,x)$ fixed, assume that the implicit equation $x = p + f(p)t$ in the unknown $p$ has a unique solution. Then $u(t,x) = f(p)$.
