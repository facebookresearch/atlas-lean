# Lemma 2.0.1 (Properties of $\hat{f}$ for $f \in L^{1}$)

Suppose that $f \in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$. Then $\hat{f}$ is a bounded, continuous function and $\|\hat{f}\|_{C_{0}} \leq \|f\|_{L^{1}}$.
