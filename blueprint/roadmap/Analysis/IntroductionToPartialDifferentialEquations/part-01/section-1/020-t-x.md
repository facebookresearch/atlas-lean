# Proposition 1.0.4 (Properties of $\Gamma_{D}(t, x)$)

$\Gamma_{D}(t, x)$ is a solution to the heat equation (with $f=0$) verifying the initial conditions $\lim_{t \rightarrow 0^{+}} \Gamma_{D}(t, x)=\delta(x)$.
