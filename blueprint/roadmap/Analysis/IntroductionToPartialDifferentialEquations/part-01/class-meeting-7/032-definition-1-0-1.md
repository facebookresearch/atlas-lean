# Definition 1.0.1 (Class Meeting #7, Section 1 (The Fundamental Solution for $\Delta$ in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$))

The fundamental solution $\Phi$ corresponding to the operator $\Delta$ is $\Phi(x) \coloneqq \frac{1}{2\pi} \ln |x|$ for $n=2$, and $\Phi(x) \coloneqq -\frac{1}{\omega_{n}|x|^{n-2}}$ for $n \geq 3$, where $|x| \coloneqq \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x^{i})^{2}}$ and $\omega_{n}$ is the surface area of a unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ (e.g. $\omega_{3}=4\pi$).
