# Definition 3.0.1 (Hadamard's classification of second order scalar PDEs)

Equation $A^{\alpha \beta} \partial_{\alpha} \partial_{\beta} u + B^{\alpha} \partial_{\alpha} u + Cu = 0$ is respectively said to be elliptic, hyperbolic, or parabolic according to the following conditions on the $(1+n) \times (1+n)$ symmetric matrix $A$: All of the eigenvalues of $A$ have the same sign — elliptic; $n$ of the eigenvalues of $A$ have the same (non-zero) sign, and the remaining one has the opposite (non-zero) sign — hyperbolic; $n$ of the eigenvalues of $A$ have the same (non-zero) sign, and the remaining one is 0 — parabolic.
