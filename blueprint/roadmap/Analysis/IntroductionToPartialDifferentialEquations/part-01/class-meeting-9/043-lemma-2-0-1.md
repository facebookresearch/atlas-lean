# Lemma 2.0.1

The Green function for a ball $B_{R}(p) \subset \mathbb{R}^{3}$ is $G(x, y)=-\frac{1}{4 \pi|x-y|}+\frac{1}{4 \pi} \frac{R}{|x-p|\left|\frac{R^{2}}{|x-p|^{2}}(x-p)-(y-p)\right|}$ for $x \neq p$, and $G(p, y)=-\frac{1}{4 \pi|y-p|}+\frac{1}{4 \pi R}$. Furthermore, if $x \in B_{R}(p)$ and $\sigma \in \partial B_{R}(p)$, then $\nabla_{\hat{N}(\sigma)} G(x, \sigma)=\frac{R^{2}-|x-p|^{2}}{4 \pi R} \frac{1}{|x-\sigma|^{3}}$.
