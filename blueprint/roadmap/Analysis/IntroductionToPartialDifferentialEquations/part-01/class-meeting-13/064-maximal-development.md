# Definition 3.0.4 (Maximal development)

The maximal future development of $S$, which we denote by $\mathcal{D}^{+}(S)$, is defined to be the union of all future developments of $S$. The maximal past development $\mathcal{D}^{-}(S)$ can be analogously defined. The maximal development of $S$ is defined to be $\mathcal{D}^{+}(S) \cup \mathcal{D}^{-}(S)$.
