# Lemma 1.0.2 (The divergence of $T^{\mu \nu}$)

Let $T_{\mu \nu}$ be the energy-momentum tensor. Then $\partial_{\mu} T^{\mu \nu}=(\square_{m} \phi)(m^{-1})^{\nu \alpha} \partial_{\alpha} \phi$. In particular, if $\phi$ is a solution to $\square_{m} \phi = 0$, then $\partial_{\mu} T^{\mu \nu}=0$.
