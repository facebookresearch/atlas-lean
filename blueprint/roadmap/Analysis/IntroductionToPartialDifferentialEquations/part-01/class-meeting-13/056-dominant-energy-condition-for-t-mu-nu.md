# Lemma 1.0.1 (Dominant Energy Condition for $T_{\mu \nu}$)

$T(X, Y) \coloneqq T_{\alpha \beta} X^{\alpha} Y^{\beta} \geq 0$ if $X, Y$ are both timelike and future-directed or timelike and past-directed.
