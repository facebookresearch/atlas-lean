# Corollary 1.0.3

We have that $\partial_{\mu}({}^{(X)} J^{\mu})=T^{\alpha \beta} {}^{(X)} \pi_{\alpha \beta}$, where ${}^{(X)} \pi_{\mu \nu} \coloneqq \frac{1}{2}(\partial_{\mu} X_{\nu}+\partial_{\nu} X_{\mu})$ is called the deformation tensor of $X$.
