# Definition 3.0.3 (Development)

Let $S \subset \{(t,x) \mid t=0\}$ be a set. Assume that we know the initial data $\phi(0,x)=f(x)$, $\partial_{t} \phi(0,x)=g(x)$ for the wave equation, but only for $x \in S$. Then a future development $\Omega$ of $S$ is defined to be a future region of spacetime $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^{1+n} \cap \{(t,x) \mid t \geq 0\}$ on which the solution $\phi(t,x)$ to (1.0.2) is uniquely determined by the initial data on $S$. A past development $\mathcal{D}^{-}(S)$ can be analogously defined (replace $t \geq 0$ with $t \leq 0$ in the previous definition).
