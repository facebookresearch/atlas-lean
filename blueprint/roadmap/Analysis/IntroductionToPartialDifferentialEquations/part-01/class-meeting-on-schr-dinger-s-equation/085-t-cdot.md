# Proposition 2.0.3 (The behavior of $K(t, \cdot) * \phi(\cdot)$ as $t \downarrow 0$)

Let $\phi \in C_{c}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$. Then $$\lim_{t \rightarrow 0^{+}} \frac{1}{(2 \pi i t)^{n / 2}} \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}} e^{i \frac{|x-y|^{2}}{2 t}} \phi(y) d^{n} y=\phi(x).$$
