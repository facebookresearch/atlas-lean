# Proposition 2.0.4 (Preservation of $L^{2}$ norm)

Under the assumptions of Theorem 2.1, we have that $$\|\psi(t, \cdot)\|_{L^{2}}=\|\phi\|_{L^{2}},$$ where the $L^{2}$ norm on the left-hand side of the above is taken over the spatial variables only. In particular, if $\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\phi(x)|^{2} d^{n} x=1$, then $\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\psi(t, x)|^{2} d^{n} x=1$ holds for all $t \geq 0$.
