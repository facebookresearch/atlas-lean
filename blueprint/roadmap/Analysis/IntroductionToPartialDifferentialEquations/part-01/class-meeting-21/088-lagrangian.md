# Definition 1.0.1 (Lagrangian)

A Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}$ is a function of $\phi$ and $\nabla \phi$ (and sometimes the spacetime coordinates $x$ and perhaps other quantities too). We indicate the dependence of $\mathcal{L}$ on e.g. $\phi$ and $\nabla \phi$ by writing $\mathcal{L}(\phi, \nabla \phi)$.
