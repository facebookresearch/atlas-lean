# Theorem 4.1 (Harnack's inequality)

Let $u$ be harmonic and non-negative in the ball $B_{R}(0) \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$. Then for any $x \in B_{R}(0)$, we have that $\frac{R^{n-2}(R-|x|)}{(R+|x|)^{n-1}} u(0) \leq u(x) \leq \frac{R^{n-2}(R+|x|)}{(R-|x|)^{n-1}} u(0)$.
