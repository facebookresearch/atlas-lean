# Theorem 4.1 (Fourier inversion theorem)

Suppose that $f: \mathbb{R}^{n} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is a continuous function, that $f \in L^{1}$, and that $\hat{f} \in L^{1}$. Then $(\hat{f})^{\vee} = (f^{\vee})^{\wedge} = f$. That is, the operators $\wedge$ and $\vee$ are inverses of each other.
