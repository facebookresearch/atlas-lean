# Lemma 4.0.4 (Interaction of the Fourier transform with the $L^{2}$ inner product)

Assume that $f, g \in L^{1}$. Then $\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}} \hat{f}(x) g(x) d^{n} x = \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}} f(x) \hat{g}(x) d^{n} x$. Alternatively, in terms of the complex $L^{2}$ inner product, we have that $\langle\hat{f}, g\rangle = \langle f, g^{\vee}\rangle$.
