# Corollary 4.0.4 (Liouville's theorem)

Suppose that $u \in C^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ is harmonic on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$. Suppose there exists a constant $M$ such that $u(x) \geq M$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$, or such that $u(x) \leq M$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$. Then $u$ is constant.
