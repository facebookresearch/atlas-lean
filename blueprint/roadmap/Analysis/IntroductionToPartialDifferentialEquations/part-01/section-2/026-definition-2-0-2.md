# Definition 2.0.2 (Section 2) (1)

We define the total thermal energy $\mathcal{T}(t)$ at time $t$ associated to $u(t, x)$ by $\mathcal{T}(t) \coloneqq \int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}} u(t, x) d^{n} x$.
