# Theorem 2.1 (Basic existence theorem)

Let $g$ be a bounded Lipschitz domain, and let $g \in C(\partial \Omega)$. Then the PDE 2.0.16 has a unique solution $u \in C^{2}(\Omega) \cap C(\bar{\Omega})$.
