# Lemma 2.0.2 (Invariance of solutions to the heat equation under translations and parabolic dilations)

Suppose that $u(t, x)$ is a solution to the heat equation. Let $A, t_{0} \in \mathbb{R}$ be constants, and $x_{0} \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$. Then the amplified and translated function $u^{*}(t, x) \coloneqq A u(t-t_{0}, x-x_{0})$ is also a solution to the heat equation. Similarly, if $\lambda>0$ is a constant, then the amplified, parabolically scaled function $u^{*}(t, x) \coloneqq A u(\lambda^{2} t, \lambda x)$ is also a solution.
