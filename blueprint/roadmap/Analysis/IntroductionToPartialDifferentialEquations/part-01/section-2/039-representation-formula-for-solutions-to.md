# Theorem 2.2 (Representation formula for solutions to the boundary value Poisson equation)

The solution $u$ to (2.0.16) can be represented as $u(x)=-\int_{\Omega} f(y) G(x, y) d^{n} y - \int_{\partial \Omega} g(\sigma) \nabla_{\hat{N}} G(x, \sigma) d \sigma$.
