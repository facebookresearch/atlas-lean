# Lemma 2.0.3

Let $u(t, x) \in C^{1,2}([0, \infty) \times \mathbb{R}^{n})$ be a solution to the heat equation $-\partial_{t} u(t, x)+ \Delta u(t, x)=0$. Assume that at each fixed $t$, $\lim_{|x| \rightarrow \infty}|x|^{n-1}|\nabla_{x} u(t, x)|=0$, uniformly in $x$. Furthermore, assume that there exists a function $f(x) \geq 0$, not depending on $t$, such that $|\partial_{t} u| \leq f(x)$ and such that $\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}} f(x) d^{n} x\lt \infty$. Then the total thermal energy of $u(t, x)$ is constant in time: $\mathcal{T}(t)=\mathcal{T}(0)$.
