# Proposition 4.0.1 (Superposition principle)

If $u_{1}, \cdots, u_{M}$ are solutions to the linear PDE $\mathcal{L} u=0$ and $c_{1}, \cdots, c_{M} \in \mathbb{R}$, then $\sum_{i=1}^{M} c_{i} u_{i}$ is also a solution.
