# Proposition 4.0.2 (Relationship between the inhomogeneous and homogeneous linear PDE solutions)

Let $S_{h}$ be the set of all solutions to the homogeneous linear PDE $\mathcal{L} u=0$, and let $u_{I}$ be a "fixed" solution to the inhomogeneous linear PDE $\mathcal{L} u=f\left(x^{1}, \cdots, x^{n}\right)$. Then the set $S_{I}$ of all solutions to $\mathcal{L} u=f\left(x^{1}, \cdots, x^{n}\right)$ is the translation of $S_{H}$ by $u_{I}$: $S_{I}=\left\{u_{I}+u_{H} \mid u_{H} \in S_{H}\right\}$.
