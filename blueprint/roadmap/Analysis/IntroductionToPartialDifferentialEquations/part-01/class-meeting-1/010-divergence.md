# Definition 7.2.2 (Divergence)

$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}$, the divergence of $\mathbf{F}$, is the scalar-valued function on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ defined by $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} \coloneqq \sum_{i=1}^{n} \partial_{i} F^{i}$.
