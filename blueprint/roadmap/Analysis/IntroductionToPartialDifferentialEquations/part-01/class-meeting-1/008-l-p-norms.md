# Definition 7.1.2 ($L^{p}$ norms)

Let $1 \leq p < \infty$ be a number, and let $f$ be a function defined on a domain $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$. We define the $L^{p}$ norm of $f$ by $\|f\|_{L^{p}(\Omega)} \coloneqq\left(\int_{\Omega}|f(x)|^{p} d^{n} x\right)^{1/p}$. We often write just $L^{p}$ instead of $L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n}\right)$.
