# Definition 7.1.1 ($C^{k}$ norms)

Let $f$ be a function defined on a domain $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}$. Then for any integer $k \geq 0$, we define the $C^{k}$ norm of $f$ on $\Omega$ by $\|f\|_{C^{k}(\Omega)} \coloneqq \sum_{a=0}^{k} \sup_{x \in \Omega}\left|f^{(a)}(x)\right|$, where $f^{(a)}(x)$ is the $a^{\text{th}}$ order derivative of $f(x)$. We often omit the symbol $\Omega$ when $\Omega=\mathbb{R}$.
