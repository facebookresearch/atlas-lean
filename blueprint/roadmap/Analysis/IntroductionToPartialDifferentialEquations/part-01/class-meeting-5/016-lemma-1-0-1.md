# Lemma 1.0.1 (Class Meeting #5, Section 1 (The Fundamental solution))

$\Gamma_{D}(t, x)$ is a solution to the heat equation $u_{t}-D \Delta u = 0$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}, t>0$.
