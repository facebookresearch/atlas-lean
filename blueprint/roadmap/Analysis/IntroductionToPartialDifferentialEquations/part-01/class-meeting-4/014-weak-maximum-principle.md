# Theorem 1.1 (Weak Maximum Principle)

Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a domain. Recall that $Q_{T} \coloneqq (0, T) \times \Omega$ is a spacetime cylinder and that $\partial_{p} Q_{T} \coloneqq\{0\} \times \bar{\Omega} \cup(0, T] \times \partial \Omega$ is its corresponding parabolic boundary. Let $w \in C^{1,2}(Q_{T}) \cap C(\bar{Q}_{T})$ be a solution to the (possibly inhomogeneous) heat equation $w_{t}-D \Delta w=f$ where $f \leq 0$. Then $w(t, x)$ obtains its max in the region $\bar{Q}_{T}$ on $\partial_{p} Q_{T}$. Thus, if $w$ is strictly negative on $\partial_{p} Q_{T}$, then $w$ is strictly negative on $\bar{Q}_{T}$.
