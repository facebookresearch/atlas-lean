# A global unstable branch gives a traveling front

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $c \ge 2\sqrt{r D}$. Suppose there are $U, V : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ solving the logistic traveling-wave system $U' = V$, $V' = (-c V - r U (1 - U))/D$ on all of $\mathbb{R}$, with $0 < U(z) < 1$ and $V(z) < 0$ for all $z < 0$, $U(z) \to 1$ and $V(z) \to 0$ as $z \to -\infty$. Then there is a strictly decreasing traveling front of speed $c$: functions $U, V$ solving the same system on $\mathbb{R}$ with $0 < U < 1$ everywhere, $U(z) \to 1$ as $z \to -\infty$ and $U(z) \to 0$ as $z \to +\infty$.
