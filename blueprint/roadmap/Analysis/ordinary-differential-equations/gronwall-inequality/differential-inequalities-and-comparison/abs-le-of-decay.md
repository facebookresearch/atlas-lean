# Two-sided decay estimate for a linear differential inequality

Let $w, a, g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $\lambda > 0$ and $G \in \mathbb{R}$. Suppose that for every $t \ge 0$, $w$ is differentiable at $t$ with $w'(t) = -a(t)\,w(t) + g(t)$, $a(t) \ge \lambda$ and $|g(t)| \le G$. Then for every $t \ge 0$,

$$|w(t)| \le |w(0)|\,e^{-\lambda t} + \frac{G}{\lambda}.$$
