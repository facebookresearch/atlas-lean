# Two-sided drift estimate

Let $d, H, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $d$ differentiable (derivative $d'$) and $H' = h$ on $[0, \infty)$, $H(0) = 0$ and $H \ge 0$ there. If $d'(t) \le h(t)$ whenever $t \ge 0$ and $d(t) \ge 0$, and $-d'(t) \le h(t)$ whenever $t \ge 0$ and $d(t) \le 0$, then $|d(t)| \le |d(0)| + H(t)$ for every $t \ge 0$.
