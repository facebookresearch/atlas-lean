# Absolute convergence of the branch series

The coefficients $a_n$ of the unstable branch are defined by $a_0 = 0$, $a_1 = 1$ and, for $n \ge 2$, $a_n = \frac{-\rho}{(n-1)(\kappa n + \rho)} \sum_{i + j = n,\ i, j \ge 1} a_i a_j$. Let $\kappa \ge 0$, $\rho > 0$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $x$ with $|x| < 1$, the series $\sum_n n^m a_n x^n$ converges absolutely.
