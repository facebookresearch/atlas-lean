# Leading term of the branch series at minus infinity

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$, $c \in \mathbb{R}$, $\lambda_+ = \frac{-c + \sqrt{c^2 + 4 r D}}{2D}$, $x(z) = \frac{1}{4} e^{\lambda_+ z}$, and let $S_m(x) = \sum_n n^m a_n x^n$ with the branch coefficients $a_n$ for $\kappa = D \lambda_+^2$ and $\rho = r$. For every $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $S_m(x(z)) / x(z) \to 1$ as $z \to -\infty$.
