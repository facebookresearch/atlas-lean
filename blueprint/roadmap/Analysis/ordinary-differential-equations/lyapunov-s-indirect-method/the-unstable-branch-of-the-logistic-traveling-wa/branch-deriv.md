# Derivative series of the unstable branch

For $D, r, c, z \in \mathbb{R}$, with $\lambda_+$ and $x(z)$ as for [the amplitude variable](branch-arg.md) and $a_n$ the coefficients of the branch series, this is the series $-\lambda_+ \sum_n n\, a_n\, x(z)^n$, defined for all parameters as an unconditional sum that is $0$ when the family is not summable. For $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $z < 0$ its terms are summable and it is the derivative $U'(z)$ of the unstable branch $U(z) = 1 - \sum_n a_n x(z)^n$.
