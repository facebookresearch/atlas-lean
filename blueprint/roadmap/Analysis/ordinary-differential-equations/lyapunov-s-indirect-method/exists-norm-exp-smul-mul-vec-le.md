# Exponential decay of a Hurwitz semigroup

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and give $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ the maximum norm. A real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix is Hurwitz if all its complex eigenvalues have negative real part. If $A$ is a Hurwitz matrix, there are $M, \alpha > 0$ with $\|e^{tA} v\| \le M e^{-\alpha t} \|v\|$ for every $t \ge 0$ and every $v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$.
