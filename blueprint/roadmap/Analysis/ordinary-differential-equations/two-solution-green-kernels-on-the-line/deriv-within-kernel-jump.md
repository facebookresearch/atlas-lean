# Derivative jump of the Green kernel at the diagonal

Let $u, v, C : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and let $G(x, y) = C(y)\, u(x)$ for $x \le y$ and $G(x, y) = C(y)\, \frac{u(y)}{v(y)}\, v(x)$ for $y < x$ be the two-solution Green kernel (real division). If $u$ and $v$ are differentiable at $y$ and $v(y) \ne 0$, the right and left derivatives of $x \mapsto G(x, y)$ at $x = y$ differ by

$$C(y) \Big(\frac{u(y)}{v(y)}\, v'(y) - u'(y)\Big).$$
