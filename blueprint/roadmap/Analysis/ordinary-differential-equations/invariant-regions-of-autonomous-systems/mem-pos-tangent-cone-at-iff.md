# Epsilon–delta characterization of the positive tangent cone

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $s \subseteq E$ and $x, v \in E$. Then $v$ lies in the positive tangent cone of $s$ at $x$ if and only if for all $\varepsilon > 0$ and $\delta > 0$ there are $h \in (0, \delta)$ and $y \in s$ with

$$\|y - x - hv\| \le \varepsilon h.$$

No membership $x \in s$, closedness or finite-dimensionality is assumed.
