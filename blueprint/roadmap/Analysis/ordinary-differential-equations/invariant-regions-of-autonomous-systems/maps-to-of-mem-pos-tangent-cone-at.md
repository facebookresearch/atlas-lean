# Tangency preserves a closed set along solutions

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space, $f : E \to E$ locally Lipschitz and $s \subseteq E$ closed, and suppose $f(x)$ lies in the positive tangent cone of $s$ at $x$ (the vectors $\lim_n c_n d_n$ with $c_n \to \infty$ and $x + d_n \in s$; see [its characterization](mem-pos-tangent-cone-at-iff.md)) for every $x \in s$. Let $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $u : \mathbb{R} \to E$ satisfy $u'(t) = f(u(t))$ for every $t \in [a, b]$, with derivatives taken within $[a, b]$. If $u(a) \in s$, then $u(t) \in s$ for every $t \in [a, b]$.
