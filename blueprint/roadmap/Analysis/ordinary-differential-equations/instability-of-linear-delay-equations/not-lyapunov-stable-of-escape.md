# Escape contradicts Lyapunov stability

Let $X$ be a normed group, $\Phi : \mathbb{R} \times X \to X$ and $u_0 \in X$. Suppose there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every $\delta > 0$ some $x$ with $\|x - u_0\| < \delta$ and some $t \ge 0$ have $\|\Phi(t, x) - u_0\| \ge \varepsilon$. Then $u_0$ is not Lyapunov stable for $\Phi$: it is not true that for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $\delta > 0$ with $\|\Phi(t, x) - u_0\| < \varepsilon$ for all $x$ with $\|x - u_0\| < \delta$ and all $t \ge 0$.
