# Second spatial derivative of a heat mode

For $D \in \mathbb{R}$ and a positive integer $k$, let $u_k(x) = \sqrt 2 \sin(k\pi x)$ and $\lambda_k = (k\pi)^2$, and let the heat mode be $w(t, x) = e^{-D \lambda_k t}\, u_k(x)$ on $[0, 1]$. For all $t, x$, $\partial_x^2 w(t, x) = -\lambda_k\, w(t, x)$.
