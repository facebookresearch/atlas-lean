# Power bound near zero for solutions of a Whittaker-type equation

Let $\nu \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|\operatorname{Re}\nu| < \tfrac12$, $k \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be such that $f$ and $f'$ are differentiable on $(0, \infty)$ and

$$y^2 f''(y) + \Big(\tfrac14 - \nu^2 + 2\pi k y - 4\pi^2 y^2\Big) f(y) = 0 \qquad (y > 0).$$

Then there are $\delta > 0$ and $C \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|f(y)| \le C\,y^\delta$ for all $0 < y \le 1$.
