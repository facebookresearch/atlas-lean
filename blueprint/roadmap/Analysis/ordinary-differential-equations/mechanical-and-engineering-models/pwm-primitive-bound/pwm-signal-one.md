# Duty one keeps the switch on

Let the pulse-width-modulated signal with carrier length $\varepsilon$ and duty $d$ be $s(t) = 1$ when $t - k\varepsilon < d(k\varepsilon)\, \varepsilon$ for $k = \lfloor t/\varepsilon \rfloor$, and $s(t) = 0$ otherwise. For $\varepsilon > 0$ and the constant duty $d \equiv 1$, $s(t) = 1$ for every $t$.
