# Conserved energy of the wave mode cos(ct) cos x

For real $c$, let $u(t, x) = \cos(ct)\cos x$, a solution of the wave equation $u_{tt} = c^2 u_{xx}$, and let its energy on $[0, \pi]$ be

$$E(t) = \frac{1}{2} \int_0^\pi \bigl(u_t(t, x)^2 + c^2\, u_x(t, x)^2\bigr)\, dx.$$

Then $E(t_1) = E(t_2)$ for all times $t_1, t_2$; both equal $c^2 \pi / 4$.
