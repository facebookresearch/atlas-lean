# Zero boundary flux of the wave mode cos(ct) cos x

For real $c$ and $t$, the spatial derivative of the wave mode $u(t, x) = \cos(ct)\cos x$ vanishes at both ends of $[0, \pi]$:

$$\partial_x u(t, 0) = \partial_x u(t, \pi) = 0.$$

This is a property of this mode, not of every smooth wave mode: $\cos(ct)\sin x$ has spatial derivative $1$ at $(0, 0)$.
