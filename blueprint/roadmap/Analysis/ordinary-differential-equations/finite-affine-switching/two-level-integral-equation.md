# The two-level trajectory solves its integral equation

With $A(x) = -x$, the forcing $u(s) = 1$ for $s < 1$ and $u(s) = 0$ for $s \ge 1$, and the patched trajectory $x$, for every $t \in [0, 2]$: $x(t) = \int_0^t \big[A(x(s)) + u(s)\big]\, ds = \int_0^t \big[-x(s) + u(s)\big]\, ds$, across the interior switch at $t = 1$.
