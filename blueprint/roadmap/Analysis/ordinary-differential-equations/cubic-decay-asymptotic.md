# Decay rate of solutions of $y' = -y^3$

Let $y : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be
differentiable at every $t > 0$ with $y'(t) = -y(t)^3$, continuous at $0$ within $[0, \infty)$,
and such that $y(0) = 1$. Then $y(t) \sqrt{t} \to 1/\sqrt{2}$ as $t \to \infty$.
