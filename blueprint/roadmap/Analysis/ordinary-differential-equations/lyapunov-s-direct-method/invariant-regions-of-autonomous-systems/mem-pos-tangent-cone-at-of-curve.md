# Velocities of curves in a set lie in its positive tangent cone

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $s \subseteq E$, $T > 0$ and $u : \mathbb{R} \to E$. If $u$ maps $[0, T]$ into $s$ and has derivative $v$ at $0$ within $[0, T]$, then $v$ lies in the positive tangent cone of $s$ at $u(0)$ ([characterization](mem-pos-tangent-cone-at-iff.md)).
