# Nagumo's invariance criterion

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space, $f : E \to E$ locally Lipschitz and $s \subseteq E$ closed. Then $f(x)$ lies in the positive tangent cone of $s$ at $x$ for every $x \in s$ if and only if $s$ is invariant: for every $T > 0$, every solution $u$ of $u' = f(u)$ on $[0, T]$ (with one-sided derivatives within $[0, T]$) with $u(0) \in s$ satisfies $u(t) \in s$ for all $t \in [0, T]$.
