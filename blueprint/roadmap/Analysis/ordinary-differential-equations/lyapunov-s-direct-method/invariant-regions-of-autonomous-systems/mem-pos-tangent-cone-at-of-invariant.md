# Invariance forces tangency

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space, $f : E \to E$ locally Lipschitz and $s \subseteq E$ any set. Suppose $s$ is invariant: for every $T > 0$, every $u : \mathbb{R} \to E$ with $u'(t) = f(u(t))$ for $t \in [0, T]$ (derivatives within $[0, T]$) and $u(0) \in s$ satisfies $u(t) \in s$ for every $t \in [0, T]$. Then $f(x)$ lies in the positive tangent cone of $s$ at $x$ (the vectors $\lim_n c_n d_n$ with $c_n \to \infty$ and $x + d_n \in s$; see [its characterization](mem-pos-tangent-cone-at-iff.md)) for every $x \in s$. The set $s$ need not be closed.
