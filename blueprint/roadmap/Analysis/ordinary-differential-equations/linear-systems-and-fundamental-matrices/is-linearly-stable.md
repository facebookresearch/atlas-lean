# Linear stability of a trace–determinant pair

A pair of real numbers $(\tau, \Delta)$ is linearly stable if every
[characteristic root](is-char-root.md) $z$ of $(\tau, \Delta)$, that is, every complex root of
$z^2 - \tau z + \Delta$, satisfies $\operatorname{Re} z < 0$. For the trace and determinant of a
real $2 \times 2$ matrix this says that the matrix is
[Hurwitz](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-hurwitz.md)
([equivalence](is-hurwitz-iff-is-linearly-stable.md)).
