# Characteristic roots of a planar linear system

For real numbers $\tau$ and $\Delta$, a complex number $z$ is a characteristic root of
$(\tau, \Delta)$ if $z^2 - \tau z + \Delta = 0$. For the trace $\tau = \operatorname{tr} M$ and the
determinant $\Delta = \det M$ of a real $2 \times 2$ matrix $M$, the characteristic roots are the
[complex eigenvalues of $M$](mem-spectrum-iff-is-char-root.md).
