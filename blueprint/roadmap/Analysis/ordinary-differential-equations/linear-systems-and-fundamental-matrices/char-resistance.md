# Characteristic resistance of a passive cable

For real numbers $r_m$ and $r_i$, the characteristic resistance is $r_\infty=\sqrt{r_m r_i}$. In
the cable model $r_m$ (membrane resistance times unit length) and $r_i$ (axial resistance per unit
length) are positive, and then $r_\infty>0$ ([positivity](char-resistance-pos.md)) and
$r_\infty=r_i\lambda=r_m/\lambda$ with $\lambda$ the [length constant](length-constant.md)
([relation](char-resistance-eq.md)).

The formula is defined for all real inputs, with the convention $\sqrt u=0$ for $u<0$:
$r_\infty=0$ when $r_m$ and $r_i$ have opposite signs, and when either is $0$.
