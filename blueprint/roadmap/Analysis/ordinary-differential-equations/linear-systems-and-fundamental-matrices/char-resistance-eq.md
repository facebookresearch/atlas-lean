# Characteristic resistance in terms of the length constant

For real numbers $r_m>0$ and $r_i>0$, the [characteristic resistance](char-resistance.md)
$r_\infty=\sqrt{r_m r_i}$ and the [length constant](length-constant.md) $\lambda=\sqrt{r_m/r_i}$
satisfy

$$r_\infty=r_i\lambda,$$

equivalently, since $r_i\lambda^2=r_m$ ([relation](length-constant-sq.md)) and $\lambda>0$,

$$r_\infty=\frac{r_m}{\lambda}.$$
