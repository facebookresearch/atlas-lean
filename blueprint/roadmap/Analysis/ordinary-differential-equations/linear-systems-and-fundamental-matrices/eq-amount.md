# Uniqueness of solutions of the constant-input relaxation equation

Let $k, R \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $A : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ solve $A' = R - kA$ on $[0, \infty)$, with a two-sided derivative at every $t \ge 0$. Then for every $t \ge 0$, $A(t)$ equals the value at $t$ of the solution with initial value $A(0)$:

$$A(t) = A(0)\,e^{-kt} + \int_0^t e^{-k(t - s)}\,R\,ds.$$
