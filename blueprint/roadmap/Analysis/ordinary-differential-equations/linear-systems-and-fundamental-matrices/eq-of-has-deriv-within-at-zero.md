# Zero derivative within a closed interval gives a constant function

Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers and $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ a function that has derivative $0$
within $[a,b]$ at every point of $[a,b]$ (one-sided at the ends). Then $f(x)=f(a)$ for every
$x\in[a,b]$. When $a>b$ the interval is empty and the statement is vacuous.
