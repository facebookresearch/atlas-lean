# Length constant of a passive cable

For real numbers $r_m$ and $r_i$, the length constant is $\lambda=\sqrt{r_m/r_i}$. In the cable
model $r_m$ is the membrane resistance (resistance times unit length) and $r_i$ the axial resistance
per unit length, both positive, and then $\lambda>0$ ([positivity](length-constant-pos.md)) with
$\lambda^2=r_m/r_i$ ([square](length-constant-sq.md)).

The formula is defined for all real inputs, with the conventions $u/0=0$ and $\sqrt u=0$ for
$u<0$: $\lambda=0$ when $r_i=0$ and when $r_m$ and $r_i$ have opposite signs.
