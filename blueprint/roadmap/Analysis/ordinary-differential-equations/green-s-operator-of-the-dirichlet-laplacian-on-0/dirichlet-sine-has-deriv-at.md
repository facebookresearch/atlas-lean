# Pointwise derivative of a Dirichlet sine eigenfunction

For an integer $k \ge 1$, let $u_k(x) = \sqrt{2} \sin(k \pi x)$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$, be the [sine basis function](sine-basis-fun.md). For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $u_k$ is differentiable at $x$ with $u_k'(x) = \sqrt{2}\, k \pi \cos(k \pi x)$.
