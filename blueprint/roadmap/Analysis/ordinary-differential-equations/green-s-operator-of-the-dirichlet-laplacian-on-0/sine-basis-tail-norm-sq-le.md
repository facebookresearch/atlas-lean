# Tail bound for finite sums in the sine basis

Let $(e_m)_{m \ge 1}$ be the orthonormal sine basis of $L^2([0,1])$, $e_m(x) = \sqrt{2}\,\sin(m\pi x)$, let $c : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $N \le K$. With coefficient $c_k$ on mode $k+1$,

$$\Big\|\sum_{k=N}^{K-1} c_k\, e_{k+1}\Big\|^2 \le \Big\|\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} c_k\, e_{k+1}\Big\|^2,$$

that is, the modes $N+1, \dots, K$ of a finite modal sum have squared norm at most that of all modes $1, \dots, K$.
