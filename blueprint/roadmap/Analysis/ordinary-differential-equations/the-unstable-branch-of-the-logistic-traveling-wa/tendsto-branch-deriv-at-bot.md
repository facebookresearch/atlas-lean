# The branch derivative vanishes at minus infinity

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$, $c \in \mathbb{R}$, $\lambda_+ = \frac{-c + \sqrt{c^2 + 4 r D}}{2D}$, $x(z) = \frac{1}{4} e^{\lambda_+ z}$, and let $a_n$ be the branch coefficients for $\kappa = D \lambda_+^2$ and $\rho = r$. The branch derivative $U'(z) = -\lambda_+ \sum_n n\, a_n x(z)^n$ tends to $0$ as $z \to -\infty$.
