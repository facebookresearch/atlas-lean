# Derivative of the unstable branch

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$, let $\lambda_+ = \frac{-c + \sqrt{c^2 + 4 r D}}{2D}$ and $x(z) = \frac{1}{4} e^{\lambda_+ z}$, and let $U(z) = 1 - \sum_n a_n x(z)^n$ be the unstable branch of $D U'' + c U' + r U (1 - U) = 0$ at $U = 1$, with the coefficients $a_n$ for $\kappa = D \lambda_+^2$ and $\rho = r$. For every $z < 0$, $U$ is differentiable at $z$ with $U'(z) = -\lambda_+ \sum_n n\, a_n x(z)^n$.
