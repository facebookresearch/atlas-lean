# Comparison with a strict barrier on an interval

Let $T \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $f, B : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable at every $t \in [0, T]$, with derivatives $f'$ and $B'$. If $f(0) \le B(0)$ and $f'(t) < B'(t)$ whenever $t \in [0, T)$ and $f(t) = B(t)$, then $f(t) \le B(t)$ for every $t \in [0, T]$.
