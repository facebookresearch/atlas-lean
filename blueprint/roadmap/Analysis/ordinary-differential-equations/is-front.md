# Traveling front of the Fisher–KPP equation

For real $D$, $r$ and $c$, a pair of functions $U, U' \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a traveling
front of speed $c$ when it is a [traveling-wave profile](is-wave-profile.md) of speed $c$,
$0 < U(z) < 1$ for every $z$, $U(z) \to 1$ as $z \to -\infty$ and $U(z) \to 0$ as $z \to +\infty$.
