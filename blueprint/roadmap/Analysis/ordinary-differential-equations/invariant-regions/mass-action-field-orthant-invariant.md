# Mass-action kinetics preserves the nonnegative orthant

Consider finitely many species $i$ and reactions $j$, integer stoichiometry $N_{ij}$, exponents $\alpha_{ji} \in \mathbb N$ and rate constants $k_j \ge 0$, with $\alpha_{ji} \ge 1$ whenever $N_{ij} < 0$, and the mass-action field $F_i(x) = \sum_j N_{ij} k_j \prod_s x_s^{\alpha_{js}}$. Let $I$ be a convex set of times and $x$ a curve with $x'(t) = F(x(t))$ for every $t \in I$. If $x(t_0) \ge 0$ componentwise for some $t_0 \in I$, then $x(t) \ge 0$ componentwise for every $t \in I$ with $t \ge t_0$.
