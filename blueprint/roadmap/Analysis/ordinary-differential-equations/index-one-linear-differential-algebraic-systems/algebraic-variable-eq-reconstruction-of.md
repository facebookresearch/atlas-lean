# In a reduced trajectory the algebraic variable is the reconstruction

Let `n` and `m` be finite index sets and `A`, `B`, `C`, `D` real matrices of sizes `n × n`, `n × m`,
`m × n` and `m × m`, with `D` invertible, and let `f : ℝ → ℝⁿ` and `g : ℝ → ℝᵐ` be forcing terms and
`x : ℝ → ℝⁿ`, `y : ℝ → ℝᵐ` be a trajectory pair that
[satisfies the reduced system](satisfies-reduced-system.md). Then at every time `t`,
`y(t) = −D⁻¹ (C x(t) + g(t))`, the [reconstruction](algebraic-reconstruction.md).
