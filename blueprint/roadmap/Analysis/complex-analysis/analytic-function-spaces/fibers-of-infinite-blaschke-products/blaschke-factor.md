# Blaschke factors

For $a, z \in \mathbb{C}$, the Blaschke factor with zero $a$ is

$$
b_a(z) = \frac{|a|}{a} \cdot \frac{a - z}{1 - \bar a z},
$$

computed with $x / 0 = 0$. For $0 < |a| < 1$ this is the classical Blaschke factor: holomorphic on a neighbourhood of the closed unit disc, of modulus $1$ on the unit circle, with a single zero at $a$ and $b_a(0) = |a| > 0$. At $a = 0$ the formula gives the zero function, since $|0|/0 = 0$; zeros at the origin are instead handled by the factor $z^m$ of a [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md).
