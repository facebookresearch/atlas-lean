# Stephenson's question on fibers of infinite Blaschke products

Stephenson's question (Hayman–Lingham Problem 5.66) asks whether, for every infinite Blaschke product $B$ in the unit disc, there is $\delta > 0$ such that the fiber $B^{-1}(\{w\})$ is infinite for every $|w| < \delta$. The proposition linked here is that affirmative statement, a proposed statement and not a theorem.
