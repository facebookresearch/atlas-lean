# Sharpness of the Dirichlet-space pointwise bound

For every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $0 < |z| < 1$, the [point evaluation](dirichlet-eval.md) at $z$ of the [Dirichlet kernel](dirichlet-kernel.md) $k_z$ is

$$
f_{k_z}(z) = -\frac{\log(1 - |z|^2)}{|z|^2} = \|k_z\|^2,
$$

so the pointwise bound $|f_a(z)| \le \|a\|\, \|k_z\|$ is attained at $a = k_z$.
