# Reproducing kernel of the Dirichlet space

In the coefficient model of the Dirichlet space, a sequence $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ stands for the function $f_a(z) = \sum_n a_n z^n/\sqrt{n+1}$ on the unit disc, whose Dirichlet norm $\left(\sum_n (n+1)|a_n/\sqrt{n+1}|^2\right)^{1/2}$ is $\|a\|$. For $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$, the **Dirichlet kernel** at $z$ is the element $k_z$ of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ with coefficients

$$
(k_z)_n = \frac{\bar z^n}{\sqrt{n+1}}, \qquad n \in \mathbb{N}.
$$
