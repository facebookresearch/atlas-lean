# Reproducing property of the Dirichlet kernel

For every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$ and every $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, the bounded [point-evaluation functional](dirichlet-eval-clm.md) at $z$ sends $a$ to $f_a(z) = \langle k_z, a \rangle$, the inner product with the [Dirichlet kernel](dirichlet-kernel.md).
