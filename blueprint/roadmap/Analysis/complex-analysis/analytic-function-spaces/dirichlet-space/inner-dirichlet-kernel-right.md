# Conjugate reproducing property of the Dirichlet kernel

For every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$ and every $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, the [Dirichlet kernel](dirichlet-kernel.md) satisfies $\langle a, k_z \rangle = \overline{f_a(z)}$, where $f_a(z) = \langle k_z, a \rangle$ is the [point evaluation](dirichlet-eval.md).
