# Logarithmic formula for the inner product of Dirichlet kernels

For all $z, w \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$, $|w| < 1$ and $z \bar w \ne 0$, the [Dirichlet kernels](dirichlet-kernel.md) satisfy

$$
\langle k_z, k_w \rangle = -\frac{\operatorname{Log}(1 - z \bar w)}{z \bar w},
$$

where $\operatorname{Log}$ is the principal branch of the logarithm.
