# Point evaluation as a bounded functional on the Hardy space

For $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$, point evaluation at $z$ is the bounded linear functional $a \mapsto \langle k_z, a \rangle$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, the coefficient model of the Hardy space $H^2$, where $k_z$ is the [Szegő kernel](szego-kernel.md).
