# Taylor coefficients of a Hardy-space function are bounded by its norm

For every $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $|a_n| \le \|a\|$: a single squared coefficient is at most the sum of all of them. In the Taylor-coefficient model of the Hardy space $H^2$, the Taylor coefficients of a function are bounded by its norm.
