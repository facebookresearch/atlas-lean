# Bergman point evaluation is the inner product with the kernel

For every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$ and every $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, the bounded [point-evaluation functional](bergman-eval-clm.md) at $z$ sends $a$ to $f_a(z) = \langle k_z, a \rangle$, the inner product with the [Bergman kernel](bergman-kernel.md).
