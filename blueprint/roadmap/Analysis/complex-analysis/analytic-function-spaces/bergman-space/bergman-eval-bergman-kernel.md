# Bergman kernel evaluated at its own point

For every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$, the [point evaluation](bergman-eval.md) at $z$ of the [Bergman kernel](bergman-kernel.md) $k_z$ is

$$
f_{k_z}(z) = \frac{1}{(1 - |z|^2)^2} = \|k_z\|^2,
$$

so the pointwise bound $|f_a(z)| \le \|a\|/(1 - |z|^2)$ is attained at $a = k_z$.
