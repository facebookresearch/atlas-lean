# Rescaled Bergman monomial

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the rescaled monomial $e_n(z) = \sqrt{n+1}\, z^n$, a unit vector of the Bergman space $A^2$ for the normalized area measure $dA/\pi$, is represented in the coefficient model $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ (see [Bergman kernel](bergman-kernel.md)) by the $n$-th standard basis vector $e_n$, whose coefficient of index $n$ is $1$ and all others $0$.
