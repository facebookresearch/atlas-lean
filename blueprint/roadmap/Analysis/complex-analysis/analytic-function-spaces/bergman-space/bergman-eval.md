# Point evaluation on the Bergman space

For $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$, point evaluation at $z$ on the coefficient model $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ of the Bergman space $A^2$ sends $a$ to $f_a(z) = \langle k_z, a \rangle$, the inner product (conjugate-linear in its first argument) with the [Bergman kernel](bergman-kernel.md) $k_z = (\sqrt{n+1}\, \bar z^n)_n$.
