# Bergman kernel

In the coefficient model of the Bergman space $A^2$ of the unit disc, with normalized area measure $dA/\pi$, a sequence $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ stands for the function $f_a(z) = \sum_n a_n \sqrt{n+1}\, z^n$, whose $A^2$ norm is $\|a\|$. For $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| < 1$, the **Bergman kernel** at $z$ is the element $k_z$ of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ with coefficients

$$
(k_z)_n = \sqrt{n+1}\, \bar z^n, \qquad n \in \mathbb{N}.
$$
