# Bergman coefficients as inner products with monomials

For every $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the coefficient $a_n$ is the inner product with the standard basis vector $e_n$, which represents the [rescaled monomial](bergman-monomial.md) $\sqrt{n+1}\, z^n$: $a_n = \langle e_n, a \rangle$.
