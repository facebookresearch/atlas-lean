# Bourgain's theorem on radial variation of bounded analytic functions

This settles [Rudin–Barth problem on radial images of infinite length](conjecture.md) in the negative: its statement is false.

Bourgain (On the radial variation of bounded analytic functions
on the disc, Duke Math. J. 69 (1993) 671-682, Theorem 1) proves that for every bounded analytic
function on the disc the set of directions with finite radial variation is nonempty (of
Hausdorff dimension 1), so no bounded analytic function has radial images of infinite length in
every direction.

## Sources

- J. Bourgain, On the radial variation of bounded analytic functions on the disc, Duke Math. J. 69 (1993) 671-682, https://doi.org/10.1215/S0012-7094-93-06928-1.
