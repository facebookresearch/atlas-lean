# Negation of the rotated-arc limit statement

The statement: there is a non-tangential arc $\gamma$ at $1$ such that for every bounded analytic function $g$ on the unit disc there is a real angle $\theta$ for which the limit $\lim g(w)$ as $w \to e^{i\theta}$ with $w \in e^{i\theta}\gamma \setminus \{e^{i\theta}\}$ exists in $\mathbb{C}$.

## Sources

W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, *Research Problems in Function Theory*, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 5.76 and Update 5.76; E. F. Collingwood and A. J. Lohwater, *The Theory of Cluster Sets*, Cambridge Univ. Press (1966).
