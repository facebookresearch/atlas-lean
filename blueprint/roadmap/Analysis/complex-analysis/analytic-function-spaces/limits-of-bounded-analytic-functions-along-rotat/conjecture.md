# Bounded analytic functions without limits along all rotated arcs

The statement: for every non-tangential arc $\gamma$ at $1$ there is a bounded analytic function $g$ on the unit disc such that, for every real angle $\theta$, the limit $\lim g(w)$ as $w \to e^{i\theta}$ with $w \in e^{i\theta}\gamma \setminus \{e^{i\theta}\}$ does not exist in $\mathbb{C}$.

A non-tangential arc at $1$ is an injective continuous image of $[0,1]$ starting at $1$, otherwise inside the open unit disc, and contained in a Stolz angle $|1 - z| \le K(1 - |z|)$.

## Sources

W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, *Research Problems in Function Theory*, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 5.76 and Update 5.76; E. F. Collingwood and A. J. Lohwater, *The Theory of Cluster Sets*, Cambridge Univ. Press (1966).
