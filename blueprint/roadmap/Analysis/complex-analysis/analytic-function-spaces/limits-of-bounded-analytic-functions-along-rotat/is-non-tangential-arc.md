# Non-tangential arc at 1

A set $\gamma \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ is a non-tangential arc at $1$ when all of the following hold:

- $1 \in \gamma$ and $\gamma$ contains some point other than $1$;
- $\gamma \setminus \{1\}$ lies in the open unit disc $\{ |z| < 1 \}$;
- $\gamma$ is compact and connected;
- $\gamma$ is the image of an injective continuous map $p : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{C}$ with $p(0) = 1$;
- $\gamma$ lies in a Stolz angle at $1$: there is $K > 0$ with $|1 - z| \le K (1 - |z|)$ for all $z \in \gamma$.
