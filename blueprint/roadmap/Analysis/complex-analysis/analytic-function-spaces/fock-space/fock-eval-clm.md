# Point evaluation as a bounded functional on the Fock space

For $z \in \mathbb{C}$, point evaluation at $z$ is the bounded linear functional $a \mapsto \langle k_z, a \rangle$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, the coefficient model of the Fock space, where $k_z$ is the [Fock kernel](fock-kernel.md).
