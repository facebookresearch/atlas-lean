# Normalized monomials in the Fock space

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the normalized monomial $e_n(z) = z^n/\sqrt{n!}$ of the Fock space is represented, in the Taylor-coefficient model $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ (see [Fock kernel](fock-kernel.md)), by the $n$-th standard basis vector $e_n$, whose coefficient of index $n$ is $1$ and all others $0$.
