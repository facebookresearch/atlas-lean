# Reproducing kernel of the Fock space

In the Taylor-coefficient model of the Fock space, a sequence $a \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ stands for the entire function $f_a(z) = \sum_n a_n z^n/\sqrt{n!}$, whose Fock norm $\left(\sum_n n!\, |a_n/\sqrt{n!}|^2\right)^{1/2}$ is $\|a\|$. For $z \in \mathbb{C}$, the **Fock kernel** at $z$ is the element $k_z$ of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ with coefficients

$$
(k_z)_n = \frac{\bar z^n}{\sqrt{n!}}, \qquad n \in \mathbb{N}.
$$
