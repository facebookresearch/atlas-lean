# Point evaluation on the Fock space

For $z \in \mathbb{C}$, point evaluation at $z$ on the coefficient model $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$ of the Fock space sends $a$ to $f_a(z) = \langle k_z, a \rangle$, the inner product (conjugate-linear in its first argument) with the [Fock kernel](fock-kernel.md) $k_z = (\bar z^n/\sqrt{n!})_n$.
