# Yakir's theorem on zeros of sign polynomials in the disc

Yakir (Studia Math. 261 (2021) 227-240, arXiv:2011.06234) proved that all but
o(2^n) Littlewood polynomials sum_\{k<n\} ±z^k have n/2 + O(n^\{9/10\}) roots in the open unit disk.

## Sources

- Oren Yakir, Approximately half of the roots of a random Littlewood polynomial are inside the disk, Studia Mathematica 261 (2021) 227-240, arXiv:2011.06234, https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06234.
