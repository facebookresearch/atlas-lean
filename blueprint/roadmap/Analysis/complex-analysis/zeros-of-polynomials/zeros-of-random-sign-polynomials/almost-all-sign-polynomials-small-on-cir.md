# Erdős Problem 525 on small values of sign polynomials on the circle

Erdős Problem 525, first question: all but `o(2 ^ n)` of the degree-`n` polynomials with
coefficients `±1` satisfy `|f(z)| < 1` for some `|z| = 1`.

Its resolution: [Kashin's theorem on small values of sign polynomials](almost-all-sign-polynomials-small-on-cir-2.md).
