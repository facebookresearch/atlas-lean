# Kashin's theorem on small values of sign polynomials

This settles [Erdős Problem 525 on small values of sign polynomials on the circle](almost-all-sign-polynomials-small-on-cir.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Kashin proved that `min_{|z| = 1} |f(z)| → 0` in probability for random `±1`
coefficients, so the proportion with `min |f| ≥ 1` tends to `0`; Konyagin sharpened this to `n ^
(-1/2 + o(1))`.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #525, https://www.erdosproblems.com/525 (status PROVED); resolution: B. S. Kashin, The properties of random trigonometric polynomials with ±1 coefficients, Vestnik Moskov. Univ. Ser. I Mat. Mekh. (1987), 40-46, erdosproblems.com/525: yes; Kashin proved min |f| = o(1) in probability (Littlewood's conjecture), Konyagin min |f| ≤ n^\{-1/2 + o(1)\} in probability, https://www.erdosproblems.com/525.
