# Bálint's theorem on gaps between critical points

Bálint proved that the gaps between consecutive zeros of `f'` increase monotonically
from the centre outward; erdosproblems.com/1114 lists the problem as proved (Lorch gave
generalizations).

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #1114, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1114 (status PROVED); resolution: E. Bálint, Proof of a conjecture of P. Erdős, Mat. Lapok (1960), 33-40, erdosproblems.com/1114: proved by Bálint; generalizations by L. Lorch, Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. (1976), 293-300, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1114.
