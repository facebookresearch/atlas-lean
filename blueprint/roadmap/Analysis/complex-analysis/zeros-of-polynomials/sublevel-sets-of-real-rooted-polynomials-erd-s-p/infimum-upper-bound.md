# Upper bound 1.835 for the infimum sublevel volume

The statement: the infimum, over all non-constant monic real polynomials $f$ whose roots counted with multiplicity all lie in $[-1, 1]$, of the Lebesgue measure of $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1\}$, computed in $[0, \infty]$, is strictly less than $1.835$.
