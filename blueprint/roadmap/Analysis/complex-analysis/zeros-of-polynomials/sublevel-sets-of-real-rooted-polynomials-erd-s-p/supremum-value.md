# Supremum sublevel volume equals 2√2

The statement: the supremum, over all monic real polynomials $f$ whose roots counted with multiplicity all lie in $[-1, 1]$ (the number of roots in $[-1,1]$ equals the degree; the constant polynomial $1$ included), of the Lebesgue measure of $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1\}$, computed in $[0, \infty]$, equals $2\sqrt{2}$:

$$\sup_{f} \lambda\big(\{ x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1 \}\big) = 2\sqrt{2}.$$
