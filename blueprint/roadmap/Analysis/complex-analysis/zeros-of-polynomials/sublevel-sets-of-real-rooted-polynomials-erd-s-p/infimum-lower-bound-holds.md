# Infimum sublevel volume is at least 2^(4/3) - 1

The infimum, over all non-constant monic real polynomials $f$ whose roots counted with multiplicity all lie in $[-1, 1]$, of the Lebesgue measure of $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1\}$ (in $[0, \infty]$) is at least $2^{4/3} - 1$.
