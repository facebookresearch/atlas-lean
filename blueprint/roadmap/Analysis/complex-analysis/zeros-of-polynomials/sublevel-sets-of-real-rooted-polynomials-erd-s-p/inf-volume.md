# Infimum sublevel volume of monic polynomials with roots in [-1, 1]

Call a real polynomial $f$ admissible when $f$ is monic and the number of its roots in $[-1, 1]$, counted with multiplicity, equals its degree; that is, all roots of $f$ are real and lie in $[-1, 1]$. The infimum volume is

$$\inf \Big\{ \lambda\big(\{ x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1 \}\big) : f \text{ admissible},\ f \neq 1 \Big\} \in [0, \infty],$$

where $\lambda$ is Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}$. The condition $f \neq 1$ excludes the constant polynomial, whose sublevel set is empty.
