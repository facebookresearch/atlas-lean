# Infimum sublevel volume is below 1.835

The infimum, over all non-constant monic real polynomials $f$ whose roots counted with multiplicity all lie in $[-1, 1]$, of the Lebesgue measure of $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1\}$ (in $[0, \infty]$) is strictly less than $1.835$.
