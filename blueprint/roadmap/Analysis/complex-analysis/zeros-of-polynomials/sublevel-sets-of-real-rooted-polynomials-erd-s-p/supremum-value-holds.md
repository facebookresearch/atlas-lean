# Maximal sublevel volume of monic polynomials with roots in [-1, 1]

The supremum, over all monic real polynomials $f$ whose roots counted with multiplicity all lie in $[-1, 1]$, of the Lebesgue measure of $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : |f(x)| < 1\}$ (in $[0, \infty]$) equals $2\sqrt{2}$.

## Sources

P. Erdős, F. Herzog and G. Piranian (1958) treated the case where all roots are $\pm 1$; the value $2\sqrt{2}$ is attributed to T. Tao at erdosproblems.com/1038, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1038.
