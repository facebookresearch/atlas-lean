# Erdős Problem 230 on the supremum of unimodular polynomials

Erdős Problem 230 (Erdős and Newman): there is `c > 0` such that every polynomial
`P(z) = ∑_{k=1}^n a_k z^k` with `|a_k| = 1` and `n ≥ 2` has `max_{|z| = 1} |P(z)| ≥ (1 + c) √n`.

Its resolution: [Kahane's ultraflat polynomials refute Erdős Problem 230](unimodular-polynomial-sup-norm-gap-refut.md).
