# Kahane's unimodular polynomials with large minimum on the circle

This settles [Erdős–Newman question on the minimum of unimodular polynomials](unimodular-min-lower-bound.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Kahane's ultraflat polynomials satisfy `|f(z)| = (1 + o(1)) n^{1/2}` uniformly on
`|z| = 1`, so for every `ε > 0` and large `n` their minimum on the circle exceeds `(1 - ε) n^{1/2}`.

## Sources

- W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 4.31 (P. Erdős and D. Newman); resolution: J.-P. Kahane, Sur les polynômes à coefficients unimodulaires, Bull. London Math. Soc. 12 (1980), 321-342; as recorded on erdosproblems.com/230 (Problem 4.31 of Hayman's list), erdosproblems.com/230 (the Erdős–Newman conjecture, 'Problem 4.31 in [Ha74]'): 'Kahane constructed ultraflat polynomials P(z) = ∑ a_k z^k with |a_k| = 1 such that P(z) = (1 + o(1)) √n uniformly for all z with |z| = 1'; Bombieri and Bourgain (JEMS 2009) improve the error to O(n^{7/18} (log n)^{O(1)}), https://www.erdosproblems.com/230.
