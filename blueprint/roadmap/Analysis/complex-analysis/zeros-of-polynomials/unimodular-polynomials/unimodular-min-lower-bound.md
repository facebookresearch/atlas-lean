# Erdős–Newman question on the minimum of unimodular polynomials

Erdős–Newman's question (D4): for every `ε > 0` and every `n > n₀(ε)` there is a polynomial
`f(z) = ∑_{k=0}^{n} a_k z^k` with `|a_k| = 1` and `min_{|z|=1} |f(z)| > (1 - ε) n^{1/2}`.

Its resolution: [Kahane's unimodular polynomials with large minimum on the circle](unimodular-min-lower-bound-holds.md).
