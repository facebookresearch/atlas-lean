# Erdős Problem 116: polynomial lower bound for lemniscate area

Erdős Problem 116, main question: there are `c > 0` and `C` such that for every `n ≥ 1` and
all `z₁, …, zₙ` in the closed unit disc, `{w : |∏ (w - zᵢ)| < 1}` has area at least `c n^{-C}`.

Its resolution: [Polynomial lower bound for the area of lemniscates with zeros in the closed unit disc](lemniscate-area-polynomial-lower-bound-h.md).
