# Pommerenke's theorem on connected lemniscates

This settles [Erdős Problem 1046 on connected lemniscates](connected-lemniscate-in-disc-radius-two.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Pommerenke proved that if $f$ is a monic complex polynomial whose lemniscate $\{z : |f(z)| < 1\}$ is connected, then the lemniscate lies in the open disc of radius $2$ centred at the mean of the zeros of $f$. erdosproblems.com/1046 states that the answer is yes, although its status field says disproved.

## Sources

J. 6 (1959), 221-225, the answer is yes, with the centre at the centroid of the zeros of f (erdosproblems.com/1046), https://doi.org/10.1307/mmj/1028998227.
