# Krishnapur–Lundberg–Ramachandran lower bound for the area of lemniscates with zeros in the closed unit disc

This settles [Erdős Problem 116: logarithmic lower bound for lemniscate area](lemniscate-area-log-lower-bound.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Krishnapur, Lundberg and Ramachandran proved that for all large `n` the
minimal area over monic polynomials of degree `n` with all zeros in the closed unit disc is at least
`c / log n` (and at most `C / log log n`); the finitely many smaller `n ≥ 2` are covered by
Pommerenke's `≫ n^{-4}` after shrinking `c`, so `C = 1` works. erdosproblems.com/116 records this
bound.
