# Erdős Problem 115 on derivatives on connected lemniscates

Erdős Problem 115: for every `ε > 0` and all large `n`, every monic complex polynomial `p` of
degree `n` whose filled lemniscate `{z : |p z| ≤ 1}` is connected satisfies `|p' z| ≤ (1/2 + ε) n²`
on that set.

Its resolution: [Erëmenko–Lempert bound on derivatives on connected lemniscates](connected-lemniscate-derivative-bound-ho.md).
