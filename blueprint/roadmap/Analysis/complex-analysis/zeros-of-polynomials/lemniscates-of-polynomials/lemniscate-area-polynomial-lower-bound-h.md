# Polynomial lower bound for the area of lemniscates with zeros in the closed unit disc

This settles [Erdős Problem 116: polynomial lower bound for lemniscate area](lemniscate-area-polynomial-lower-bound.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

A result of Pommerenke gives a lower bound of order $n^{-4}$: there is $c > 0$ such that for every $n \ge 1$ and all $z_1, \dots, z_n$ in the closed unit disc, the set $\{w : |\prod_i (w - z_i)| < 1\}$ has area at least $c\, n^{-4}$. erdosproblems.com/116 lists the problem as proved.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #116, https://www.erdosproblems.com/116 (status PROVED (LEAN)); resolution: C. Pommerenke, On metric properties of complex polynomials, Michigan Math. J. 8 (1961), 97-115.
