# Erdős Problem 116: logarithmic lower bound for lemniscate area

Erdős Problem 116, stronger form: there are a real number $c > 0$ and a natural number $C$ such that for every integer $n \ge 2$ and all points $z_1, \dots, z_n$ of the closed unit disc $|z| \le 1$, the open lemniscate

$$\{w \in \mathbb{C} : |(w - z_1)(w - z_2) \cdots (w - z_n)| < 1\}$$

of the monic polynomial with roots $z_1, \dots, z_n$ has Lebesgue area at least $c / (\log n)^C$. The constants $c$ and $C$ are the same for every $n$ and every choice of roots.
