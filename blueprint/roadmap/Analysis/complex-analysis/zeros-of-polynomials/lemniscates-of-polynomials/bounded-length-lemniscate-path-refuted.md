# MacLane's counterexample on paths in lemniscates

This settles [Erdős Problem 1215 on short paths in lemniscates](bounded-length-lemniscate-path.md) in the negative: its statement is false.

MacLane proved that for every simply connected compact `A ⊆ {|z| < 1}` with `0 ∉ A`
and all large `d` there is such a `P` of degree `d` with `|P| > 2` on `A`; taking for `A` a
labyrinth around which every path from `0` to the unit circle has length `> C` defeats every `C`
(such a path avoids `A`, where `|P| > 2`). erdosproblems.com/1215 lists the problem as disproved.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #1215, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1215 (status DISPROVED); resolution: G. R. MacLane, On a conjecture of Erdős, Herzog and Piranian, Michigan Math. J. 2 (1953-54), 147-148, erdosproblems.com/1215: for every simply connected compact A ⊆ {|z| < 1} with 0 ∉ A and all large d there is such a P of degree d with |P| > 2 on A; a labyrinth A forces long paths.
