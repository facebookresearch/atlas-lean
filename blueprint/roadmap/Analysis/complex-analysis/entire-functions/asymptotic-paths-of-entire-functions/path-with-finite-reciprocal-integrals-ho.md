# Lewis–Rossi–Weitsman theorem on paths with finite reciprocal integrals

Lewis, Rossi and Weitsman proved it (for `e ^ u` with `u` subharmonic, so for `|f| =
e ^ (log |f|)`); Huber had a path `C_λ` for each single `λ`, and Zhang the finite-order case.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #515, https://www.erdosproblems.com/515 (status PROVED); resolution: J. Lewis, J. Rossi and A. Weitsman, On the growth of subharmonic functions along paths, Ark. Mat. (1984), 109-119, erdosproblems.com/515: true; finite order by Zhang (1977), general case by Lewis, Rossi and Weitsman (for e^u, u subharmonic), https://www.erdosproblems.com/515.
