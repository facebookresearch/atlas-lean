# Entire functions of finite order without asymptotic paths of linear length

Gol'dberg and Erëmenko constructed entire functions of finite order (indeed with
`log M(r) ≪ φ(r) (log r)²` for any `φ → ∞`) admitting no path `Γ` with `f → ∞` and `ℓ(r) ≪ r`,
answering Hayman's question (which he proved has a positive answer when `log M(r) ≪ (log r)²`).
erdosproblems.com/1115 records the disproof.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #1115, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1115 (status SOLVED); resolution: A. A. Gol'dberg and A. È. Erëmenko, Asymptotic curves of entire functions of finite order, Mat. Sb. (N.S.) (1979), 555-581, for every φ(r) → ∞ there is an entire f with log M(r) ≪ φ(r) (log r)² and no path Γ with f → ∞ and ℓ(r) ≪ r; also of any prescribed finite order (erdosproblems.com/1115), https://www.erdosproblems.com/1115.
