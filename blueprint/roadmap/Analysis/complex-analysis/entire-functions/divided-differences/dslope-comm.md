# The divided difference is symmetric

Let $f : \mathbb{K} \to E$ from a nontrivially normed field to a normed space over it, and let $\operatorname{dslope}_f(a, b) = (f(b) - f(a))/(b - a)$ for $b \ne a$ and $f'(a)$ for $b = a$, where $f'(a)$ is the derivative of $f$ at $a$ when $f$ is differentiable there and $0$ otherwise. Then $\operatorname{dslope}_f(a, b) = \operatorname{dslope}_f(b, a)$ for all $a, b$.
