# Eremenko's question on entire solutions of higher-order Briot–Bouquet equations

Eremenko (Meromorphic solutions of Briot–Bouquet type equations, remaining 2024 case) asks whether every entire solution in the remaining case of higher-order Briot–Bouquet equations is an exponential polynomial. The proposition linked here is the affirmative answer, a proposed statement and not a theorem: for all integers $k \ge 2$ and $d \ge 1$, every irreducible polynomial $F \in \mathbb{C}[X_0, X_1]$ of total degree $d$ of the form $F = (X_0 - a X_1)^d + Q$ with $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $\deg Q \le d - 1$, and every entire function $f$ with $F(f^{(k)}(z), f(z)) = 0$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$ (the [remaining Briot–Bouquet data](satisfies-remaining-briot-bouquet.md)), the function $f$ is an [exponential polynomial](is-exponential-polynomial.md): $f(z) = \sum_{j=1}^{n} P_j(z) e^{\lambda_j z}$ for some $n \ge 0$, polynomials $P_j$ and $\lambda_j \in \mathbb{C}$.
