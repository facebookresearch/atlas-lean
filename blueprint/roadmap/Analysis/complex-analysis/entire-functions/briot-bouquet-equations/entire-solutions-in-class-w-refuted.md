# Entire solutions of algebraic differential equations outside the class W

This settles [Eremenko's question on entire solutions of higher-order algebraic equations](entire-solutions-in-class-w.md) in the negative: its statement is false.

Eremenko, Liao and Ng note that `y‴ = y` (`n = 3`, `F = X₀ - X₁`) has solutions
outside `W`, for instance `y(z) = e^z + e^{ωz}` with `ω = e^{2πi/3}`, an entire solution that is not
rational, not doubly periodic, and not `R(e^{az})`; the list's Update 1.36 records the problem as
solved by this example.

## Sources

- W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 1.36 (A. Eremenko); resolution: A. Eremenko, L. Liao and T. W. Ng, Meromorphic solutions of higher order Briot–Bouquet differential equations, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 146 (2009), no. 1, 197-206, arXiv:0707.2898; recorded in Hayman–Lingham, Update 1.36, §1: W is the class of doubly periodic functions, rational functions and R(e^\{az\}) with R rational; Theorem 1: every meromorphic solution with at least one pole belongs to W; 'It is not true that all meromorphic solutions of higher order Briot–Bouquet equations belong to W, a simple counterexample is y‴ = y'; Update 1.36: they 'give an example of an entire solution which is neither rational, nor a rational function of an exponential', https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2898.
