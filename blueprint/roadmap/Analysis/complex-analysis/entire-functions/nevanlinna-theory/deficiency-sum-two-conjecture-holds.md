# Drasin's proof of F. Nevanlinna's conjecture

Drasin proved F. Nevanlinna's conjecture: a meromorphic function of finite order `ρ`
with deficiency sum `2` has `2ρ` an integer `≥ 2`, and each of its deficiencies is a positive
integer multiple of `1/ρ` or `0`, hence rational (Hayman–Lingham Update 1.3).

## Sources

- W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 1.3 (conjecture of F. Nevanlinna); resolution: D. Drasin, Proof of a conjecture of F. Nevanlinna concerning functions which have deficiency sum two, Acta Math. 158 (1987), 1-94; recorded in Hayman–Lingham, Update 1.3, Section 1.1, THEOREM: "F. Nevanlinna's conjecture is correct": if f is meromorphic in the plane of finite order λ = limsup log T(r)/log r and ∑ δ(a) = 2, then 2λ is an integer ≥ 2 (1.4) and every deficient value has δ(a) = λ⁻¹ p(a) with p(a) a positive integer (1.5); Hayman–Lingham Update 1.3: "The conjecture was completely proved by Drasin", https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02392256.
