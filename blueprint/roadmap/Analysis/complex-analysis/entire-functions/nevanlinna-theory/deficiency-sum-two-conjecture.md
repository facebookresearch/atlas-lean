# F. Nevanlinna's conjecture on maximal deficiency sum

F. Nevanlinna's conjecture (Hayman–Lingham Problem 1.3): let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be meromorphic on $\mathbb{C}$ and nonconstant in the sense that for no $c \in \mathbb{C}$ does $f(z) = c$ hold for all $z$ outside a set without accumulation points in $\mathbb{C}$. Suppose $f$ has finite [order](order.md) $\rho < \infty$ and

$$
\sum_{a \in \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}} \delta(a, f) = 2,
$$

the sum running over the whole Riemann sphere, infinity included, of the [deficiencies](deficiency.md), each replaced by $\max(\delta(a, f), 0)$ and summed in $[0, \infty]$. Then $\rho = n / 2$ for some integer $n \ge 2$, and $\delta(a, f)$ is rational for every $a \in \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$.

Its resolution: [Drasin's proof of F. Nevanlinna's conjecture](deficiency-sum-two-conjecture-holds.md).
