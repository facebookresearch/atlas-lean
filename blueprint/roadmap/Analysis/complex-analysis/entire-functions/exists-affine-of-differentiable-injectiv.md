# Injective entire functions are affine

If $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ is entire and
injective, then there are $a, b \in \mathbb{C}$ with $a \ne 0$ such that $f(z) = az + b$ for all
$z \in \mathbb{C}$.
