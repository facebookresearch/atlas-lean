# Integral of the divided difference off the path

Let $\gamma$ be a closed piecewise $C^1$ path in $\mathbb{C}$, $f$ continuous on the image of $\gamma$, and $z$ not on $\gamma$. With $g(z, \zeta) = (f(\zeta) - f(z))/(\zeta - z)$ (the diagonal value is never used, since $z$ is not on $\gamma$),

$$\int_\gamma g(z, \zeta)\, d\zeta = \int_\gamma \frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\, d\zeta - 2\pi i\, n(\gamma, z)\, f(z).$$
