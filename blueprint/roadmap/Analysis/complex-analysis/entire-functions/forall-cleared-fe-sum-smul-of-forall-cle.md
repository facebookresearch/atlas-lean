# Cleared functional equations are preserved by finite sums

Let $N > 1$ be an integer, $C \in \mathbb{C}$, $r \in \mathbb{Z}$, and for a finite family indexed by $j$ let $\kappa_j \in \mathbb{C}$ and $A_j, B_j : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$. Say that functions $(A, B)$ satisfy the cleared functional equation with data $(P, P_d, Q, Q_d, m, m_d, \sigma, \sigma_d)$, where $P, P_d, Q, Q_d$ are complex polynomials with $Q, Q_d \ne 0$, $m, m_d \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $\sigma, \sigma_d \in \mathbb{R}$, when

- $A(s)\, Q(N^{-s}) = N^{m s} P(N^{-s})$ for $\operatorname{Re} s > \sigma$,
- $B(s)\, Q_d(N^{-s}) = N^{m_d s} P_d(N^{-s})$ for $\operatorname{Re} s > \sigma_d$, and
- $N^{m_d s} P_d(N^{-s})\, Q(N^{s}) = C N^{r s}\, N^{-m s} P(N^{s})\, Q_d(N^{-s})$ for all $s \in \mathbb{C}$.

Suppose each $(A_j, B_j)$ satisfies this with some data. Then for any data $(P, P_d, Q, Q_d, m, m_d, \sigma, \sigma_d)$ for which the weighted sums $\sum_j \kappa_j A_j$ and $\sum_j \kappa_j B_j$ satisfy the first two conditions, they also satisfy the third: $N^{m_d s} P_d(N^{-s})\, Q(N^{s}) = C N^{r s}\, N^{-m s} P(N^{s})\, Q_d(N^{-s})$ for all $s$.
