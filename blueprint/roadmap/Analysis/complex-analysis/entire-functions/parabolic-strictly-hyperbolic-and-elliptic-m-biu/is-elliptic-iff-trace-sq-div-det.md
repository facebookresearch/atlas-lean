# Elliptic Möbius transformations by trace

Let $g \in GL_2(\mathbb{C})$ act as a Möbius transformation on the Riemann sphere. Call $g$ elliptic when its multiplier $k$ in the normal form has $|k| = 1$ and $g$ is not the identity transformation. Then $g$ is elliptic if and only if $\operatorname{tr}(g)^2 / \det(g)$ is a real number in $[0, 4)$.
