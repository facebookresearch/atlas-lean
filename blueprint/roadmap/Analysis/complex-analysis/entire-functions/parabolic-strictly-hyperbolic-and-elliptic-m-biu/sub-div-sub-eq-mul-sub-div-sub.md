# The normal form as an identity of complex numbers

Let $\alpha \ne \beta$ be finite and suppose every Möbius map $T$ with $T\alpha = 0$ and $T\beta = \infty$ satisfies $T(g x) = k \cdot T x$ for all $x$. Then

$$\frac{w - \alpha}{w - \beta} = k\, \frac{z - \alpha}{z - \beta}$$

whenever $z \ne \beta$ and $w = g z$ is finite.
