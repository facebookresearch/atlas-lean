# Erdős Problem 227 on the ratio of maximum term and maximum modulus

Erdős Problem 227: for every sequence $a_n \in \mathbb{C}$ such that $\sum a_n z^n$ converges for every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and infinitely many $a_n$ are nonzero (an entire function $f(z) = \sum a_n z^n$ that is not a polynomial), if $\mu(r) / M(r)$ tends to a real limit $L$ as $r \to \infty$, then $L = 0$. Here $\mu$ is the [maximum term](max-term.md) and $M$ the [maximum modulus](max-modulus.md).

Its resolution: [Clunie–Hayman theorem on the ratio of maximum term and maximum modulus](maximum-term-ratio-limit-is-zero-refuted.md).
