# Maximum modulus of a power series

For coefficients $a_n \in \mathbb{C}$ ($n \ge 0$) and real $r$, the maximum modulus is the real supremum over the circle of radius $r$,

$$
M(r) = \sup_{|z| = r} \Bigl|\sum_{n \ge 0} a_n z^n\Bigr|,
$$

where the series is an unconditional sum, taken to be $0$ where it does not converge, and the supremum of an empty set or of a set not bounded above is $0$. In particular $M(r) = 0$ for $r < 0$, where the circle is empty.

Classically, when the series converges on all of $\mathbb{C}$ (so that $f(z) = \sum a_n z^n$ is entire) and $r \ge 0$, $|f|$ is continuous on the compact circle, the supremum is attained, and $M(r) = \max_{|z| = r} |f(z)|$.
