# Clunie–Hayman theorem on the ratio of maximum term and maximum modulus

Clunie and Hayman showed that the limit of `μ(r) / M(r)` can take any value in `[0,
1/2]` (Clunie had proved the answer yes when all `a_n ≥ 0`); erdosproblems.com/227 lists the problem
as disproved.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #227, https://www.erdosproblems.com/227 (status DISPROVED); resolution: J. Clunie and W. K. Hayman, The maximum term of a power series, J. Analyse Math. 12 (1964), 143-186, erdosproblems.com/227: disproved by Clunie and Hayman, who showed that the limit can take any value in [0, 1/2], https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02807433.
