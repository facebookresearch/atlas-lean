# Schwarz–Pick lemma

Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic on the open unit disc
$\mathbb{D} = \{z : |z| < 1\}$ and map $\mathbb{D}$ into itself. Then for all $z, w \in \mathbb{D}$,
$|f(z) - f(w)| / |1 - \overline{f(w)}\, f(z)| \le |z - w| / |1 - \bar w z|$.

## Sources

- Schwarz–Pick lemma, G. Pick (1916), building on Schwarz's lemma; see Ahlfors. The statement here is the inequality saying that a holomorphic self-map of the disc does not increase the pseudo-hyperbolic distance $|z - w| / |1 - \bar w z|$.
