# Length of the projection of a polynomial sublevel set

For a complex polynomial $f$ and a complex number $u$, regard $\mathbb{C}$ as a real inner product space and let $\pi_u$ be the orthogonal projection of $\mathbb{C}$ onto the real line $\mathbb{R} u$ spanned by $u$. The projection length is

$$\mathcal{H}^1\big(\pi_u(\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le 1 \})\big) \in [0, \infty],$$

the one-dimensional Hausdorff measure, in the line $\mathbb{R}u$, of the image of the sublevel set. For $u = 0$ the line is $\{0\}$ and the value is $0$.
