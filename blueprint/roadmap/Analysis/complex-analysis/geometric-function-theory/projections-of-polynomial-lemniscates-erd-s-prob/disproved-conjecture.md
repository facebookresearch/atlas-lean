# Negation of the length-2 projection conjecture

The statement: there exists a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ such that, for every unit complex number $u$, the orthogonal projection of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le 1 \}$ onto the real line $\mathbb{R}u$ has one-dimensional Hausdorff measure greater than $2$.
