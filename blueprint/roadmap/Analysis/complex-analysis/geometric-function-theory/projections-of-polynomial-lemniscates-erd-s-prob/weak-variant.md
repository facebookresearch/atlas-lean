# Projection bound 3.3 for polynomial sublevel sets

The statement: for every monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ there exists a unit complex number $u$, $|u| = 1$, such that the orthogonal projection of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le 1 \}$ onto the real line $\mathbb{R}u$ has one-dimensional Hausdorff measure at most $3.3$.
