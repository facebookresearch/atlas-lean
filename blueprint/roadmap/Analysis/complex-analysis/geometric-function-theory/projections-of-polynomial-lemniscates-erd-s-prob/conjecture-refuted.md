# Some polynomial sublevel set has all projections longer than 2

There exists a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ such that for every unit complex number $u$, $|u| = 1$, the orthogonal projection of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le 1 \}$ onto the real line $\mathbb{R}u$ has one-dimensional Hausdorff measure strictly greater than $2$. This is the negation of the Erdős length-2 projection conjecture.

## Sources

erdosproblems.com/1043, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1043. Disproved by Ch. Pommerenke (1961), who exhibited a monic polynomial all of whose line projections of $\{|f| \le 1\}$ have measure at least $2.386$.

Ch. Pommerenke, On metric properties of complex polynomials, *Michigan Math. J.* (1961), 97–115.
