# Forward extension of a Loewner trajectory

Let $\mathbb{H}$ be the upper half-plane, $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ a driving function, $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$. The Loewner trajectory from $z$ extends in $\mathbb{H}$ beyond time $t$ if $z \in \mathbb{H}$ and there are $T > t$ and a curve $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ with $\gamma(0) = z$ such that, for every $s \in [0, T]$, $\gamma(s) \in \mathbb{H}$ and $\gamma$ has derivative $2/(\gamma(s) - W(s))$ at $s$ within $[0, T]$. For $t \ge 0$ this condition describes the unswallowed domain $H_t$ of the Loewner flow: [$z \in H_t$ exactly when the trajectory from $z$ extends beyond $t$](../maximal-solutions-of-the-chordal-loewner-equatio/loewner-unswallowed-domain-eq-set-of-has.md). For $t < 0$ it does not: taking $t < T < 0$ makes $[0, T]$ empty, so every $z \in \mathbb{H}$ qualifies, while $H_t$ is empty.
