# Solution of the Loewner equation on a set of times

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a driving function, $(g_u)_{u \in \mathbb{R}}$ a family of maps $\mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$, $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $s \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. The trajectory $u \mapsto g_u(z)$ solves the Loewner equation on $s$ if, for every $t \in s$, it has derivative $2/(g_t(z) - W(t))$ at $t$ within $s$, the value of the [Loewner vector field](loewner-field.md), with the convention $2/0 = 0$ when $g_t(z) = W(t)$.
