# Local existence and uniqueness for the chordal Loewner equation

Let $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im} z > 0\}$, let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and let $z \in \mathbb{H}$. There are $\varepsilon > 0$ and $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ with $g(0) = z$, $g(t) \in \mathbb{H}$ and

$$
g'(t) = \frac{2}{g(t) - W(t)}
$$

for every $t \in [0, \varepsilon]$ (derivatives within $[0, \varepsilon]$). Moreover, every $h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ with $h(0) = z$ that is continuous on $[0, \varepsilon]$, has right derivative $2/(h(t) - W(t))$ at every $t \in [0, \varepsilon)$ and stays in $\mathbb{H}$ on $[0, \varepsilon]$ agrees with $g$ on $[0, \varepsilon]$.
