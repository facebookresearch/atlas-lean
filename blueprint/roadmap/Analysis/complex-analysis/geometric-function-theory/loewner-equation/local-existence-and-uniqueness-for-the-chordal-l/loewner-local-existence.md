# Local existence for the chordal Loewner equation

Let $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im} z > 0\}$, let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and let $z \in \mathbb{H}$. There are $\varepsilon > 0$ and $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ with $g(0) = z$, $g(t) \in \mathbb{H}$ and $g'(t) = 2/(g(t) - W(t))$ for every $t \in [0, \varepsilon]$, the derivative being taken within $[0, \varepsilon]$, one-sided at the endpoints (Lawler 2005, Chapter 4).
