# Additivity of half-plane capacity under composition

Let $f, g : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be hydrodynamically normalized at infinity with [half-plane capacity](../basic-objects-of-the-chordal-loewner-equation/has-half-plane-capacity.md) $a$ and $b$ respectively. Then $g \circ f$ is hydrodynamically normalized with half-plane capacity $a + b$.
