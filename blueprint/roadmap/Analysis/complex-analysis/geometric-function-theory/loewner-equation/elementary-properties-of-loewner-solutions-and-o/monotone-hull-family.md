# Increasing family of compact hulls

A family $(K_t)_{t \in \mathbb{R}}$ of subsets of $\mathbb{C}$ is an increasing family of compact $\mathbb{H}$-hulls if every $K_t$ is a [compact $\mathbb{H}$-hull](../basic-objects-of-the-chordal-loewner-equation/is-compact-hull.md) and $K_s \subseteq K_t$ whenever $s \le t$.
