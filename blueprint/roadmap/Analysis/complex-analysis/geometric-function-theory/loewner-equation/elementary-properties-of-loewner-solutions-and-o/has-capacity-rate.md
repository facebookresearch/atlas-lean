# Half-plane capacity rate of a Loewner family

Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$. A family $(g_t)_{t \in \mathbb{R}}$ of maps $\mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ has capacity rate $a$ if, for every $t \ge 0$, $g_t$ is hydrodynamically normalized at infinity with [half-plane capacity](../basic-objects-of-the-chordal-loewner-equation/has-half-plane-capacity.md) $a t$.
