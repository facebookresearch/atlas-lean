# Inverse of the maximal Loewner map

Let $\mathbb{H}$ be the upper half-plane, let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and $t \ge 0$. The [Loewner map](loewner-maximal-maps.md) $g_t$ maps the [unswallowed domain](loewner-unswallowed-domain.md) $H_t$ onto $\mathbb{H}$, and its inverse $g_t^{-1}$ sends each $y \in \mathbb{H}$ to a point of $H_t$ mapped to $y$ by $g_t$; outside $\mathbb{H}$ it is set to $0$.
