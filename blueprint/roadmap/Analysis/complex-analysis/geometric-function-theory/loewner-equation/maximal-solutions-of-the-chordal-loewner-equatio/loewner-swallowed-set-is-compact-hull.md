# Loewner hulls are compact hulls

Let $\mathbb{H}$ be the upper half-plane, let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and $t \ge 0$. The [Loewner hull](loewner-swallowed-set.md) $K_t$ is a compact $\mathbb{H}$-hull: a bounded subset of $\mathbb{H}$, relatively closed in $\mathbb{H}$, whose complement $\mathbb{H} \setminus K_t$ is open and simply connected (G. F. Lawler, *Conformally Invariant Processes in the Plane*, Amer. Math. Soc. (2005), Chapter 4). The statement does not include the normalization of the half-plane capacity of $K_t$.
