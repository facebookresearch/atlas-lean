# Maximal solution of the chordal Loewner equation

Let $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im} z > 0\}$, let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and let $z \in \mathbb{H}$, with [lifetime](loewner-lifetime.md) $T_z \in [0, \infty]$ and [maximal curve](loewner-maximal-curve.md) $t \mapsto g_t(z)$. Then $T_z > 0$, $g_0(z) = z$, $g_t(z) \in \mathbb{H}$ for $0 \le t < T_z$, and the Loewner equation $\partial_t g_t(z) = 2/(g_t(z) - W(t))$ holds at $t = 0$ from the right and at every $0 < t < T_z$ (Lawler 2005, Chapter 4).
