# Unswallowed domain of the Loewner flow

Let $\mathbb{H} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im} z > 0\}$, $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$. The domain of points not swallowed by time $t$ is

$$
H_t = \{ z \in \mathbb{H} : 0 \le t < T_z \},
$$

where $T_z$ is the [lifetime](loewner-lifetime.md) of $z$ under the Loewner flow driven by $W$; it is empty for $t < 0$.
