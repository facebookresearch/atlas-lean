# Lifetime of a point under the Loewner flow

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $z \in \mathbb{C}$. The lifetime $T_z \in [0, \infty]$ of $z$ under the Loewner flow driven by $W$ is the supremum of the [admissible durations](is-loewner-admissible-duration.md) for $z$, with $T_z = 0$ when there is none.
