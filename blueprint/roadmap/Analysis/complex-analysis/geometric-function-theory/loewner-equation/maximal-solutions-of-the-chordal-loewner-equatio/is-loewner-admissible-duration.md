# Admissible duration for the Loewner equation

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $z \in \mathbb{C}$. A real number $T$ is an admissible duration for $z$ under the Loewner flow driven by $W$ if $T > 0$ and there is a [forward solution](loewner-forward-solution.md) of the Loewner equation from $z$ on $[0, T]$.
