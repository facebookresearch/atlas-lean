# Chosen admissible Loewner duration

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $z \in \mathbb{C}$, and let $t$ be a time with $0 \le t < T_z$, where $T_z$ is the [lifetime](loewner-lifetime.md) of $z$. Then some [admissible duration](is-loewner-admissible-duration.md) $T$ for $z$ exceeds $t$; the chosen admissible duration is one such $T > t$, fixed once and for all.
