# Time shift of a Loewner forward solution

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $z \in \mathbb{C}$, $T \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s \ge 0$. If $\gamma$ is a [forward solution](loewner-forward-solution.md) of the Loewner equation driven by $W$ from $z$ on $[0, T]$, then the restarted curve $u \mapsto \gamma(s + u)$ is a forward solution of the Loewner equation driven by $u \mapsto W(s + u)$ from $\gamma(s)$ on $[0, T - s]$.
