# Maximal Loewner trajectory

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with [lifetime](loewner-lifetime.md) $T_z$. The maximal trajectory $t \mapsto g_t(z)$ is defined as follows: for $0 \le t < T_z$, $g_t(z)$ is the value at time $t$ of the [chosen forward solution](selected-loewner-solution.md) on an interval $[0, T]$ with $T > t$ (by uniqueness of solutions it does not depend on the choice); for $t < 0$ and for $t \ge T_z$, $g_t(z) = z$ by convention.
