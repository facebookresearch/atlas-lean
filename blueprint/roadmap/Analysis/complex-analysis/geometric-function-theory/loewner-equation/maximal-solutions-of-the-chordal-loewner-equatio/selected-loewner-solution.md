# Chosen Loewner forward solution

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $0 \le t < T_z$, where $T_z$ is the [lifetime](loewner-lifetime.md) of $z$. The chosen forward solution is a [forward solution](loewner-forward-solution.md) of the Loewner equation driven by $W$ from $z$ on $[0, T]$, where $T > t$ is the [chosen admissible duration](selected-loewner-duration.md), fixed once and for all.
