# Loewner map as a homeomorphism onto the half-plane

Let $\mathbb{H}$ be the upper half-plane, let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and $t \ge 0$. The [Loewner map](loewner-maximal-maps.md) $g_t$ restricts to a homeomorphism from the [unswallowed domain](loewner-unswallowed-domain.md) $H_t$ onto $\mathbb{H}$, with inverse the [inverse Loewner map](loewner-maximal-maps-inverse.md).
