# Reflection of a Loewner forward solution

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $T \in \mathbb{R}$. If $\gamma$ is a [forward solution](loewner-forward-solution.md) of the Loewner equation driven by $W$ from $z$ on $[0, T]$, then the reflected curve $t \mapsto -\overline{\gamma(t)}$ is a forward solution of the Loewner equation driven by $-W$ from $-\bar z$ on $[0, T]$.
