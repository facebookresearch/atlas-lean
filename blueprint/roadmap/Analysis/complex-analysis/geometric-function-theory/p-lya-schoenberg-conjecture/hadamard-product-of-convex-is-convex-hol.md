# Ruscheweyh–Sheil-Small proof of the Pólya–Schoenberg conjecture

This settles [Pólya–Schoenberg conjecture](hadamard-product-of-convex-is-convex.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Ruscheweyh and Sheil-Small proved the Pólya–Schoenberg conjecture: the Hadamard
product of two convex univalent functions normalized as in `S` is again convex univalent and in `S`
(Hayman–Lingham Update 6.9).

## Sources

- W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 6.9 (Schoenberg's conjecture); resolution: St. Ruscheweyh and T. Sheil-Small, Hadamard products of Schlicht functions and the Pólya-Schoenberg conjecture, Comment. Math. Helv. 48 (1973), 119-135; recorded in Hayman–Lingham, Update 6.9, Hayman–Lingham Update 6.9: "Ruscheweyh and Sheil-Small have proved this result" (the Hadamard product of two convex functions in S is a convex function in S).
