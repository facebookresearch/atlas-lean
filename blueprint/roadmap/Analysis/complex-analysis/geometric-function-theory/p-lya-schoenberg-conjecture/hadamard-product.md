# Hadamard product of power series

For $f, g : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ with Taylor coefficients $a_n = f^{(n)}(0)/n!$ and $b_n = g^{(n)}(0)/n!$ at $0$, the Hadamard product is

$$
(f * g)(z) = \sum_{n \ge 0} a_n b_n z^n .
$$

The definition is total: an iterated derivative that does not exist is taken to be $0$, and the series is an unconditional sum, taken to be $0$ at any $z$ where it does not converge. For $f$ and $g$ holomorphic near $0$, $a_n$ and $b_n$ are the classical Taylor coefficients and $f * g$ is the classical Hadamard product wherever the series converges; for $f, g$ in the class $S$ this includes the open unit disc, where $|a_n b_n| \le n^2$.
