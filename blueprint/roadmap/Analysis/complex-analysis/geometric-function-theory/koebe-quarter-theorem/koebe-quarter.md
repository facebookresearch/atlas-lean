# Koebe quarter theorem

Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic and injective on the
open unit disk $\mathbb{D} = \{z : |z| < 1\}$, with $f(0) = 0$ and $f'(0) = 1$. Then the image
$f(\mathbb{D})$ contains the open disk $\{w : |w| < 1/4\}$.

## Sources

- Koebe quarter theorem, conjectured by P. Koebe in 1907 and proved by L. Bieberbach, Sitzungsberichte der Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften (1916), 940–955; see Pommerenke, Univalent Functions. The statement here is the inclusion of the disc of radius $1/4$ about $0$ in the image of the unit disc under a normalized univalent function.
