# Quartic with simple roots and a non-convex sublevel component

There exist a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree $4$ with $4$ distinct roots and a real $c > 0$ such that $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le c \}$ has exactly $4$ connected components, at least one of which is not convex.
