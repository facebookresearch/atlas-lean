# Non-convex sublevel component of z(z⁵ - 1)

The statement: the strict sublevel set

$$\Big\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : \big|z (z^5 - 1)\big| < 5 \cdot 6^{-6/5} \Big\}$$

has exactly $6$ connected components, and at least one of them is not convex.

## Sources

The referee of A. W. Goodman's 1966 paper, as recorded at erdosproblems.com/1047, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1047.
