# Negation of the sublevel-set convexity conjecture

The statement: there exist a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ with exactly $m$ distinct roots and a real $c > 0$ such that the closed sublevel set $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le c \}$ has exactly $m$ connected components, at least one of which is not convex.
