# Non-convex sublevel component with simple roots

The statement: there exist a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree $4$ with $4$ distinct roots and a real $c > 0$ such that the closed sublevel set $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le c \}$ has exactly $4$ connected components and at least one of them is not convex.

## Sources

A. W. Goodman (1966), as recorded at erdosproblems.com/1047, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1047.
