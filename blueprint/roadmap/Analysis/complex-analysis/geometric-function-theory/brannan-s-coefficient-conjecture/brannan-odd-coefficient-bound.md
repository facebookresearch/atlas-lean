# Brannan's conjecture for odd coefficients

Brannan's Problem 5.44 in Hayman and Lingham's list concerns the coefficients $A_n(x)$ of $(1 + xz)^\alpha / (1 - z) = \sum_n A_n(x) z^n$ ([Brannan's coefficients](brannan-coeff.md)), the case $\beta = 1$ of $(1 + xz)^\alpha (1 - z)^{-\beta}$. It asks whether $|A_{2n+1}(x)| \le |A_{2n+1}(1)|$ for $n \ge 2$, the case $n = 1$ being already known and the inequality failing for even indices.

The proposition linked here covers every odd index $2n + 1 \ge 3$, the previously known case $n = 1$ included: for every real $\alpha$ with $0 < \alpha < 1$, every $x \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|x| = 1$ and every integer $n \ge 1$,

$$
|A_{2n+1}(x)| \le |A_{2n+1}(1)|.
$$

Its resolution: [Proof of Brannan's conjecture for odd coefficients](brannan-odd-coefficient-bound-holds.md).
