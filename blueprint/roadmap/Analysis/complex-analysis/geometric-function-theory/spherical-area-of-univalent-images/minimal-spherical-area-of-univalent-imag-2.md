# The image of the disc under a normalized univalent function has spherical area at least half the sphere

This settles [Minimal spherical area of the image of a univalent function](minimal-spherical-area-of-univalent-imag.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Dufresnoy's lemma, stated with its equality case as Yamashita's Lemma (2.1)-(2.2),
gives `f^#(0)² ≤ β / (π - β)` (sphere of area `π`) for meromorphic `f` on `𝔻` with image of
spherical area at most `β < π`, with equality only for Möbius maps onto a spherical cap. For `f ∈
S`, `f^#(0) = 1`, so the image covers at least half the sphere, and equality forces `f(z) = z`.
