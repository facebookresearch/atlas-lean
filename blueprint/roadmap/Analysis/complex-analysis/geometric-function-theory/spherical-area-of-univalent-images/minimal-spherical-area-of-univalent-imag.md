# Minimal spherical area of the image of a univalent function

Hayman–Lingham Problem 6.78 (Y. Avci), answered: the minimum over `f ∈ S` of the spherical
area of `f(𝔻)` is `2π` (half the sphere), attained only by `f(z) = z`.

Its resolution: [The image of the disc under a normalized univalent function has spherical area at least half the sphere](minimal-spherical-area-of-univalent-imag-2.md).
