# Bishop–Jones theorem on preimages of regular curves

This settles [Preimages of regular curves under conformal maps have finite length](conjecture.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Bishop and Jones (Harmonic measure and arclength, Ann. of Math. 132 (1990) 511-547, Theorem 3) prove that for a connected Ahlfors-regular curve $\Gamma$ there is $C_\Gamma$ with $\operatorname{length}(\Phi^{-1}(\Gamma \cap \Omega)) < C_\Gamma$ for every Riemann map $\Phi$ of the disc onto a simply connected domain $\Omega$. Their theorem does not require $\Gamma$ to be compact; the linked statement is the special case of a regular set that is a continuous image of a compact interval, and such an image is connected. A univalent $f$ on the disc is a Riemann map onto $f(D)$, so $f^{-1}(\Gamma) \cap D$ has finite length.
