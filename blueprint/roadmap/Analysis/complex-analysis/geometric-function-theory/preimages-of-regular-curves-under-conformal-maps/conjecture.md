# Preimages of regular curves under conformal maps have finite length

For every [regular curve](is-regular-curve.md) $\Gamma$, in particular a continuous image of a compact interval, and every $f$ that is holomorphic on the open unit disc $D$, has nonvanishing derivative there and is injective on $D$, the preimage $f^{-1}(\Gamma) \cap D$ has finite one-dimensional Hausdorff measure.

Conformal includes global injectivity on the disc: without it, $\exp(-(1 + z)/(1 - z))$ pulls the compact segment $[0, 1]$ back to infinitely many arcs whose lengths are asymptotic to $1/(2|k|)$, a set of infinite length, and $[0, 1]$ is a regular curve.

Its resolution: [Bishop–Jones theorem on preimages of regular curves](conjecture-holds.md).
