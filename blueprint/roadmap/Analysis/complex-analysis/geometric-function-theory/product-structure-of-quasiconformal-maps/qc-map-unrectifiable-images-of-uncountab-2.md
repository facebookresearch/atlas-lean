# Bishop–Hakobyan–Williams theorem on unrectifiable images of lines

Bishop, Hakobyan and Williams constructed a planar quasiconformal map and a set `S ⊆
ℝ` of Hausdorff dimension arbitrarily close to 1, or equal to 1, such
that the image of every line `ℝ × {y}`, `y ∈ S`, has no rectifiable subarc, and state that this
answers AimPL Problem 5.3; such an `S` is totally disconnected, so the image of the union of these
lines contains no rectifiable curve either (module documentation).

## Sources

- AimPL: Mapping theory in metric spaces, Problem 5.3 (H. Hakobyan, L. Kovalev, J. Tyson), http://aimpl.org/mappingmetric/5/; resolution: C. J. Bishop, H. Hakobyan and M. Williams, Quasisymmetric dimension distortion of Ahlfors regular subsets of a metric space, Geom. Funct. Anal. 26 (2016), 379-421, arXiv:1211.0233, Section 1.4: Problem 5.3 of [10] (AimPL mapping theory list) asks for an uncountable family of lines whose quasiconformal images contain no rectifiable subarcs, and Corollary 1.8 answers it 'with a resounding yes'; Theorem 1.9 even gives a compact S ⊂ [0, 1] of Hausdorff dimension 1 and a quasiconformal f with no rectifiable subarc in f([0, 1] × \{y\}) for every y ∈ S, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-016-0368-5.
