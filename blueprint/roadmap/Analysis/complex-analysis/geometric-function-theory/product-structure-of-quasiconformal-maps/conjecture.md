# Quasiconformal maps preserving coordinate directions split

Every quasiconformal homeomorphism $f$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ whose differential at almost every point maps each coordinate direction to a multiple of a coordinate direction (with the same pattern, diagonal or anti-diagonal, for both directions) splits: $f(x_1, x_2) = (g(x_1), h(x_2))$ or $f(x_1, x_2) = (g(x_2), h(x_1))$ for some functions $g, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$.
