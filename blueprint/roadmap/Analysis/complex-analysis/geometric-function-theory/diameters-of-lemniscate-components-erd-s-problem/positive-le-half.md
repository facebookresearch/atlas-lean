# Sublevel component of 0 has diameter at least 2 for roots in the half disc

The statement: for every real $r$ with $0 \le r \le 1/2$ and every monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ all of whose roots satisfy $|z| \le r$, the connected component containing $0$ of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le 1 \}$ has diameter at least $2$. Diameters are taken in $[0, \infty]$.
