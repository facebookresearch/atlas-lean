# Erdős large-component conjecture for polynomial sublevel sets

The statement: for every real $r$ with $0 < r < 2$ and every monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ all of whose roots satisfy $|z| \le r$, some connected component of the strict sublevel set $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| < 1 \}$ has diameter strictly greater than $2 - r$. Diameters are taken in $[0, \infty]$.

## Sources

erdosproblems.com/1048, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1048. Disproved by Ch. Pommerenke (1961) for $r > 1$.

Ch. Pommerenke, On metric properties of complex polynomials, *Michigan Math. J.* (1961), 97–115.
