# Polynomial sublevel sets need not have a component of diameter above 2 - r

There exist a real $r$ with $0 < r < 2$ and a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$, all of whose roots satisfy $|z| \le r$, such that every connected component of the strict sublevel set $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| < 1 \}$ has diameter at most $2 - r$. This is the negation of the Erdős large-component conjecture.

## Sources
