# Lower bound 1/r for the sublevel component of the origin

For every real $r$ with $\tfrac12 < r \le \tfrac{\sqrt5 - 1}{2}$ and every monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ all of whose roots satisfy $|z| \le r$, the connected component containing $0$ of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| \le 1 \}$ has diameter strictly greater than $1/r$. Diameters are taken in $[0, \infty]$.
