# Diameters of sublevel components of zⁿ - rⁿ tend to zero

For every real $r > 1$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$, for all sufficiently large natural numbers $n$, every connected component of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |z^n - r^n| \le 1 \}$ has diameter less than $\varepsilon$. Diameters are taken in $[0, \infty]$.
