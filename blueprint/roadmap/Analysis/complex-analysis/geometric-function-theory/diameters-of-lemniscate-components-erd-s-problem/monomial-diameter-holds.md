# Unit sublevel component of zⁿ at the origin has diameter 2

For every natural number $n \ge 1$, the connected component containing $0$ of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |z^n| \le 1 \}$ has diameter exactly $2$ (in $[0, \infty]$).
