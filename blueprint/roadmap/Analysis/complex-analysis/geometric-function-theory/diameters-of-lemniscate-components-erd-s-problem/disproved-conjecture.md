# Negation of the large-component conjecture

The statement: there exist a real $r$ with $0 < r < 2$ and a monic polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ of degree at least $1$ with all roots in $|z| \le r$ such that every connected component of $\{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| < 1 \}$ has diameter at most $2 - r$.
