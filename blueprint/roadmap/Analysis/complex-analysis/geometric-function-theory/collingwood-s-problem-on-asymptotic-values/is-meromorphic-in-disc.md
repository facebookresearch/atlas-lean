# Meromorphic functions in the unit disc

The notion of meromorphic in the open unit disc $D$ used in Problem 5.19, for a function $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ together with a set $P$ of poles: $P \subseteq D$; $P$ is discrete in its own topology; $f$ is complex differentiable on $D \setminus P$; and $|f(w)| \to \infty$ as $w$ approaches each pole off the poles, that is, for every $p \in P$ and every real $M$ there is a neighbourhood $U$ of $p$ with $|f(w)| \ge M$ for every $w \in U \setminus P$. The values of $f$ at the poles play no role.

The pole condition is a limit, not mere unboundedness: $e^{1/z}$ is unbounded near $0$ but tends to $0$ along the negative real axis, so $0$ is not a pole of it in this sense.
