# Logarithmic coefficients of a univalent function

For $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ and $\gamma : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$, the numbers $\gamma_k$ ($k \ge 1$) are logarithmic coefficients of $f$ when there is a function $L$, holomorphic on the open unit disc $D$, such that

- $L(0) = 0$;
- $e^{L(z)} = f(z)/z$ for every $z \in D$ with $z \ne 0$;
- $L(z) = 2 \sum_{k \ge 1} \gamma_k z^k$, the series converging, for every $z \in D$.

So $L$ is the branch of $\log(f(z)/z)$ through the removable extension at the origin: the equation $e^{L(z)} = f(z)/z$ is imposed only for $z \ne 0$, and $L(0) = 0$ is required separately. The value $\gamma_0$ plays no role.
