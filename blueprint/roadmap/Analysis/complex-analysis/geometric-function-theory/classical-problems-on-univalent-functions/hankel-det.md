# Hankel determinants of Taylor coefficients

For $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ and integers $k, n \ge 0$, the Hankel determinant is

$$
A_n^{(k)} = \det\bigl(a_{n+i+j}\bigr)_{0 \le i, j < k}, \qquad a_m = \frac{f^{(m)}(0)}{m!},
$$

where an iterated derivative that does not exist is taken to be $0$. For $f$ holomorphic near $0$, such as $f$ in the class $S$, the $a_m$ are the Taylor coefficients of $f$ at $0$.
