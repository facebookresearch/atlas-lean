# Symmetry of bounded semialgebraic sets with compact holomorphic quotient

Let $V\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ be the common zero set of a family of complex polynomials. Let $U\subseteq V$ be relatively open, connected (hence nonempty), bounded, and semialgebraic in the real and imaginary coordinates, using finite Boolean combinations of polynomial equalities and strict inequalities. Suppose a discrete group acts properly on $U$ with compact orbit space. For each group element, its action on $U$ must extend holomorphically to an ambient neighborhood of each point of $U$. Then $U$ is smooth and biholomorphic to a bounded symmetric domain $D\subseteq\mathbb C^m$ for some $m\geq0$. Smoothness means there are relative neighborhoods biholomorphic to open subsets of complex affine space. The biholomorphisms are homeomorphisms with both directions locally extendable holomorphically in the ambient coordinates. Here $D$ is open, connected and bounded, and each point of $D$ is an isolated fixed point of a biholomorphic involution of $D$. The action need not be free and $V$ need not be smooth.

## Depends on

Use relative analytic charts, ambient holomorphic extensions, semialgebraic sets in real coordinates, and the topological quotient by a proper group action.

## Proof depends on

The compact-quotient argument needs separate smoothness of the relative domain and extension of its local symmetries to globally defined involutions.

## Expansion

Formalize the ambient-extension condition even at singular points of $V$, and prove that the compact quotient rules out singularities on $U$. Then isolate the automorphism-group argument producing the isolated fixed-point involutions. This is not a classification of all projective universal covers.
