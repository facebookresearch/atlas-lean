# Is every complex surface exhausted by balls biholomorphic to the ball, Δ × ℂ or ℂ²?

SCV Problem List, Problem 4.3.2, asks whether a two-dimensional complex manifold in which every
compact set lies in an open subset biholomorphic to the unit ball must be biholomorphic to the
ball, to $\Delta \times \mathbb{C}$ or to $\mathbb{C}^2$. This page states that proposed answer as
a claim, not as a theorem: every Hausdorff, second countable two-dimensional complex manifold $M$
(charts in $\mathbb{C}^2$ with holomorphic transition maps) in which every compact set lies in an
open subset biholomorphic to the unit ball of $\mathbb{C}^2$ is biholomorphic to that ball, to
$\Delta \times \mathbb{C}$ or to $\mathbb{C}^2$.

Its resolution: [Fornæss's short ℂ² refutes the trichotomy](union-of-balls-trichotomy-refuted.md).
