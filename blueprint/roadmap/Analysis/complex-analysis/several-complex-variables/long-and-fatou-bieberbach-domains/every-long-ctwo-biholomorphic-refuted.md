# Wold's long ℂ² that is not biholomorphic to ℂ²

Wold constructs a long ℂ² (an increasing union of proper subdomains biholomorphic to
ℂ²) which is not Stein, hence not biholomorphic to ℂ².

## Sources

- G. Buzzard, J. E. Fornæss, E. A. Gavosto, S. G. Krantz (eds.), The Several Complex Variables Problem List, arXiv:math/9509202 (1995), Problem 14; resolution: E. F. Wold, A long ℂ² which is not Stein, Ark. Mat. 48 (2010), 207-210, Theorem 1.1 (a complex manifold X = ⋃ Xᵢ with Xᵢ ⊂ Xᵢ₊₁ and Xᵢ biholomorphic to ℂ², and X not Stein), answering negatively the question 'Is any long ℂ² biholomorphic to ℂ²?' posed in §1, https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/116/7969-11512_2008_Article_84.pdf.
