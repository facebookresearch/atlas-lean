# Gompf's pseudoconvex domains homotopy equivalent to the 2-sphere

This settles [Forstnerič's question on pseudoconvex domains homotopic to the 2-sphere](pseudoconvex-domain-homotopy-sphere-exis.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Gompf's Stein open subsets of ℂ² homeomorphic to S² × ℝ² are pseudoconvex domains
homotopy equivalent to S², counterexamples to Forstnerič's conjecture that no Stein open subset of
ℂ² is homotopy equivalent to S².

## Sources

- G. Buzzard, J. E. Fornæss, E. A. Gavosto, S. G. Krantz (eds.), The Several Complex Variables Problem List, arXiv:math/9509202 (1995), Problem 7.1.1; resolution: R. E. Gompf, Stein surfaces as open subsets of ℂ², J. Symplectic Geom. 3 (2005), 565-587, arXiv:math/0501509 (numbering of arXiv version v3), Examples 2.5(a): for every smooth 2-knot K ⊂ ℂ², a Stein open subset U ⊂ ℂ² homeomorphic to S² × ℝ², giving 'a plethora of counterexamples to the conjecture of [Fo1] that no Stein open subset of ℂ² is homotopy equivalent to S²' ([Fo1] = Forstnerič, Duke Math. J. 67 (1992)), https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0501509.
