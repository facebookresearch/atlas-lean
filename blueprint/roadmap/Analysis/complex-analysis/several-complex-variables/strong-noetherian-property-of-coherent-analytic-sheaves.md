# Strong Noetherian property of coherent analytic sheaves

This node is still to be broken down: the statement is precise, but its proof is not.

Let $M$ be a complex manifold, $\mathcal{F}$ a coherent analytic sheaf on $M$ and $\mathcal{F}_1
\subseteq \mathcal{F}_2 \subseteq \cdots$ an increasing sequence of coherent subsheaves of
$\mathcal{F}$. Then the sequence is stationary on every compact subset of $M$: for every compact $K
\subseteq M$ there is $k_0$ such that $\mathcal{F}_k$ and $\mathcal{F}_{k_0}$ have the same stalks
at all points of $K$ for $k \ge k_0$.

Demailly's proof reduces to subsheaves of $\mathcal{O}_M$, $\mathcal{F}$ being locally a quotient of
$\mathcal{O}_M^{\oplus q}$. If some $\mathcal{F}_{k_0}$ is nonzero on a connected $M$, its stalks
are nonzero everywhere by analytic continuation, so it contains a Weierstrass polynomial $P$ of some
degree $\mu$ in $z_n$ near any point; division by $P$ shows that the stalks of the $\mathcal{F}_k$,
$k \ge k_0$, are generated by $P$ and by polynomials of degree less than $\mu$ in $z_n$ with
coefficients holomorphic in $z' = (z_1, \dots, z_{n-1})$, and induction on $n$ applies. Demailly
uses the property for the coherence of multiplier ideal sheaves (*Analytic Methods*, Proposition
(5.7)).

## Proof depends on

- [Weierstrass preparation theorem](weierstrass-preparation.md), for the Weierstrass polynomial $P$
  in $\mathcal{F}_{k_0}$ near a point

The other steps of its proof are not pages yet: the reduction to subsheaves of
$\mathcal{O}_M^{\oplus q}$ and then of $\mathcal{O}_M$, analytic continuation, the Weierstrass
division theorem and the induction on the dimension (Demailly, Chapter II, (3.22)).
