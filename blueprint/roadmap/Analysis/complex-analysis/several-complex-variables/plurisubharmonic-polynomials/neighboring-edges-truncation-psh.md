# Are truncations to neighboring extreme edges plurisubharmonic near the origin?

The remark to Problem 2 of the AIM list (B. Stensønes) asserts that the truncation $q$ of $p$ to two neighboring extreme edges is plurisubharmonic, and Simon and Stensønes ask the local form of this as the first item of their Question 3.6. This page states that proposed claim, not a theorem: for every real-valued polynomial $p$ on $\mathbb{C}^2$ that is plurisubharmonic, and all distinct extreme edges $\Gamma_1, \Gamma_2$ of $N(p)$ with $\Gamma_1 \cap \Gamma_2 \ne \emptyset$, there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that the complex Hessian of the truncation $p_{\Gamma_1 \cup \Gamma_2}$ is positive semidefinite at every point $x$ with $\|x\| < \varepsilon$, that is, the truncation is plurisubharmonic on a neighborhood of the origin.

Its resolution: [Simon–Stensønes counterexample on truncations of plurisubharmonic polynomials](neighboring-edges-truncation-psh-refuted.md).
