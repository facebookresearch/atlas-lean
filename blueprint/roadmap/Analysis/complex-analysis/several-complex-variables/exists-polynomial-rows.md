# Polynomial numerators of rows of a bivariate series

Let $A : \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$, let $D_1, D_2$ be complex polynomials with $D_2(0) \ne 0$, let $P$ be a complex polynomial in two variables, and let $r > 0$. Suppose that for all $|X|, |Y| < r$ the series $\sum_{m} A_{m_1 m_2} X^{m_1} Y^{m_2}$ converges absolutely and

$$\Big(\sum_m A_{m_1 m_2} X^{m_1} Y^{m_2}\Big)\, D_1(X)\, D_2(Y) = P(X, Y).$$

Then there are polynomials $q_j$ and $r_2 > 0$ such that for every $j$ and $|X| < r$ the row series $\sum_m A_{m j} X^m$ converges absolutely with $\big(\sum_m A_{mj} X^m\big) D_1(X) = q_j(X)$, and for every complex $X$ and $|Y| < r_2$ the series $\sum_j q_j(X) Y^j$ converges absolutely with $\big(\sum_j q_j(X) Y^j\big) D_2(Y) = P(X, Y)$.
