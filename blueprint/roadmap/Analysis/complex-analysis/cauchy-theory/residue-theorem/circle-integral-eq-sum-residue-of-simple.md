# Circle integrals of functions with simple poles

Let $R > 0$, $z_0 \in \mathbb{C}$, $Z$ a finite set of points of the open disc of radius $R$ about $z_0$, and $h, c : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$. If $h$ is analytic at every point of the closed disc outside $Z$, and for each $a \in Z$ there is $g$ analytic at $a$ with $h(z) = c(a)/(z - a) + g(z)$ for all $z \ne a$ in a neighbourhood of $a$ (a punctured neighbourhood), then $\oint_{|z - z_0| = R} h(z)\,dz = 2\pi i \sum_{a \in Z} c(a)$.
