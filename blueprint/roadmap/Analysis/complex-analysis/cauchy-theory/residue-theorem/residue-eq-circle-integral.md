# The residue as a circle integral

Let $E$ be a complex Banach space and $f : \mathbb{C} \to E$ holomorphic on $\Omega \setminus \{a\}$, and let $r > 0$ with the closed disc of radius $r$ about $a$ contained in $\Omega$. Then the residue of $f$ at $a$, defined as the limit of $(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_{|z-a|=\rho} f(z)\,dz$ as $\rho \downarrow 0$, equals $(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_{|z-a|=r} f(z)\,dz$.
