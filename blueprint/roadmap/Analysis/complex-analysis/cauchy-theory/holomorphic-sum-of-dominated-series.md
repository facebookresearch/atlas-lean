# A dominated series of holomorphic functions is holomorphic

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open, let $E$ be a complex Banach space, and let $(F_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of
functions $\mathbb{C} \to E$, indexed by any set $I$, each complex differentiable on $U$. Suppose that
$\|F_i(w)\| \le u_i$ for all $i \in I$ and all $w \in U$, where $(u_i)_{i \in I}$ is a summable family of real numbers.
Then the function $w \mapsto \sum_{i \in I} F_i(w)$ is complex differentiable on $U$.
