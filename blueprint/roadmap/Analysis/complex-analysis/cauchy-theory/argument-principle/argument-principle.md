# Argument principle for a simple closed curve

Let $f$ be meromorphic on an open set $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$, and let $\gamma$ be a simple closed piecewise $C^1$ curve in $\Omega$ whose inside lies in $\Omega$, which winds once around every point of its inside (positive orientation), and which passes through no zero or pole of $f$. Then $f$ has finitely many zeros and poles inside $\gamma$, and

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_\gamma \frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz = N - P,$$

where $N$ and $P$ are the numbers of zeros and of poles of $f$ inside $\gamma$, counted with multiplicity.
