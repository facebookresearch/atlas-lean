# Locally uniform limits of holomorphic functions are holomorphic

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $E$ a complex Banach space. Let $(F_i)$ be a family of functions
$\mathbb{C} \to E$, indexed along a nontrivial filter (for instance a sequence), that converges locally uniformly on
$U$ to a function $f$, and suppose that eventually each $F_i$ is complex differentiable on $U$. Then $f$ is complex
differentiable on $U$.
