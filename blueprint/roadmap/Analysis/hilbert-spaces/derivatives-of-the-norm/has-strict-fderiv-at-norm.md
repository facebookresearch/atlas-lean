# Strict Fréchet derivative of the norm at a nonzero point

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For every $x \ne 0$ in $E$, the norm is strictly Fréchet differentiable at $x$, with derivative the norming functional

$$
h \mapsto \frac{\operatorname{Re} \langle x, h \rangle}{\|x\|}.
$$
