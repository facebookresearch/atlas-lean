# Strict derivative of the squared norm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For every $x \in E$, the map $y \mapsto \|y\|^2$ is strictly Fréchet differentiable at $x$ with derivative $h \mapsto 2 \operatorname{Re} \langle x, h \rangle$.
