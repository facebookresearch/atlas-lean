# Strict derivative of the squared projection residual

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space, and let $S$ be a subspace of $E$ that admits an orthogonal projection $P_S$. For every $x \in E$, the map $y \mapsto \|y - P_S y\|^2$ is strictly Fréchet differentiable at $x$ with derivative $h \mapsto 2 \operatorname{Re} \langle x - P_S x, h \rangle$; this uses that $I - P_S$ is self-adjoint and idempotent.
