# Real part of the inner product as a real-linear functional

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For $x \in E$, the real part of the inner product with $x$ is the bounded real-linear functional $y \mapsto \operatorname{Re} \langle x, y \rangle$ on $E$. It is the derivative at $x$ of half the squared norm, $y \mapsto \tfrac12 \|y\|^2$, and for $x \ne 0$ the derivative of the norm at $x$ is this functional divided by $\|x\|$.
