# Derivative of the squared distance to a point applied to a vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For all $x, a, y \in E$, the Fréchet derivative of $z \mapsto \operatorname{dist}(z, a)^2$ at $x$, applied to $y$, is $2 \operatorname{Re} \langle x - a, y \rangle$.
