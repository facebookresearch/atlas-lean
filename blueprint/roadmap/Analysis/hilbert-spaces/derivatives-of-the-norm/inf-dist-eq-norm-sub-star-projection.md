# Distance to a subspace as the norm of the projection residual

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $S$ be a subspace of $E$ that admits an orthogonal projection $P_S$ (for instance a complete subspace). For every $y \in E$, the distance from $y$ to $S$ is $\operatorname{dist}(y, S) = \|y - P_S y\|$.
