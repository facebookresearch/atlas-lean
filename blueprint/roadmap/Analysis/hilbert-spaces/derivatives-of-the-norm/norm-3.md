# Derivative of the norm along a curve

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to E$ be differentiable at $t$ with derivative $v$, and suppose $f(t) \ne 0$. Then $s \mapsto \|f(s)\|$ is differentiable at $t$ with derivative $\operatorname{Re} \langle f(t), v \rangle / \|f(t)\|$, the normalized real inner product of the position with the velocity.
