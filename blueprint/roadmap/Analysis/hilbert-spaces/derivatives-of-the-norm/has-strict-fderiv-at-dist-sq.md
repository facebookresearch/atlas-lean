# Strict derivative of the squared distance to a point

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For all $x, a \in E$, the map $y \mapsto \operatorname{dist}(y, a)^2$ is strictly Fréchet differentiable at $x$ with derivative $h \mapsto 2 \operatorname{Re} \langle x - a, h \rangle$.
