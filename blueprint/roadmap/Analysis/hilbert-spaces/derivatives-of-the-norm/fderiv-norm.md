# Formula for the derivative of the norm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For every $x \ne 0$ in $E$, the Fréchet derivative of the norm at $x$ is the functional $\|x\|^{-1} \operatorname{Re} \langle x, \cdot \rangle$.
