# A radial primitive is continuously differentiable

Let $E$ be a real inner product space, let $z_0 \in E$, and let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous on $[0, \infty)$ with $f(0) = 0$. Write $F(w) = \int_0^{\|w - z_0\|} f(t)\, dt$ for the radial primitive of $f$ centred at $z_0$. Then $F$ is continuously differentiable on all of $E$, including at the centre $z_0$; $f$ itself need not be differentiable.
