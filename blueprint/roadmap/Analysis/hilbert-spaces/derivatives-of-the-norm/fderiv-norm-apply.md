# Derivative of the norm applied to a vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For all $x, y \in E$ with $x \ne 0$, the Fréchet derivative of the norm at $x$, applied to $y$, is $\operatorname{Re} \langle x, y \rangle / \|x\|$.
