# Operator norm of the real inner product functional

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. For every $x \in E$, the functional $y \mapsto \operatorname{Re} \langle x, y \rangle$ has operator norm $\|x\|$; for $x \ne 0$ it attains this value at $x/\|x\|$.
