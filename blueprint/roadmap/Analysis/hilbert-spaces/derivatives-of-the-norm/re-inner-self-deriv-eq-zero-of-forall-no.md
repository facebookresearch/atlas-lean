# Curves on the unit sphere have velocity orthogonal to position

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space. If $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to E$ is differentiable and $\|\gamma(t)\| = 1$ for every $t$, then $\operatorname{Re} \langle \gamma(t), \gamma'(t) \rangle = 0$ for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
