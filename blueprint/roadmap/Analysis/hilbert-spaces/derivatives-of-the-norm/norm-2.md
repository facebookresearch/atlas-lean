# Chain rule for the Fréchet derivative of a norm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, regarded as a real normed space, and let $G$ be a real normed space. Let $f : G \to E$ be Fréchet differentiable at $x \in G$ with derivative $f'$, and suppose $f(x) \ne 0$. Then $y \mapsto \|f(y)\|$ is Fréchet differentiable at $x$ with derivative $h \mapsto \operatorname{Re} \langle f(x), f'(h) \rangle / \|f(x)\|$.
