# Vectors with the same inner product against every vector are equal

Let $V$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $y, z \in V$. If $\langle x, y \rangle = \langle x, z \rangle$ for every $x \in V$, then $y = z$.

## Sources

- Stephen H. Friedberg, Arnold J. Insel and Lawrence E. Spence, Linear Algebra, 4th ed., Prentice Hall, 2003, §6.1, Theorem 6.1(e), p. 333.
