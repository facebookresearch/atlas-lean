# Inner product on a finite ℓ² product of inner product spaces

Let $\mathbb{F}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\iota$ be a finite index set and let $(H_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a family of inner product spaces over $\mathbb{F}$, not necessarily complete. Here an inner product space over $\mathbb{F}$ is a normed vector space $H$ over $\mathbb{F}$ with a map $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle\colon H \times H \to \mathbb{F}$ that is conjugate-linear in the first argument, satisfies $\langle y, x\rangle = \overline{\langle x, y\rangle}$, and has $\|x\|^2 = \operatorname{Re}\langle x, x\rangle$ for every $x \in H$; following Mathlib, it is therefore linear in the second argument. Write $\mathrm{PiLp}\,2\,H$ for the product $\prod_{i \in \iota} H_i$ of the families $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ with $x_i \in H_i$, with coordinatewise addition and scalar multiplication and the ℓ² norm $\|x\| = \bigl(\sum_{i \in \iota} \|x_i\|^2\bigr)^{1/2}$; Mathlib defines this space, its operations and its norm separately from the inner product. Then $\langle x, y\rangle = \sum_{i \in \iota} \langle x_i, y_i\rangle$ is an inner product on $\mathrm{PiLp}\,2\,H$ whose norm is the ℓ² norm, so $\mathrm{PiLp}\,2\,H$ is an inner product space over $\mathbb{F}$. When every $H_i$ is complete, that is, a Hilbert space, $\mathrm{PiLp}\,2\,H$ is complete as well, by a separate result; it is then the direct sum $H_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus H_n$ of finitely many Hilbert spaces, with this inner product.
