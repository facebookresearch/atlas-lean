# A complete subspace has an orthogonal projection

Let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb K$, where $\mathbb K$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, and let $K$ be a
subspace of $E$ that is complete for the norm of $E$. Then $K$ has an orthogonal projection: every $v \in E$ can be written
as $v = w + (v - w)$ with $w \in K$ and $v - w$ orthogonal to every vector of $K$.
