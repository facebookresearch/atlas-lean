# Paley–Wiener stability theorem

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ with a Hilbert basis $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$, and let $(f_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a family in $E$ such that the series $\sum_i \|f_i - b_i\|^2$ converges with sum less than $1$. Then $(f_i)$ is a [Riesz basis](is-riesz-basis.md) with respect to $(b_i)$.
