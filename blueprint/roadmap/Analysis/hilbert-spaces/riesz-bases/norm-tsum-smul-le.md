# Norm bound for a synthesis series

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $(d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ is a family in $E$ with $\sum_i \|d_i\|^2 < \infty$ and $c \in \ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{K})$, then, by the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality in $\ell^2$,

$$
\Big\| \sum_{i \in \iota} c_i d_i \Big\| \le \Big( \sum_{i \in \iota} \|d_i\|^2 \Big)^{1/2} \|c\|.
$$

Here $\sum_i c_i d_i$ is the unconditional sum in $E$, read as $0$ when the family $(c_i d_i)$ has no sum in $E$. Since $\sum_i \|c_i d_i\| < \infty$, the sum exists when $E$ is complete; in an incomplete $E$ it may not.
