# Riesz basis of a Hilbert space

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a Hilbert basis of $E$. A family $(f_i)_{i \in \iota}$ in $E$ is a **Riesz basis** with respect to $(b_i)$ if there is a bounded invertible operator $T$ on $E$, with bounded inverse, such that $T b_i = f_i$ for every $i$.
