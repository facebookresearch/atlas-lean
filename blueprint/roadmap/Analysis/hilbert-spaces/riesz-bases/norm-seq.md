# Norm sequence of a square-summable family

Let $E$ be a normed additive commutative group, for instance a normed space (it is used below for families in a Hilbert space). For a family $(d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ in $E$ with $\sum_i \|d_i\|^2 < \infty$, the norm sequence is the element $(\|d_i\|)_{i \in \iota}$ of $\ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{R})$.
