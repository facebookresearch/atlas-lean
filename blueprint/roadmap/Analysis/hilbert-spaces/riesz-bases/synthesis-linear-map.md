# Linear map underlying the synthesis operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $(d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a family in $E$ with $\sum_i \|d_i\|^2 < \infty$. The synthesis linear map is the linear map $\ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{K}) \to E$, $c \mapsto \sum_i c_i d_i$.
