# Summability of a synthesis series

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $(d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ is a family in $E$ with $\sum_i \|d_i\|^2 < \infty$ and $c \in \ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{K})$, then the family $(c_i d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ is summable in $E$.
