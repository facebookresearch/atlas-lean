# Riesz bases have dense span

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ with a Hilbert basis $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$. Every [Riesz basis](is-riesz-basis.md) $(f_i)_{i \in \iota}$ with respect to $(b_i)$ has dense span: the closure of the linear span of the $f_i$ is $E$.
