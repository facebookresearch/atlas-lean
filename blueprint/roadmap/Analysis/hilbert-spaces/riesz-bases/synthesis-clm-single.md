# Synthesis operator on a basis vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $(d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a family in $E$ with $\sum_i \|d_i\|^2 < \infty$. For every $i \in \iota$, the [synthesis operator](synthesis-clm.md) $S$ maps the standard basis vector $e_i$ of $\ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ to $S e_i = d_i$.
