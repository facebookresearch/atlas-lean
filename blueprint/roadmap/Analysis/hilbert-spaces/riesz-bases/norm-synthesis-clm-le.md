# Operator norm bound for the synthesis operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $(d_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a family in $E$ with $\sum_i \|d_i\|^2 < \infty$. The [synthesis operator](synthesis-clm.md) $S$ of $(d_i)$ satisfies $\|S\| \le \left(\sum_i \|d_i\|^2\right)^{1/2}$.
