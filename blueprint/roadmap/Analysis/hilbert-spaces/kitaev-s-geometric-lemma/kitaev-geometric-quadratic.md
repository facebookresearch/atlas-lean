# Kitaev's geometric lemma, quadratic-form version

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $A, B$ be positive operators on $E$ whose kernels intersect only in $0$. Let $v \ge 0$ and suppose every eigenvalue $\mu$ of $A$, and every eigenvalue of $B$, is $0$ or has real part at least $v$. Let $\theta$ be the angle between $\ker A$ and $\ker B$, with $\cos\theta = \|P_{\ker A} P_{\ker B}\|$ for the orthogonal projections. Then for every $x \in E$,

$$2 v \sin^2(\theta/2)\, \|x\|^2 \le \operatorname{Re} \langle (A + B) x, x \rangle.$$
