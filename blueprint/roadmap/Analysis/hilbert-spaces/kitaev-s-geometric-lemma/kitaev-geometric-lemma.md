# Kitaev's geometric lemma

Let $A$ and $B$ be positive operators on a finite-dimensional real or complex inner product space, let $v \ge 0$, and suppose that every eigenvalue $\mu$ of $A$, and every eigenvalue of $B$, is either $0$ or has real part at least $v$, and that the kernels of $A$ and $B$ meet only in $0$. With $\vartheta$ the angle between the two kernels, $A + B \ge 2v\sin^2(\vartheta/2)\,I$ in the Loewner order.
