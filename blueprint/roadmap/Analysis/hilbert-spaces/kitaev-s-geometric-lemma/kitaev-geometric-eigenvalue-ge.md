# Eigenvalue form of Kitaev's geometric lemma

Under the hypotheses of Kitaev's geometric lemma (positive operators $A$, $B$ on a finite-dimensional real or complex inner product space, $v \ge 0$, every eigenvalue of $A$ and of $B$ either $0$ or of real part at least $v$, and kernels meeting only in $0$), every eigenvalue $\mu$ of $A + B$ has real part at least $2v\sin^2(\vartheta/2)$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle between the kernels of $A$ and $B$.
