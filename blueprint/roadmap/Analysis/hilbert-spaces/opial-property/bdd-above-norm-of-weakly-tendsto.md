# Weakly convergent sequences are norm-bounded

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. If a sequence $(u_n)$ in $E$ converges weakly to $x$ (that is, $f(u_n) \to f(x)$ for every bounded linear functional $f$ on $E$), then $(\|u_n\|)$ is bounded, by the Banach–Steinhaus theorem applied to the evaluation maps on the dual space.
