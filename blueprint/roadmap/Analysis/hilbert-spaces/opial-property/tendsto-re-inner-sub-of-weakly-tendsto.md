# Cross term vanishes under weak convergence

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$. If a sequence $(u_n)$ in $E$ converges weakly to $x$ (that is, $f(u_n) \to f(x)$ for every bounded linear functional $f$ on $E$), then for every $y \in E$, $\operatorname{Re} \langle u_n - x, x - y \rangle \to 0$: this is the cross term in the expansion of $\|u_n - y\|^2$, and it tends to $0$ because $z \mapsto \langle x - y, z \rangle$ is a bounded linear functional.
