# Opial's property

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$. If a sequence $(u_n)$ converges weakly to $x$ (that is, $f(u_n) \to f(x)$ for every bounded linear functional $f$) and $y \ne x$, then

$$
\liminf_{n \to \infty} \|u_n - x\| < \liminf_{n \to \infty} \|u_n - y\| .
$$
