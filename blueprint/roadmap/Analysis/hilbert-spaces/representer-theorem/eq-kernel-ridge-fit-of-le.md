# Kernel ridge fit is the unique minimizer

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions $X \to \mathbb{R}$, let $x_1, \dots, x_n \in X$, $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $\lambda > 0$. If $g \in H$ has [kernel ridge objective](kernel-ridge-risk.md) at most that of the [kernel ridge fit](kernel-ridge-fit.md), then $g$ is the kernel ridge fit: the fit is the unique minimizer.
