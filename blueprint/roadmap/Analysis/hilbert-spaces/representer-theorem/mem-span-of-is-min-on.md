# Representer theorem

Let $H$ be a real Hilbert space, let $L_1, \dots, L_m$ be bounded linear functionals on $H$ with Riesz representers $h_1, \dots, h_m$ (so $L_i(g) = \langle h_i, g \rangle$), let $F : \mathbb{R}^m \to \mathbb{R}$ be any function and let $\Lambda > 0$. If $f \in H$ minimizes $g \mapsto F(L_1 g, \dots, L_m g) + \Lambda \|g\|^2$ over all of $H$, then $f$ lies in the span of $h_1, \dots, h_m$.
