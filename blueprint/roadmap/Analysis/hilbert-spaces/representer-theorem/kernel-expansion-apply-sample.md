# Values of a kernel expansion at the sample points

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions $X \to \mathbb{R}$ with kernel functions $k_x$, let $x_1, \dots, x_n \in X$ and let $K$ be the [kernel Gram matrix](kernel-gram.md). For every $c \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and every $j$, the [kernel expansion](kernel-expansion.md) satisfies

$$
\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{n} c_i k_{x_i}\Big)(x_j) = (K c)_j.
$$
