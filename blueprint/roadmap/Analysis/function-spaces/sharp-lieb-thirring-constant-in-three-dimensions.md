# Sharp Lieb–Thirring kinetic constant in three dimensions

Determine whether the semiclassical constant is the optimal constant in the three-dimensional Lieb–Thirring kinetic energy inequality for orthonormal functions.

## What would settle it

Let $K_3$ be the optimal constant in $\sum_j \|\nabla u_j\|_2^2 \ge K_3 \int \bigl(\sum_j |u_j|^2\bigr)^{5/3}$ over all finite orthonormal families in $H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)$, and let $K_3^{\mathrm{cl}}$ be the semiclassical constant in the same normalization. Prove $K_3 = K_3^{\mathrm{cl}}$, or find an orthonormal family that violates the inequality with $K_3^{\mathrm{cl}}$. Spin multiplicity and the normalization of the kinetic energy change the constants and must be fixed.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Frank states the determination of the optimal constant as Open Problem 2 and the Lieb–Thirring conjecture as Conjecture 4, which in dimension three asserts that the optimal constant equals the semiclassical constant; the best bound he records in dimension three (Theorem 5) is that the optimal constant is at least about 0.7785 times the semiclassical constant.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
