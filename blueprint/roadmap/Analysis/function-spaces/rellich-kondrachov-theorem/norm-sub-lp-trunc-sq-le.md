# Tail estimate for the Rellich ball

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. If $x$ lies in the [Rellich ball](rellich-ball.md) of radius $M$, then for every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ the truncation $x^{(N)} = (x_0, \dots, x_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$ satisfies

$$
\|x - x^{(N)}\|^2 \le \frac{M^2}{1 + N^2}.
$$
