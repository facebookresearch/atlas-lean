# Finitely supported sequences in the Rellich ball

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. Let $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, let $S \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ be a finite set containing every $n$ with $x_n \ne 0$, and let $M \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\sum_{n \in S} (1 + n^2) |x_n|^2 \le M^2$. Then $x$ lies in the [Rellich ball](rellich-ball.md) of radius $M$.
