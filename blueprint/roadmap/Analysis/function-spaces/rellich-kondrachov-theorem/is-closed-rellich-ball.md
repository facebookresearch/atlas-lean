# Rellich–Kondrachov, closedness form

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $M \in \mathbb{R}$. The [Rellich ball](rellich-ball.md) of radius $M$, the set of $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ with $\sum_n (1 + n^2) |x_n|^2 \le M^2$, is closed in $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
