# Membership in the Rellich ball via partial sums

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. A sequence $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ belongs to the Rellich ball of radius $M \in \mathbb{R}$ if every finite weighted partial sum is bounded by $M^2$: $\sum_{n \in F} (1 + n^2) |x_n|^2 \le M^2$ for every finite set $F \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, where $w_n = 1 + n^2$ is the [Rellich weight](rellich-weight.md).
