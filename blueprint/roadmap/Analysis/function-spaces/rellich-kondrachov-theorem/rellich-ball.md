# Rellich ball of weighted square-summable sequences

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. The Rellich ball of radius $M \in \mathbb{R}$ is the set of $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ whose weighted energy $\sum_n (1 + n^2) |x_n|^2$ is at most $M^2$, that is, whose [finite weighted partial sums](mem-rellich-ball.md) are all at most $M^2$.
