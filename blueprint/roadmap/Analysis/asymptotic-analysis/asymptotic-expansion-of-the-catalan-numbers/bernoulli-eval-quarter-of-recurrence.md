# Odd Bernoulli polynomials at one quarter via Euler numbers

Let $E : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}$ satisfy $E_0 = 1$ and
$\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \binom{n}{2j} E_{n-2j} = 0$ for every positive integer $n$.
Then for every integer $m \ge 0$, the Bernoulli polynomial of degree $2m+1$ satisfies

$$B_{2m+1}(1/4) = -\frac{(2m+1) E_{2m}}{4^{2m+1}}.$$

The Bernoulli polynomial addition formula at $1/4 \pm 1/4$ gives the Euler recurrence for the
scaled odd values.
