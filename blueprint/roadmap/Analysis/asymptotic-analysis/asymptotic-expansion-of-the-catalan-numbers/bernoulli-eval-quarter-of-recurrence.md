# Bernoulli eval quarter of recurrence

Let $E\colon\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{Z}$ be a sequence with $E(0)=1$ satisfying
the Euler number recurrence for every integer $n\ge 1$,

$$
\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\binom{n}{2j}E(n-2j)=0.
$$

Then for every integer $m\ge 0$, the Bernoulli polynomial $B_{2m+1}$ satisfies

$$
B_{2m+1}\!\left(\tfrac14\right)=-\frac{(2m+1)E(2m)}{4^{2m+1}}.
$$
