# Truncated power-sum expansion

For $r \in \mathbb{C}$ and $N, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the power-sum expansion through $N$ Bernoulli corrections is $\zeta(-r) + \frac{n^{r+1}}{r+1} + \frac{n^r}{2} + \sum_{j < N} T_j(r, n)$, with $T_j$ the [Bernoulli corrections](chapter7-entry1-term.md), principal complex powers, and total complex division ($z/0 = 0$, so the second term is $0$ when $r = -1$).
