# Bernoulli correction in the power-sum expansion

For $r \in \mathbb{C}$ and $n, j \in \mathbb{N}$, with $k = j + 1$, the Bernoulli correction of order $2k$ in the power-sum expansion at $n$ is $T_j(r, n) = \frac{B_{2k}}{(2k)!} \prod_{i < 2k - 1} (r - i)\; n^{\,r - 2k + 1}$, with $B_{2k}$ the Bernoulli number and the principal complex power.
