# Power sums over integers and over natural numbers

For $r \in \mathbb{C}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the sum of $k^r$ over the integers $1 \le k \le n$ (each $k$ read as a real and then a complex number) equals the power sum $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^r$ over natural numbers $k$, both with the principal complex power.
