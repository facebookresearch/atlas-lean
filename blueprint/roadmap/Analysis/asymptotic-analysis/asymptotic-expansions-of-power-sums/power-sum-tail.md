# Integral tail of the power-sum expansion

For $N, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $r \in \mathbb{C}$, the power-sum tail is $\int_{(n, \infty)} x^{\,r - (2N+1)}\, B_{2N+1}(\{x\})\,dx$, with $B_{2N+1}$ the Bernoulli polynomial, $\{x\}$ the fractional part of $x$ and the principal complex power. The integral is a Bochner integral, taken as $0$ when the integrand is not integrable; it has its classical meaning when the integrand is integrable.
