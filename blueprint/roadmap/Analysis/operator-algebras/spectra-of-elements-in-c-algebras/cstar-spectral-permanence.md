# Spectral permanence for closed unital star-subalgebras of a C*-algebra

Let $A$ be a C\*-algebra with identity $1$, and let $B$ be a C\*-subalgebra of $A$ with $1 \in B$, that is, a norm-closed subalgebra of $A$ that contains $1$ and is closed under the involution. Then $\sigma_B(b) = \sigma_A(b)$ for every $b \in B$, where $\sigma_B$ and $\sigma_A$ denote spectra in $B$ and in $A$.

## Sources

- John B. Conway, A Course in Functional Analysis, 2nd ed., Graduate Texts in Mathematics 96, Springer, 1990.
- *Lecture Notes for MA8107: Operator Algebraic Methods for Quantum Information*, NTNU, 2026, https://wiki.math.ntnu.no/_media/ma8107/2026h/main.pdf, Proposition 2.15 (Spectral permanence), p. 13: for a closed unital star-subalgebra B of a C\*-algebra A, the spectra of b in B and in A are equal for every b in B.
