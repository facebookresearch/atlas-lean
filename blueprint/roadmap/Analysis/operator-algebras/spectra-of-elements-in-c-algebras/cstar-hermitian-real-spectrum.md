# A self-adjoint element of a unital C*-algebra has real spectrum

Let $A$ be a C\*-algebra with identity and let $a \in A$ be hermitian, that is, $a^* = a$. Then the spectrum $\sigma(a)$ of $a$ in $A$ is contained in $\mathbb{R}$.

## Sources

- John B. Conway, A Course in Functional Analysis, 2nd ed., Graduate Texts in Mathematics 96, Springer, 1990.
- Cho-Ho Chu, *C\*-algebras*, lecture notes for a London Taught Course Centre postgraduate course, Queen Mary, University of London, https://www.ltcc.ac.uk/media/london-taught-course-centre/documents/c_algebra.pdf, Proposition 2.11, p. 8: in a unital C\*-algebra the spectrum of a self-adjoint element is contained in ℝ.
