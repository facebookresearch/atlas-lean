# Gelfand–Naimark theorem

For every unital C*-algebra $A$ there are a complex Hilbert space $H$
and a unital $*$-homomorphism $\pi : A \to B(H)$ into the algebra of bounded operators on $H$ such
that $\pi$ is injective and isometric: $\|\pi(a)\| = \|a\|$ for every $a \in A$.

## Sources

- Gelfand–Naimark theorem, I. M. Gelfand–M. A. Naimark, Mat. Sb. 12 (1943); see G. J. Murphy, C*-Algebras and Operator Theory, and J. B. Conway, A Course in Operator Theory, AMS Graduate Studies in Mathematics 21 (2000). The statement here is the form with an isometric injective star-homomorphism into $B(H)$.
