# A quasinilpotent element without nontrivial invariant projections in a finite diffuse factor

There exists a concrete von Neumann algebra $M$ on a complex Hilbert space of the base universe. Its center is scalar; every isometry of $M$ is unitary; each nonzero projection $p$ has some projection $q\in M$ with $q\ne0,p$ and $qp=q$. The continuous dual of a separable complex Banach space in the same universe is conjugate-linearly isometric to $M$ with its operator norm. There is $T\in M$, $T\ne0$, with $\|T^n\|^{1/n}\to0$ and such that $p\in M$, $p=p^*=p^2$ and $(1-p)Tp=0$ imply $p=0$ or $p=1$. This only excludes projections in $M$.

## Depends on

- [Concrete generated von Neumann algebras](../../not-yet-classified/generated/generated-by.md), scalar center, finiteness, diffuse projections and the conjugate-linear predual convention.

## Proof depends on

- A concrete finite diffuse factor realization with separable predual and a nonzero quasinilpotent member having the invariant-projection obstruction in that factor.

## Expansion

Separate the factor and predual constructions from the spectral-radius estimate and the internal projection obstruction. If using a product rotation, show its predual meets the comparator's conjugate-linear convention.
