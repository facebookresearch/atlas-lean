# Invariant-projection counterexample for every prescribed irrational rotation

Let $0<\theta<1$ be irrational. Put $X=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ with Haar probability and let $H=L^2(X;\ell_2(\mathbb Z;\mathbb C))$. Let $U$ be the fiber shift $(U\xi)(x,k)=\xi(x,k-1)$, and let $\pi(a)$ multiply the $(x,k)$ coordinate by $a(x+k\theta)$. Let $M$ be the von Neumann algebra generated by $U$ and $\pi(x\mapsto e^{2\pi i x})$, and $e$ the constant fiber vector $\delta_0$. There is a continuous $f:X\to[0,1]$ vanishing precisely at $0$ with $\int-\log f=+\infty$, using extended cost at zero. Its operator $T=U\pi(f)$ belongs to $M$, is nonzero, has $\lim_{n\to\infty}\|T^n\|^{1/n}=0$, and every star projection $p\in M$ with $(1-p)Tp=0$ is trivial. Also

$$\inf_{\epsilon>0}\exp\left(\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}\langle e,\log(T^*T+\epsilon I)e\rangle\right)=0.$$

This determinant uses the functional calculus logarithm of the regularized positive operator.

## Depends on

- [Generated concrete von Neumann algebra](../../not-yet-classified/generated/generated-by.md), the fiber shift/orbit multipliers, and the regularized determinant above.

## Proof depends on

- Continuous weights with specified zero for every irrational angle, uniform orbit-product control, and the invariant-projection obstruction for this realization.

## Expansion

Verify the sign of the orbit action against the direction of the shift, then separate the infinite logarithmic integral, determinant and operator norm-power estimates from the projection argument. Do not infer the latter merely from the point spectrum or the determinant.
