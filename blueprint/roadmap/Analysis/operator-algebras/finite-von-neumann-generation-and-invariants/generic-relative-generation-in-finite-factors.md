# Generic relative generation in finite diffuse factors

Let $N\subset M\subset B(H)$ be unital weak-operator-closed complex star algebras on a complex Hilbert space. Each is nonzero, has scalar center, all its isometries are unitary, and every nonzero projection properly dominates another nonzero projection. Assume $N'\cap M=\mathbb C1$ and that the continuous dual of a same-universe separable complex Banach space is conjugate-linearly isometric to $M$.

There exists a bounded complex-linear trace $\tau$ on $M$ with $\tau(1)=1$, $\tau(a^*a)$ nonnegative, $\tau(a^*)=\overline{\tau(a)}$, $\tau(ab)=\tau(ba)$ and $\tau(a^*a)=0$ only at $a=0$. It is continuous in the topology induced by the operator functionals $T\mapsto\sum_i\langle \xi_i,T\eta_i\rangle$ for $\sum_i\|\xi_i\|\|\eta_i\|<\infty$. In the topology generated by positive-radius balls for $d_\tau(u,v)=\sqrt{\operatorname{Re}\tau((u-v)^*(u-v))}$ on the full unitary group of $M$, the set of $u$ for which $N\cup\{u\}$ generates $M$ as a weak-operator-closed star algebra is dense and a $G_\delta$.

## Proof depends on

- Existence of the normalized faithful normal trace, the $G_\delta$ description of the generator locus and its density via relative unitary approximation.

## Expansion

Construct the trace as an output, not an added hypothesis. Prove the $G_\delta$ and density statements separately in the same trace topology, retaining scalar relative commutant and the separable-predual condition only for $M$.
