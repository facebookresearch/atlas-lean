# Infinitely many nonisomorphic property T groups with the same tracial group factor

There exist a countable discrete group $\Lambda$ and a sequence of countable discrete groups $(\Gamma_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}$, all finitely generated, ICC and with property T. Every $L(\Gamma_n)$ is normally trace-preservingly isomorphic to $L(\Lambda)$, and every pair $L(\Gamma_m),L(\Gamma_n)$ is normally trace-preservingly isomorphic. The groups $\Gamma_m,\Gamma_n$ are not isomorphic when $m\ne n$, and no $\Gamma_n$ is isomorphic to $\Lambda$.
