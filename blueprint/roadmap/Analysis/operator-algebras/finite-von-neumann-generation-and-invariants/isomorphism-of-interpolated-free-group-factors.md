# Normal tracial isomorphisms of interpolated free group factors

For parameters $r,s\in(1,\infty]$, the following interpolated algebras are isomorphic by a complex unital star isomorphism preserving the declared traces and continuous in both directions for the ultraweak topologies. At a finite integer $n$ use the double commutant of the left translations of the free group on $n$ generators on $\ell^2(G)$, with trace $\langle\delta_e,T\delta_e\rangle$; at infinity use the countably generated free group. At a noninteger $r$ use the corner $pMp$ with unit $p$, where $M$ is the commutant on $\ell^2(\mathbb N\times\mathbb F_2)$ of the right translations on the group variable. Select a projection $p$ of semifinite trace $(\sqrt{r-1})^{-1}$, using $\sum_k\|p\delta_{(k,e)}\|^2$ for its trace. The corner trace is $\sum_k\langle\delta_{(k,e)},T\delta_{(k,e)}\rangle$ divided by that parameter. The comparator uses a choice operation for selection; its agreement with these corners needs existence at the specified traces. The ultraweak topology is generated by functionals $T\mapsto\sum_k\langle\eta_k,T\xi_k\rangle$ for square-summable vector sequences and restricted to the subalgebras.

## Depends on
The commutant of a set of operators consists of the operators commuting with each member; take it twice for the double commutant. Use the specified ultraweak topology, stabilized semifinite trace, and corner selection. The bicommutant theorem and a family of abstract isomorphic factors are not part of these definitions.

## Proof depends on
Existence of projections of all needed traces and normal trace-preserving identifications across rank, amplification and infinite parameter.

## Expansion
Establish the standard trace and projection existence before interpreting the chosen corner; connect the three separate model cases and prove normality for both directions of each isomorphism. No separate fundamental-group calculation is included.
