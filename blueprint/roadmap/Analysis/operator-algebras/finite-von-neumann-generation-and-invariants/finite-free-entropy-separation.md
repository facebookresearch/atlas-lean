# Finite microstates and nonmicrostates free entropies separated by one half

There exist a complex Hilbert space, a concrete von Neumann algebra $M$, a unit vector $e$ separating for $M$ whose functional $\tau(a)=\langle e,ae\rangle$ is tracial, and $n\ge2$ self-adjoint $n$-tuples $X,S$ in $M$. The $S_i$ have the standard semicircular moments (zero at odd orders, Catalan numbers at even orders), and the unital algebra generated by $X$, together with each separately generated unital algebra of an $S_i$, is free: alternating products of centered elements from distinct adjacent algebras have trace zero.

For each cutoff $R>0$, moment length $m$ and tolerance $\epsilon>0$, take the Lebesgue volume of tuples of $d$-by-$d$ Hermitian matrices of operator norms at most $R$ with normalized traces matching every word moment of length at most $m$ to within $\epsilon$. The real coordinates are the real diagonals and the off-diagonal real and imaginary parts scaled by $\sqrt2$. Take $\limsup_{d\to\infty}$ of $d^{-2}\log\operatorname{vol}+(n/2)\log d$ over positive sizes, then the infimum over $m,\epsilon$ and supremum over $R$ to obtain $\chi(X)$.

For $Y=X+\sqrt s\,S$, let $F(s)$ be the infimum of $\sum_i\|\xi_i\|^2$ over conjugate vectors in the closed span of $w(Y)e$, where $\langle\xi_i,w(Y)e\rangle$ is the sum, over occurrences of $i$ in $w$, of the products of the prefix and suffix trace moments on either side of that occurrence. The empty infimum is $+\infty$. With $b(s)=n/(1+s)$, the nonmicrostates value $\chi^*(X,S)$ is $(n/2)\log(2\pi e)$ plus half the integral of $(b-F)_+$ minus half the integral of $(F-b)_+$ over positive $s$, computed via the two extended nonnegative integrals in the comparator. The witnesses satisfy

$$-\infty<\chi(X)\le\chi^*(X,S)-\tfrac12<+\infty.$$

## Depends on

- Tracial vectors, free words, Hermitian matrix microstates, the free Fisher infimum and the two extended integrals just specified.

## Proof depends on

- A joint witness with freely independent semicircular noise and quantitative estimates proving both finiteness and the strict separation.

## Expansion

Develop the cutoff/moment/volume theory separately from the conjugate-system/Fisher theory. Identify the extended-real subtraction convention before comparing the upper and lower entropy estimates; prove the asserted finite bounds for the same witness.
