# Concrete operator system

Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space. A *(concrete) operator system* in $B(H)$ is a linear subspace $\mathcal S \subseteq B(H)$ that contains the identity operator $I$ and is self-adjoint, meaning that $T^* \in \mathcal S$ whenever $T \in \mathcal S$. We do not require $\mathcal S$ to be closed in the operator norm. Passing to the norm closure does not change the C\*-algebra generated by $\mathcal S$, and the matrix cones of $\mathcal S$ defined below are the restrictions of those of its closure.

For each $n \ge 1$, identify $M_n(B(H))$ with $B(H^n)$, where $H^n = H \oplus \cdots \oplus H$ ($n$ copies), so that $[T_{ij}]$ acts by $(x_j)_j \mapsto (\sum_j T_{ij} x_j)_i$. The space $M_n(\mathcal S)$ of $n\times n$ matrices with entries in $\mathcal S$ is then a subspace of $B(H^n)$. It carries the operator norm of $B(H^n)$ and the positive cone $M_n(\mathcal S)^+ = M_n(\mathcal S) \cap B(H^n)^+$, the set of its elements that are positive operators on $H^n$. The family of cones $(M_n(\mathcal S)^+)_{n \ge 1}$ is the *matrix order* of $\mathcal S$.
