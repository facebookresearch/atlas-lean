# A fixed proper-infinite amplification descends in exact C*-algebras

Let $A$ be a possibly nonunital complex C*-algebra with the star order. Exactness here means that for each onto nonunital star homomorphism $q:C\to D$ of complex C*-algebras, the kernel of the spatial tensor map induced by $q$ lies in the closed star algebra generated by $a\otimes c$ with $q(c)=0$. The spatial norm is the supremum of the operator norms in all admissible factored representations on Hilbert tensor products. For positive matrix corners write $b\preceq a$ if for every $\epsilon>0$ a compatible rectangular $v$ has $\|v^*av-b\|<\epsilon$. Proper infiniteness means $a\oplus a\preceq a$.

Suppose there is a single integer $n\ge1$ such that for every nonzero positive $a$ in $A$, the $n$-copy diagonal of $a$ is properly infinite. Then the one-copy corner of every positive element, including zero, is properly infinite.

## Depends on

- Spatial exactness and rectangular Cuntz comparison at the indicated matrix corners.

## Proof depends on

- A descent result from a fixed matrix amplification under exactness, and the direct zero case.

## Expansion

Relate the spatial product representation and kernel ideal to the native minimal tensor product, and bridge rectangular comparison with stabilized comparison. Prove the descent without changing the uniform choice of $n$ to an element-dependent choice.
