# Rational noninjectivity of a constructed coarse index map

For $j\in\mathbb N$ let the base disk have vertices $u=(a,b)\in\mathbb Z^2$ of radius $r(u)=\max(|a|,|b|,|a+b|)\le j$, with distinct vertices adjacent when the radius of their difference is at most one. Over $u$ put the fiber $\mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb Z/2^{j-r(u)}\mathbb Z)$. The chosen holonomy is the reduction of $\operatorname{diag}(t,t^{-1},1)$ for the source’s transcendental principal $2$-adic unit $t$. A lifted edge either stays at the same $u$, using an elementary matrix move or its inverse (including the identity among allowed moves), or joins adjacent base vertices using the identity move. At every congruence level common to the two depths, the new coordinate is the move times the old coordinate times the holonomy to the power of the edge charge. The charge is $1$ for a move from negative second coordinate onto the positive first-axis ray, $-1$ for the reverse, and zero otherwise. Self-edges are discarded. All these graphs are finite and connected, with degrees uniformly bounded in $j$.

Give their disjoint union $X$ the graph distances within components; between distinct components $i,j$ use the distances to/from the distinguished boundary vertices $((i,0),1)$, $((j,0),1)$ plus $i+j+2$. Then $X$ is proper, distinct vertices have distance at least one, and each fixed-radius closed ball has cardinality bounded uniformly over its center.

For the domain, use the direct limit over integer Rips scales of the following odd-cycle groups. A Rips point is a finitely supported probability vector on $X$ supported on a set of diameter at most the scale, with the topology from its coordinate functions. At each scale use the free abelian group on cycles $(H,\rho,F)$ over the algebra of continuous functions vanishing at infinity on the Rips realization. Here $\rho$ is a possibly nonunital star representation on a separable complex Hilbert space $H$ (the source requires its topology to be second countable), and $F$ is bounded, with $[F,\rho(a)]$, $(F^2-1)\rho(a)$ and $(F^*-F)\rho(a)$ compact for each $a$. Kill the subgroup generated by degenerate cycles (all three expressions vanish), differences of unitarily equivalent cycles and of norm-continuous homotopy endpoints, and the relations expressing direct sums as sums of classes. The scale maps are induced by precomposing with pullback of functions along the Rips inclusions.

The target is the model $K_1$ of the concrete operator-norm closure of the nonunital star algebra on $\ell^2(X;\ell^2(\mathbb N;\mathbb C))$ generated by operators of finite propagation with compact matrix entries, also requiring this entry condition for the adjoint. Form the free abelian group of invertible matrices of all finite sizes over its unitization, taking multiplication to addition, identifying a matrix with its block stabilization by $1$, and equating the ends of continuous paths of invertible matrices. The target is the kernel of the map to the same construction over $\mathbb C$ induced by the scalar projection of the unitization.

Use the **constructed** index homomorphism in the cited source. At scale $r$ on the separated graph union, precompose a cycle with the homomorphism from the $i$th component's Rips functions into the union's functions vanishing at infinity: extend by zero along the component Rips inclusion if $r<i$, and use the zero homomorphism if $i\le r$. The cutoff is on the component index, not on whether the component is isolated at that scale. Regularize using the characters that evaluate each component at its distinguished boundary vertex. Take the block-diagonal operator $F_{\mathrm{sep}}$ formed from the uniformly controlled self-adjoint Rips operators on the ample module. It is a multiplier of the Roe algebra and $F_{\mathrm{sep}}^2-I$ belongs to that algebra. Form the rational Cayley unitary $U=-((F_{\mathrm{sep}}-\mathrm{i}I)(F_{\mathrm{sep}}+\mathrm{i}I)^{-1})^2$. Since $U-I$ belongs to the Roe algebra, take the relative $K_1$ class represented by $1+(U-I)$ in its unitization, with scalar part $1$. The free-group homomorphism descends if it annihilates the odd-cycle relations, and is set to zero otherwise. If these descended homomorphisms respect all scale inclusions they give the compatible family, and otherwise the whole family is replaced by zero. Transport this family from the separated union along the canonical association of the dependent sums and the reindexing of the concrete operator algebra. Take the induced homomorphism $\mu_{\mathrm{constr}}$ from the direct limit.

There is a class $\alpha$ of infinite order with $\mu_{\mathrm{constr}}(\alpha)=0$ and $1\otimes\alpha\ne0$ in the rationalized domain. Neither $\mu_{\mathrm{constr}}$ nor its scalar extension to $\mathbb Q$ is injective. The assertion is about exactly this map and does not identify it with a separately defined ordinary coarse assembly map or assert failure for the maximal Roe algebra.

## Depends on

- Congruence-fiber graph model, separated metric unions, Rips scale colimit, odd analytic cycles and locally compact reduced Roe operators, nonunital operator K-theory.

## Proof depends on

- Uniform ball and degree bounds, cyclic class of infinite order, descent through the odd-cycle relations, relative index computation and compatibility under scale inclusions.

## Expansion

Separate finite congruence and holonomy calculations from the analytic index theory. Prove descent, compatibility and the reindexing law for this particular construction. A statement about the ordinary coarse Novikov conjecture would additionally need a separate comparison with the native assembly map; it is not part of this endpoint. Do not infer maximal coarse assembly results.
