# Grid products with a two-piece factor

Let $\mathcal A$ be a complete normed $\mathbb{R}$-algebra with $\|1\| = 1$, $A, B \in \mathcal A$ and $0 \le t_1 \le t$. With $Y(s) = A$ for $s < t_1$ and $Y(s) = B$ otherwise, the ordered products $\prod_{g < \lceil t M \rceil} \big(1 + \frac{1}{M} Y(g/M)\big)$, factors ordered by increasing $g$ from left to right, converge to $e^{t_1 A}\, e^{(t - t_1) B}$ as $M \to \infty$.
