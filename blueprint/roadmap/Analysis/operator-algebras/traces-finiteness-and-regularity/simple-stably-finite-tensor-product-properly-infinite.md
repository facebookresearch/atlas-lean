# Simple stably finite factors with a properly infinite spatial product model

Let $F=\mathbb F_2$ and $C$ be the norm-closed unital star algebra generated by its left regular representation on $\ell^2(F)$. Then $C$ is simple and stably finite. There is another nonzero simple stably finite unital complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ with a faithful unital representation $\rho$ on a complete complex Hilbert space $H$ for which the following algebra on $\ell^2(F,H)$ is properly infinite: the norm-closed unital star algebra generated by the diagonal $\rho(a)$ and the left translations $(\lambda(g)\xi)(h)=\xi(g^{-1}h)$. Properly infinite means it contains two isometries $v,w$ with $v^*w=0$. Simplicity concerns closed two-sided star ideals; stable finiteness means that every isometry in every positive-size matrix algebra is unitary.

## Depends on
The reduced group algebra $C$, a faithful representation of $A$, and the specified spatial algebra rather than an algebraic or maximal tensor product.

## Proof depends on
Simplicity and stable finiteness of each factor and a properly infinite pair of isometries in the generated product.

## Expansion
Show the concrete product identifies with the spatial tensor product in the claimed representations. Separate stable finiteness of the two factors from construction of the product isometries. Do not substitute ordinary finiteness for stability under matrices.
