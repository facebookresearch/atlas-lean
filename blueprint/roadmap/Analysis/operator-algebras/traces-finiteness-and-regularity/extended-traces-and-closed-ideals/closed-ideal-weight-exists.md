# Extended trace supported off a closed ideal

Use the notation of [Extended traces and their finite square-trace domains](extended-trace-and-finite-domain.md).

For a norm-closed two-sided star ideal $I$, its weight $w_I$ takes the value zero on positive elements of $I$ and $+\infty$ on all other positive elements.

For every norm-closed two-sided star ideal this $w_I$ exists as an extended trace.

## Depends on

- [Extended traces and their finite square-trace domains](extended-trace-and-finite-domain.md).

## Expansion

Square-root/quotient detection of ideal membership, lower semicontinuity of the indicator weight and traciality via the C*-ideal quotient. Use the shared extended-valued trace model, not a finite positive functional.
