# Loss of normalized two-quasitraces in a reduced free group product model

There exists a nonzero separable unital complex $C^*$-algebra $B$ with a normalized two-quasitrace and a faithful unital star representation $\rho:B\to\mathcal B(H)$ on a complete complex Hilbert space. On $\ell^2(\mathbb F_2,H)$ form the norm-closed unital star algebra generated by diagonal $\rho(B)$ and left regular translations of the rank-two free group. This algebra contains isometries $v,w$ with $v^*w=0$ and has no normalized two-quasitrace. A normalized two-quasitrace is normalized at the unit, positive and cyclic on squares, consistent with real/imaginary decomposition, linear on closed abelian star subalgebras, and extendable in the same sense to the upper-left matrix corner.

## Depends on
Use the two-quasitrace convention given in the statement, with an extension to the matrix corner of the same algebra. Use the norm-closed algebra of represented operators and the injectivity of $\rho$.

## Proof depends on

A concrete factor admitting a normalized two-quasitrace, and a two-isometry construction in its reduced spatial product from a finite column identity and strict row contraction.

## Expansion

Isolate the Cuntz dimension-state construction and its passage to a normalized two-quasitrace, and the product isometry construction from the finite tuple whose column sum is the unit and whose row sum is bounded by $2/3$ of the unit. Then use cyclicity on squares, positivity and additivity on the two orthogonal range projections to exclude a normalized two-quasitrace in the product.
