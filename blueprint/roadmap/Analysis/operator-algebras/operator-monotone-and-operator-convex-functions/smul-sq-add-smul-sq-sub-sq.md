# Convex-combination identity for squares

In any real algebra $A$, for all $a, b \in A$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$
t a^2 + (1 - t) b^2 - (t a + (1 - t) b)^2 = t (1 - t) (a - b)^2 .
$$

It is the algebraic heart of
the operator convexity of $t \mapsto t^2$ (Carlen, *Trace inequalities and quantum entropy*, 2009
draft, Section 2.1).

The identity holds in every real algebra, commutative or not; noncommutativity enters only through the two cross terms $ab$ and $ba$, which appear symmetrically on both sides.
