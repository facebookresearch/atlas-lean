# Linear bound on differences of powers for the Trotter formula

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a normed ring with $\|1\| = 1$. For all $X, Y \in \mathcal{A}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
\|X^n - Y^n\| \le n\, \|X - Y\|\, \max(\|X\|, \|Y\|, 1)^n.
$$

The term $1$ in the maximum is needed: without it the bound fails when both norms are less than $1$.
