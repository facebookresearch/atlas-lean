# One-step Trotter estimate

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a complete normed ring with $\|1\| = 1$ that is a normed algebra over $\mathbb{Q}$, and let $\exp$ be the exponential series in $\mathcal{A}$. For all $x, y \in \mathcal{A}$, $\exp x \exp y$ differs from $\exp(x + y)$ by a quadratic error:

$$
\|\exp x \exp y - \exp(x + y)\| \le \|x\| \|y\| + \|x\|^2 e^{\|x\|} e^{\|y\|} + (1 + \|x\|) \|y\|^2 e^{\|y\|} + (\|x\| + \|y\|)^2 e^{\|x\| + \|y\|}.
$$
