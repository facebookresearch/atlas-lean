# Telescoping estimate for differences of powers in a normed ring

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a normed ring with $\|1\| = 1$. For all $a, b \in \mathcal{A}$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
\|a^m - b^m\| \le m\, \|a - b\|\, \max(\|a\|, \|b\|)^{m-1},
$$

where for $m = 0$ the exponent $m - 1$ is read as $0$; both sides then vanish.
