# Trotter product formula

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a complete normed ring with $\|1\| = 1$ that is a normed algebra over $\mathbb{Q}$, and let $\exp$ be the exponential series in $\mathcal{A}$. For all $a, b \in \mathcal{A}$,

$$
\big(\exp(a/n) \exp(b/n)\big)^n \to \exp(a + b) \qquad (n \to \infty)
$$

in norm.
