# Compound-interest limit for the exponential in a Banach algebra

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be a unital real Banach algebra with $\|1\| = 1$. If real numbers $\varepsilon_N \to 0$ with $\varepsilon_N > 0$ eventually, $Y_N \to X$ in $\mathbb{A}$, and natural numbers $m_N$ satisfy $m_N \varepsilon_N \to t$, then $(1 + \varepsilon_N Y_N)^{m_N} \to \exp(tX)$. This generalizes `NormedSpace.tendsto_one_add_inv_smul_pow`, replacing the time scale $1/M$ by an arbitrary vanishing scale (for a Cannings model, the pair-coalescence probability $c_N$).
