# Norm of the exponential is bounded by the exponential of the norm

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a normed ring with $\|1\| = 1$ that is a normed algebra over $\mathbb{Q}$, and let $\exp x$ be the sum of the exponential series $\sum_n x^n/n!$, taken to be $0$ if the series diverges. For every $x \in \mathcal{A}$, $\|\exp x\| \le e^{\|x\|}$.
