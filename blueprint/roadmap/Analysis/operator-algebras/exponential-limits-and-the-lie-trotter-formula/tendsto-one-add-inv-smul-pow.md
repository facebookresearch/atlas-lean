# Compound-interest limit in a Banach algebra

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a Banach algebra over $\mathbb{R}$ with $\|1\| = 1$. Let $(Y_M)_{M \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence in $\mathcal{A}$ with $Y_M \to X$, and let $(m_M)$ be a sequence of natural numbers with $m_M/M \to t \in \mathbb{R}$. Then

$$
\Big(1 + \frac{1}{M} Y_M\Big)^{m_M} \to \exp(t X) \qquad (M \to \infty).
$$
