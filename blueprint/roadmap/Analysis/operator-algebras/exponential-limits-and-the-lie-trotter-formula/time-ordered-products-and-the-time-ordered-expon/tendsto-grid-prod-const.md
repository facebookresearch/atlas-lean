# Grid products with a constant factor converge to the exponential

Let $\mathcal A$ be a complete normed $\mathbb{R}$-algebra with $\|1\| = 1$, $A \in \mathcal A$ and $t \ge 0$. The ordered products $\prod_{g < \lceil t M \rceil} \big(1 + \frac{1}{M} A\big)$ converge to $e^{tA}$ as $M \to \infty$.
