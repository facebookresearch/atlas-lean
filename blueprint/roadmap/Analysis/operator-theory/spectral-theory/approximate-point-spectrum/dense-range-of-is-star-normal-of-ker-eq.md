# Normal operator with trivial kernel has dense range

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $T$ is a normal bounded operator on $E$ with $\ker T = \{0\}$, then $T$ has dense range; equivalently, the residual spectrum of a normal operator is empty.
