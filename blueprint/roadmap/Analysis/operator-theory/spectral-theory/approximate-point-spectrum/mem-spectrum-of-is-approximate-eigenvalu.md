# Approximate eigenvalues lie in the spectrum

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. For every bounded operator $T$ on $E$, normal or not, every [approximate eigenvalue](is-approximate-eigenvalue.md) of $T$ lies in the spectrum of $T$.
