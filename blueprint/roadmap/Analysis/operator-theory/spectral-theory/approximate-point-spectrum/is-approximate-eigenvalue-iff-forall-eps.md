# Epsilon characterization of approximate eigenvalues

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. For a bounded operator $T$ on $E$ and $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, $\mu$ is an [approximate eigenvalue](is-approximate-eigenvalue.md) of $T$ if and only if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a unit vector $x \in E$ with $\|(\mu I - T) x\| < \varepsilon$.
