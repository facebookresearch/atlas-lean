# Spectral values of a normal operator are approximate eigenvalues

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $T$ is a normal bounded operator on $E$, every $\mu$ in the spectrum of $T$ is an [approximate eigenvalue](is-approximate-eigenvalue.md) of $T$.
