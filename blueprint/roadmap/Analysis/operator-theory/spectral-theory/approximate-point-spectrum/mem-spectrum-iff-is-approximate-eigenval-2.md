# Weyl's criterion for self-adjoint operators

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $T$ is a self-adjoint bounded operator on $E$, then $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ lies in the spectrum of $T$ if and only if $\mu$ is an [approximate eigenvalue](is-approximate-eigenvalue.md) of $T$.
