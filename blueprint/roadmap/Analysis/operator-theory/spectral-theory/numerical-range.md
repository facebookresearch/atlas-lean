# Numerical range of a matrix

Let

A∈ Mₙ(ℂ).

The numerical range of A is

W(A)=\{⟨ x,Ax⟩ : x∈ℂⁿ, ‖x‖₂=1\}.

Equivalently,

W(A)=\{x^*Ax:‖x‖₂=1\}.

The inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument, so $\langle x, Ax\rangle = x^*Ax$. The [numerical range of an operator](numerical-range-and-numerical-radius/numerical-range.md) is written with $\langle Ax, x\rangle$, the complex conjugate, so for the operator $x \mapsto Ax$ the two sets are complex conjugates of each other.
