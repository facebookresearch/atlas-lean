# The ratio $\mathcal{R}(v)$

For a potential $v : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}$ with
scattering length $a(v)$, let $\mathcal{R}(v) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} v(x)/(8\pi a(v))\,dx$. When $a(v) \neq 0$ this is the ratio of $(8\pi)^{-1} \int v$ to $a(v)$; when $a(v) = 0$ the integrand is $0$ (division by zero giving $0$) and so is $\mathcal{R}(v)$. An integral whose integrand is not integrable is read as $0$.
