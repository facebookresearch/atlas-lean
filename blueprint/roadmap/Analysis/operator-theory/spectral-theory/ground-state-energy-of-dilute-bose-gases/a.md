# Scattering length

Let $v : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}$ be a potential. If there is a
radial scattering solution $w$ to the scattering equation with potential $v$, the scattering
length of $v$ is $a(v) = (8\pi)^{-1} \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} v(x)\,(1 - w(x))\,dx$, where $w$ is
one such solution, chosen arbitrarily, and the integral is read as $0$ if the integrand is not integrable; if there is no radial scattering solution, $a(v) = 0$.

This is Equation (3.5) of Fournais–Solovej. Under the assumptions of Theorem 1.2 a radial scattering solution exists and is unique up to equality almost everywhere, so the integral does not depend on the choice there.
