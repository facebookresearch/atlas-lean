# Bound on the size of the spectrum of a finite-rank operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Banach space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $E$ with finite-dimensional range. Then the spectrum of $T$ has at most $\dim \operatorname{ran} T + 1$ points.
