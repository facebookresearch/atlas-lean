# Independent eigenvectors for distinct nonzero eigenvalues

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a field, $E$ a vector space over $\mathbb{k}$ and $T$ a linear map $E \to E$. One can choose, for each nonzero eigenvalue $\mu$ of $T$, an eigenvector $x_\mu$ for $\mu$ so that the family $(x_\mu)$ is linearly independent and each $x_\mu$ lies in the range of $T$.
