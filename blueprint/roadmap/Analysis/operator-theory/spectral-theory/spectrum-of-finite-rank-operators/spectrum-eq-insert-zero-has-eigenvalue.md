# Spectrum of a finite-rank operator on an infinite-dimensional space

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional Banach space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $E$ with finite-dimensional range. Then the spectrum of $T$ is exactly $\{0\} \cup \{\text{eigenvalues of } T\}$.
