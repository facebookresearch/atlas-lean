# Nonzero spectral values of a finite-rank operator are eigenvalues

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Banach space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $E$ with finite-dimensional range. Then the spectrum of $T$ is contained in $\{0\} \cup \{\text{eigenvalues of } T\}$: every nonzero spectral value is an eigenvalue, by the Fredholm alternative for compact operators.
