# Finite-rank operators are compact

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $E$ with finite-dimensional range. Then $T$ is compact: it factors through its finite-dimensional range, and every bounded linear map with values in a finite-dimensional space maps bounded sets to relatively compact sets.
