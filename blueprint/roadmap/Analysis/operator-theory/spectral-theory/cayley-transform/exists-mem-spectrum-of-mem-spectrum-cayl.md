# Spectral mapping for the Cayley transform, preimage direction

Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space, $A$ a bounded self-adjoint operator on $H$ and $U$ its [Cayley transform](cayley-transform.md). Every point $\mu$ of the spectrum of $U$ is of the form $\mu = (w - i)/(w + i)$ for some $w$ in the spectrum of $A$.
