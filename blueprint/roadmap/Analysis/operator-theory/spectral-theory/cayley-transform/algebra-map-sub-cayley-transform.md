# Resolvent factorization for the Cayley transform

Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space, $A$ a bounded self-adjoint operator on $H$ and $U = (A - iI)(A + iI)^{-1}$ its [Cayley transform](cayley-transform.md). For every complex number $\mu \ne 1$, with $\lambda = i(1 + \mu)/(1 - \mu)$,

$$
\mu I - U = (\mu - 1)(A - \lambda I)(A + iI)^{-1}.
$$

This factorization is behind the spectral mapping theorem for the Cayley transform.
