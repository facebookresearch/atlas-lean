# Spectrum of the Cayley transform

Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space, $A$ a bounded self-adjoint operator on $H$ and $U$ its [Cayley transform](cayley-transform.md). The spectrum of $U$ is the image of the spectrum of $A$ under the Möbius map $w \mapsto (w - i)/(w + i)$:

$$
\sigma(U) = \left\{ \frac{w - i}{w + i} : w \in \sigma(A) \right\}.
$$
