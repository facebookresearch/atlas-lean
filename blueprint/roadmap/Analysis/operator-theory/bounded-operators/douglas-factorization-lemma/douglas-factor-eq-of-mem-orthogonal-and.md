# Uniqueness of the Douglas factor

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. If a bounded operator $C' : E \to E$ satisfies $C' x \in (\ker B)^\perp$ and $B(C' x) = A x$ for every $x \in E$, then $C'$ is the [canonical factor](douglas-factor.md).
