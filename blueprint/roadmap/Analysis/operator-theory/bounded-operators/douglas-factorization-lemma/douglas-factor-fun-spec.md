# Defining properties of the Douglas preimage

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. For every $x \in E$, the [chosen preimage](douglas-factor-fun.md) $z$ of $A x$ satisfies $z \in (\ker B)^\perp$ and $B z = A x$.
