# Douglas' lemma: majorization iff factorization

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators. There is $c \ge 0$ with $\|A^* y\| \le c\, \|B^* y\|$ for every $y \in F$ if and only if $A = BC$ for some bounded operator $C : E \to E$.
