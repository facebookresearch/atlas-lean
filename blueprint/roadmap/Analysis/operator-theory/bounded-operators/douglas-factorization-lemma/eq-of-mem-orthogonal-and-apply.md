# Injectivity of an operator on the orthogonal complement of its kernel

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $B : E \to F$ be a bounded operator. If $z_1, z_2 \in (\ker B)^\perp$ satisfy $B z_1 = B z_2$, then $z_1 = z_2$, since $z_1 - z_2$ lies in $\ker B \cap (\ker B)^\perp = \{0\}$.
