# Underlying function of the Douglas factor

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. The underlying function of the Douglas factor sends $x \in E$ to a chosen preimage of $A x$ under $B$ in $(\ker B)^\perp$, which exists by [range inclusion](exists-mem-orthogonal-apply.md) and is unique by [injectivity on the orthogonal complement](eq-of-mem-orthogonal-and-apply.md).
