# Canonical factor in Douglas' lemma

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. The canonical factor in Douglas' lemma is the bounded operator $C : E \to E$ sending $x$ to the unique element of $(\ker B)^\perp$ mapped by $B$ to $A x$.
