# Preimages in the orthogonal complement of the kernel under range inclusion

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. Then for every $x \in E$ there is $z \in (\ker B)^\perp$ with $B z = A x$.
