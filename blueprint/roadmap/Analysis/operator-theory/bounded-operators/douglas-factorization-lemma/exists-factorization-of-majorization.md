# Douglas' lemma: majorization gives a norm-bounded factor

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators. If $c \ge 0$ satisfies $\|A^* y\| \le c\, \|B^* y\|$ for every $y \in F$, then there is a bounded operator $C : E \to E$ with $A = BC$ and $\|C\| \le c$.
