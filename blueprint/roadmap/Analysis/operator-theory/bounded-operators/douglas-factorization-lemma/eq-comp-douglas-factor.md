# Douglas factor factors A through B

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. The [canonical factor](douglas-factor.md) $C$ factors $A$ through $B$: $A = BC$.
