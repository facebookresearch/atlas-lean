# Douglas' lemma: range inclusion iff majorization

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators. Then $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$ if and only if there is $c \ge 0$ with $\|A^* y\| \le c\, \|B^* y\|$ for every $y \in F$.
