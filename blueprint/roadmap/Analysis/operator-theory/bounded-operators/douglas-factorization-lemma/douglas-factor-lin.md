# Douglas factor as a linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ and $F$ be Hilbert spaces over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $A, B : E \to F$ be bounded operators with $\operatorname{ran} A \subseteq \operatorname{ran} B$. The map sending $x \in E$ to the unique $z \in (\ker B)^\perp$ with $B z = A x$ is linear; it is the Douglas factor as a linear map $E \to E$.
