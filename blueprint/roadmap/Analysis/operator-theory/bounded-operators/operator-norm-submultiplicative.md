# The operator norm is submultiplicative

Let $E$, $F$ and $G$ be seminormed spaces over nontrivially normed fields, and let $f:E\to F$ and $h:F\to G$ be continuous semilinear maps, with operator norm $\lVert f\rVert=\inf\{c\ge0:\lVert f x\rVert\le c\lVert x\rVert\text{ for all }x\}$. Then

$$\lVert h\circ f\rVert\le\lVert h\rVert\,\lVert f\rVert .$$

This covers bounded linear operators between real or complex normed spaces, and $d\times d$ matrices acting on $\mathbb R^d$ with the operator norm induced by a norm on $\mathbb R^d$.
