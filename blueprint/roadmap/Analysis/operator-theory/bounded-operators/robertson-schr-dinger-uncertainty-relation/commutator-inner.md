# Commutator expectation as the skew part of the centered inner product

Let $E$ be a complex Hilbert space, whose inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument, and let $A, B$ be bounded self-adjoint operators on $E$ with commutator $[A, B] = AB - BA$. For every unit vector $x \in E$, the [centered vectors](centered.md) satisfy

$$
\langle [A, B] x, x \rangle = \langle \tilde B x, \tilde A x \rangle - \langle \tilde A x, \tilde B x \rangle.
$$
