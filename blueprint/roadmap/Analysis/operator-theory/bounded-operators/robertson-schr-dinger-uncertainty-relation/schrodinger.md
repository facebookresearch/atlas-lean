# Robertson–Schrödinger uncertainty principle

Let $E$ be a complex Hilbert space and $A, B$ bounded self-adjoint operators on $E$. For every unit vector $x \in E$, the product of the [normalized variances](variance.md) is at least the squared covariance plus a quarter of the squared modulus of the commutator expectation:

$$
\left( \operatorname{Re} \langle \tilde A x, \tilde B x \rangle \right)^2 + \frac{1}{4} |\langle (AB - BA) x, x \rangle|^2 \le \operatorname{Var}_x(A) \operatorname{Var}_x(B),
$$

where $\tilde A x$ and $\tilde B x$ are the [centered vectors](centered.md).
