# Variance as second moment minus squared mean

Let $E$ be a complex Hilbert space and $A$ a bounded self-adjoint operator on $E$. For every unit vector $x \in E$, the [normalized variance](variance.md) is the second moment minus the squared mean:

$$
\operatorname{Var}_x(A) = \operatorname{Re} \langle A^2 x, x \rangle - \langle A \rangle_x^2.
$$
