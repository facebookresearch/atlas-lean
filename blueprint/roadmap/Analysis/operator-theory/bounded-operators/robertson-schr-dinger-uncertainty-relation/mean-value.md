# Normalized expectation value of an observable

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space, whose inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument, and let $A$ be a bounded operator on $E$. The normalized expectation of $A$ in the state $x \in E$ is the Rayleigh quotient

$$
\langle A \rangle_x = \frac{\operatorname{Re} \langle A x, x \rangle}{\|x\|^2},
$$

which is $0$ at $x = 0$ (division by zero giving $0$). For a nonzero state and a self-adjoint observable $A$ it is the physical expectation value.
