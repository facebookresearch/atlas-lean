# Robertson's uncertainty principle

Let $E$ be a complex Hilbert space, let $A$ and $B$ be bounded self-adjoint operators on $E$, and let $x \in E$ be a unit vector. Then

$$
\frac{1}{4} \bigl| \langle (AB - BA) x, x \rangle \bigr|^2 \le \operatorname{Var}_x(A)\, \operatorname{Var}_x(B),
$$

where $\operatorname{Var}_x$ is the [normalized variance](variance.md): the product of the variances is at least a quarter of the squared modulus of the commutator expectation.
