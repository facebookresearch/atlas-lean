# Centered state vector of an observable

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space, $A$ a bounded operator on $E$ and $x \in E$. The centered vector of $A$ at $x$ is

$$
\tilde A x = A x - \langle A \rangle_x\, x,
$$

where $\langle A \rangle_x$ is the [normalized expectation](mean-value.md). Its squared norm divided by $\|x\|^2$ is the [normalized variance](variance.md).
