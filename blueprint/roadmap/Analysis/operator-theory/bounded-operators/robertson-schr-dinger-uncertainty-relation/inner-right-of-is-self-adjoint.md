# Flipped expectation inner product of a self-adjoint operator

Let $E$ be a complex Hilbert space and $A$ a bounded self-adjoint operator on $E$. For every $x \in E$, $\langle x, A x \rangle = \langle A \rangle_x \|x\|^2$, where $\langle A \rangle_x$ is the [normalized expectation](mean-value.md).
