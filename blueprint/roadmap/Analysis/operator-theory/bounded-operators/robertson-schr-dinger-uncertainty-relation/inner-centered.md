# Inner product of two centered vectors

Let $E$ be a complex Hilbert space and $A, B$ bounded self-adjoint operators on $E$. For every unit vector $x \in E$, the [centered vectors](centered.md) $\tilde A x = A x - \langle A \rangle_x x$ and $\tilde B x$ satisfy

$$
\langle \tilde A x, \tilde B x \rangle = \langle A x, B x \rangle - \langle A \rangle_x \langle B \rangle_x.
$$
