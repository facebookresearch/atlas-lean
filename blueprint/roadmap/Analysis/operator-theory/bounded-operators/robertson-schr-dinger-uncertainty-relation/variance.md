# Normalized variance of an observable

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space, $A$ a bounded operator on $E$ and $x \in E$. The normalized variance of $A$ in the state $x$ is

$$
\operatorname{Var}_x(A) = \frac{\|A x - \langle A \rangle_x\, x\|^2}{\|x\|^2},
$$

the squared norm of the [centered vector](centered.md) divided by $\|x\|^2$; it is $0$ at $x = 0$. For a nonzero state and a self-adjoint observable $A$ it is the physical variance, and on unit vectors it is the squared norm of the centered vector.
