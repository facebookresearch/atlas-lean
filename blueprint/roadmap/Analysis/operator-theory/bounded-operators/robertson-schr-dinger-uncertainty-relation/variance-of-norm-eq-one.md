# Normalized variance on the unit sphere

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space and $A$ a bounded operator on $E$. For every unit vector $x \in E$, the [normalized variance](variance.md) is the squared norm of the [centered vector](centered.md): $\operatorname{Var}_x(A) = \|A x - \langle A \rangle_x\, x\|^2$.
