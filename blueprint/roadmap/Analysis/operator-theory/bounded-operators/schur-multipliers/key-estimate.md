# Tensoring with the identity preserves the operator norm bound

Let $X$ be a finite set, $A$ a complex $X \times X$ matrix with operator norm $\|A\|$, and $E$ a complex inner
product space. For every family $(z_y)_{y \in X}$ in $E$,

$$\sum_{x \in X} \Bigl\| \sum_{y \in X} A_{xy}\, z_y \Bigr\|^2 \le \|A\|^2 \sum_{y \in X} \|z_y\|^2,$$

that is, $A \otimes \mathrm{Id}_E$ has operator norm at most $\|A\|$.
