# Schur multiplier bound for Gram matrices

Let $X$ be a finite set, $A$ a complex $X \times X$ matrix and $u, v : X \to E$ two families in a complex inner product space $E$. The Hadamard product of $A$ with the Gram matrix $(\langle u_x, v_y\rangle)_{x,y}$ has operator norm on $\ell^2(X)$ at most $\|A\| \cdot \sup_x \|u_x\| \cdot \sup_y \|v_y\|$, where $\|A\|$ is the operator norm of $A$ on $\ell^2(X)$.
