# Are all operators of a chaotic semigroup chaotic?

Question 1 (Peris-Conejero): in a chaotic `C₀`-semigroup on a separable complex Banach space,
every operator `T t`, `t > 0`, is chaotic.

Its resolution: [Chaotic semigroups with non-chaotic operators](chaotic-semigroup-every-operator-chaotic-2.md).
