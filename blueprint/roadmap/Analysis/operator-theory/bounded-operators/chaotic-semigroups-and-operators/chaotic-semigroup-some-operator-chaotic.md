# Does every chaotic semigroup contain a chaotic operator?

Question 2 (Peris-Conejero): every chaotic `C₀`-semigroup on a separable complex Banach space
contains a chaotic operator `T t₀`, `t₀ > 0`.

Its resolution: [Chaotic semigroups containing no chaotic operator](chaotic-semigroup-some-operator-chaotic-2.md).
