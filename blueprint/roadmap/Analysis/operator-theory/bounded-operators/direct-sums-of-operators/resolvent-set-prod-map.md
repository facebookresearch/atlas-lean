# Resolvent set of a direct sum operator

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. For bounded operators $T$ on $E$ and $S$ on $F$, the resolvent set of $T \oplus S$ is the intersection of the resolvent sets of $T$ and $S$.
