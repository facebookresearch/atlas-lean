# Composition of direct sum operators is componentwise

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. For bounded operators $T' : E \to E'$, $T : E' \to E''$, $S' : F \to F'$ and $S : F' \to F''$, where $E''$ and $F''$ are further normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$,

$$
(T \oplus S)(T' \oplus S') = (T T') \oplus (S S').
$$
