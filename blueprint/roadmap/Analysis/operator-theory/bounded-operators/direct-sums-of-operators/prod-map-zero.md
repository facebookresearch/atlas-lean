# Direct sum of zero operators is zero

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. The direct sum of the zero operators $E \to E'$ and $F \to F'$ is the zero operator $E \times F \to E' \times F'$.
