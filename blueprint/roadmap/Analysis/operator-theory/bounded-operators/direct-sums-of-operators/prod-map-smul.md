# Scalar multiplication of a direct sum operator

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. For every $c \in \mathbb{k}$ and bounded operators $T : E \to E'$ and $S : F \to F'$, $(cT) \oplus (cS) = c\,(T \oplus S)$.
