# Compactness of a direct sum operator

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. For bounded operators $T : E \to E'$ and $S : F \to F'$, the direct sum $T \oplus S$ is compact if and only if $T$ and $S$ are both compact.
