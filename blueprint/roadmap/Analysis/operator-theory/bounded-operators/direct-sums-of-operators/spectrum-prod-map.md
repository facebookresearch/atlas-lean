# Spectrum of a direct sum operator is the union of spectra

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. For bounded operators $T$ on $E$ and $S$ on $F$, $\sigma(T \oplus S) = \sigma(T) \cup \sigma(S)$, spectra being taken over $\mathbb{k}$ in the algebras of bounded operators.
