# Direct sum of identity operators is the identity

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field and let $E, E', F, F'$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$; products of normed spaces carry the maximum norm, and $T \oplus S$ denotes the operator $(x, y) \mapsto (Tx, Sy)$. The direct sum of the identity operators of $E$ and $F$ is the identity operator of $E \times F$.
