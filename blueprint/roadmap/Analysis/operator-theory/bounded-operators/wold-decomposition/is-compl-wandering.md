# Wandering subspace and range are complementary

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. The [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$ and the range of $V$ are complementary subspaces of $H$: their intersection is $\{0\}$ and their sum is $H$.
