# Isometry restricted to the residual subspace preserves inner products

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. For all $x, y$ in the [residual subspace](residual-sub.md) $H_\infty$, $\langle V x, V y \rangle = \langle x, y \rangle$.
