# Orthogonality of pushed-forward wandering vectors, symmetric form

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. If $k < l$, $a$ lies in the [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$ and $b \in H$, then $\langle V^l b, V^k a \rangle = 0$.
