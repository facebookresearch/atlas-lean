# Uniqueness of the finite Wold decomposition

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $(w_k)$ and $(w'_k)$ be sequences in the [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$ and $y, y' \in H$ with

$$
x = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} V^k w_k + V^n y = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} V^k w'_k + V^n y'.
$$

Then $y = y'$ and $\sum_{k<n} V^k w_k = \sum_{k<n} V^k w'_k$.
