# Powers of an isometry preserve inner products

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. For all $k, d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $a, b \in H$,

$$
\langle V^k a, V^{k+d} b \rangle = \langle a, V^d b \rangle.
$$
