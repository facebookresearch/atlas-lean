# Unitary part of an isometry on its residual subspace

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. The restriction of $V$ to its [residual subspace](residual-sub.md) $H_\infty$ is a unitary operator $U$ of $H_\infty$, that is, a surjective linear isometry of $H_\infty$ onto itself.
