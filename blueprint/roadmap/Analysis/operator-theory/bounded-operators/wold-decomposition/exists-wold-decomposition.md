# Finite Wold decomposition

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. For every $x \in H$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there are vectors $w_0, w_1, w_2, \dots$ in the [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$, with $w_k = 0$ for $k \ge n$, and $y \in H$ such that

$$
x = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} V^k w_k + V^n y.
$$
