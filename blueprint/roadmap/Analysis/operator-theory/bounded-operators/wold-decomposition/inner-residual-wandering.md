# Residual subspace is orthogonal to the wandering part

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. If $x$ lies in the [residual subspace](residual-sub.md) $H_\infty$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $a$ lies in the [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$, then $\langle x, V^k a \rangle = 0$: the wandering part and the residual part of the Wold decomposition are orthogonal.
