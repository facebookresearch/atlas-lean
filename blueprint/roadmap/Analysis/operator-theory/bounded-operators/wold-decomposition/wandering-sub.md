# Wandering subspace of an isometry

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. The **wandering subspace** of $V$ is the orthogonal complement $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$ of its range: its vectors are orthogonal to every vector in the range of $V$.
