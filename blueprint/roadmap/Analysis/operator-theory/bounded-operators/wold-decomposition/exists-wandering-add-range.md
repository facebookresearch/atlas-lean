# One-step Wold splitting into wandering vector plus range

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. Every $x \in H$ can be written $x = w + V y$ with $w$ in the [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$ and $y \in H$.
