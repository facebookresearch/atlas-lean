# Action of the residual unitary of an isometry

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. The [residual unitary](wold-residual-equiv.md) $U$ of $V$ acts as $V$: $U x = V x$ for every $x$ in the residual subspace $H_\infty$.
