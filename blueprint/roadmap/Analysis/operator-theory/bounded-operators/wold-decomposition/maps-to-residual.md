# An isometry maps its residual subspace into itself

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. Then $V$ maps its [residual subspace](residual-sub.md) $H_\infty = \bigcap_n \operatorname{ran} V^n$ into itself.
