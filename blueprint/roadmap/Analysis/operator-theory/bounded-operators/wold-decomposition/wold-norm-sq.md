# Pythagorean identity for the Wold decomposition

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. If $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the vectors $w_0, w_1, \dots$ lie in the [wandering subspace](wandering-sub.md) $(\operatorname{ran} V)^\perp$, $y \in H$ and $x = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} V^k w_k + V^n y$, then

$$
\|x\|^2 = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \|w_k\|^2 + \|y\|^2.
$$
