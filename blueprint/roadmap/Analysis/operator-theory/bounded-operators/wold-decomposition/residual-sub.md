# Residual subspace of an isometry

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $V : H \to H$ be a linear isometry. The **residual subspace** of $V$ is $H_\infty = \bigcap_{n \ge 0} \operatorname{ran} V^n$, the set of vectors in the range of every power of $V$.
