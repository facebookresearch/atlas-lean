# Truncation of a matrix to its top-left block

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. The truncation $a^{(N)}$ of $a$ to its top-left $N \times N$ block has entries $a^{(N)}_{ij} = a_{ij}$ if $i < N$ and $j < N$, and $a^{(N)}_{ij} = 0$ otherwise.
