# Action of a rank-one Hilbert–Schmidt operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $u, v \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and let $T$ be the [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md) of the [rank-one matrix](hs-rank-one.md) $(u_i \overline{v_j})_{i, j}$. For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, $(Tx)_i = u_i \langle v, x \rangle$, the inner product being conjugate-linear in its first argument.
