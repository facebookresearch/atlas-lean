# Squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of a matrix

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. The squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md) $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ is the finite sum

$$
\|a\|_{HS}^2 = \sum_{(i, j) \in \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}} |a_{ij}|^2.
$$

It is defined only for Hilbert–Schmidt matrices; for operators the squared norm may be infinite, see the [squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm in a family](hs-norm-sq-2.md), which takes values in $[0, \infty]$.
