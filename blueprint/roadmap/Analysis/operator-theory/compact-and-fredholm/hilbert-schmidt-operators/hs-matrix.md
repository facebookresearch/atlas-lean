# Matrix of a bounded operator on $\ell^2$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $(e_j)_{j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be the standard basis vectors of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. The matrix of a bounded operator $T$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is the infinite matrix whose entry $(i, j)$ is the coordinate of index $i$ of $T e_j$.
