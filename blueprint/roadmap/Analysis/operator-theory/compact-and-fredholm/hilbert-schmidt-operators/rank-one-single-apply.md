# Scaled rank-one operator on standard basis vectors

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $(e_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ be the standard basis vectors of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For $b \in \mathbb{K}$ and $i, j \in \mathbb{N}$, let $R$ be the operator $z \mapsto b \langle e_j, z \rangle e_i$. Then for every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $(Rx)_k = b x_j$ if $k = i$ and $(Rx)_k = 0$ otherwise.
