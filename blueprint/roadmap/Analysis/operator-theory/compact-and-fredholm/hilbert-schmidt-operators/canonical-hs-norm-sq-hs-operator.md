# Canonical Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the constructed operator

Let $(e_j)_{j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be the standard basis vectors of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{R})$ and let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a real [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md) with [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md) $T_a$. Then the [squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of $T_a$ in the family](hs-norm-sq-2.md) $(e_j)$, the extended nonnegative real $\sum_j \|T_a e_j\|^2$, equals the finite sum $\sum_{i, j} a_{ij}^2$.
