# Operator norm is at most the Hilbert–Schmidt norm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md) and $T_a$ its [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md). Then $\|T_a\| \le \|a\|_{HS} = \left(\sum_{i, j} |a_{ij}|^2\right)^{1/2}$.
