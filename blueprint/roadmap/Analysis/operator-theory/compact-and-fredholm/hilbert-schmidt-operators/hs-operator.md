# Hilbert–Schmidt operator on ℓ²

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). The Hilbert–Schmidt operator of $a$ is the bounded operator $T_a$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ given by $(T_a x)_i = \sum_j a_{ij} x_j$; it is bounded with norm at most $\|a\|_{HS} = \left(\sum_{i, j} |a_{ij}|^2\right)^{1/2}$.
