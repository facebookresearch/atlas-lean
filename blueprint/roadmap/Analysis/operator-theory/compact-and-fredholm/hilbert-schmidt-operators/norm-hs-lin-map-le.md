# Norm bound for the Hilbert–Schmidt linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md) and $T_a$ its [linear map](hs-lin-map.md). For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$,

$$
\|T_a x\| \le \|a\|_{HS}\, \|x\|, \qquad \|a\|_{HS} = \Big(\sum_{i, j} |a_{ij}|^2\Big)^{1/2}.
$$
