# Pointwise action of the Hilbert–Schmidt linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md) and $T_a$ its [linear map](hs-lin-map.md). For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, $(T_a x)_i = \sum_j a_{ij} x_j$.
