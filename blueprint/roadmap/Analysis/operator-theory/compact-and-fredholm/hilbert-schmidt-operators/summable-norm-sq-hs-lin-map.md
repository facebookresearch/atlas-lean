# Square-summability of a Hilbert–Schmidt image

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md) and $T_a$ its [linear map](hs-lin-map.md). For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the squared moduli of the coordinates of $T_a x$ are summable: $\sum_i |(T_a x)_i|^2 < \infty$.
