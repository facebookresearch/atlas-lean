# Hilbert–Schmidt coordinates as inner products with rows

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} a_{ij} x_j = \langle r_i, x \rangle,
$$

where $r_i = (\overline{a_{ij}})_{j}$ is the [row vector](hs-row.md) of index $i$ and the inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument.
