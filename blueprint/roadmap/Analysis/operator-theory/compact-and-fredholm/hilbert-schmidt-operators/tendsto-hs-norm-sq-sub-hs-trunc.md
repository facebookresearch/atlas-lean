# Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the truncation error tends to zero

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). Then $\|a - a^{(N)}\|_{HS}^2 \to 0$ as $N \to \infty$, where $a^{(N)}$ is the [truncation](hs-trunc.md) of $a$ to its top-left $N \times N$ block.
