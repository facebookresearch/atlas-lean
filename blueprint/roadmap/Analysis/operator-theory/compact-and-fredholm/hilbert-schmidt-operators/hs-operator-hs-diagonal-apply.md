# Diagonal Hilbert–Schmidt operator acts by pointwise multiplication

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $d : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_i |d_i|^2 < \infty$. The [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md) $T$ of the [diagonal matrix](hs-diagonal.md) $\operatorname{diag}(d)$ acts by pointwise multiplication: $(Tx)_i = d_i x_i$ for every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$.
