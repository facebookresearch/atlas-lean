# Operator norm bounded by the Hilbert–Schmidt norm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ whose [matrix](hs-matrix.md) $a$ is [Hilbert–Schmidt](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). Then $\|T\| \le \|a\|_{HS} = \left(\sum_{i, j} |a_{ij}|^2\right)^{1/2}$.
