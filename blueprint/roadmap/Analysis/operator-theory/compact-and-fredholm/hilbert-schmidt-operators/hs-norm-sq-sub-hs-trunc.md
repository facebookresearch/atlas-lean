# Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the truncation error as a tail sum

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the [truncation](hs-trunc.md) $a^{(N)}$ satisfies

$$
\|a - a^{(N)}\|_{HS}^2 = \|a\|_{HS}^2 - \sum_{i < N,\ j < N} |a_{ij}|^2.
$$
