# Hilbert–Schmidt operator as a linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). The Hilbert–Schmidt linear map of $a$ is the linear map $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K}) \to \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$,

$$
x \mapsto \Big(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} a_{ij} x_j\Big)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}.
$$
