# Hilbert–Schmidt matrix maps ℓ² vectors into ℓ²

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the sequence $\left(\sum_j a_{ij} x_j\right)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ belongs to $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
