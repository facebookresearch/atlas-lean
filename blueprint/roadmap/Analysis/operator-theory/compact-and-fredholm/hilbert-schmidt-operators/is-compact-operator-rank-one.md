# Rank-one operators on $\ell^2$ are compact

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For all $x, y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the rank-one operator $z \mapsto \langle y, z \rangle x$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is compact: it factors through the one-dimensional space $\mathbb{K}$.
