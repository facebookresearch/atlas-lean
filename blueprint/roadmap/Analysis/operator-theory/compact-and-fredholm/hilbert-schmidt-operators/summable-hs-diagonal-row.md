# Rows of a diagonal matrix are square-summable

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $d : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, the row of index $i$ of the [diagonal matrix](hs-diagonal.md) $\operatorname{diag}(d)$ is square-summable: $\sum_j |\operatorname{diag}(d)_{ij}|^2 < \infty$.
