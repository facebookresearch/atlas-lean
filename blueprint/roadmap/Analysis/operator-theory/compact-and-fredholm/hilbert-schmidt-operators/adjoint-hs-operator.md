# Adjoint of a Hilbert–Schmidt operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). The adjoint of its [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md) $T_a$ is the Hilbert–Schmidt operator of the [conjugate transpose](hs-transpose-star.md): $T_a^* = T_{a^*}$.
