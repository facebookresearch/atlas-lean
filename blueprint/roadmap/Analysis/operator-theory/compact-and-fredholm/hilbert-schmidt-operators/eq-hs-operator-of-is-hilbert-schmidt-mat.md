# Operator with Hilbert–Schmidt matrix is the operator of its matrix

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ whose [matrix](hs-matrix.md) $a$ is [Hilbert–Schmidt](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). Then $T$ is the [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md) $T_a$ of $a$.
