# Canonical Hilbert–Schmidt norm equals the matrix norm

Let $(e_j)_{j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be the standard basis vectors of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{R})$ and let $T$ be a bounded operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{R})$ whose [matrix](hs-matrix.md) $a$ is [Hilbert–Schmidt](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). Then the [squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of $T$ in the family](hs-norm-sq-2.md) $(e_j)$, the extended nonnegative real $\sum_j \|T e_j\|^2$, equals the finite sum $\sum_{i, j} a_{ij}^2$.
