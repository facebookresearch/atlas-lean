# Hilbert–Schmidt norm of a diagonal matrix

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. If $d : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $\sum_i |d_i|^2 < \infty$, then the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the [diagonal matrix](hs-diagonal.md) $\operatorname{diag}(d)$ is $\|\operatorname{diag}(d)\|_{HS}^2 = \sum_i |d_i|^2$.
