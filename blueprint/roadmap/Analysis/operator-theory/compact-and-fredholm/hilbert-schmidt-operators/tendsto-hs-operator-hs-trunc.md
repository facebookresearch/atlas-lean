# Truncated operators converge in norm to the Hilbert–Schmidt operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a$ be a [Hilbert–Schmidt matrix](is-hilbert-schmidt.md). Then $T_{a^{(N)}} \to T_a$ in operator norm as $N \to \infty$, where $T$ denotes the [Hilbert–Schmidt operator](hs-operator.md) and $a^{(N)}$ the [truncation](hs-trunc.md) of $a$ to its top-left $N \times N$ block.
