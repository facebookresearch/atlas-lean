# Subtracting a finite-rank operator preserves the Fredholm index

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Banach space and $F$ a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $T : E \to F$ is a Fredholm operator and $G : E \to F$ is a bounded operator with finite-dimensional range, then $\operatorname{ind}(T - G) = \operatorname{ind} T$.
