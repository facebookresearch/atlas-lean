# Fredholm index is constant on preconnected families

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a complete nontrivially normed field, let $E$ be a Banach space and $F$ a normed space over $\mathbb{k}$. Let $X$ be a topological space, $s \subseteq X$ a preconnected set and $\gamma : X \to L(E, F)$ a map continuous on $s$ such that $\gamma(z)$ is a Fredholm operator for every $z \in s$. Then $\operatorname{ind} \gamma(x) = \operatorname{ind} \gamma(y)$ for all $x, y \in s$: the index is locally constant on Fredholm operators and takes values in the discrete space $\mathbb{Z}$.
