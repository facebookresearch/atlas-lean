# Cauchy interlacing, lower half

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ of finite dimension $n$, and let $T$ be a self-adjoint operator on $E$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$. Let $S \subseteq E$ be a subspace of dimension $n - 1$, and let $\mu_0 \ge \dots \ge \mu_{n-2}$ be the eigenvalues of the [compression](compression.md) $P_S T|_S$, which is self-adjoint. For every $k$ with $k + 1 < n$, $\lambda_{k+1} \le \mu_k$.
