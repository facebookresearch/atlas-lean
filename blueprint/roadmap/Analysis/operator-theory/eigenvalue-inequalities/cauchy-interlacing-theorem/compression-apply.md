# Action of the compression of an operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $S \subseteq E$ be a subspace admitting an orthogonal projection $P_S$ (for instance a complete subspace). For every linear operator $T$ on $E$ and every $y \in S$, the [compression](compression.md) of $T$ to $S$ maps $y$ to $P_S(T y)$.
