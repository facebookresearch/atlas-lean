# Compression of an operator to a subspace

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, let $T$ be a linear operator on $E$ and let $S \subseteq E$ be a subspace that admits an orthogonal projection $P_S$ (for instance a finite-dimensional or complete subspace). The compression of $T$ to $S$ is the operator $P_S T|_S$ on $S$: apply $T$ and project the result orthogonally back onto $S$.
