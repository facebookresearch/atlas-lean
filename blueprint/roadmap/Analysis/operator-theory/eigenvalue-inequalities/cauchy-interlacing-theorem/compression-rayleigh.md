# Rayleigh form of a compression

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $S \subseteq E$ be a subspace admitting an orthogonal projection $P_S$ (for instance a complete subspace). For every linear operator $T$ on $E$ and every $y \in S$, the Rayleigh form of the [compression](compression.md) at $y$ equals that of $T$: $\operatorname{Re} \langle P_S T y, y \rangle = \operatorname{Re} \langle T y, y \rangle$.
