# Compression of a symmetric operator is symmetric

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $S \subseteq E$ be a subspace admitting an orthogonal projection $P_S$ (for instance a complete subspace). If $T$ is a symmetric linear operator on $E$, that is, $\langle T x, y \rangle = \langle x, T y \rangle$ for all $x, y$, then its [compression](compression.md) $P_S T|_S$ is symmetric on $S$.
