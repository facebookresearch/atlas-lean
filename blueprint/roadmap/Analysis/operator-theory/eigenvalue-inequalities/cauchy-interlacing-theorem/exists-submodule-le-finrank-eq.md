# Subspaces of every smaller dimension inside a given subspace

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. Let $W \subseteq E$ be a subspace and let $d = \dim W$ when $W$ is finite-dimensional, $d = 0$ otherwise. If $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $m \le d$, then there is a subspace $W' \subseteq W$ with $\dim W' = m$ (in the same convention). For infinite-dimensional $W$ this only allows $m = 0$, although subspaces of every finite dimension exist there too.
