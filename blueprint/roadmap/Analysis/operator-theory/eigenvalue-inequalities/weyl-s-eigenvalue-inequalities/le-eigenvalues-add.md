# Weyl's inequality (lower bound), matrix form

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $A, B$ be Hermitian $n \times n$ matrices over $\mathbb{K}$, with eigenvalues listed with multiplicity in decreasing order, $\lambda_0 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$. For all $a, b < n$ with $a + b \ge n - 1$,

$$
\lambda_a(A) + \lambda_b(B) \le \lambda_{a+b-(n-1)}(A + B).
$$
