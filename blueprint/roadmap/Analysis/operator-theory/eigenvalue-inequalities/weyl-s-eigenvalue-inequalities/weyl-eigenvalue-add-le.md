# Weyl's inequality (upper bound)

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ of finite dimension $n$, and let $T, S$ be self-adjoint operators on $E$. Eigenvalues are listed with multiplicity in decreasing order, $\lambda_0 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$. For all $a, b$ with $a + b < n$,

$$
\lambda_{a+b}(T + S) \le \lambda_a(T) + \lambda_b(S).
$$
