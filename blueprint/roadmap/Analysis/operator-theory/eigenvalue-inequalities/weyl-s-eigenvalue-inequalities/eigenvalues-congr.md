# Eigenvalues of equal Hermitian matrices agree

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. If $A$ and $B$ are Hermitian $n \times n$ matrices over $\mathbb{K}$ with $A = B$, then their eigenvalue lists, in decreasing order with multiplicity, coincide. This transports eigenvalue computations along matrix identities such as $\operatorname{diag}(d) + \operatorname{diag}(d') = \operatorname{diag}(d + d')$.
