# Eigenvalues of a diagonal matrix with decreasing entries

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $d_0 \ge d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_{n-1}$ be real numbers. The eigenvalues of the diagonal matrix $\operatorname{diag}(d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ over $\mathbb{K}$, listed with multiplicity in decreasing order, are $d_0, \dots, d_{n-1}$: $\lambda_i = d_i$ for every $i$.
