# Eigenbasis expansion of the quadratic form of a self-adjoint operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ of finite dimension $n$, and let $T$ be a self-adjoint linear operator on $E$, with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$ (repeated according to multiplicity, in decreasing order) and a corresponding orthonormal eigenbasis $e_0, \dots, e_{n-1}$, $T e_i = \lambda_i e_i$. For every $x \in E$,

$$
\langle x, T x \rangle = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \lambda_i\, |\langle e_i, x \rangle|^2.
$$
