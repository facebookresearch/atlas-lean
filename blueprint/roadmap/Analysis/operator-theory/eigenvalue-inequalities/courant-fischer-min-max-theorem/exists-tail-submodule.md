# Courant–Fischer min–max, extremal subspace form

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ of finite dimension $n$, and let $T$ be a self-adjoint linear operator on $E$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$ (repeated according to multiplicity, in decreasing order). Write $R_T(x) = \operatorname{Re} \langle T x, x \rangle$ for the [Rayleigh form](rayleigh-form.md). Let $k < n$. There is a subspace $W \subseteq E$ of dimension $n - k$ such that $R_T(x) \le \lambda_k$ for every unit vector $x \in W$, with equality for some unit vector of $W$.
