# Rayleigh form of an operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ and let $T$ be a linear operator on $E$. The Rayleigh form of $T$ is $R_T(x) = \operatorname{Re} \langle T x, x \rangle$ for $x \in E$. On unit vectors it is the real part of the Rayleigh quotient $\langle T x, x \rangle$, and it is the Rayleigh quotient itself when $T$ is symmetric, since $\langle T x, x \rangle$ is then real.
