# Upper bound for the Rayleigh form on the eigenbasis tail

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ of finite dimension $n$, and let $T$ be a self-adjoint linear operator on $E$, with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$ (repeated according to multiplicity, in decreasing order) and a corresponding orthonormal eigenbasis $e_0, \dots, e_{n-1}$, $T e_i = \lambda_i e_i$. For $k < n$ and every $x$ in the span of the [tail](tail-family.md) $(e_k, \dots, e_{n-1})$, the [Rayleigh form](rayleigh-form.md) satisfies $R_T(x) \le \lambda_k \|x\|^2$.
