# Eigenvalues of a positive definite operator are positive

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space of finite dimension $n$ and let $T$ be a self-adjoint operator on $E$ with eigenvalues $\mu_0 \ge \dots \ge \mu_{n-1}$ (repeated according to multiplicity). If $\operatorname{Re} \langle x, T x \rangle > 0$ for every nonzero $x \in E$, then $\mu_j > 0$ for every $j$.
