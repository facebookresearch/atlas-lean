# Eigenvalues of a positive semidefinite operator are nonnegative

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space of finite dimension $n$ and let $T$ be a self-adjoint operator on $E$ with eigenvalues $\mu_0 \ge \dots \ge \mu_{n-1}$ (repeated according to multiplicity). If $\operatorname{Re} \langle x, T x \rangle \ge 0$ for every $x \in E$, then $\mu_j \ge 0$ for every $j$: an eigenvalue is the value of the quadratic form at a unit eigenvector.
