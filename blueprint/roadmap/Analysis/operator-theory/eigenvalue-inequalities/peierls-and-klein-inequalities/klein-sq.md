# Klein's inequality for the square function

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space of finite dimension $n$, let $T$ and $S$ be self-adjoint operators on $E$ with eigenvalues $\mu_0 \ge \dots \ge \mu_{n-1}$ and $\lambda_0 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{n-1}$ respectively (repeated according to multiplicity), and let $(b_i)_{i < n}$ be an orthonormal basis of $E$. With $t_i = \operatorname{Re} \langle b_i, T b_i \rangle$,

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \big( \lambda_i^2 + 2 \lambda_i (t_i - \lambda_i) \big) \le \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} \mu_j^2.
$$
