# Peierls' inequality for the exponential

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space of finite dimension $n$ and let $T$ be a self-adjoint operator on $E$ with eigenvalues $\mu_0 \ge \dots \ge \mu_{n-1}$ (repeated according to multiplicity). For every orthonormal basis $(b_i)_{i < n}$ of $E$,

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \exp\big(\operatorname{Re} \langle b_i, T b_i \rangle\big) \le \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} e^{\mu_j}.
$$
