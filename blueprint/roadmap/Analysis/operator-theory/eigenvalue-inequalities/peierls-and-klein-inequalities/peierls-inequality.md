# Peierls' inequality

The diagonal of a self-adjoint operator in
any orthonormal basis is majorized by its eigenvalues, as measured by
any convex function.

Carlen states it for a Hermitian matrix $A$ and any orthonormal basis $u_1, \dots, u_n$: $\sum_j f(\langle u_j, A u_j\rangle) \le \operatorname{tr} f(A)$ for every convex $f$. Taking $u$ to be an eigenbasis of $A + B$ is the standard route to convexity of $A \mapsto \operatorname{tr} f(A)$ (Carlen, Theorem 2.10) and to the Peierls–Bogoliubov inequality.
