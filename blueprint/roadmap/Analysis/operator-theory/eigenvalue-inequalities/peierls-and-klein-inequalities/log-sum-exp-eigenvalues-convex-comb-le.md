# Peierls–Bogoliubov inequality, logarithmic form

Let $E$ be a complex inner product space of finite dimension $n$, let $S$ and $T$ be self-adjoint operators on $E$, let $0 \le t \le 1$ and $C = tS + (1 - t)T$, which is again self-adjoint. For a self-adjoint operator $X$ write $\operatorname{tr} e^X = \sum_j e^{\lambda_j(X)}$, the sum over its eigenvalues with multiplicity. Then

$$
\log \operatorname{tr} e^{C} \le t \log \operatorname{tr} e^S + (1 - t) \log \operatorname{tr} e^T:
$$

the map $X \mapsto \log \operatorname{tr} e^X$ is convex (E. A. Carlen, *Trace inequalities and quantum entropy: an introductory course*, lecture-notes draft of May 3, 2009, Theorem 2.12). In dimension $0$ all three traces vanish and, with $\log 0 = 0$, both sides are $0$.
