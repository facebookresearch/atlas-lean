# Per-row estimate in Schur's test on $\ell^2$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$. Then for every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and every $i$,

$$
\Big|\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} a_{ij} x_j\Big|^2 \le R \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} |a_{ij}|\, |x_j|^2.
$$
