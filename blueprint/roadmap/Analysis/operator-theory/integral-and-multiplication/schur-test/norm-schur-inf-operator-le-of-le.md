# Schur's test with a single row and column bound

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $B \ge 0$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that every row series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ and every column series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $B$. Then the [Schur operator](schur-inf-operator.md) $T_a$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ satisfies $\|T_a\| \le B$.
