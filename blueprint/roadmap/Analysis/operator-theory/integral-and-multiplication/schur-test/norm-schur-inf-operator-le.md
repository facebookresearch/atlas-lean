# Schur's test

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix over $\mathbb{K}$ whose row sums $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converge with sum at most $R$ and whose column sums $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converge with sum at most $C$. Then the [Schur operator](schur-inf-operator.md) $T_a$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, $(T_a x)_i = \sum_j a_{ij} x_j$, has operator norm at most $\sqrt{R C}$.
