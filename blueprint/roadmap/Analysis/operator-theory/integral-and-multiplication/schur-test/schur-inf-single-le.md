# Entries of a Schur matrix are bounded by the row bound

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$. Then $|a_{ij}| \le R$ for all $i, j \in \mathbb{N}$.
