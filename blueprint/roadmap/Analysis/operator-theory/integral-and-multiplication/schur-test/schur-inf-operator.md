# Schur operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$, and for every $j$, the series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $C$. The Schur operator of $a$ is the bounded operator $T_a$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ given by $(T_a x)_i = \sum_j a_{ij} x_j$, which has norm at most $\sqrt{RC}$.
