# Product summability for the Schur bilinear form

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$, and for every $j$, the series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $C$. Then for all $x, v \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the family $\left(|a_{ij}|\, |x_i|\, |v_j|\right)_{(i, j) \in \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}}$ is summable.
