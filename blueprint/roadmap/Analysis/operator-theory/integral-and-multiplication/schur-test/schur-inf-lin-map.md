# Schur operator of an infinite matrix as a linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$, and for every $j$, the series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $C$. The Schur linear map of $a$ is the linear map on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$

$$
x \mapsto \Big(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} a_{ij} x_j\Big)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}.
$$
