# Weighted partial sums grouped by the second index

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $j$, the series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $C$. Then for every $v \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and every finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$,

$$
\sum_{(i, j) \in S} |a_{ij}|\, |v_j|^2 \le C\, \|v\|^2.
$$
