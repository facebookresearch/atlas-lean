# Row summability of the conjugate-transpose Schur matrix

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $j$, the series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges. Then the rows of the conjugate transpose are absolutely summable: for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |\overline{a_{ji}}|$ converges.
