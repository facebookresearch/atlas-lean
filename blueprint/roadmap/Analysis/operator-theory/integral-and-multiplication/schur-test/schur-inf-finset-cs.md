# Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz for one row of a Schur matrix

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$. For every finite set $s \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, every sequence $x : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ and every $i \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
\Big(\sum_{j \in s} |a_{ij}|\, |x_j|\Big)^2 \le \Big(\sum_{j \in s} |a_{ij}|\Big) \Big(\sum_{j \in s} |a_{ij}|\, |x_j|^2\Big).
$$
