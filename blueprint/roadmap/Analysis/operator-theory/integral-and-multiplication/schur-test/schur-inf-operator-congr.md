# Schur operator depends only on the matrix

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. The [Schur operator](schur-inf-operator.md) of an infinite matrix does not depend on the bounds used to construct it: if $a = b$, where $a$ satisfies the row and column bounds $R$ and $C$ and $b$ satisfies the row and column bounds $R'$ and $C'$ (each row and column series of absolute values converging), then the two Schur operators coincide.
