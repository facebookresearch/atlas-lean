# Weighted partial sums grouped by the first index

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$. Then for every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and every finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$,

$$
\sum_{(i, j) \in S} |a_{ij}|\, |x_i|^2 \le R\, \|x\|^2.
$$
