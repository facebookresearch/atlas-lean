# Summability of the double weighted series in Schur's test

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a = (a_{ij})_{i, j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be an infinite matrix with entries in $\mathbb{K}$ such that, for every $i$, the series $\sum_j |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $R$, and for every $j$, the series $\sum_i |a_{ij}|$ converges with sum at most $C$. Then for every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the series $\sum_i \sum_j |a_{ij}|\, |x_j|^2$ converges.
