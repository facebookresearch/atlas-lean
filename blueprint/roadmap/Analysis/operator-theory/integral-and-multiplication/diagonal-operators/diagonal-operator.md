# Bounded diagonal operator on a Hilbert basis

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $H$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ with a Hilbert basis $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$, and let $(a_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be scalars with $|a_i| \le C$ for every $i$. The diagonal operator with coefficients $(a_i)$ is the bounded operator $x \mapsto \sum_i a_i \langle b_i, x \rangle\, b_i$ on $H$, which maps $b_i$ to $a_i b_i$: in coordinates it multiplies the sequence $(\langle b_i, x \rangle)_i \in \ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ by $(a_i)$.
