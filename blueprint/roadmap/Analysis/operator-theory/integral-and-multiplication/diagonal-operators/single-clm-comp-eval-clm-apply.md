# Rank-one term of the finite-support decomposition on $\ell^2$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $(\beta_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be scalars and let $i \in \iota$. The rank-one operator on $\ell^2(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ that multiplies the $i$-th coordinate by $\beta_i$ and places the result at index $i$ maps $x$ to $\beta_i x_i\, e_i$, where $e_i$ is the $i$-th standard basis vector.
