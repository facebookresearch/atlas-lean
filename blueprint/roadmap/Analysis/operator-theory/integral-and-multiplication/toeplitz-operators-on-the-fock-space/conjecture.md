# Berger–Coburn conjecture

Let $d\mu = (2\pi)^{-1} e^{-|z|^2/2}\,dA$ on $\mathbb{C}$ and let $g$ be measurable with $g\,k_a \in L^2(d\mu)$ for every normalized coherent state $k_a(z) = e^{z\bar a/2 - |a|^2/4}$. The Berger–Coburn conjecture asserts that the Toeplitz operator $T_g$ is bounded on the Fock space if and only if the heat transform $\pi^{-1}\int g(w) e^{-|z-w|^2}\,dA(w)$ of $g$ is bounded.
