# Dense periodic points of the Rolewicz operator for $|\mu| > 1$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the backward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. If $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $|\mu| > 1$, then the periodic points of $\mu B$ (the vectors $x$ with $(\mu B)^n x = x$ for some $n \ge 1$) are dense in $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
