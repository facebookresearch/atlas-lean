# Norm of a term of the periodic approximant

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the forward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, $N, m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, with $y^{(N)} = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$ its truncation,

$$
\left\| (\mu^{-N})^m\, S^{Nm} y^{(N)} \right\| = |\mu^{-N}|^m\, \|y^{(N)}\|,
$$

where $0^{-1} = 0$.
