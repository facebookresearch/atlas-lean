# Backward shift annihilates truncations after $N$ steps

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the backward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the truncation $y^{(N)} = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$ satisfies $B^N y^{(N)} = 0$.
