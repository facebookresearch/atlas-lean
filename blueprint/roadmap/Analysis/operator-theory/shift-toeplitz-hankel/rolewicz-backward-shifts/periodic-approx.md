# Periodic approximant of a truncated sequence

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $S$ be the forward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, and for $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$ let $y^{(N)} = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, 0, \dots)$ be the truncation of $y$ to its first $N$ coordinates. For $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, the periodic approximant of $y$ is

$$
P_{\mu, N}(y) = \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} (\mu^{-N})^m\, S^{Nm} y^{(N)},
$$

the extension of $y^{(N)}$ by blocks of length $N$ scaled geometrically. Here $0^{-1} = 0$ and a divergent series is given the value $0$; the series converges when $|\mu| > 1$ and $N \ge 1$.
