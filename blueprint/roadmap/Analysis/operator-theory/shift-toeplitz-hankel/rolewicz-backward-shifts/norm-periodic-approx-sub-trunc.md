# Distance from the periodic approximant to the truncation

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| > 1$ and $N \ge 1$, and put $r = |\mu|^{-N}$. For every $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the [periodic approximant](periodic-approx.md) and the truncation $y^{(N)} = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$ satisfy

$$
\left\| P_{\mu, N}(y) - y^{(N)} \right\| \le \frac{r}{1 - r}\, \|y\|.
$$
