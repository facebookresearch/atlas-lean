# No nonzero periodic points of $\mu B$ when $|\mu| \le 1$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $B$ be the backward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| \le 1$. If $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ satisfies $(\mu B)^n x = x$ for some $n \ge 1$, then $x = 0$.
