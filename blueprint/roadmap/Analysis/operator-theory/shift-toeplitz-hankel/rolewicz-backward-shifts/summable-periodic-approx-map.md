# Summability of the shifted periodic approximant terms

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $S$ and $B$ be the forward and backward shifts on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| > 1$ and $N \ge 1$. For every $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ with truncation $y^{(N)}$, the series $\sum_{m \ge 0} (\mu B)^N \left( (\mu^{-N})^m\, S^{Nm} y^{(N)} \right)$ is summable in $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
