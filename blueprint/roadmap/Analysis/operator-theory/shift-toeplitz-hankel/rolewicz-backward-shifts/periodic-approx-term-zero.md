# First term of the periodic approximant is the truncation

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the term of index $m = 0$ of the [periodic approximant](periodic-approx.md) series is the truncation $y^{(N)} = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$.
