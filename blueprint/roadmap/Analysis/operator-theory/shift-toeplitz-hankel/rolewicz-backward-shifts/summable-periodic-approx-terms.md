# Summability of the periodic approximant terms

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $S$ be the forward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| > 1$ and $N \ge 1$. For every $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, with $y^{(N)}$ its truncation to the first $N$ coordinates, the series $\sum_{m \ge 0} (\mu^{-N})^m\, S^{Nm} y^{(N)}$ defining the [periodic approximant](periodic-approx.md) is summable in $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
