# Vectors in the span of coordinate vectors have finite support

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. If $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ lies in the linear span of the standard basis vectors $e_i$, $i \in \mathbb{N}$, then there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ with $x_i = 0$ for every $i \ge N$.
