# Coordinates of powers of a scaled backward shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the backward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
((\mu B)^n x)_i = \mu^n\, x_{i+n}.
$$
