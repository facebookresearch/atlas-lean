# Summability of the geometric bound for periodic approximants

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| > 1$ and $N \ge 1$. For every $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, with $y^{(N)} = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$ its truncation, the series $\sum_{m \ge 0} |\mu^{-N}|^m\, \|y^{(N)}\|$ converges.
