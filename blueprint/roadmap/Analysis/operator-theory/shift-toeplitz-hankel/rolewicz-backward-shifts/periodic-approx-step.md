# Action of the scaled backward shift power on approximant terms

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $S$ and $B$ be the forward and backward shifts on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| > 1$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ with truncation $y^{(N)}$. For every $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the power $(\mu B)^N$ kills the term of index $0$ of the [periodic approximant](periodic-approx.md) and shifts every later term down by one:

$$
(\mu B)^N \left( (\mu^{-N})^m\, S^{Nm} y^{(N)} \right) = \begin{cases} 0, & m = 0, \\ (\mu^{-N})^{m-1}\, S^{N(m-1)} y^{(N)}, & m \ge 1. \end{cases}
$$
