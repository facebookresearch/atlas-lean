# Coordinate recurrence for periodic points of $\mu B$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $B$ be the backward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. If $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ satisfies $(\mu B)^n x = x$, then for all $k, m \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
x_k = \mu^{nm}\, x_{k + nm}.
$$
