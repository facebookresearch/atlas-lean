# Anti-diagonal slice does not increase the sum of squared norms

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the [anti-diagonal slice](hankel-slice-fun.md) satisfies

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{\infty} |(\eta_k x)_i|^2 \le \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} |x_i|^2,
$$

since only the first $k + 1$ coordinates survive, in reverse order.
