# Pointwise action of the anti-diagonal slice operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, the [anti-diagonal slice operator](hankel-slice.md) satisfies $(\eta_k x)_i = x_{k-i}$ if $i \le k$ and $(\eta_k x)_i = 0$ otherwise.
