# Hankel operator with absolutely summable symbol is compact

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. The [Hankel operator](hankel-operator.md) $H_a$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is compact: it is the norm limit of its finite partial sums, each of which has finite-dimensional range.
