# Summability of weighted anti-diagonal slices

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. Then the family $(a_k \eta_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ of weighted [anti-diagonal slice operators](hankel-slice.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is summable in operator norm.
