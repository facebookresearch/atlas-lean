# Anti-diagonal slice operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$. The anti-diagonal slice operator of index $k$ is the bounded operator $\eta_k$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, $(\eta_k x)_i = x_{k-i}$ for $i \le k$ and $0$ otherwise; its norm is at most $1$.
