# Hankel operator as the sum of its slices

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. The family $(a_k \eta_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ of weighted [anti-diagonal slices](hankel-slice.md) has sum the [Hankel operator](hankel-operator.md) $H_a$ in the space of bounded operators on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
