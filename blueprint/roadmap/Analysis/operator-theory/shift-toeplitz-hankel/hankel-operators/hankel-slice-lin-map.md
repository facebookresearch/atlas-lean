# Anti-diagonal slice as a linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$. The anti-diagonal slice of index $k$, as a linear map, is $x \mapsto \eta_k x$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, where $(\eta_k x)_i = x_{k-i}$ for $i \le k$ and $0$ otherwise (see [anti-diagonal slice](hankel-slice-fun.md)).
