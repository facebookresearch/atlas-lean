# Anti-diagonal slice of a sequence

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$. The anti-diagonal slice of index $k$ sends a sequence $x : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ to the sequence $\eta_k x$ with

$$
(\eta_k x)_i = \begin{cases} x_{k-i}, & i \le k, \\ 0, & i > k: \end{cases}
$$

it reverses the first $k + 1$ coordinates and kills the rest.
