# Partial sums of the Hankel slice series are compact

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the finite partial sum $\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} a_k \eta_k$ of [anti-diagonal slice operators](hankel-slice.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is compact, since it has finite-dimensional range.
