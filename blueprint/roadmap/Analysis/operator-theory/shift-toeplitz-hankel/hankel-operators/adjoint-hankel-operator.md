# Adjoint of a Hankel operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. The adjoint of the [Hankel operator](hankel-operator.md) $H_a$ is the Hankel operator of the conjugate symbol: $H_a^* = H_{\bar a}$, where $\bar a_k = \overline{a_k}$. The proof passes the adjoint through the finite partial sums and takes the limit.
