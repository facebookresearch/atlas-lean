# Hankel operator with absolutely summable symbol

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. The Hankel operator with symbol $a$ is the bounded operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$

$$
H_a = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} a_k \eta_k,
$$

a series of [anti-diagonal slice operators](hankel-slice.md) converging in operator norm.
