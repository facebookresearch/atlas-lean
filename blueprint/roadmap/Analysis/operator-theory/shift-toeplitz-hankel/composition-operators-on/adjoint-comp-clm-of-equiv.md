# Adjoint of a composition operator with a permutation symbol

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ be a bijection. The adjoint of the [composition operator](comp-clm.md) $C_e$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is the composition operator of the inverse bijection: $C_e^* = C_{e^{-1}}$.
