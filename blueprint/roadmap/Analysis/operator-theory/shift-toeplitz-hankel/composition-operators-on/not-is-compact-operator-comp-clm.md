# Composition operators on $\ell^2$ are never compact

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the [composition operator](comp-clm.md) $C_\psi$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ is not compact: the range of $\psi$ is infinite, and the images $C_\psi e_m$ of the basis vectors indexed by the range are pairwise orthogonal with norm at least $1$, so they have no convergent subsequence.
