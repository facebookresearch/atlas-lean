# Operator norm of a composition operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. The [composition operator](comp-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ has operator norm

$$
\|C_\psi\| = \sqrt{s_\psi}, \qquad s_\psi = \sup_{m \in \mathbb{N}} |\psi^{-1}(m)|,
$$

the square root of the largest fiber cardinality.
