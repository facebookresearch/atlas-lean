# Squared-norm sum bound for a precomposed sequence

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$, with [fiber supremum](fiber-sup.md) $s_\psi = \sup_m |\psi^{-1}(m)|$. For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$,

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} |x_{\psi(n)}|^2 \le s_\psi \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} |x_m|^2.
$$
