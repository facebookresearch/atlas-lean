# Precomposition preserves square-summability

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the sequence $x \circ \psi = (x_{\psi(n)})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ belongs to $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
