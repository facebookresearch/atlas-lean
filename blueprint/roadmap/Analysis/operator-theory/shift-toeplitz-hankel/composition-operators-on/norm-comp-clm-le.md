# Operator-norm bound for the composition operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$, with [fiber supremum](fiber-sup.md) $s_\psi$. The [composition operator](comp-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ satisfies $\|C_\psi\| \le \sqrt{s_\psi}$.
