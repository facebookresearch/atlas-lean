# Composition operator of a pointwise identity symbol

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. If $\psi(n) = n$ for every $n$, then the [composition operator](comp-clm.md) $C_\psi$ is the identity operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
