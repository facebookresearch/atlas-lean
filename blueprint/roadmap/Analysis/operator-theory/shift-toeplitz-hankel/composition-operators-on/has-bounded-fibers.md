# Uniformly bounded fibers of a map on the naturals

A map $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ has fibers bounded by $M \in \mathbb{N}$ if, for every $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the fiber $\psi^{-1}(m)$ is finite and has at most $M$ elements: every value is taken at most $M$ times. This is the boundedness hypothesis for the [composition operator](comp-clm.md) of $\psi$.
