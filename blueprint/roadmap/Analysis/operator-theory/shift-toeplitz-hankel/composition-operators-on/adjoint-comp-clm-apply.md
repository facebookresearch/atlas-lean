# Adjoint of a composition operator is the fiber-sum operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. The adjoint of the [composition operator](comp-clm.md) $C_\psi$ is the fiber-sum operator: for every $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
(C_\psi^* y)_m = \sum_{n \in \psi^{-1}(m)} y_n.
$$
