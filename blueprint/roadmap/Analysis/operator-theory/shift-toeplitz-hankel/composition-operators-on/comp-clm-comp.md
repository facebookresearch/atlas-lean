# Composition of composition operators

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\varphi, \psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by $M_\varphi$ and $M_\psi$. The [composition operators](comp-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ compose in the reversed order: $C_\varphi C_\psi = C_{\psi \circ \varphi}$.
