# Bounded composition operator on $\ell^2$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. The composition operator of $\psi$ is the bounded operator $C_\psi$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ given by $C_\psi x = x \circ \psi$; its norm is at most $\sqrt{s_\psi}$, where $s_\psi = \sup_m |\psi^{-1}(m)|$ is the [fiber supremum](fiber-sup.md).
