# Pointwise action of the bounded composition operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. The [composition operator](comp-clm.md) satisfies $(C_\psi x)_i = x_{\psi(i)}$ for every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$.
