# Composition operator is surjective iff its symbol is injective

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. The [composition operator](comp-clm.md) $C_\psi$ is surjective if and only if $\psi$ is injective. If $\psi(a) = \psi(b)$ with $a \ne b$, every vector in the range has equal coordinates of indices $a$ and $b$, so the basis vector $e_a$ is not in the range; if $\psi$ is injective, $C_\psi^* y$, the sequence of fiber sums of $y$, is a preimage of $y$.
