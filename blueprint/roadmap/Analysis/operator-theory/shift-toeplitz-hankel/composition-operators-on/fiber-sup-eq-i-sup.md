# Fiber supremum as a supremum of cardinalities

Let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. The [fiber supremum](fiber-sup.md) of $\psi$ is the supremum of the cardinalities of the fibers, $s_\psi = \sup_{m} |\psi^{-1}(m)|$; in particular it does not depend on the bound $M$.
