# Norm of the composition operator on a basis vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $(e_m)_{m \in \mathbb{N}}$ be the standard basis vectors of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and let $\psi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ have [fibers bounded](has-bounded-fibers.md) by some $M \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the [composition operator](comp-clm.md) satisfies $\|C_\psi e_m\| = \sqrt{|\psi^{-1}(m)|}$.
