# Diagonal slices are powers of the forward or backward shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $S$ and $B$ be the unilateral shift and the backward shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, the [diagonal slice](toeplitz-slice.md) $\sigma_k$ equals $S^k$ if $k \ge 0$ and $B^{-k}$ if $k < 0$.
