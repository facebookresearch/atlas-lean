# Operator norm of a Toeplitz operator with delta symbol

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $m \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $c \in \mathbb{K}$, the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) of the [delta symbol](toeplitz-delta.md) $\delta_{m,c}$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ has operator norm $|c|$.
