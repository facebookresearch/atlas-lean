# Toeplitz operator as the sum of its diagonal slices

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} |a_k| < \infty$. The family $(a_k \sigma_k)_{k \in \mathbb{Z}}$ of weighted [diagonal slices](toeplitz-slice.md) has sum the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) $T_a$ in the space of bounded operators on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
