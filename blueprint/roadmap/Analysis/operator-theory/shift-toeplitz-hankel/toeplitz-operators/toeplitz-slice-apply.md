# Pointwise action of the diagonal slice operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, the [diagonal slice operator](toeplitz-slice.md) satisfies $(\sigma_k x)_i = x_{i-k}$ if $k \le i$ and $(\sigma_k x)_i = 0$ otherwise.
