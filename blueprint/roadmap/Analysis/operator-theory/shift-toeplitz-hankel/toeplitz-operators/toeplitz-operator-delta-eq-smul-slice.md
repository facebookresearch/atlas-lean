# Toeplitz operator of a delta symbol is a scaled slice

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $m \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $c \in \mathbb{K}$, the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) of the [delta symbol](toeplitz-delta.md) $\delta_{m,c}$ is $c\, \sigma_m$, where $\sigma_m$ is the [diagonal slice](toeplitz-slice.md) of index $m$.
