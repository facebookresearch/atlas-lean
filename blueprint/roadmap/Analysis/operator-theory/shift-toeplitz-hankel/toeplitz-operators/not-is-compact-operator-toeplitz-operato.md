# Toeplitz operators with nonzero symbol are not compact

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. If $a_{k_0} \ne 0$ for some $k_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$, then the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) $T_a$ is not compact: the images $T_a e_j$ of the standard basis vectors converge coordinatewise to $0$ but each keeps a coordinate equal to $a_{k_0}$ once $j + k_0 \ge 0$.
