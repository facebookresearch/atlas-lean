# Toeplitz operator with absolutely summable symbol

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} |a_k| < \infty$. The Toeplitz operator with symbol $a$ is the bounded operator on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$

$$
T_a = \sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} a_k \sigma_k,
$$

a series of [diagonal slices](toeplitz-slice.md) converging in operator norm.
