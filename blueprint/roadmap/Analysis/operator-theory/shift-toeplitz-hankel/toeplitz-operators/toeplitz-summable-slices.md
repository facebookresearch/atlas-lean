# Summability of weighted diagonal slices

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} |a_k| < \infty$. Then the family $(a_k \sigma_k)_{k \in \mathbb{Z}}$ of bounded operators on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, where $\sigma_k$ is the [diagonal slice](toeplitz-slice.md), is summable in operator norm.
