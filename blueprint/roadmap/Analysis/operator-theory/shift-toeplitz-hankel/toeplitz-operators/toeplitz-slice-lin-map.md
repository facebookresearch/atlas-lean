# Diagonal slice as a linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. The diagonal slice of index $k$, as a linear map, is $x \mapsto \sigma_k x$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, where $(\sigma_k x)_i = x_{i-k}$ for $k \le i$ and $0$ otherwise (see [diagonal slice](toeplitz-slice-fun.md)).
