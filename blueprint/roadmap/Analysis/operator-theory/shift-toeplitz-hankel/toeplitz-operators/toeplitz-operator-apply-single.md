# Toeplitz operator applied to a standard basis vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $(e_j)_{j \in \mathbb{N}}$ be the standard basis vectors of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. For all $i, j \in \mathbb{N}$, the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) satisfies $(T_a e_j)_i = a_{i-j}$.
