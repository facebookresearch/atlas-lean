# Adjoint of a Toeplitz operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k} |a_k| < \infty$. The adjoint of the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) $T_a$ is the Toeplitz operator of the [adjoint symbol](toeplitz-adjoint-symbol.md): $T_a^* = T_{a^*}$, where $a^*_k = \overline{a_{-k}}$.
