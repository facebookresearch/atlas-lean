# Pointwise action of a Toeplitz operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $a : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfy $\sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} |a_k| < \infty$. For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, the [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) satisfies

$$
(T_a x)_i = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} a_{i-j}\, x_j.
$$
