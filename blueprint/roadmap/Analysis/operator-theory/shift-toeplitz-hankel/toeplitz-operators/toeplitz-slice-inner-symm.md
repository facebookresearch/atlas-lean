# Inner product symmetry of opposite diagonal slices

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $x, y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the [diagonal slices](toeplitz-slice.md) satisfy

$$
\langle \sigma_k x, y \rangle = \langle x, \sigma_{-k} y \rangle.
$$
