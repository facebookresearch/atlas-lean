# Diagonal slice as a bounded operator

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. The diagonal slice of index $k$ is the bounded operator $\sigma_k$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, $(\sigma_k x)_i = x_{i-k}$ for $k \le i$ and $0$ otherwise; its norm is at most $1$.
