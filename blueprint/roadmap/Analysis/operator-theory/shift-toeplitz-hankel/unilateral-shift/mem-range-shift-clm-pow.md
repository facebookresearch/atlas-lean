# Range of a power of the unilateral shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a vector $y \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ lies in the range of $S^n$ if and only if $y_i = 0$ for every $i < n$.
