# Quadratic form of the backward shift as a series

Let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, whose inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument. For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$,

$$
\langle Bx, x \rangle = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \overline{x_{n+1}}\, x_n.
$$
