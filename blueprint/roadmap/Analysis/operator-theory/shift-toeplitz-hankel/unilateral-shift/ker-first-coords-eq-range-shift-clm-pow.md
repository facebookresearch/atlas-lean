# Kernel of the first-coordinates map is the range of the shift power

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The kernel of the [first-coordinates map](first-coords.md) $x \mapsto (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ equals the range of $S^n$: both consist of the sequences whose first $n$ coordinates vanish.
