# One minus a small multiple of the backward shift is invertible

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. If $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $|\mu| < 1$, then $I - \mu B$ is invertible, by the Neumann series, since $\|\mu B\| \le |\mu| < 1$.
