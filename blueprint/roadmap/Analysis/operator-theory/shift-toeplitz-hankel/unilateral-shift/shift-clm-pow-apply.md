# Pointwise action of a power of the unilateral shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, the power $S^n$ inserts $n$ leading zeroes:

$$
(S^n x)_i = \begin{cases} 0, & i < n, \\ x_{i-n}, & i \ge n. \end{cases}
$$
