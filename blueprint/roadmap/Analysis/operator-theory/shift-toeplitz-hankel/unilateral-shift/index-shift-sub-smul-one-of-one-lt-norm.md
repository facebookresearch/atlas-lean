# Shifted unilateral shift has index zero outside the unit disk

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. If $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $|\mu| > 1$, then $S - \mu I$ has Fredholm index $0$, since it is invertible.
