# Pointwise action of the extension-by-zero map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $v \in \mathbb{K}^n$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, the coordinate of index $j$ of the image of $v$ under the [extension-by-zero map](ext-zero.md) is the term of index $j$ of the [extension of $v$ by zeroes](ext-zero-fun.md): $v_j$ for $j < n$ and $0$ for $j \ge n$.
