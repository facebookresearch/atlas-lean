# Fredholm index of a backward shift power is $n$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the Fredholm index of $B^n$ is $n$: $B^n$ is surjective and its kernel has dimension $n$.
