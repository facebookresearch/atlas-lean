# Powers of the unilateral shift as iterates of the shift map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the sequence $S^n x$ is the $n$-fold iterate of the [forward shift](shift-fun.md) of sequences applied to $x$.
