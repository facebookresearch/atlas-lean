# Powers of the backward shift are Fredholm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $S$ and $B$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) and the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the operator $B^n$ is Fredholm: $S^n$ inverts it modulo operators of finite rank.
