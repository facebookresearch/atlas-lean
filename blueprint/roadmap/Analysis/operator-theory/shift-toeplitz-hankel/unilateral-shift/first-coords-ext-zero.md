# First coordinates of a zero extension recover the tuple

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $v \in \mathbb{K}^n$, the first $n$ coordinates of the extension of $v$ by zeroes are $v$ itself: the [first-coordinates map](first-coords.md) composed with the [extension-by-zero map](ext-zero.md) is the identity of $\mathbb{K}^n$.
