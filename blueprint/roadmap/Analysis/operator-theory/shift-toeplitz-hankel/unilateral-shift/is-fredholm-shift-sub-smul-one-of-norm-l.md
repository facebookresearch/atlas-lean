# Shifted unilateral shift is Fredholm inside the unit disk

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $S$ and $B$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) and the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. If $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $|\mu| < 1$, then $S - \mu I$ is a Fredholm operator: it is the Fredholm operator $S(I - \mu B)$ plus the finite-rank operator $\mu(SB - I)$.
