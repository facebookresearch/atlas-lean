# Backward shift quadratic form at a geometric vector

Let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$. If $u \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfies $|u| < 1$ and $g_u = \left(\sqrt{1 - |u|^2}\, u^n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ is the [geometric vector](geom.md) attached to $u$, then $\langle B g_u, g_u \rangle = \bar u$, the inner product being conjugate-linear in its first argument.
