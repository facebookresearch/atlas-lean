# Shift composed with backward shift is Fredholm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $S$ and $B$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) and the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. The operator $SB$ is Fredholm, as the composite of two Fredholm operators.
