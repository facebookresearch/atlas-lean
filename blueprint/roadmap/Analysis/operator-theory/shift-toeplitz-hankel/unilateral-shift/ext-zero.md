# Linear extension-by-zero map into $\ell^2$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The extension-by-zero map is the linear map $\mathbb{K}^n \to \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ sending $v = (v_0, \dots, v_{n-1})$ to its [extension by zeroes](ext-zero-fun.md) $(v_0, \dots, v_{n-1}, 0, 0, \dots)$.
