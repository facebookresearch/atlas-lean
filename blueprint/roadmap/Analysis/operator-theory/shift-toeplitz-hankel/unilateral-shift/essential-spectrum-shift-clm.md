# Essential spectrum of the unilateral shift is the unit circle

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. The [essential spectrum](essential-spectrum.md) of $S$ is the unit sphere $\{\mu \in \mathbb{K} : |\mu| = 1\}$ of $\mathbb{K}$ (the unit circle when $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{C}$): $S - \mu I$ is a Fredholm operator exactly when $|\mu| \ne 1$.
