# Squared norm of the truncated geometric vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| = 1$. For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the [truncated geometric vector](approx-vec.md) $v_N = (1, \bar\mu, \dots, \bar\mu^N, 0, 0, \dots)$ satisfies $\|v_N\|^2 = N + 1$.
