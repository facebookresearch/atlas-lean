# Backward shift's numerical range lies in the open unit disc

Let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$. Every point $w$ of the numerical range $W(B) = \{\langle Bx, x \rangle : \|x\| = 1\}$ satisfies $|w| < 1$, by the [strict bound](sum-norm-mul-succ-lt-one.md) $\sum_n |x_n|\,|x_{n+1}| < 1$ for unit vectors $x$.
