# Kernel of the backward shift power as a space of finite tuples

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The map $x \mapsto (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ is a linear isomorphism from the kernel of $B^n$ onto $\mathbb{K}^n$, whose inverse is the extension by zeroes.
