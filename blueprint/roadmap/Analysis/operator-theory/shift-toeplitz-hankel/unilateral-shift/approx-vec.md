# Truncated geometric vector in $\ell^2$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. The truncated geometric vector $v_N$ is the [truncated geometric sequence](approx-fun.md) $(1, \bar\mu, \dots, \bar\mu^N, 0, 0, \dots)$, regarded as an element of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
