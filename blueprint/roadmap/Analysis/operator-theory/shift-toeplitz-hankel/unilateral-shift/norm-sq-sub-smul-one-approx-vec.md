# Squared norm of the shifted shift on the truncated geometric vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ with $|\mu| = 1$. For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the [truncated geometric vector](approx-vec.md) $v_N = (1, \bar\mu, \dots, \bar\mu^N, 0, 0, \dots)$ satisfies $\|(S - \mu I)v_N\|^2 = 2$: the coordinates of $(S - \mu I)v_N$ are $-\mu$ at index $0$, $\bar\mu^N$ at index $N + 1$, and $0$ elsewhere.
