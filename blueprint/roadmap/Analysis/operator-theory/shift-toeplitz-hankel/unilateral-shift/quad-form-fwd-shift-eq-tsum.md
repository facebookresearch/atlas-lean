# Quadratic form of the forward shift as a series

Let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$, whose inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument. For every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$,

$$
\langle Sx, x \rangle = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \overline{x_n}\, x_{n+1}.
$$
