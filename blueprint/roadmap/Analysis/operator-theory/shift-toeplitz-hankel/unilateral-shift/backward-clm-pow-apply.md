# Pointwise action of a power of the backward shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, the power $B^n$ drops the first $n$ coordinates: $(B^n x)_i = x_{i+n}$.
