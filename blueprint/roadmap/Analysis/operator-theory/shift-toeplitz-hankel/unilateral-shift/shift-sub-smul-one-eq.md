# Factorization of the shifted unilateral shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $S$ and $B$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) and the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$,

$$
S - \mu I = S(I - \mu B) + \mu(SB - I).
$$
