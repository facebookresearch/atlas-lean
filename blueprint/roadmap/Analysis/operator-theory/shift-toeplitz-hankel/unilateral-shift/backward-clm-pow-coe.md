# Powers of the backward shift as iterates of the backward map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the sequence $B^n x$ is the $n$-fold iterate of the [backward shift](backward-fun.md) of sequences applied to $x$.
