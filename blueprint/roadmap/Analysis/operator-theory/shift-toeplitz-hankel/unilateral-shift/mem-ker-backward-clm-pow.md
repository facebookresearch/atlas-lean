# Kernel of a power of the backward shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, $B^n x = 0$ if and only if $x_{i+n} = 0$ for every $i \in \mathbb{N}$, that is, all coordinates of $x$ from index $n$ onwards vanish.
