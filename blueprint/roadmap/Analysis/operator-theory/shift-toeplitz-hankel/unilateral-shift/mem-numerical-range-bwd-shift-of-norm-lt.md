# Open unit disc lies in the backward shift's numerical range

Let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$. Every $w \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|w| < 1$ lies in the numerical range $W(B) = \{\langle Bx, x \rangle : \|x\| = 1\}$, witnessed by the [geometric vector](geom.md) attached to $\bar w$.
