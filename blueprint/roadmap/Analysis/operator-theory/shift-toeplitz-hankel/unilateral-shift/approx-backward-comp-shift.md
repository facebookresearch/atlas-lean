# Backward shift power inverts the shift power modulo finite rank

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $S$ and $B$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) and the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the linear maps $B^n S^n$ and $I$ differ by a linear map of finite rank; in fact $B^n S^n = I$.
