# Shifted unilateral shift has trivial kernel

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, the operator $S - \mu I$ is injective: the coordinate equations $-\mu x_0 = 0$ and $x_{j-1} - \mu x_j = 0$ for $j \ge 1$ force every coordinate of a vector in its kernel to vanish.
