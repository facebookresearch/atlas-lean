# First coordinate of the shifted unilateral shift on the truncated geometric vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the coordinate of index $0$ of $(S - \mu I)v_N$, where $v_N = (1, \bar\mu, \dots, \bar\mu^N, 0, 0, \dots)$ is the [truncated geometric vector](approx-vec.md), equals $-\mu$.
