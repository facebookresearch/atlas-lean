# Kernel vectors of a backward shift power are zero-extended tuples

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. If $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ lies in the kernel of $B^n$, then extending its first $n$ coordinates $(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ by zeroes gives back $x$.
