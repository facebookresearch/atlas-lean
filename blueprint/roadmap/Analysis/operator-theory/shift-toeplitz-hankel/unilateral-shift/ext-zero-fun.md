# Extension of a finite tuple by zeroes

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $v = (v_0, \dots, v_{n-1}) \in \mathbb{K}^n$. The extension of $v$ by zeroes is the sequence $(v_0, \dots, v_{n-1}, 0, 0, \dots)$, whose term of index $j$ is $v_j$ for $j < n$ and $0$ for $j \ge n$.
