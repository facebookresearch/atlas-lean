# Open unit disc lies in the forward shift's numerical range

Let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{C})$. Every $u \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|u| < 1$ lies in the numerical range $W(S) = \{\langle Sx, x \rangle : \|x\| = 1\}$, witnessed by the [geometric vector](geom.md) attached to $u$.
