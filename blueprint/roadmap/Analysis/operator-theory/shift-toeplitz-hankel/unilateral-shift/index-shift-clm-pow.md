# Fredholm index of a unilateral shift power is $-n$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the Fredholm index of $S^n$ is $-n$: $S^n$ is injective and its cokernel has dimension $n$.
