# Membership in the essential spectrum of the unilateral shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. A scalar $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ lies in the [essential spectrum](essential-spectrum.md) of $S$ if and only if $|\mu| = 1$.
