# Pointwise action of one minus a scaled backward shift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $B$ be the [backward shift](backward-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, every $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and every $j \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$
((I - \mu B)x)_j = x_j - \mu x_{j+1}.
$$
