# Shifted unilateral shift is not Fredholm on the unit circle

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $S$ be the [unilateral shift](shift-clm.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$. If $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ satisfies $|\mu| = 1$, then $S - \mu I$ is not a Fredholm operator. Indeed, $S - \mu I$ is injective, so a closed range would make it bounded below, whereas the truncated geometric vectors satisfy $\|v_N\|^2 = N + 1$ and $\|(S - \mu I)v_N\|^2 = 2$ for every $N$.
