# Truncated geometric sequence is square-summable

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the [truncated geometric sequence](approx-fun.md) $(1, \bar\mu, \dots, \bar\mu^N, 0, 0, \dots)$ is square-summable, since it has finite support.
