# Failure of the separable quotient assertion under the continuum hypothesis

Assume $|\mathbb R|=\aleph_1$. For each of the scalar fields $\mathbb R$ and $\mathbb C$, the assertion that every infinite-dimensional Banach space has a bounded linear surjection onto an infinite-dimensional separable Banach space is false. In each instance the ambient and quotient space range over the same arbitrary universe. The conclusion does not establish the positive consistency direction or remove the continuum hypothesis.

## Depends on

- Cardinal equality, Banach spaces over the two indicated fields and bounded linear surjections.

## Proof depends on

- The continuum-hypothesis construction excluding every bounded surjection onto a separable infinite-dimensional Banach space.

## Expansion

Extract the precise conditional Banach space construction and its obstruction to each scalar-field quotient. Verify universe bounds and separate any real-to-complex transfer from the conditional existence step.
