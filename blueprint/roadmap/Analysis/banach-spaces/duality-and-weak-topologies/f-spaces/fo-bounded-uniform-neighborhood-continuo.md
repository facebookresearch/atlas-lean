# Mazur and Orlicz's Problem 26 has a negative answer

The claim of Scottish Book Problem 26
is false: there are a real vector space $E$ with a topology, of type $\{F_o\}$, and a sequence
$(F_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of continuous linear functionals $E \to \mathbb{R}$ that converges to $0$
uniformly on every bounded subset of $E$ but does not converge to $0$ uniformly on any open set
containing $0$.

## Sources

- S. Mazur and W. Orlicz, Problem 26 of the Scottish Book (prize: one small beer); S. M. Ulam, *The Scottish Book*, Los Alamos report LA-6832 (1977), Problem 26, p. 8, which records the answer "Ad 26. The answer is negative." (M. Eidelheit, June 4, 1938).
