# Hahn–Banach extension of a linear functional dominated by a sublinear functional

Let $X$ be a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ and let $p \colon X \to \mathbb{R}$ be a sublinear functional: $p(x + y) \le p(x) + p(y)$ for all $x, y \in X$, and $p(tx) = t\,p(x)$ for all $x \in X$ and all real $t > 0$. Taking $t = 2$ and $x = 0$ gives $p(0) = 0$, so the identity $p(tx) = t\,p(x)$ also holds for $t = 0$. Let $M$ be a linear subspace of $X$, and let $f \colon M \to \mathbb{R}$ be a linear functional with $f(x) \le p(x)$ for all $x \in M$. Then there is a linear functional $F \colon X \to \mathbb{R}$ with $F|_M = f$ and $F(x) \le p(x)$ for all $x \in X$.
