# A Banach space with a countable Hamel basis is finite-dimensional

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. If a Banach space $E$ over $\mathbb{K}$ has a Hamel basis indexed by a countable set, then $E$ is finite-dimensional. Completeness is essential: the proof is a Baire category argument.
