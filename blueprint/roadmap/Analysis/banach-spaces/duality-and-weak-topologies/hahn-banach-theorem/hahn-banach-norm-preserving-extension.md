# Hahn–Banach extension with the same norm

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a seminormed space over $\mathbb{K}$
and let $p \subseteq E$ be a linear subspace, with the seminorm of $E$. Every continuous linear
functional $f \colon p \to \mathbb{K}$ extends to a continuous linear functional $g \colon E \to
\mathbb{K}$ with $g(x) = f(x)$ for all $x \in p$ and $\lVert g \rVert = \lVert f \rVert$. The
subspace $p$ need not be closed.

## Sources

- W. Rudin, *Real and Complex Analysis*, 3rd ed., McGraw-Hill, 1987: Theorem 5.16.
