# Weakly open sets contain lines through each point

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$. If $U \subseteq E$ is weakly open and $x \in U$, then there is $v \ne 0$ in $E$ such that $x + t v \in U$ for every $t \in \mathbb{K}$: take for $v$ a common kernel vector of the finitely many functionals that define a basic weak neighbourhood of $x$ inside $U$.
