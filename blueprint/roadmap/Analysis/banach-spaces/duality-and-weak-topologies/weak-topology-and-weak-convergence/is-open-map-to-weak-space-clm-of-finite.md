# Weak topology equals norm topology in finite dimensions

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a finite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. The identity map from $E$ with its norm topology to $E$ with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$ is an open map: the weak topology agrees with the norm topology.
