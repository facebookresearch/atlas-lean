# Finitely many functionals have a common nonzero kernel vector

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. For every finite set $S$ of bounded linear functionals on $E$ there is $v \ne 0$ in $E$ with $f(v) = 0$ for every $f \in S$.
