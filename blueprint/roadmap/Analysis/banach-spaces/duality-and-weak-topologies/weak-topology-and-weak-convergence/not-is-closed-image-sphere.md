# Unit sphere is not weakly closed in infinite dimensions

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$. The unit sphere $\{x \in E : \|x\| = 1\}$ is not closed in the weak topology: its weak closure is the whole closed unit ball.
