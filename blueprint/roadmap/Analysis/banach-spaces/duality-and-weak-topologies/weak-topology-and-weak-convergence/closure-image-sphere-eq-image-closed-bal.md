# Weak closure of the unit sphere is the closed unit ball

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$. The closure of the unit sphere $\{x \in E : \|x\| = 1\}$ in the weak topology is the closed unit ball $\{x \in E : \|x\| \le 1\}$.
