# Weak topology is strictly coarser in infinite dimensions

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$. The identity map from $E$ with its norm topology to $E$ with its weak topology is not an open map: the open unit ball is norm-open but not weakly open.
