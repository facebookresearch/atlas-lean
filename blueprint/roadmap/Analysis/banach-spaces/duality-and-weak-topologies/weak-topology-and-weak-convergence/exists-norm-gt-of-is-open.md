# Weakly open sets are unbounded in infinite dimensions

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$. Every nonempty weakly open set $U \subseteq E$ is unbounded in norm: for every $R \in \mathbb{R}$ there is $y \in U$ with $\|y\| > R$.
