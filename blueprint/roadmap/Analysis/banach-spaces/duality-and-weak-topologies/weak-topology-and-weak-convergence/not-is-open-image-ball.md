# Open unit ball is not weakly open in infinite dimensions

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an infinite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, with its weak topology $\sigma(E, E^*)$. The open unit ball $\{x \in E : \|x\| < 1\}$ is not open in the weak topology.
