# Duality pairing has operator norm at most one

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\iota$ be an index set and let $p, q \in [1, \infty]$ be conjugate exponents, $1/p + 1/q = 1$. The [duality pairing](lp-dual-pairing.md) $\ell^q(\iota, \mathbb{K}) \to \ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})^*$ has operator norm at most $1$.
