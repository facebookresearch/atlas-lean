# Canonical duality pairing between $\ell^q$ and $\ell^p$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\iota$ be an index set and let $p, q \in [1, \infty]$ be conjugate exponents, $1/p + 1/q = 1$. The canonical duality pairing is the bounded linear map from $\ell^q(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ to the dual of $\ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ sending $a$ to the functional $x \mapsto \sum_{i \in \iota} a_i x_i$.
