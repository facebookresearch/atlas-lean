# Dual of $\ell^p$ is isometric to $\ell^q$

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\iota$ be an index set and let $p, q \in [1, \infty]$ be conjugate exponents, $1/p + 1/q = 1$. If $p < \infty$, the [duality pairing](lp-dual-pairing.md) is an isometric isomorphism from $\ell^q(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ onto the dual of $\ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})$.
