# Duality pairing as a sum of coordinatewise products

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\iota$ be an index set and let $p, q \in [1, \infty]$ be conjugate exponents, $1/p + 1/q = 1$. For every $a \in \ell^q(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ and $x \in \ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})$, the [duality pairing](lp-dual-pairing.md) of $a$ evaluated at $x$ is $\sum_{i \in \iota} a_i x_i$.
