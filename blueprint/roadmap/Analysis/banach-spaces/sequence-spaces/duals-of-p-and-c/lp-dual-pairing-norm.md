# Duality pairing is norm-preserving

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $\iota$ be an index set and let $p, q \in [1, \infty]$ be conjugate exponents, $1/p + 1/q = 1$. If $p < \infty$, then for every $a \in \ell^q(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ the functional $x \mapsto \sum_i a_i x_i$ on $\ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})$ has norm $\|a\|_q$.
