# Truncation equals the Schauder basis projection

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $1 \le p < \infty$. For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, the [truncation](lp-trunc.md) $x^{(N)}$ is the image of $x$ under the $N$-th canonical projection $P_N x = \sum_{i<N} x_i e_i$ of the standard Schauder basis $(e_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of $\ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$.
