# Pointwise action of the finite $\ell^p$ lift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $p \in [0, \infty]$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $y : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, the coordinate of index $j$ of the [lift](lp-lift.md) of $y$ is $y_j$ if $j < N$ and $0$ otherwise.
