# Tail of a single $\ell^p$ vector can be made small

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $1 \le p < \infty$. For every $x \in \ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\|x - x^{(N)}\|_p < \varepsilon$, where $x^{(N)}$ is the [truncation](lp-trunc.md).
