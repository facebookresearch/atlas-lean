# Pointwise action of $\ell^p$ truncation

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $p \in [0, \infty]$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $x \in \ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, the [truncation](lp-trunc.md) satisfies $x^{(N)}_j = x_j$ if $j < N$ and $x^{(N)}_j = 0$ otherwise.
