# Norm bound for the finite $\ell^p$ lift

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $1 \le p < \infty$. For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $y : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$, the [lift](lp-lift.md) $(y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)$ satisfies

$$
\big\|(y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, \dots)\big\|_p \le N^{1/p} \max_{i < N} |y_i|,
$$

where the maximum is $0$ when $N = 0$.
