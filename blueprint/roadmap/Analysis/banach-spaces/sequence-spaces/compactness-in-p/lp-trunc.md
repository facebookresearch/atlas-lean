# Truncation of an $\ell^p$ sequence, zeroing all but its first coordinates

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $p \in [0, \infty]$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. The truncation of $x \in \ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ to its first $N$ coordinates is the [lift](lp-lift.md) $x^{(N)} = (x_0, \dots, x_{N-1}, 0, 0, \dots)$; it is defined for every exponent, including $p = \infty$. For finite $p \ge 1$ it is the [canonical Schauder basis projection](lp-trunc-eq-lp-schauder-basis-proj.md).
