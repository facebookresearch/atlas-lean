# Lift of a coordinate function to a truncated $\ell^p$ sequence

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $p \in [0, \infty]$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. The lift of a sequence $y : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{K}$ is the finitely supported element

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{N-1} y_i\, e_i = (y_0, \dots, y_{N-1}, 0, 0, \dots)
$$

of $\ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$, where $e_i$ is the sequence equal to $1$ at $i$ and $0$ elsewhere: only the first $N$ coordinates of $y$ are kept.
