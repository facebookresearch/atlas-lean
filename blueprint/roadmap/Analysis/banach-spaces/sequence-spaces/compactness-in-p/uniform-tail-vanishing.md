# Uniform vanishing of tails

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $1 \le p \le \infty$. The tails of a set $K \subseteq \ell^p(\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{K})$ vanish uniformly if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\|x - x^{(N)}\|_p < \varepsilon$ for every $x \in K$, where $x^{(N)}$ is the [truncation](lp-trunc.md) to the first $N$ coordinates.
