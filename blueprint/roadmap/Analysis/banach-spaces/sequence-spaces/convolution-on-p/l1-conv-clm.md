# Convolution action of $\ell^1$ on $\ell^p$ as a bounded bilinear map

Let $1 \le p \le \infty$. The convolution action of $\ell^1$ on $\ell^p$ is the bounded linear map from $\ell^1(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ to the space of bounded operators on $\ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ that sends $f$ to the [operator of convolution by $f$](l1-conv-clmof.md), $g \mapsto f * g$. Its norm is at most $1$.
