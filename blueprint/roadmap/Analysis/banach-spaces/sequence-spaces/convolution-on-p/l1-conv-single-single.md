# Convolution of coordinate vectors

Let $1 \le p \le \infty$ and, for $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, let $e_k$ be the sequence equal to $1$ at $k$ and $0$ elsewhere. For all $i, j \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{C}$, the [convolution](l1-conv.md) of $a e_i \in \ell^1(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ with $b e_j \in \ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ is

$$
(a e_i) * (b e_j) = ab\, e_{i+j}:
$$

positions add and coefficients multiply.
