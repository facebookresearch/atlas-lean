# Summability of the Banach-space series for $\ell^1$ convolution

Let $1 \le p \le \infty$, $f \in \ell^1(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ and $g \in \ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$. The family $(f(m)\, \tau_m g)_{m \in \mathbb{Z}}$ of elements of $\ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$, where $\tau_m g$ is the [translation](shift-lp.md) $n \mapsto g(n - m)$, is summable in $\ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$.
