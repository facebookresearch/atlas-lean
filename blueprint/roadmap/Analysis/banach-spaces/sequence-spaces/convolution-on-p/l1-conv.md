# Convolution of an $\ell^1$ sequence with an $\ell^p$ sequence

Let $1 \le p \le \infty$. The convolution of $f \in \ell^1(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ with $g \in \ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$ is the element of $\ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$

$$
f * g = \sum_{m \in \mathbb{Z}} f(m)\, \tau_m g,
$$

the sum being taken in the Banach space $\ell^p(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{C})$, where $\tau_m g$ is the [translation](shift-lp.md) $n \mapsto g(n - m)$.
