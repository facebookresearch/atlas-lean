# Unique nearest point from a minimizing sequence in finite dimensions

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional strictly convex real normed space. Let $K \subseteq E$ be a closed convex set, let $x \in E$, and let $(u_n)$ be a sequence in $K$ with $\|u_n - x\| \to d(x, K)$, the distance from $x$ to $K$. Then there is exactly one $y \in K$ with $\|y - x\| = d(x, K)$. The uniqueness clause concerns every point of $K$ attaining the distance, not only limits of the minimizing sequence.
