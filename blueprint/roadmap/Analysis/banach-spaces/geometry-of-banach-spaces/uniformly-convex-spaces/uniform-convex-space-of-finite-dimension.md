# Strict convexity implies uniform convexity in finite dimensions

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space. If $E$ is strictly convex, then $E$ is uniformly convex. The proof maximizes $\|x + y\|$ over the compact set of pairs of unit vectors at distance at least $\varepsilon$; strict convexity makes the maximum less than $2$, and the gap is the required $\delta$.
