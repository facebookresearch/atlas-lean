# Convergence of minimizing sequences in uniformly convex spaces

Let $E$ be a uniformly convex real Banach space. Let $K \subseteq E$ be a closed convex set, let $x \in E$, and let $(u_n)$ be a sequence in $K$ with $\|u_n - x\| \to d(x, K)$, the distance from $x$ to $K$. Then there is $y \in K$ with $\|y - x\| = d(x, K)$ and $u_n \to y$. If the sequence were not Cauchy, two far-apart almost-minimizing terms would have normalized displacements far apart on the unit sphere, and uniform convexity would make their midpoint, which lies in $K$, closer to $x$ than $d(x, K)$.
