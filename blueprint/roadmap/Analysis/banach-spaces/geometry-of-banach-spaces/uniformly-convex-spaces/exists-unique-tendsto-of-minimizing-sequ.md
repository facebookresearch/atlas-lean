# Minimizing sequences converge to the unique nearest point

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional strictly convex real normed space. Let $K \subseteq E$ be a closed convex set, let $x \in E$, and let $(u_n)$ be a sequence in $K$ with $\|u_n - x\| \to d(x, K)$, the distance from $x$ to $K$. Then there is exactly one $y \in K$ with $\|y - x\| = d(x, K)$, and $u_n \to y$. This combines the uniqueness of the nearest point with the convergence of minimizing sequences.
