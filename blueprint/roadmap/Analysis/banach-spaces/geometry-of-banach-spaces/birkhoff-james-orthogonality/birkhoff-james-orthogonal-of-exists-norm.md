# Annihilating norming functional implies Birkhoff–James orthogonality

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. Let $x, y \in E$. If there is a bounded linear functional $f$ on $E$ with $\|f\| = 1$, $f(x) = \|x\|$ and $f(y) = 0$, then $x$ is [Birkhoff–James orthogonal](birkhoff-james-orthogonal.md) to $y$.
