# Birkhoff–James orthogonality gives an annihilating norming functional

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $x \ne 0$ is [Birkhoff–James orthogonal](birkhoff-james-orthogonal.md) to $y$, then there is a bounded linear functional $f$ on $E$ with $\|f\| = 1$, $f(x) = \|x\|$ and $f(y) = 0$. Indeed, orthogonality makes the distance from $x$ to the line $\mathbb{K} y$ equal to $\|x\|$, and the Hahn–Banach distance formula supplies a norming functional of $x$ vanishing on that line.
