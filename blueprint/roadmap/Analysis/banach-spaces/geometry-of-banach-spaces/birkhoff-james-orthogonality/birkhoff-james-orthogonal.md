# Birkhoff–James orthogonality

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. A vector $x \in E$ is Birkhoff–James orthogonal to $y \in E$ if $\|x\| \le \|x + c y\|$ for every $c \in \mathbb{K}$, that is, if $x$ is a shortest vector on the affine line $x + \mathbb{K} y$.
