# Norming-functional characterization of Birkhoff–James orthogonality

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. For $x \ne 0$ and $y$ in $E$, $x$ is [Birkhoff–James orthogonal](birkhoff-james-orthogonal.md) to $y$ if and only if there is a bounded linear functional $f$ on $E$ with $\|f\| = 1$, $f(x) = \|x\|$ and $f(y) = 0$.
