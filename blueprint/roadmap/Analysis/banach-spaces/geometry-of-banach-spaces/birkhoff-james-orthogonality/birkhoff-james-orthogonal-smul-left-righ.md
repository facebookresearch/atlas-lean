# Homogeneity of Birkhoff–James orthogonality

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $x$ is [Birkhoff–James orthogonal](birkhoff-james-orthogonal.md) to $y$, then $a x$ is Birkhoff–James orthogonal to $b y$ for all $a, b \in \mathbb{K}$.
