# Auerbach basis

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a finite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a basis of $E$ with coordinate functionals $b_i^*$, so that $x = \sum_i b_i^*(x)\, b_i$. The basis is an **Auerbach basis** if $\|b_i\| = 1$ and $\|b_i^*\| = 1$ for every $i$: a basis of unit vectors whose coordinate functionals all have norm $1$.
