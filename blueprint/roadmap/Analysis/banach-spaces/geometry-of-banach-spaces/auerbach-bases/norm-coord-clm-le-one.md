# Operator norm of a norm-bounded coordinate functional is at most 1

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a finite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a basis of $E$ with coordinate functionals $b_i^*$, so that $x = \sum_i b_i^*(x)\, b_i$. If $|b_i^*(y)| \le \|y\|$ for every $y \in E$, then $\|b_i^*\| \le 1$.
