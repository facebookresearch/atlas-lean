# Coordinate functional of a basis as a continuous linear map

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a finite-dimensional normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a basis of $E$ with coordinate functionals $b_i^*$, so that $x = \sum_i b_i^*(x)\, b_i$. For each $i$, the coordinate functional $b_i^*$ is a bounded linear functional on $E$: in finite dimensions every linear functional is continuous.
