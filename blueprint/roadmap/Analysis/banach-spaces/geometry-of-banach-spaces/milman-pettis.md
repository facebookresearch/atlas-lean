# Milman–Pettis theorem

Let $E$ be a uniformly convex Banach space over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$
or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $E^*$ be the space of continuous linear functionals on $E$. Then every
continuous linear functional $\Phi$ on $E^*$ is evaluation at a point: there is $x \in E$ with
$\Phi(f) = f(x)$ for every $f \in E^*$.

## Sources

- D. Milman, On some criteria for the regularity of spaces of the type (B), C. R. (Doklady) Acad. Sci. URSS 20 (1938), and B. Pettis, Duke Math. J. 1939, Milman-Pettis theorem that uniformly convex Banach spaces are reflexive; Clarkson uniform convexity 1936. The statement here is the case of a uniformly convex Banach space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, as the surjectivity of the canonical map into the bidual.
