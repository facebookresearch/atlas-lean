# Rademacher sign of a coordinate

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. A sign pattern of length $n$ is a vector $\omega$ of $n$ Boolean values (true or false). The Rademacher sign of $\omega$ at the coordinate $i$ is the scalar $\varepsilon_i \in \mathbb{K}$ equal to $1$ if $\omega_i$ is true and to $-1$ if $\omega_i$ is false.
