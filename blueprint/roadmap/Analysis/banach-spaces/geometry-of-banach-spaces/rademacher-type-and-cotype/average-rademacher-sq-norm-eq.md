# Average squared norm of a Rademacher sum

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x_1, \dots, x_n \in E$, the average of the squared Rademacher sums over all sign vectors is

$$
2^{-n} \sum_{\varepsilon \in \{-1, 1\}^n} \Big\| \sum_{i=1}^{n} \varepsilon_i x_i \Big\|^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \|x_i\|^2.
$$
