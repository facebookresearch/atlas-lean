# Rademacher type

Let $X$ be a real normed space, $C \in [0, \infty]$ and $p$ a real
number. We say that $X$ has Rademacher type $p$ with constant $C$ if for every $n \ge 1$ and all
$x_1, \dots, x_n \in X$,

$$
\mathbb{E}\Bigl\|\sum_{j=1}^n \varepsilon_j x_j\Bigr\|^p \le C^p \sum_{j=1}^n \|x_j\|^p,
$$

where $\varepsilon_1, \dots, \varepsilon_n$ are independent Rademacher random variables. Both sides are computed in $[0, \infty]$, with $a^0 = 1$ for every $a$ (so $0^0 = \infty^0 = 1$), $0^p = 0$ and
$\infty^p = \infty$ for $p > 0$, $0^p = \infty$ and $\infty^p = 0$ for $p < 0$, and $0 \cdot \infty = 0$.
