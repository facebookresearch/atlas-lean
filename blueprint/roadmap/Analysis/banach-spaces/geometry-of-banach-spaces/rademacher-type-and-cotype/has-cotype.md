# Rademacher cotype of a Banach space

A real normed space $X$ has (Rademacher) cotype $q$ if there is one constant $C$ such that for every $n$
and every family $x_1, \dots, x_n \in X$,
$(\sum_i \|x_i\|^q)^{1/q} \le C \, 2^{-n} \sum_{\varepsilon \in \{-1, 1\}^n} \|\sum_i \varepsilon_i x_i\|$.
The right side is $C$ times the [Rademacher average](rademacher-average.md).
