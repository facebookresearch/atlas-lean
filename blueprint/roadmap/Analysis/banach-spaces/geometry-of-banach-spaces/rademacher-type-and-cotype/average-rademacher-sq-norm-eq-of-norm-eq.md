# Average squared Rademacher sum of unit vectors

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. If $x_1, \dots, x_n \in E$ are unit vectors, then

$$
2^{-n} \sum_{\varepsilon \in \{-1, 1\}^n} \Big\| \sum_{i=1}^{n} \varepsilon_i x_i \Big\|^2 = n.
$$
