# Second moment of a Rademacher sum in a Hilbert space

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x_1, \dots, x_n \in E$,

$$
\sum_{\varepsilon \in \{-1, 1\}^n} \Big\| \sum_{i=1}^{n} \varepsilon_i x_i \Big\|^2 = 2^n \sum_{i=1}^{n} \|x_i\|^2.
$$

The cross terms cancel: the induction step pairs the two values of the last sign and applies the parallelogram law.
