# Banach spaces with a Schauder basis have the approximation property

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. Every Banach space over $\mathbb{K}$ with a Schauder basis has the [approximation property](has-approximation-property.md). The partial-sum projections of the basis have finite rank, converge pointwise to the identity, and are uniformly bounded by the Banach–Steinhaus theorem; pointwise convergence and uniform boundedness give uniform convergence on compact sets by a finite $\varepsilon$-net argument.
