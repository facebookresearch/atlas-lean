# Approximation property passes to complemented subspaces

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$ with the [approximation property](has-approximation-property.md), and let $P$ be a bounded operator on $E$ with $P^2 = P$ and range a subspace $F$. Then $F$ has the approximation property. Indeed, a finite-rank approximant $S$ of the identity of $E$ yields the finite-rank operator $P S$ restricted to $F$, and since $P$ fixes every point of $F$, $P(S x) - x = P(S x - x)$ for $x \in F$.
