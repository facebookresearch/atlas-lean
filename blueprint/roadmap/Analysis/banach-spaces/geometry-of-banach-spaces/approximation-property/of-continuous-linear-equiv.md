# Approximation property is invariant under linear isomorphism

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $E$, $F$ be normed spaces over $\mathbb{K}$. If $E$ has the [approximation property](has-approximation-property.md) and there is a bounded linear isomorphism $E \to F$ with bounded inverse, then $F$ has the approximation property: conjugate a finite-rank approximant by the isomorphism.
