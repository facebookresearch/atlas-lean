# Hilbert spaces with a Hilbert basis indexed by $\mathbb{N}$ have the approximation property

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. Every Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$ with a Hilbert basis indexed by $\mathbb{N}$ has the [approximation property](has-approximation-property.md), since such a basis is a Schauder basis.
