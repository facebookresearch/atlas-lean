# Map LCompletion Linear mul

Let `𝕜` be a real or complex scalar field (`RCLike 𝕜`) and let `H` and `K` be
independent complete normed inner-product spaces over `𝕜`, possibly zero. For all algebraic
tensors `z,w` of bounded endomorphisms of `H` and `K`, the actual completed tensor
operator action preserves multiplication on `Completion (H ⊗[𝕜] K)`.
