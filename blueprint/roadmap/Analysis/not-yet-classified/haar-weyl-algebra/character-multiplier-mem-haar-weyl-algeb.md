# Continuous-character multiplier belongs to the Weyl algebra

Let `G` be a locally
compact topological group with its Borel measurable structure, and `μ` a regular Haar
measure. On `L²(G,μ)`, let `λ_g ξ(s)=ξ(g⁻¹s)` and `U_χ ξ(s)=χ(s)ξ(s)` for a continuous
circle-valued character `χ`. for every `χ∈PontryaginDual G`, `U_χ∈haarWeylAlgebra μ`.
