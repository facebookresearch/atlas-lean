# Operator value of a Weyl translation unitary

Let `G` be a locally compact topological
group with its Borel measurable structure, and `μ` a regular Haar measure. On `L²(G,μ)`,
let `λ_g ξ(s)=ξ(g⁻¹s)` and `U_χ ξ(s)=χ(s)ξ(s)` for a continuous circle-valued character
`χ`. the bounded operator underlying `haarWeylTranslationUnitary μ g` is `λ_g`.
