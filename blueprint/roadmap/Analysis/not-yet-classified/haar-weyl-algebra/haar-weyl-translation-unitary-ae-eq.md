# Almost-everywhere action of a Weyl translation

Let `G` be a locally compact
topological group with its Borel measurable structure, and `μ` a regular Haar measure. On
`L²(G,μ)`, let `λ_g ξ(s)=ξ(g⁻¹s)` and `U_χ ξ(s)=χ(s)ξ(s)` for a continuous circle-valued
character `χ`. for `ξ∈L²(G,μ)`, `(haarWeylTranslationUnitary μ g)ξ(s)=ξ(g⁻¹s)` for
`μ`-almost every `s`.
