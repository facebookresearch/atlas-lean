# Containment of the scalar Weyl algebra tested by generators

Let `G` be a locally
compact topological group with its Borel measurable structure, and `μ` a regular Haar
measure. On `L²(G,μ)`, let `λ_g ξ(s)=ξ(g⁻¹s)` and `U_χ ξ(s)=χ(s)ξ(s)` for a continuous
circle-valued character `χ`. for a von Neumann algebra `N` on `L²(G,μ)`, `haarWeylAlgebra
μ≤N` exactly when all `λ_g` and `U_χ` belong to `N`.
