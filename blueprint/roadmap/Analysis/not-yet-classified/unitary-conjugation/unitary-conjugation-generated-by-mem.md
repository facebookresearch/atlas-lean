# Forward preservation of a generated von Neumann algebra

Let `H` be a complete complex
Hilbert space, `S ⊆ B(H)` and `U ∈ U(H)`. Assume `U a U*∈StarAlgebra.adjoin ℂ S` for every
`a∈S`. Then `U x U*∈generatedBy S` for every `x∈generatedBy S`, where `generatedBy S` is
the double commutant of the unital complex star algebra generated by `S`.
