# Normal automorphism of a unitary-normalized algebra

Let `H` be a complete complex
Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write `ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for
its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex span in the continuous dual
`M*`. Equip `M` with the ultraweak topology `σ(M,M_*)`, induced by `x ↦ φ(x)` for `φ ∈
M_*`. Let `U ∈ B(H)` be unitary and assume both `U M U* ⊆ M` and `U* M U ⊆ M`.
`unitaryConjugationNormalEquiv M U` is the star equivalence `x ↦ U x U*` with both forward
and inverse maps ultraweakly continuous.
