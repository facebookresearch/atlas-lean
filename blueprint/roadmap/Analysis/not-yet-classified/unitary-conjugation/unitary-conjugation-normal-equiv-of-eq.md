# Normal conjugation automorphism from setwise normalization

Let `H` be a complete
complex Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write
`ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex
span in the continuous dual `M*`. Equip `M` with the ultraweak topology `σ(M,M_*)`,
induced by `x ↦ φ(x)` for `φ ∈ M_*`. Let `U ∈ B(H)` be unitary with `U M U*=M`.
`unitaryConjugationNormalEquivOfEq M U : NormalStarAlgEquiv M M` is the ultraweakly
bicontinuous star map `x ↦ U x U*`, with inverse `x ↦ U* x U`.
