# Forward value of the normalized unitary star equivalence

Let `H` be a complete
complex Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write
`ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex
span in the continuous dual `M*`. Equip `M` with the ultraweak topology `σ(M,M_*)`,
induced by `x ↦ φ(x)` for `φ ∈ M_*`. Let `U ∈ B(H)` be unitary and assume both `U M U* ⊆
M` and `U* M U ⊆ M`. for `x ∈ M`, the bounded operator underlying
`unitaryConjugationStarAlgEquiv M U x` is `U x U*`.
