# C*-identity in an isometric directed limit

**C*-identity in an isometric directed limit.** Let `I` be a nonempty directed preorder and
`(Gᵢ,fᵢⱼ)` a directed system of nonunital normed rings with ring involutions satisfying the
C*-identity. If each connecting ring homomorphism preserves both the involution and the norm, the
algebraic direct limit, with its induced norm and involution, also satisfies the C*-identity.
