# Projection approximation in a dense increasing family

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra
and let `Aₖ ⊆ A`, for `k ∈ ℕ`, be increasing closed nonunital star subalgebras with norm-dense
union. For a finite index type `n`, every projection `p ∈ Mₙ(A)` and `ε > 0` admit a projection in
one `Mₙ(Aₖ)` whose image is within `ε` of `p` in the inherited C*-matrix norm.
