# Spatial value of the double Radon source coefficient

Let `G` be a locally compact
topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`, `H` a complete
complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with norm-continuous inverse
predual orbits. Put `T=Completion(H ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 μ)` and let `U₀ : T ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] Lp H 2 μ` send
`ξ⊗f` to the L² class represented μ-almost everywhere by `s ↦ f(s) • ξ`. For `P_x ξ =ᵐ[μ]
(s ↦ α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s))`, put `Q_x=U₀⁻¹P_xU₀`. Let `C₁` be generated by `Q_x` and
`V_g=I_H⊗λ_g`, where `λ_g f =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ f(g⁻¹s))`. Write `Γ=PontryaginDual G`, with its
Borel measurable structure, and let `ν` be a regular Haar measure on `Γ`. Let `β` be the
normal character action on `C₁`, and set `C₂=C₁⋊_β Γ` on `D=Completion(T ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 ν)`.
Assume also that `G` is Hausdorff. Let `U : D ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] L_R` be the tensor-to-Radon unitary,
where `L_R=Lp H 2 (radonProd μ ν)` on the Borel topological product `G×Γ`. Write `j₂ : C₁
→ C₂` for the second regular coefficient map. for `x∈M`, the operator of the normal Radon
coefficient is `U Q₂(j₁(x)) U⁻¹`, where `Q₂` is the second tensor coefficient and `j₁` the
first inclusion.

Here `=ᵐ[μ]` denotes equality of measurable representatives μ-almost everywhere, not point
evaluation of an L² class. Operator identities are equalities of bounded operators. The
product-space L² class uses the stated measure `radonProd μ ν`.
