# Normal crossed-product automorphism from a character multiplier

Let `G` be a locally
compact topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`, `H` a
complete complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with norm-continuous
inverse predual orbits. On `L=Lp H 2 μ`, put `π_α(x)ξ(s)=α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s)`,
`λ_gξ(s)=ξ(g⁻¹s)` and `U_χξ(s)=χ(s) • ξ(s)`. Let `C=M⋊_α G` be generated by `π_α(M)` and
`λ(G)`. `characterConjugationNormalEquiv μ α χ : C ≃ C` is the complex star equivalence
`β_χ(T)=U_χ T U_χ*`, ultraweakly continuous in both directions.
