# Convergence of Tikhonov regularization with parameter equal to noise level

Let $A : H \to K$ be a bounded linear operator between real Hilbert spaces, let $x^\dagger = A^* w$ for some $w \in K$ (source condition) and $b = A x^\dagger$. Suppose that for every $\delta > 0$, $x_\delta$ solves $(A^*A + \delta I)x_\delta = A^* b_\delta$ with $\|b_\delta - b\| \le \delta$. Then $x_\delta \to x^\dagger$ as $\delta \to 0^+$.
