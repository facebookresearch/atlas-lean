# Multiplication of spatial factor pairs

Let `H,K` be complete complex Hilbert spaces,
`M ⊆ B(H)` and `N ⊆ B(K)` concrete von Neumann algebras. Write `L=Completion (H ⊗[ℂ] K)`
for their Hilbert tensor product, and `x⊗y` for the bounded factor operator on `L`. for
`x₁,x₂ ∈ M`, `y₁,y₂ ∈ N`, `(x₁x₂)⊗(y₁y₂)=(x₁⊗y₁)(x₂⊗y₂)` as elements of the spatial tensor
algebra.
