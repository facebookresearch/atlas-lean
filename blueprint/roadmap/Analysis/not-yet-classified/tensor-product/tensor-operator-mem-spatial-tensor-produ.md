# Spatial factor operator belongs to the tensor algebra

Let `H,K` be complete complex
Hilbert spaces, `M ⊆ B(H)` and `N ⊆ B(K)` concrete von Neumann algebras. Write
`L=Completion (H ⊗[ℂ] K)` for their Hilbert tensor product, and `x⊗y` for the bounded
factor operator on `L`. for `x ∈ M`, `y ∈ N`, the operator
`x⊗y=TensorProduct.mapLCompletion x y` belongs to `spatialTensorProduct M N`.
