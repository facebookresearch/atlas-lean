# Operator-norm bound for a spatial factor pair

Let `H,K` be complete complex Hilbert
spaces, `M ⊆ B(H)` and `N ⊆ B(K)` concrete von Neumann algebras. Write `L=Completion (H
⊗[ℂ] K)` for their Hilbert tensor product, and `x⊗y` for the bounded factor operator on
`L`. for `x ∈ M`, `y ∈ N`, `‖spatialTensor M N x y‖≤‖x‖*‖y‖`.
