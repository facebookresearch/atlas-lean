# Line integral of a gradient field

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ (Euclidean) be open, $\varphi : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ continuously differentiable on $U$, and $E : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ with $E(x) = -∇\varphi(x)$ for $x \in U$. Let $\gamma$ be a path from $a$ to $b$ whose range lies in $U$ and whose extension to $\mathbb{R}$ is continuously differentiable on $[0, 1]$. Then the curve integral of the 1-form $x \mapsto \langle E(x), \cdot\rangle$ along $\gamma$ is

$$\int_\gamma \langle E(x), dx\rangle = \varphi(a) - \varphi(b).$$
