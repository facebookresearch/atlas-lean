# Line integral of a gradient field along the reversed path

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ (Euclidean) be open, $\varphi : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ continuously differentiable on $U$, and $E : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ with $E(x) = -∇\varphi(x)$ for $x \in U$. Let $\gamma$ be a path from $a$ to $b$ whose range lies in $U$ and whose extension to $\mathbb{R}$ is continuously differentiable on $[0, 1]$. Then along the reversed path $\gamma^{-1}$ from $b$ to $a$,

$$\int_{\gamma^{-1}} \langle E(x), dx\rangle = \varphi(b) - \varphi(a).$$
