# Gradient theorem for curve integrals

Let $F$ be a real normed space, $U \subseteq F$ open, and $\varphi : F \to \mathbb{R}$ continuously differentiable on $U$. Let $\gamma$ be a path from $a$ to $b$ whose range lies in $U$ and whose extension to $\mathbb{R}$ is continuously differentiable on $[0, 1]$. Then the curve integral of the differential $d\varphi$ along $\gamma$ is

$$\int_\gamma d\varphi = \varphi(b) - \varphi(a).$$
