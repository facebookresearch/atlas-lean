# Strict continuity of multiplier grading automorphisms

Let `B` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra
with grading `γ`, let `M(B)` be its multiplier algebra, and let `M(B)/B` be the outer multiplier
algebra. For every degree `n`, the induced automorphism of `M(B)` is continuous for the strict
topology generated by the left and right coefficient seminorms.
