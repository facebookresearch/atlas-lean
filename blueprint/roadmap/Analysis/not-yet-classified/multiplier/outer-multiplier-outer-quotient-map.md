# Equivariance of the outer quotient projection

Let `B` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra with
grading `γ`, let `M(B)` be its multiplier algebra, and let `M(B)/B` be the outer multiplier algebra.
For every degree `n` and `T ∈ M(B)`, grading its quotient class equals taking the quotient class of
its graded multiplier.
