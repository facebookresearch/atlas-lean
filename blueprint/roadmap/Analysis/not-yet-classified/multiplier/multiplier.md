# Grading of a multiplier algebra

Let `B` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra with grading `γ`, let
`M(B)` be its multiplier algebra, and let `M(B)/B` be the outer multiplier algebra. Transport of
both multiplier centralizer actions by each coefficient automorphism `γ(n)` gives a `ℤ/2ℤ` grading
on `M(B)`.
