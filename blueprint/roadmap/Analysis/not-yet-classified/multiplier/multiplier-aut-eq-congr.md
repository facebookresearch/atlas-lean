# Degree automorphism of a multiplier grading

Let `B` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra with
grading `γ`, let `M(B)` be its multiplier algebra, and let `M(B)/B` be the outer multiplier algebra.
For every degree `n`, the multiplier grading automorphism is the centralizer transport induced by
the coefficient star automorphism `γ(n)`.
