# Grading of the outer multiplier algebra

Let `B` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra with grading
`γ`, let `M(B)` be its multiplier algebra, and let `M(B)/B` be the outer multiplier algebra. The
multiplier grading preserves the embedded coefficient ideal and descends to a `ℤ/2ℤ` grading of
`M(B)/B`.
