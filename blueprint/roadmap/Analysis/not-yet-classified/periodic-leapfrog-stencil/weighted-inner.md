# Weighted grid pairing

For $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^N$, the weighted pairing is

$$\langle u, v\rangle_h = h \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} u_j\,v_j.$$

On a nonempty grid ($N > 0$) it is an inner product on $\mathbb{R}^N$ exactly when $h > 0$: for $h = 0$ it is identically $0$, and for $h < 0$ it is negative definite. For $N = 0$ the space $\mathbb{R}^0$ is $\{0\}$ and every $h$ gives the zero pairing.
