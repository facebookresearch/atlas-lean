# Quotient projection maps open seminorm balls onto quotient balls

Let 𝕜 be a normed
field, X a 𝕜-module with additive commutative group, p a seminorm and Y a submodule. For
every real ε, the image of the open p-ball centered at zero with radius ε under X → X/Y is
precisely the open quotient-seminorm ball centered at zero with radius ε.
