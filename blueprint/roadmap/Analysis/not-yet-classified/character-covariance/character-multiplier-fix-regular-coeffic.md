# Character conjugation fixes a regular coefficient operator

Let `G` be a locally
compact topological group with its Borel σ-algebra and a regular Borel measure `μ`. Let
`H` be a complete complex Hilbert space, `M⊆B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra, and `α :
G→Aut_normal(M)` a normal action whose inverse predual orbits `g ↦ φ∘α_g⁻¹` are
norm-continuous for every `φ∈M_*`. Let `χ∈PontryaginDual G` be a continuous circle-valued
character.

On `L=Lp H 2 μ`, set `π_α(x)=regularCoefficient μ α x` for `x∈M`, and let
`U_χ=characterMultiplierUnitary μ χ`. Their measurable representatives satisfy `π_α(x)ξ
=ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s))` and `U_χ ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ χ(s) • ξ(s))` for `ξ∈L`, with circle
values embedded in `ℂ`. Then `U_χ π_α(x) U_χ*=π_α(x)` as an equality of bounded operators
on `L`. The coordinate formulas are μ-almost-everywhere equalities of representatives.
