# Cyclic orbit projection is self-adjoint and idempotent

Let `H` be a complete complex Hilbert
space, let `S` be a unital complex star subalgebra of the bounded operators on `H`, and let `ξ ∈ H`.
The orthogonal projection onto the closed cyclic orbit subspace of `ξ` is a self-adjoint idempotent
bounded operator.
