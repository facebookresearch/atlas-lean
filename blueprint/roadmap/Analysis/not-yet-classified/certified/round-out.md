# Outward rounding of a rational interval

For a rational interval $I = [a, b]$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the outward rounding of $I$ to the grid $10^{-k}$ is the interval $\left[\lfloor a\, 10^k \rfloor / 10^k,\ \lceil b\, 10^k \rceil / 10^k\right]$: the lower endpoint is rounded down and the upper endpoint up.
