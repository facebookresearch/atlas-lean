# Initial ultraweak topology of bounded operators

Let `H` be a complex normed
inner-product space, with no completeness assumption. Write `ℓ²(H)=lp (fun _ : ℕ => H) 2`
for its square-summable vector sequences. For `x,y ∈ ℓ²(H)`, put `ω_(x,y)(T)=Σ_n ⟨y_n,T
x_n⟩`. `topology H` is the topology induced by `T ↦ (ω_(x,y)(T))_(x,y)` into the product
of copies of `ℂ`, equivalently the coarsest topology making every `ω_(x,y)` continuous.
