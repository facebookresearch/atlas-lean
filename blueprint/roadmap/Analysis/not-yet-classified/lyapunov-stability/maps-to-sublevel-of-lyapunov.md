# Sublevel invariance

Let $\gamma$ be a solution of $\gamma'(t) = f(t, \gamma(t))$ for $t \ge 0$ that stays in a set $s$, let $V$ be differentiable at every point of $s$ with derivative $V'(x)$, and suppose the orbital derivative is nonpositive: $V'(x)\big(f(t, x)\big) \le 0$ for all $t \ge 0$ and $x \in s$. If $V(\gamma(0)) \le c$, then $V(\gamma(t)) \le c$ for every $t \ge 0$.
