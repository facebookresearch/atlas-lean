# Lyapunov's direct method, exponential decay of the state

Let $\gamma$ be an integral curve of $\dot x = f(t, x)$ on $[0, \infty)$ in a real normed space that stays in a set $s$, and let $V$ be differentiable on $s$ with derivative along the flow at most $-a V$, that is $V'(x)\, f(t, x) \le -a V(x)$ for $t \ge 0$ and $x \in s$. Assume $c_1 \|x - x_0\|^2 \le V(x) \le c_2 \|x - x_0\|^2$ on $s$ with $c_1 > 0$. Then for every $t \ge 0$, $\|\gamma(t) - x_0\| \le \sqrt{c_2/c_1}\, e^{-a t/2}\, \|\gamma(0) - x_0\|$.
