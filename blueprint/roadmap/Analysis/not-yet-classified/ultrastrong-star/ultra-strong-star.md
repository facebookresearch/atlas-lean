# Algebra-element carrier for ultrastrong-star topology

Let `H` be a complete complex
Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write `ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for
its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex span in the continuous dual
`M*`. `UltraStrongStar M` is a type copy of the elements of `M`, on which translated
normal quadratic gauge balls define the ultrastrong-star topology.
