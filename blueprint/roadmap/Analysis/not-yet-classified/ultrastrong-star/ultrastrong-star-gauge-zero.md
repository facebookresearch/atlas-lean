# Zero vector of an ultrastrong-star gauge

Let `H` be a complete complex Hilbert space
and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write `ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for its vector
functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex span in the continuous dual `M*`. For
a positive normal functional `φ`, put `q_φ(x)=√(Re φ(x* x+x x*))`. `q_φ(0)=0` for every
positive normal functional `φ`.
