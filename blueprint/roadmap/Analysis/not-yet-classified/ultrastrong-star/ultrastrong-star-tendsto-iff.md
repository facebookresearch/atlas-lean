# Subbasic-ball criterion for ultrastrong-star convergence

Let `H` be a complete
complex Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write
`ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex
span in the continuous dual `M*`. For a positive normal functional `φ`, put `q_φ(x)=√(Re
φ(x* x+x x*))`. along any filter `l`, a family `x_i` converges to `x` exactly when for
every positive normal `ψ`, center `a` and `r>0` with `q_ψ(x-a)<r`, eventually
`q_ψ(x_i-a)<r`.
