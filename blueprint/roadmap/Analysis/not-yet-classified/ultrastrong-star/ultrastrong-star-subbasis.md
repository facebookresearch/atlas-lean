# Positive-radius subbasis of normal gauge balls

Let `H` be a complete complex Hilbert
space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write `ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for its
vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex span in the continuous dual
`M*`. For a positive normal functional `φ`, put `q_φ(x)=√(Re φ(x* x+x x*))`. the subbasis
contains all sets `{x : q_ψ(x-a)<r}` for every positive normal functional `ψ`, center
`a∈M` and `r>0`.
