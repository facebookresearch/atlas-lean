# Translated ultrastrong-star quadratic gauge ball

Let `H` be a complete complex
Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write `ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for
its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex span in the continuous dual
`M*`. For a positive normal functional `φ`, put `q_φ(x)=√(Re φ(x* x+x x*))`. for `a∈M` and
any `r : ℝ`, `ultrastrongStarBall M φ a r={x : q_φ(x-a)<r}` in the ultrastrong-star
carrier.
