# Normal-monotone functional description of ultrastrong-star topology

Let `H` be a
complete complex Hilbert space and `M ⊆ B(H)` a concrete von Neumann algebra. Write
`ω_(ξ,η)(x)=⟨ξ,xη⟩` for its vector functionals. Let `M_*` be their norm-closed complex
span in the continuous dual `M*`. the topology is generated by `{x : √(Re
ψ((x-a)*(x-a)+(x-a)(x-a)*))<r}` over all monotone normal functionals `ψ∈M_*`, centers
`a∈M` and `r>0`.
