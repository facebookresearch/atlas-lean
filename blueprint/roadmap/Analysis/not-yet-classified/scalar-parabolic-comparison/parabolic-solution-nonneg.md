# Nonnegativity for a scalar parabolic equation

Let $D > 0$, $L > 0$, $T > 0$, and let $c$ be continuous on $[0, T] \times [0, L]$ with partial derivatives $c_t$, $c_x$, $c_{xx}$ there, satisfying $c_t - D c_{xx} + k c = s$ with $k \ge 0$ and $s \ge 0$ on the cylinder, $c(0, x) \ge 0$ on $[0, L]$, and $c(t, 0) \ge 0$, $c(t, L) \ge 0$ on $[0, T]$. Then $c(t, x) \ge 0$ for all $(t, x) \in [0, T] \times [0, L]$.
