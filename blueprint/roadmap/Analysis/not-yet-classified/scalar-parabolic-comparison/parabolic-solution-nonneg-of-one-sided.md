# Nonnegativity for a parabolic equation with one-sided reaction bound

Let $D \ge 0$ and let $c$ be continuous on $[0, T] \times [0, L]$ with the partial derivatives $c_t$ (within $[0, T]$), $c_x$, $c_{xx}$ existing on $(0, T] \times (0, L)$, where $c_t - D c_{xx} = r(c, x, t)$. Suppose a constant $K$ satisfies $K c \le r(c, x, t)$ at every interior point where $c \le 0$. If $c(0, x) \ge 0$ on $[0, L]$ and $c(t, 0) \ge 0$, $c(t, L) \ge 0$ on $[0, T]$, then $c \ge 0$ on $[0, T] \times [0, L]$.
