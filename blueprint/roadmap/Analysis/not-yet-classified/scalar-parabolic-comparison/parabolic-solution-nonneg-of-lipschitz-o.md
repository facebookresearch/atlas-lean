# Nonnegativity for a scalar reaction-diffusion equation

Let $D \ge 0$ and let $c$ be continuous on $[0, T] \times [0, L]$, with the needed derivatives in the interior $t \in (0, T]$, $x \in (0, L)$, solving $c_t - D c_{xx} = r(c, x, t)$ there. Suppose that in the interior $c \ge -M$, $u \mapsto r(u, x, t)$ is $K$-Lipschitz on $[-M, 0]$ uniformly, and $r(0, x, t) \ge 0$. If $c(0, x) \ge 0$ on $[0, L]$ and $c(t, 0) \ge 0$, $c(t, L) \ge 0$ on $[0, T]$, then $c \ge 0$ on $[0, T] \times [0, L]$.
