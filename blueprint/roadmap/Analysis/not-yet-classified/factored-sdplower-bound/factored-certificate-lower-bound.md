# Factored dual certificate lower bound

Let $C, A_1, \dots, A_m, E_1, \dots, E_p$ be Hermitian $n \times n$ complex matrices, $c_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $d \in \mathbb{R}^p$. Suppose $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^m$ with $\lambda \ge 0$, $\mu \in \mathbb{R}^p$ and $F \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times k}$ satisfy

$$C + \sum_i \lambda_i A_i + \sum_j \mu_j E_j = F F^{\mathsf H}.$$

Then every positive semidefinite $W$ with $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(A_i W) \le b_i$ for all $i$ and $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(E_j W) = d_j$ for all $j$ satisfies

$$c_0 - \sum_i \lambda_i b_i - \sum_j \mu_j d_j \le \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(C W) + c_0.$$
