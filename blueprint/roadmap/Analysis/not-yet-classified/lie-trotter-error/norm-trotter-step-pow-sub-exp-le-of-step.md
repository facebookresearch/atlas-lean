# Number of Lie–Trotter steps for a given accuracy

Let $A$ and $B$ be Hermitian $n \times n$ complex matrices, $t \ge 0$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. If $r \ge 1$ and $r \ge t^2\lVert BA - AB\rVert/(2\varepsilon)$, then $r$ Lie–Trotter steps of length $t/r$ approximate $e^{-it(A+B)}$ within $\varepsilon$ in operator norm.
