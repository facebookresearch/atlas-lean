# Affine nonnegativity over a convex hull is checked at the vertices

Let $s$ be a finite set of points in $\mathbb R^\iota$ ($\iota$ finite) and, for each activity $j$ in a finite set $A$, let $d_j(x) = a_j + \sum_i b_{j,i} x_i$ be an affine duration. Then $d_j(x) \ge 0$ for every $x$ in the convex hull of $s$ and every $j \in A$ if and only if $d_j(v) \ge 0$ for every vertex $v \in s$ and every $j \in A$.
