# Zero-gap rational LP certificate gives optimality

Let $A \in \mathbb Q^{m\times n}$, $b \in \mathbb Q^m$, $c \in \mathbb Q^n$, $x \in \mathbb Q^n$ and $y \in \mathbb Q^m$ satisfy $x \ge 0$, $Ax \le b$, $y \ge 0$, $A^\top y \ge c$ and $b\cdot y - c\cdot x = 0$. Then over the reals, $x$ is feasible and maximizes $c\cdot x'$ over all $x' \ge 0$ with $Ax' \le b$, and $y$ is feasible and minimizes $b\cdot y'$ over all $y' \ge 0$ with $A^\top y' \ge c$.
