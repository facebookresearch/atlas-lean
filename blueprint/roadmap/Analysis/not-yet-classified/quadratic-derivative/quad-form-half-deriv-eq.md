# Derivative of half a quadratic form along a curve

Let $C$ be a symmetric real matrix indexed by a finite set, and let $x : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ have coordinate derivatives $x_i'(t) = \dot x_i$ at $t$. Then the derivative at $t$ of $s \mapsto \tfrac12\, x(s)^{\mathsf T}Cx(s)$ is $\sum_{i,j}\dot x_i\,C_{ij}\,x_j(t)$.
