# Nonunital star map into the all-state enveloping algebra

Let `A` be a nonunital
complex C-star algebra. Let `H_u` be the Hilbert direct sum of the GNS spaces of all
positive bounded functionals of norm one, and `π_u : A →⋆ₙₐ[ℂ] B(H_u)` their
coordinatewise GNS representation. `toUniversalStateEnvelopingAlgebra A : A →⋆ₙₐ[ℂ]
universalStateEnvelopingAlgebra A` sends `a` to the element represented by `π_u(a)`.
