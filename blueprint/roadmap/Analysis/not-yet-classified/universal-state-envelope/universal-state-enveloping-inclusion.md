# Operator inclusion of the all-state von Neumann envelope

Let `A` be a nonunital
complex C-star algebra. Let `H_u` be the Hilbert direct sum of the GNS spaces of all
positive bounded functionals of norm one, and `π_u : A →⋆ₙₐ[ℂ] B(H_u)` their
coordinatewise GNS representation. `universalStateEnvelopingInclusion A` is the nonunital
complex star homomorphism including the generated algebra into `B(H_u)`.
