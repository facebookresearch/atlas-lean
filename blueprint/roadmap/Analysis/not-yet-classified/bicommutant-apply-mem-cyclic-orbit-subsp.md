# Bicommutant preserves the cyclic orbit closure

Let `H` be a complete complex Hilbert space and
`S` a unital complex star subalgebra of the bounded operators on `H`. If a bounded operator `T`
commutes with every operator commuting with `S`, then for every `ξ ∈ H`, the vector `Tξ` belongs to
the closed linear subspace generated by `Sξ`.
