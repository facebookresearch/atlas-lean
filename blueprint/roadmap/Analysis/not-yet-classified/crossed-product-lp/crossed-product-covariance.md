# Covariance inside the Haar crossed-product algebra

Let `G` be a locally compact
topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`, `H` a complete
complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with norm-continuous inverse
predual orbits. On `L=Lp H 2 μ`, put `π_α(x)ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s))`, `λ_g ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s
↦ ξ(g⁻¹s))` and `U_χ ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ χ(s) • ξ(s))`. Let `C=M⋊_α G` be generated by `π_α(M)`
and `λ(G)`. for `g∈G`, `x∈M`, the coefficient and group maps satisfy `λ_g π_α(x)
λ_g*=π_α(α_g(x))` as elements of `C`.

Here `=ᵐ[μ]` denotes equality of measurable representatives μ-almost everywhere, not point
evaluation of an L² class. Operator identities are equalities of bounded operators.
