# Second dual action scales the dual Haar implementer

Let `G` be a locally compact
topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`, `H` a complete
complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with norm-continuous inverse
predual orbits. Put `T=Completion(H ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 μ)` and let `U₀ : T ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] Lp H 2 μ` send
`ξ⊗f` to `s ↦ f(s) • ξ`. For `P_xξ(s)=α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s)`, put `Q_x=U₀⁻¹P_xU₀`. Let `C₁` be
generated by `Q_x` and `V_g=I_H⊗λ_g`, where `λ_g f(s)=f(g⁻¹s)`. Write `Γ=PontryaginDual
G`, with its Borel measurable structure, and let `ν` be a regular Haar measure on `Γ`. Let
`β` be the normal character action on `C₁`, and set `C₂=C₁⋊_β Γ` on `D=Completion(T ⊗[ℂ]
Lp ℂ 2 ν)`. for `g∈G`, `χ∈Γ`, the second dual action sends
`crossedProductTensorGroupUnitary ν β χ` to `χ(g)` times that unitary.
