# Second dual action fixes the first crossed-product coefficient image

Let `G` be a
locally compact topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`,
`H` a complete complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with
norm-continuous inverse predual orbits. Put `T=Completion(H ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 μ)` and let `U₀ :
T ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] Lp H 2 μ` send `ξ⊗f` to `s ↦ f(s) • ξ`. For `P_xξ(s)=α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s)`, put
`Q_x=U₀⁻¹P_xU₀`. Let `C₁` be generated by `Q_x` and `V_g=I_H⊗λ_g`, where `λ_g
f(s)=f(g⁻¹s)`. Write `Γ=PontryaginDual G`, with its Borel measurable structure, and let
`ν` be a regular Haar measure on `Γ`. Let `β` be the normal character action on `C₁`, and
set `C₂=C₁⋊_β Γ` on `D=Completion(T ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 ν)`. for `g∈G` and `x∈C₁`, the second dual
action fixes `j₂(x)`, where `j₂=crossedProductTensorCoefficient ν β : C₁ → C₂`.
