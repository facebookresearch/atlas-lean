# Second regular tensor crossed product with an explicit dual Haar measure

Let `G` be a
locally compact topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`,
`H` a complete complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with
norm-continuous inverse predual orbits. Put `T=Completion(H ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ 2 μ)` and let `U₀ :
T ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] Lp H 2 μ` send `ξ⊗f` to the L² class represented μ-almost everywhere by `s ↦ f(s)
• ξ`. For `P_x ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s))`, put `Q_x=U₀⁻¹P_xU₀`. Let `C₁` be generated
by `Q_x` and `V_g=I_H⊗λ_g`, where `λ_g f =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ f(g⁻¹s))`. Write `Γ=PontryaginDual G`,
with its Borel measurable structure, and let `ν` be a regular Haar measure on `Γ`. Let `β`
be the normal character action on `C₁`, and set `C₂=C₁⋊_β Γ` on `D=Completion(T ⊗[ℂ] Lp ℂ
2 ν)`. `doubleCrossedProductTensor μ ν α=C₂` is the von Neumann algebra of the regular
construction for `β` and `ν` on the nested Hilbert tensor `D`.

Here `=ᵐ[μ]` denotes equality of measurable representatives μ-almost everywhere, not point
evaluation of an L² class. Operator identities are equalities of bounded operators.
