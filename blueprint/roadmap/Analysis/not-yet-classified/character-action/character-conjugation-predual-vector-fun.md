# Covariant character predual action on crossed-product vector functionals

Let `G` be a
locally compact topological group with its Borel structure and regular Haar measure `μ`,
`H` a complete complex Hilbert space, and `α` a normal action on `M⊆B(H)` with
norm-continuous inverse predual orbits. On `L=Lp H 2 μ`, put `π_α(x)ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦
α_(s⁻¹)(x)ξ(s))`, `λ_g ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ ξ(g⁻¹s))` and `U_χ ξ =ᵐ[μ] (s ↦ χ(s) • ξ(s))`. Let
`C=M⋊_α G` be generated by `π_α(M)` and `λ(G)`. for `ξ,η∈L`, the inverse-automorphism
pullback `ω_(ξ,η)∘β_χ⁻¹` equals `ω_(U_χξ,U_χη)`, where `ω_(ξ,η)(T)=⟨ξ,Tη⟩` on `C`.

Here `=ᵐ[μ]` denotes equality of measurable representatives μ-almost everywhere, not point
evaluation of an L² class. Operator identities are equalities of bounded operators.
