# Rotation matrix of a quarter turn about the i axis

The quaternion $q = \tfrac{\sqrt 2}{2}(1 + i)$ has norm $1$, and the matrix of $v \mapsto q v \bar q$ in the basis $i, j, k$ is

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix},$$

which is not the identity; it sends $j \mapsto k$ and $k \mapsto -j$.
