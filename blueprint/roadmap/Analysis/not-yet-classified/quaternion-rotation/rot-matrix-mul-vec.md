# Rotation matrix of a quaternion

For a quaternion $q$, let $R(q)$ be the $3 \times 3$ real matrix whose column $b$ holds the $(i, j, k)$ coordinates of $q\, e_b\, \bar q$, where $e_0, e_1, e_2$ are $i, j, k$. For every pure imaginary quaternion $v$, $R(q)$ applied to the coordinates of $v$ gives the coordinates of $q v \bar q$.
