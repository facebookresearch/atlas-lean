# Negative second derivative along a line excludes a minimum

Let $\Pi$ be a real function on a real normed space, $x_0$ a point, $v \ne 0$, and $g(t) = \Pi(x_0 + t v)$. If $g$ is continuous at $0$, $g'(0) = 0$ and $g''(0) < 0$, then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $t \ne 0$ with $\lvert t\rvert \lVert v\rVert < \varepsilon$ and $\Pi(x_0 + t v) < \Pi(x_0)$.
