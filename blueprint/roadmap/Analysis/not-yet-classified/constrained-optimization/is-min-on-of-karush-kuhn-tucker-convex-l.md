# Convex KKT sufficiency with affine equality constraints

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $f$ and $g_1, \dots, g_m$ convex on $E$ and differentiable at $x$, and $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_p$ continuous linear functionals with constants $c_j$ such that $\ell_j(x) + c_j = 0$ for all $j$. Suppose $\lambda_i \ge 0$, $\lambda_i g_i(x) = 0$ for all $i$, and

$$Df(x) + \sum_i \lambda_i Dg_i(x) + \sum_j \mu_j \ell_j = 0.$$

Then $f(x) \le f(y)$ for every $y$ with $g_i(y) \le 0$ for all $i$ and $\ell_j(y) + c_j = 0$ for all $j$. If also $g_i(x) \le 0$ for all $i$, then $x$ is a minimizer of $f$ on this feasible set.
