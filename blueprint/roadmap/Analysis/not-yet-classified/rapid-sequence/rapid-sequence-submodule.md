# The submodule of rapidly decreasing sequences

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. These
sequences form a 𝕜-submodule of E^ℕ under coordinatewise addition and scalar multiplication.
