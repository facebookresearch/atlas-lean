# The seminorm family of the rapid-sequence topology

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. The
countable family indexed by k ∈ ℕ is pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖. It defines the weighted
supremum topology on s(𝕜,E).
