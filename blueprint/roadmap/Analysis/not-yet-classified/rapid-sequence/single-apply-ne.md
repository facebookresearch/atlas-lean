# Vanishing away from a singleton coordinate

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. For δₙ(x)
the sequence equal to x at n and zero elsewhere, one has δₙ(x)ₘ = 0 whenever m ≠ n.
