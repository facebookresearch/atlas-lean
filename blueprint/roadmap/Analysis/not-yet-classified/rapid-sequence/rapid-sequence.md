# The space of rapidly decreasing sequences

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. Equip s(𝕜,E)
with the locally convex topology generated by the seminorms pₖ. This defines the
rapid-sequence space with its weighted supremum topology.
