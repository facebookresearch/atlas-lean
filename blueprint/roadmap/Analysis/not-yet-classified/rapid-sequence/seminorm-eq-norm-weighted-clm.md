# The norm of a continuous weighted image

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. Equip s(𝕜,E)
with the locally convex topology generated by the seminorms pₖ. Define Wₖf = ((n+1)^k fₙ)ₙ ∈
ℓ∞(ℕ,E). For every k and f ∈ s(𝕜,E), pₖ(f) = ‖Wₖf‖∞.
