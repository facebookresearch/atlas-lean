# The weighted seminorm of a singleton

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. For the
singleton sequence δₙ(x), equal to x at n and zero elsewhere, one has pₖ(δₙ(x)) = (n+1)^k‖x‖
for every k,n.
