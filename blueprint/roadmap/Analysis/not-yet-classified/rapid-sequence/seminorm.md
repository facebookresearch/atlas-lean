# The weighted supremum seminorm

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. Define Wₖf =
((n+1)^k fₙ)ₙ ∈ ℓ∞(ℕ,E). For each k, the seminorm pₖ on s(𝕜,E) is the pullback of the ℓ∞ norm:
pₖ(f) = ‖Wₖf‖∞.
