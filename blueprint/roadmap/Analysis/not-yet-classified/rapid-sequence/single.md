# A rapid sequence supported at one coordinate

Let 𝕜 be a real or complex scalar field and E a normed 𝕜-vector space. Write s(𝕜,E) for the
sequences f : ℕ → E such that pₖ(f) = supₙ (n+1)^k‖fₙ‖ is finite for every k ∈ ℕ. For n ∈ ℕ
and x ∈ E, define δₙ(x) by δₙ(x)ₘ = x when m = n and zero otherwise. Every polynomially
weighted norm of this sequence is bounded.
