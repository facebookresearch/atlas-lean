# Tail bound for finite sums in the sine basis

Let $(b_n)_{n \ge 1}$ be the sine Hilbert basis of the real space $L^2([0, 1])$, $b_n(x) = \sqrt{2}\sin(n\pi x)$ for almost every $x \in [0,1]$. Let $c : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $N \le K$. Then

$$\left\| \sum_{k=N}^{K-1} c_k\, b_{k+1} \right\|^2 \le \left\| \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} c_k\, b_{k+1} \right\|^2,$$

that is, the squared norm of the modes $N+1, \dots, K$ is at most that of the modes $1, \dots, K$.
