# Linear programming integrality

Let $A$ be a totally unimodular real $m \times n$ matrix, $b \in \mathbb{Z}^m$, and $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $x \ge 0$ and $A x \le b$. Suppose $n$ rows chosen from the stacked matrix $\begin{pmatrix} A \\ -I \end{pmatrix}$ form a nonsingular square matrix $M$ that is tight at $x$: $M x$ equals the corresponding entries of $\begin{pmatrix} b \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$. Then $x$ is an integer vector.
