# Generators lie in their ultraweak algebra

Let `H` be a complex normed inner-product
space, with no completeness assumption. Write `ℓ²(H)=lp (fun _ : ℕ => H) 2` for its
square-summable vector sequences. For `x,y ∈ ℓ²(H)`, put `ω_(x,y)(T)=Σ_n ⟨y_n,T x_n⟩`. for
`A ⊆ B(H)`, `A ⊆ algebraGenerated A`.
