# Least ultraweakly closed algebra of an operator set

Let `H` be a complex normed
inner-product space, with no completeness assumption. Write `ℓ²(H)=lp (fun _ : ℕ => H) 2`
for its square-summable vector sequences. For `x,y ∈ ℓ²(H)`, put `ω_(x,y)(T)=Σ_n ⟨y_n,T
x_n⟩`. for `A ⊆ B(H)`, `algebraGenerated A` is the intersection of all unital complex
subalgebras containing `A` and closed in the coefficient ultraweak topology.
