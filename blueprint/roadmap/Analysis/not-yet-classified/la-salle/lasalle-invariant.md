# LaSalle's invariance principle

Let $K$ be compact and forward invariant for $x' = f(x)$, with $f$ Lipschitz on $K$, and let $\gamma$ be a solution on $[0, \infty)$ staying in $K$. Let $V$ be $C^1$ with $DV(x)\,f(x) \le 0$ on $K$. Then the $\omega$-limit set of $\gamma$ is nonempty and contained in the largest invariant subset $M$ of $\{x \in K : DV(x)\,f(x) = 0\}$, and $\gamma(t)$ eventually lies in every open set containing $M$.
