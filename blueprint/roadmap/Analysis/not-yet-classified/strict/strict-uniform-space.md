# Strict uniformity on a multiplier algebra

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)`
its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and
`T a` in the norm of `A`. Its strict uniformity is generated by the seminorms `pₐ(T) = ‖a T‖ + ‖T
a‖`, for all coefficients `a ∈ A`.
