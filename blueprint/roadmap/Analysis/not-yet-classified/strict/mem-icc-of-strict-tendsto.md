# Positive contractions under strict limits

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)`
its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and
`T a` in the norm of `A`. If a multiplier-valued function converges strictly to `T₀` along a proper
filter and eventually satisfies `0 ≤ T ≤ 1`, then `0 ≤ T₀ ≤ 1` in spectral order.
