# Strict limit of a norm-bounded Cauchy filter

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and
`M(A)` its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a
T` and `T a` in the norm of `A`. A strictly Cauchy filter on `M(A)` with an eventual uniform norm
bound `‖T‖ ≤ C` has a unique strict limit.
