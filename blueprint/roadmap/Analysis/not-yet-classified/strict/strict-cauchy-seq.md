# Strict Cauchy sequence

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)` its multiplier
algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and `T a` in the
norm of `A`. A sequence `T : ℕ → M(A)` is strictly Cauchy when its image filter at infinity is
Cauchy for the coefficient-seminorm uniformity.
