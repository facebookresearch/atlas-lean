# Strict topology on a multiplier algebra

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)`
its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and
`T a` in the norm of `A`. The strict topology is the topology generated by the coefficient seminorms
`T ↦ ‖a T‖ + ‖T a‖`.
