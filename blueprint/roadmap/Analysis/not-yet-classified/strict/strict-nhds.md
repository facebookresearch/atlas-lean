# Strict neighborhoods of a multiplier

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)` its
multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and `T
a` in the norm of `A`. For `T ∈ M(A)`, this is its neighborhood filter in the topology generated by
all coefficient seminorms.
