# Positivity detected by coefficient compressions

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and
`M(A)` its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a
T` and `T a` in the norm of `A`. A self-adjoint multiplier `T` is positive exactly when `ι(a)* T
ι(a) ≥ 0` for every `a ∈ A`, in spectral order.
