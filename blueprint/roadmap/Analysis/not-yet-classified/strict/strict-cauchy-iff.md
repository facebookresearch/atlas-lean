# Coefficientwise characterization of strict Cauchy filters

Let `A` be a nonunital complex
C*-algebra and `M(A)` its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two
coefficient products `a T` and `T a` in the norm of `A`. A filter on `M(A)` is strictly Cauchy
exactly when both its left- and right-centralizer evaluation filters are norm-Cauchy for every
coefficient `a ∈ A`.
