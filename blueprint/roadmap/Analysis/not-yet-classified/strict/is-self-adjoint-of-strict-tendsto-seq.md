# Strict limit of self-adjoint multipliers

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)`
its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and
`T a` in the norm of `A`. A sequence of self-adjoint multipliers converging strictly to `T₀` has
self-adjoint limit.
