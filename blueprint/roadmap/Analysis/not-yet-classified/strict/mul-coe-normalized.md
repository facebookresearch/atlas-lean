# Left-centralizer formula for coefficient multiplication

Let `A` be a nonunital complex
C*-algebra and `M(A)` its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two
coefficient products `a T` and `T a` in the norm of `A`. For `T ∈ M(A)` and `a ∈ A`, the product `T
ι(a)` is the embedded coefficient `ι(T.fst(a))`.
