# Cauchy filter in the strict uniformity

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)` its
multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and `T
a` in the norm of `A`. A filter of multipliers is strictly Cauchy when it is a Cauchy filter for the
uniformity generated by all coefficient seminorms.
