# Strict limit of a bounded Cauchy sequence

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)`
its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and
`T a` in the norm of `A`. A strictly Cauchy sequence of multipliers with a common bound `‖Tₙ‖ ≤ C`
for every `n` has a unique strict limit.
