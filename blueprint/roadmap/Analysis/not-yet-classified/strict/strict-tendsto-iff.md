# Coefficientwise characterization of strict convergence

Let `A` be a nonunital complex
C*-algebra and `M(A)` its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two
coefficient products `a T` and `T a` in the norm of `A`. For a filter `l` on a set `X`, `f : X →
M(A)` converges strictly to `T` exactly when both `(f(x)).fst(a)` and `(f(x)).snd(a)` converge in
norm to `T.fst(a)` and `T.snd(a)` for every `a ∈ A`.
