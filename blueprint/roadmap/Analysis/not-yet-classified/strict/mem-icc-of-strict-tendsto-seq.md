# Strict limit of positive contractive multipliers

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and
`M(A)` its multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a
T` and `T a` in the norm of `A`. A sequence with `0 ≤ Tₙ ≤ 1` in spectral order, converging strictly
to `T₀`, satisfies `0 ≤ T₀ ≤ 1`.
