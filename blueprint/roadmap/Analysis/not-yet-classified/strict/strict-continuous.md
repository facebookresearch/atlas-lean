# Continuity into the strict topology

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)` its
multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and `T
a` in the norm of `A`. For a topological space `X`, a function `f : X → M(A)` is strictly continuous
when it is continuous for the strict topology on its target.
