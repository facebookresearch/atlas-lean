# Self-adjointness under strict limits

Let `A` be a nonunital complex C*-algebra and `M(A)` its
multiplier algebra. For `a ∈ A`, strict convergence tests the two coefficient products `a T` and `T
a` in the norm of `A`. If a multiplier-valued function converges strictly to `T₀` along a proper
filter and is eventually self-adjoint, then `T₀` is self-adjoint.
