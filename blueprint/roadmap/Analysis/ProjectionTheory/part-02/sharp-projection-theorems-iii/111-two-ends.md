# Lemma (two ends)

Suppose that $E$ is a well-spaced set in $B^1 \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ in the sense that $|E| \sim N$ and $|E \cap B(x, N^{-1/2})| \lesssim 1$. Suppose that $\delta \leq \rho \leq N^{-1/2}$. Suppose $T_\rho$ is a $\rho$-tube with $|T_\rho \cap E|_\rho \sim \tilde{R}$, and suppose that each $\delta$-tube $T \in \mathcal{T}_R(E, T_\rho)$ obeys the two ends condition. Then $|\mathcal{T}_R(E, T_\rho)| \lesssim \frac{\tilde{R}^2}{R^2}$.
