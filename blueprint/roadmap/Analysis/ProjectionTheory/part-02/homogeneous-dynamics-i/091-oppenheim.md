# Conjecture (Oppenheim)

If $n \geq 3$, the signature of $Q$ is mixed, and the coefficients of $Q$ are not contained in $\mathbb{Z} \alpha$ for any $\alpha$, then $Q(\mathbb{Z}^n)$ is dense in $\mathbb{R}$.
