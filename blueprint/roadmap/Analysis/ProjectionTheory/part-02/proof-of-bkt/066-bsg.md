# Theorem (BSG)

Let $X, A, B$ be subsets of an abelian group $(G, +)$. For $t \in G$, let $\pi_t \colon G \times G \to G$ denote the projection operator $(g_1, g_2) \mapsto g_1 + t g_2$. Assume that $|A|, |B| \leq N$, $X \subset A \times B$ and $|X| \geq K^{-1} N^2$, $|\pi_t X| \leq KN$ for some $t \in G$. Then there exist $A'' \subset A$, $B'' \subset B$ such that $|X''| \gtrsim K^{-O(1)} N^2$, $\pi_t(A'' \times B'') \leq K^{O(1)} N$, where $X'' = X \cap (A'' \times B'')$.
