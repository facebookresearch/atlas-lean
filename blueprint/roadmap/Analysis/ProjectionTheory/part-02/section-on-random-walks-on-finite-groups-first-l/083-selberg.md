# Theorem (Selberg)

There exists a universal constant $c > 0$ so that for every prime $p$, $\sigma_1(T_A) \leq 1 - c$, where $A = \left\{\begin{pmatrix} 1 & \pm 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ \pm 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\right\}$.
