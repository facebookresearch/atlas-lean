# Proposition (Section on Random walks on finite groups (first lecture))

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)$. Then $\sigma_1(T_\mu)^2 \frac{p+1}{2} \leq |\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)| \cdot \|\mu\|_{L^2}^2$. In particular, since $|\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)| \sim p^3$, this implies $\sigma_1(T_\mu) \lesssim p \|\mu\|_{L^2}$.
