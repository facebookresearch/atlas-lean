# Theorem (BSG var)

Let $A, B$ be subsets of $(G, +)$ with $|A|, |B| = N$ and $E(A, B) \geq K^{-1} N^3$. There exist $A' \subset A$, $B' \subset B$ with $|A'|, |B'| \geq K^{-O(1)} N$ such that $|A' + B'| \leq K^{O(1)} N$.
