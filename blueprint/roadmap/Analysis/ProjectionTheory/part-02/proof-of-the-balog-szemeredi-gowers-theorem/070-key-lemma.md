# Lemma (Key Lemma)

If $X \subseteq A \times B$ and $|X| \geq K^{-1} |A| |B|$, then there are $A' \subset A$ and $B' \subset B$ such that $|X'| \geq K^{-O(1)} |A| |B|$ where $X' = X \cap (A' \times B')$ and for any $a \in A'$, $b \in B'$, $P_3(a, b) \geq K^{-O(1)} |A| |B|$.
