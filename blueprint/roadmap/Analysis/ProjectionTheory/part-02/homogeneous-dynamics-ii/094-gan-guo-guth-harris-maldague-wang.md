# Theorem (Gan-Guo-Guth-Harris-Maldague-Wang)

If $X \subseteq B^3$ is a $(\delta, 2, C)$-set and $\gamma$ is non-degenerate, then $\operatorname{Avg}_{\theta \in \gamma} |\pi_\theta(X)|_\delta \geq C_\epsilon \delta^{-2 + \epsilon}$ for any $\epsilon > 0$.
