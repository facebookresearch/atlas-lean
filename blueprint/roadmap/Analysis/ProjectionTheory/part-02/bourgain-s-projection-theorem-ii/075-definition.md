# Definition (Section: Bourgain's Projection Theorem II)

Given $\Delta \in 1/\mathbb{N}$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, a set $X \subset [0,1]^d$ is $(\Delta, m)$-uniform if for any $j \in \{0, \ldots, m-1\}$ and for any cube $Q \in \mathcal{D}_\delta(X)$ we have $$|Q \cap X|^*_{\Delta^{j+1}} = R_j$$ where $R_j$ is independent of $Q$. The numbers $R_j$ are called branching factors of $X$.
