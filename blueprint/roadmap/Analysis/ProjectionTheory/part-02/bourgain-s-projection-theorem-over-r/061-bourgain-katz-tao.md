# Theorem (Bourgain-Katz-Tao) (Section: Bourgain's projection theorem over R, part 1 (Finite field case))

Let $0 < t < 2$, $0 < s \leq 1$, and $p$ be a prime. Then there exists some $\varepsilon = \varepsilon(s, t) > 0$ such that for all $X \subset \mathbb{F}_p^2$ with $|X| = p^t$ and all $D \subset \mathbb{F}_p$ with $|D| = p^s$, we have $\max_{\theta \in D} |\pi_\theta X| \geq p^{t/2 + \varepsilon}$ and $\max_{\theta \in D} \min_{Y \subset X,\, |Y| \geq p^{-\varepsilon} |X|} |\pi_\theta Y| \geq p^{t/2 + \varepsilon}$.
