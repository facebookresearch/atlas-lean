# Definition (Sharp Projection Theorems II: AD Regular Case, Section on General AD vs Projective AD)

We say $(E, \mathbb{T})$ is projective if $\operatorname{Dir}(\mathbb{T}_{x_1}) = \operatorname{Dir}(\mathbb{T}_{x_2})$ for any $x_1, x_2 \in E$.
