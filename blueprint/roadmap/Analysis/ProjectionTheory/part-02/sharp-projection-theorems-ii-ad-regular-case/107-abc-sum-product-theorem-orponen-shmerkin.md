# Theorem (ABC sum product theorem, Orponen-Shmerkin)

Under the hypotheses that $A$ is a $(\rho, a)$-set with $|A| \sim \rho^{-a}$, $B$ is a $(\rho, b)$-set with $|B| \sim \rho^{-b}$, $C$ is a $(\rho, c)$-set with $|C| \sim \rho^{-c}$, and for any $c \in C$, $|A + cB|_\rho \lessapprox |A|_\rho$, then $a \geq b + c$.
