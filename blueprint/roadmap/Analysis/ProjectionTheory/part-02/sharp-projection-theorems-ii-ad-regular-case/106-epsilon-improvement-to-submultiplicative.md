# Lemma ($\epsilon$-improvement to submultiplicative lemma)

Fix $s, t$. For every $\alpha > 0$ there is some $\epsilon > 0$ so that either $R_{AD, \mathrm{proj}}(\delta^{1/2}) \lesssim \delta^{-\alpha} \cdot \mathrm{RHS}$, or $R_{AD, \mathrm{proj}}(\delta) \lesssim \delta^\epsilon R_{AD, \mathrm{proj}}(\delta^{1/2})^2$.
