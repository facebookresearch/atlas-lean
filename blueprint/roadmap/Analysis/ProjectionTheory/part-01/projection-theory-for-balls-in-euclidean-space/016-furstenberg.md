# Conjecture (Furstenberg)

If SETUP, $X$ and $D$ have Hausdorff spacing, and $$S \leq R^{-\epsilon} \min(R, |X|),$$ then $$|D| \lessapprox \frac{|S|^2}{|R|}.$$
