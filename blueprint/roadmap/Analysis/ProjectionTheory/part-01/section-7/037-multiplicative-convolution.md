# Definition (Multiplicative Convolution)

If $f, g \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$, then their multiplicative convolution is the function $$f *_M g(n) = \sum_{\substack{n_1, n_2 \\ n_1 n_2 = n}} f(n_1) g(n_2).$$
