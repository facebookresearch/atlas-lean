# Theorem (Renyi, Bombieri--Vinogradov)

For all $\epsilon > 0$ and all $A$, $$\sum_{q \leq N^{1/2 - \epsilon}} \Delta_q(N) \leq C(\epsilon, A) N (\log N)^{-A}.$$
