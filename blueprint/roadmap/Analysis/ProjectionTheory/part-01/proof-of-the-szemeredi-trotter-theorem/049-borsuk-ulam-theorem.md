# Theorem (Borsuk Ulam Theorem)

Let $f \colon S^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a continuous function that is antipodal, i.e., for every $\theta \in S^n$, $f(\theta) = -f(-\theta)$. Then $0$ is in the image of $f$.
