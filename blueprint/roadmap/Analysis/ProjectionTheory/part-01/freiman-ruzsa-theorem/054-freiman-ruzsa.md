# Theorem (Freiman-Ruzsa)

If $A \subset \mathbb{Z}$ and $|A + A| \leq K|A|$, then $A$ is contained in a GAP of dimension $r(K)$ and $\operatorname{vol} \leq V(K) \cdot |A|$.
