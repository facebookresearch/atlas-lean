# Lemma (Double Counting)

Suppose $X \subseteq \mathbb{F}_p^2$ and $D \subseteq \mathbb{F}_p$. Then $\max_{t \in D} |\pi_t(X)| \gtrsim \min(|X|, |D|)$.
