# Theorem 6.1

If $f \in L^2(\mathbb{R}^d)$, $\operatorname{supp}(f) \subset B_1$, then provided that $\frac{d-1}{2} > \frac{1}{2} + k$, it holds $$\int_{\mathbb{S}^{d-1}} \|\pi_\theta f\|_{C^k}^2 \operatorname{d}\!\theta \lesssim \|f\|_{L^2}^2.$$
