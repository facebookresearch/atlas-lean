# Lemma (Main Lemma 2F)

If $\mathcal{L}$ is a collection of lines in $\mathbb{F}_q^2$, and $L(x)$ is the characteristic function for $L \in \mathcal{L}$, then we can decompose $f(x) = \sum_{L \in \mathcal{L}} L(x)$ as $f = f_0 + f_h$, where $f_0 = \frac{|\mathcal{L}|}{q}$, $f_0$ is orthogonal to $f_h$, and $\|f_0\|^2_{L^2} \lesssim |\mathcal{L}|^2$, $\|f_h\|^2_{L^2} \lesssim |\mathcal{L}| q$.
