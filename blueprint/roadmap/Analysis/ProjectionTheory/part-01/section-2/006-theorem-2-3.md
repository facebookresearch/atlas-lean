# Theorem 2.3

(Orthogonality/Fourier) Suppose $X \subset \mathbb{F}_q^2$, $D \subset \mathbb{F}_q$, and $S = \max_{\theta \in D} |\pi_\theta(X)|$. If $S \leq q/2$, then $$|D| \lesssim \frac{S q}{|X|}.$$
