# Theorem 2.2

(Double counting) Suppose $X \subset \mathbb{F}_q^2$, $D \subset \mathbb{F}_q$, and $S = \max_{\theta \in D} |\pi_\theta(X)|$. If $S \leq |X|/2$, then $$|D| \lesssim S.$$
