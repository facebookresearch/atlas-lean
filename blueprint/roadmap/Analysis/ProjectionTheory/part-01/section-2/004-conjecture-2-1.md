# Conjecture 2.1

Suppose $X \subset \mathbb{F}_p^2$, $D \subset \mathbb{F}_p$, and $S = \max_{\theta \in D} |\pi_\theta(X)|$. If $S \leq p/2$, then $$|D| \lesssim \frac{S^2}{|X|}.$$
