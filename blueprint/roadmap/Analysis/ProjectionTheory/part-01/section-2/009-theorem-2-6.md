# Theorem 2.6

If $f, g \colon \mathbb{F}_q^d \to \mathbb{C}$, then $$\sum_{x \in \mathbb{F}_q^d} f(x) \overline{g(x)} = \frac{1}{q^d} \sum_{\xi \in \mathbb{F}_q^d} \hat{f}(\xi) \overline{\hat{g}(\xi)}.$$
