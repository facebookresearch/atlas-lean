# Lemma 2.7

If $P(x)$ is the characteristic function of an affine $k$-plane in $\mathbb{F}_q^d$, then $$|\hat{P}(\xi)| = \begin{cases} q^k & \xi \in P^\perp \\ 0 & \xi \notin P^\perp \end{cases}.$$
