# Lemma (Section: The large sieve, Subsection: Proof of Linnik's Large Sieve inequality)

If $\frac{\alpha_1}{p_1}, \frac{\alpha_2}{p_2} \in Q_M$ are not equal, then $\left|\frac{\alpha_1}{p_1} - \frac{\alpha_2}{p_2}\right| \geq \frac{1}{M^2}$.
