# Corollary (Section: The large sieve, Subsection: The Large Sieve)

If $A \subseteq [N]$ then $$\operatorname{Avg}_{p \in P_{N^{1/2}}} \operatorname{Avg}_{a \in \mathbb{Z}_p} \left| \pi_p \mathbf{1}_A(a) - \frac{|A|}{p} \right| \lessapprox N^{1/4}.$$
