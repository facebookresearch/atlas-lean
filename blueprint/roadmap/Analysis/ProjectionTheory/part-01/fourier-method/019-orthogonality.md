# Lemma (Orthogonality)

If $T_1, T_2$ are $1 \times R$ tubes, then $|\langle \phi_{T_1, r}, \phi_{T_2, r} \rangle| \lesssim R^{-1000}$ unless there exists $\tilde{T}$, an $R^\epsilon r \times R^{1+\epsilon}$ rectangle, such that $T_1, T_2 \in \tilde{T}$.
