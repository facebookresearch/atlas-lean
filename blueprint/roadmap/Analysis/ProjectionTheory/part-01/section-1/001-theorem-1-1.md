# Theorem 1.1

(Szemeredi--Trotter 1982) If $X$ is a finite subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$, and $S < \frac{1}{2} |X|$, then $$|E_S(X)| \leq C S^2 |X|^{-1} + 1.$$
