# Theorem 1.3

(Orponen--Shmerkin--Ren--Wang) If $X \subset \mathbb{R}^2$, and $s < \operatorname{HD}(X)$, then $$\operatorname{HD}(E_s(X)) \leq \max(2s - \operatorname{HD}(X), 0).$$
