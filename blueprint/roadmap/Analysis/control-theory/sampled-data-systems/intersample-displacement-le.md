# Intersample displacement enclosure

Let $E$ be a normed abelian group, written additively, $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to E$, $\hat x \in E$ and $t_s, h, \bar v, \varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\bar v \ge 0$. Suppose $\|\gamma(t) - \gamma(t_s)\| \le \bar v\,(t - t_s)$ for every $t \in [t_s, t_s + h]$ and $\|\gamma(t_s) - \hat x\| \le \varepsilon$. Then $\|\gamma(t) - \hat x\| \le \varepsilon + \bar v\,h$ for every $t \in [t_s, t_s + h]$. The motion bound is a hypothesis on the trajectory $\gamma$, not a consequence of agreement at the sample time.
