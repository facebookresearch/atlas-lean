# Growth bound for discrete-time trajectories

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field, $X$ and $U$ normed spaces over $\mathbb{k}$, $A : X \to X$ and $B : U \to X$ bounded linear maps, $u : \mathbb{N} \to U$ and $x_0 \in X$, and let $x_0, x_1, \dots$ be the trajectory $x_{k+1} = Ax_k + Bu_k$. Let $M, K, \rho, r \ge 0$ and $q \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\rho < q$ and $r < q$. If $\|A^k x\| \le M\rho^k\|x\|$ for all $k$ and $x$, and $\|u_k\| \le K r^k$ for all $k$, then for every $k$,

$$\|x_k\| \le M\Big(\|x_0\| + \frac{\|B\|\,K}{q}\Big)(1 + k)\,q^k.$$

The factor $1 + k$ covers the case where the two rates are equal.
