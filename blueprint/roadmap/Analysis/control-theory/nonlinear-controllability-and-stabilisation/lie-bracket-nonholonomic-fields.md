# The bracket of the nonholonomic integrator

At every $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$, $[f_1, f_2](x) = Df_2(x) f_1(x) - Df_1(x) f_2(x) = (0, 0, 2)$ (Coron, *Control and Nonlinearity*, AMS 2007, Example 3.20, with his bracket convention of Definition 3.10). Both fields are affine, so their derivatives are constant: $Df_2(x) v = (0, 0, v_1)$ and $Df_1(x) v = (0, 0, -v_2)$.
