# The nonholonomic integrator as a driftless system

The nonholonomic integrator is the driftless control-affine system $f(x, u) = u_1 f_1(x) + u_2 f_2(x)$ defined by its two vector fields (Coron, *Control and Nonlinearity*, AMS 2007, (3.41) and Example 3.20).
