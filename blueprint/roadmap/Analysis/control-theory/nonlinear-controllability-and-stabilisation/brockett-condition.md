# Brockett's condition

A map $f : E \times U \to E$ satisfies **Brockett's condition** if the image under $f$ of every neighbourhood of $(0, 0)$ is a neighbourhood of $0$ in $E$ (Coron, *Control and Nonlinearity*, AMS 2007, Theorem 11.1, after Brockett 1983). When $E$ and $U$ are finite-dimensional and $f$ is smooth with $f(0, 0) = 0$, this is necessary for local asymptotic stabilisation of $\dot x = f(x, u)$ by continuous stationary feedback ([Brockett's theorem](brockett-condition-of-is-locally-asympto.md)).
