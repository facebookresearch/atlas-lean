# Iterated Lie brackets of a family of vector fields

For vector fields $f_1, \dots, f_m$ on a real normed space $E$, the set of **iterated Lie brackets** is the smallest set of vector fields containing every $f_i$ and closed under the Lie bracket

$$[X, Y](x) = DY(x) \, X(x) - DX(x) \, Y(x).$$

Here $D$ is the Fréchet derivative, taken to be $0$ at a point where it does not exist. This is the sign convention of Coron, *Control and Nonlinearity* (AMS 2007), Definition 3.10. For smooth fields, the linear span of the iterated brackets is the Lie algebra $\operatorname{Lie}(\{f_1, \dots, f_m\})$ generated by the fields (Coron, Definition 3.13), since by bilinearity every element of that Lie algebra is a linear combination of iterated brackets.
