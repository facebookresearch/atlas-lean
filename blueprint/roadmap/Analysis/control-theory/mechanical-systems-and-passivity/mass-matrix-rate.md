# Rate of change of a mass matrix along a velocity

For a finite index set $\iota$, a derivative tensor $D : \iota^3 \to \mathbb{R}$ (with $D_{ijk}$ the derivative of $M_{ij}$ along coordinate $k$) and a velocity $v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, the mass-matrix rate is $\dot M(v)_{ij} = \sum_k D_{ijk}\,v_k$.
