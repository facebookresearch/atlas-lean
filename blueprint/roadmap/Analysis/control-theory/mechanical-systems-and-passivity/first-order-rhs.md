# First-order form of the mass equation

For a real $n \times n$ mass matrix $M$, a velocity $v$ and a net force $F$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$, the first-order right-hand side of the mass equation $M\ddot q = F$ is the pair $(v, M^{-1}F)$ of velocity and acceleration, where the inverse of a singular matrix is taken to be $0$.
