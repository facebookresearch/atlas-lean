# Homogeneous coordinates distinguish points from vectors

For a finite index set $m$, a real $m \times m$ matrix $R$, $p, q \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $w \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\begin{pmatrix} R & p \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} q \\ w \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} Rq + wp \\ w \end{pmatrix}.$$

At $w = 1$ this is the point action $q \mapsto Rq + p$; at $w = 0$ the translation drops out and $R$ acts on vectors.
