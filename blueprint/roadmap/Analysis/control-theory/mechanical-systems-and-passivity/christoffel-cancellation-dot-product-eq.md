# Power cancellation for the Christoffel correction

For a finite index set $\iota$, a derivative tensor $D : \iota^3 \to \mathbb{R}$ and $v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, let $\dot M(v)_{ij} = \sum_k D_{ijk}v_k$ be the [mass-matrix rate](mass-matrix-rate.md) and $C(v)_{ij} = \sum_k \Gamma_{ijk}v_k$ the [Christoffel correction matrix](christoffel-matrix.md). Then

$$v^\top\big(\dot M(v) - 2\,C(v)\big)\,v = 0.$$

No symmetry of $D$ is assumed.
