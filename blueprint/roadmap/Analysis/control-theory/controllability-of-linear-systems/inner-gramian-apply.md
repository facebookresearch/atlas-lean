# Quadratic form of the Gramian

For every $\eta \in E$,

$$\langle \eta, W_T \eta \rangle = \int_0^T \lVert B^* e^{sA^*} \eta \rVert^2 \, ds.$$

In particular $\langle \eta, W_T \eta \rangle \ge 0$ for $T \ge 0$. This is Coron, *Control and Nonlinearity* (AMS 2007), Remark 1.12, for time-invariant systems.
