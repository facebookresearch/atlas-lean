# Soundness of the discrete dynamic-logic fragment of dL

Consider the hybrid programs without differential equations, generated by assignment $x := e$, test $?Q$, sequential
composition $\alpha;\beta$, choice $\alpha \cup \beta$ and iteration $\alpha^*$, with formulas of first-order real arithmetic extended by
box modalities and the relational semantics on real-valued states. The axioms $[x := e]P \leftrightarrow P[e/x]$ (for admissible
substitutions, which introduce no capture), $[?Q]P \leftrightarrow (Q \to P)$, $[\alpha;\beta]P \leftrightarrow [\alpha][\beta]P$,
$[\alpha \cup \beta]P \leftrightarrow [\alpha]P \wedge [\beta]P$ and $[\alpha^*]P \to P$, and the loop induction rule (from $J \to [\alpha]J$
infer $J \to [\alpha^*]J$), are sound: every axiom instance is valid and the rule preserves validity.
