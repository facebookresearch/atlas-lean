# Neumann-series inverses of prefix-causal operators are prefix causal

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field, $X$ a normed space over $\mathbb{k}$, and $(P_t)_{t \in I}$ a family of bounded linear maps $X \to X$ with $P_t \circ P_t = P_t$ for every $t$. Assume moreover that $X$ is complete. If $G$ is prefix causal ($P_t \circ G = P_t \circ G \circ P_t$ for every $t$) and $\|G\| < 1$, then the Neumann series $\sum_{n \ge 0} G^n$, the inverse of $1 - G$, is prefix causal.
