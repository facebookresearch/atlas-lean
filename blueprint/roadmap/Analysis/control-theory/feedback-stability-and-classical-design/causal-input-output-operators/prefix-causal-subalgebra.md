# Prefix-causal operators form a unital subalgebra

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field, $X$ a normed space over $\mathbb{k}$, and $(P_t)_{t \in I}$ a family of bounded linear maps $X \to X$ with $P_t \circ P_t = P_t$ for every $t$. The prefix-causal operators, those $G$ with $P_t \circ G = P_t \circ G \circ P_t$ for every $t$, form a unital subalgebra of the algebra of bounded linear operators on $X$. Only the idempotence of each $P_t$ is used.
