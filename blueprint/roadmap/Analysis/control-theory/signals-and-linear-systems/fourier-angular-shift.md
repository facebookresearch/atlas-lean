# Time-shift rule for the angular Fourier transform

For an integrable $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ and $a, \omega \in \mathbb{R}$, the angular-frequency transform $\int e^{-i\omega t} f(t)\,dt$ of the delayed signal $t \mapsto f(t - a)$ at $\omega$ is $e^{-ia\omega}$ times the transform of $f$ at $\omega$.
