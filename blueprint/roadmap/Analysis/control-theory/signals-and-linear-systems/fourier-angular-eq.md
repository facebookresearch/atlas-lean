# Angular and hertz Fourier transforms agree

For an integrable $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ and every $\omega \in \mathbb{R}$, the angular-frequency transform $\int e^{-i\omega t} f(t)\,dt$ equals the hertz-convention Fourier transform $\hat f(\xi) = \int e^{-2\pi i t\xi} f(t)\,dt$ at $\xi = \omega/(2\pi)$.
