# Prefix-causal operators form a closed set

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field, $X$ a normed space over $\mathbb{k}$, and $(P_t)_{t \in I}$ a family of bounded linear maps $X \to X$ with $P_t \circ P_t = P_t$ for every $t$. Then the set of prefix-causal operators, those $G$ with $P_t \circ G = P_t \circ G \circ P_t$ for every $t$, is closed in the operator norm.
