# The information matrix is positive definite

If $P_0$ is positive definite, then $P_0^{-1} + \sum_{k<t} \varphi_k \varphi_k^\top$ is positive definite for every
regressor sequence and every $t$: it is a positive definite matrix plus a sum of positive semidefinite rank-one
matrices.
