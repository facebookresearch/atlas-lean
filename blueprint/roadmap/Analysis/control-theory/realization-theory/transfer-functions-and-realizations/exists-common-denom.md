# Common-denominator form of a proper rational matrix

Over a field $\mathbb{k}$, every proper rational matrix $G$ with finitely many rows and columns (every entry has numerator degree at most denominator degree) can be written $G = D + N/d$ with a constant matrix $D$, a polynomial matrix $N$ and a single monic polynomial $d$ whose degree strictly exceeds the degree of every entry of $N$ (the zero polynomial is allowed as an entry of $N$).
