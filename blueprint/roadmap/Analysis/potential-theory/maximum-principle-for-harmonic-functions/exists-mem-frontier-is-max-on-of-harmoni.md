# Weak maximum principle for harmonic functions

Let $U$ be a nonempty bounded open subset of a nontrivial finite-dimensional real inner product
space, and let $u$ be harmonic on $U$ and continuous
on $\overline U$. Then $u$ attains its maximum over $\overline U$ at a point of $\partial U$.

This is the weak form. Schmidt deduces it from the strong maximum principle; here it is the case
$\Delta u = 0$ of the subharmonic version, which does not use the mean-value property.
