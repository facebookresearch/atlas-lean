# The function 1/|x| is harmonic on ℝ³ away from the origin

Let $\Delta = \partial_1^2 + \partial_2^2 + \partial_3^2$ be the Laplacian on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^3$. For every $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$ with $x \neq 0$,

$$\Delta \bigl(y \mapsto |y|^{-1}\bigr)(x) = 0,$$

where $|y|$ is the Euclidean norm. The value at $y = 0$, taken to be $0$, plays no role, since the Laplacian at $x \neq 0$ depends only on the function near $x$. This is the case $m = 3$ of [the harmonicity of the Newtonian kernel](harmonic-on-nhd-one-div-norm-pow-sub-two.md).
