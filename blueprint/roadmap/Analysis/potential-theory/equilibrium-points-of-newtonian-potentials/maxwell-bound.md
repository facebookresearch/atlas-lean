# Maxwell's bound on the number of equilibrium points

Maxwell's bound (Eremenko, Question 2 on equilibrium points of Newtonian potentials): for `n`
positive charges at distinct points of `ℝ³`, a finite equilibrium set of `u` has at most `(n - 1)²`
points.

Its resolution: [Positive point charges with more equilibrium points than Maxwell’s bound allows](maxwell-bound-refuted.md).
