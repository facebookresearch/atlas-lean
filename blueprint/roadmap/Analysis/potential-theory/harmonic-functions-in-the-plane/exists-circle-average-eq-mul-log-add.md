# Circle averages of harmonic functions on an annulus

Let $u : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{R}$ be harmonic on a neighbourhood of the closed annulus $r_1 \le |z - z_0| \le r_2$, with $0 < r_1$. Then there exist real $\alpha$ and $\beta$ such that for every $r \in [r_1, r_2]$ the circle average of $u$ about $z_0$ of radius $r$ is $\alpha \log r + \beta$: one pair for the whole annulus.
