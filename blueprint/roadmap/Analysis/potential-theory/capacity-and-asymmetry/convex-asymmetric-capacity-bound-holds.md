# Hall–Hayman–Weitsman bound on capacity and asymmetry

Hall, Hayman and Weitsman obtain `cap E ≥ C (1 +
k δ^\{N+1\})`, with `k > 0` depending only on the dimension, for every convex set `E` whose
same-volume balls all have at least the proportion `δ` of their volume outside `E`.

## Sources

- W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 3.18 (E. Fraenkel); resolution: R. R. Hall, W. K. Hayman and A. W. Weitsman, On asymmetry and capacity, J. Analyse Math. 56 (1991), 87–123, DOI 10.1007/BF02820461, as recorded in Hayman–Lingham, Update 3.18: 'Hall, Hayman and Weitsman obtain for general sets if n = 2, and for convex sets if n ≥ 3, cap E ≥ C(1 + kδ^\{n+1\}), where k is a constant depending on the dimension n', https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07200.
