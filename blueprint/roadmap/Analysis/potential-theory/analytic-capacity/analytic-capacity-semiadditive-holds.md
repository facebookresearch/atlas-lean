# Tolsa's theorem on the semiadditivity of analytic capacity

This settles [Semiadditivity of analytic capacity](analytic-capacity-semiadditive.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Tolsa proves that analytic capacity is semiadditive, `γ(E ∪ F) ≤ C (γ(E) + γ(F))` for
compact `E, F ⊆ ℂ` with an absolute constant `C`; Hayman and Lingham record (Update 7.39) that the
problem has been proved by Tolsa.

## Sources

- W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory, Fiftieth Anniversary Edition (2019), arXiv:1809.07200, Problem 7.39 (J. Korevaar); resolution: X. Tolsa, Painlevé's problem and the semiadditivity of analytic capacity, Acta Math. 190 (2003), 105-149; arXiv:math/0204027, abstract: γ(E) ≈ γ₊(E) with constants independent of E, and as a corollary γ is semiadditive; Hayman-Lingham, Update 7.39: 'This has been proved by Tolsa', https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02393237.
