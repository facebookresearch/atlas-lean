# Analysis

Analysis studies limits, approximation, continuity, and infinite-dimensional spaces of functions and operators.

Operator theory is the largest field, with a quarter of the subject's articles. Its
largest part, shift, Toeplitz and Hankel operators, starts from the
unilateral shift on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$, whose
spectrum is the closed unit disc and whose Fredholm index is $-1$. Concrete operators such as the
Cesàro operator, bounded on $\ell^2$ but not
compact, sit beside general theory: the
spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators,
the homotopy invariance of the Fredholm index
and the Courant–Fischer min–max theorem.

Complex analysis runs from Cauchy theory and
the great Picard theorem through the
Riemann mapping theorem to the Hardy,
Bergman, Dirichlet and Fock spaces of analytic functions.
Harmonic analysis covers Fourier series,
with Fejér's theorem on the uniform
convergence of the Cesàro means of continuous periodic functions,
Fourier interpolation of
radial Schwartz functions in dimensions $8$ and $24$, and
discrete Fourier analysis on finite abelian groups and the
Boolean cube, and wavelets, from the Haar basis and conjugate mirror filters.

Banach spaces are studied through
duality and weak topologies, from the Hahn–Banach and
Banach–Alaoglu theorems to
James' reflexivity theorem, and
through the classical sequence spaces $\ell^p$ and $c_0$. The rest of the
subject includes [special functions](special-functions/README.md) such as the gamma function and polylogarithms;
[real functions](real-functions/README.md), from nowhere-differentiable functions to the Hyers–Ulam stability of
additive maps; [sequences and series](sequences-and-series/README.md), with Tauberian theorems and series for
constants such as Catalan's constant; and numerical analysis, from Newton's method to
Céa's lemma for Galerkin approximation.
Control theory proves the Kalman rank condition, the Hautus test and the pole-shifting
theorem for linear systems, and records the Rashevskii–Chow theorem and Brockett's obstruction to continuous feedback
stabilisation for nonlinear ones.
