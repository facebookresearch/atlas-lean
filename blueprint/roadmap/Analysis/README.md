# Analysis

Analysis studies limits, approximation, continuity, and infinite-dimensional spaces of functions and operators.

[Operator theory](operator-theory/README.md) is the largest field, with a quarter of the subject's articles. Its
largest part, [shift, Toeplitz and Hankel operators](operator-theory/shift-toeplitz-hankel/README.md), starts from the
[unilateral shift](operator-theory/shift-toeplitz-hankel/unilateral-shift/README.md) on $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$, whose
spectrum is the closed unit disc and whose Fredholm index is $-1$. Concrete operators such as the
[Cesàro operator](operator-theory/integral-and-multiplication/ces-ro-operator/README.md), bounded on $\ell^2$ but not
compact, sit beside general theory: the
[spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators](operator-theory/spectral-theory/spectral-theorem-for-compact-self-adjoint-operat/README.md),
the [homotopy invariance of the Fredholm index](operator-theory/compact-and-fredholm/homotopy-invariance-of-the-fredholm-index/README.md)
and the [Courant–Fischer min–max theorem](operator-theory/eigenvalue-inequalities/courant-fischer-min-max-theorem/README.md).

[Complex analysis](complex-analysis/README.md) runs from [Cauchy theory](complex-analysis/cauchy-theory/README.md) and
the [great Picard theorem](complex-analysis/cauchy-theory/great-picard-theorem/README.md) through the
[Riemann mapping theorem](complex-analysis/geometric-function-theory/riemann-mapping-theorem/README.md) to the Hardy,
Bergman, Dirichlet and Fock [spaces of analytic functions](complex-analysis/analytic-function-spaces/README.md).
[Harmonic analysis](harmonic-analysis/README.md) covers [Fourier series](harmonic-analysis/fourier-series/README.md),
with [Fejér's theorem](harmonic-analysis/fourier-series/fej-r-kernel-and-fej-r-s-theorem/README.md) on the uniform
convergence of the Cesàro means of continuous periodic functions,
[Fourier interpolation](harmonic-analysis/fourier-transform/fourier-interpolation-in-dimensions-8-and-24/README.md) of
radial Schwartz functions in dimensions $8$ and $24$, and
[discrete Fourier analysis](harmonic-analysis/discrete-fourier-analysis/README.md) on finite abelian groups and the
Boolean cube.

[Banach spaces](banach-spaces/README.md) are studied through
[duality and weak topologies](banach-spaces/duality-and-weak-topologies/README.md), from the Hahn–Banach and
Banach–Alaoglu theorems to
[James' reflexivity theorem](banach-spaces/duality-and-weak-topologies/james-reflexivity-theorem/README.md), and
through the [classical sequence spaces](banach-spaces/sequence-spaces/README.md) $\ell^p$ and $c_0$. The rest of the
subject includes [special functions](special-functions/README.md) such as the gamma function and polylogarithms;
[real functions](real-functions/README.md), from nowhere-differentiable functions to the Hyers–Ulam stability of
additive maps; [sequences and series](sequences-and-series/README.md), with Tauberian theorems and series for
constants such as Catalan's constant; and [numerical analysis](numerical-analysis/README.md), from Newton's method to
[Céa's lemma](numerical-analysis/c-a-s-lemma/README.md) for Galerkin approximation.
