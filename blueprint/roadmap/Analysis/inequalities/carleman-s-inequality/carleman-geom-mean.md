# Geometric mean of the initial terms of a sequence

For a real sequence $(a_k)_{k \ge 0}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $G_n = (a_0 a_1 \cdots a_n)^{1/(n+1)}$, the geometric
mean of the first $n + 1$ terms. For nonnegative terms this is the usual geometric mean; for a negative product the
real power follows the convention $x^y = |x|^y \cos(\pi y)$.
