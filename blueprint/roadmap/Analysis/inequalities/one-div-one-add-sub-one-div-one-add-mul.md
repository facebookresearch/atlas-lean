# A bound on a difference of reciprocals

For all real numbers $a > 1$ and $x \ge 1$,

$$
\frac{1}{1+x}-\frac{1}{1+ax}\le \frac{\sqrt{a}-1}{\sqrt{a}+1}.
$$
