# M

Pazuki's upper constant
$M(r, g) = \tfrac{g}{4} \log(4\pi) + g \log r + \tfrac{g}{2} \log\bigl(2 + \tfrac{2}{3^{1/4}}
2^{g^3/4}\bigr)$ of Theorem 1.1.

## Sources

- F. Pazuki, *Theta height and Faltings height*, Bull. Soc. Math. France 140 (2012), 19–49, <https://doi.org/10.24033/bsmf.2623>, Theorem 1.1, p. 21.
