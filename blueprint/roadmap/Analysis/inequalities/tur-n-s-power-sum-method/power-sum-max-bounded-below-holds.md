# Atkinson's lower bound for power sums

This settles [Erdős Problem 519 on Turán's power sums](power-sum-max-bounded-below.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Atkinson proved that `c = 1/6` works; Biró improved this to `1/2` and then to an
absolute constant above `1/2`.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #519, https://www.erdosproblems.com/519 (status PROVED (LEAN)); resolution: F. V. Atkinson, On sums of powers of complex numbers, Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. (1961), 185-188, erdosproblems.com/519: solved by Atkinson with c = 1/6; Biró later c = 1/2 and some c > 1/2, https://www.erdosproblems.com/519.
