# Tijdeman's theorem on runs of vanishing power sums

This settles [Erdős Problem 974 on runs of vanishing power sums](vanishing-power-sum-runs-configuration.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Already two runs of `n - 1` vanishing power sums force the configuration (the `n`-th
roots of unity for odd `n`, two regular `(n/2)`-gons inscribed in the unit circle for even `n`), as
proved by Tijdeman and, without assuming the `z_i` distinct, by Hu, Tang and Zhang;
erdosproblems.com/974 lists the problem as proved.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem \#974, https://www.erdosproblems.com/974 (status PROVED (LEAN)); resolution: R. Tijdeman, On a conjecture of Turán and Erdős, Indag. Math. (1966), 374-383, erdosproblems.com/974: true even with two such runs; for odd n the z_i are the n-th roots of unity, for even n the vertices of two regular (n/2)-gons with the same circumscribed circle centred at the origin; first proved by Tijdeman, with an independent proof by Hu, Tang and Zhang in the comments (whose Proposition 1 removes Tijdeman's assumption that the z_i are distinct), https://www.erdosproblems.com/974.
