# Landau's inequality $M_1 \le 2\sqrt{M_0 M_2}$

For $f \in C^2(\mathbb{R})$, let $M_k = \sup_x |f^{(k)}(x)|$.
Prove that $M_1 \le 2\sqrt{M_0 M_2}$.
