# Landau–Kolmogorov inequality, complex-valued case

Constant $4$. If $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ is twice continuously differentiable with $|f(x)| \le M_0$ and
$|f''(x)| \le M_2$ for all $x$, then $|f'(x)|^2 \le 4 M_0 M_2$ for every $x$. The constant $4$ comes from applying the
real inequality to the real and imaginary parts and adding.
