# Statement of a dyadic inequality for square-summable sequences

The statement that, if `∑_{n ≥ 1} ‖α n‖²` converges, then for every `k` the series
`βₖ = ∑_{l ≥ 0} α (2 ^ k * (2 * l + 1)) / (l + 1)` converges (absolutely, as `ℂ` is finite
dimensional), `∑ₖ ‖βₖ‖²` converges, and `∑_{n ≥ 1} ‖α n‖² ≥ 6 / π ^ 2 * ∑ₖ ‖βₖ‖²`.
