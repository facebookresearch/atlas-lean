# Wirtinger's inequality on an interval, with a continuous derivative within the interval

Let $a < b$ and let $f, f' : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, where $f'(x)$ is a derivative of $f$ within $[a, b]$ at every $x \in [a, b]$ and $f'$ is continuous on $[a, b]$. If $f(a) = f(b) = 0$, then

$$\int_a^b f(x)^2 \, dx \le \left(\frac{b - a}{\pi}\right)^2 \int_a^b f'(x)^2 \, dx.$$
