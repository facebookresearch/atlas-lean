# Kantorovich's inequality for symmetric operators

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$, and let $0 < m$ and $M$
be real numbers with $m\|x\|^2 \le \langle x, Tx \rangle \le M\|x\|^2$ for all $x \in E$. If $S$ is a two-sided inverse of $T$, then for every unit vector $x$,

$$
\langle x, Tx \rangle \, \langle x, Sx \rangle \le \frac{(M + m)^2}{4 m M}.
$$
