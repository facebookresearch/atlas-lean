# Eigenvalues lie within the Rayleigh quotient bounds

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ of dimension $n$, with orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_i)_{i < n}$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. If $m\|x\|^2 \le \langle x, Tx \rangle \le M\|x\|^2$ for all $x \in E$, then every eigenvalue satisfies $m \le \lambda_i \le M$.
