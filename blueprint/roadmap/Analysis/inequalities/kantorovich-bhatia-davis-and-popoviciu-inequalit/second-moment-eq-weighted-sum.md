# Quadratic form of the square of a symmetric operator as a weighted sum

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ of dimension $n$, with orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_i)_{i < n}$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. Then for every $x \in E$, $\langle x, T^2 x \rangle = \sum_{i < n} \langle e_i, x \rangle^2 \lambda_i^2$, a weighted sum of the squared eigenvalues with weights
$\langle e_i, x \rangle^2$ summing to $\|x\|^2$; for a unit vector $x$ it is a weighted average.
