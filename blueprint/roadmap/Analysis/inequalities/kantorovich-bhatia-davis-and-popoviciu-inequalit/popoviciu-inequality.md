# Popoviciu's inequality for symmetric operators

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$, and let $m, M$ be real
numbers with $m\|x\|^2 \le \langle x, Tx \rangle \le M\|x\|^2$ for all $x \in E$. Then for every unit vector $x$,

$$
\langle x, T^2 x \rangle - \langle x, Tx \rangle^2 \le \Bigl(\frac{M - m}{2}\Bigr)^2.
$$
