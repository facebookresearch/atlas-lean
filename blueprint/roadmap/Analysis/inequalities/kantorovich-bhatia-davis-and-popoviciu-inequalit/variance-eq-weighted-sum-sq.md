# Operator variance as weighted squared deviation of eigenvalues

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ of dimension $n$, with orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_i)_{i < n}$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. Then for every unit vector $x$, with $\mu = \langle x, Tx \rangle$,

$$
\langle x, T^2 x \rangle - \mu^2 = \sum_{i < n} \langle e_i, x \rangle^2 (\lambda_i - \mu)^2.
$$
