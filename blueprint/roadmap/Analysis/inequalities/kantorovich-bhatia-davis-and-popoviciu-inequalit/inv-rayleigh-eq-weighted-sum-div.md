# Quadratic form of the inverse as a weighted sum of reciprocal eigenvalues

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ of dimension $n$, with orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_i)_{i < n}$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. Suppose that $m\|x\|^2 \le \langle x, Tx \rangle \le M\|x\|^2$ for all $x \in E$ with $m > 0$, and that $S$ is a two-sided inverse of $T$. Then for
every $x \in E$,

$$
\langle x, Sx \rangle = \sum_{i < n} \frac{\langle e_i, x \rangle^2}{\lambda_i}.
$$
