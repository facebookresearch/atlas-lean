# Quadratic form of a symmetric operator as a weighted sum of eigenvalues

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ of dimension $n$, with orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_i)_{i < n}$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. Then for every $x \in E$, $\langle x, Tx \rangle = \sum_{i < n} \langle e_i, x \rangle^2 \lambda_i$: the
quadratic form is a weighted sum of the eigenvalues with weights $\langle e_i, x \rangle^2$, which sum to
$\|x\|^2$. For a unit vector $x$ the weights sum to $1$, and the Rayleigh quotient $\langle x, Tx \rangle$ is a weighted
average of the eigenvalues.
