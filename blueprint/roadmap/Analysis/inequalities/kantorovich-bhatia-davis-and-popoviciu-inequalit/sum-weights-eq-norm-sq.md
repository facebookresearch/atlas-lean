# Eigenbasis weights sum to the squared norm

Let $T$ be a symmetric linear operator on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ of dimension $n$, with orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_i)_{i < n}$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. Then for every $x \in E$, $\sum_{i < n} \langle e_i, x \rangle^2 = \|x\|^2$.
