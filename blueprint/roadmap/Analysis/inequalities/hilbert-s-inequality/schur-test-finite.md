# Schur test for finite bilinear forms

Let $N \in \mathbb{N}$, let $A_{mn} \ge 0$, $a_n \ge 0$, $b_n \ge 0$, let $r_n > 0$ and $c_n > 0$ be weights, and let
$\alpha, \beta \ge 0$. If $\sum_{n < N} A_{mn} r_n \le \alpha c_m$ for every $m$ and
$\sum_{m < N} A_{mn} c_m \le \beta r_n$ for every $n$, then

$$
\sum_{m < N} \sum_{n < N} A_{mn} a_m b_n \le \sqrt{\alpha \beta}\, \Bigl(\sum_{m < N} a_m^2\Bigr)^{1/2} \Bigl(\sum_{n < N} b_n^2\Bigr)^{1/2}.
$$
