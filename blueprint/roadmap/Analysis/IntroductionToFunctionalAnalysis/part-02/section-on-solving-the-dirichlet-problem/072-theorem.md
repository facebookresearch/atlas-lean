# Theorem (Section on solving the Dirichlet problem)

Let $V \in C([0, 1])$ be a nonnegative real-valued continuous function, and let $f \in C([0, 1])$. Then there exists a (unique) twice-differentiable solution $u \in C^2([0, 1])$ such that $-u'' + Vu = f$ for all $x \in [0, 1]$, with $u(0) = u(1) = 0$.
