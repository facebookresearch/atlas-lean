# Theorem (Spectral theorem)

Let $A = A^\ast$ be a self-adjoint compact operator on a separable Hilbert space $H$. If $|\lambda_1| \geq |\lambda_2| \geq \cdots$ are the nonzero eigenvalues of $A$, counted with multiplicity and with corresponding orthonormal eigenvectors $\{u_k\}_k$, then $\{u_k\}_k$ is an orthonormal basis for $\operatorname{Range}(A)$ and also of $\overline{\operatorname{Range}(A)}$, and there is an orthonormal basis $\{f_j\}_j$ of $\operatorname{Null}(A)$ so that $\{u_k\}_k \cup \{f_j\}_j$ form an orthonormal basis of $H$.
