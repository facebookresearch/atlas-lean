# Definition (May 4, 2021)

If $A \in \mathcal{B}(H)$ and $A - \lambda$ is not injective, then there exists some $u \in H \setminus \{0\}$ with $Au = \lambda u$, and we call $\lambda$ an eigenvalue of $A$ and $u$ the associated eigenvector.
