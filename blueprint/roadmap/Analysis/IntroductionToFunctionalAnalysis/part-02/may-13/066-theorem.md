# Theorem (May 13, 2021)

We have $\operatorname{Null}(A) = \{0\}$, and the orthonormal eigenvectors for $A$ are given by $u_k(x) = \sqrt{2} \sin(k \pi x)$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, with associated eigenvalues $\lambda_k = \frac{1}{k^2\pi^2}$.
