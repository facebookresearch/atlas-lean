# Theorem (Fatou's lemma)

Let $\{f_n\}_n$ be a sequence in $L^+(E)$. Then $\int_E \liminf_{n \to \infty} f_n(x) \leq \liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_E f_n(x)$.
