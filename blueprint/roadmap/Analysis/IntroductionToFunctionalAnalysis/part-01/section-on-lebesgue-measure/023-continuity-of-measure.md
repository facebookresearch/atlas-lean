# Theorem (Continuity of measure)

Suppose $\{E_k\}$ is a countable collection of measurable sets such that $E_1 \subset E_2 \subset \cdots$. Then $m\left(\bigcup_{k=1}^{\infty} E_k\right) = \lim_{n \to \infty} m\left(\bigcup_{k=1}^{n} E_k\right) = \lim_{n \to \infty} m(E_n)$.
