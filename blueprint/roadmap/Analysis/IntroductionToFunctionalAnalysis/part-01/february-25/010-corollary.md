# Corollary (February 25, 2021 lecture)

If $B_1, B_2$ are two Banach spaces, and $T \in \mathcal{B}(B_1, B_2)$ is a bijective map, then $T^{-1}$ is in $\mathcal{B}(B_2, B_1)$.
