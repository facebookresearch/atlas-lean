# Proposition (February 25, 2021 lecture)

If $B_1, B_2$ are Banach spaces, then $B_1 \times B_2$ (with operations done entry by entry) with norm $||(b_1, b_2)|| = ||b_1|| + ||b_2||$ is a Banach space.
