# Theorem (Closed Graph Theorem)

Let $B_1, B_2$ be two Banach spaces, and let $T: B_1 \to B_2$ be a (not necessarily bounded) linear operator. Then $T \in \mathcal{B}(B_1, B_2)$ if and only if the graph of $T$, defined as $\Gamma(T) = \{(u, Tu) \mid u \in B_1\}$, is closed in $B_1 \times B_2$.
