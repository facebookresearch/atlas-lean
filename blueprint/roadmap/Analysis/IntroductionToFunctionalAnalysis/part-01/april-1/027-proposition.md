# Proposition (April 1, 2021)

Suppose $f, g: E \to \mathbb{R}$ are measurable functions. Then we have the following: (1) If $f$ is integrable, then $\left|\int_E f \right| \leq \int_E |f|$. (2) If $g$ is integrable, and $f = g$ almost everywhere, then $f$ is integrable and $\int_E f = \int_E g$. (3) If $f, g$ are integrable and $f \leq g$ almost everywhere, then $\int_E f \leq \int_E g$.
