# Proposition (April 22, 2021)

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space, and let $W \subset H$ be a closed subspace. Then the map $\Pi_W: H \to H$ sending $v = w + w^\perp$ (for $w \in W, w^\perp \in W^\perp$) to $w$ is a projection operator.
