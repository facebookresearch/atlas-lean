# Theorem (Baire Category Theorem)

Let $M$ be a complete metric space, and let $\{C_n\}_n$ be a collection of closed subsets of $M$ such that $M = \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} C_n$. Then at least one of the $C_n$ contains an open ball $B(x, r) = \{y \in M \mid d(x, y) < r\}$. (In other words, at least one $C_n$ has an interior point.)
