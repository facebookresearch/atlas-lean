# Corollary (March 18, 2021)

Let $E \subset \mathbb{R}$ be measurable, and let $f_n: E \to [-\infty, \infty]$ be measurable for all $n$. If $\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x) = f(x)$, then $f$ is measurable.
