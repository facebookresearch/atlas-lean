# Definition (April 13, 2021)

Let $f \in L^2([-\pi, \pi])$. The $N$th Cesaro-Fourier mean of $f$ is $\sigma_N f(x) = \frac{1}{N+1} \sum_{k=0}^N S_k f(x)$.
