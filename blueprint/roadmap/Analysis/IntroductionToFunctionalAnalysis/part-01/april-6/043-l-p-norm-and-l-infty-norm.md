# Definition ($L^p$ norm and $L^\infty$ norm)

Let $f: E \to \mathbb{C}$ be a measurable function. For any $1 \leq p < \infty$, we define the $L^p$ norm $||f||_{L^p(E)} = \left(\int_E |f|^p\right)^{1/p}$. Furthermore, we define the $L^\infty$ norm or essential supremum of $f$ as $||f||_{L^\infty(E)} = \inf\{M > 0 \mid m(\{x \in E \mid |f(x)| > M \}) = 0\}$.
