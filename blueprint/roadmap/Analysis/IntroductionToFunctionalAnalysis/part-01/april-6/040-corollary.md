# Corollary (April 6, 2021 section)

If $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ is a measurable function, and there exists some $C \geq 0$ and $q > 1$ so that for almost every $x \in \mathbb{R}$, we have $|f(x)| \geq C(1 + |x|)^{-q}$, then $f \in L^p(\mathbb{R})$ for all $p \geq 1$.
