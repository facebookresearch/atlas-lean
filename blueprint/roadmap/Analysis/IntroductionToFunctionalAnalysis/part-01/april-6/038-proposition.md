# Proposition (April 6, 2021 section)

Let $a < b$ and $1 \leq p < \infty$ so that $f \in L^p([a, b])$, and take some $\varepsilon > 0$. Then there exists some $g \in C([a, b])$ such that $g(a) = g(b) = 0$, so that $||f-g||_p < \varepsilon$.
