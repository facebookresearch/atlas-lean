# Definition (February 18, 2021)

Let $V$ be a normed vector space (over $\mathbb{K}$). Then $V' = \mathcal{B}(V, \mathbb{K})$ is called the dual space of $V$, and because $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}$ are both complete, $V'$ is then a Banach space. An element of the dual space $\mathcal{B}(V, \mathbb{K})$ is called a functional.
