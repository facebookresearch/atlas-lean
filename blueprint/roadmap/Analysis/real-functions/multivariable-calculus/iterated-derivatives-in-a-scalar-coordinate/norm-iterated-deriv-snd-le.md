# Norm bound for a spatial derivative

For $n\in\mathbb N$ and a jointly $C^n$ real scalar function $q$ of two real variables,

$$
|\partial_x^n q(t,x)|\le\|D^n q(t,x)\|\qquad(t,x\in\mathbb R).
$$

The norm on the right is the multilinear operator norm for the product norm on $\mathbb R^2$. Evaluating in the unit direction $(0,1)$ introduces neither an additional multiplicative constant nor a factorial. Order zero is included.
