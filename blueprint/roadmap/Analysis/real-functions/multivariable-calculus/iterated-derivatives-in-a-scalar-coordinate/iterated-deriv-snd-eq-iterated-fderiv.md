# Spatial derivatives as directional Frechet derivatives

Let $n\in\mathbb N$ and let $q:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ be jointly $C^n$. Write $e_2=(0,1)$. At every $(t,x)\in\mathbb R^2$,

$$
\partial_x^n q(t,x)=D^n q(t,x)(e_2,\ldots,e_2).
$$

The left side is the $n$th iterated ordinary derivative of the function $x\mapsto q(t,x)$. The right side evaluates the continuous multilinear derivative on $n$ copies of the unit coordinate vector. This is an actual derivative, with no Taylor factorial, and includes order zero. The affine chain rule proves the identity.
