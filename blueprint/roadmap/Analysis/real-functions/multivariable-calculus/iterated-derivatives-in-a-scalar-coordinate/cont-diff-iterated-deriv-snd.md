# Joint smoothness of spatial derivatives

If $q:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ is jointly $C^\infty$, then for every $n\in\mathbb N$ the actual spatial derivative field

$$
(t,x)\longmapsto\partial_x^n q(t,x)
$$

is jointly $C^\infty$ on $\mathbb R^2$. The conclusion retains the dependence on both coordinates, not only smoothness of individual spatial slices. It follows by evaluating the smooth iterated Frechet derivative on a fixed tuple of coordinate vectors.
