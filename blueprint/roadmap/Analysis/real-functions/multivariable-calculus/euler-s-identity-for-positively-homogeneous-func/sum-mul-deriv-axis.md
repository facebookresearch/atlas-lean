# Euler's homogeneous function theorem

Let $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be positively homogeneous of degree one ($f(t x) = t f(x)$ for all $t > 0$ and $x$) and differentiable at $x$. Then

$$\sum_{i} x_i\, \partial_i f(x) = f(x),$$

where $\partial_i f(x)$ is the derivative at $0$ of $t \mapsto f(x + t e_i)$.
