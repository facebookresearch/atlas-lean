# Differentiated Euler identity

Let $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be positively homogeneous of degree one ($f(t x) = t f(x)$ for all $t > 0$ and $x$) and $C^2$ at $x$. For every coordinate $j$,

$$\sum_{i} x_i\, \partial_j \partial_i f(x) = 0,$$

where $\partial_j \partial_i f(x)$ is the derivative at $s = 0$ of the derivative at $t = 0$ of $f(x + s e_j + t e_i)$.

## Sources

- W. Rudin, Principles of Mathematical Analysis, 3rd ed., Ch. 9.
