# Quadratic growth of a potential along a stationary affine direction

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $S \subseteq E$ a linear subspace, $\Pi : E \to \mathbb{R}$, $x_0, h \in E$ with $h \in S$, and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$. Suppose that

- the derivative of $\Pi$ at $x_0$ vanishes on $S$: $D\Pi(x_0)\,v = 0$ for all $v \in S$;
- $\Pi$ is $C^2$ at every point $x_0 + t h$, $t \in [0,1]$;
- the second derivative in direction $h$ satisfies $D^2\Pi(x_0 + t h)(h, h) \ge \alpha \|h\|^2$ for all $t \in [0,1]$.

Then

$$\Pi(x_0 + h) - \Pi(x_0) \ge \frac{\alpha \|h\|^2}{2}.$$
