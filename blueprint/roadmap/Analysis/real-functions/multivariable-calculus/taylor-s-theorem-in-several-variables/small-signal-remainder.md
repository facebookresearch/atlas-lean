# Remainder of the linearization at an operating point

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $i : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, an operating point $v_0$ and a
perturbation $dv$, the small-signal remainder $r = i(v_0 + dv) - i(v_0) - Di(v_0)[dv]$, where
$Di(v_0)$ is the derivative of $i$ at $v_0$; if $i$ is not differentiable at $v_0$, the derivative
is taken to be $0$ and $r = i(v_0 + dv) - i(v_0)$.
