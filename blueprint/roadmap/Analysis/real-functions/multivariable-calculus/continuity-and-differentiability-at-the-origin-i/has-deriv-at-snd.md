# Second partial derivative

Let $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $f(0, 0) = 0$ and $f(x, y) = x^3/(x^2 + y^2)$ for $(x, y) \ne (0, 0)$. At every $(x, y)$, the origin included, $t \mapsto f(x, t)$ is differentiable at $y$ with derivative $\frac{-2x^3 y}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}$ (which is $0$ at the origin by real division).
