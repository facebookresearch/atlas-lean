# The signed radial function is not differentiable at the origin

Let
$f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ be the function

$$
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}, & y>0,\\
x, & y=0,\\
-\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}, & y<0.
\end{cases}
$$

Then $f$ is not differentiable at $(0, 0)$.
