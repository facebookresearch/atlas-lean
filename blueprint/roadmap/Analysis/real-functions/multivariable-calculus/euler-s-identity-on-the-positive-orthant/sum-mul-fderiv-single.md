# Euler's identity in coordinates

Let $\iota$ be finite, $r \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $f : \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$ be homogeneous of degree $r$ on the positive orthant: $f(t x) = t^r f(x)$ for every $x$ with all coordinates positive and every $t > 0$. If every $x_i > 0$ and $f$ is differentiable at $x$, then $\sum_i x_i\, \partial_i f(x) = r f(x)$, with $\partial_i f(x)$ the partial derivative in the $i$-th coordinate.
