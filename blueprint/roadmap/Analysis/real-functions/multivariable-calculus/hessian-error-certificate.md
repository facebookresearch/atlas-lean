# Hessian bound on the error of the first-order model on a ball

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $g : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $R \ge 0$ and $M \in \mathbb{R}$. If $g$ is $C^2$ at every point of the closed ball of radius $R$ about $0$ and the operator norm of its second Fréchet derivative is at most $M$ there, then for every $z$ in that ball $|g(z) - (g(0) + Dg(0)\,z)| \le M R^2/2$: the first-order model at the origin approximates $g$ on the ball with error at most $M R^2/2$.
