# Binary digits of a natural number

For a natural number $n$ and an integer $i$, the binary
digit $n_i \in \{0, 1\}$ of $n$ is the coefficient of $2^i$ in the binary expansion of $n$ when
$i \ge 0$, and $n_i = 0$ when $i < 0$.
