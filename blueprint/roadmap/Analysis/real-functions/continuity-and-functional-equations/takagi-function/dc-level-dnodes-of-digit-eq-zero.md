# Level count of D-nodes when the binary digit is zero

Let $n$ and $i$ be natural numbers
such that the binary digit $n_i$ of $n$ (the coefficient of $2^i$ in the binary expansion of
$n$) equals $0$. Then $\lambda_i(n) = n \bmod 2^i$. For $n > 0$ and
$i < \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$, this is the number of D-nodes on level $i$.

## Sources

- Laura Monroe, *Takagi Function Identities on Dyadic Rationals*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.2.7, Theorem 11.
