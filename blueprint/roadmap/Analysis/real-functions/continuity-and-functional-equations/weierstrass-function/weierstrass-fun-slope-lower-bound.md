# Slope blow-up lemma

For every real $c$ and every natural number $n$, there exists a point $y \ne c$ with $|y - c| < 3\pi / 160^n$ such
that the difference quotient of the Weierstrass function $W$ between $y$ and $c$ satisfies

$$
\left| \frac{W(y) - W(c)}{y - c} \right| \ge \frac{40^n}{117 \pi}.
$$

Since $40^n / (117 \pi) \to \infty$, this shows that $W$ has no finite derivative at $c$.
