# Auerbach's Hölder condition problem (Scottish Book 83)

Auerbach's Problem 83 from the Scottish Book. Let $0 < a < 1$ and $M > 0$, and let $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$
be continuous. Suppose that at every point $x$ there are $M' < M$ and $\delta > 0$ such that
$|f(x + h) - f(x)| / |h|^a \le M'$ whenever $0 < |h| < \delta$; that is, the limsup of these quotients as $h \to 0$ is
less than $M$. Does $f$ then satisfy a Hölder condition of exponent $a$ on $[0, 1]$, that is, is there a constant $C$
with $|f(x) - f(y)| \le C |x - y|^a$ for all $x, y \in [0, 1]$? The conclusion is asked on a compact interval: on all
of $\mathbb{R}$, $f(x) = x$ already fails it.
