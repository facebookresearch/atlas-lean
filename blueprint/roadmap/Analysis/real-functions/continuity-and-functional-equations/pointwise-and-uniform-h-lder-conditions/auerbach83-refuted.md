# Pointwise difference-quotient bounds do not imply Hölder continuity

Auerbach's Problem 83 has a negative answer: there are $0 < a < 1$, $M > 0$ and a continuous function
$f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ whose difference quotients $|f(x + h) - f(x)| / |h|^a$ are, at every point $x$,
bounded by some $M' < M$ for all sufficiently small $h \ne 0$, but which satisfies no Hölder condition of exponent $a$
on $[0, 1]$. Source: H. Auerbach, Problem 83 of the Scottish Book; typescript Ad 83 records the negative answer, with
Marcinkiewicz's counterexample: a triangular function on the intervals $[1/n - x_n, 1/n + x_n]$ with height $1/n$ and
suitably small half-widths $x_n$, whose difference quotients are small near every point but which is not globally
Hölder.
