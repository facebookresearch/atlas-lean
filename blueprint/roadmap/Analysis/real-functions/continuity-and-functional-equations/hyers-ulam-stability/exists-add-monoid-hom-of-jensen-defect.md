# Hyers–Ulam stability of Jensen's functional equation

Let $E$ and $F$ be real normed spaces with $F$ complete. If $f \colon E \to F$ has midpoint (Jensen) defect
$\bigl\|f\bigl(\tfrac12 (x + y)\bigr) - \tfrac12 (f(x) + f(y))\bigr\| \le \delta$ for all $x, y$, then there is an
additive map $A \colon E \to F$ with $\|f(x) - A(x) - f(0)\| \le 4\delta$ for all $x$: $f$ is within $4\delta$ of an
additive map plus the constant $f(0)$. The factor $4$ comes from doubling the two Jensen estimates at $(x + y, 0)$ and
$(x, y)$.
