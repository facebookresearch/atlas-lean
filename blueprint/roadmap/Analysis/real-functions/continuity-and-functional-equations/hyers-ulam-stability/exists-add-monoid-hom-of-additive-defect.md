# Hyers' theorem (Hyers–Ulam stability of the Cauchy equation)

Let $E$ and $F$ be real normed spaces with $F$ complete. If $f \colon E \to F$ satisfies
$\|f(x + y) - f(x) - f(y)\| \le \delta$ for all $x, y$, then there is an additive map $A \colon E \to F$ with
$\|f(x) - A(x)\| \le \delta$ for all $x$. The bound $\delta$ is sharp: for $f(x) = x + c$ on $\mathbb{R}$ the defect
is $|c|$ and the best additive approximant is the identity, at distance exactly $|c|$.
