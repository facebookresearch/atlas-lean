# Uniqueness in Hyers' theorem

Let $E$ be a normed additive commutative group, $F$ a real normed space, $f \colon E \to F$ and
$\delta \in \mathbb{R}$. Two
additive maps $A_1, A_2 \colon E \to F$ with $\|f(x) - A_1(x)\| \le \delta$ and $\|f(x) - A_2(x)\| \le \delta$ for all
$x$ are equal: their difference is additive, hence $n$-homogeneous, while staying bounded by $2\delta$.
