# Khinchin's conjecture on strong uniform distribution

Erdős Problem 994 (Khinchin's conjecture): for every Lebesgue measurable `E ⊆ (0, 1)`, for
almost every `α`, `(1/n) #{k ≤ n : {kα} ∈ E} → λ(E)`.

Its resolution: [Marstrand's refutation of Khinchin's conjecture](khinchin-strong-uniform-distribution-ref.md).
