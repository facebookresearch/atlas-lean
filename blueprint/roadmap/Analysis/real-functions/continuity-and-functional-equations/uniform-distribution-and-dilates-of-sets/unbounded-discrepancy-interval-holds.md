# Schmidt's theorem on intervals of unbounded discrepancy

This settles [Erdős Problem 255 on intervals of unbounded discrepancy](unbounded-discrepancy-interval.md) in the affirmative: its statement holds.

Schmidt proved that for every sequence in `[0, 1]` some interval has unbounded
discrepancy, and later that all but countably many intervals `[0, x]` do. erdosproblems.com/255
lists the problem as proved.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #255, https://www.erdosproblems.com/255 (status PROVED (LEAN)); resolution: W. M. Schmidt, Irregularities of distribution, Quart. J. Math. Oxford Ser. (2) (1968), 181-191, erdosproblems.com/255: answered yes by Schmidt [Sc68]; Schmidt, Irregularities of distribution. VI, Compositio Math. (1972), 63-74, shows it for all but countably many intervals [0, x], https://www.erdosproblems.com/255.
