# Rolle's theorem

Let $a < b$ be real numbers and let $f, f'\colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be functions such that the restriction of $f$ to $[a, b]$ is continuous, $f(a) = f(b)$, and $f$ has derivative $f'(x)$ at every point $x$ of the open interval $(a, b)$. Then $f'(c) = 0$ for some $c \in (a, b)$.
