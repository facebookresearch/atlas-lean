# The two-tag integral with unit right-endpoint factor

For $F, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $a, b, I \in \mathbb{R}$, the two-tag sums $\sum F(u)\,(h(x) - h(u))\,G(x)$ with right-endpoint factor $G = 1$ converge to $I$ along refinement exactly when the left-endpoint sums of $F$ against $h$ converge to $I$: the two-tag integral with unit right-endpoint factor is the left-endpoint integral $\int_a^b F\,dh$. The increments in the sums are those of $h$; $G$ only multiplies them at the right endpoint.
