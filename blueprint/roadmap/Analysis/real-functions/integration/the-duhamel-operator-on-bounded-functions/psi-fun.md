# Value of the Duhamel operator

For $A, B : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, a bounded $h$ on $[0, \tau]$ and $t \in [0, \tau]$, the value of the Duhamel operator: the limit along the fixed refining ultrafilter of the two-tag sums of $u \mapsto \prod_{(0,u]}(1 + dA)$ and $x \mapsto \prod_{(x,t]}(1 + dB)$ against the zero extension of $h$ over partitions of $[0, t]$.
