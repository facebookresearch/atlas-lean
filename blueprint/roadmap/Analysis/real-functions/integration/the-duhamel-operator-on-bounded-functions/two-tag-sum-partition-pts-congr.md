# Two-tag sums only read the integrator on the interval

The two-tag sum of $F, G$ against $h$ over a finite set $P$ is $\sum_{x \in P} F(\operatorname{prev}_P(x))\,(h(x) - h(\operatorname{prev}_P(x)))\, G(x)$, and the partition of $[a, b]$ generated by a finite set $s$ consists of $a$, $b$ and the points of $s$ strictly between them. If $a \le b$ and $h = g$ on $[a, b]$, then for every $s$ the two-tag sums of $F, G$ against $h$ and against $g$ over the partition generated by $s$ agree.
