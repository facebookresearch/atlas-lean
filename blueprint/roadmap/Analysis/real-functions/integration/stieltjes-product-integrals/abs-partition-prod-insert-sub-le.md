# Majorant inequality for one insertion

A function $V$ controls $A$ on $[a, b]$ when $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for all $a \le y \le x \le b$. For $a \le b$ and a finite set $s$, let the partition product of $A$ be the grid product $\prod (1 + A(x) - A(\operatorname{prev}(x)))$ over the partition of $[a, b]$ with points $s \cap [a, b]$ together with $a$ and $b$. If $V$ controls $A$ on $[a, b]$, then inserting any point $p$ into $s$ changes the partition product of $A$ by at most the change in the partition product of $V$.
