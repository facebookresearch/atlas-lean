# The product integral multiplies over cells

A function $V$ controls $A$ on $[a, b]$ when $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for all $a \le y \le x \le b$. The product integral $\prod_{(a, b]}(1 + dA)$ is the limit of the partition products $\prod (1 + A(t_i) - A(t_{i-1}))$ over partitions of $[a, b]$ along refinement. Let $a \le b$ and let $V$ control $A$ on $[a, b]$. For every partition of $[a, b]$ (the points of a finite set $s$ in $[a, b]$ together with $a$ and $b$), the product over its cells $(t_{i-1}, t_i]$ of $\prod_{(t_{i-1}, t_i]}(1 + dA)$ equals $\prod_{(a,b]}(1 + dA)$.
