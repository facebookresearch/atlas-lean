# Controlled grid products are dominated

A function $V$ controls $A$ on $[a, b]$ when $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for all $a \le y \le x \le b$. For a finite set $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, let $\operatorname{prev}_P(x)$ be the largest element of $P$ below $x$ (or $x$ if there is none), and let the grid product of $A$ over $P$ be $\prod_{x \in P} \big(1 + A(x) - A(\operatorname{prev}_P(x))\big)$. If $V$ controls $A$ on $[a, b]$ and $P \subseteq [a, b]$, then the grid product of $A$ over $P$ is at most the grid product of $V$ over $P$ in absolute value.
