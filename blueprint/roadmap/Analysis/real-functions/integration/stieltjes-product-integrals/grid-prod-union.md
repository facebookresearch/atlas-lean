# Grid products multiply over adjacent sets

For a finite set $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, let $\operatorname{prev}_P(x)$ be the largest element of $P$ below $x$ (or $x$ if there is none), and let the grid product of $A$ over $P$ be $\prod_{x \in P} \big(1 + A(x) - A(\operatorname{prev}_P(x))\big)$. If $c \in P \cap Q$, every element of $P$ is at most $c$ and every element of $Q$ is at least $c$, then the grid product over $P \cup Q$ is the product of the grid products over $P$ and over $Q$.
