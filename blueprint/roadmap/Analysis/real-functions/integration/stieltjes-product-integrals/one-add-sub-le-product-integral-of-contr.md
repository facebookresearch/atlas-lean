# Lower bound for the product integral of a control

A function $V$ controls $A$ on $[a, b]$ when $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for all $a \le y \le x \le b$. The product integral $\prod_{(a, b]}(1 + dA)$ is the limit of the partition products $\prod (1 + A(t_i) - A(t_{i-1}))$ over partitions of $[a, b]$ along refinement. If $a \le b$ and $V$ controls some $A$ on $[a, b]$, then $\prod_{(a,b]}(1 + dV) \ge 1 + V(b) - V(a)$.
