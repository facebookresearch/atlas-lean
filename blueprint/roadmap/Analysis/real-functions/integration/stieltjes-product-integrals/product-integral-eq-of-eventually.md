# An eventually constant product integral

The product integral $\prod_{(a, b]}(1 + dA)$ is the limit of the partition products $\prod (1 + A(t_i) - A(t_{i-1}))$ over partitions of $[a, b]$ along refinement. If the partition products of $A$ over $[a, b]$ are eventually (along refinement) equal to $c$, then $\prod_{(a,b]}(1 + dA) = c$.
