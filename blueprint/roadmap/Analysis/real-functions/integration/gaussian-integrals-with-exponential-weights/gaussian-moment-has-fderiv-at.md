# Derivatives of Gaussian moments

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be measurable and bounded, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p = (a, b)$ with $a > 0$, and write $M_n(a, b) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} y^n e^{-a y^2 + b y} f(y) \, dy$. Then $M_n$ is differentiable at $p$, with partial derivatives $-M_{n+2}(p)$ in $a$ and $M_{n+1}(p)$ in $b$.
