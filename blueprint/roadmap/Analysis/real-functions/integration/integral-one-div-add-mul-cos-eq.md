# Integral of $1/(a + b\cos\theta)$ over a period

Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers with
$a > |b|$. Then

$$\int_0^{2\pi} \frac{d\theta}{a + b\cos\theta} = \frac{2\pi}{\sqrt{a^2 - b^2}}.$$
