# Uniformly continuous integrable functions vanish at infinity

Let
$f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be uniformly continuous and Lebesgue integrable on $\mathbb{R}$.
Then $f(x) \to 0$ as $|x| \to \infty$.
