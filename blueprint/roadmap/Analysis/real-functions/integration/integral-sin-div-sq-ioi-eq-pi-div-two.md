# Integral of the squared sinc function over the half-line

The integral of
$(\sin x / x)^2$ over $(0, \infty)$ is $\pi/2$:

$$\int_0^\infty \left(\frac{\sin x}{x}\right)^2 dx = \frac{\pi}{2}.$$
