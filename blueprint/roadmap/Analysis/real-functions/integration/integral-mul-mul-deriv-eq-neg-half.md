# The integral of $x f(x) f'(x)$ equals $-1/2$

Let $a\lt b$ and let
$f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuously differentiable on $[a,b]$ with $f(a)=f(b)=0$ and

$$
\int_a^b f(x)^2\,dx=1.
$$

Then

$$
\int_a^b x\,f(x)\,f'(x)\,dx=-\frac12.
$$
