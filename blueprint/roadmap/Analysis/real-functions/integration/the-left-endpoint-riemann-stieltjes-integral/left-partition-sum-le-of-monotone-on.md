# Upper bound for left-endpoint sums of monotone functions

The left-endpoint Riemann–Stieltjes integral $\int_a^b f\, dh = I$ means that the sums $\sum_i f(t_{i-1})(h(t_i) - h(t_{i-1}))$ over the partitions of $[a, b]$ converge to $I$ along refinement; the partition generated by a finite set $s$ consists of $a$, $b$ and the points of $s$ strictly between them. If $a \le b$ and $f$, $h$ are monotone on $[a, b]$, then every left-endpoint sum of $f$ against $h$ over a partition of $[a, b]$ is at most $f(b)\,(h(b) - h(a))$.
