# Left-endpoint sums are Riemann–Stieltjes sums

Let $P$ be a partition $a = x_0 < x_1 < \dots < x_n = b$ of $[a, b]$ and $f, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. The left-endpoint sum of $f$ against $h$ over the points of $P$, $\sum_{i=1}^{n} f(x_{i-1})\,(h(x_i) - h(x_{i-1}))$, equals the Riemann–Stieltjes sum of $f$ against $h$ for $P$ tagged at the left endpoints of its cells.
