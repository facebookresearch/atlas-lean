# Left-endpoint integral against a closed step

The left-endpoint Riemann–Stieltjes integral $\int_a^b f\, dh = I$ means that the sums $\sum_i f(t_{i-1})(h(t_i) - h(t_{i-1}))$ over the partitions of $[a, b]$ converge to $I$ along refinement; the partition generated by a finite set $s$ consists of $a$, $b$ and the points of $s$ strictly between them. Let $a < c \le b$ and let $f$ be left-continuous at $c$. Then $\int_a^b f\, d\mathbf 1_{[c, \infty)} = f(c)$.
