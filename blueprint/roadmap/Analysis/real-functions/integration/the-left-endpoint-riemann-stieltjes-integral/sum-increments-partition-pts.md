# Increments over a partition sum to the total increment

For $a \le b$, every $h$ and every finite $s$, the increments $h(x) - h(\operatorname{prev}(x))$ of $h$ over the cells of the partition of $[a, b]$ generated by $s$ (the points $a$, $b$ and the points of $s$ strictly between them) sum to $h(b) - h(a)$.
