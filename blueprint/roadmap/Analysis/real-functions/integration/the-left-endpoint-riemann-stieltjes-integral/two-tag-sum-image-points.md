# Two-tag sums over the points of a partition

Let $P$ be a partition $a = x_0 < \dots < x_n = b$ of $[a, b]$ and $F, G, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. The two-tag sum over the points of $P$ is $\sum_{i < n} F(x_i)\,(h(x_{i+1}) - h(x_i))\,G(x_{i+1})$: $F$ is evaluated at the left endpoint and $G$ at the right endpoint of each cell.
