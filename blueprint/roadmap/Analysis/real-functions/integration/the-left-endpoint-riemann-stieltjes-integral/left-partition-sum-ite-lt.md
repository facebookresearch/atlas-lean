# Left-endpoint sums against an open step

The left-endpoint Riemann–Stieltjes integral $\int_a^b f\, dh = I$ means that the sums $\sum_i f(t_{i-1})(h(t_i) - h(t_{i-1}))$ over the partitions of $[a, b]$ converge to $I$ along refinement; the partition generated by a finite set $s$ consists of $a$, $b$ and the points of $s$ strictly between them. Let $c < b$ and let the partition generated by $s$ contain $c$. Then the left-endpoint sum of $f$ against $\mathbf 1_{(c, \infty)}$ over it equals $f(c)$.
