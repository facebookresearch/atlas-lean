# Existence against a bounded-variation integrator, one-sided form

Let `a ≤ b`, let `g` be regulated on
`[a, b]` and let `f` have bounded variation on `[a, b]`. If `g` and `f` have no common
discontinuity on the same side (at every point one of them is right-continuous and one of them is
left-continuous, within `[a, b]`), then `∫ₐᵇ g df` exists. The order `a ≤ b` matters: for `a > b` the regularity conditions hold vacuously on the empty interval, but no increasing partition runs from `a` to `b`, so the integral does not exist even for zero functions.
