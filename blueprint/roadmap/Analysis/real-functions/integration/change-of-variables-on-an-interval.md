# Change of variables in the Lebesgue integral on the line

Let $s\subseteq\mathbb R$ be measurable and let $\varphi:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be injective on $s$ and differentiable within $s$ at every point of $s$, with derivative $\varphi'$. Then for every $g:\mathbb R\to[0,\infty]$,

$$\int_{\varphi(s)}g(x)\,dx=\int_sg(\varphi(t))\,\lvert\varphi'(t)\rvert\,dt$$

as lower Lebesgue integrals. For example, $\varphi(t)=e^{-t}$ maps $s=(0,\infty)$ onto $(0,1)$ with $\lvert\varphi'(t)\rvert=e^{-t}$.
