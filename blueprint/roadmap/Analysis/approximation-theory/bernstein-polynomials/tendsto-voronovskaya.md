# Voronovskaya's theorem

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be of class $C^2$ and $x \in [0, 1]$, and let
$B_n f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} f(k/n) \binom{n}{k} x^k (1 - x)^{n-k}$ be the Bernstein approximations
of $f$ on $[0, 1]$. Then

$$n\big(B_n f(x) - f(x)\big) \to \frac{x(1 - x)}{2} f''(x) \qquad (n \to \infty) .$$

## Sources

- E. V. Voronovskaya, *Détermination de la forme asymptotique d'approximation des fonctions par
  les polynômes de M. Bernstein*, C. R. Acad. Sci. URSS (1932), 79–85.
- G. G. Lorentz, *Bernstein Polynomials*, University of Toronto Press, 1953, Chapter 1.
