# Existence of best approximations in finite-dimensional subspaces

Let $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$ and
$F \subseteq E$ a finite-dimensional subspace. Then every $x \in E$ has a nearest point in $F$:
some $y \in F$ satisfies $\|x - y\| \le \|x - z\|$ for every $z \in F$. No completeness of $E$ is
needed: completeness enters only through $F$, whose closed balls are compact.
