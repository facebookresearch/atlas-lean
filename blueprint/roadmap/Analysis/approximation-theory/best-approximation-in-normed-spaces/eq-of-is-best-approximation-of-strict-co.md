# Uniqueness of best approximation in strictly convex spaces

Let $E$ be a strictly convex real normed space, $K \subseteq E$ convex and $x \in E$. If
$y_1, y_2 \in K$ both satisfy $\|x - y_i\| \le \|x - z\|$ for every $z \in K$, then $y_1 = y_2$: the
midpoint of two distinct nearest points would be strictly closer.
