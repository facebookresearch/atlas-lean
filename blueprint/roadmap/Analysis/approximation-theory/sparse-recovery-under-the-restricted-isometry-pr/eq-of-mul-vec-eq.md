# Uniqueness of sparse solutions under $\delta_{2s} < 1$

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set and $\Phi \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times \iota}$ with the [restricted
isometry property](has-rip.md) of order $2s$ with a constant $\delta < 1$. If
$x, x' \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ each have at most $s$ nonzero entries and $\Phi x = \Phi x'$, then
$x = x'$.

## Sources

- E. J. Candès, *The restricted isometry property and its implications for compressed sensing*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris, Ser. I 346 (2008) 589–592, remark after Theorem 1.2: "if $\delta_{2s} < 1$, the $\ell_0$ problem has a unique $s$-sparse solution"
