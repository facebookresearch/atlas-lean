# Isometry constants are nonnegative

Let $\iota$ be a nonempty finite index set, $s \ge 1$, and $\Phi \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times \iota}$ with
the [restricted isometry property](has-rip.md) of order $s$ with constant $\delta$. Then
$\delta \ge 0$ (apply the definition to a standard basis vector).
