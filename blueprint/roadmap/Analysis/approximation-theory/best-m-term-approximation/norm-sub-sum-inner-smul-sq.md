# Error of keeping the coefficients on a set

For a finite orthonormal basis $(b_i)$ and a set $S$ of indices,

$$\Big\|f - \sum_{i \in S} \langle b_i, f\rangle b_i\Big\|^2 = \sum_{i \notin S} |\langle b_i, f\rangle|^2 .$$

With $S = \{1, \dots, n\}$ this is the error (2.8) of the linear approximation by the first $n$ basis vectors in R. A. DeVore, *Nonlinear approximation*, Acta Numerica 7 (1998); when $S$ holds the $n$ largest coefficients it is the best $n$-term error (2.19).
