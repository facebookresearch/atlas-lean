# Error of an $M$-term approximation in an orthonormal basis

Let $(b_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a finite orthonormal basis of an inner product space $E$ over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $f \in E$, let $T \subseteq \iota$ and let $(a_i)$ be any coefficients. Then

$$\Big\|f - \sum_{i \in T} a_i b_i\Big\|^2 = \sum_{i \in T} |\langle b_i, f\rangle - a_i|^2 + \sum_{i \notin T} |\langle b_i, f\rangle|^2 .$$

This is Parseval's identity for the coefficients of $f - \sum_{i \in T} a_i b_i$, the computation behind (2.8) and (2.19) of R. A. DeVore, *Nonlinear approximation*, Acta Numerica 7 (1998), Section 2.
