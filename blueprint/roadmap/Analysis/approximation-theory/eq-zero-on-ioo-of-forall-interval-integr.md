# Vanishing of all Chebyshev U moments forces a function to vanish

Let $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be continuous on $(0, \pi)$ and integrable on $[0, \pi]$. If

$$\int_0^\pi g(\theta)\, U_j(\cos\theta)\, d\theta = 0$$

for every $j \ge 0$, where $U_j$ is the $j$-th Chebyshev polynomial of the second kind, then $g(\theta) = 0$ for every $\theta \in (0, \pi)$. Neither continuity at the endpoints nor square integrability is assumed.
