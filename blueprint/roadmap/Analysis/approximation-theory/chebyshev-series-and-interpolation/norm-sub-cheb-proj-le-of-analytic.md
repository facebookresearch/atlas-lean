# Convergence of Chebyshev projections for analytic functions

Under the hypotheses of Theorem 8.1, $|f(x) - f_n(x)| \le 2M\rho^{-n}/(\rho - 1)$ for every
$x \in [-1, 1]$ and $n \ge 0$.

## Sources

- L. N. Trefethen, Approximation Theory and Approximation Practice, SIAM (2013; extended edition 2019), Theorem 8.2.
