# Chebyshev interpolant

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$. For $n \ge 1$, the Chebyshev interpolant $p_n$ is the complex
polynomial of degree at most $n$ interpolating $f$ at the $n + 1$ Chebyshev points
$x_j = \cos(j\pi/n)$, $0 \le j \le n$. For $n = 0$ the single node is $x_0 = 1$ and $p_0$ is the
constant $f(1)$.
