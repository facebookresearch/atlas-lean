# Open Bernstein ellipse

For $\rho > 1$, the open Bernstein ellipse $E_\rho = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - 1| + |z + 1| <
\rho + \rho^{-1}\}$ is the open region bounded by the ellipse with foci $\pm 1$ and semi-axis sum
$\rho$; it is the image of the annulus $1 < |w| < \rho$ under the Joukowsky map
$w \mapsto (w + w^{-1})/2$, together with $[-1, 1]$.
