# Existence and equioscillation of the best approximation

If $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ with $a < b$, there is a polynomial $p$ of degree at most $n$
such that $f - p$ equioscillates, with amplitude $\max_{[a,b]}|f - p|$, at $n + 2$ points
$a \le x_0 < \dots < x_{n+1} \le b$. By the sufficiency and uniqueness halves of the equioscillation theorem this $p$ is
the unique best uniform approximation.
