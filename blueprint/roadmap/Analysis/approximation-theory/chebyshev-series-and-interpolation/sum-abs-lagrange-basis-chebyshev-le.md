# Lebesgue constants of Chebyshev points

For $n \ge 1$ the Lagrange basis polynomials $\ell_j$ of the $n + 1$ Chebyshev points satisfy
$\sum_j |\ell_j(x)| \le \frac{2}{\pi}\log(n + 1) + 1$ for every $x \in [-1, 1]$; the Lebesgue
constant grows only logarithmically (for equispaced points it grows like $2^n$).

## Sources

- L. N. Trefethen, Approximation Theory and Approximation Practice, SIAM (2013; extended edition 2019), Theorem 15.2.
