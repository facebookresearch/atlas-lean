# Chebyshev coefficients of analytic functions

Let $\rho > 1$ and let $f$ be analytic in the open Bernstein ellipse $E_\rho$ with
$|f(z)| \le M$ there. Then $|a_0| \le M$ and $|a_k| \le 2M\rho^{-k}$ for every $k \ge 1$.

## Sources

- L. N. Trefethen, Approximation Theory and Approximation Practice, SIAM (2013; extended edition 2019), Theorem 8.1.
