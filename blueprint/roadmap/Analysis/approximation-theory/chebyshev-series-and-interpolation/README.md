# Chebyshev series and interpolation

The convergence theory of Chebyshev series and Chebyshev interpolants, following Trefethen's *Approximation Theory and Approximation Practice*: Lipschitz functions have absolutely convergent Chebyshev series (Theorem 3.1); analytic functions in a Bernstein ellipse $E_\rho$ have coefficients and interpolation errors decaying like $\rho^{-n}$ (Theorems 8.1–8.2); best approximations exist and equioscillate (Theorem 10.1); the Lebesgue constant of Chebyshev points grows like $\frac{2}{\pi}\log n$, so Chebyshev interpolants are near-best (Theorems 15.2 and 16.1).
