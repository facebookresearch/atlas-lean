# Convergence of Chebyshev interpolants for analytic functions

Under the hypotheses of Theorem 8.1, the Chebyshev interpolant satisfies
$|f(x) - p_n(x)| \le 4M\rho^{-n}/(\rho - 1)$ for every $x \in [-1, 1]$ (recorded for $n \ge 1$).
Geometric convergence at the rate $\rho^{-n}$ set by the largest Bernstein ellipse of
analyticity is the central fact of Chebyshev technology (Chebfun).
