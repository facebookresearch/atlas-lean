# Chebyshev series of Lipschitz functions

If $f$ is Lipschitz continuous on $[-1, 1]$, then $\sum_k |a_k| < \infty$ and
$f(x) = \sum_{k \ge 0} a_k T_k(x)$ for every $x \in [-1, 1]$: the Chebyshev series converges
absolutely and uniformly to $f$.

## Sources

- L. N. Trefethen, Approximation Theory and Approximation Practice, SIAM (2013; extended edition 2019), Theorem 3.1.
