# OMP residuals stay in the optimal span

If the signal $s$ lies in a subspace $K$ and the atoms selected so far span a subspace $L \le K$ (with an orthogonal projection $P_L$), then the OMP residual $s - P_L s$ lies in $K$.

## Sources

- J. A. Tropp, Greed is good: algorithmic results for sparse approximation, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 50 (2004), 2231–2242, DOI 10.1109/TIT.2004.834793, proof of Theorem 3.1.
