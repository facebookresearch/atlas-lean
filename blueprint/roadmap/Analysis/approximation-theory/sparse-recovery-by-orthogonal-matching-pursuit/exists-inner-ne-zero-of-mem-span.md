# A nonzero vector of the span sees some atom

If $r \ne 0$ lies in the span of vectors $\varphi_i$, then $\langle\varphi_i, r\rangle \ne 0$ for some $i$: otherwise $r$ would be orthogonal to the span, hence to itself. This makes the greedy selection ratio $\rho(r)$ well defined for a nonzero residual.
