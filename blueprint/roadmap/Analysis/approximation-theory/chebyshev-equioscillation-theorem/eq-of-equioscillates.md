# Uniqueness of the equioscillating best approximation

If the polynomial $p$ [equioscillates](equioscillates.md) against $f$ on $[a, b]$ with amplitude $E$
at $n + 2$ points, and a real polynomial $q$ of degree at most $n$ satisfies $|f(y) - q(y)| \le E$
for every $y \in [a, b]$, then $q = p$. In particular $p$ is the unique best uniform approximation
to $f$ on $[a, b]$ among polynomials of degree at most $n$.
