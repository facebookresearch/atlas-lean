# Chebyshev equioscillation theorem (sufficiency)

If the polynomial $p$ [equioscillates](equioscillates.md) against $f$ on $[a, b]$ with amplitude $E$
at $n + 2$ points, then no real polynomial $q$ of degree at most $n$ satisfies
$|f(y) - q(y)| < E$ for every $y \in [a, b]$: $p$ is a best uniform approximation to $f$ on
$[a, b]$ among polynomials of degree at most $n$.
