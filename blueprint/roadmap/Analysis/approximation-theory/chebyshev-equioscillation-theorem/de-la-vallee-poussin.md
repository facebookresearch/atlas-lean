# De la Vallée Poussin's theorem

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\sigma \in \{1, -1\}$,
$\eta \in \mathbb{R}$ and $p$ a real polynomial of degree at most $n$. Let
$x_0 < x_1 < \dots < x_{n+1}$ be points of $[a, b]$ with
$\eta \le \sigma (-1)^i \big(f(x_i) - p(x_i)\big)$ for $0 \le i \le n + 1$. Then no real polynomial
$q$ of degree at most $n$ satisfies $|f(y) - q(y)| < \eta$ for every $y \in [a, b]$.
