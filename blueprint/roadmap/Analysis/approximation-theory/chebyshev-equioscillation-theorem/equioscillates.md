# Equioscillation of a polynomial approximation

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $a, b, E \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. A real polynomial
$p$ equioscillates against $f$ on $[a, b]$ with amplitude $E$ and sign $\sigma$ at the points
$x_0, \dots, x_{n+1}$ if $\sigma \in \{1, -1\}$, $p$ has degree at most $n$,
$x_0 < x_1 < \dots < x_{n+1}$ all lie in $[a, b]$, $f(x_i) - p(x_i) = \sigma (-1)^i E$ for
$0 \le i \le n + 1$, and $|f(y) - p(y)| \le E$ for every $y \in [a, b]$.
