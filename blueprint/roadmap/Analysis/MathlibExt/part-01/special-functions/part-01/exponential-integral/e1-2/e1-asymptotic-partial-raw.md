# E1 Asymptotic Partial Raw

Totalized sum of the first `n` raw asymptotic terms.

Junk-value extension: at `z = 0` this is `0`. Use `e1AsymptoticPartial` with
a `z ≠ 0` proof for the domain-safe API.
