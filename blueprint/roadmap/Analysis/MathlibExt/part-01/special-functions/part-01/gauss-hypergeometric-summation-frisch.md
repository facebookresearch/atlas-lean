# Gauss Hypergeometric Summation Frisch

Gauss summation at the Frisch specialization: `₂F₁(1 + b - c, n; b + 1; 1)`
equals `Γ(c - n) Γ(b + 1) / (Γ(c) Γ(b - n + 1))` for `n ≥ 1`, `c > n`, and `b > -1`.
