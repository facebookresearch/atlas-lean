# Is Locally Uniformly Bounded On

Local uniform boundedness of `F` on `U`: every `x ∈ U` has neighbourhood `V ⊆ U`
and `C ≥ 0` with `‖F n y‖ ≤ C` for all `n` and `y ∈ V`.
