# Chapter5 Entry30 Signed Coeff of ne zero

For `n ≠ 0`, the signed coefficient is `-n ^ (-s)` or `n ^ (-s)` when `n` is squarefree
with an odd or even number of prime factors, and `0` when `n` is not squarefree.
