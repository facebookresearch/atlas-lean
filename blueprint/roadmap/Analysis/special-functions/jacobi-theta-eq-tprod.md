# Jacobi triple product for the two-variable theta function

Jacobi's triple product in the `jacobiTheta₂` normalisation: for
`0 < im τ`, `jacobiTheta₂ z τ` is the product over `n : ℕ` of
`(1 - exp (2πi(n+1)τ)) (1 + exp (πi(2n+1)τ + 2πiz))
(1 + exp (πi(2n+1)τ - 2πiz))`.

The same identity in the variables $q = e^{\pi i \tau}$ and $w = e^{2\pi i z}$ is the [Jacobi triple product](../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/theta-q-series-partitions/jacobi-triple-product/jacobi-triple-product.md).
