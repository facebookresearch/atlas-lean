# The count of parameters integral at p

For multisets $\alpha, \beta$ of rational numbers and a natural number $p$, the count is $\lambda_p = \#\{i : p \nmid \operatorname{den}(\alpha_i)\} - \#\{j : p \nmid \operatorname{den}(\beta_j)\}$, an integer defined for every natural $p$. When $p$ is prime, $p \nmid \operatorname{den}(t)$ is equivalent to $t \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ (the $p$-adic integers), which is the form used by Delaygue–Rivoal–Roques, §2.1.
