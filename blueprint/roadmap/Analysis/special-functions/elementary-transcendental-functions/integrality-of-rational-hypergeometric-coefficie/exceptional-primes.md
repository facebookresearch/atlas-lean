# Exceptional primes

For finite multisets $\alpha, \beta$ of rationals with $r = |\alpha|$ and $s = |\beta|$, the finite set of exceptional primes in the least-scaling formula: the primes dividing the least common denominator $d_{\alpha,\beta}$ of the parameters, together with the primes $p$ with $2 \le p$ and $p + s \le r + 1$ (that is, $p \le r - s + 1$ without integer subtraction).
