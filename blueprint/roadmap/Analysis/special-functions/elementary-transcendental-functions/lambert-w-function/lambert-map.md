# Lambert map

The Lambert map is the function $h(y) = y e^y$ of a real variable $y$.

The inverse of $h$ restricted to $y > -1$ is the principal real branch of the Lambert W function.

## Sources

- Vincent Ouellet, *On the Sum of the Reciprocals of the Middle Prime Factors of an Integer*, Journal of Integer Sequences 20 (2017), Article 17.10.1, definition of the Lambert W function, lines 426-432, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL20/Ouellet/ou2.tex.
