# The complex exponential is holomorphic and equal to its own derivative

For $z \in \mathbb{C}$ let $e^z = \sum_{n \ge 0} z^n / n!$. Then the function $z \mapsto e^z$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{C}$, and its complex derivative at every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ is $e^z$.
