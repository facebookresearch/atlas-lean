# Pairs of consecutive Gegenbauer polynomials

For $\nu \in \mathbb{R}$, the pairs $(C_n^{(\nu)}, C_{n+1}^{(\nu)})$ of consecutive Gegenbauer polynomials are defined by recursion on $n$: the pair at $n = 0$ is $(1,\ 2\nu X)$, and if $(P, Q)$ is the pair at $n$, the pair at $n + 1$ is $\big(Q,\ \frac{1}{n+2}\big(2(n + 1 + \nu)\,X\,Q - (n + 2\nu)\,P\big)\big)$.
