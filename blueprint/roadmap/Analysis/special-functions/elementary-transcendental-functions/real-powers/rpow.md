# Real power of a real number

For real numbers $x$ and $y$, the real power $x^y$ is the real part of the complex power $x^y$, where
$x^y = \exp(y \log x)$ for $x \ne 0$, with the principal complex logarithm, $0^0 = 1$ and $0^y = 0$ for $y \ne 0$.
So $x^y = \exp(y \log x)$ for $x > 0$, $0^0 = 1$ and $0^y = 0$ for $y \ne 0$, and $x^y = \exp(y \log |x|) \cos(\pi y)$
for $x < 0$.
