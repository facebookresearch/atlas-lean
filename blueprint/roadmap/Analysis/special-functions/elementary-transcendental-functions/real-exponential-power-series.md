# Power series of the exponential function

Let $\mathbb A$ be a complete normed division ring that is a normed algebra over $\mathbb Q$, for example $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. For every $x\in\mathbb A$ the series $\sum_{n\ge0}x^n/n!$ has sum $\exp x$:

$$\exp x=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{x^n}{n!}.$$

For real $x$ this is $e^x=\sum_{n\ge0}x^n/n!$; for $x\ge0$ the terms are nonnegative, so the partial sums increase to $e^x$.
