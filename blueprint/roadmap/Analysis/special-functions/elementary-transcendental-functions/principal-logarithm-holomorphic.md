# The principal logarithm is holomorphic on the slit plane with derivative 1/z

Let $\Omega = \mathbb{C} \setminus (-\infty, 0]$ be the complex plane slit along the nonpositive real axis. Every $w \in \Omega$ can be written uniquely as $w = r e^{i\theta}$ with $r > 0$ and $-\pi < \theta < \pi$, and the principal branch of the logarithm is $\operatorname{Log} w = \log r + i\theta$. Then $\operatorname{Log}$ is holomorphic on $\Omega$, and $\operatorname{Log}'(w) = 1/w$ for every $w \in \Omega$.
