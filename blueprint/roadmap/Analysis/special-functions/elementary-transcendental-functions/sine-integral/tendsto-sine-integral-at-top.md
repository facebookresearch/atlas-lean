# The Dirichlet integral

For real $T$ let $\operatorname{Si}(T) = \int_0^T \frac{\sin u}{u} \, du$, the integral over the interval from
$0$ to $T$ of the function $u \mapsto \frac{\sin u}{u}$, which is continuous on $\mathbb R$ once given the value $1$ at
$u = 0$. Then

$$\lim_{T \to \infty} \operatorname{Si}(T) = \frac{\pi}{2}.$$

The limit cannot be replaced by an integral over $(0, \infty)$ for Lebesgue measure: $\frac{\sin u}{u}$ is not
integrable there.
