# Uniform bound for the sine integral

For every real $z$, $|\operatorname{Si}(z)| \le 4$, where $\operatorname{Si}(z) = \int_0^z \operatorname{sinc}(u)\,du$ with $\operatorname{sinc}(0) = 1$ and $\operatorname{sinc}(u) = \sin u/u$ for $u \ne 0$. The bound is for real arguments: on the imaginary axis the complex sine integral is unbounded.
