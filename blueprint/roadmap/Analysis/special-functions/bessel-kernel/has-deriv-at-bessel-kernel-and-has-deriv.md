# Derivatives of the Bessel kernel

Let $\nu \in \mathbb{C}$ and let $K_\nu(x) = \int_0^\infty e^{-x(t + t^{-1})/2}\, t^{\nu - 1}\, dt$ be the archimedean Bessel kernel. For every real $x > 0$, $K_\nu$ and $K_\nu'$ are differentiable at $x$, with

$$K_\nu'(x) = -K_{\nu+1}(x) + \frac{\nu}{x} K_\nu(x), \qquad K_\nu''(x) = \Big(1 + \frac{\nu^2}{x^2}\Big) K_\nu(x) - \frac{K_\nu'(x)}{x}.$$
