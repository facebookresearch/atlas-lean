# Opposite sides of the period parallelogram of a period pair cancel

Let $L$ be a period
pair with periods $\omega_1, \omega_2$ and period lattice $\Lambda$, and let
$g : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ satisfy $g(z + \omega) = g(z)$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and
$\omega \in \Lambda$. Then for every $z_0 \in \mathbb{C}$ the boundary integral of $g$ over the
parallelogram with vertex $z_0$ and sides $\omega_1$, $\omega_2$ is $0$.

## Sources

- Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, 2nd ed., Chapter III, proof of Proposition 2.1, p. 87: "Because $f$ is periodic, the integrals over opposite sides of $D$ cancel, and so the integral is zero." No integrability or meromorphy of $g$ is needed.
