# Homothety covariance of the Weierstrass ℘-function

For a period lattice $L$, every nonzero complex number $\alpha$ and every complex $z$,

$$\wp_{\alpha L}(\alpha z) = \alpha^{-2}\, \wp_L(z):$$

scaling the lattice by $\alpha$ and the argument by $\alpha$ scales $\wp$ by $\alpha^{-2}$. Here $\wp_L$ agrees with the classical function off $L$ and is assigned the complex value $0$ on $L$. The scaling law at a fixed argument, $\wp_{\alpha L}(z) = \alpha^{-2}\,\wp_L(z)$, does not hold in general; even the poles move with the lattice.
