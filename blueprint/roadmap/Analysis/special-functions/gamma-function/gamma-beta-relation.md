# The beta integral in terms of the gamma function

For complex numbers $s$ and $t$ with $\operatorname{Re} s > 0$ and $\operatorname{Re} t > 0$, let

$$\mathrm{B}(s, t) = \int_0^1 x^{s - 1} (1 - x)^{t - 1} \, dx$$

be Euler's beta integral. Then $\Gamma(s) \, \Gamma(t) = \Gamma(s + t) \, \mathrm{B}(s, t)$.

## Sources

- F. W. J. Olver et al. (eds.), *NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions*, Version 1.2.8 of 2026-09-15,
  <https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.12>, equation 5.12.1, $\mathrm{B}(a, b) = \int_0^1 t^{a-1} (1 - t)^{b-1} \, dt = \Gamma(a) \Gamma(b) / \Gamma(a + b)$.
