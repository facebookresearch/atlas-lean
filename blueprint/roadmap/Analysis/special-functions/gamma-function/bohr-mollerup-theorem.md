# Bohr–Mollerup theorem

Let $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a function such that $f(x) > 0$ and $f(x + 1) = x f(x)$ for every
$x > 0$, $f(1) = 1$, and $\log f$ is convex on $(0, \infty)$. Then $f(x) = \Gamma(x)$ for every $x > 0$.

## Sources

- F. W. J. Olver et al. (eds.), *NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions*, Version 1.2.8 of 2026-09-15,
  <https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5>, §5.5(iv) (the Bohr–Mollerup theorem: a positive function $f$ on $(0, \infty)$ with
  $f(x + 1) = x f(x)$, $f(1) = 1$ and $\ln f$ convex is $\Gamma$).
