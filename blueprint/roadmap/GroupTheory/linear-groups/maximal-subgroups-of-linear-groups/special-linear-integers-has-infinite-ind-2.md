# SL_n(Z) has maximal subgroups of infinite index

For every $n \ge 2$ the group $\mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb{Z})$ has a maximal subgroup of infinite index; Kourovka Problem 2.55(b) has a positive answer. It follows from the theorem of Margulis and Soifer that a finitely generated linear group that is not virtually soluble has a maximal subgroup of infinite index.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.55 (V. P. Platonov); resolution: G. A. Margulis and G. A. Soifer, Soviet Math. Dokl. 18 (1977), 847-851.
