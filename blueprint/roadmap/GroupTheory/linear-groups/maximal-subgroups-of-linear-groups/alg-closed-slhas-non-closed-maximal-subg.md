# Kourovka Problem 2.54 over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero

The proposition that for every algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $0$ there is $n \ge 2$ such that $\mathrm{SL}_n(k)$ has a maximal subgroup that is not Zariski closed, where a set of matrices in $\mathrm{SL}_n(k)$ is Zariski closed when it is the set of common zeros in $\mathrm{SL}_n(k)$ of some family of polynomials over $k$ in the $n^2$ matrix entries.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.54 (V. P. Platonov); resolution: N. S. Romanovskii, Algebra i Logika 6 (1967), no. 4, 75-82; 7 (1968), no. 3, 123.
