# Linear groups in characteristic zero without identities contain free subgroups

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and $G \le \mathrm{GL}_n(K)$ a subgroup satisfying no non-trivial identity. Then $G$ contains a free subgroup of rank $2$. This follows from the Tits alternative in characteristic $0$: $G$ contains a non-abelian free subgroup or has a soluble subgroup of finite index, and the latter satisfies a non-trivial identity.
