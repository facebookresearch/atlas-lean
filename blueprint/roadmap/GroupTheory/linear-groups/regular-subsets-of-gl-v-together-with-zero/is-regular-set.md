# Regular subset of GL(V) together with zero

For natural numbers $p, m$ and a set $R$ of $m \times m$ matrices over $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ (acting on row vectors), the proposition that $R$ is regular: $|R| = p^m$, $0 \in R$, $1 \in R$, every nonzero element of $R$ is invertible, and $vx \ne vy$ for every nonzero row vector $v$ and all distinct $x, y \in R$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 10.48 (N. D. Podufalov).
