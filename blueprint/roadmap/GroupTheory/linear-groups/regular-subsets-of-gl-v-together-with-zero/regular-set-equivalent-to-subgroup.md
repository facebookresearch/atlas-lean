# Kourovka Problem 10.48(b) (regular sets and subgroups)

This is the proposition that for every prime $p$, every $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and every regular subset $R$ of the $m \times m$ matrices over $\mathbb{F}_p$, there is a subgroup $G \le \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb{F}_p)$ such that $G \cup \{0\}$ is reached from $R$ by finitely many steps $\tau$, $\varepsilon$, $\mu_g$. A set $R$ of matrices is regular when $|R| = p^m$, $0, 1 \in R$, every nonzero element of $R$ is invertible, and $vx \ne vy$ for every nonzero row vector $v \in \mathbb{F}_p^m$ and all distinct $x, y \in R$; a step replaces $R$ by $\{x^{-1} : x \in R\}$ (with $0^{-1} = 0$), by $\{1 - x : x \in R\}$, or by $\{x g^{-1} : x \in R\}$ for some nonzero $g \in R$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 10.48 (N. D. Podufalov).
