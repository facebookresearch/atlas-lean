# Kourovka Problem 2.58 (locally finitely stable realizations)

The proposition that every locally nilpotent (finitely generated subgroups nilpotent) torsion-free group $G$ has a faithful representation $\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)$ on a vector space $V$ over a field of characteristic $0$ in which the image of every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is stable. A set $S$ of linear automorphisms of a vector space $V$ is stable when there is a chain of subspaces $0 = W_0 \le W_1 \le \cdots \le W_m = V$ with $g(w) - w \in W_i$ for all $g \in S$, $i < m$ and $w \in W_{i+1}$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.58 (B. I. Plotkin).
