# A locally nilpotent torsion-free group without a locally finitely stable realization

There is a locally nilpotent torsion-free group that admits no faithful representation by linear automorphisms of a vector space over a field of characteristic $0$ in which every finitely generated subgroup acts stably. Kourovka Problem 2.58 has a negative answer. A set $S$ of linear automorphisms of a vector space $V$ is stable when there is a chain of subspaces $0 = W_0 \le W_1 \le \cdots \le W_m = V$ with $g(w) - w \in W_i$ for all $g \in S$, $i < m$ and $w \in W_{i+1}$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.58 (locally finitely stable realizations)](locally-nilpotent-torsion-free-is-locall.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.58 (B. I. Plotkin); resolution: L. A. Simonyan, Siberian Math. J. 12 (1971), 602-606.
