# Finitely generated linear groups in characteristic p are virtually residually p

Let $p$ be a prime, $K$ a field of characteristic $p$ and $G$ a finitely generated subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$. Then $G$ has a subgroup of finite index that is residually a finite $p$-group. A group $H$ is residually a finite $p$-group if every element $h \ne 1$ lies outside some normal subgroup of $H$ of index $p^k$ for some $k \ge 0$. This is the positive-characteristic part of the answer to Kourovka Problem 2.46.
