# Kourovka Problem 1.88 (linear groups without identities)

The proposition that for every field $K$ of characteristic $0$, every $n \in \mathbb N$ and every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$ that satisfies no non-trivial identity (every $w \ne 1$ in the free group on countably many generators takes a value $\ne 1$ on some elements of $G$), $G$ contains a non-abelian free subgroup: there is an injective homomorphism from the free group of rank $2$ to $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.88 (A. L. Shmel'kin).
