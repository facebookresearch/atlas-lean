# Non-free points outside the rhombus

Some complex number $z$ with $|\operatorname{Re} z|/2 + |\operatorname{Im} z| > 1$ (outside the closed rhombus with vertices $\pm 2$, $\pm i$) is not a free point. A complex number $z$ is a free point if the matrices $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ z & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ freely generate a free group of rank $2$: the homomorphism from $F_2$ sending its two basis elements to them is injective. This answers Kourovka Problem 4.41 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 4.41 (free points outside the rhombus)](points-outside-rhombus-free.md).
