# Elementary transformations of regular sets

For sets $R, R'$ of $m \times m$ matrices over $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, the proposition that $R'$ is obtained from $R$ by one step: $R' = \{x^{-1} : x \in R\}$ (matrix inverse, with $x^{-1} = 0$ for non-invertible $x$, in particular $0 \mapsto 0$), or $R' = \{1 - x : x \in R\}$, or $R' = \{x g^{-1} : x \in R\}$ for some nonzero $g \in R$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 10.48 (N. D. Podufalov).
