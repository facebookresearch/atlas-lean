# Kourovka Problem 2.55(b) (maximal subgroups of infinite index in SL_n(Z))

The proposition that for every $n \ge 2$ the group $\mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb{Z})$ of integer $n \times n$ matrices of determinant $1$ has a maximal subgroup (a proper subgroup contained in no larger proper subgroup) of infinite index.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.55 (V. P. Platonov); resolution: G. A. Margulis and G. A. Soifer, Soviet Math. Dokl. 18 (1977), 847-851.
