# Kourovka Problem 1.73 (conjugacy classes of maximal periodic subgroups)

The proposition that for every $n$ and every finitely generated subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Z})$, the maximal periodic subgroups of $G$ lie in finitely many conjugacy classes of $G$: there is a finite set $\mathcal{F}$ of subgroups of $G$ such that every maximal periodic subgroup of $G$ equals $gKg^{-1}$ for some $K \in \mathcal{F}$ and $g \in G$. A subgroup is periodic if all its elements have finite order, and maximal periodic if it is periodic and contained in no strictly larger periodic subgroup.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.73 (V. P. Platonov).
