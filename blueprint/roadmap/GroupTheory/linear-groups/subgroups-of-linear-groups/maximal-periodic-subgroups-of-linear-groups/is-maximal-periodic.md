# Maximal periodic subgroups

A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is maximal periodic if it is periodic (all its elements have finite order) and every periodic subgroup $K$ of $G$ with $H \le K$ equals $H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.73 (V. P. Platonov).
