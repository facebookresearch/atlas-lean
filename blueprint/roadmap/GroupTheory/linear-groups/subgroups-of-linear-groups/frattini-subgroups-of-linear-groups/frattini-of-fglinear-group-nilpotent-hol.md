# The Frattini subgroup of a finitely generated linear group is nilpotent

Let $K$ be a field, $n$ a natural number and $G$ a finitely generated subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$. Then the Frattini subgroup of $G$, the intersection of all maximal subgroups of $G$ (all of $G$ if there are none), is nilpotent. This answers Kourovka Problem 1.32 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.32 (M. I. Kargapolov); resolution: V. P. Platonov, Soviet Math. Dokl. 7 (1966), 1557-1560.
