# Embedding $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Q})$ in a division ring (Kourovka 11.110)

The statement that the group $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Q})$ embeds in the multiplicative group of some
division ring: there are a division ring $D$ and an injective group homomorphism
$\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Q}) \to D^\times$.
