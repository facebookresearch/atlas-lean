# Kourovka Problem 3.14 (finite linear groups over skew fields of characteristic zero)

The proposition that there is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for every skew field (division ring) $T$ of characteristic zero, every $n$ and every finite subgroup $G$ of the group of invertible $n \times n$ matrices over $T$, $G$ has a soluble normal subgroup of index at most $f(n)$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.14 (A. E. Zalesskiĭ).
