# Finite linear groups over skew fields have soluble normal subgroups of bounded index

There is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for every division ring $T$ of characteristic zero and every $n$, every finite group of invertible $n \times n$ matrices over $T$ has a soluble normal subgroup of index at most $f(n)$. This is a Jordan-type theorem for skew fields; Hartley and Shahabi Shojaei prove it using the classification of finite simple groups, answering Kourovka Problem 3.14.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.14 (A. E. Zalesskiĭ); resolution: B. Hartley and M. A. Shahabi Shojaei, Math. Proc. Cambridge Phil. Soc. 92 (1982), 55-64.
