# Kourovka Problem 2.46 in characteristic zero

The proposition that for every field $K$ of characteristic $0$, every $n$ and every finitely generated subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$, all sufficiently large primes $p$ have the property that $G$ is virtually residually a finite $p$-group (the finite-index subgroup may depend on $p$). A subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$ is virtually residually a finite $p$-group if some subgroup of finite index in $G$ is residually a finite $p$-group. A group $H$ is residually a finite $p$-group if every element $h \ne 1$ lies outside some normal subgroup of $H$ of index $p^k$ for some $k \ge 0$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.46 (Yu. I. Merzlyakov).
