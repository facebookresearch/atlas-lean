# Linear groups that are virtually residually a finite p-group

For a natural number $p$, a field $K$, a natural number $n$ and a subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$, $G$ is virtually residually a finite $p$-group if it has a subgroup $H$ of finite index such that every element $h \ne 1$ of $H$ lies outside some normal subgroup of $H$ whose index is $p^k$ for some $k \ge 0$, an infinite index counting as $0$ (this matters only for $p = 0$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.46 (Yu. I. Merzlyakov).
