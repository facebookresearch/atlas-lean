# The one-relator group of Kourovka Problem 18.86 is linear

Let $G$ be the group with generators
$a, b$ and the single defining relation $[[a, b], b] = 1$, where $[x, y] = x^{-1} y^{-1} x y$. Then
$G$ is linear: there are a field $K$, a natural number $n$ and an injective group homomorphism
$G \to \mathrm{GL}_n(K)$.

Roman'kov); resolution: V. G. Bardakov and O. V. Bryukhanov, Vestnik Novosibirsk Univ. Ser. Mat. Mekh. Inf. 7 (2007), no. 3, 45-58 (Russian); V. A. Roman'kov, J. Siberian Federal Univ. Math. Phys. 6 (2013), no. 4, 516-520, Kourovka Archive, Problem 18.86: Yes, it is linear, since it embeds in Hol F_2 (adding c = [a, b] gives G = ⟨a, b, c | a^b = ac, c^b = c⟩), and Hol F_2 is linear by Corollary 3 of Bardakov-Bryukhanov; another proof by Roman'kov, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
