# The one-relator group of Kourovka Problem 18.86

This is the group
$G = \langle a, b \mid [[a, b], b] = 1 \rangle$ with generators $a, b$ and the single defining
relation $[[a, b], b] = 1$, where $[x, y] = x^{-1} y^{-1} x y$; that is, $G$ is the quotient of the
free group on $a$ and $b$ by the normal subgroup generated by $[[a, b], b]$.
