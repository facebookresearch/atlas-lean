# Kourovka Problem 1.32 (Frattini subgroups of finitely generated linear groups)

The proposition that for every field $K$ and natural number $n$, the Frattini subgroup (the intersection of all maximal subgroups) of every finitely generated subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$ is nilpotent.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.32 (M. I. Kargapolov).
