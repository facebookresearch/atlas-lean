# Generation of the special linear group by elementary transvections

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the generation of $\mathrm{SL}_n$ over a field by
elementary transvections, and its consequence that a homomorphism from $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ to an abelian group
that is trivial on the upper unitriangular matrices factors through the determinant. For a
non-archimedean local field $F$ every smooth character of $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ is trivial on these matrices (its open
kernel contains small elementary transvections, and conjugation by diagonal matrices gives all of them), so
the smooth characters are those of $F^\times$ composed with the determinant. Without that hypothesis the
conclusion fails: the sign character of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_2) \cong S_3$ is nontrivial, although the determinant is
trivial there. Let $F$ be a field and $n \ge 1$.

- **Generation.** $\mathrm{SL}_n(F)$ is generated by the
  [elementary transvections](double-commutators-of-transvections/is-elementary-transvection.md)
  $1 + a e_{ij}$, $i \ne j$, $a \in F^\times$: elementary row operations of this type reduce a matrix of
  determinant $1$ to the identity (for $n = 1$ the group is trivial).
- **Conjugates of the unipotent radical.** Consequently $\mathrm{SL}_n(F)$ is generated by the
  $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$-conjugates of the group of upper unitriangular matrices, every transvection
  $1 + a e_{ij}$ with $i > j$ being conjugate by a permutation matrix to one with $i < j$; so a
  homomorphism from $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ to an abelian group that is trivial on the upper unitriangular
  matrices is trivial on $\mathrm{SL}_n(F)$, and factors through $\det$.

reduction as its proof, and one for the consequence on homomorphisms to abelian groups, each citing a
