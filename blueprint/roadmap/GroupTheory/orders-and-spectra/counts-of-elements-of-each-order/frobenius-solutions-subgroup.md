# Frobenius's conjecture on the solutions of x^n = 1 (statement)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $G$ be a finite group and $n$ a positive integer dividing $|G|$. If the equation $x^n = 1$ has exactly $n$ solutions in $G$, then the set $\{x \in G : x^n = 1\}$ is a subgroup of $G$. By Frobenius's theorem the number of solutions is always a multiple of $n$.
