# Iiyori–Yamaki theorem (Frobenius's conjecture)

Let $G$ be a finite group and $n$ a positive integer dividing $|G|$. If the equation $x^n = 1$ has exactly $n$ solutions in $G$, then the set $\{x \in G : x^n = 1\}$ is a subgroup of $G$. Such a subgroup is characteristic. This is the theorem of Iiyori and Yamaki, proved using the classification of finite simple groups.
