# Kourovka Problem 21.43 (many elements of one order force solubility)

This is the proposition that for every finite group $G$ and every positive integer $k$, if at least half of the elements of $G$ have order exactly $k$ (that is, $2\,|\{g \in G : \operatorname{ord}(g) = k\}| \ge |G|$), then $G$ is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.43.
