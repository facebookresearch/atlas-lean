# Kourovka Problem 5.11 (isolated subgroups from centralizer conditions)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $G$ be a finite group and $\pi$ a nonempty set of primes dividing $|G|$ that does not contain every prime divisor of $|G|$. Suppose that for every nonidentity element $g$ whose order has all prime divisors in $\pi$, the centralizer $C_G(g)$ has order with all prime divisors in $\pi$. Then $G$ has a subgroup $U$ with $U \ne 1$ and $U \ne G$ such that $U \cap gUg^{-1}$ is $1$ or $U$ for every $g \in G$, and $C_G(u) \le U$ for every $u \in U \setminus \{1\}$. The subgroups $1$ and $G$ always satisfy the two conditions, so they are excluded.

Gruenberg); resolution: J. S. Williams, Prime graph components of finite groups, J. Algebra 69 (1981), 487-513, https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(81)90218-0.
