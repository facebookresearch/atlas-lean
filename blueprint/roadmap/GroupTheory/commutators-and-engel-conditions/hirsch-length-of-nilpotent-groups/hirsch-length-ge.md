# Cyclic series with at least h infinite factors

A group $G$ satisfies this condition when it has a finite cyclic series $1 = G_0 \le G_1 \le \cdots \le G_m = G$, each $G_i$ normal in $G_{i+1} = \langle G_i, g_i \rangle$, at least $h$ of whose factors are infinite ($G_i$ of infinite index in $G_{i+1}$). For a polycyclic group (for example a finitely generated nilpotent group) every cyclic series has the same number of infinite factors, the Hirsch length, so the condition says that the Hirsch length is at least $h$. A group with no finite cyclic series satisfies it for no $h$: the additive group of rationals has torsion-free rank one but is not finitely generated.
