# Hirsch length at least h

A group $G$ has torsion-free rank (Hirsch length) at least $h$ when it has a cyclic series $1 = G_0 \le G_1 \le \cdots \le G_m = G$, each $G_i$ normal in $G_{i+1} = \langle G_i, g_i \rangle$, at least $h$ of whose factors are infinite ($G_i$ of infinite index in $G_{i+1}$). For a polycyclic group every cyclic series has the same number of infinite factors.
