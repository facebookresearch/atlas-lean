# Kourovka Problem 8.76 (Hirsch length versus ranks of abelian subgroups)

The proposition that there is a real $c > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$ some finitely generated nilpotent group all of whose abelian subgroups have torsion-free rank at most $n$ has Hirsch length at least $c n^2$. With $f(n)$ the largest Hirsch length of a finitely generated nilpotent group whose abelian subgroups have torsion-free rank at most $n$, this says $f(n)$ is bounded below by a quadratic.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.76 (John S. Wilson).
