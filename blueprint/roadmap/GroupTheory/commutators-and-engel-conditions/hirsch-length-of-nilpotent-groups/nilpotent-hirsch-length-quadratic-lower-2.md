# Hirsch length can be quadratic in the ranks of abelian subgroups

There is $c > 0$ such that for all large $n$ some finitely generated nilpotent group has all abelian subgroups of torsion-free rank at most $n$ and Hirsch length at least $cn^2$. Milenteva proves $f(n) \ge (n^2 - 4)/8 + n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.76 (John S. Wilson); resolution: M. V. Milenteva, On the torsion-free ranks of finitely generated nilpotent groups and of their abelian subgroups, J. Group Theory 7 (2004), no. 3, 403-408, https://doi.org/10.1515/jgth.2004.015.
