# Kourovka Problem 2.48 (local identities in connected topological groups)

The proposition that whenever $G$ is a connected Hausdorff topological group, $f$ is a word in $k$ variables (an element of the free group of rank $k$), and there is a neighbourhood $U$ of $1$ in $G$ with $f(u_1, \dots, u_k) = 1$ for all $u_1, \dots, u_k \in U$, then $f(g_1, \dots, g_k) = 1$ for all $g_1, \dots, g_k \in G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 2.48 (V. P. Platonov; question of N. Aronszajn; marked solved in the main list); resolution: I. N. Zyabrev and E. A. Reznichenko, On identities in connected topological groups, arXiv:2406.05203 (2024).
