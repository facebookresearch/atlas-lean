# Torsion-free groups with a nontrivial upper central quotient of bounded exponent

There are a torsion-free group $G$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $Z_n(G) \ne G$ and $g^e \in Z_n(G)$ for all $g \in G$ for some fixed $e \ge 1$: the quotient $G/Z_n(G)$ by a term of the upper central series is nontrivial and periodic of bounded exponent. This answers Kourovka Problem 2.15 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.15 (G. A. Karasëv); resolution: S. I. Adian, Proc. Steklov Inst. Math. 112 (1971), 61-69.
