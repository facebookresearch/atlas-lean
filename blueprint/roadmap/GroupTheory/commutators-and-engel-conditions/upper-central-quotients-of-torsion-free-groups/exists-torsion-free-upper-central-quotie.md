# Kourovka Problem 2.15 (torsion-free groups with a periodic upper central quotient)

The proposition that there are a torsion-free group $G$ and a natural number $n$ such that the $n$-th term $Z_n(G)$ of the upper central series is a proper subgroup and $G/Z_n(G)$ has finite exponent: some $e \ge 1$ satisfies $g^e \in Z_n(G)$ for all $g \in G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.15 (G. A. Karasëv).
