# Kourovka Problem 3.46 (finitely many maximal locally soluble normal subgroups)

The proposition that there is a group with more than one, but only finitely many, maximal locally soluble normal subgroups (locally soluble normal subgroups not properly contained in another one).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 3.46 (B. I. Plotkin).
