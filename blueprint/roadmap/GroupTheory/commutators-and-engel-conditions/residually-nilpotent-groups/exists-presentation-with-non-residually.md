# Mikhailov–Passi question on free central extensions (Kourovka 16.65)

The assertion that
there exist a free group $F$ and a normal subgroup $R$ of $F$ such that $F/R$ is finitely
presented and residually torsion-free nilpotent, while $F/[F, R]$ is not residually nilpotent,
where $[F, R]$ is the subgroup of $F$ generated by all commutators $[f, r]$ with $f \in F$ and
$r \in R$.
