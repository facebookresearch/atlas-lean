# Residually torsion-free nilpotent group

A group $G$ is residually torsion-free nilpotent
if for every element $g \ne 1$ of $G$ there is a normal subgroup $N$ of $G$ with $g \notin N$ such
that the quotient $G/N$ is nilpotent and torsion-free, that is, every nontrivial element of $G/N$
has infinite order.
