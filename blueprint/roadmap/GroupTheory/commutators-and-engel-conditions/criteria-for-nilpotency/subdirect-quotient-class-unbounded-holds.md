# Quotients of subdirect products have unbounded nilpotency class

For every $c$ there are groups $G_1, \dots, G_n$ and subdirect products $H \trianglelefteq G \le \prod_i G_i$ (both projecting onto each $G_i$) such that $G/H$ is nilpotent of class at least $c$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.8 (Yu. M. Gorchakov); resolution: E. I. Khukhro, On nilpotent subdirect products, Sibirsk. Mat. Zh. 23, no. 6 (1982), 178-180.
