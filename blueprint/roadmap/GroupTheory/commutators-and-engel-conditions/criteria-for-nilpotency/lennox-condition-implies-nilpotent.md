# Kourovka Problem 8.32 (Lennox's index condition)

The proposition that a finitely generated group $G$ is nilpotent if, for every set $\pi$ of natural numbers and every subgroup $M$, whenever $G/M^G$ is a finite group whose order is a $\pi$-number ($M^G$ the normal closure of $M$), the index $|G : M|$ is finite and a $\pi$-number. A $\pi$-number is one all of whose prime divisors lie in $\pi$; $\pi = \emptyset$ is allowed.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.32 (J. C. Lennox).
