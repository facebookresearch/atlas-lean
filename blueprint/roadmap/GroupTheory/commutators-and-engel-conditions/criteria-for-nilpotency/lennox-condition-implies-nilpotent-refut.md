# Lennox's index condition does not imply nilpotency

There is a finitely generated group $G$ that is not nilpotent such that, for every set $\pi$ of natural numbers and every subgroup $M$ of $G$, whenever $G/M^G$ is a finite group whose order is a $\pi$-number ($M^G$ the normal closure of $M$), the index $|G : M|$ is finite and a $\pi$-number. An example is Obraztsov's $2$-generator group $G$ with a normal simple infinite subgroup $L$ and $G/L \cong \mathbb Z^2$, in which every subgroup $M$ with $G/M^G$ finite is normal. Kourovka Problem 8.32 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 8.32 (Lennox's index condition)](lennox-condition-implies-nilpotent.md).

Lennox); resolution: V. N. Obraztsov, A new embedding scheme for groups and some applications, J. Austral. Math. Soc. Ser. A 61 (1996), 267-288, Corollary to Theorem G, https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788700000252.
