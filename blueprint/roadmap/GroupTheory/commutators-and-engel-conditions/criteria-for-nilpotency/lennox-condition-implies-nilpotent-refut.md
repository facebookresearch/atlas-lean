# Lennox's index condition does not imply nilpotency

There is a finitely generated non-nilpotent group satisfying Lennox's condition: Obraztsov's $2$-generator group $G$ with a normal simple infinite subgroup $L$ and $G/L \cong \mathbb Z^2$, in which every subgroup $M$ with $G/M^G$ finite is normal. Kourovka Problem 8.32 has a negative answer.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.32 (J. C. Lennox); resolution: V. N. Obraztsov, A new embedding scheme for groups and some applications, J. Austral. Math. Soc. Ser. A 61 (1996), 267-288, Corollary to Theorem G, https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788700000252.
