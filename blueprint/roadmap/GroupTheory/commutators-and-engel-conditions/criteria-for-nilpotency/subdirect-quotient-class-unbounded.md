# Kourovka Problem 7.8 (nilpotency class of quotients of subdirect products)

The proposition that for every $c \in \mathbb N$ there are $n$, groups $G_1, \dots, G_n$ and subgroups $H \le G$ of $\prod_i G_i$, both projecting onto every factor, with $H$ normal in $G$ and $G/H$ nilpotent of class at least $c$: the nilpotency class of $G/H$ is unbounded as $n$ grows.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.8 (Yu. M. Gorchakov).
