# Kourovka Problem 2.59 (faithful stable actions of nilpotent groups)

The proposition that every nilpotent group $\Gamma$ of nilpotency class exactly $c$ acts faithfully by automorphisms on some abelian group $A$ with a series $0 = A_0 \le A_1 \le \cdots \le A_{c+1} = A$ of subgroups of length $c + 1$ that is stable: $g a - a \in A_i$ for all $g \in \Gamma$ and $a \in A_{i+1}$. (The problem's class $n - 1$ and length $n$.)

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.59 (B. I. Plotkin).
