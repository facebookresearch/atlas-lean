# A nilpotent group of class c without a faithful stable action of length c + 1

For some $c$ there is a nilpotent group of class $c$ admitting no faithful action by automorphisms on an abelian group that stabilizes a series of length $c + 1$. Kourovka Problem 2.59 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.59 (faithful stable actions of nilpotent groups)](nilpotent-group-has-faithful-stable-acti.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.59 (B. I. Plotkin); resolution: E. Rips, Israel J. Math. 12 (1972), 342-346.
