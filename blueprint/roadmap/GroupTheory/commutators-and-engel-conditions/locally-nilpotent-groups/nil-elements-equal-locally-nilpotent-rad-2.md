# Nil-elements of a linear group need not form its locally nilpotent radical

There are a division ring $D$ and a subgroup $G \le \mathrm{GL}_n(D)$ whose set of nil-elements differs from its Hirsch–Plotkin radical. Golod's non-nilpotent $3$-generator nil-group is residually torsion-free nilpotent, hence orderable, hence embeds in the multiplicative group of a division ring (Mal'cev); all its elements are nil-elements, but it is not locally nilpotent. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.64 (nil-elements and the locally nilpotent radical)](nil-elements-equal-locally-nilpotent-rad.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.64 (B. I. Plotkin); resolution: V. A. Roman'kov, 1978, using E. S. Golod's nil-group and Mal'cev's embedding of orderable groups in division rings.
