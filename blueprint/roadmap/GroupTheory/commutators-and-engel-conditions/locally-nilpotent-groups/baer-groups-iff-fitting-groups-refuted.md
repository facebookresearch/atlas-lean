# A Baer group that is not a Fitting group

The classes of Baer groups and Fitting groups differ; since every Fitting group is a Baer group, there is a group all of whose cyclic subgroups are subnormal that is not generated by its nilpotent normal subgroups. Kourovka Problem 2.21 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.21 (Baer groups and Fitting groups)](baer-groups-iff-fitting-groups.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.21 (Sh. S. Kemkhadze); resolution: R. S. Dark, A prime Baer group, Math. Z. 105 (1968), 294-298.
