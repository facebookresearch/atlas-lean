# Kourovka Problem 1.39 (products of normal binary nilpotent subgroups)

The proposition that a group $G = AB$ that is the product (join) of two normal subgroups $A$, $B$, each binary nilpotent, is binary nilpotent.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.39 (Sh. S. Kemkhadze).
