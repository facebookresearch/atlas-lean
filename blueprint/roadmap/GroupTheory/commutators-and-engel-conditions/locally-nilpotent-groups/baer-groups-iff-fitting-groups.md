# Kourovka Problem 2.21 (Baer groups and Fitting groups)

The proposition that a group is a Baer group (every cyclic subgroup subnormal) if and only if it is a Fitting group (generated by its nilpotent normal subgroups).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.21 (Sh. S. Kemkhadze).
