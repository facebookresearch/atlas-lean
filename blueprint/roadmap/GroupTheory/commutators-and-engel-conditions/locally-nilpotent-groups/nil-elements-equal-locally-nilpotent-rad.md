# Kourovka Problem 2.64 (nil-elements and the locally nilpotent radical)

The proposition that for every division ring $D$, every $n$ and every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(D)$, the set of nil-elements of $G$ equals its locally nilpotent (Hirsch–Plotkin) radical, the join of all locally nilpotent normal subgroups of $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.64 (B. I. Plotkin).
