# Locally nilpotent groups with the minimal condition on centralizers are hypercentral

Every
locally nilpotent group satisfying the minimal condition on centralizers is hypercentral. This
answers Kourovka Problem 13.16 (F. O. Wagner) in the affirmative (V. V. Bludov, 1998).

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 13.16 (F. O. Wagner); resolution: V. V. Bludov, Algebra and Logic 37 (1998), no. 3, 151-156, Kourovka Archive, Problem 13.16: Yes, it is.
