# A product of two normal binary nilpotent subgroups need not be binary nilpotent

There is a group that is the product of two normal binary nilpotent subgroups but is not binary nilpotent. Kourovka Problem 1.39 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 1.39 (products of normal binary nilpotent subgroups)](product-of-normal-binary-nilpotent-is-bi.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.39 (Sh. S. Kemkhadze); resolution: A. I. Sozutov, Algebra and Logic 30 (1991), no. 1, 70-72.
