# Hypercentral group (Kourovka 13.16)

A group $G$ is hypercentral if for every proper normal
subgroup $N$ of $G$ there is some $g \in G$ with $g \notin N$ and $g h g^{-1} h^{-1} \in N$ for all
$h \in G$; that is, every nontrivial quotient $G/N$ has nontrivial centre (equivalently, the
transfinite upper central series of $G$ reaches $G$).
