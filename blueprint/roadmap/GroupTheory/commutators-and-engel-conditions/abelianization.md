# Abelianization of a group

Let $G$ be a group and let $[G, G]$ be its commutator subgroup, the subgroup of $G$ generated by all commutators of elements of $G$; it is a normal subgroup of $G$. The *abelianization* of $G$ is the quotient group $G^{\mathrm{ab}} = G/[G, G]$.
