# Finitely generated perfect groups of weight greater than one

There is an infinite finitely generated perfect group that is not the normal closure of any single element; Kourovka Problem 5.52 has a negative answer. Chen and Lodha show that a free product of nontrivial left-orderable groups is never the normal closure of one element, and take free products of finitely generated perfect left-orderable groups. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.52 (the Wiegold problem)](finitely-generated-perfect-normally-mono.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 5.52 (J. Wiegold; marked solved in the main list); resolution: L. Chen and Y. Lodha, The Wiegold problem and free products of left-orderable groups, arXiv:2510.26073 (2025).
