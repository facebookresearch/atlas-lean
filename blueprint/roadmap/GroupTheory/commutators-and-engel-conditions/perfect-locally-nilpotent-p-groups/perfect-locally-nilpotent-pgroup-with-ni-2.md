# No infinite perfect locally nilpotent $p$-group has only nilpotent proper subgroups

There is
no prime $p$ and no infinite locally nilpotent $p$-group $G$ (every element of $G$ has order a
power of $p$) such that $G = [G, G]$ and every proper subgroup of $G$ is nilpotent.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 14.86 (J. Wiegold); resolution: A. O. Asar, Locally nilpotent p-groups whose proper subgroups are hypercentral or nilpotent-by-Chernikov, J. London Math. Soc. (2) 61 (2000), no. 2, 412-422, Kourovka Archive, Problem 14.86 (answer: No, it does not exist).
