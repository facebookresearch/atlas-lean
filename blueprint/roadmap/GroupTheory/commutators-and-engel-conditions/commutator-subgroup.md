# Commutator subgroup of a group

Let $G$ be a group. Here we use the convention that the *commutator* of $g, h \in G$ is $[g, h] = ghg^{-1}h^{-1}$. The *commutator subgroup* $[G, G]$ of $G$ is the subgroup of $G$ generated by all commutators $[g, h]$ with $g, h \in G$.

## Sources

- David S. Dummit and Richard M. Foote, Abstract Algebra, 3rd ed., John Wiley & Sons, 2004, §5.4, p. 169, where the commutator is defined as g⁻¹h⁻¹gh; this convention gives the same set of commutators, and hence the same commutator subgroup.
