# Kourovka Problem 6.36 (gamma-diagrams and nilpotent completions)

The proposition that there is a $\gamma$-diagram not isomorphic to the nilpotent-completion diagram of any group $H$, isomorphism meaning group isomorphisms $G_s \cong H/\gamma_s H$ commuting with the maps. A $\gamma$-diagram is a sequence of groups $G_1 \leftarrow G_2 \leftarrow \cdots$ with surjections $G_{s+1} \to G_s$ whose kernel is $\gamma_s(G_{s+1})$ (where $\gamma_1$ is the whole group); the nilpotent-completion diagram of $H$ is $H/\gamma_1 H \leftarrow H/\gamma_2 H \leftarrow \cdots$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.36 (Yu. I. Merzlyakov; question of J. W. Grossman).
