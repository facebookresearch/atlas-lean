# Stabilizer of a point under a group action

Let a group $G$ act on a set $X$, and let $x \in X$. The *stabilizer* of $x$ is the subgroup $G_x = \{g \in G : g \cdot x = x\}$ of $G$, made up of the elements of $G$ that fix $x$.
