# Kourovka Problem 6.53, first part (groups embedding in Frobenius kernels)

The proposition that every group $K$ admits an injective homomorphism into the kernel $F$ of some Frobenius group $G$ with kernel $F$ and complement $H$. Here $G$ is a Frobenius group with kernel $F$ and complement $H$ when $F$ is a normal subgroup, $F \cap H = 1$, $FH = G$, $F \ne 1$, $H \ne 1$, $H \cap gHg^{-1} = 1$ for every $g \in G \setminus H$, and the nonidentity elements of $F$ are exactly the elements of $G$ lying in no conjugate of $H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.53 (V. P. Shunkov); resolution: V. V. Bludov, On Frobenius groups, Siberian Math. J. 38 (1997), no. 6, 1054-1056, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02675933.
