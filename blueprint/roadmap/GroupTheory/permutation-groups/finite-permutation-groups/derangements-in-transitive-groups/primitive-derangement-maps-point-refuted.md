# Primitive groups with no derangement mapping one given point to another

There are a finite primitive permutation group $G$ on a set $\Omega$ and distinct points $\alpha, \beta$ such that every $g \in G$ with $g(\alpha) = \beta$ fixes some point; Kourovka Problem 8.75 has a negative answer. Müller's example is a primitive action of the Steinberg triality group ${}^3D_4(2)$ of degree $4064256$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 8.75 (Thompson's question on derangements in primitive groups)](primitive-derangement-maps-point.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 8.75 (J. G. Thompson; marked solved in the main list); resolution: P. Müller, On transitive sets of derangements in primitive groups, arXiv:2304.08459 (2023).
