# Kourovka Problem 8.75 (Thompson's question on derangements in primitive groups)

The proposition that for every finite set $\Omega$, every primitive group $G$ of permutations of $\Omega$ (transitive with no nontrivial blocks) and all distinct $\alpha, \beta \in \Omega$, some $g \in G$ maps $\alpha$ to $\beta$ and fixes no point of $\Omega$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 8.75 (J. G. Thompson; marked solved in the main list); resolution: P. Müller, On transitive sets of derangements in primitive groups, arXiv:2304.08459 (2023).
