# Kourovka Problem 7.16 (elements of prime-power order outside a proper subgroup)

The proposition that for every proper subgroup $H$ of a finite group $G$ there is an element of $G$ whose order is a positive power of a prime and which is conjugate to no element of $H$. Equivalently, every finite transitive permutation group of degree at least $2$ contains a fixed-point-free element of prime-power order.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.16 (R. L. Griess); resolution: B. Fein, W. M. Kantor and M. Schacher, Relative Brauer groups II, J. Reine Angew. Math. 328 (1981), 39-57, https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1981.328.39.
