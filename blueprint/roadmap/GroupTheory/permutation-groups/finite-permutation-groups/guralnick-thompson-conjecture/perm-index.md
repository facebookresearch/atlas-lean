# Index of a permutation

For a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the index $\operatorname{ind}\sigma$ is the sum of $\ell - 1$ over the cycle lengths $\ell \ge 2$ in the cycle type of $\sigma$; fixed points contribute $0$. Equivalently, it is $n$ minus the number of orbits of $\langle\sigma\rangle$.

Source: R. M. Guralnick and J. G. Thompson, Finite groups of genus zero, J. Algebra 131 (1990), 303-341, as recalled in K. Magaard, S. Shpectorov and G. Wang, Generating sets of affine groups of low genus, arXiv:1108.4833, Section 3.
