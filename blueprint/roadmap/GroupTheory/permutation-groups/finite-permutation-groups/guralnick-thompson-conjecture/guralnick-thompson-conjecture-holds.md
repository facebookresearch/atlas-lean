# Composition factors of monodromy groups of genus g

For every natural number $g$ there is $N$ such that every composition factor of the group generated by a genus $g$ system that is neither cyclic nor isomorphic to an alternating group has order at most $N$. A genus $g$ system of degree $n$ is a tuple $x_0, \dots, x_{r-1}$ of non-identity permutations of $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ with $x_0 \cdots x_{r-1} = 1$ that generate a transitive subgroup of $S_n$ and satisfy $\sum_i \operatorname{ind} x_i = 2(n + g - 1)$ (subtraction in $\mathbb{N}$, which truncates only when $n = g = 0$; see [genus $g$ systems](is-genus-system.md)), where the index of a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ is $\operatorname{ind}\sigma = \sum (\ell - 1)$ over the cycle lengths $\ell$ of $\sigma$ ($n$ minus the number of orbits of $\langle\sigma\rangle$). A composition factor of a group is a simple group that is a homomorphic image of a subnormal subgroup. This is the Guralnick–Thompson conjecture, proved by Frohardt and Magaard.
