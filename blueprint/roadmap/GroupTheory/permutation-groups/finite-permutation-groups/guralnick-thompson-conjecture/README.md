# Guralnick–Thompson conjecture

The conjecture of Guralnick and Thompson that for every genus $g$, apart from the alternating groups
$A_n$ with $n > 4$ and the cyclic groups of prime order, only finitely many simple groups occur as
composition factors of the monodromy groups of meromorphic functions on compact connected Riemann
surfaces of genus $g$.
