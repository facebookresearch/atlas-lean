# Composition factor of a group

A group $S$ is a composition factor of a group $G$ when $S$ is simple and some subnormal subgroup $H$
of $G$ has a surjective homomorphism onto $S$; for finite $G$ this means that $S$ occurs as a factor
of a composition series of $G$. The definition is the same as that of
[composition factors via subnormal subgroups](../../../soluble-finite-groups/solubility-nilpotency-and-subnormality/is-composition-factor.md);
it is stated again for the Guralnick–Thompson conjecture.
