# Kourovka Problem 21.3(a) (soluble subgroups of symmetric and alternating groups)

The proposition that there is $N$ such that for every $n \ge N$, both the symmetric group $S_n$ and the alternating group $A_n$ have the property that any two soluble subgroups $H, K$ admit $x$ with $H \cap x^{-1}Kx = 1$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.3, part a).
