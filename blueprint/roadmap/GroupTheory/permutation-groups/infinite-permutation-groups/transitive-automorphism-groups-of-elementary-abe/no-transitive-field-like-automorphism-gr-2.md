# No field-like transitive automorphism group of a countably infinite elementary abelian $2$-group

**No field-like transitive automorphism group of a countably infinite elementary abelian
$2$-group.** The conjecture of Kourovka Problem 18.60 holds: for every countably infinite
elementary abelian $2$-group $V$ (an abelian group with $v + v = 0$ for all $v \in V$) there is no
subgroup $G$ of the symmetric group $\mathrm{Sym}(V)$ consisting of additive bijections such that
(a) $G$ is transitive on $V \setminus \{0\}$, and (b) for every nonzero $v \in V$, writing
$H = G_v$ for the stabilizer of $v$ in $G$, there are a subgroup $V_v$ of $V$ with
$V = \langle v \rangle \oplus V_v$ that every element of $H$ maps into itself, a locally finite
field $P$ of characteristic $2$, a group isomorphism $\psi : H \to P^\times$ onto the
multiplicative group of $P$, and a bijection $\theta : V_v \to P$ such that
$\theta(h w) = \psi(h)\,\theta(w)$ for all $h \in H$ and $w \in V_v$.

Muliarchyk's Theorem 1 excludes a group transitive on the nonzero vectors of an
$\mathbb{F}_2$-space of dimension at least $4$ whose point stabilizer preserves a complement to
$\mathbb{F}_2 v$ and is transitive on its nonzero vectors; a similarity with $P^\times$ acting on
$P$ gives that transitivity (Corollary 2).
