# Embedding the free group of rank 2 in $\mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{N})$ with finitely many orbits (Kourovka 12.10)

**Embedding the free group of rank 2 in $\mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{N})$ with finitely many orbits
(Kourovka 12.10).** The statement, posed by P. Neumann and A. M. W. Glass, that there is an
injective group homomorphism $f$ from the free group $F_2$ of rank $2$ into the symmetric group
$\mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{N})$ of all permutations of $\mathbb{N}$ such that, for every non-identity
element $w$ of $F_2$, the cyclic subgroup generated by $f(w)$ has only finitely many orbits on
$\mathbb{N}$.
