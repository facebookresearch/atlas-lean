# The free group of rank 2 embeds in $\mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{N})$ with finitely many orbits

There
is an injective group homomorphism $f$ from the free group $F_2$ of rank $2$ into the symmetric
group $\mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{N})$ of all permutations of $\mathbb{N}$ such that, for every
non-identity element $w$ of $F_2$, the cyclic subgroup generated by $f(w)$ has only finitely many
orbits on $\mathbb{N}$. This answers Kourovka Problem 12.10 positively.

The Kourovka Archive answers 12.10 "Yes, it can" (Macpherson).
