# Highly transitive groups not acting by homeomorphisms of the rationals

There are a countable group $G$ and an action such that a countable group $G$ acts faithfully on an infinite set $\Omega$, $k$-transitively for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, and every nonidentity element of $G$ moves infinitely many points, but no bijection $\Omega \to \mathbb{Q}$ makes every element of $G$ act continuously on $\mathbb{Q}$; Kourovka Problem 5.40 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.40 (highly transitive groups of homeomorphisms of the rationals)](highly-transitive-acts-by-homeomorphisms.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.40 (P. M. Neumann); resolution: A. H. Mekler, Groups embeddable in the autohomeomorphisms of Q, J. London Math. Soc. (2) 33 (1986), 49-58, https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-33.1.49.
