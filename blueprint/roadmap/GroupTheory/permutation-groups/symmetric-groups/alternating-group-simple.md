# The alternating group on a finite set with at least five elements is simple

Let $X$ be a finite set with at least $5$ elements. Then the alternating group of $X$, the group of even permutations of $X$ (the kernel of the sign homomorphism from the symmetric group of $X$ to $\{\pm 1\}$), is a simple group: it has more than one element, and its only normal subgroups are the trivial subgroup and the whole group.
