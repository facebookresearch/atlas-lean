# Alternating group on a finite set

Let $X$ be a finite set, let $S_X$ be the group of permutations of $X$, and let $\operatorname{sign}\colon S_X \to \{\pm 1\}$ be the sign homomorphism. The *alternating group* $A_X$ on $X$ is the kernel of $\operatorname{sign}$: the subgroup of $S_X$ made up of the even permutations, those $\sigma$ with $\operatorname{sign}(\sigma) = 1$. For $X = \{1, \dots, n\}$ it is the alternating group $A_n$ of degree $n$.
