# Irreducible characters of degree prime to ℓ

For a natural number $\ell$ and a finite group $X$, $\mathrm{Irr}_{\ell'}(X)$ is the set of functions $X \to \mathbb{C}$ that are characters of simple finite-dimensional complex representations of $X$ whose dimension is coprime to $\ell$. For prime $\ell$ this is the same set as the irreducible characters of degree not divisible by $\ell$; for $\ell = 1$ it is the set of all irreducible characters.

Source: M. Cabanes and B. Späth, The McKay conjecture on character degrees, Ann. of Math. 203 (2026), no. 3, Theorem 1.1, arXiv:2410.20392.
