# The McKay conjecture

Let $X$ be a finite group, $\ell$ a prime, and $S$ a Sylow $\ell$-subgroup of $X$ with normalizer $N_X(S)$. For a finite group $Y$, let $\mathrm{Irr}_{\ell'}(Y)$ be the set of characters $Y \to \mathbb{C}$ of simple finite-dimensional complex representations of $Y$ whose dimension (degree) is coprime to $\ell$. The statement is

$$\lvert \mathrm{Irr}_{\ell'}(X) \rvert = \lvert \mathrm{Irr}_{\ell'}(N_X(S)) \rvert.$$
