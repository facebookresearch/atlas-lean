# Double coset fibre

Let $G$ be a group, let $\Gamma \le U$ and $V$ be subgroups of $G$, and let $\beta \in G$. Consider the natural map $\Gamma \backslash G / V \to U \backslash G / V$, $\Gamma g V \mapsto U g V$. Its fibre over $U \beta V$, the set of double cosets $\Gamma g V$ with $U g V = U \beta V$, has the same cardinality as the double coset space

$$\Gamma \backslash U / \left(U \cap \beta V \beta^{-1}\right),$$

where both $\Gamma$ and $U \cap \beta V \beta^{-1}$ are regarded as subgroups of $U$. Cardinalities are natural numbers, with an infinite set counted as $0$.
