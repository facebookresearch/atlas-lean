# Cd

Let $G$ be a finite group. The set of character degrees of $G$ is

$$\mathrm{cd}(G) = \{ \dim_{\mathbb{C}} V : V \text{ a simple finite-dimensional complex representation of } G \},$$

equivalently $\{\chi(1) : \chi \in \mathrm{Irr}(G)\}$, where $\mathrm{Irr}(G)$ is the set of irreducible complex characters of $G$.
