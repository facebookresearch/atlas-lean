# Is Discrete

Let $K_0 \subseteq K$ be fields ($K$ a $K_0$-algebra), let $v$ be a valuation on $K$ with values in a linearly ordered commutative group with zero $\Gamma_0$, and let $\rho : G \to \mathrm{PGL}_2(K_0)$ be a group homomorphism. Then $\rho$ is discrete if for every $\varepsilon \in \Gamma_0$ with $\varepsilon \neq 0$ the set

$$\left\{ \gamma \in G : \exists\, g \in \mathrm{GL}_2(K_0),\ [g] = \rho(\gamma),\ v(g_{ij}) \le 1 \text{ for all } i, j,\ \varepsilon \le v(\det g) \right\}$$

is finite, where $[g]$ is the class of $g$ in $\mathrm{PGL}_2(K_0)$ and entries and determinant are mapped into $K$ before applying $v$.
