# No insoluble group has independent permutation characters (Kourovka 11.9)

There is no
finite non-soluble group $G$ such that, choosing a representative $H$ of each conjugacy class of
subgroups of $G$, the rational-valued permutation characters $1_H^G$ of $G$ on the left cosets of
these subgroups $H$ are linearly independent over $\mathbb{Q}$.

Brescia, Ingrosso and Trombetti prove that these permutation characters are linearly independent
if and only if $G$ is cyclic; the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 11.9 solved, "No, there
are no such groups".

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 11.9 (Ya. G. Berkovich); resolution: M. Brescia, E. Ingrosso and M. Trombetti, On the independence of permutation characters, arXiv:2608.16957 (2026), main theorem: the characters `π_H`, `H` over representatives of the conjugacy classes of subgroups, are linearly independent iff `G` is cyclic; the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 11.9 solved, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16957.
