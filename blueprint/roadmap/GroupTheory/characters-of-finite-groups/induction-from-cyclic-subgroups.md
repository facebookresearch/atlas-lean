# Induction from cyclic subgroups in Grothendieck groups of modular representations

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the induction theorems for Grothendieck groups of
representations of finite groups that reduce the local and global Euler characteristic formulas to cyclic
Galois groups. Let $G$ be a finite group and $\ell$ a prime, and let $R_F(G)$ be the Grothendieck group of finitely
generated $F[G]$-modules for a field $F$. The statement used is: $R_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ is generated by
the images of the induction maps $\operatorname{Ind}_H^G \colon R_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(H) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to R_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$,
where $H$ runs over the cyclic subgroups of $G$ of order prime to $\ell$ (Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg, Lemma (7.3.4);
Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, Lemma I.2.10).

A good expansion has pages for:

1. Artin's induction theorem: for a field $F$ of characteristic $0$, $R_F(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ is generated by the
   images of $\operatorname{Ind}_H^G$ for cyclic $H$;
2. the decomposition map in the rationalised form that Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg use, a surjective homomorphism
   $R_{\mathbb{Q}_\ell}(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to R_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, natural in $G$ (Brauer);
3. the fact that the cyclic subgroups of order divisible by $\ell$ give no independent generators after tensoring
   with $\mathbb{Q}$: for a cyclic $H = H_{\ell'} \times H_\ell$, with $H_\ell$ its $\ell$-Sylow subgroup, and a simple
   $\mathbb{F}_\ell[H]$-module $M$, the group $H_\ell$ acts trivially on $M$ and
   $[\operatorname{Ind}_{H_{\ell'}}^H \operatorname{Res}^H_{H_{\ell'}} M] = |H_\ell| \, [M]$ in $R_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(H)$;
4. the statement above, deduced from 1–3,

each citing Serre's *Linear Representations of Finite Groups* at the locators given by Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg
and Milne (Theorem 26 of §12.5, Theorem 33 of §16.1, and Proposition 26 of §8.3, as they cite it), after checking
them against a pinned edition.

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links:

- the Grothendieck group $R_F(G)$ of finitely generated $F[G]$-modules and the induction maps $\operatorname{Ind}_H^G$ (Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg, (7.3.4), where it is written $K_0'(F[G])$), for the statement
- Artin's induction theorem in characteristic $0$ (item 1; Serre, Theorem 26 of §12.5, as Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg cite it in the proof of (7.3.4)), for the deduction of the statement (item 4)
- the rationalised decomposition map of item 2, a surjection $R_{\mathbb{Q}_\ell}(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to R_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(G) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ natural in $G$ (Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg, proof of (7.3.4), citing Serre, Theorem 33 of §16.1), for the passage from $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$ to $\mathbb{F}_\ell$ in that deduction
- the identity $[\operatorname{Ind}_{H_{\ell'}}^H \operatorname{Res}^H_{H_{\ell'}} M] = |H_\ell| \, [M]$ of item 3 (Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg, proof of (7.3.4)), for the restriction to cyclic subgroups of order prime to $\ell$ in that deduction
