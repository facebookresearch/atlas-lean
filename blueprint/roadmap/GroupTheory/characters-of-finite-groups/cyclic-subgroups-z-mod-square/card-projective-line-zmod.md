# Card projective Line zmod

Let $N \neq 0$ be a natural number. The projective line over $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ has $\psi(N)$ elements, where $\psi$ is the Dedekind psi function,

$$\left|\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})\right| = \psi(N) = \prod_{p^k \parallel N} p^{k-1} (p + 1),$$

the product running over the primes $p$ dividing $N$, with $p^k$ the exact power of $p$ dividing $N$.
