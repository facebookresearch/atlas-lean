# Field of values of a character

For a group $G$ and a finite-dimensional complex representation $V$ of $G$ with character $\chi$, the field of values $\mathbb{Q}(\chi)$ is the subfield of $\mathbb{C}$ generated over $\mathbb{Q}$ by the values $\chi(g)$, $g \in G$.

Source: G. Navarro, in Mini-Workshop: Kronecker, Plethysm, and Sylow Branching Coefficients and their Applications to Complexity Theory, Oberwolfach Reports 17 (2020), no. 9, pp. 526-527, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2020/09; resolution: G.
