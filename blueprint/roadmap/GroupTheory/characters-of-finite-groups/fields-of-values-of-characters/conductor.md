# Conductor of an abelian subfield of C

For a subfield $F$ of $\mathbb{C}$, the conductor of $F$ is the least $f \ge 1$ with $F \subseteq \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_f)$, where $\zeta_f = e^{2\pi i/f}$; when no such $f$ exists (by the Kronecker–Weber theorem, exactly when $F$ is not an abelian extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ of finite degree) it is $0$ by convention.

Navarro and P. H. Tiep, The fields of values of characters of degree not divisible by p, Forum Math. Pi 9 (2021), e2, Theorems A1 and A2, https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2021.1.
