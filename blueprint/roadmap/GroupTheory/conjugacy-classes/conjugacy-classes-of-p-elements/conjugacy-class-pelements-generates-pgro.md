# Kourovka Problem 5.32 (classes of p-elements with p-element quotients)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $p$ be a prime, $G$ a finite group and $x \in G$ an element of order a power of $p$ (possibly $x = 1$), with conjugacy class $C$. If $yz^{-1}$ has order a power of $p$ for all $y, z \in C$, then the subgroup generated by $C$ is a $p$-group.

Mazurov); resolution: W. Xiao, Sci. in China 34 (1991), no. 9, 1025-1031 (p > 2); V. I. Zenkov, in: Algebra, Proc. IIIrd Int. Conf. on Algebra, Krasnoyarsk, 1993, de Gruyter, Berlin, 1996, 297-302 (p = 2).
