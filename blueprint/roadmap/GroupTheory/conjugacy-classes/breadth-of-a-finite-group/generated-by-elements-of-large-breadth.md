# Kourovka Problem 4.69 (Wiegold's conjecture on elements of large breadth)

The proposition that the following holds for all such data. Let $p$ be a prime, $G$ a finite $p$-group and $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|G'| > p^{n(n-1)/2}$, where $G' = [G, G]$. Then $G$ is generated by its elements of breadth at least $n$. The breadth $b(x)$ of $x \in G$ is $\lfloor \log_p |x^G| \rfloor$, where $x^G$ is the conjugacy class of $x$; in a $p$-group $|x^G| = |G : C_G(x)| = p^{b(x)}$. For $n = 0$ the hypothesis is $|G'| > 1$ and every element has breadth at least $0$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.69 (J. Wiegold); resolution: A. Skutin, Proof of a conjecture of Wiegold, J. Algebra 526 (2019), 1-5, arXiv:1804.01745.
