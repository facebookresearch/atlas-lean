# Kourovka Problem 9.10 (finitely generated groups with two conjugacy classes)

The proposition that there is a finitely generated group, not isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, with exactly two conjugacy classes (so all its nonidentity elements are conjugate).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.10 (V. S. Guba); resolution: D. Osin, Small cancellations over relatively hyperbolic groups and embedding theorems, Ann. of Math. 172 (2010), 1-39, https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2010.172.1.
