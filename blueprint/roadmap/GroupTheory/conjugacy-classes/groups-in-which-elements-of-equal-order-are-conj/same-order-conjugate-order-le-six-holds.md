# Finite groups in which elements of equal order are conjugate have order at most 6

Every finite group $G$ in which any two elements of the same order are conjugate has $|G| \le 6$; the only such groups are the trivial group, $\mathbb{Z}/2$ and $S_3$. This is the positive answer to Kourovka Problem 7.48, using the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.48 (S. A. Syskin); resolution: P. Fitzpatrick, Order conjugacy in finite groups, Proc. Roy. Irish Acad. Sect. A 85 (1985), 53-58; W. Feit and G. M. Seitz, On finite rational groups and related topics, Illinois J. Math. 33 (1989), 103-131, https://doi.org/10.1215/ijm/1255988808.
