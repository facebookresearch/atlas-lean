# PSU3 Model

`PSU(3, 2ⁿ)`: the isometries of determinant one of the Hermitian form with antidiagonal
Gram matrix over `GF(2^(2n))`, taken modulo scalars.  The field involution is the Frobenius
`z ↦ z^(2ⁿ)`, whose fixed field is `GF(2ⁿ)`, and `Aᴴ` is `(Aᴴ)ᵢⱼ = (Aⱼᵢ)^(2ⁿ)`.
