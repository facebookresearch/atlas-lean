# Conjecture

[OWR-16167-018] `F_τ` embeds in `F`: some map sends `F_τ` injectively into `F` and is
multiplicative on `F_τ`. Since `F_τ` is a group under composition, this is an injective group
homomorphism `F_τ → F`.
