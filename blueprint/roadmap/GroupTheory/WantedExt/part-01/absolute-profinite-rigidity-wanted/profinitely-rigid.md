# Profinitely Rigid

A finitely generated residually finite group `Γ` is said to be *profinitely rigid* if `Γ` is
isomorphic to every finitely generated residually finite group `Λ` with profinite completion
isomorphic to the profinite completion of `Γ`.

Note that any such isomorphism of profinite completions is automatically a homeomorphism. This is a
consequence of the Nikolov-Segal theorem which states that for a finitely generated group `Γ`,
every finite index subgroup of the profinite completion of `Γ` is automatically open. Conversely,
every open subgroup has finite index by compactness, so in this case the open subgroups (which
generate the topology) are exactly the subgroups of finite index. Thus, the topology on the
profinite completion of `Γ` can be characterized algebraically as the topology generated by the
finite index subgroups. In particular, any isomorphism between profinite completions of finitely
generated groups automatically preserves the topology and is thus a homeomorphism.
