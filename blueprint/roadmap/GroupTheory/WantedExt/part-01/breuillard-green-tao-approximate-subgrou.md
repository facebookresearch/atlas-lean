# Approximate Subgroup Structure Wanted

The **Breuillard–Green–Tao structure theorem** (approximate-subgroup form):
a `K`-approximate subgroup `A` of an arbitrary group is controlled by a genuine
subgroup with nilpotent quotient of bounded rank. There is a subgroup `H` with
a normal subgroup `N` such that `H ⧸ N` is nilpotent of bounded class and is
generated by a finite set of bounded size, `N` sits inside a bounded power of
`A / A`, and `A` is covered by `F * H` for a finite set `F` of bounded size.
