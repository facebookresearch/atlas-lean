# Kourovka Problem 7.2 (transience of random walks on free Burnside groups)

The proposition that for all natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and odd $n \ge 665$ the simple random walk on the free Burnside group $B(m, n)$ with respect to the standard generators is transient: the series $\sum_{k \ge 0} r(k)/(2m)^k$ converges, where $B(m, n)$ is the free Burnside group: the free group on $m$ generators $a_1, \dots, a_m$ modulo the normal closure of all $n$-th powers and $r(k)$ is the number of words $w$ of length $k$ in the $2m$ letters $a_i^{\pm 1}$ that represent $1$ in $B(m, n)$, so that $r(k)/(2m)^k$ is the probability that the simple random walk on $B(m, n)$ with respect to these generators is back at $1$ after $k$ steps.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.2 (S. I. Adian).
