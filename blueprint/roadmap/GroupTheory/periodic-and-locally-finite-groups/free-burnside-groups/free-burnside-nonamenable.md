# Kourovka Problem 7.2 (non-amenability of free Burnside groups)

The proposition that for all natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and odd $n \ge 665$ the free Burnside group $B(m, n)$ is not amenable, where $B(m, n)$ is the free Burnside group: the free group on $m$ generators $a_1, \dots, a_m$ modulo the normal closure of all $n$-th powers. A group $G$ is amenable if it satisfies the Følner condition: for every finite $K \subseteq G$ and real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a finite nonempty $F \subseteq G$ with $|sF \mathbin{\triangle} F| < \varepsilon |F|$ for every $s \in K$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.2 (S. I. Adian).
