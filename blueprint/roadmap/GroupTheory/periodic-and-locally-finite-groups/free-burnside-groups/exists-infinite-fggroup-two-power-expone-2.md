# Infinite finitely generated groups of exponent a power of 2

For some $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there is an infinite finitely generated group satisfying the identity $x^{2^n} = 1$; Kourovka Problem 4.1 has a positive answer. By Ivanov, the free Burnside group $B(2, 2^k)$ is infinite for $k \ge 48$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.1 (S. I. Adian); resolution: S. V. Ivanov, The free Burnside groups of sufficiently large exponents, Int. J. Algebra Comput. 4 (1994), no. 1-2, 1-308, https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218196794000026; I. G. Lysenok, Infinite Burnside groups of even exponent, Izv. Math. 60 (1996), no. 3, 453-654.
