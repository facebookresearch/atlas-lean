# Infinite groups without infinite abelian subgroups

There is an infinite group all of whose abelian subgroups are finite; Kourovka Problem 1.24 has a negative answer. For $m \ge 2$ and odd $n \ge 4381$ the free Burnside group $B(m, n)$ is infinite and all its abelian subgroups are cyclic, hence finite. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 1.24 (infinite abelian subgroups)](infinite-group-has-infinite-abelian-subg.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.24 (M. I. Kargapolov); resolution: P. S. Novikov and S. I. Adian, Math. USSR-Izv. 2 (1968), 1131-1144.
