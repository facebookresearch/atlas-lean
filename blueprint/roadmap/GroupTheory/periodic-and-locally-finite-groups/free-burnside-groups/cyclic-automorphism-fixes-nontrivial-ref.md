# Mazurov's fixed-point question for free Burnside groups has a negative answer

There exist
primes $q$ and $p$ such that the free Burnside group $B(q, p)$ of rank $q$ and exponent $p$ is
infinite and the automorphism of $B(q, p)$ induced by the cyclic permutation $x_i \mapsto x_{i+1}$
(indices mod $q$) of its free generators $x_0, \dots, x_{q-1}$ fixes no nontrivial element of
$B(q, p)$.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 17.70 (V. D. Mazurov); resolution: V. S. Atabekyan, Mazurov's question on fixed points of automorphisms of free Burnside groups, preprint, arXiv:2609.33941 (2026), Theorem 1.1 (for every odd m ≥ 3 there is q_m such that for every prime q > q_m the automorphism of B(m, q) of order m cyclically permuting the free generators has no nontrivial fixed points; B(m, n) is infinite for all odd n ≥ 665 and m > 1 by Adian's theorem, as recalled there); the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 17.70 solved (No, it does not for all q ≥ 3 and all sufficiently large p), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33941.
