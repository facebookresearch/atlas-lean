# Mazurov's fixed-point question for free Burnside groups (Kourovka 17.70)

The assertion
that for all primes $q$ and $p$ such that the free Burnside group $B(q, p)$ of rank $q$ and
exponent $p$ is infinite, the automorphism of $B(q, p)$ induced by the cyclic permutation
$x_i \mapsto x_{i+1}$ (indices mod $q$) of its free generators $x_0, \dots, x_{q-1}$ fixes some
nontrivial element of $B(q, p)$.
