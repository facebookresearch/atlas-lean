# Random walks on free Burnside groups of large odd exponent are transient

For all natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and odd $n \ge 665$, the return probabilities of the simple random walk on $B(m, n)$ with respect to the standard generators are summable: $\sum_{k \ge 0} r(k)/(2m)^k < \infty$, where $B(m, n)$ is the free Burnside group: the free group on $m$ generators $a_1, \dots, a_m$ modulo the normal closure of all $n$-th powers and $r(k)$ is the number of words $w$ of length $k$ in the $2m$ letters $a_i^{\pm 1}$ that represent $1$ in $B(m, n)$, so that $r(k)/(2m)^k$ is the probability that the simple random walk on $B(m, n)$ with respect to these generators is back at $1$ after $k$ steps. This proves the second assertion of Kourovka Problem 7.2.
