# Return counts of the simple random walk on a free Burnside group

For natural numbers $m$, $n$ and $k$, the number of maps $w : \{0, \dots, k - 1\} \to \{1, \dots, m\} \times \{\pm 1\}$, read as words $a_{i_0}^{\varepsilon_0} \cdots a_{i_{k-1}}^{\varepsilon_{k-1}}$ of length $k$ in the letters $a_i^{\pm 1}$, that represent $1$ in the free Burnside group $B(m, n)$ (the free group on $a_1, \dots, a_m$ modulo the normal closure of all $n$-th powers). For $m \ge 1$, dividing by $(2m)^k$ gives the return probability for $k$ independent uniform signed letters: the probability that the simple random walk on $B(m, n)$ is at $1$ after $k$ steps.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.2 (S. I. Adian).
