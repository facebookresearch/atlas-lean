# Uncountable soluble groups with the minimal condition on normal subgroups

There is an uncountable soluble group satisfying the minimal condition on normal subgroups. Kourovka Problem 5.60 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.60 (countability of soluble groups with Min-n)](soluble-min-normal-countable.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.60 (B. Hartley); resolution: B. Hartley, Uncountable Artinian modules and uncountable soluble groups satisfying Min-n, Proc. London Math. Soc. (3) 35 (1977), 55-75, https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-35.1.55.
