# The minimal condition on abelian normal subgroups is not inherited by subgroups of finite index

There is a group $G$ with no infinite strictly descending chain of abelian normal subgroups that has a subgroup $H$ of finite index with such a chain of abelian normal subgroups of $H$. In Chechin's example $H$ has index $2$, and every abelian normal subgroup of $G$ even satisfies the minimal condition on subgroups, so the answer is negative for either reading of the condition. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 6.49 (minimal condition on abelian normal subgroups and finite index)](min-abelian-normal-inherited-finite-inde.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.49 (S. A. Chechin); resolution: S. A. Chechin, Abstracts of the 15th All-USSR Algebraic Conference, Krasnoyarsk, 1979; construction given in the Archive.
