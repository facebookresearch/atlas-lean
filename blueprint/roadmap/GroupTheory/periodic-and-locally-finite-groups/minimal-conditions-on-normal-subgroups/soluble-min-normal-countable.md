# Kourovka Problem 5.60 (countability of soluble groups with Min-n)

The proposition that every soluble group satisfying the minimal condition on normal subgroups (no infinite strictly descending sequence of normal subgroups) is countable.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.60 (B. Hartley).
