# The weak minimal condition on normal subgroups

A group $G$ satisfies the weak minimal condition on normal subgroups when for every descending sequence $N_0 \ge N_1 \ge N_2 \ge \cdots$ of normal subgroups some index $|N_n : N_{n+1}|$ is finite. This is equivalent to the usual form (in every such chain the indices are finite from some point on) by passing to subchains.
