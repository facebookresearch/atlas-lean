# Maximal π-subgroups of a periodic group

For a set $\pi$ of natural numbers, a maximal $\pi$-subgroup of a group $G$ is a subgroup all of whose elements are $\pi$-elements (an element of finite order all of whose prime divisors lie in $\pi$) and that is contained in no strictly larger subgroup with this property.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.13 (S. G. Ivanov); resolution: Yu. I. Merzlyakov (1973), using S. I. Adian, On some torsion-free groups, Math. USSR-Izv. 5 (1971), 475-484.
