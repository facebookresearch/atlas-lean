# Kourovka Problem 2.13 (π × π' decompositions of periodic groups)

The proposition that whenever $\pi$ is a set of natural numbers, $\pi'$ its complement, $G$ is a periodic group (every element has finite order), and every $\pi$-element of $G$ commutes with every $\pi'$-element, where a $\pi$-element is an element of finite order all of whose prime divisors lie in $\pi$, then $G$ is the internal direct product of a maximal $\pi$-subgroup $P$ and a maximal $\pi'$-subgroup $Q$: $P \cap Q = 1$, $P$ and $Q$ generate $G$, and they commute elementwise.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.13 (S. G. Ivanov); resolution: Yu. I. Merzlyakov (1973), using S. I. Adian, On some torsion-free groups, Math. USSR-Izv. 5 (1971), 475-484.
