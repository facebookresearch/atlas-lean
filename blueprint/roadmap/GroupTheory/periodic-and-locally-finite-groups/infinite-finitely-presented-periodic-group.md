# An infinite finitely presented periodic group

There is an infinite finitely presented group in which for every group element $g$ there is a positive natural number $m$ with $g^m=1$. The integer $m$ may depend on $g$.

## Depends on

- Finite group presentations and elementwise finite order.

## Proof depends on

- [The characteristic-two periodic Steinberg group](characteristic-two-periodic-steinberg-group.md).

## Proof outline

Forget the Steinberg presentation while retaining infinitude, finite presentability and elementwise finite order.
