# Residual finiteness of proper quotients of Golod groups (Kourovka 12.102)

This is the
statement that the following holds for every field $k$ of prime characteristic $p$, every
non-unital associative $k$-algebra $N$, every $r \ge 2$ and all $y_1, \dots, y_r \in N$ such that
$N$ is generated by $y_1, \dots, y_r$ as a non-unital $k$-algebra, every element of $N$ is
nilpotent, and for every natural number $n$ some product of $n$ elements of $N$ is nonzero: if
$\Gamma$ is the Golod group of $y$, then for every nontrivial normal subgroup $K$ of $\Gamma$ and
every $x \in \Gamma$ with $x \notin K$ there is a normal subgroup $M$ of finite index in $\Gamma$
with $K \le M$ and $x \notin M$. In other words, every proper factor group $\Gamma / K$ of a
Golod group $\Gamma$ is residually finite. This is Shunkov's question, Kourovka Problem 12.102.
