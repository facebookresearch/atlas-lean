# Golod group (Kourovka 11.101)

Let $k$ be a field, $N$ a non-unital associative
$k$-algebra and $y_1, \dots, y_r \in N$. The Golod group of $y$ is the subgroup of the group of
units of the unitization $k \oplus N$ generated by the units that are equal to $1 + y_i$ for some
$i$. When every $1 + y_i$ is invertible (for example when $N$ is nil), this is the subgroup
$\langle 1 + y_1, \dots, 1 + y_r \rangle$ of the adjoint group $1 + N$.
