# Kourovka Problem 6.54 (infinite finitely generated Frobenius groups)

The proposition that there is an infinite finitely generated group $G$ that is a Frobenius group with some kernel $F$ and complement $H$. Here $G$ is a Frobenius group with kernel $F$ and complement $H$ when $F$ is a normal subgroup, $F \cap H = 1$, $FH = G$, $F \ne 1$, $H \ne 1$, $H \cap gHg^{-1} = 1$ for every $g \in G \setminus H$, and the nonidentity elements of $F$ are exactly the elements of $G$ lying in no conjugate of $H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.54 (V. P. Shunkov); resolution: A. I. Sozutov, On the structure of the noninvariant factor in some Frobenius groups, Siberian Math. J. 35 (1994), no. 4, 795-801, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02106623.
