# Kourovka Problem 2.83 (periodic products of two locally finite groups)

The proposition that if a periodic group $G$ is the product $G = AB$ of two locally finite subgroups (every element is $ab$ with $a \in A$, $b \in B$), then $G$ is locally finite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.83 (V. P. Shunkov).
