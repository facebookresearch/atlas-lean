# A periodic product of two locally finite subgroups need not be locally finite

There is a periodic group $G = AB$ with $A$ and $B$ locally finite subgroups that is not locally finite. Kourovka Problem 2.83 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.83 (periodic products of two locally finite groups)](periodic-product-locally-finite-is-local.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.83 (V. P. Shunkov); resolution: V. I. Sushchanskii, Math. USSR-Sb. 67 (1990), no. 2, 535-553.
