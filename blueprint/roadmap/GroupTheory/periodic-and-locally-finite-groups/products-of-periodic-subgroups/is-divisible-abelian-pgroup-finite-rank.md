# Divisible abelian p-groups of finite rank

For a natural number $p$, a group $G$ is a divisible abelian $p$-group of finite rank when it is abelian, every element $g$ satisfies $g^{p^k} = 1$ for some natural number $k$ (for prime $p$, every element has order a power of $p$), every element has an $n$-th root for every $n \ge 1$, and only finitely many elements satisfy $x^p = 1$ (for prime $p$, the rank is the dimension of this socle over $\mathbb{F}_p$). The trivial group qualifies.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.78 (N. F. Sesekin); resolution: N. F. Sesekin, Siberian Math. J. 9 (1968), 1070-1072.
