# Kourovka Problem 1.78 (products of divisible abelian p-groups of finite rank)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $p$ be a prime and $G = AB$ a group that is the product of subgroups $A$ and $B$ (every element is a product $ab$) each of which is a divisible abelian $p$-group of finite rank. Then $G$ is itself a divisible abelian $p$-group of finite rank. Here a group $G$ is a divisible abelian $p$-group of finite rank when it is abelian, every element has order a power of $p$, every element has an $n$-th root for every $n \ge 1$, and only finitely many elements satisfy $x^p = 1$ (the rank is the dimension of this socle over $\mathbb{F}_p$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.78 (N. F. Sesekin); resolution: N. F. Sesekin, Siberian Math. J. 9 (1968), 1070-1072.
