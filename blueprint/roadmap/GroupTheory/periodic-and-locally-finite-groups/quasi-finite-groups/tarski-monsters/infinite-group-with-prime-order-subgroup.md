# Kourovka Problem 2.73 (infinite groups whose proper subgroups have prime order)

The proposition that there is an infinite group $G$ every proper nontrivial subgroup of which is finite of prime order. The trivial subgroup is excluded.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.73 (A. I. Starostin; well-known problem); resolution: A. Yu. Ol'shanskii, Algebra and Logic 21 (1982), 369-418.
