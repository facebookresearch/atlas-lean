# Central elements of quasi-finite groups are not characterized by lying in infinitely many subgroups

There are a quasi-finite group $G$ (an infinite group all of whose proper subgroups are finite) and an element of $G$ that is central but lies in only finitely many subgroups, or lies in infinitely many subgroups but is not central; Kourovka Problem 9.82 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 9.82 (central elements of quasi-finite groups)](quasi-finite-central-iff-infinitely-many.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.82 (V. P. Shunkov); resolution: K. I. Lossov, deposited at VINITI, no. 5529-V89, Moscow, 1988 (Russian).
