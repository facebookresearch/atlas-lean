# Minimal condition on centralizers

For a subset $X$ of a group $G$, write $C_G(X)$ for the
centralizer of $X$ in $G$. The group $G$ satisfies the minimal condition on centralizers when there
is no sequence of subsets $X_0, X_1, X_2, \ldots$ of $G$ whose centralizers form a strictly
descending chain $C_G(X_0) > C_G(X_1) > C_G(X_2) > \cdots$, where $H > K$ means that $K$ is a
proper subgroup of $H$.
