# Jabara's conjecture on groups with $\{3, 5\}$-involutions

Kourovka 19.36 (E. Jabara):
let $G$ be a periodic group (every element has finite order) that is generated by its involutions
(elements $x \ne 1$ with $x^2 = 1$). Suppose that the order of $xy$ lies in $\{1, 2, 3, 5\}$ for
all involutions $x, y$ of $G$, and that each of these four values is the order of $xy$ for some
involutions $x, y$ of $G$. Then $G$ is isomorphic to the alternating group $A_5$ or to the
projective special unitary group $\mathrm{PSU}_3(4)$.
