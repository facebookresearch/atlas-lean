# Kourovka Problem 2.75 (infinite centralizers in periodic groups)

The proposition that if a periodic group $G$ has an infinite set of distinct finite subgroups whose intersection is non-trivial, then some non-identity element of $G$ has infinite centralizer.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.75 (S. P. Strunkov).
