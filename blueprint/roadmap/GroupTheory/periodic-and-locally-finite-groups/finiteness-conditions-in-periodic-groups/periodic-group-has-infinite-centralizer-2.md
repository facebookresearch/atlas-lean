# A periodic group with infinitely many finite subgroups meeting non-trivially but finite centralizers

There is a periodic group with infinitely many distinct finite subgroups whose intersection is non-trivial, in which every non-identity element has finite centralizer. Kourovka Problem 2.75 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.75 (infinite centralizers in periodic groups)](periodic-group-has-infinite-centralizer.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.75 (S. P. Strunkov); resolution: K. I. Lossov, Dep. no. 5528-V88, VINITI, Moscow, 1988.
