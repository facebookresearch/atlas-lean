# Kourovka Problem 3.9 (infinite periodic groups with a finite maximal subgroup)

The proposition that there is an infinite periodic group (every element of finite order) with a finite maximal subgroup (a coatom of the subgroup lattice). No locally finite group qualifies: a finite maximal subgroup and one further element would generate the whole group.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.9 (Yu. M. Gorchakov).
