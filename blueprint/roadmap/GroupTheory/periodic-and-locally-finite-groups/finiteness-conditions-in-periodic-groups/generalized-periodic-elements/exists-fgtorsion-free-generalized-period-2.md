# Finitely generated torsion-free groups of generalized periodic elements

There is a nontrivial finitely generated torsion-free group in which every element is generalized periodic. An element $g$ of a group is generalized periodic if $x_1^{-1}gx_1 \cdots x_n^{-1}gx_n = 1$ for some $n \ge 1$ and elements $x_1, \dots, x_n$. An example is $\langle a, b \mid (b^2)^a = b^{-2},\ (a^2)^b = a^{-2} \rangle$, in which $x^2 (x^2)^a (x^2)^b (x^2)^{ab} = 1$ for every $x$. This answers Kourovka Problem 3.11 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.11 (Yu. M. Gorchakov); resolution: A. P. Goryushkin, Siberian Math. J. 14 (1973), 146-148; example of V. A. Churkin (1973), as recorded in the Kourovka Archive.
