# Kourovka Problem 3.11 (torsion-free groups of generalized periodic elements)

The proposition that there is a nontrivial finitely generated torsion-free group (every element other than $1$ has infinite order) all of whose elements are generalized periodic. An element $g$ of a group is generalized periodic if $x_1^{-1}gx_1 \cdots x_n^{-1}gx_n = 1$ for some $n \ge 1$ and elements $x_1, \dots, x_n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.11 (Yu. M. Gorchakov).
