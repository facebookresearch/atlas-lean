# An infinite periodic group with a finite maximal subgroup

There is an infinite group in which every element has finite order and which has a finite maximal subgroup.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.9 (Yu. M. Gorchakov); resolution: S. I. Adian, On subgroups of free periodic groups of odd exponent, Proc. Steklov Inst. Math. 112 (1971), 61-69.
