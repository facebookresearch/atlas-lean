# Kourovka Problem 6.57 (elements of finite order in biprimitively finite groups)

The proposition that in every biprimitively finite group the elements of finite order form a subgroup: the product of two elements of finite order has finite order.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.57 (V. P. Shunkov).
