# Kourovka Problem 9.79 (countability of groups with the minimum condition)

The proposition that every group satisfying the minimum condition on subgroups (every nonempty set of subgroups has a minimal member) is countable.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.79 (V. P. Shunkov (A. G. Kurosh)); resolution: V. N. Obraztsov, An imbedding theorem for groups and its corollaries, Math. USSR-Sb. 66 (1990), 541-553, https://doi.org/10.1070/SM1990v066n02ABEH001184.
