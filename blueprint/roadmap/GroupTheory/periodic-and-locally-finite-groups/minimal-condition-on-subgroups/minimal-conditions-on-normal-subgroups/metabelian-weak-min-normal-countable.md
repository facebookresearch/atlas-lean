# Kourovka Problem 6.12(a) (countability of metabelian groups with weak Min-n)

The proposition that every metabelian group satisfying the weak minimal condition on normal subgroups is countable. The weak minimal condition on normal subgroups says that no descending chain $N_0 \ge N_1 \ge \cdots$ of normal subgroups has all indices $|N_n : N_{n+1}|$ infinite. A group is metabelian when its second derived subgroup is trivial.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.12 (D. I. Zaitsev).
