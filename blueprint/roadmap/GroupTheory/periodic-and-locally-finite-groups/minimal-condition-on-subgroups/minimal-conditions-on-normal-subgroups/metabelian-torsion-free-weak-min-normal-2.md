# Torsion-free metabelian groups with weak Min-n are minimax

Every torsion-free metabelian group satisfying the weak minimal condition on normal subgroups is minimax. The weak minimal condition on normal subgroups says that no descending chain $N_0 \ge N_1 \ge \cdots$ of normal subgroups has all indices $|N_n : N_{n+1}|$ infinite. A group is metabelian when its second derived subgroup is trivial. A group is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = H_0 \trianglelefteq H_1 \trianglelefteq \cdots \trianglelefteq H_n = G$ each of whose factors satisfies the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.
