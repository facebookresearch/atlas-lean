# Kourovka Problem 6.49 (minimal condition on abelian normal subgroups and finite index)

The proposition that if a group $G$ has no infinite strictly descending sequence of abelian normal subgroups, then neither has any subgroup $H$ of finite index (abelian normal subgroups of $H$ being taken in $H$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.49 (S. A. Chechin).
