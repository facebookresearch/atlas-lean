# Metabelian groups with the weak minimal condition on normal subgroups are countable

Every metabelian group satisfying the weak minimal condition on normal subgroups is countable. The weak minimal condition on normal subgroups says that no descending chain $N_0 \ge N_1 \ge \cdots$ of normal subgroups has all indices $|N_n : N_{n+1}|$ infinite. A group is metabelian when its second derived subgroup is trivial.

Zaitsev); resolution: D. I. Zaitsev, L. A. Kurdachenko and A. V.
