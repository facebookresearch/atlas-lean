# Kourovka Problem 7.42(b) (radicable part of F*-groups with the minimal condition)

The proposition that every $F^*$-group satisfying the minimal condition on subgroups has an abelian radicable subgroup of finite index. The minimal condition is assumed for all subgroups, which is stronger than for abelian subgroups. A group $U$ is an $F_q$-group when for every finite subgroup $K$ and any two elements $a, b$ of order $q$ in $N_U(K)/K$ there is $c \in N_U(K)/K$ with $\langle a, c^{-1}bc \rangle$ finite; $U$ is an $F^*$-group when every subgroup $H$ is an $F_q$-group for every prime $q$ that is the order of an element of $H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.42 (A. N. Ostylovskiĭ).
