# F_q-groups (Shunkov)

For $q \in \mathbb N$, a group $U$ is an $F_q$-group when for every finite subgroup $K$ and any two elements $a, b$ of order $q$ in $N_U(K)/K$ (for $q = 0$, elements of infinite order, as the order of an element of infinite order is taken to be $0$) there is $c \in N_U(K)/K$ such that $a$ and $c^{-1}bc$ generate a finite subgroup.
