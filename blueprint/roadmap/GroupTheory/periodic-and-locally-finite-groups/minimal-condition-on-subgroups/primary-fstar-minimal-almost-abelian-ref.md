# A primary F*-group with the minimal condition that is not almost abelian

There is a $p$-group that is an $F^*$-group and satisfies the minimal condition on subgroups but has no abelian subgroup of finite index. Kourovka Problem 7.42(a) has a negative answer. A group $U$ is an $F_q$-group when for every finite subgroup $K$ and any two elements $a, b$ of order $q$ in $N_U(K)/K$ there is $c \in N_U(K)/K$ with $\langle a, c^{-1}bc \rangle$ finite; $U$ is an $F^*$-group when every subgroup $H$ is an $F_q$-group for every prime $q$ that is the order of an element of $H$. Ol'shanskiĭ's infinite $p$-group (for large primes $p$) all of whose proper non-trivial subgroups are conjugate of order $p$ is an $F^*$-group with the minimal condition on subgroups whose proper subgroups are all finite. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 7.42(a) (primary F*-groups with the minimal condition)](primary-fstar-minimal-almost-abelian.md).

Ostylovskiĭ); resolution: A. Yu. Ol'shanskiĭ, An infinite group with subgroups of prime orders, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat. 44 (1980), 309-321; Math. USSR-Izv. 16 (1981), 279-289.
