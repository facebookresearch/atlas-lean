# An F*-group with the minimal condition without a radicable part

There is an $F^*$-group satisfying the minimal condition on subgroups with no radicable abelian subgroup of finite index. Kourovka Problem 7.42(b) has a negative answer. A group $U$ is an $F_q$-group when for every finite subgroup $K$ and any two elements $a, b$ of order $q$ in $N_U(K)/K$ there is $c \in N_U(K)/K$ with $\langle a, c^{-1}bc \rangle$ finite; $U$ is an $F^*$-group when every subgroup $H$ is an $F_q$-group for every prime $q$ that is the order of an element of $H$. Ol'shanskiĭ's infinite $p$-group (for large primes $p$) all of whose proper non-trivial subgroups are conjugate of order $p$ is an $F^*$-group with the minimal condition on subgroups whose proper subgroups are all finite, so it has no abelian subgroup of finite index at all. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 7.42(b) (radicable part of F*-groups with the minimal condition)](f-star-minimal-has-radicable-part.md).

Ostylovskiĭ); resolution: A. Yu. Ol'shanskiĭ, An infinite group with subgroups of prime orders, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat. 44 (1980), 309-321; Math. USSR-Izv. 16 (1981), 279-289.
