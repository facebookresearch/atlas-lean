# F*-groups (Shunkov)

A group $U$ is an $F^*$-group when every subgroup $H$ of $U$ is an $F_q$-group for every prime $q$ such that $H$ has an element of order $q$. A group $U$ is an $F_q$-group when for every finite subgroup $K$ and any two elements $a, b$ of order $q$ in $N_U(K)/K$ there is $c \in N_U(K)/K$ with $\langle a, c^{-1}bc \rangle$ finite; $U$ is an $F^*$-group when every subgroup $H$ is an $F_q$-group for every prime $q$ that is the order of an element of $H$.
