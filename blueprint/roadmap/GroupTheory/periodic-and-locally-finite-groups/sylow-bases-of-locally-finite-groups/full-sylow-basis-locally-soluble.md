# Kourovka Problem 3.12 (locally finite groups with a full Sylow basis)

The proposition that every locally finite group (finitely generated subgroups finite) with a full Sylow basis is locally soluble: every finitely generated subgroup is soluble. A full Sylow basis in Golberg's sense is a choice, for each prime $p$, of a Sylow $p$-subgroup $P_p$ (a maximal $p$-subgroup) such that for every finite set $J$ of primes the subgroup generated by the $P_p$ with $p \in J$ is a $J$-group (its elements have finite order with all prime divisors in $J$), and the $P_p$ together generate $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 3.12 (Yu. M. Gorchakov).
