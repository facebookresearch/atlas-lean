# Full Sylow bases (Golberg)

For a group $G$, a family $(P_p)$ indexed by the primes, with $P_p$ a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $G$ (maximal $p$-subgroup), is a full Sylow basis when, for every finite set $J$ of primes, every element of the subgroup generated by the $P_p$, $p \in J$, has finite order whose prime divisors all lie in $J$, and the subgroups $P_p$ generate $G$. The convention requiring $P_pP_q = P_qP_p$ is not part of this definition.
