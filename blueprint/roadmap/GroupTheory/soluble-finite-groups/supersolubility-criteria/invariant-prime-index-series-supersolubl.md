# Kourovka Problem 4.71 (invariant series of prime indices)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $G$ be a finite group and $A$ a group of automorphisms of $G$. If there is a series $G = G_0 \ge G_1 \ge \cdots \ge G_k = 1$ of subgroups of $G$ (not necessarily normal), each mapped into itself by every element of $A$, with every index $|G_i : G_{i+1}|$ prime, then $A$ is supersoluble: $A$ has a series $1 = S_0 \le S_1 \le \cdots \le S_m = A$ of normal subgroups of $A$ with every $S_{i+1}/S_i$ cyclic.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.71 (L. A. Shemetkov); resolution: L. A. Shemetkov, Graduated formations of groups, Math. USSR-Sb. 23 (1974), 593-611, https://doi.org/10.1070/SM1974v023n04ABEH002184.
