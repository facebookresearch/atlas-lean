# Nilpotent length at most n

A group $G$ has nilpotent (Fitting) length at most $n$ when there is a chain $1 = N_0 \le N_1 \le \cdots \le N_n = G$ of normal subgroups of $G$ with nilpotent factors: for each $i < n$ some term of the lower central series of $N_{i+1}$ (iterated commutator subgroups $[\cdots[N_{i+1}, N_{i+1}], \dots, N_{i+1}]$ computed in $G$) lies in $N_i$. For $n = 0$ this says $G$ is trivial.
