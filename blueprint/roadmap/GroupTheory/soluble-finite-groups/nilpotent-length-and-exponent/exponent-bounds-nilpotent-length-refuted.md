# A finite group of exponent p^α q^β with nilpotent length above α + β

There are primes $p, q$, exponents $\alpha, \beta$ and a finite group of exponent dividing $p^\alpha q^\beta$ whose nilpotent length exceeds $\alpha + \beta$. Kourovka Problem 3.26 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.26 (nilpotent length of groups of exponent p^α q^β)](exponent-bounds-nilpotent-length.md).
