# Embedding finite modular lattices in the lattice of formations (Kourovka 13.51)

The statement that for every finite modular lattice $L$ there is an injective map $f$ from $L$ to
the formations of finite groups such that, for all $a, b \in L$, $f(a \wedge b) = f(a) \cap f(b)$
and $f(a \vee b)$ is the smallest formation containing $f(a) \cup f(b)$.

The map is not required to preserve the least or the greatest element.
