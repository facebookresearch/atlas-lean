# The distributivity question for nilpotent class-4 formations (Kourovka 10.56)

The statement that for all formations $A$, $B$, $C$ of finite nilpotent groups of class at most $4$,
a finite group lies in $A \cap (B \vee C)$ if and only if it lies in $(A \cap B) \vee (A \cap C)$,
where $X \vee Y$ denotes the formation generated by $X \cup Y$. That is, the statement that the
lattice of formations of finite nilpotent groups of class at most $4$ is distributive (A. N. Skiba).
