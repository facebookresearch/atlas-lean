# Local formations without a minimal local non-H-subformation

There are local formations $\mathfrak F \not\subseteq \mathfrak H$ of finite groups such that the set of local formations contained in $\mathfrak F$ and not in $\mathfrak H$ has no minimal element. Kourovka Problem 9.60 has a negative answer. A local formation is a formation containing the trivial group that is saturated: $G/\Phi(G) \in \mathfrak F$ implies $G \in \mathfrak F$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 9.60 (minimal local non-H-subformations)](minimal-local-non-subformation-exists.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.60 (A. N. Skiba and L. A. Shemetkov); resolution: V. P. Burichenko, Some finiteness questions about formations, Trudy Inst. Mat. (Minsk) 21 (2013), no. 1, 15-24.
