# The product of classes of finite groups

For classes $\mathfrak F, \mathfrak H$ of finite groups, the product $\mathfrak F\mathfrak H$ consists of the finite groups $G$ whose $\mathfrak H$-residual $G^{\mathfrak H}$ (the intersection of all normal subgroups $N$ with $G/N \in \mathfrak H$) lies in $\mathfrak F$.
