# Kourovka Problem 9.60 (minimal local non-H-subformations)

The proposition that for all local formations $\mathfrak F$, $\mathfrak H$ of finite groups with $\mathfrak F \not\subseteq \mathfrak H$, $\mathfrak F$ has a minimal local non-$\mathfrak H$-subformation. Classes of finite groups are predicates on finite groups; a formation is closed under homomorphic images and finite subdirect products ($G/N_1, G/N_2 \in \mathfrak F \Rightarrow G/(N_1 \cap N_2) \in \mathfrak F$), the empty class included. A local formation is a formation containing the trivial group that is saturated: $G/\Phi(G) \in \mathfrak F$ implies $G \in \mathfrak F$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.60 (A. N. Skiba and L. A. Shemetkov).
