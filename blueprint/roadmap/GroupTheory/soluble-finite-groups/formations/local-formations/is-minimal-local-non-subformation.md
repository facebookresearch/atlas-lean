# Minimal local non-H-subformations

For classes $\mathfrak M, \mathfrak F, \mathfrak H$ of finite groups, $\mathfrak M$ is a minimal local non-$\mathfrak H$-subformation of $\mathfrak F$ when $\mathfrak M$ is a local formation contained in $\mathfrak F$ but not in $\mathfrak H$, and every local formation properly contained in $\mathfrak M$ is contained in $\mathfrak H$. A local formation is a formation containing the trivial group that is saturated: $G/\Phi(G) \in \mathfrak F$ implies $G \in \mathfrak F$.
