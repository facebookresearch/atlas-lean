# Non-local formations with a local product

There are non-local formations $\mathfrak F$, $\mathfrak H$ of finite groups (each containing the trivial group) whose product $\mathfrak F\mathfrak H = \{G : G^{\mathfrak H} \in \mathfrak F\}$ is local. A local formation is a formation containing the trivial group that is saturated: $G/\Phi(G) \in \mathfrak F$ implies $G \in \mathfrak F$.
