# Fitting classes of finite soluble groups

A [class of groups](../injectors-of-fitting-classes/group-class.md) $\mathfrak{X}$ is a Fitting class of finite soluble groups if it is a [Fitting class of finite groups](../injectors-of-fitting-classes/is-fitting.md), that is, for every finite group $G$ every normal subgroup of $G$ lies in $\mathfrak{X}$ whenever $G$ does and the product of two normal subgroups of $G$ lies in $\mathfrak{X}$ whenever both do, and every finite group in $\mathfrak{X}$ is soluble.

Only the finite members of $\mathfrak{X}$ are constrained; infinite groups may or may not lie in $\mathfrak{X}$.
