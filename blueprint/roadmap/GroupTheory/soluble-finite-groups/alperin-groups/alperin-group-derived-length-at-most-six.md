# Kourovka Problem 18.33 on the derived length of finite Alperin groups

This proposition
asserts that every finite Alperin group $G$ has derived length at most $6$, that is, its sixth
derived subgroup $G^{(6)}$ is trivial. Here $G$ is an Alperin group when, for all $x, y \in G$, the
derived subgroup of the subgroup $\langle x, y \rangle$ generated by $x$ and $y$ is cyclic.

Problem 18.33 (B. M. Veretennikov) asks whether such a bound exists; $6$ is the bound given in the
answer.
