# Kourovka Problem 5.47 (countable abelian groups in centres of finitely presented groups)

The proposition that every countable abelian group $A$ admits an injective homomorphism into the centre of some finitely presented group $G$. Finite abelian groups are included.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 5.47 (V. N. Remeslennikov; marked solved in the main list); resolution: A. Ould Houcine, Embeddings in finitely presented groups which preserve the center, J. Algebra 307 (2007), no. 1, 1-23, Corollary 3, http://math.univ-lyon1.fr/~ould/Fiches-papiers/embeddings.pdf.
