# Some free-abelian-by-free groups with fibre of rank four have unsolvable conjugacy problem

There are groups $\mathbb{Z}^4 \rtimes F$, with $F$ a finitely generated free group, whose conjugacy problem is unsolvable; for $n \ge 4$ the group $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Z})$ contains finitely generated orbit undecidable subgroups.

A subgroup $A \le \mathrm{Aut}(H)$ of the automorphism group of a group $H$ is orbit decidable if there is an algorithm that, given $u, v \in H$, decides whether some $\alpha \in A$ maps $u$ to a conjugate of $v$; otherwise it is orbit undecidable. Then (source):

1. for every $n \ge 4$, $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Z}) = \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}^n)$ contains a finitely generated orbit undecidable subgroup (Proposition 7.5);
2. there exist groups $\mathbb{Z}^4 \rtimes F$, with $F$ a finitely generated free group acting through a homomorphism $F \to \mathrm{GL}_4(\mathbb{Z})$, with unsolvable conjugacy problem (Corollary 7.6).

These were the first known examples of [finitely generated free abelian]-by-[finitely generated free] groups with unsolvable conjugacy problem.

## Expansion

The main theorem of the source says that, for an algorithmic short exact sequence $1 \to H \to G \to F \to 1$ in which $H$ has solvable twisted conjugacy problem, $F$ has solvable conjugacy problem, and every non-trivial $h \in F$ generates a subgroup of finite index in its centralizer with computable coset representatives (as for free $F$), $G$ has solvable conjugacy problem exactly when the action subgroup $A \le \mathrm{Aut}(H)$ is orbit decidable. The free subgroup $\langle P, Q \rangle \le \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ meets the conjugacy stabilizer of $(1, 0)$ in an infinite cyclic subgroup; a free subgroup $\langle P', Q' \rangle \le \langle P, Q \rangle$ of rank two meeting that cyclic subgroup trivially gives a copy $B \cong F_2 \times F_2$ in $\mathrm{GL}_4(\mathbb{Z})$, block-diagonal in two copies of $\langle P', Q' \rangle$, that meets the conjugacy stabilizer of $v = (1, 0, 1, 0)$ trivially (Proposition 7.5); Mihailova's construction gives a finitely generated $A \le B$ with unsolvable membership problem, from a finitely presented group with unsolvable word problem, and the source's key proposition turns this into orbit undecidability of $A$, hence unsolvable conjugacy problem for $\mathbb{Z}^4 \rtimes F_m$ with $F_m$ a finitely generated free group acting through a surjection onto $A$.
