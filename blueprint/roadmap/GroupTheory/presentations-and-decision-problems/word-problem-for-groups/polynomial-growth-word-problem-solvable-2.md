# Finitely presented groups of polynomial growth have solvable word problem

Let $G$ be a group with a finite presentation $\varphi : F_n \to G$, that is, $\varphi$ is surjective and its kernel is the normal closure of a finite set, and suppose $G$ has polynomial growth with respect to some finite generating family. Then the word problem of $G$ with respect to $\varphi$ is solvable: the set of words $w \in F_n$ with $\varphi(w) = 1$ is decidable. This follows from Gromov's theorem that finitely generated groups of polynomial growth are virtually nilpotent.
