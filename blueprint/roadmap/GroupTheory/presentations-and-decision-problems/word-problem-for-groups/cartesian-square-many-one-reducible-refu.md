# The Cartesian square of a normal subgroup of a free group need not be m-reducible to it

There are a free group $F$ on a computably coded countable set and a normal subgroup $N$ of $F$ such that no computable map $(w_1, w_2) \mapsto w$ on words satisfies $w_1, w_2 \in N \iff w \in N$. Kourovka Problem 4.20(a) has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 4.20(a) (m-reducibility of the Cartesian square of a normal subgroup)](cartesian-square-many-one-reducible.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.20(a) (M. K. Valiev); resolution: O. V. Belegradek, m-Degrees of the word problem, Siberian Math. J. 19 (1978), 867-870, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00972790.
