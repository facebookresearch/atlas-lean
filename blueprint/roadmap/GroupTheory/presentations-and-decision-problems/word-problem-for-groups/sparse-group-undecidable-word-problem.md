# Kourovka 7.24: sparse groups with undecidable word problem

For a surjective homomorphism $\varphi : F_n \to G$ from the free group on $n$ generators, the word problem of $G$ with respect to $\varphi$ is solvable when the set of words $w \in F_n$ with $\varphi(w) = 1$ is computable (decidable). Kourovka problem 7.24 (A. V. Kuznetsov) asks for a finitely generated group $G$ with a surjection $\varphi : F_n \to G$ such that $G$ is sparse (the variety it generates has at most countably many subvarieties) and the word problem of $G$ with respect to $\varphi$ is not solvable.
