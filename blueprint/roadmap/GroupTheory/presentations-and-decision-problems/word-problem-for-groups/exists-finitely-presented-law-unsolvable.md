# Kourovka 4.3: finitely presented groups with a law and unsolvable word problem

For a surjective homomorphism $\varphi : F_n \to G$ from the free group on $n$ generators, the word problem of $G$ with respect to $\varphi$ is solvable when the set of words $w \in F_n$ with $\varphi(w) = 1$ is computable (decidable). Kourovka problem 4.3 (S. I. Adian) asks for a finitely presented group $G = \langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid R\rangle = F_n/\langle\langle R\rangle\rangle$, with $R$ a finite set of words, such that

- the word problem of $G$ with respect to the quotient map $F_n \to G$ is not solvable;
- $G$ satisfies a non-trivial law: there are $k$ and a word $w \neq 1$ in $F_k$ with $w(g_1, \dots, g_k) = 1$ for all $g_1, \dots, g_k \in G$.
