# A finitely generated sparse group with undecidable word problem

There is a finitely generated group $G$, with a surjection $\varphi : F_n \to G$, that is sparse (the variety it generates has at most countably many subvarieties) and whose word problem with respect to $\varphi$ is undecidable. An example is a free group of the variety $\mathfrak{N}_3\mathfrak{A}$ of nilpotent-of-class-at-most-3-by-abelian groups: every subvariety of $\mathfrak{N}_3\mathfrak{A}$ is finitely based (Krasil'nikov), so there are only countably many, and its word problem is undecidable (Kharlampovich).
