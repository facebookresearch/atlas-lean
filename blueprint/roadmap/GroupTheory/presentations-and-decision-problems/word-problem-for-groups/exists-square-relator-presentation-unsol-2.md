# A finitely presented group with square relators and unsolvable word problem

There is a finitely presented group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid R\rangle$, with $R$ a finite set of words each of the form $w^2$, whose word problem is not solvable. Sarkisyan constructed such a group, all of whose defining relations have the form $A^2 = 1$.
