# Kernel of a presentation in the variety of metabelian groups

For $n \in \mathbb N$ and a finite set $R$ of elements of the free group $F_n$, the normal closure in $F_n$ of $R$ together with the second derived subgroup $F_n''$. The quotient of $F_n$ by it is the metabelian group presented by $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid R \rangle$ in the variety of metabelian groups.
