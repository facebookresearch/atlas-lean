# The conjugacy problem is solvable in finitely generated metabelian groups

For every finitely generated metabelian group, presented as $F_n / \langle\langle R \cup F_n''\rangle\rangle$ with $R$ finite, there is an algorithm deciding whether two words represent conjugate elements.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.32 (M. I. Kargapolov); resolution: G. A. Noskov, Conjugacy problem in metabelian groups, Math. Notes 31 (1982), 252-258, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01138933.
