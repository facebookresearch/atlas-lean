# A finitely presented group with a non-trivial law and unsolvable word problem

There is a finitely presented group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid R\rangle$, with $R$ a finite set of words, whose word problem is not solvable and which satisfies a non-trivial law $w(g_1, \dots, g_k) = 1$ for some word $w \neq 1$ in $F_k$. Kharlampovich constructed a finitely presented soluble group with unsolvable word problem; being soluble, it satisfies a non-trivial law.
