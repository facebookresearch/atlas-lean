# Kourovka Problem 4.20(a) (m-reducibility of the Cartesian square of a normal subgroup)

The proposition that for every computably coded countable set $\alpha$ and every normal subgroup $N$ of the free group on $\alpha$, the Cartesian square of $N$ is $m$-reducible to $N$: some computable function sends each pair of words $(w_1, w_2)$ (lists of letters $a$ or $a^{-1}$, $a \in \alpha$) to a word $w$ with $w_1 \in N$ and $w_2 \in N$ if and only if $w \in N$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.20(a) (M. K. Valiev).
