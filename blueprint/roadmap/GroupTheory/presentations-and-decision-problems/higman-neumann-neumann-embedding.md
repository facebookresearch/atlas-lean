# Higman–Neumann–Neumann embedding theorem

Every countable group $G$ embeds in a group
generated by two elements: there are a group $H$, elements $a, b \in H$ that generate $H$, and an
injective group homomorphism $f \colon G \to H$.

## Sources

- G. Higman, B. H. Neumann, H. Neumann, J. London Math. Soc. 24 (1949) 247–254 via HNN extensions; Lean states countable embedding into 2-generator group including finite case.
