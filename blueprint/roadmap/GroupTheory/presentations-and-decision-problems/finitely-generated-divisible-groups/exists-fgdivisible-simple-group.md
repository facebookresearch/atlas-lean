# Kourovka Problem 1.1 (finitely generated divisible groups)

The proposition that there is a finitely generated simple group $G$ that is divisible: for every $g \in G$ and every $n \ge 1$ some $h \in G$ has $h^n = g$. The problem states this as equivalent to the existence of a nontrivial finitely generated divisible group, since a simple quotient of such a group by a maximal normal subgroup is again finitely generated and divisible.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.1 (Yu. A. Bogan); resolution: V. S. Guba, A finitely generated complete group, Math. USSR-Izv. 29 (1987), 233-277.
