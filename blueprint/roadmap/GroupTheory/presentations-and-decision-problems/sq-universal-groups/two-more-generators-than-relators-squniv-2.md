# Groups with two more generators than relators are SQ-universal

Every group with a presentation on $r$ generators and at most $r - 2$ relators is SQ-universal. A group is SQ-universal when every countable group embeds in a quotient of it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.39 (A. M. Macbeath, P. M. Neumann); resolution: B. Baumslag and S. J. Pride, Groups with two more generators than relators, J. London Math. Soc. (2) 17 (1978), 425-426, https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-17.3.425.
