# Kourovka Problem 4.39 (groups with two more generators than relators)

The proposition that every group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_r \mid R_1, \dots, R_m \rangle$ with $m + 2 \le r$ (so $r \ge 2$) is SQ-universal. A group is SQ-universal when every countable group embeds in a quotient of it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.39 (A. M. Macbeath, P. M. Neumann).
