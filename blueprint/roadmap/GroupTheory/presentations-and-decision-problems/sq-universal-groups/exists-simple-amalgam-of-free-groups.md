# Kourovka Problem 4.45(b) (simple amalgams of free groups)

The proposition that there are $r_0, r_1, c \in \mathbb N$ and injective homomorphisms from the free group $F_c$ to $F_{r_0}$ and $F_{r_1}$, both with proper image, such that the amalgamated free product $F_{r_0} *_{F_c} F_{r_1}$ is simple.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.45 (P. M. Neumann).
