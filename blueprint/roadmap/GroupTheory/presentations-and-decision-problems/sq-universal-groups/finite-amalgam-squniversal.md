# Kourovka Problem 4.45(a) (SQ-universality of amalgams over finite subgroups)

The proposition that for countable groups $A, B$, a finite group $C$ and injective homomorphisms $C \to A$, $C \to B$ with proper images $H \le A$, $K \le B$ such that $|A : H| \ne 2$, the free product $A *_C B$ amalgamating $H$ with $K$ is SQ-universal. So $|A : H| > 2$ and $|B : K| \ge 2$, infinite indices included. A group is SQ-universal when every countable group embeds in a quotient of it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.45 (P. M. Neumann).
