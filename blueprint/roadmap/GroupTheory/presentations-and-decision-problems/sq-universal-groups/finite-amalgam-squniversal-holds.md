# Amalgams of countable groups over finite proper subgroups, of index greater than two in one factor, are SQ-universal

Let $A, B$ be countable groups and $G = A *_C B$ their free product amalgamating finite proper subgroups $H \le A$ and $K \le B$ (isomorphic to a finite group $C$) with $|A : H| > 2$, indices possibly infinite. Then $G$ is SQ-universal. A group is SQ-universal when every countable group embeds in a quotient of it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.45 (P. M. Neumann); resolution: K. I. Lossov, SQ-universality of free products with amalgamated finite subgroups, Siberian Math. J. 27 (1986), no. 6, 890-899, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00970007; M. Burger and S. Mozes, Lattices in product of trees, Publ. Math. IHÉS 92 (2000), 151-194, Theorem 5.5(5).
