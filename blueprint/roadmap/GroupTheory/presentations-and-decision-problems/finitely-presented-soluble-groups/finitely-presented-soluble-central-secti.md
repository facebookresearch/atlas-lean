# Kourovka Problem 8.47 (finitely presented soluble groups without max-n)

The proposition that there is a finitely presented soluble group $G$ that does not satisfy the maximal condition on normal subgroups (some strictly ascending infinite chain of normal subgroups exists) but all of whose central sections are finitely generated. A central section of $G$ is $H/K$ with $K$ normal in $G$, $K \le H$ and $[H, G] \le K$ (so $H/K$ is central in $G/K$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.47 (Yu. I. Merzlyakov).
