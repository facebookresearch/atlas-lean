# A finitely presented soluble group without max-n with finitely generated central sections

There is a finitely presented soluble group that does not satisfy the maximal condition on normal subgroups but all of whose central sections are finitely generated. A central section of $G$ is $H/K$ with $K$ normal in $G$, $K \le H$ and $[H, G] \le K$ (so $H/K$ is central in $G/K$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.47 (Yu. I. Merzlyakov); resolution: Yu. V. Sosnovskii, Finitely presented solvable groups that do not satisfy the maximal condition for normal subgroups, Math. Notes 36 (1984), 577-580, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01137405.
