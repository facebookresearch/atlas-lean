# Recursively presented soluble groups embed in finitely presented soluble groups of a variety

Every finitely generated recursively presented soluble group embeds in a group finitely presented in the variety $\mathfrak A^n$ of soluble groups of derived length at most $n$, for a suitable $n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.46 (V. N. Remeslennikov); resolution: G. P. Kukin, Soviet Math. Dokl. 21 (1980), 378-381.
