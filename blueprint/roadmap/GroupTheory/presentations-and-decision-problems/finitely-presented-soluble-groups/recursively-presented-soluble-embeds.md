# Kourovka Problem 5.46 (recursively presented soluble groups)

The proposition that every finitely generated, recursively presented soluble group $G$ (a surjection $F_m \to G$ whose set of relator words is recursively enumerable) embeds in a group $H$ finitely presented in the variety $\mathfrak A^n$ of soluble groups of derived length at most $n$, for some $n$: $H \cong F_k / (\langle\langle R \rangle\rangle F_k^{(n)})$ for a free group $F_k$ of finite rank, a finite set $R$ of words and the $n$-th derived subgroup $F_k^{(n)}$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.46 (V. N. Remeslennikov).
