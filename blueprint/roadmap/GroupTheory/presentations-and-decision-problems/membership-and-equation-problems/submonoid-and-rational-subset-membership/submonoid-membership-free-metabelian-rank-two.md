# A fixed submonoid of the free metabelian group of rank two with undecidable membership

The free metabelian group of rank two contains a fixed finitely generated submonoid whose membership problem is undecidable.

Let $M_2 = F_2 / F_2''$ be the free metabelian group of rank two, the quotient of the free group $F_2$ by the second derived subgroup. There is a fixed finitely generated submonoid of $M_2$ whose membership problem within $M_2$ is undecidable (Theorem 6 of the source).

## Expansion

The derived subgroup $[M_2, M_2]$, viewed as a module over a finite-index subgroup $m\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ of the abelianization $\mathbb{Z}^2$, contains a free module of rank $m - 1$ as a direct summand. This lets the source reduce the subsemimodule membership problem of Theorem 4, which underlies the result for $\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$, to the submonoid membership problem for $M_2$.
