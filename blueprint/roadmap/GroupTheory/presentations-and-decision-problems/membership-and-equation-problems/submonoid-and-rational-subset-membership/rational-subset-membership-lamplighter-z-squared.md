# A fixed rational subset of the two-dimensional lamplighter group with undecidable membership

For every $n \ge 2$, the wreath product $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ contains a fixed rational subset whose membership problem is undecidable; hence $G \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ has undecidable rational subset membership problem for every non-trivial group $G$.

For $n \ge 2$ let $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ be the restricted wreath product. Then (source):

1. there is a fixed rational subset of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ whose membership problem is undecidable (Theorem 10);
2. for every non-trivial group $G$, the wreath product $G \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ has a fixed rational subset with undecidable membership problem, so its rational subset membership problem is undecidable (Corollary 11).

## Expansion

For a ring $R = \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ the source constructs a free $R[\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}]$-module $M$ of finite rank and a fixed finite subset $F$ of non-zero elements whose subset sum problem (whether a given element is a sum of translates of elements of $F$) is undecidable (Theorem 8), again from a tiling problem. A fixed rational subset of $M \rtimes (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ then encodes the subset sum problem (Proposition 9), and $M \rtimes (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ embeds in $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$. For the corollary, a non-trivial group $G$ contains an infinite cyclic or a non-trivial finite cyclic subgroup, so $G \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$ contains a copy of $\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$, which has a fixed finitely generated submonoid (a rational subset) with undecidable membership, or of some $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \wr (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})$.
