# A quasi-positive one-relator presentation with undecidable prefix membership problem

There are a finite alphabet $A$ and positive words $u, v$ over $A$, with $uv^{-1}$ freely reduced, such that the prefix membership problem of the one-relator presentation $\mathrm{Gp}\langle A \mid uv^{-1} = 1 \rangle$ is undecidable.

Precisely, there exist a finite set $A$ and nonempty words $u, v \in A^+$ such that the word $uv^{-1}$ is freely reduced and, for the group $G = \mathrm{Gp}\langle A \mid uv^{-1} = 1 \rangle$, no algorithm decides, given a word $w$ over $A \cup A^{-1}$, whether $w$ represents an element of the submonoid of $G$ generated by the elements represented by the prefixes of $uv^{-1}$ (Theorem 4.1 of the source).

## Expansion

The proof encodes the membership problem of a fixed finitely generated submonoid $Q$ of a positive one-relator group $G$, here a group $G_{m,n}$ with $m, n \ge 2$, into prefix membership. From a one-relation monoid presentation $\mathrm{Mon}\langle A \mid q = 1 \rangle$ of $G$ (Perrin and Schupp) and positive words $w_1, \dots, w_k$ generating $Q$, Construction 4.2 replaces each occurrence of the first letter $a$ of $q$ by $tx$ and builds a positive two-relator presentation $\mathrm{Gp}\langle Y \mid u = 1, v = 1 \rangle$ of $G * \mathbb{Z}$ in which membership in $Q$ reduces to prefix membership (Theorem 7.5). The identity map on $Y$ induces an isomorphism onto $\mathrm{Gp}\langle Y \mid vuv^{-1} = 1 \rangle$, and since $v = 1$ in this group both prefix monoids are generated by the prefixes of $u$ and of $v$; the one-relator presentation whose relator is the free reduction of $vuv^{-1}$ has the required form (Theorem 4.3). Dolinka and Gray had earlier given a one-relator group with undecidable prefix membership problem; here the relator has the quasi-positive form $uv^{-1}$ that arises in Guba's reduction of the word problem for one-relation monoids.
