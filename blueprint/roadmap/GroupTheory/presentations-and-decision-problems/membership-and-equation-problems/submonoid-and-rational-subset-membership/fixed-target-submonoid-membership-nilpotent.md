# Fixed-target submonoid membership is undecidable in some nilpotent group

There are a finitely generated nilpotent group $G$ and a fixed element $g_0 \in G$ such that no algorithm decides, for a finite set $S$ of elements of $G$, whether $g_0$ lies in the submonoid generated by $S$.

There exist a finitely generated nilpotent group $G = \langle X \rangle$ and an element $g_0 \in G$ such that the following problem is undecidable (Proposition 26 of the source):

- input: a finite set $S$ of words over $X \cup X^{-1}$;
- question: does $g_0$ belong to the submonoid $S^*$ of $G$ generated by the elements that the words of $S$ represent?

This fixed-target submonoid membership problem lies between the identity problem, which asks whether the identity lies in the subsemigroup generated by $S$ and which the source proves decidable in every finitely generated nilpotent group, and the full submonoid membership problem.

## Expansion

Fix $d, k$ such that solvability of Diophantine equations of degree $d$ in $k$ variables over $\mathbb{Z}$ is undecidable. By a lemma of Pak and Soukup, for each polynomial $f$ of degree $d$ in $k$ variables there are computable matrices $P, Q, A_1, \dots, A_k$ in $\mathrm{UT}(m, \mathbb{Z})$, with $m$ depending only on $d$ and $k$, such that $P W_1 Q W_2 P^{-1} W_3 Q^{-1} W_4 = e$ has a solution with $W_1, \dots, W_4$ in $\langle A_1, \dots, A_k \rangle$ exactly when $f = 0$ has an integer solution. The source turns this equation into membership of a fixed element of a group built from $\mathrm{UT}(m, \mathbb{Z})$ in the submonoid generated by a finite set computed from $f$.
