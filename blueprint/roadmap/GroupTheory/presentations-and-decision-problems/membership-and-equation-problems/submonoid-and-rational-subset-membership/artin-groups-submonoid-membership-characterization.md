# An Artin group has undecidable submonoid membership exactly when it contains A(P4) or A(C4)

For Artin groups, submonoid membership and rational subset membership are decidable or undecidable together, and both are undecidable exactly when the group contains the right-angled Artin group of the path or of the square on four vertices.

For an Artin graph $\Gamma$ (a finite simplicial graph with edge labels $m \ge 2$), the Artin group is $A(\Gamma) = \langle V \mid (u, v)_{m} = (v, u)_{m} \text{ for each edge } \{u, v\} \text{ with label } m \rangle$, where $(u, v)_m$ is the alternating word $uvu\cdots$ of length $m$. Let $P_4$ and $C_4$ be the path and the cycle on four vertices with all labels $2$. For every Artin group $A(\Gamma)$ the following are equivalent (Theorem 3.4 of the source):

1. the submonoid membership problem of $A(\Gamma)$ is decidable: some algorithm decides, given a finite set $S$ of words and a word $w$ over the generators and their inverses, whether $w$ represents an element of the submonoid generated by $S$;
2. the rational subset membership problem of $A(\Gamma)$ is decidable;
3. $A(\Gamma)$ is subgroup separable;
4. $A(\Gamma)$ contains no subgroup isomorphic to $A(P_4)$ or $A(C_4) = F_2 \times F_2$.

The source also gives the condition in terms of induced subgraphs of $\Gamma$, extending the result of Lohrey and Steinberg for right-angled Artin groups (Theorem 2.1).

## Expansion

$A(C_4) = F_2 \times F_2$ has a finitely generated subgroup with undecidable membership (Mihailova) and $A(P_4)$ a finitely generated submonoid with undecidable membership (Lohrey and Steinberg); these pass to every group containing them. Neither theorem has a page here. Conversely, Artin groups without such subgroups are built from Artin groups of rank at most $2$ by free products and direct products with $\mathbb{Z}$, which preserve decidability of rational subset membership; the source combines this with the Almeida–Lima characterization of subgroup separable Artin groups.
