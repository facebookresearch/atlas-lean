# A one-relator group with reduced relator and undecidable prefix membership problem

There is a one-relator presentation $\langle X \mid w = 1 \rangle$ with $w$ a reduced word whose prefix membership problem is undecidable: no algorithm decides membership in the submonoid generated by the prefixes of $w$.

For a group presentation $\langle X \mid w = 1 \rangle$, the prefix monoid is the submonoid of the group generated by the elements represented by the prefixes of $w$, and the prefix membership problem asks for an algorithm deciding membership in it. There is a finite alphabet $X$ and a reduced word $w$ over $X \cup X^{-1}$ such that $\langle X \mid w = 1 \rangle$ has undecidable prefix membership problem (Theorem 8.2 of the source). Earlier work of Gray already gave a one-relator group with undecidable prefix membership problem; the point here is that the relator can be taken reduced.

Whether the problem is decidable for every cyclically reduced relator is open (a question of Bestvina's problem list recalled in the source). The source proves decidability for many one-relator groups low in the Magnus–Moldavanskii hierarchy.

## Expansion

Let $H = \langle a, b \mid abab^{-1}a^{-1}ba^{-1}b^{-1} = 1 \rangle$; by Gray's work a fixed finitely generated submonoid of $H$ has undecidable membership. The source builds a reduced word $w$ from conjugates of this relator and its cyclic shift, interleaved with the submonoid generators, so that the prefix monoid of $\langle X \mid w = 1 \rangle$ encodes membership in that submonoid. For $E$-unitary one-relator special inverse monoids, prefix membership is tied to the word problem (Ivanov, Margolis and Meakin).
