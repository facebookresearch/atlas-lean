# Knapsack is undecidable for direct powers of the Heisenberg group

There is a $d$ such that the knapsack problem for the direct power $H_3(\mathbb{Z})^d$ of the discrete Heisenberg group is undecidable; already membership in a fixed product of cyclic subgroups is undecidable.

Let $H_3(\mathbb{Z})$ be the discrete Heisenberg group of upper unitriangular $3 \times 3$ integer matrices. Then (source):

1. there are a constant $d$ and fixed elements $g_1, \dots, g_\lambda \in H_3(\mathbb{Z})^d$ such that membership in the product $\langle g_1 \rangle \langle g_2 \rangle \cdots \langle g_\lambda \rangle$ of cyclic subgroups is undecidable (Theorem 6.4);
2. there is a constant $d$ such that the knapsack problem for $H_3(\mathbb{Z})^d$ is undecidable (Theorem 6.5);
3. there are a constant $d$ and four fixed abelian subgroups $G_1, \dots, G_4 \le H_3(\mathbb{Z})^d$ such that membership in the product $G_1 G_2 G_3 G_4$ is undecidable (Theorem 6.6).

By contrast, the knapsack problem for $H_3(\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathbb{Z}^e$ is decidable for every $e \ge 0$ (source).

## Expansion

Products of powers of fixed elements of $H_3(\mathbb{Z})$ have central coordinate a quadratic polynomial in the exponents. Using a fixed polynomial whose solvability in a parameter is undecidable (by the DPRM theorem), the source builds a fixed exponential expression $E$ over $H_3(\mathbb{Z})^d \times \mathbb{Z}^e$ for which it is undecidable whether $E = g$ has a solution, given $g$ (Proposition 6.2), and converts it into membership in a product of cyclic subgroups, with $\mathbb{Z} \le H_3(\mathbb{Z})$ absorbing the abelian factor.
