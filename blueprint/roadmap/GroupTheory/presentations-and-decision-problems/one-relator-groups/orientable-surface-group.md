# Orientable closed surface groups

For a natural number $g$, the group $\langle a_1, b_1, \dots, a_g, b_g \mid [a_1, b_1] \cdots [a_g, b_g] \rangle$ with $[a, b] = aba^{-1}b^{-1}$, the fundamental group of the closed orientable surface of genus $g$. For $g = 0$ it is trivial.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.36 (O. V. Mel'nikov).
