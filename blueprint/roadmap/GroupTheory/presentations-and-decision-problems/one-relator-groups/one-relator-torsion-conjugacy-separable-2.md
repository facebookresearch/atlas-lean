# One-relator groups with torsion are conjugacy separable

Every one-relator group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid r \rangle$ with a non-identity element of finite order is conjugacy separable. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$. Minasyan and Zalesskii prove the stronger statement that such groups are hereditarily conjugacy separable. This answers Kourovka Problem 8.69 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.69 (C. Y. Tang); resolution: A. Minasyan and P. Zalesskii, One-relator groups with torsion are conjugacy separable, J. Algebra 382 (2013), 39-45, Theorem 1.1, https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.0488.
