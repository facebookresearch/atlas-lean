# Kourovka Problem 8.68 (residual finiteness of one-relator groups with torsion)

The proposition that for every element $r$ of the free group on $a, b$ whose reduced word is cyclically reduced and which is not a proper power ($r \ne s^k$ for all $s$ and $k \ge 2$), if $\langle a, b \mid r \rangle$ is residually finite then $\langle a, b \mid r^t \rangle$ is residually finite for every $t > 1$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.68 (C. Y. Tang).
