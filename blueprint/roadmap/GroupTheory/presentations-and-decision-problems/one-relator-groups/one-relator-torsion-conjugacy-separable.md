# Kourovka Problem 8.69 (conjugacy separability of one-relator groups with torsion)

The proposition that every one-relator group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid r \rangle$ that has a non-identity element of finite order is conjugacy separable. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.69 (C. Y. Tang).
