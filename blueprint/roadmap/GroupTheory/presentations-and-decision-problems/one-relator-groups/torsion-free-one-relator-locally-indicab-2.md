# Torsion-free one-relator groups are locally indicable

Every torsion-free one-relator group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid r \rangle$ is locally indicable. A group is locally indicable if every nontrivial finitely generated subgroup admits a surjective homomorphism onto $\mathbb{Z}$. This is Brodskiĭ's theorem, answering Kourovka Problem 4.10 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.10 (G. Baumslag); resolution: S. D. Brodskiĭ, Equations over groups, and groups with one defining relation, Siberian Math. J. 25 (1984), 235-251.
