# A braid in the derived subgroup of B3 that is not a commutator

In the braid group $B_3 = \langle x, y \mid xyx = yxy \rangle$ (the Artin group of type $A_2$, with $x = \sigma_1$ and $y = \sigma_2$), the braid

$$\beta = (xyxyx)^{12}\,(xy)^{-12}\,(xyx)^{-12}$$

lies in the derived subgroup $[B_3, B_3]$ but is not a commutator $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$ of any $a, b \in B_3$. This answers Kourovka Problem 6.22.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.22 (G. S. Makanin); resolution: Yu. S. Semënov, Abstracts of the 10th All-Union Symposium on Group Theory, Minsk, 1986, p. 207 (Russian).
