# Braid groups on three or more strands have proper non-abelian torsion-free quotients

For
every $n \ge 3$, the braid group $B_n$ on $n$ strands has a nontrivial normal subgroup $N$ such that
the quotient $B_n / N$ is non-abelian and torsion-free (no element other than the identity has
finite order). For $n > 4$ this answers Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.102(c).

## Sources

- Direct construction: $N$ is the kernel of Artin's action of $B_n$ on the free group of rank $n$, read through the Magnus expansion modulo the words of length greater than $n$. The case $n > 4$ is Problem 14.102(c) of E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026).
