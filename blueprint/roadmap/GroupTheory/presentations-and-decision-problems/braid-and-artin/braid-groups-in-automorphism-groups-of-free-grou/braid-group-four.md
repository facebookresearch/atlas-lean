# The braid group $B_4$

The braid group on four strands, given by Artin's presentation: the
group generated by $\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3$ subject to the relations
$\sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1 = \sigma_2 \sigma_1 \sigma_2$, $\sigma_2 \sigma_3 \sigma_2 =
\sigma_3 \sigma_2 \sigma_3$ and $\sigma_1 \sigma_3 = \sigma_3 \sigma_1$.
