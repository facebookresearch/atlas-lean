# The conjugacy problem in braid groups (Kourovka 1.11)

The statement (Kourovka Problem 1.11, E. Artin; M. D. Greendlinger) that for every $n > 4$ the conjugacy problem in the [braid group](../endomorphisms-of-braid-groups/braid-group.md) $B_n$ is solvable: there is an algorithm that decides, for any two words $u$ and $v$ in the Artin generators $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ and their inverses, whether the [braids they represent](braid-of-word.md) are conjugate in $B_n$. Here words are finite sequences of letters, and the set of pairs $(u, v)$ whose braids are conjugate is required to be a computable predicate on pairs of such sequences.

The statement holds: [Garside's algorithm solves the conjugacy problem in every braid group](braid-conjugacy-problem-solvable-holds.md).
