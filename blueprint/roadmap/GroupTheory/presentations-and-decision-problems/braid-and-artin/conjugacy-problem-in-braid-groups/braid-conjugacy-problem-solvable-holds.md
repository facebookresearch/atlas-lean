# Garside's solution of the conjugacy problem in braid groups

For every $n > 4$ the conjugacy problem in the [braid group](../endomorphisms-of-braid-groups/braid-group.md) $B_n$ is solvable: whether two words in the Artin generators $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ and their inverses [represent](braid-of-word.md) conjugate braids is decidable. This answers [Kourovka Problem 1.11](braid-conjugacy-problem-solvable.md) in the affirmative.

The Kourovka Archive records the solution of Makanin (1968) and Garside (1969). Garside's algorithm, through the normal form of a braid as a power of the fundamental braid times a positive braid and the finite summit set of a conjugacy class, decides conjugacy in $B_n$ for every $n$, not only for $n > 4$.
