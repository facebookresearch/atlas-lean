# Artin braid groups

For a natural number $n$, the braid group $B_n$ on $n$ strands is the Artin group of the Coxeter matrix of type $A_{n-1}$: it is generated by $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ subject to $\sigma_i\sigma_j = \sigma_j\sigma_i$ for $|i - j| > 1$ and $\sigma_i\sigma_j\sigma_i = \sigma_j\sigma_i\sigma_j$ for $|i - j| = 1$. Here $n - 1$ is truncated subtraction, so $B_0 = B_1$ is trivial.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 12.23 (Birman's conjecture), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
