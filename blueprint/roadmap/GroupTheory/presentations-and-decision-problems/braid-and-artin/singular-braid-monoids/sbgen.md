# Generators of the singular braid monoid

For a natural number $m$, the generating set of the singular braid monoid with $m$ standard generators: a formal symbol $\sigma_i$, a formal symbol $\sigma_i^{-1}$ and a singular generator $\tau_i$ for each $i \in \{0, \dots, m - 1\}$, all distinct (an inductive type with three constructors indexed by $i$).

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 12.23 (Birman's conjecture), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
