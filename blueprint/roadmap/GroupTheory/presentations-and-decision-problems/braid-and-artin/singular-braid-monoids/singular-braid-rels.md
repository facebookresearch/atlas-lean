# Birman's relations for the singular braid monoid

For a natural number $m$, the relation on words in the free monoid over the generators $\sigma_i, \sigma_i^{-1}, \tau_i$ ($i < m$) that relates exactly the following pairs: $\sigma_i\sigma_i^{-1}$ and $\sigma_i^{-1}\sigma_i$ to the empty word, and $\sigma_i\tau_i$ to $\tau_i\sigma_i$, for every $i$; $\sigma_i\sigma_j$ to $\sigma_j\sigma_i$, $\sigma_i\tau_j$ to $\tau_j\sigma_i$ and $\tau_i\tau_j$ to $\tau_j\tau_i$ whenever $|i - j| \ge 2$; and $\sigma_i\sigma_j\sigma_i$ to $\sigma_j\sigma_i\sigma_j$ and $\sigma_i\sigma_j\tau_i$ to $\tau_j\sigma_i\sigma_j$ whenever $|i - j| = 1$.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 12.23 (Birman's conjecture), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
