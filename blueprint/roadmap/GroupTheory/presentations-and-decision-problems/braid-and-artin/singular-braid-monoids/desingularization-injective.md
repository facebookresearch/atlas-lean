# Birman's conjecture on singular braid monoids (Bestvina Q 12.23)

The proposition that for every $n$ there is an injective monoid homomorphism $\Phi : SB_n \to \mathbb{Z}[B_n]$ into the integral group ring of the braid group with $\Phi(\sigma_i) = \sigma_i$, $\Phi(\sigma_i^{-1}) = \sigma_i^{-1}$ and $\Phi(\tau_i) = \sigma_i - \sigma_i^{-1}$ for every $i$. Here $B_n$ is the braid group on generators $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ with $\sigma_i\sigma_j = \sigma_j\sigma_i$ for $|i - j| > 1$ and $\sigma_i\sigma_j\sigma_i = \sigma_j\sigma_i\sigma_j$ for $|i - j| = 1$, and $SB_n$ is the monoid generated by $\sigma_i, \sigma_i^{-1}, \tau_i$ ($1 \le i \le n - 1$) subject to $\sigma_i\sigma_i^{-1} = \sigma_i^{-1}\sigma_i = 1$, $\sigma_i\tau_i = \tau_i\sigma_i$; $\sigma_i\sigma_j = \sigma_j\sigma_i$, $\sigma_i\tau_j = \tau_j\sigma_i$, $\tau_i\tau_j = \tau_j\tau_i$ for $|i - j| > 1$; and $\sigma_i\sigma_j\sigma_i = \sigma_j\sigma_i\sigma_j$, $\sigma_i\sigma_j\tau_i = \tau_j\sigma_i\sigma_j$ for $|i - j| = 1$. A monoid homomorphism on $SB_n$ is determined by these values, so $\Phi$ is the desingularization map.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 12.23 (Birman's conjecture), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
