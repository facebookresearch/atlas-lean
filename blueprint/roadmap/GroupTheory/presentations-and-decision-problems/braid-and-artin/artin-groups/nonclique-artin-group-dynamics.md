# Acylindrical hyperbolicity and weak malnormality for non-clique irreducible Artin groups

Let $M$ be a Coxeter matrix on a finite linearly ordered set $S$, with zero encoding an infinite off-diagonal label. Let $G=A_M$ be the Artin group, and for $X\subseteq S$ let $A_X$ be the subgroup generated by the corresponding standard generators. A parabolic is a subgroup $gA_Xg^{-1}$, without any spherical-type restriction.

Assume every nontrivial partition of $S$ has a cross-label different from $2$ and some off-diagonal label is infinite. Then $G$ acts isometrically on a geodesic Gromov-hyperbolic metric space with an acylindrical, non-elementary action. In the source the four-point Gromov-product inequality has a uniform additive constant; non-elementarity is witnessed by three orbit sequences whose internal Gromov products tend jointly to infinity but whose pairwise cross-products are uniformly bounded. For each $\epsilon>0$ acylindricity bounds, uniformly for sufficiently distant $x,y$, the number of elements that move both $x,y$ by at most $\epsilon$. Also for every proper parabolic $P$ there is $g$ for which $P\cap gPg^{-1}$ is finite.

## Expansion

Prove equivalence of the orbit-sequence witness to the boundary convention actually used and retain irreducibility and infinite-edge assumptions. Do not substitute a merely unbounded action.
