# Unique smallest Artin parabolic containing a subset

Let $M$ be a Coxeter matrix on a finite linearly ordered set $S$, with zero encoding an infinite off-diagonal label. Let $G=A_M$ be the Artin group, and for $X\subseteq S$ let $A_X$ be the subgroup generated by the corresponding standard generators. A parabolic is a subgroup $gA_Xg^{-1}$, without any spherical-type restriction.

For every subset $E\subseteq G$, there is exactly one parabolic $P$ with $E\subseteq P$ such that $P\subseteq Q$ for every parabolic $Q$ containing $E$.

## Depends on

- [Artin group presentation](artin-group.md)
- Parabolics and arbitrary intersections of subgroups.

## Proof depends on

- [Arbitrary parabolic intersections](arbitrary-parabolic-intersection-rank-bound.md)
- The family of parabolics containing $E$ is nonempty (it contains $G$) and its intersection is parabolic.

## Expansion

Specialize the intersection result to all parabolic supersets of $E$ and prove minimality and uniqueness by the two subgroup inclusions.
