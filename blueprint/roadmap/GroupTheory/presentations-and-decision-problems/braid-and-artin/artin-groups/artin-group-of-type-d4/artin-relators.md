# Relators of the group M of Kourovka Problem 8.11

The set of six relators in the free group on $x, y, z, t$:

$$x^{-1}y^{-1}xy,\quad y^{-1}z^{-1}yz,\quad z^{-1}x^{-1}zx,\quad x^{-1}t^{-1}x^{-1}txt,\quad y^{-1}t^{-1}y^{-1}tyt,\quad z^{-1}t^{-1}z^{-1}tzt.$$

They express that $x, y, z$ commute pairwise and that each of $x, y, z$ satisfies the braid relation $utu = tut$ with $t$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 8.11 (D. L. Johnson).
