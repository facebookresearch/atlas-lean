# Generator $z$ for Kourovka Problem 8.11

The element $z$ of the free group $F_4$ on the four letters $0, 1, 2, 3$ is the basis element indexed by $2$. The four basis elements $x, y, z, t$ (indexed by $0, 1, 2, 3$) are the generators of the presentation of the group $M$ of Kourovka Problem 8.11.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 8.11 (D. L. Johnson).
