# Kourovka Problem 8.11 (N is free of rank 3)

The proposition that the normal closure $N$ of $zx^{-1}$ in the group $M = \langle x, y, z, t \mid [x, y] = [y, z] = [z, x] = (x, t) = (y, t) = (z, t) = 1 \rangle$ with $[u, v] = u^{-1}v^{-1}uv$ and $(u, t) = u^{-1}t^{-1}u^{-1}tut$ is isomorphic to the free group of rank $3$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 8.11 (D. L. Johnson).
