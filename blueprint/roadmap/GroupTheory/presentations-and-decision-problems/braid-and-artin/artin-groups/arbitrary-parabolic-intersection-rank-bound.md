# Arbitrary Artin parabolic intersections reduce to at most the rank

Let $M$ be a Coxeter matrix on a finite linearly ordered set $S$, with zero encoding an infinite off-diagonal label. Let $G=A_M$ be the Artin group, and for $X\subseteq S$ let $A_X$ be the subgroup generated by the corresponding standard generators. A parabolic is a subgroup $gA_Xg^{-1}$, without any spherical-type restriction.

For any set $\mathcal F$ of parabolic subgroups of $G$, there is a finite subfamily $\mathcal F_0\subseteq\mathcal F$ of size at most $|S|$ such that $\bigcap\mathcal F=\bigcap\mathcal F_0$. The common intersection is parabolic. The empty intersection is the whole group.

## Depends on

- [Artin group presentation](artin-group.md)
- Arbitrary infima of subgroups; rank of a generating diagram.

## Proof depends on

- [Binary parabolic intersections](two-parabolic-subgroups-intersect-parabolically.md)
- Strict descent in rank along proper inclusions of parabolic subgroups, to obtain the bound on the selected subfamily.

## Expansion

First obtain binary intersections and compare ranks after each strict descent. Stop when every member contains the current intersection; the result must handle the empty family.
