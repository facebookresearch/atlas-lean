# Intersection of two Artin parabolics

Let $M$ be a Coxeter matrix on a finite linearly ordered set $S$, with zero encoding an infinite off-diagonal label. Let $G=A_M$ be the Artin group, and for $X\subseteq S$ let $A_X$ be the subgroup generated by the corresponding standard generators. A parabolic is a subgroup $gA_Xg^{-1}$, without any spherical-type restriction.

For every $X,Y\subseteq S$ and $g,h\in G$, there exist $Z\subseteq S$ and $k\in G$ such that $g A_X g^{-1}\cap h A_Y h^{-1}=kA_Zk^{-1}$.

## Depends on

- [Artin group presentation](artin-group.md)
- Parabolic subgroup as subgroup closure of the appropriate generator image, followed by conjugation.

## Proof depends on

- Unconditional intersection theorem for standard generators of all Coxeter types, including infinite edges.

## Expansion

The existing presentation omits literal identity relators for diagonal and infinite labels; prove the presentation comparison, then derive the two-conjugate normal form for the intersection.
