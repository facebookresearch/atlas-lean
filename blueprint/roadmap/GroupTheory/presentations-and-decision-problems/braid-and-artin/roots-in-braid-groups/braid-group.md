# The braid group $B_n$ by Artin's presentation

For a natural number $n$, the braid group
$B_n$ on $n$ strands is the group generated by $\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{n-2}$ subject to the
relations $\sigma_i \sigma_j = \sigma_j \sigma_i$ for $j \ge i + 2$ and
$\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i = \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}$ for $0 \le i \le n - 3$; it is
the trivial group when $n \le 1$.
