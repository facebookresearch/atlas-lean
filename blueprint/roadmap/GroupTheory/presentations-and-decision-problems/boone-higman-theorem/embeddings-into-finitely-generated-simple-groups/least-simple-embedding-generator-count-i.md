# Kourovka Problem 4.59 (embedding countable groups in 2-generated simple groups)

The proposition that the least positive integer $n$ such that every countable group admits an injective homomorphism into some simple group generated by $n$ elements (the entries of an $n$-tuple) is $n = 2$. For $n = 1$ the simple groups in question are cyclic of prime order, which do not contain $\mathbb{Z}$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.59 (D. M. Smirnov; question of P. Hall); resolution: A. P. Goryushkin, Imbedding of countable groups in 2-generated simple groups, Math. Notes 16 (1974), 725-727, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01105577.
