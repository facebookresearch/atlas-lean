# Simple type F-infinity overgroups for decidable word problems

Every finitely generated group with decidable word problem admits an injective homomorphism into a nontrivial simple group of type $F_\infty$. Decidable word problem means that for some finite generating tuple, membership in the identity language of finite words in that tuple and its formal inverses is computable. Type $F_\infty$ here means having the fundamental group of a connected Hausdorff CW complex with finitely many cells in each degree and a surjective covering by a contractible space. The complex may have arbitrarily high-dimensional cells.

## Depends on

- Computable signed-word problems, simple groups, and CW classifying spaces of finite type.

## Proof depends on

- Construction of simple overgroups that have finitely many cells in every degree and verification of a contractible cover.

## Expansion

Prove that the selected overgroup is nontrivial and simple, prove the embedding, and then construct the finite-type classifying space. A finite group presentation alone is not the higher-finiteness requirement.
