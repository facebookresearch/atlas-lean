# Generators of a presentation of non-negative deficiency freely generating a free subgroup

In every group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r_1, \dots, r_n \rangle$ with $n \le m$, some $m - n$ of the given generators freely generate a free subgroup of rank $m - n$. This answers Kourovka Problem 5.29 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.29 (R. C. Lyndon); resolution: N. S. Romanovskii, Free subgroups of finitely presented groups, Algebra and Logic 16 (1977), 62-68.
