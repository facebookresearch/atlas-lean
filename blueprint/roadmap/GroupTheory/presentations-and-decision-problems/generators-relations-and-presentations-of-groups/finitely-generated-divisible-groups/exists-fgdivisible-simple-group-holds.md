# Finitely generated divisible simple groups exist

There is a finitely generated simple group in which every element has an $n$-th root for every $n \ge 1$; Kourovka Problem 1.1 has a positive answer. Guba constructs a nontrivial finitely generated group with roots of all degrees, and its quotient by a maximal normal subgroup is such a group.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.1 (Yu. A. Bogan); resolution: V. S. Guba, A finitely generated complete group, Math. USSR-Izv. 29 (1987), 233-277.
