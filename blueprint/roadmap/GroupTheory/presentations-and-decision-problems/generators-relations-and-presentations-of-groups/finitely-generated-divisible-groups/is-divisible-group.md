# Divisible groups

A group $G$ is divisible when for every $g \in G$ and every integer $n \ge 1$ there is $h \in G$ with $h^n = g$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.1 (Yu. A. Bogan); resolution: V. S. Guba, A finitely generated complete group, Math. USSR-Izv. 29 (1987), 233-277.
