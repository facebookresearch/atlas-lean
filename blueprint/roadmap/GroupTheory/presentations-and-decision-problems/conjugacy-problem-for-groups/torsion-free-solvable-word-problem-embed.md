# Kourovka Problem 5.21 (solvable word problem versus solvable conjugacy problem)

The proposition that every finitely generated torsion-free group with solvable word problem embeds in a finitely generated group with solvable conjugacy problem. Finitely generated groups are written $F_n / M$ for a normal subgroup $M$ of a free group of finite rank; the word problem is solvable when membership of words in $M$ is decidable, and the conjugacy problem for $F_m / N$ is solvable when the set of pairs of words $(w, v)$ with $u w u^{-1} v^{-1} \in N$ for some $u$ is decidable. Torsion-free: no non-trivial element of finite order.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.21 (D. J. Collins).
