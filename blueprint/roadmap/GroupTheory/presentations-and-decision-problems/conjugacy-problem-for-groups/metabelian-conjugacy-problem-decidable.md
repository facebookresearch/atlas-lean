# Kourovka Problem 4.32 (the conjugacy problem for metabelian groups)

The proposition that for every $n$ and every finite set $R$ of elements of $F_n$, conjugacy in the metabelian group $F_n / \langle\langle R \cup F_n''\rangle\rangle$ is decidable: the set of pairs of words $(w_1, w_2)$ such that $g w_1 g^{-1} w_2^{-1}$ lies in the kernel for some $g \in F_n$ is computable. Every finitely generated metabelian group has such a presentation (P. Hall).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.32 (M. I. Kargapolov).
