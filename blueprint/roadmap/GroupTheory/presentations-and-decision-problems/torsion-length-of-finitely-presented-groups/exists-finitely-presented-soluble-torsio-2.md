# Finitely presented soluble groups of torsion length greater than two

There is a finitely presented soluble group $G$ of torsion length greater than $2$. Here the torsion
series is $\mathrm{Tor}_0(G) = 1$, with $\mathrm{Tor}_{i+1}(G)$ the normal closure of the elements
having a positive power in $\mathrm{Tor}_i(G)$, and torsion length greater than $2$ means that
$G/\mathrm{Tor}_2(G)$ is not torsion-free.

This answers Kourovka Problem 19.23 (M. Chiodo, R. Vyas): the Kourovka Archive answers "Yes, such
groups exist"; Leary and Minasyan (Theorem 2.14) construct polycyclic groups, which are finitely
presented and soluble, of every torsion length $n \ge 1$, in particular of torsion length $3$.
