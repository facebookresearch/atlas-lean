# The Andrews–Curtis group $AC_2$ is not finitely presented (Kourovka 18.89)

The Andrews–Curtis
group $AC_2$, the subgroup of the permutation group of the set $F_2^2$ of pairs of elements of the
free group $F_2$ generated by the elementary Andrews–Curtis moves, is not finitely presented.

The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) records this, citing Krstić–McCool (1997) and Roman'kov (2023),
who also shows that the group of moves on all relator tuples is isomorphic to the Andrews–Curtis
group of $F_2$.
