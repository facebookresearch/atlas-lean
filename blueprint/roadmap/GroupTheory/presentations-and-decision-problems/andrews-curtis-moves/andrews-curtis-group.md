# The Andrews–Curtis group $AC_n$

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ let $F_n$ be the free group on $n$
generators. The Andrews–Curtis group $AC_n$ is the subgroup of the group of permutations of the set
$F_n^n$ of $n$-tuples $(u_1, \dots, u_n)$ of elements of $F_n$ generated by the elementary
Andrews–Curtis moves that are permutations: $u_i \mapsto u_i u_j^{\pm 1}$ and
$u_i \mapsto u_j^{\pm 1} u_i$ for indices $i \ne j$, $u_i \mapsto u_i^{-1}$, and
$u_i \mapsto w u_i w^{-1}$ for $w \in F_n$, all other entries unchanged. Viewing a tuple as the
relators of a balanced presentation $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid u_1, \dots, u_n \rangle$, these
are the Nielsen transformations of the relator tuple together with conjugations of relators.
