# Johnson's two-generator groups

For a natural number $n$, the group $\langle a, b \mid a^n = 1,\ ab = b^3a^3 \rangle$: the quotient of the free group on $a, b$ by the normal closure of $a^n$ and $ab\,(b^3a^3)^{-1}$. For $n = 0$ the relator $a^0$ is trivial.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.10 (D. L. Johnson).
