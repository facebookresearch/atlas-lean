# A group of type F-infinity containing every finitely presented group

There is one group $H$ of type $F_\infty$ such that every finitely presented group $G$ in the same universe admits an injective group homomorphism to $H$. Type $F_\infty$ here means having the fundamental group of a connected Hausdorff CW complex with finitely many cells in each degree and a surjective covering by a contractible space. The complex may have arbitrarily high-dimensional cells. The universal group is chosen before $G$, and no word-problem hypothesis on $G$ or simplicity conclusion for $H$ is included.

## Depends on

- Finite presentations and connected Hausdorff classifying complexes with finitely many cells in each dimension.

## Proof depends on

- A fixed universal construction that preserves embeddings of arbitrary finitely presented groups and admits a finite-type classifying complex.

## Expansion

Keep the diagonal enumeration or universal presentation separate from its classifying-space argument. Prove the embedding uniformity without assuming decidable word problems.
