# Kourovka Problem 1.13 (replacing a relator of the trivial group by a primitive element)

The proposition that whenever $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid r_1, \dots, r_m \rangle$ with $n \ge 1$ presents the trivial group, some relator $r_i$ can be replaced by a primitive element $w$ of $F_n$ so that the resulting presentation still presents the trivial group. An element of the free group $F_n$ is primitive if it belongs to some free basis of $F_n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.13 (J. Stallings; M. D. Greendlinger).
