# Free products of three cyclic groups

For natural numbers $p, q, r$, the group $C_p * C_q * C_r = \langle a, b, c \mid a^p, b^q, c^r \rangle$: the quotient of the free group on $a, b, c$ by the normal closure of $a^p$, $b^q$, $c^r$, which is the free product of cyclic groups of orders $p$, $q$, $r$ (infinite cyclic factors when an exponent is $0$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.53 (J. Wiegold, after P. Scott).
