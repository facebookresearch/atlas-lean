# Kourovka Problem 7.7 (finiteness of Burns's group)

The proposition that the group $\langle a, b \mid a^9 = 1,\ ab = b^2a^2 \rangle$ is finite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.7 (R. G. Burns).
