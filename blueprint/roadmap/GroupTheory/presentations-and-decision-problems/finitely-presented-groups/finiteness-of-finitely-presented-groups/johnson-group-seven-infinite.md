# Kourovka Problem 8.10 for n = 7

The proposition that the group $\langle a, b \mid a^{7} = 1,\ ab = b^3a^3 \rangle$ is infinite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.10 (D. L. Johnson).
