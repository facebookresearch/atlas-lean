# Burns's group is infinite

The group $\langle a, b \mid a^9 = 1,\ ab = b^2a^2 \rangle$ is infinite. It contains the Fibonacci group $F(2, 9)$, which Newman proved infinite. This answers Kourovka Problem 7.7 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 7.7 (finiteness of Burns's group)](burns-group-finite.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.7 (R. G. Burns); resolution: M. F. Newman, Proving a group infinite, Arch. Math. 54 (1990), 209-211.
