# Countable abelian groups embed in centres of finitely presented groups

For every countable abelian group $A$ there are a finitely presented group $G$ and an injective homomorphism $A \to G$ with image in the centre $Z(G)$; Kourovka Problem 5.47 has a positive answer. Ould Houcine proves more: a single finitely presented group with soluble word problem contains a copy of every countable abelian group in its centre.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 5.47 (V. N. Remeslennikov; marked solved in the main list); resolution: A. Ould Houcine, Embeddings in finitely presented groups which preserve the center, J. Algebra 307 (2007), no. 1, 1-23, Corollary 3, http://math.univ-lyon1.fr/~ould/Fiches-papiers/embeddings.pdf.
