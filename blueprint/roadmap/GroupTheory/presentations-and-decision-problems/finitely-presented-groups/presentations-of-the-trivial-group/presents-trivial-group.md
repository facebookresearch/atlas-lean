# Presentations of the trivial group

A finite list of relators $r_1, \dots, r_m$ in the free group $F_n$ presents the trivial group if the group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid r_1, \dots, r_m \rangle$ has at most one element.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.13 (J. Stallings; M. D. Greendlinger).
