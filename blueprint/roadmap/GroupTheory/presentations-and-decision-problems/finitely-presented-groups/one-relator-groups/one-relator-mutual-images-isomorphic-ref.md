# Non-isomorphic one-relator groups that are images of each other

There are one-relator groups $G = \langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r \rangle$ and $H = \langle y_1, \dots, y_n \mid s \rangle$ with surjective homomorphisms $G \to H$ and $H \to G$ but $G \not\cong H$. This answers Kourovka Problem 3.33 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.33 (one-relator groups that are images of each other)](one-relator-mutual-images-isomorphic.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.33 (D. I. Moldavanskiĭ); resolution: A. V. Borshchev and D. I. Moldavanskiĭ, Math. Notes 79 (2006), no. 1, 31-40.
