# Kourovka Problem 4.10 (torsion-free one-relator groups are locally indicable)

The proposition that for every $n$ and every $r$ in the free group $F_n$, if the one-relator group $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid r \rangle = F_n/\langle\langle r \rangle\rangle$ is torsion-free then it is locally indicable. A group is locally indicable if every nontrivial finitely generated subgroup admits a surjective homomorphism onto $\mathbb{Z}$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.10 (G. Baumslag).
