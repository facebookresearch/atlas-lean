# Kourovka Problem 3.33 (one-relator groups that are images of each other)

The proposition that for all one-relator groups $G = \langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r \rangle$ and $H = \langle y_1, \dots, y_n \mid s \rangle$ (finitely many generators, one relator each), if there are surjective homomorphisms $G \to H$ and $H \to G$, then $G \cong H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.33 (D. I. Moldavanskiĭ).
