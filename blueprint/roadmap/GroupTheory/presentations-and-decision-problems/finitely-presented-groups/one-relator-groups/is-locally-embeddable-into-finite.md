# Groups locally embeddable into finite groups

A group $G$ is locally embeddable into finite groups ($U$-embeddable in the class of finite groups) if for every finite subset $M \subseteq G$ there are a finite group $A$ and a map $f : G \to A$ that is injective on $M$ and satisfies $f(xy) = f(x)f(y)$ whenever $x, y, xy \in M$. This is equivalent to Kargapolov's formulation by finite submodels.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.15 (M. I. Kargapolov).
