# Two-generator one-relator groups with torsion are residually finite

Let $r$ be an element of the free group on $a, b$ whose reduced word is cyclically reduced and which is not a proper power. If $\langle a, b \mid r \rangle$ is residually finite, then $\langle a, b \mid r^t \rangle$ is residually finite for every $t > 1$. This answers Kourovka Problem 8.68 positively; in fact one-relator groups with torsion are virtually compact special and hence residually finite, by Wise's work.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.68 (C. Y. Tang); resolution: D. T. Wise, The structure of groups with a quasiconvex hierarchy, Annals of Mathematics Studies 209, Princeton University Press, 2021.
