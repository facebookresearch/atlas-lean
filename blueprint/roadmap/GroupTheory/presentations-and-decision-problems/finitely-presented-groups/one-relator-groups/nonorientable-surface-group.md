# Non-orientable closed surface groups

For a natural number $k$, the group $\langle c_1, \dots, c_k \mid c_1^2 \cdots c_k^2 \rangle$, the fundamental group of the closed non-orientable surface of genus $k$ when $k \ge 1$. For $k = 0$ the relator is the empty product and the group is trivial.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.36 (O. V. Mel'nikov).
