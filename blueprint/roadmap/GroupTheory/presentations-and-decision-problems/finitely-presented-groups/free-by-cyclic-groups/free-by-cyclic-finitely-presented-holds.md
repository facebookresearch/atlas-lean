# Finitely generated free-by-cyclic groups are finitely presented and coherent

Let $G$ be a finitely generated group with a free normal subgroup $N$ (of any rank) such that $G/N$ is cyclic. Then $G$ is finitely presented, and every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is finitely presented. This answers Kourovka Problem 4.8 positively, in the stronger form recorded in the Archive.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.8 (G. Baumslag); resolution: M. Feighn and M. Handel, Mapping tori of free group automorphisms are coherent, Ann. of Math. 149 (1999), 1061-1077, arXiv:math/9905209 (infinite cyclic quotients; finite cyclic quotients by an easier argument).
