# Kourovka Problem 4.8 (finitely generated free-by-cyclic groups)

The proposition that every finitely generated group $G$ with a normal subgroup $N$ that is free (of any rank) and has cyclic quotient $G/N$ (finite or infinite) is finitely presented, and moreover every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is finitely presented.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.8 (G. Baumslag).
