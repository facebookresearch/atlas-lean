# Presentations counted by Arzhantseva and Olshanskii

For natural numbers $m$, $n$ and $d$,
this is the set of $n$-tuples $(r_1, \dots, r_n)$ of elements of the free group $F_m$ on
generators $x_1, \dots, x_m$ such that the reduced word of each $r_i$ is cyclically reduced (the
empty word counts as cyclically reduced) and the reduced word lengths satisfy
$|r_1| + \dots + |r_n| = d$. Each tuple stands for the presentation
$\langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r_1, \dots, r_n \rangle$.
