# Scott–Wiegold theorem on free products of three cyclic groups

For distinct primes $p, q, r$, the free product $C_p * C_q * C_r = \langle a, b, c \mid a^p, b^q, c^r \rangle$ is not the normal closure of any single element. This is the Scott–Wiegold conjecture, proved by Howie, answering Kourovka Problem 5.53.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.53 (J. Wiegold, after P. Scott); resolution: J. Howie, A proof of the Scott-Wiegold conjecture on free products of cyclic groups, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 173 (2002), no. 2, 167-176.
