# Kourovka Problem 5.53 (Scott–Wiegold conjecture)

The proposition that for distinct primes $p, q, r$, no single element of $C_p * C_q * C_r = \langle a, b, c \mid a^p, b^q, c^r \rangle$ has normal closure equal to the whole group.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.53 (J. Wiegold, after P. Scott).
