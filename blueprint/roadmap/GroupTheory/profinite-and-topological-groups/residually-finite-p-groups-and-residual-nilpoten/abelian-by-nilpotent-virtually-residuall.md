# Kourovka Problem 4.52 (abelian-by-nilpotent groups and residually finite p-groups)

The proposition that every finitely generated torsion-free group $G$ that has a normal abelian subgroup $A$ with $G/A$ nilpotent is virtually residually a finite $p$-group for almost all primes $p$: the set of primes $p$ for which no subgroup of finite index in $G$ is residually a finite $p$-group is finite.

A group $H$ is residually a finite $p$-group if every element $h \ne 1$ of $H$ lies outside some normal subgroup of $H$ whose index is $p^k$ for some $k \ge 0$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.52 (V. N. Remeslennikov).
