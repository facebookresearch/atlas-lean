# Abelian-by-nilpotent groups are virtually residually p for almost all p

Let $G$ be a finitely generated torsion-free group with a normal abelian subgroup $A$ such that $G/A$ is nilpotent. Then for all but finitely many primes $p$, some subgroup of finite index in $G$ is residually a finite $p$-group: each of its non-identity elements lies outside some normal subgroup of $p$-power index. This answers Kourovka Problem 4.52 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.52 (V. N. Remeslennikov); resolution: G. A. Noskov, Almost approximability of finitely generated AB-groups without torsion with finite p-groups, Algebra and Logic 13 (1974), 388-393; D. Segal, A residual property of finitely generated abelian-by-nilpotent groups, J. Algebra 32 (1974), 389-399.
