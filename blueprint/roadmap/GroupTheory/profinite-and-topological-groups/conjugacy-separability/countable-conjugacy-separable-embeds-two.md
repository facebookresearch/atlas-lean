# Kourovka Problem 8.71 (embedding into 2-generator conjugacy separable groups)

The proposition that every countable conjugacy separable group embeds (by an injective homomorphism) into a conjugacy separable group generated by two elements. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements $x, y \in G$ whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.71 (C. Y. Tang).
