# Kourovka Problem 2.17 (conjugacy separability of wreath products)

The proposition that for all conjugacy separable groups $A$ and $B$, the restricted wreath product $A \wr B$ is conjugacy separable if and only if $A$ is abelian or $B$ is finite. The (restricted) wreath product $A \wr B$ is the subgroup of $A^B \rtimes B$ generated by the elements whose function part $B \to A$ has finite support. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.17 (M. I. Kargapolov).
