# Conjugacy separability of wreath products is not characterized by A abelian or B finite

There are conjugacy separable groups $A$ and $B$ for which "$A \wr B$ is conjugacy separable" is not equivalent to "$A$ is abelian or $B$ is finite". The (restricted) wreath product $A \wr B$ is the subgroup of $A^B \rtimes B$ generated by the elements whose function part $B \to A$ has finite support. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$. This answers Kourovka Problem 2.17 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.17 (conjugacy separability of wreath products)](wreath-conjugacy-separable-iff.md).
