# Kourovka Problem 9.2 (conjugacy separability of triangle groups)

The proposition that for all positive integers $l, m, n$ the group $\langle a, b \mid a^l = b^m = (ab)^n = 1 \rangle$ is conjugacy separable. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.2 (R. B. J. T. Allenby).
