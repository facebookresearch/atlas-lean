# Triangle groups

For natural numbers $l, m, n$, the group $\langle a, b \mid a^l = b^m = (ab)^n = 1 \rangle$: the quotient of the free group on $a, b$ by the normal closure of $a^l$, $b^m$ and $(ab)^n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.2 (R. B. J. T. Allenby).
