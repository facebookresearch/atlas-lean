# Countable conjugacy separable groups embed in 2-generator ones

Every countable conjugacy separable group $G$ admits an injective homomorphism into a conjugacy separable group generated by two elements. A group $G$ is conjugacy separable if any two elements $x, y \in G$ whose images are conjugate in every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup $N$ of finite index are conjugate in $G$. This answers Kourovka Problem 8.71 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.71 (C. Y. Tang); resolution: V. A. Roman'kov, Embedding theorems for residually finite groups, Preprint 84-515, Computing Centre, Novosibirsk, 1984 (Russian).
