# Finite-index subgroups of units of a finitely generated domain of positive characteristic are congruence

For every integral domain $R$ that is finitely generated as a ring and has positive characteristic, every subgroup $H$ of finite index in the unit group $R^\times$ contains the congruence subgroup $\{u \in R^\times : u \equiv 1 \pmod I\}$ of some ideal $I$ with $R/I$ finite; equivalently, the ideal (profinite) topology of $R$ induces the profinite topology on $R^\times$. Kourovka Problem 5.6 has a positive answer.
