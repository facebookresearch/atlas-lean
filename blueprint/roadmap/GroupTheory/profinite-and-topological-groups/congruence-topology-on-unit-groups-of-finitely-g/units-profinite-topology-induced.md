# Kourovka Problem 5.6 (units of finitely generated domains of positive characteristic)

This is the proposition that for every integral domain $R$ that is finitely generated as a ring and has positive characteristic, every subgroup $H$ of finite index in the unit group $R^\times$ contains the congruence subgroup $\{u \in R^\times : u \equiv 1 \pmod I\}$ of some ideal $I$ with $R/I$ finite; equivalently, the ideal (profinite) topology of $R$ induces the profinite topology on $R^\times$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.6 (B. A. F. Wehrfritz).
