# Large group

A group is large if it has a subgroup of finite index that maps onto a non-abelian free group.

A group $\Gamma$ is large if there are a subgroup $K \le \Gamma$ of finite index and a surjective homomorphism from $K$ onto a free group of rank at least $2$. Equivalently, since a free group of rank $2$ contains free subgroups of every finite rank as finite-index subgroups, some finite-index subgroup surjects onto the free group of rank $2$.

## Expansion

A large group has infinite virtual first Betti number: if $K$ surjects onto a free group of rank $r \ge 2$, then the finite-index subgroups of $K$ surject onto free groups of arbitrarily large rank, so their abelianizations have arbitrarily large rank. Largeness passes to finite-index overgroups and subgroups.
