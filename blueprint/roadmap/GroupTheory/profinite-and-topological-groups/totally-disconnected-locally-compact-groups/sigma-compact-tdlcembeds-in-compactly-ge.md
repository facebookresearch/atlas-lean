# Leary–Castellano question on embedding σ-compact TDLC groups

The proposition that every $\sigma$-compact TDLC group $G$ admits a continuous injective homomorphism into some compactly generated TDLC group. A TDLC group is a Hausdorff, totally disconnected, locally compact topological group; it is compactly generated if it is generated as a group by a compact subset.

Source: Open problems on TDLC-groups (I. Leary and I. Castellano), in Mini-Workshop: Homological aspects of TDLC-groups, Oberwolfach Reports 20 (2023), no. 55, p. 3135, Question 5, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2023/55.
