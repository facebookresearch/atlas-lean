# The set of compact elements of a totally disconnected locally compact group is closed

In every totally disconnected locally compact Hausdorff topological group $G$, the set of elements $g$ for which the closure of $\langle g \rangle$ is compact is closed. This answers Kourovka Problem 9.48 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.48 (I. V. Protasov); resolution: G. A. Willis, The structure of totally disconnected, locally compact groups, Math. Ann. 300 (1994), 341-363; G. A. Willis, Totally disconnected groups and proofs of conjectures of Hofmann and Mukherjea, Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 51 (1995), 489-494, Theorem 2.
