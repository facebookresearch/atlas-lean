# Kourovka Problem 9.48 (closedness of the set of compact elements)

The proposition that in every totally disconnected locally compact Hausdorff topological group $G$ the set of compact elements, the $g \in G$ for which the closure of the cyclic subgroup $\langle g \rangle$ is compact, is a closed subset of $G$. For locally compact groups, totally disconnected is the same as null-dimensional.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.48 (I. V. Protasov).
