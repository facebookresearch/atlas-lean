# Two elements generate a dense subgroup of a connected semisimple Lie group

Every connected
real analytic Lie group $G$ whose Lie algebra is semisimple contains two elements $a$ and $b$ such
that the subgroup of $G$ generated by $a$ and $b$ is dense in $G$. In particular, for connected
groups the number 4 in Problem 90 of the Scottish Book (four elements generating a dense
subgroup) can be lowered to 2.

## Sources

- S. Ulam and H. Auerbach, Problem 90 of the Scottish Book (every semi-simple Lie group contains four elements generating a dense subgroup; can the number 4 be lowered?); Mauldin commentary: every connected semi-simple Lie group has a free subgroup with two generators, and such a free subgroup can be chosen dense.
