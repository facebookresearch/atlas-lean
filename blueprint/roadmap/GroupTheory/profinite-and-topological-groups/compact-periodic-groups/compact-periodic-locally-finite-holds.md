# Compact periodic groups are locally finite

Let $G$ be a compact Hausdorff topological group in which every element has finite order. Then every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is finite. This answers Kourovka Problem 3.41 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.41 (V. P. Platonov); resolution: E. I. Zel'manov, Israel J. Math. 77 (1992), 83-95.
