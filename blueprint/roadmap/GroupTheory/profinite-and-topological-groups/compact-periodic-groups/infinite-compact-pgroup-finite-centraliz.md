# Kourovka Problem 9.34 (infinite compact p-groups with finite centralizers)

The proposition that for some prime $p$ there is an infinite compact Hausdorff topological group $G$ that is a $p$-group (every element has order a power of $p$) in which the centralizer of every element $g \ne 1$ is finite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.34 (Yu. N. Mukhin) (after S. K. Grosser and W. N. Herfort).
