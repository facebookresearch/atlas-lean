# Kourovka Problem 3.41 (compact periodic groups are locally finite)

The proposition that every compact Hausdorff topological group $G$ in which every element has finite order is locally finite: every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is finite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.41 (V. P. Platonov).
