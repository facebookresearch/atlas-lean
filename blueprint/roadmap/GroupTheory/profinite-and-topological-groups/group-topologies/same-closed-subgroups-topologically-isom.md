# Kourovka Problem 3.35 (locally compact group topologies with the same closed subgroups)

The proposition that whenever two topologies $\sigma$ and $\tau$ on a group $G$ both make $G$ a Hausdorff locally compact topological group and have the same closed subgroups, there is a group automorphism $\varphi$ of $G$ that is a homeomorphism from $(G, \sigma)$ to $(G, \tau)$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.35 (K. Ross; Yu. N. Mukhin).
