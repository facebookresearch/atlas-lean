# Locally compact group topologies are not determined by their closed subgroups

There are a group $G$ and two topologies $\sigma$, $\tau$ on $G$, each making $G$ a Hausdorff locally compact topological group, with exactly the same closed subgroups, such that no automorphism of $G$ is a homeomorphism from $(G, \sigma)$ to $(G, \tau)$. This answers Kourovka Problem 3.35 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.35 (locally compact group topologies with the same closed subgroups)](same-closed-subgroups-topologically-isom.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.35 (K. Ross; Yu. N. Mukhin); resolution: A. I. Moskalenko, Ukrain. Math. J. 30 (1978), 199-201.
