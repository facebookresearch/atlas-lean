# Kourovka Problem 3.58 (complements in compact groups with elementary Sylow subgroups)

The proposition that in every compact Hausdorff totally disconnected topological group $G$ all of whose closed pro-$p$ subgroups are abelian with $x^p = 1$ for every $x$ (for every prime $p$), every closed normal subgroup $N$ has a closed complement: a closed subgroup $K$ with $N \cap K = 1$ and $NK = G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.58 (V. S. Charin).
