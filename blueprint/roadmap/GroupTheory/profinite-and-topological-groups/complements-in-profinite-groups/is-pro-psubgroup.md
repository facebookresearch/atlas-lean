# Pro-p subgroups of a topological group

A subgroup $P$ of a topological group $G$ is pro-$p$ when for every open normal subgroup $N$ of $G$ the index $|P : P \cap N|$ is $p^k$ for some $k \ge 0$, an infinite index counting as $0$. For a prime $p$ the index is then finite, and the condition says that the image of $P$ in every quotient $G/N$ by an open normal subgroup is a finite $p$-group.
