# Pro-p subgroups of a topological group

A subgroup $P$ of a topological group $G$ is pro-$p$ when for every open normal subgroup $N$ of $G$ the index $|P : P \cap N|$ is a power of $p$ (in particular finite), i.e. the image of $P$ in every quotient $G/N$ by an open normal subgroup is a finite $p$-group.
