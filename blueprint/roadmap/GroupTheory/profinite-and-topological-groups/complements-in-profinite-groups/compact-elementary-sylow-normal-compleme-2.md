# Closed normal subgroups of profinite groups with elementary Sylow subgroups are complemented

Let $G$ be a compact $0$-dimensional (Hausdorff, totally disconnected) group whose Sylow $p$-subgroups are direct products of cyclic groups of order $p$, expressed as: every closed pro-$p$ subgroup is abelian with $x^p = 1$ for every $x$. Then every closed normal subgroup $N$ of $G$ has a closed complement $K$ ($N \cap K = 1$, $NK = G$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.58 (V. S. Charin); resolution: M. I. Kabenyuk, On the complementability of subgroups in compact groups, Siberian Math. J. 13 (1972), 654-657, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00971059.
