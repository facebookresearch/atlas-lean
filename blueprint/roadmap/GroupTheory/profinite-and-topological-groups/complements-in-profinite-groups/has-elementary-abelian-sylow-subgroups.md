# Profinite groups with elementary abelian Sylow subgroups

A topological group $G$ has elementary abelian Sylow subgroups when for every prime $p$ every closed pro-$p$ subgroup of $G$ is abelian of exponent $p$. For a compact group, where every closed pro-$p$ subgroup lies in a Sylow $p$-subgroup, this says that the Sylow $p$-subgroups are topological direct products of cyclic groups of order $p$.
