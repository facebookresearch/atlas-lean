# Profinite groups with elementary abelian Sylow subgroups

A topological group $G$ has elementary abelian Sylow subgroups when for every prime $p$ every closed subgroup of $G$ satisfying the [pro-$p$ index condition](is-pro-psubgroup.md) is abelian with $x^p = 1$ for every $x$ (the trivial subgroup qualifies). For a profinite group, where these are the closed pro-$p$ subgroups and each lies in a Sylow $p$-subgroup, this says that the Sylow $p$-subgroups are topological direct products of cyclic groups of order $p$.
