# Kourovka Problem 6.30 (Hopficity of profinite completions)

The proposition that for every residually finite Hopfian group $G$, the profinite completion $\widehat G$ is topologically Hopfian. A group is Hopfian if every surjective endomorphism is injective; the profinite completion $\widehat G$ (the inverse limit of the finite quotients of $G$) is topologically Hopfian if every continuous surjective endomorphism of $\widehat G$ is injective.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 6.30 (O. V. Mel'nikov).
