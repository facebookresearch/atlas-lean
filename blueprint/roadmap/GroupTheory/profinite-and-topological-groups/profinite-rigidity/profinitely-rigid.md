# Profinitely rigid group

A group $\Gamma$ is *profinitely rigid* if it is finitely
generated and residually finite, and $\Gamma$ is isomorphic to every finitely generated residually
finite group $\Lambda$ whose profinite completion is isomorphic, as a group, to the profinite
completion of $\Gamma$.

Note that any such isomorphism of profinite completions is automatically a homeomorphism. This is
a consequence of the Nikolov-Segal theorem which states that for a finitely generated group
$\Gamma$, every finite index subgroup of the profinite completion of $\Gamma$ is automatically
open. Conversely, every open subgroup has finite index by compactness, so in this case the open
subgroups (which generate the topology) are exactly the subgroups of finite index. Thus, the
topology on the profinite completion of $\Gamma$ can be characterized algebraically as the
topology generated by the finite index subgroups. In particular, any isomorphism between
profinite completions of finitely generated groups automatically preserves the topology and is
thus a homeomorphism.
