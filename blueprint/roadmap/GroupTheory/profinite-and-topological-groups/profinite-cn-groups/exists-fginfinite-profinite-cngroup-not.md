# Problem of a finitely generated non-prosoluble profinite CN-group (Kourovka 20.90)

The
statement that there is an infinite profinite group $G$, that is, a compact, Hausdorff, totally
disconnected topological group, such that $G$ is topologically finitely generated (the closure of
the subgroup generated by some finite set is $G$), the centralizer of every non-trivial element of
$G$ is pronilpotent (so $G$ is a CN-group), and $G$ is not prosoluble: for some open normal
subgroup $N$ of $G$, the quotient $G/N$ is not soluble.
