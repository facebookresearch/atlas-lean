# Subgroup separability of soluble groups without Baumslag–Solitar subgroups (Kourovka 16.2)

The statement that a finitely generated solvable group $G$ is locally subgroup-separable if and only
if there is no $n > 1$ such that $G$ has a subgroup isomorphic to the Baumslag–Solitar group
$\mathrm{BS}(1, n)$.
