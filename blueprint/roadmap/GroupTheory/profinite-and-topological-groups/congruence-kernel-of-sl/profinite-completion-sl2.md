# Profinite completion of SL₂(ℤ)

The profinite completion $\widehat{\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})}$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$: the inverse limit of the finite quotients $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})/N$ over the normal subgroups $N$ of finite index, as a profinite group.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.40 (I. R. Shafarevich; V. P. Platonov).
