# Principal congruence subgroups of SL₂(ℤ)

For a natural number $n$, the principal congruence subgroup $\Gamma(n + 1)$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, the kernel of reduction modulo $n + 1$, regarded as a normal subgroup of finite index. The index is shifted, so $n = 0$ gives $\Gamma(1) = \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.40 (I. R. Shafarevich; V. P. Platonov).
