# Congruence kernel of SL₂(ℤ)

The congruence kernel of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is the subgroup of the profinite completion $\widehat{\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})}$ consisting of the elements whose image under the limit projection to $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})/\Gamma(N)$ is trivial for every $N \ge 1$, where $\Gamma(N)$ is the principal congruence subgroup of level $N$. Since the principal congruence subgroups are cofinal among the congruence subgroups, it is the kernel of the natural homomorphism from the profinite completion onto the congruence completion.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.40 (I. R. Shafarevich; V. P. Platonov).
