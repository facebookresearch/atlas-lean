# Segal's theorem on upper composition factors, 3 generators (Kourovka Problem 11.57)

For every
family $(T_i)_{i \in I}$ of finite non-abelian simple groups there is a group $G$ generated by $3$
elements such that a finite non-abelian simple group $Q$ is an upper composition factor of $G$ (a
composition factor of some finite quotient of $G$) if and only if $Q$ is isomorphic to $T_i$ for
some $i \in I$. Abelian upper composition factors of $G$ are not restricted.
