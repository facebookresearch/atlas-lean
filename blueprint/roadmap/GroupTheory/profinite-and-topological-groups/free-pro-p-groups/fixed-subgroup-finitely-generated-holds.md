# Fixed subgroup of a free pro-p group is finitely generated

Let $p$ be a prime and let $F$ be a free pro-$p$ group of finite rank. If $\Delta \ne 1$ is a
continuous automorphism of $F$ whose order is a power of $p$, then the fixed subgroup
$\mathrm{Fix}_F(\Delta) = \{z \in F : \Delta(z) = z\}$ is topologically finitely generated: it is
the closure of the subgroup generated by finitely many of its elements.

This answers Kourovka Problem 12.70 (L. Ribes): the Kourovka Archive answers "Yes, it is finite" (C.
Scheiderer, 1999); moreover, the fixed points of a finite $p$-group of automorphisms of a free
pro-$p$ group form a free factor (Herfort, Ribes and Zalesskii, 1999), which has finite rank when
$F$ does.
