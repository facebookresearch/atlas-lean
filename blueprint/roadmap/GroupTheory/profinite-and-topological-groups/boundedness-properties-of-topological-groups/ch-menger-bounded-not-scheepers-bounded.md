# Tsaban Problem 10.2(1) (Menger-bounded groups that are not Scheepers-bounded)

The proposition that, if the Continuum Hypothesis $\mathfrak{c} = \aleph_1$ holds, there is a separable metrizable topological group that is Menger-bounded but not Scheepers-bounded. A topological group $G$ is Menger-bounded if for every sequence $(U_n)$ of neighbourhoods of $1$ there are finite sets $F_n \subseteq G$ with $G = \bigcup_n F_nU_n$, and Scheepers-bounded if for every such sequence there are finite $F_n$ such that every finite subset of $G$ lies in some $F_nU_n$.

Source: B. Tsaban, Selection principles and special sets of reals, in Open Problems in Topology II (E. Pearl, ed.), Elsevier 2007, 91-108; revised version arXiv:math/0609601, Problem 10.2, part (1) (M. Machura and B. Tsaban).
