# Menger-bounded topological groups

A group $G$ with a topology is Menger-bounded ($o$-bounded, $S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{nbd}}, \mathcal{O})$) if for every sequence $(U_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of neighbourhoods of $1$ there are finite sets $F_n \subseteq G$ with $G = \bigcup_n F_nU_n$, where $F_nU_n = \{fu : f \in F_n,\ u \in U_n\}$.

Source: B. Tsaban, Selection principles and special sets of reals, in Open Problems in Topology II (E. Pearl, ed.), Elsevier 2007, 91-108; revised version arXiv:math/0609601, Problem 10.2, part (1) (M. Machura and B. Tsaban).
