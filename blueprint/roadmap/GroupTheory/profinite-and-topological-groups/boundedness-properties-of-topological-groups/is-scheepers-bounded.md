# Scheepers-bounded topological groups

A group $G$ with a topology is Scheepers-bounded ($S_1(\Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}}, \Omega)$, in Kočinac's form) if for every sequence $(U_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of neighbourhoods of $1$ there are finite sets $F_n \subseteq G$ such that every finite subset of $G$ is contained in $F_nU_n$ for some $n$.

Source: B. Tsaban, Selection principles and special sets of reals, in Open Problems in Topology II (E. Pearl, ed.), Elsevier 2007, 91-108; revised version arXiv:math/0609601, Problem 10.2, part (1) (M. Machura and B. Tsaban).
