# Do amenable groups coincide with elementary amenable groups? (Kourovka 8.6)

The statement (Kourovka Problem 8.6, R. I. Grigorchuk, a question of M. M. Day) that a group is [amenable](../amenable-groups/is-amenable.md) if and only if it is [elementary amenable](is-elementary-amenable.md). Here a group is amenable if it satisfies the Følner condition, and the elementary amenable groups form the smallest class of groups that contains every finite group and every abelian group and is closed under isomorphisms, subgroups, quotients, extensions and directed unions.

Every elementary amenable group is amenable, so the statement asks whether every amenable group is elementary amenable. The answer is no: [an amenable group need not be elementary amenable](amenable-iff-elementary-amenable-refuted.md).
