# Amenable groups need not be elementary amenable

There is an [amenable](../amenable-groups/is-amenable.md) group that is not [elementary amenable](is-elementary-amenable.md), so the [statement of Kourovka Problem 8.6](amenable-iff-elementary-amenable.md), that the amenable groups are exactly the elementary amenable groups, is false.

The Kourovka Archive answers Problem 8.6 "No", citing Grigorchuk: his finitely generated groups of intermediate growth are amenable, since a finitely generated group of subexponential growth satisfies the Følner condition, but they are not elementary amenable, since a finitely generated elementary amenable group has either polynomial or exponential growth (Chou).

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.6 (R. I. Grigorchuk; question of M. M. Day): No.
- R. I. Grigorchuk, On the Milnor problem of group growth, Soviet Math. Dokl. 28 (1983), 23-26.
- C. Chou, Elementary amenable groups, Illinois J. Math. 24 (1980), 396-407.
