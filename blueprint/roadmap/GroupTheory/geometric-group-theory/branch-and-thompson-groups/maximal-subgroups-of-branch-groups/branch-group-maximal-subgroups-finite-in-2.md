# A finitely generated branch group with a maximal subgroup of infinite index

The
Kourovka 15.15 statement that every maximal subgroup of a finitely generated branch group has
finite index is false: some finitely generated branch group has a maximal subgroup of infinite
index.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 15.15 (L. Bartholdi, R. I. Grigorchuk, Z. Šunić); resolution: I. V. Bondarenko, Finite generation of iterated wreath products, Arch. Math. (Basel) 95 (2010), no. 4, 301-308, arXiv:1002.0320, abstract and the example after Corollary 3 (a finitely generated branch group with maximal subgroups of infinite index, answering Question 14 of Bartholdi, Grigorchuk and Šunić, Branch groups); Kourovka Archive, Problem 15.15 (answer: No, not necessarily), https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.0320.
