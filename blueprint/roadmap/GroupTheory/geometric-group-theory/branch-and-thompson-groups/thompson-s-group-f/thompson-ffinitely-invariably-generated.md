# AimPL amenability Problem 1.9 (invariable generation of Thompson's group F)

The proposition that Thompson's group $F$ is invariably generated by a finite set: there are $n$ and $x_1, \dots, x_n \in F$ such that for all $g_1, \dots, g_n \in F$ the conjugates $g_i^{-1}x_ig_i$ generate $F$. Thompson's group $F$ is given by the presentation $\langle A, B \mid [AB^{-1}, A^{-1}BA] = [AB^{-1}, A^{-2}BA^2] = 1 \rangle$ (Cannon–Floyd–Parry).

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 1.9, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/1/.
