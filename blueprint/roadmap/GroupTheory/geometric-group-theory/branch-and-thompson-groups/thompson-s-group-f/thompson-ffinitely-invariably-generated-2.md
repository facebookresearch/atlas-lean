# Thompson's group F is finitely invariably generated

Thompson's group $F$ is invariably generated by a finite set. A group is invariably generated by $x_1, \dots, x_n$ if for every choice of $g_1, \dots, g_n$ the conjugates $g_1^{-1}x_1g_1, \dots, g_n^{-1}x_ng_n$ generate it. Thompson's group $F$ is given by the presentation $\langle A, B \mid [AB^{-1}, A^{-1}BA] = [AB^{-1}, A^{-2}BA^2] = 1 \rangle$ (Cannon–Floyd–Parry). Gelander, Golan and Juschenko show that $\{x_0, x_1, x_0x_1\}$ invariably generates $F$, whereas Thompson's groups $T$ and $V$ are not invariably generated.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 1.9, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/1/; resolution: T. Gelander, G. Golan and K. Juschenko, Invariable generation of Thompson groups, J. Algebra 478 (2017), 261-270, arXiv:1611.08264, Theorem 1.
