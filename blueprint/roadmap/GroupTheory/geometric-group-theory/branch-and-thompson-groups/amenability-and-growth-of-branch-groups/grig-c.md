# Generator $c$ of the first Grigorchuk group

The generator $c = (a, d)$ of the first Grigorchuk group is the map of finite words over $\{0, 1\}$ that fixes the empty word, sends $0w$ to $0\,a(w)$ and sends $1w$ to $1\,d(w)$, where $a$ flips the first letter and $d$ is the generator $d = (1, b)$.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
