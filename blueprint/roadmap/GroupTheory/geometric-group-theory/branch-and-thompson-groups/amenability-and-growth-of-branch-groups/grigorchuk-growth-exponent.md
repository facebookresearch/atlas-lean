# AimPL amenability Problem 3.6 (growth exponent of the Grigorchuk group)

The proposition that for the positive real root $r_0$ of $X^3 - X^2 - 2X - 4$,

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\log \log |B(n)|}{\log n} = \frac{\log 2}{\log r_0},$$

where $B(n)$ is the ball of radius $n$ of the first Grigorchuk group for the generating set $\{a, b, c, d\}$ and $\log$ is the natural logarithm. The first Grigorchuk group is generated by the maps $a, b, c, d$ of the vertex set of the binary rooted tree (finite words over $\{0, 1\}$): $a$ flips the first letter, and $b = (a, c)$, $c = (a, d)$, $d = (1, b)$ fix the first letter and act on the rest of the word by the indicated maps after $0$ and after $1$ respectively.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
