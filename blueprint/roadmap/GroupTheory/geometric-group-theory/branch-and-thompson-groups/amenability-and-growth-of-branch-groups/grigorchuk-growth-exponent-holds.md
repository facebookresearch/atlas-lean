# Growth exponent of the first Grigorchuk group

Let $r_0$ be the positive real root of $X^3 - X^2 - 2X - 4$. Then the balls $B(n)$ of the first Grigorchuk group for the generating set $\{a, b, c, d\}$ satisfy

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\log \log |B(n)|}{\log n} = \frac{\log 2}{\log r_0} \approx 0.7674.$$

The first Grigorchuk group is generated by the maps $a, b, c, d$ of the vertex set of the binary rooted tree (finite words over $\{0, 1\}$): $a$ flips the first letter, and $b = (a, c)$, $c = (a, d)$, $d = (1, b)$ fix the first letter and act on the rest of the word by the indicated maps after $0$ and after $1$ respectively. Erschler and Zheng prove the matching lower bound to Bartholdi's upper bound; in particular the limit asked for in AimPL Problem 3.6 exists.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/; resolution: A. Erschler and T. Zheng, Growth of periodic Grigorchuk groups, Invent. Math. 219 (2020), 1069-1155, arXiv:1802.09077, Theorem A.
