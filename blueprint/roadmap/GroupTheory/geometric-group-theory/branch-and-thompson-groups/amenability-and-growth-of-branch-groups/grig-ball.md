# Balls in the first Grigorchuk group

For a natural number $n$, the ball of radius $n$ of the first Grigorchuk group is the set of maps of finite words over $\{0, 1\}$ that are composites of at most $n$ of the generators $a, b, c, d$ (the empty composite being the identity). Since the action on the tree is faithful, these are the group elements of word length at most $n$ for the generating set $\{a, b, c, d\}$. The first Grigorchuk group is generated by the maps $a, b, c, d$ of the vertex set of the binary rooted tree (finite words over $\{0, 1\}$): $a$ flips the first letter, and $b = (a, c)$, $c = (a, d)$, $d = (1, b)$ fix the first letter and act on the rest of the word by the indicated maps after $0$ and after $1$ respectively.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
