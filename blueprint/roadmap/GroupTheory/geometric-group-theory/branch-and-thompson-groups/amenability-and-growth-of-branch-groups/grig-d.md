# Generator $d$ of the first Grigorchuk group

The generator $d = (1, b)$ of the first Grigorchuk group is the map of finite words over $\{0, 1\}$ that fixes the empty word, sends $0w$ to $0w$ and sends $1w$ to $1\,b(w)$, where $b$ is the generator $b = (a, c)$.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
