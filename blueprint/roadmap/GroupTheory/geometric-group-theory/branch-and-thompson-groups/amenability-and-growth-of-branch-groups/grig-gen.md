# Standard generators of the first Grigorchuk group

The family indexed by $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ listing the four generators $a, b, c, d$ (in this order) of the first Grigorchuk group, as maps of finite words over $\{0, 1\}$. The first Grigorchuk group is generated by the maps $a, b, c, d$ of the vertex set of the binary rooted tree (finite words over $\{0, 1\}$): $a$ flips the first letter, and $b = (a, c)$, $c = (a, d)$, $d = (1, b)$ fix the first letter and act on the rest of the word by the indicated maps after $0$ and after $1$ respectively.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
