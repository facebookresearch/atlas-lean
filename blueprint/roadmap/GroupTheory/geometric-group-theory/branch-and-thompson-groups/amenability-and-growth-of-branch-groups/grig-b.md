# Generator $b$ of the first Grigorchuk group

The generator $b = (a, c)$ of the first Grigorchuk group is the map of finite words over $\{0, 1\}$ that fixes the empty word, sends $0w$ to $0\,a(w)$ and sends $1w$ to $1\,c(w)$, where $a$ flips the first letter and $c$ is the generator $c = (a, d)$.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
