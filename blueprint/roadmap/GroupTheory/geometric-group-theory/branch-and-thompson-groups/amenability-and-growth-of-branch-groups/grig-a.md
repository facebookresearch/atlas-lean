# Generator $a$ of the first Grigorchuk group

The generator $a$ of the first Grigorchuk group is the map of finite words over $\{0, 1\}$ (vertices of the binary rooted tree) that flips the first letter and leaves the empty word fixed.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 3.6, http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/3/.
