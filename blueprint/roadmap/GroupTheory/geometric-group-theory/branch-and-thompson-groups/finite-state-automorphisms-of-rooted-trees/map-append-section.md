# Section decomposition

For a tree automorphism $g$ and words $v$ and $w$, the image of the
concatenation $vw$ is $g(v)$ followed by the image of $w$ under the section of $g$ at $v$.

## Sources

- Ya. Lavrenyuk, *Minimal generating sets in wreath products*, arXiv:1210.0150v1 (2012), Section 3, p. 7 (for an automorphism $g$ of the regular rooted tree and a vertex $v$, the state of $g$ at $v$ is the automorphism $h$ of the tree with $g(vu) = g(v)h(u)$ for every word $u$).
