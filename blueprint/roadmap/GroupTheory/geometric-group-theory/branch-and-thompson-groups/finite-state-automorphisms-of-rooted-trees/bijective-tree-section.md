# Bijectivity of sections

For a tree automorphism $g$ and a vertex $v$, the section of $g$
at $v$ is a bijection of the set of all words.

## Sources

- Ya. Lavrenyuk, *Minimal generating sets in wreath products*, arXiv:1210.0150v1 (2012), Section 3, p. 7 (the state of an automorphism of the regular rooted tree at a vertex is again an automorphism of the tree).
