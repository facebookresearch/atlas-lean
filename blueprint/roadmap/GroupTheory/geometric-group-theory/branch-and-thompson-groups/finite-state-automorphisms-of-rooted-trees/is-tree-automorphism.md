# Automorphisms of the regular rooted tree

For a natural number $d$, the $d$-regular rooted
tree has as vertices the finite words over the alphabet $\{0, \dots, d-1\}$; write $vw$ for the
concatenation of words $v$ and $w$. The root is the empty word and the children of a word $v$ are
the words $vi$ for letters $i$. A permutation $g$ of the words is a tree automorphism when for all
words $v$ and $w$ there is a word $u$ of the same length as $w$ with $g(vw) = g(v)u$.

For a permutation of the words this says that $g$ preserves lengths and the prefix order, so it is
an automorphism of the tree as a graph.
