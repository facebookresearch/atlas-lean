# Membership in the finite-state automorphism group

A permutation of the finite words over
$\{0, \dots, d-1\}$ belongs to the group of finite-state automorphisms of the $d$-regular rooted
tree if and only if it preserves word lengths and prefixes and has finitely many distinct sections.

## Sources

- Ya. Lavrenyuk, *Minimal generating sets in wreath products*, arXiv:1210.0150v1 (2012), Section 3, p. 7 (the finite-state automorphisms of a regular rooted tree form a group).
