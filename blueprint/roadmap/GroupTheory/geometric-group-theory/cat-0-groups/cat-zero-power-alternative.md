# Bestvina Question 2.7 (power alternative for CAT(0) groups)

The proposition that whenever a group $G$ acts geometrically on a proper CAT(0) space, for all $a, b \in G$ there is $n > 0$ such that the subgroup $\langle a^n, b^n \rangle$ is free (of any rank, including trivial and cyclic) or abelian. A group acts geometrically on a metric space $X$ when it acts by isometries, properly discontinuously (for compact $K, L \subseteq X$ only finitely many $g$ have $gK \cap L \ne \emptyset$) and cocompactly (some compact $K$ has $X = \bigcup_g gK$). Here $X$ is a proper CAT(0) space: a nonempty proper geodesic metric space satisfying the four-point condition $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$ for all $x, y, z, w$, which characterizes CAT(0) among geodesic spaces (Berg–Nikolaev).

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 2.7 (D. Wise), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
