# CAT(0) groups whose direct powers have bounded rank

There is a nontrivial group $G$ acting geometrically on a proper CAT(0) space such that the direct powers $G^n$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, are all generated by at most $C$ elements for a single constant $C$. A group acts geometrically on a metric space $X$ when it acts by isometries, properly discontinuously (for compact $K, L \subseteq X$ only finitely many $g$ have $gK \cap L \ne \emptyset$) and cocompactly (some compact $K$ has $X = \bigcup_g gK$). Here $X$ is a proper CAT(0) space: a nonempty proper geodesic metric space satisfying the four-point condition $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$ for all $x, y, z, w$, which characterizes CAT(0) among geodesic spaces (Berg–Nikolaev). The Burger–Mozes groups are examples: they are infinite simple and act freely and cocompactly by isometries on a product of two trees, and their direct powers $\Gamma^n$ are generated by $d(\Gamma) + 1$ elements.
