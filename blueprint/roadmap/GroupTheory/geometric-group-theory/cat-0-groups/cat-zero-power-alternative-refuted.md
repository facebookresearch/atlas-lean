# The power alternative fails for CAT(0) groups

There are a group $G$ acting geometrically on a proper CAT(0) space and elements $a, b \in G$ such that for every $n > 0$ the subgroup $\langle a^n, b^n \rangle$ is neither free nor abelian. A group acts geometrically on a metric space $X$ when it acts by isometries, properly discontinuously (for compact $K, L \subseteq X$ only finitely many $g$ have $gK \cap L \ne \emptyset$) and cocompactly (some compact $K$ has $X = \bigcup_g gK$). Here $X$ is a proper CAT(0) space: a nonempty proper geodesic metric space satisfying the four-point condition $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$ for all $x, y, z, w$, which characterizes CAT(0) among geodesic spaces (Berg–Nikolaev). Leary and Minasyan's group $G_P$, acting geometrically on $\mathbb{E}^2 \times T$ for a regular tree $T$, with its elements $a, t$, is such an example. This answers Wise's question (Bestvina Q 2.7) negatively in the CAT(0) case. It is the negation of the proposition on [Bestvina Question 2.7 (power alternative for CAT(0) groups)](cat-zero-power-alternative.md).

Minasyan, Commensurating HNN extensions: nonpositive curvature and biautomaticity, Geom. Topol. 25 (2021), 1819-1860, arXiv:1907.03515, Corollary 9.6.
