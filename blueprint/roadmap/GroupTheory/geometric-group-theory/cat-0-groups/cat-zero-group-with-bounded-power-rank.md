# Bestvina Question 1.17 (CAT(0) groups whose direct powers have bounded rank)

The proposition that some nontrivial group $G$ acting geometrically on a proper CAT(0) space has a constant $C$ such that for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the direct power $G^n$ is generated by at most $C$ elements. A group acts geometrically on a metric space $X$ when it acts by isometries, properly discontinuously (for compact $K, L \subseteq X$ only finitely many $g$ have $gK \cap L \ne \emptyset$) and cocompactly (some compact $K$ has $X = \bigcup_g gK$). Here $X$ is a proper CAT(0) space: a nonempty proper geodesic metric space satisfying the four-point condition $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$ for all $x, y, z, w$, which characterizes CAT(0) among geodesic spaces (Berg–Nikolaev). Since the rank of $G^n$ is non-decreasing in $n$, this says that the conjecture $\operatorname{rank}(G^n) \to \infty$ fails for some CAT(0) group.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 1.17 (Dani Wise), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
