# Limit groups are CAT(0)

Every limit group acts properly discontinuously, cocompactly and by isometries on a proper CAT(0) space. A limit group is a finitely generated group $G$ such that for every finite subset $W \subseteq G$ there is a homomorphism from $G$ to some free group that is injective on $W$. A group acts geometrically on a metric space $X$ when it acts by isometries, properly discontinuously (for compact $K, L \subseteq X$ only finitely many $g$ have $gK \cap L \ne \emptyset$) and cocompactly (some compact $K$ has $X = \bigcup_g gK$). A proper CAT(0) space is a nonempty proper geodesic metric space satisfying $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$ for all $x, y, z, w$ (the Berg–Nikolaev four-point condition, which characterizes CAT(0) among geodesic spaces). Alibegović and Bestvina even obtain a CAT(0) space with isolated flats. This proves Sela's conjecture (Bestvina Q 3.5).

Bestvina, Limit groups are CAT(0), J. London Math. Soc. (2) 74 (2006), no. 1, 259-272, DOI 10.1112/S0024610706023155, arXiv:math/0410198, Theorem 4.9.
