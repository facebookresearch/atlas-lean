# Limit groups

A group $G$ is a limit group ($\omega$-residually free) if it is finitely generated and for every finite subset $W \subseteq G$ there are a set $X$ and a homomorphism $\varphi : G \to F(X)$ to the free group on $X$ that is injective on $W$.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 3.5 (Z. Sela), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
