# Word distance of a finite subset of a group

For a group $G$, a finite subset $S \subseteq G$ and $g, h \in G$, $d_S(g, h)$ is the graph distance between $g$ and $h$ in the Cayley graph of $S$, the simple graph on $G$ in which distinct $u, v$ are adjacent when $us = v$ or $vs = u$ for some $s \in S$, viewed as a real number. When $S$ generates $G$ it is the length of a shortest word in $S \cup S^{-1}$ representing $g^{-1}h$; when $h$ cannot be reached from $g$ the distance is $0$ by convention.

Source: G. N. Arzhantseva, Uniform embeddings of groups into a Hilbert space, in Manifold Perspectives, Oberwolfach Reports 6 (2009), no. 27, pp. 1527-1529, open problem (ii), p. 1529, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2009/27.
