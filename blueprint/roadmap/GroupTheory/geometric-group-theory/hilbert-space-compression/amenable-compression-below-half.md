# Arzhantseva's question on amenable groups of compression below 1/2

The proposition that there are a group $G$, a finite generating set $S$ of $G$ and a real number $\alpha < 1/2$ such that $G$ is amenable and $\alpha$ is not attained, where an exponent $\alpha$ is attained if there are a separable real Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$, a map $f : G \to \mathcal{H}$ with $\lVert f(g) - f(h) \rVert \le d_S(g, h)$ for all $g, h$, and constants $C > 0$ and $D \in \mathbb{R}$ with $C\, d_S(g, h)^{\alpha} - D \le \lVert f(g) - f(h) \rVert$ for all $g, h$. $d_S$ denotes the word distance of a finite subset $S \subseteq G$: the graph distance in the Cayley graph of $S$, in which distinct $u, v \in G$ are adjacent when $us = v$ or $vs = u$ for some $s \in S$. A group $G$ is amenable if it satisfies the Følner condition: for every finite $K \subseteq G$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a finite nonempty $F \subseteq G$ with $|sF \mathbin{\triangle} F| < \varepsilon |F|$ for all $s \in K$. Since the attained exponents form a down-set, this says that $G$ has Hilbert space compression less than $1/2$.

Source: G. N. Arzhantseva, Uniform embeddings of groups into a Hilbert space, in Manifold Perspectives, Oberwolfach Reports 6 (2009), no. 27, pp. 1527-1529, open problem (ii), p. 1529, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2009/27.
