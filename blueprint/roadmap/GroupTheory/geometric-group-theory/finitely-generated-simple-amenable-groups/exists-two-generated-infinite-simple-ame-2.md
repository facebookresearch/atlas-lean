# Two-generated infinite simple amenable groups

There is an infinite simple amenable group generated by two elements. A group $G$ is amenable if it satisfies the Følner condition: for every finite $K \subseteq G$ and real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a finite nonempty $F \subseteq G$ with $|sF \mathbin{\triangle} F| < \varepsilon |F|$ for every $s \in K$. Kionke and Schesler construct an infinite $2$-generated amenable residually finite group with an infinite simple quotient; that quotient is such a group.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 4.65 (= Problem 4.7), http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/4/; resolution: S. Kionke and E. Schesler, From telescopes to frames and simple groups, J. Comb. Algebra (2024), arXiv:2304.09307, Theorem 1.1.
