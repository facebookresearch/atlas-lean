# AimPL amenability Problem 4.65 (2-generated infinite simple amenable groups)

The proposition that there is an infinite simple amenable group generated by two elements. A group $G$ is amenable if it satisfies the Følner condition: for every finite $K \subseteq G$ and real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a finite nonempty $F \subseteq G$ with $|sF \mathbin{\triangle} F| < \varepsilon |F|$ for every $s \in K$.

Source: AimPL: Amenability of discrete groups, Problem 4.65 (= Problem 4.7), http://aimpl.org/amenablediscrete/4/.
