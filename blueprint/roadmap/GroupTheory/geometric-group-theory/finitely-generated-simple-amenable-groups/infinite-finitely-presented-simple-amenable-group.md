# An infinite finitely presented simple amenable group

There is an infinite finitely presented simple group $G$ satisfying the Følner condition: for every finite $K\subset G$ and $\varepsilon>0$, there is a nonempty finite $D\subset G$ with $|gD\mathbin{\triangle}D|<\varepsilon |D|$ for all $g\in K$. Finite presentation and not only finite generation is essential.

## Depends on

- Finite presentation, simplicity and the left Følner condition.

## Proof depends on

- Construction of an infinite simple finitely presented group together with Følner estimates for that same group.

## Expansion

Compare the source Følner inequality to the Atlas amenability predicate. Build the source overgroup and prove that the finite presentation, simplicity, and the Følner argument apply to one carrier. Do not transfer finite presentability from a finitely generated example.
