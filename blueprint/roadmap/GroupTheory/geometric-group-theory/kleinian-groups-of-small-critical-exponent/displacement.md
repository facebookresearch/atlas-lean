# Displacement of the base point of hyperbolic space

For an invertible real $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ matrix $A$, the displacement is $\operatorname{arcosh}(A_{00})$; for a hyperbolic isometry it is the hyperbolic distance $d(o, Ao)$ from the base point $o = e_0$ of the hyperboloid model to its image.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 5.6 (Misha Kapovich), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
