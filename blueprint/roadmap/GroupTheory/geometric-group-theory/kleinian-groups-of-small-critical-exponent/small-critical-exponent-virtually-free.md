# Bestvina Question 5.6 (Kleinian groups of small critical exponent)

The proposition that for every $n$ there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that every finitely generated discrete group $\Gamma$ of isometries of hyperbolic $n$-space whose Poincaré series $\sum_{\gamma \in \Gamma} e^{-s\,d(o, \gamma o)}$ converges for some real $s < \varepsilon$ (that is, whose critical exponent is less than $\varepsilon$) has a free subgroup of finite index. Isometries of hyperbolic $n$-space are the real $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ matrices $A$ with $A^{\mathsf T}JA = J$ for $J = \mathrm{diag}(-1, 1, \dots, 1)$ and $A_{00} > 0$, acting on the hyperboloid model; the displacement of the base point $o = e_0$ is $d(o, Ao) = \operatorname{arcosh} A_{00}$. Discreteness is for the subspace topology of the matrix group.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 5.6 (Misha Kapovich), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
