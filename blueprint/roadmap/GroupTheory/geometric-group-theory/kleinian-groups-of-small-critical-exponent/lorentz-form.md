# The Lorentzian form diag(−1, 1, …, 1)

For a natural number $n$, the real diagonal $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ matrix $J = \mathrm{diag}(-1, 1, \dots, 1)$, whose entry at index $0$ is $-1$ and all other diagonal entries are $1$.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 5.6 (Misha Kapovich), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
