# Isometries of the hyperboloid model

An invertible real $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ matrix $A$ is a hyperbolic isometry (an element of $\mathrm{O}^+(n, 1)$) if $A^{\mathsf T}JA = J$ for $J = \mathrm{diag}(-1, 1, \dots, 1)$ and $A_{00} > 0$. These matrices are exactly the isometries of the hyperboloid model $\{x : -x_0^2 + x_1^2 + \dots + x_n^2 = -1,\ x_0 > 0\}$ of hyperbolic $n$-space.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 5.6 (Misha Kapovich), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
