# Walking along a geodesic

Let a group $G$ act by isometries on a geodesic metric space $X$, let $x_0 \in X$, and let $R$ be a
covering radius for the orbit of $x_0$: every point of $X$ is within distance $R$ of some $g x_0$.
Put $S = \{s \in G : d(x_0, s x_0) \le 2R + 1\}$, and let $Q$ be a relation between elements of $G$
and natural numbers such that $Q(s, 1)$ holds for every $s \in S$ and $Q(a, m)$ implies
$Q(as, m + 1)$ for every $s \in S$. Then for every natural number $n$, every $h \in G$ and every
$y \in X$ with $d(x_0, y) \le n$ and $d(h x_0, y) \le R$, there is $m \le n + 1$ with $Q(h, m)$.
