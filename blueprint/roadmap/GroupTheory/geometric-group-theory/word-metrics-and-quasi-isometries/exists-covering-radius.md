# Cocompactness gives a covering radius

Let a group $G$ act by isometries on a metric space $X$, and let $K \subseteq X$ be a compact set
meeting every $G$-orbit. Then for every point $x_0 \in X$ there is $R \ge 0$ such that every point
of $X$ is within distance $R$ of the orbit of $x_0$: for each $x \in X$ some $g \in G$ has
$d(g x_0, x) \le R$.
