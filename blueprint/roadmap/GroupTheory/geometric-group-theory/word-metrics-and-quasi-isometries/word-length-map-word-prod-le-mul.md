# Images of words under a homomorphism

Let $\varphi \colon G \to H$ be a group homomorphism, $Q$ a generating family of $G$ and $P$ a
generating family of $H$. If $\varphi$ sends every element of $Q$ to an element of $P$-length at
most $K$, then for every word $w$ in the elements of $Q$ and their inverses, the image under
$\varphi$ of the element represented by $w$ has $P$-length at most $K$ times the length of $w$.
