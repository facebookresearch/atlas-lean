# Change of finite generating set is bi-Lipschitz

For two finite generating families $P$ and $Q$ of a group $G$ there is an integer $K \ge 1$ with $|g|_P \le K |g|_Q$ and $|g|_Q \le K |g|_P$ for every $g \in G$. Since the word metric is $d(g, h) = |g^{-1} h|$, the identity of $G$ is a $K$-bi-Lipschitz map between the two word metrics. One can take for $K$ the maximum of $1$ and the lengths of the generators of each family written in the other, which is $1$ for the trivial group.

So the word metric of a finitely generated group is well defined up to bi-Lipschitz equivalence, and every quasi-isometry invariant of $(G, d_P)$ is an invariant of the group $G$.
