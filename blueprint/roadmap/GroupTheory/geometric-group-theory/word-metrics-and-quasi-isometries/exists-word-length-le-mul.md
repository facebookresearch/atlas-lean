# Word lengths of two generating families are comparable

Let $P$ and $Q$ be families generating a group $G$, with $Q$ finite. Then there is
$K \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|g|_P \le K\, |g|_Q$ for every $g \in G$ (Gromov, *Groups of polynomial
growth and expanding maps*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 53 (1981), p. 53). Here $|g|_P$ is the word length of
$g$ for a generating family $P$ of $G$: the least length of a word in the elements of $P$ and their
inverses representing $g$.
