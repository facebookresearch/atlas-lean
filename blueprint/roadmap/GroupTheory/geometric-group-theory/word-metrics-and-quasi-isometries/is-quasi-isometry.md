# Švarc–Milnor as a quasi-isometry

Under the hypotheses of the Švarc–Milnor lemma there is a finite generating family $P$ of $G$ such
that the orbit map $g \mapsto g x_0$ is a quasi-isometry from $G$, with the word metric
$d(g, h) = |g^{-1} h|_P$, to $X$. In particular $G$ is quasi-isometric to $X$: for instance the
fundamental group of a closed Riemannian manifold is quasi-isometric to its universal cover.
