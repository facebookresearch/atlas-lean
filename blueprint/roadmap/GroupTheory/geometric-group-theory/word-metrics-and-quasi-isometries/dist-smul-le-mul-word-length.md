# Orbit maps are Lipschitz for the word length

Let a group $G$ act by isometries on a metric space $X$, let $x_0 \in X$, and let $P$ be a family
generating $G$ such that every element of $P$ moves $x_0$ by at most $M$. Then
$d(x_0, g x_0) \le M\, |g|_P$ for every $g \in G$, where $|g|_P$ is the word length.
