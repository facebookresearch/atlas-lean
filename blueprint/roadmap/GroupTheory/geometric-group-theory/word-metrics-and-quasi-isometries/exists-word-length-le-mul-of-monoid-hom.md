# Homomorphisms are Lipschitz for word lengths

Let $\varphi \colon G \to H$ be a group homomorphism, $Q$ a finite generating family of $G$ and $P$
any generating family of $H$. Then there is $K \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|\varphi(g)|_P \le K\, |g|_Q$
for every $g \in G$ (Gromov, *Asymptotic invariants of infinite groups*, 1993, §0.2.C: "every
homomorphism between finitely generated groups is Lipschitz"). Here $|g|_P$ is the word length of
$g$ for a generating family $P$ of a group: the least length of a word in the elements of $P$ and
their inverses representing $g$.
