# Polynomial growth with respect to a finite subset

Let $S$ be a finite subset of a group
$\Gamma$, and let $B_S(n)$ be the ball of radius $n$ for $S$, the set of products of at most $n$
elements of $S \cup S^{-1}$. Then $\Gamma$ has polynomial growth with respect to $S$ if there are
natural numbers $C$ and $d$ such that for every natural number $n$ the ball $B_S(n)$ has at most
$C\,(n + 1)^d$ elements.
