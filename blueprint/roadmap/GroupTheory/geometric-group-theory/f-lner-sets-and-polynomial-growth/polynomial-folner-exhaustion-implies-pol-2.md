# Følner exhaustions of polynomial size need not give polynomial growth

There exist a group
$\Gamma$ generated by a finite subset $S$ and finite subsets
$F_0 \subsetneq F_1 \subsetneq F_2 \subsetneq \cdots$ of $\Gamma$ whose union is $\Gamma$ such that
$|\partial F_k| / |F_k| \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$, where $\partial F_k$ is the boundary of $F_k$
with respect to $S$, and such that for some real $c \ge 0$ and some natural number $d \ge 1$ we
have $|F_k| \le c\,(k + 1)^d$ for every $k$, while $\Gamma$ does not have polynomial growth with
respect to $S$. That is, the answer to Kourovka Problem 14.27 is negative.

By Bardakov, every finitely generated group of intermediate growth (such groups exist by
Grigorchuk) has such a sequence of subsets.

Vaillant); resolution: V. G. Bardakov, Algebra and Logic 40 (2001), no. 1, 12-16, Kourovka Archive, Problem 14.27: every group of intermediate growth has such a sequence (answer: No, not always).
