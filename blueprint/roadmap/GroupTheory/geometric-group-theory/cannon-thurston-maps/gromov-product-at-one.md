# Gromov product at the identity for a word metric

For a group $G$ with a generating family $P$ and $x, y \in G$, the Gromov product at the identity is

$$(x \cdot y)_1 = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(d(1, x) + d(1, y) - d(x, y)\bigr),$$

where $d$ is the word metric of $P$, a real number.

Source: M. Kapovich, Problems on boundaries of groups and Kleinian groups (collected at a 2005 AIM workshop), Problem 67 (M. Mitra).
