# Equivalent sequences at infinity

For a group $G$ with a generating family $P$, two sequences $(u_m)$ and $(v_n)$ in $G$ are equivalent at infinity (define the same Gromov boundary point) if $(u_m \cdot v_n)_1 \to +\infty$ as $m, n \to \infty$ jointly, for the word metric of $P$. For a finite generating family $P$ of a group, $(x \cdot y)_1 = \tfrac12(|x| + |y| - d(x, y))$ is the Gromov product at the identity for the word metric of $P$.

Source: M. Kapovich, Problems on boundaries of groups and Kleinian groups (collected at a 2005 AIM workshop), Problem 67 (M. Mitra).
