# Sequences converging to the Gromov boundary

For a group $G$ with a generating family $P$, a sequence $(u_m)_{m \in \mathbb{N}}$ in $G$ converges at infinity if the Gromov products $(u_m \cdot u_n)_1$ at the identity, for the word metric of $P$, tend to $+\infty$ as $m$ and $n$ tend to infinity jointly. For a finite generating family $P$ of a group, $(x \cdot y)_1 = \tfrac12(|x| + |y| - d(x, y))$ is the Gromov product at the identity for the word metric of $P$.

Source: M. Kapovich, Problems on boundaries of groups and Kleinian groups (collected at a 2005 AIM workshop), Problem 67 (M. Mitra).
