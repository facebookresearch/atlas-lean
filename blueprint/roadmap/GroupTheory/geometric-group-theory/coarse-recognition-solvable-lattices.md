# Coarse recognition of finite-covolume solvable lattices

Let $S$ be a simply connected finite-dimensional real solvable Lie group with Hausdorff second-countable topology. Let $\Lambda$ be a discrete subgroup and require that $S/\Lambda$ carry a Borel $S$-invariant probability measure. If $\Lambda$ is coarsely equivalent to a finitely generated group $J$, then some finite-index subgroup of $J$ is isomorphic to a uniform lattice in a simply connected solvable Lie group, possibly different from $S$. The coarse equivalence consists of maps in both directions such that pairs whose group difference $x^{-1}y$ lies in any fixed finite set have image differences in a fixed finite set, and both inverse-composite errors $x^{-1}g(f(x))$ lie in finite sets. A uniform lattice here is a discrete subgroup $\Lambda$ for which a compact set $K$ satisfies $\Lambda K=S$; the ambient group is a simply connected finite-dimensional real solvable Lie group with Hausdorff second-countable topology.

## Depends on

- Borel invariant probability measure on the coset space, group-difference coarse equivalence and the distinct uniform-lattice notion.

## Proof depends on

- Recognition of finite-covolume input lattices and the passage to a uniform lattice for a finite-index subgroup of the target.

## Expansion

Prove the characterization needed for the measured input and transfer to the recognition theorem without assuming at the outset that the input lattice is uniform. Keep the existential replacement ambient group.
