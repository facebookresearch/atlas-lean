# Bridson–Vogtmann Question 2.4 (property (T) for Aut(F_n), n ≥ 5)

The proposition that for every $n > 4$ the automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n)$ of the free group on $n$ generators has Kazhdan's property (T). Property (T) is taken in Ozawa's form: there are a finite symmetric generating set $S$ of the group, a real $\lambda > 0$ and finitely many elements $\xi_i$ of the real group ring with $\Delta_S^2 - \lambda \Delta_S = \sum_i \xi_i^{*}\xi_i$, where $\Delta_S = |S| - \sum_{s \in S} s$ and $*$ is the linear involution extending inversion.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 20 (M. Bridson and K. Vogtmann), Question 2.4.
