# Property (T) for Out(F_n) when n ≥ 5

For every $n \ge 5$, the group $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ has Kazhdan's property (T). $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the automorphism group of the free group $F_n$ by the subgroup of inner automorphisms. Property (T) is taken in Ozawa's form: there are a finite symmetric generating set $S$, a real $\lambda > 0$ and finitely many elements $\xi_i$ of the real group ring with $\Delta_S^2 - \lambda\Delta_S = \sum_i \xi_i^{*}\xi_i$, where $\Delta_S = |S| - \sum_{s \in S} s$ and $*$ is the linear involution extending inversion. Property (T) implies property $(\tau)$, so this answers AIM Problem 3.1 for $n \ge 5$.
