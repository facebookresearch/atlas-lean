# AIM Out(F_n) Problem 3.2 (finite-index subgroups with infinite abelianization)

The proposition that for some $n \ge 5$ the group $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ has a subgroup of finite index whose abelianization is infinite. $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the automorphism group of the free group $F_n$ by the subgroup of inner automorphisms.

Source: AIM workshop The geometry of the outer automorphism group of a free group, problem list (ed. J. Mangahas), https://aimath.org/WWN/outerauto/outerauto.pdf, Problem 3.2.
