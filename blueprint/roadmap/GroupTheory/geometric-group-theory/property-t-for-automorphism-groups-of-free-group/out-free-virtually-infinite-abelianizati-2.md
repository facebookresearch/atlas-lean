# Finite-index subgroups of Out(F_n), n ≥ 5, have finite abelianization

For every $n \ge 5$, every subgroup of finite index in $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ has finite abelianization. $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the automorphism group of the free group $F_n$ by the subgroup of inner automorphisms. This follows because $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ has Kazhdan's property (T) for $n \ge 5$, property (T) passes to finite-index subgroups, and a countable group with property (T) has finite abelianization. It answers AIM Problem 3.2 negatively for $n \ge 5$. It is the negation of the proposition on [AIM Out(F_n) Problem 3.2 (finite-index subgroups with infinite abelianization)](out-free-virtually-infinite-abelianizati.md).
