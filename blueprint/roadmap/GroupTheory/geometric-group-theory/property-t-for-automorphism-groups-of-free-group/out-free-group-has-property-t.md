# AIM Out(F_n) Problem 3.1 (property (T) for Out(F_n), n ≥ 5)

The proposition that for every $n \ge 5$ the group $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ has Kazhdan's property (T). $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the automorphism group of the free group $F_n$ by the subgroup of inner automorphisms. Property (T) is taken in Ozawa's form: there are a finite symmetric generating set $S$, a real $\lambda > 0$ and finitely many elements $\xi_i$ of the real group ring with $\Delta_S^2 - \lambda\Delta_S = \sum_i \xi_i^{*}\xi_i$, where $\Delta_S = |S| - \sum_{s \in S} s$ and $*$ is the linear involution extending inversion.

Source: AIM workshop The geometry of the outer automorphism group of a free group, problem list (ed. J. Mangahas), https://aimath.org/WWN/outerauto/outerauto.pdf, Problem 3.1.
