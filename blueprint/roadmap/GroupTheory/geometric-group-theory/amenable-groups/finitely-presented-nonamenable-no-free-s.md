# Kourovka Problem 8.7 (finitely presented non-amenable groups without free subgroups)

The proposition that there is a finitely presented group $G$ that is not amenable and admits no injective homomorphism from the free group of rank $2$. A group $G$ is amenable if it satisfies the Følner condition: for every finite $K \subseteq G$ and real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a finite nonempty $F \subseteq G$ with $|sF \mathbin{\triangle} F| < \varepsilon |F|$ for every $s \in K$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.7 (R. I. Grigorchuk).
