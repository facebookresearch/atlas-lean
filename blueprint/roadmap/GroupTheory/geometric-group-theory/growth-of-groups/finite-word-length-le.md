# Word balls of a finite generating family are finite

Let $P$ be a finite family generating a group $G$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the ball
$\{g \in G : |g|_P \le n\}$ is finite: it is the image of the finite set of words of length at most
$n$ in the elements of $P$ and their inverses. Here $|g|_P$ is the word length of $g$ for a
generating family $P$ of $G$: the least length of a word in the elements of $P$ and their inverses
representing $g$.
