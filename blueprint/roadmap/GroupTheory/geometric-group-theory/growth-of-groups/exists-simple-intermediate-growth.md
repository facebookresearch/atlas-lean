# Kourovka Problem 9.8 (finitely generated simple groups of intermediate growth)

The proposition that there is a simple group $G$ with a finite generating family $P$ whose growth function is neither polynomially bounded nor exponential: it is not bounded by $C(n + 1)^d$ for any natural $C, d$, and for every real $c > 1$ it is at most $c^n$ for all sufficiently large $n$. A finitely generated group has intermediate growth if, for a finite generating family $P$ with growth function $f(n)$ (the number of elements of word length at most $n$), $f$ is not bounded by any $C(n + 1)^d$ and for every real $c > 1$ one has $f(n) \le c^n$ for all sufficiently large $n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.8 (R. I. Grigorchuk).
