# Kourovka Problem 5.2 (local convexity of growth functions)

The proposition that for every infinite group $G$ and every finite generating family $P$ of $G$, the growth function $f$ of $P$ satisfies $2f(n) \le f(n - 1) + f(n + 1)$ for all sufficiently large $n$. For a finite generating family $P$ of a group $G$, the growth function $f(n)$ is the number of elements of $G$ of word length at most $n$ with respect to $P$ (words in the generators and their inverses).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.2 (V. V. Belyaev, N. F. Sesekin).
