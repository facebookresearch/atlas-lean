# Kourovka Problem 7.4 (groups of quadratic growth)

The proposition that every group $G$ with a finite generating family $P$ whose growth function satisfies $f(n) \le C(n + 1)^2$ for some constant $C$ and all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ has an abelian subgroup of finite index. Here $f(n)$ is the number of elements of word length at most $n$ with respect to $P$ and the inverses of its members.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.4 (V. V. Belyaev).
