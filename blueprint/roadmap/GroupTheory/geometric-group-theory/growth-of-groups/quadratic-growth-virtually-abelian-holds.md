# Groups of at most quadratic growth are virtually abelian

Let $G$ be a group with a finite generating family $P$ whose growth function satisfies $f(n) \le C(n + 1)^2$ for all $n$, where $f(n)$ is the number of elements of word length at most $n$. Then $G$ has an abelian subgroup of finite index. By Gromov's theorem $G$ is virtually nilpotent, and Wolf's growth formula for virtually nilpotent groups then forces a virtually abelian group. This answers Kourovka Problem 7.4 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.4 (V. V. Belyaev); resolution: M. Gromov, Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps, Publ. Math. IHÉS 53 (1981), 53-73; J. A. Wolf, Growth of finitely generated solvable groups and curvature of Riemannian manifolds, J. Differential Geom. 2 (1968), 421-446, Theorem 3.2.
