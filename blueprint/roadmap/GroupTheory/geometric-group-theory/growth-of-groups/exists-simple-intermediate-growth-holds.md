# Finitely generated simple groups of intermediate growth

There is a finitely generated simple group of intermediate growth. A finitely generated group has intermediate growth if, for a finite generating family $P$ with growth function $f(n)$ (the number of elements of word length at most $n$), $f$ is not bounded by any $C(n + 1)^d$ and for every real $c > 1$ one has $f(n) \le c^n$ for all sufficiently large $n$. Nekrashevych gives the first examples: simple periodic groups built from minimal actions of the infinite dihedral group on a Cantor set with linearly repetitive Schreier graphs. This answers Kourovka Problem 9.8 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.8 (R. I. Grigorchuk); resolution: V. Nekrashevych, Palindromic subshifts and simple periodic groups of intermediate growth, Ann. of Math. 187 (2018), no. 3, 667-719.
