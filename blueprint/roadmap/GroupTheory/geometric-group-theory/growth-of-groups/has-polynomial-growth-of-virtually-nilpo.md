# Virtually nilpotent groups have polynomial growth

If a group $G$ has a nilpotent subgroup of finite index, then every finite generating family of $G$ has polynomial growth. This is due to Wolf, Bass and Guivarc'h; Gromov recalls it in the introduction of his paper (his reference [14] and Tits's appendix) as the converse of his theorem.

## Sources

- M. Gromov, *Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 53 (1981), 53–73, p. 54 ("If a finitely generated group Γ has a nilpotent subgroup of finite index then Γ has polynomial growth").
- J. A. Wolf, *Growth of finitely generated solvable groups and curvature of Riemannian manifolds*, J. Differential Geom. 2 (1968).
- H. Bass, *The degree of polynomial growth of finitely generated nilpotent groups*, Proc. London Math. Soc. 25 (1972).
