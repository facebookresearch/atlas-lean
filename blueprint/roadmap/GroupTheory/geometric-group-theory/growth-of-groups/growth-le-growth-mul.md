# Growth functions of two generating families dominate each other

Let $P$ and $Q$ be families generating a group $G$, with $P$ finite and the balls of $Q$ finite.
Then there is $K \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\gamma_P(n) \le \gamma_Q(Kn)$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.
Here $\gamma_P(n)$ is the number of elements of $G$ of word length at most $n$ for the generating
family $P$.
