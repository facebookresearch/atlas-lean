# Reid's question on surface subgroups of Kleinian groups

The proposition that every discrete torsion-free subgroup $\Gamma$ of $\mathrm{PSL}(2, \mathbb C)$ of finite covolume contains a closed surface group: for every Borel Haar measure $\mu$ on $\mathrm{PSL}(2, \mathbb C)$ such that the left action of $\Gamma$ has a measurable fundamental domain of finite measure, there are $g \ge 2$ and an injective homomorphism from the fundamental group of the closed orientable surface of genus $g$ into $\Gamma$. Such $\Gamma$ are the fundamental groups of finite-volume hyperbolic $3$-manifolds $\mathbb H^3/\Gamma$.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 17 (A. W. Reid), Question 2.1.
