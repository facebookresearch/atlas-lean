# Fundamental groups of closed orientable surfaces

For $g \in \mathbb N$, the group $\langle a_0, b_0, \dots, a_{g-1}, b_{g-1} \mid \prod_{i<g} [a_i, b_i] \rangle$, the fundamental group of the closed orientable surface of genus $g$. The same group, with the generators indexed differently, is described on [orientable closed surface groups](../../presentations-and-decision-problems/one-relator-groups/orientable-surface-group.md).
