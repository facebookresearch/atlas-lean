# Finite-volume hyperbolic 3-manifold groups contain closed surface groups

Every discrete torsion-free subgroup $\Gamma$ of $\mathrm{PSL}(2, \mathbb C)$ of finite covolume (a measurable fundamental domain of finite Haar measure) contains a subgroup isomorphic to the fundamental group of a closed orientable surface of genus at least $2$. The non-compact case is due to Cooper, Long and Reid, the closed case to Kahn and Markovic.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 17 (A. W. Reid), Question 2.1; resolution: J. Kahn and V. Markovic, Immersing almost geodesic surfaces in a closed hyperbolic three manifold, Ann. of Math. (2) 175 (2012), 1127-1190, arXiv:0910.5501, Theorem 1.1 (closed case); D. Cooper, D. D. Long and A. W. Reid, Essential closed surfaces in bounded 3-manifolds, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 10 (1997), 553-563, Theorem 1.1 (non-compact case).
