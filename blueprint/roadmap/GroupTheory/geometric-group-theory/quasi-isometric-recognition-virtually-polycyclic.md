# Coarse recognition of virtually polycyclic groups

Let finitely generated groups $P,J$ be coarsely equivalent in the group-difference sense below. If $P$ has a finite-index subgroup with a finite subnormal series of cyclic quotients, then $J$ does as well. Moreover a finite-index subgroup of $J$ is isomorphic to a uniform lattice in some simply connected solvable Lie group. The coarse equivalence consists of maps in both directions such that pairs whose group difference $x^{-1}y$ lies in any fixed finite set have image differences in a fixed finite set, and both inverse-composite errors $x^{-1}g(f(x))$ lie in finite sets. A uniform lattice here is a discrete subgroup $\Lambda$ for which a compact set $K$ satisfies $\Lambda K=S$; the ambient group is a simply connected finite-dimensional real solvable Lie group with Hausdorff second-countable topology.

## Depends on

- [Polycyclic series](../group-rings/projective-modules-over-group-rings/is-polycyclic.md), group-difference coarse equivalence and uniform lattice realization.

## Proof depends on

- Finite-index reduction and quasi-isometry rigidity for solvable Lie models, with an existence theorem for an ambient Lie group.

## Expansion

Prove the bridge from finite-displacement control to word metrics and show the recognized group admits the required finite-index lattice. Do not assume the ambient Lie group is fixed in advance.
