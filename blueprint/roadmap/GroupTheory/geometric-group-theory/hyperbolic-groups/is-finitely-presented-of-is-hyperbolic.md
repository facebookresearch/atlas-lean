# Hyperbolic groups are finitely presented

Every word-hyperbolic group is finitely presented. Gromov derives it from Rips's theorem: for $d \ge 4\delta + 1$ the Rips complex $P_d(\Gamma)$ is contractible, and $\Gamma$ acts on it properly and cocompactly.

## Sources

- M. Gromov, *Hyperbolic groups*, in *Essays in Group Theory* (S. M. Gersten, ed.), Math. Sci. Res. Inst. Publ. 8, Springer, 1987, 75–263, Theorem 2.2 (Rips) and Corollary 2.2.A.
