# Word metrics isometric to a hyperbolic space are hyperbolic

Let $P$ be a family generating a group $G$, let $X$ be a $\delta$-hyperbolic metric space, and let
$f \colon G \to X$ satisfy $d(f(g), f(h)) = |g^{-1} h|_P$ for all $g, h \in G$. Then $G$ with the
word metric $d(g, h) = |g^{-1} h|_P$ is $\delta$-hyperbolic.
