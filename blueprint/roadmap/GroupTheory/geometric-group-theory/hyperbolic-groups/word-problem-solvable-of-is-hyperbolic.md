# Hyperbolic groups have solvable word problem

If $G$ is word-hyperbolic and $P$ is a generating family of $G$ indexed by $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, then there is an algorithm deciding whether a word in the generators and their inverses represents the identity of $G$, in the same sense as in the Boone–Higman and Novikov entries.

Gromov derives this from his Corollary 2.3.B: for every finite presentation of a hyperbolic group, the area of a relation is at most a constant times its length (a linear isoperimetric inequality), "in particular, the word problem is solvable"; the classical algorithm goes back to Dehn.
