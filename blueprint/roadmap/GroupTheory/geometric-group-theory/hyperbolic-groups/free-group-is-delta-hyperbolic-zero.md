# Free groups are $0$-hyperbolic

The word metric of the free group $F(\iota)$ with respect to its free basis is $0$-hyperbolic, for every set $\iota$ (for $\iota$ empty the group is trivial). Gromov notes that simplicial trees are $0$-hyperbolic, the Gromov product of two points being the distance from the base point to the segment joining them; the Cayley graph of a free group on a free basis is a tree, and the word metric is its path metric restricted to the vertices.
