# The infinite cyclic group is word-hyperbolic

For the generator $1$ of the infinite cyclic group $\mathbb{Z}$, the word metric is
$d(m, n) = |m - n|$, so $\mathbb{Z}$ with this metric is isometric to
$\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, hence $0$-hyperbolic, and $\mathbb{Z}$ is word-hyperbolic
(Gromov, *Hyperbolic groups*, 1987, §1.2(a)).
