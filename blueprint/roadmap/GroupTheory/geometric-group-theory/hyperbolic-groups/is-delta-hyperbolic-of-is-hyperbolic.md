# Hyperbolicity does not depend on the finite generating set

If $G$ is word-hyperbolic, then for every finite generating family $P$ of $G$ the word metric of $P$ is $\delta$-hyperbolic for some $\delta$ (depending on $P$). Gromov deduces it from the linear isoperimetric inequality (Corollary 2.3.B) and its converse (Theorem 2.3.D). It also follows from the quasi-isometry invariance of hyperbolicity for quasi-geodesic spaces (word metrics are quasi-geodesic, Gromov 1993, §0.2.D).
