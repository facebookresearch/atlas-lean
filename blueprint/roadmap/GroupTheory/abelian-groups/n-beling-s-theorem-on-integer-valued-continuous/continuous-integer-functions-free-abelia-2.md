# Continuous integer-valued functions on a compact Hausdorff space form a free abelian group

For every compact Hausdorff space $X$, the additive group $C(X, \mathbb{Z})$ of continuous functions $X \to \mathbb{Z}$ ($\mathbb{Z}$ discrete) is a free abelian group. Kourovka Problem 2.36 has a positive answer.
