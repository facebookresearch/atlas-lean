# Kourovka Problem 2.36 (continuous integer-valued functions on a compact space)

This is the proposition that for every compact Hausdorff space $X$ the additive group $C(X, \mathbb{Z})$ of continuous functions $X \to \mathbb{Z}$ ($\mathbb{Z}$ discrete) is a free abelian group.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.36 (V. I. Kuz'minov; question of J. de Groot).
