# Kourovka Problem 3.22 (locally pure-free abelian groups and summands ℤ)

The proposition that every nonzero abelian group $A$ in which every finite subset lies in a pure finitely generated free abelian subgroup has a direct summand isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$: there are complementary subgroups $C, D$ ($C \cap D = 0$, $C + D = A$) with $C \cong \mathbb{Z}$. A subgroup $B$ of an abelian group $A$ is pure if $nA \cap B = nB$ for every integer $n$. The problem gives this as equivalent to asking whether the inverse limit of a surjective inverse system of nonzero finitely generated free abelian groups is nonzero.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.22 (V. I. Kuz'minov).
