# Exact sequence pow index

Let $U$, $G$, $F$ be commutative groups and let $\iota : U \to G$ and $\varphi : G \to F$ be homomorphisms with $\iota$ injective, $\varphi$ surjective and $\operatorname{im} \iota = \ker \varphi$. Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and assume that $F$ has no nontrivial element killed by the $n$-th power map: $x^n = 1$ implies $x = 1$ for every $x \in F$. Then the indices of the subgroups of $n$-th powers satisfy

$$[G : G^n] = [F : F^n] \cdot [U : U^n],$$

where $H^n = \{h^n : h \in H\}$ and an infinite index is counted as $0$.
