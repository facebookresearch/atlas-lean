# The isomorphism A/B ≅ f(A)/f(B) on representatives

Let $f : A \to C$ be a homomorphism of commutative groups and $B \le A$ a subgroup containing $\ker f$. The canonical isomorphism $A/B \cong f(A)/f(B)$, where $f(B)$ is regarded as a subgroup of the image $f(A)$, maps the class of every $a \in A$ to the class of $f(a) \in f(A)$. The same holds for the additive version.
