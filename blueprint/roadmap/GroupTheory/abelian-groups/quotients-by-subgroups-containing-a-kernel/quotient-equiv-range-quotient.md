# Quotients by subgroups containing a kernel

Let $f : A \to C$ be a homomorphism of commutative groups and $B \le A$ a subgroup containing $\ker f$. The canonical isomorphism $A/B \cong f(A)/f(B)$, where $f(B)$ is regarded as a subgroup of the image $f(A)$, sends the class of $a$ to the class of $f(a)$. The additive version is stated alongside.
