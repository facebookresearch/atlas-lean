# Cardinality of the $n$-torsion of a vector space modulo a lattice

Let $V$ be a vector space over a division ring of characteristic zero, and let $L \subseteq V$ be
an additive subgroup that is a free abelian group of finite rank $r$. Then for every integer
$n \ge 1$ the $n$-torsion subgroup $(V/L)[n] = \{x \in V/L : nx = 0\}$ of the quotient group has
exactly $n^r$ elements.

This counts torsion points, for torsion in abelian varieties and elliptic curves, for class group
computations, and for lattice index arguments. Neither the characteristic nor the condition
$n \ge 1$ can be dropped: for $n = 0$ the $0$-torsion is all of $V/L$, and over $\mathbb{F}_p$ the
space $V = \mathbb{F}_p$ with $L = 0$ has $|(V/L)[p]| = p$ rather than $p^0 = 1$.
