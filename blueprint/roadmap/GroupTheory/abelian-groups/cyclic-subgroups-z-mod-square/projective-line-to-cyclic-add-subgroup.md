# Projective Line To Cyclic Add Subgroup

Let $N$ be a positive integer. The map from the projective line $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ to the set of cyclic subgroups of order $N$ of $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2$ sends the class of a unimodular row $v$ to $\langle v \rangle = \{k v : k \in \mathbb{Z}\}$. It is well defined because $\langle u v \rangle = \langle v \rangle$ for every unit $u \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times$.
