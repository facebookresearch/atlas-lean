# Unimodular Row To Cyclic Add Subgroup

Let $N$ be a positive integer and let $v = (a, c)$ be a unimodular row over $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$. The associated subgroup is the cyclic subgroup

$$\langle v \rangle = \{ k v : k \in \mathbb{Z} \} \subseteq (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2,$$

which is cyclic of order exactly $N$.
