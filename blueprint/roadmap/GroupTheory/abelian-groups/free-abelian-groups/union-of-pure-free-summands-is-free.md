# Kourovka Problem 2.4 (unions of ω₁-chains of pure free summands)

The proposition that every abelian group $A$ is free abelian whenever $A$ is the union of a chain of pure subgroups $A_\alpha$, $\alpha < \omega_1$, each free abelian of countably infinite rank, with $A_\beta$ a direct summand of $A_\alpha$ whenever $\beta < \alpha < \omega_1$; a subgroup $B$ is pure if $nA \cap B = nB$ for every integer $n$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.4 (Yu. A. Bogan; question of S. Chase).
