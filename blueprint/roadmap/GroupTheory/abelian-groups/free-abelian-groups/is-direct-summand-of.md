# Direct summands of a subgroup

For subgroups $S$ and $T$ of an abelian group $A$, $S$ is a direct summand of $T$ if there is a subgroup $C$ of $A$ with $S \cap C = 0$ and $S + C = T$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.4 (Yu. A. Bogan; question of S. Chase).
