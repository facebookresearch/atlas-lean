# Ultraproduct kernel

For an ultrafilter $U$ on $\mathbb{N}$ and a sequence
$(G_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of groups, the normal subgroup of $\prod_i G_i$ generated by the
sequences $(g_i)_i$ with $\{i : g_i = 1\} \in U$. These sequences already form a normal subgroup,
so the kernel consists of exactly them.
