# Vdovin–Revin question on Hall subgroups and their normal closures

This is the statement
that for every finite group $G$ and every Hall subgroup $H$ of $G$ (a subgroup whose order $|H|$
is coprime to its index $|G : H|$), $H$ is pronormal in its normal closure $\langle H^G \rangle$,
the smallest normal subgroup of $G$ containing $H$: for every $g \in \langle H^G \rangle$ there is
an element $x$ of the subgroup $\langle H, gHg^{-1} \rangle$ generated by $H$ and $gHg^{-1}$ with
$xHx^{-1} = gHg^{-1}$.

This is Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.32 (E. P. Vdovin, D. O. Revin).
