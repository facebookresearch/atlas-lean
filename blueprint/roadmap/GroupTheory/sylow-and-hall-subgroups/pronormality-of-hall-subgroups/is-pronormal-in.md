# Pronormality in a subgroup

Let $H$ and $K$ be subgroups of a group $G$. Then $H$ is
pronormal in $K$ when for every $g \in K$ there is an element $x$ of the subgroup
$\langle H, gHg^{-1} \rangle$ generated by $H$ and $gHg^{-1}$ such that $xHx^{-1} = gHg^{-1}$,
that is, $H$ and its conjugate $gHg^{-1}$ are conjugate by an element of
$\langle H, gHg^{-1} \rangle$.
