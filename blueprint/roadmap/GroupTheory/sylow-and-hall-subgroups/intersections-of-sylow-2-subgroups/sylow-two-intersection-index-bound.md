# Indices of intersections of Sylow 2-subgroups (Kourovka 5.12)

The statement (Kourovka Problem 5.12, V. V. Kabanov) that the following holds for every finite group $G$ with trivial soluble radical, that is, with no non-trivial soluble normal subgroup, which has two distinct Sylow $2$-subgroups with non-trivial intersection. If $n \in \mathbb{N}$ is such that $|P : P \cap Q| \le 2^n$ for all Sylow $2$-subgroups $P$ and $Q$ of $G$ with $P \cap Q \ne 1$, then $|P| \le 2^{2n}$ for every Sylow $2$-subgroup $P$ of $G$.

The intersecting pair is required to be distinct: with $P = Q$ allowed the hypothesis would only say that $|G|$ is even, and the simple groups $\mathrm{PSL}_2(2^k)$, $k \ge 2$, whose distinct Sylow $2$-subgroups intersect trivially, would satisfy the index bound with $n = 0$ but not $|P| \le 1$.

The statement holds, by the classification of finite simple groups: [Zenkov's bound for intersections of Sylow 2-subgroups](sylow-two-intersection-index-bound-holds.md).
