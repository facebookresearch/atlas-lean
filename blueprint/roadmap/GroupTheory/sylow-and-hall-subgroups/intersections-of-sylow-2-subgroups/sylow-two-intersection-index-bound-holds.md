# Zenkov's bound for intersections of Sylow 2-subgroups

Let $G$ be a finite group with trivial soluble radical that has two distinct Sylow $2$-subgroups with non-trivial intersection, and let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ be such that $|P : P \cap Q| \le 2^n$ for all Sylow $2$-subgroups $P$ and $Q$ of $G$ with $P \cap Q \ne 1$. Then $|P| \le 2^{2n}$ for every Sylow $2$-subgroup $P$ of $G$. This answers [Kourovka Problem 5.12](sylow-two-intersection-index-bound.md) in the affirmative.

The Kourovka Archive answers Problem 5.12 "Yes, it is, mod CFSG", citing Zenkov, whose paper settles the problem, using the classification of finite simple groups, for finite groups $G$ with $O_p(G) = 1$ for every prime $p$, which are exactly the finite groups with trivial soluble radical.
