# Order of Zenkov's group

Zenkov's permutation group has order $29484$.

The orbit–stabilizer argument counts $28$ points in the orbit of $\infty$ and $1053$ elements
in its stabilizer, giving $28 \cdot 1053 = 29484$.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 18.31, which attributes this example to V. I. Zenkov (Siberian Math. J. 63 (2022), no. 4, 720-722).
