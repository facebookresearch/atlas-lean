# Zenkov's group `L₂(27) ⋊ ⟨φ⟩` (Kourovka 18.31)

The group of permutations of the projective
line `ℙ¹(𝔽₂₇) = 𝔽₂₇ ∪ {∞}` generated by the translation `x ↦ x + 1`, the multiplication
`x ↦ g * x` by an element `g` of order `13`, the inversion `x ↦ -1 / x` and the field automorphism
`φ : x ↦ x ^ 3` of order `3`. The first three generate `L₂(27) = PSL(2, 27)`, so this is the
extension of `L₂(27)` by a field automorphism of order `3`, and the stabilizer of `∞` is the
extension of a Borel subgroup of `L₂(27)` by `φ`.

Here `𝔽₂₇ = 𝔽₃[g]` with `g ^ 3 = g + 1`, the element `c₀ + c₁ g + c₂ g ^ 2` is the point
`c₀ + 3 c₁ + 9 c₂` of `Fin 28`, and `∞` is the point `27`.
