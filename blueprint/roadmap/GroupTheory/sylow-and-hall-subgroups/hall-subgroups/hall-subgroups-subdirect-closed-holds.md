# Subdirect products of groups with Hall π-subgroups

Let $\pi$ be a set of natural numbers, $G$ a finite group and $N_1$, $N_2$ normal subgroups of $G$ with $N_1 \cap N_2 = 1$. If $G/N_1$ and $G/N_2$ both have Hall $\pi$-subgroups, then so does $G$. A Hall $\pi$-subgroup of a group is a subgroup whose order has all its prime divisors in $\pi$ and whose index has none of its prime divisors in $\pi$. By induction on the number of factors this says that the class of finite groups with a Hall $\pi$-subgroup is closed under finite subdirect products. This is Revin and Vdovin's positive answer to Kourovka Problem 5.65.

Shemetkov); resolution: D. O. Revin and E. P. Vdovin, An existence criterion for Hall subgroups of finite groups, J. Group Theory 14 (2011), 93-101, Corollary 7, arXiv:0803.3868.
