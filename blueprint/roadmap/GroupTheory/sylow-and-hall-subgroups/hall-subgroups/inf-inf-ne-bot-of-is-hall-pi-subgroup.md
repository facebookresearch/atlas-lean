# Any three Hall $\{3, 13\}$-subgroups of Zenkov's group meet nontrivially

For any Hall
$\{3, 13\}$-subgroups $H_1$, $H_2$, $H_3$ of $L_2(27) \rtimes \langle \varphi \rangle$, not
necessarily distinct, $H_1 \cap H_2 \cap H_3 \ne 1$.

Each of them has order $1053 = 3^4 \cdot 13$ and index $28$ in this group of order
$29484 = 28 \cdot 1053$, so their intersection has index at most $28^3 = 21952 < 29484$.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 18.31, which attributes this example to V. I. Zenkov (Siberian Math. J. 63 (2022), no. 4, 720-722).
