# Kourovka Problem 2.30 (Sylow subgroups covered by the other Sylow subgroups)

The proposition that for every prime $p$ there are a finite group $G$ and a Sylow $p$-subgroup $P$ of $G$ that is contained in the union of the other Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$. The problem asks for one such group; it is stated here for every prime, as the Archive answers it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.30 (P. G. Kontorovich, A. L. Starostin); resolution: V. D. Mazurov, Ural. Gos. Univ. Mat. Zap. 7 (1969/70), no. 3, 129-132 (Russian).
