# Kourovka Problem 6.7 (a characteristic subgroup for S₄-free groups)

The proposition that every nontrivial finite $2$-group $P$ has a nontrivial characteristic subgroup $L(P)$ such that for every finite $S_4$-free group $H$ containing $P$ as a Sylow $2$-subgroup (through an injective homomorphism whose image is a Sylow $2$-subgroup) and satisfying $C_H(O_2(H)) \le O_2(H)$, the image of $L(P)$ is normal in $H$. A finite group $H$ is $S_4$-free if no section $K/L$ ($L$ normal in a subgroup $K$ of $H$) is isomorphic to the symmetric group $S_4$; $O_2(H)$ is the largest normal $2$-subgroup of $H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.7 (G. Glauberman).
