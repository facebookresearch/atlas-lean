# Stellmacher's characteristic subgroup for S₄-free groups

Every nontrivial finite $2$-group $P$ has a nontrivial characteristic subgroup $L(P)$ that is normal in every finite $S_4$-free group $H$ having $P$ as a Sylow $2$-subgroup and satisfying $C_H(O_2(H)) \le O_2(H)$. A finite group $H$ is $S_4$-free if no section $K/L$ ($L$ normal in a subgroup $K$ of $H$) is isomorphic to the symmetric group $S_4$; $O_2(H)$ is the largest normal $2$-subgroup of $H$. This answers Kourovka Problem 6.7 positively.
