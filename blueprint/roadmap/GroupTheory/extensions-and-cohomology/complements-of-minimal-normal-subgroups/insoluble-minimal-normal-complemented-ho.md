# Insoluble minimal normal subgroups with cyclic Sylow subgroups are complemented

Let $H$ be an insoluble minimal normal subgroup of a finite group $G$ such that, for every prime $p$ dividing $|G : H|$, the Sylow $p$-subgroups of $H$ are cyclic. Then $H$ has a complement in $G$.

Shemetkov); resolution: S. A. Syskin, Complementarity in finite groups, Siberian Math. J. 12 (1971), 342-344, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00969061; L. A. Shemetkov, On the existence of π-complements for normal subgroups of finite groups, Soviet Math. Dokl. 11 (1970), 1436-1438.
