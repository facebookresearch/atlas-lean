# Kourovka Problem 3.59 (complements of insoluble minimal normal subgroups)

The proposition that if $H$ is an insoluble minimal normal subgroup of a finite group $G$ whose Sylow $p$-subgroups are cyclic for every prime $p$ dividing $|G : H|$, then $H$ has a complement in $G$: a subgroup $K$ with $H \cap K = 1$ and $HK = G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.59 (L. A. Shemetkov).
