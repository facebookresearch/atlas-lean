# A torsion-free group of type FP∞ with infinite cohomological dimension

There is a torsion-free group of type $\mathrm{FP}_\infty$ whose cohomological dimension is infinite. A group $G$ is of type $\mathrm{FP}_\infty$ if the trivial $\mathbb{Z}G$-module $\mathbb{Z}$ has a projective resolution by finitely generated $\mathbb{Z}G$-modules, and has finite cohomological dimension if $\mathbb{Z}$ has finite projective dimension over $\mathbb{Z}G$. This answers Kourovka Problem 6.4 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 6.4 (torsion-free groups of type FP∞)](torsion-free-fpinfinity-finite-cohomolog.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.4 (R. Bieri); resolution: K. S. Brown and R. Geoghegan, An infinite-dimensional torsion-free FP∞ group, Invent. Math. 77 (1984), 367-381.
