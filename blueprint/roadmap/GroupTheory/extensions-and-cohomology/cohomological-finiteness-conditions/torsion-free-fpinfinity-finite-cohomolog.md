# Kourovka Problem 6.4 (torsion-free groups of type FP∞)

The proposition that every torsion-free group of type $\mathrm{FP}_\infty$ has finite cohomological dimension. A group $G$ is of type $\mathrm{FP}_\infty$ if the trivial $\mathbb{Z}G$-module $\mathbb{Z}$ has a projective resolution by finitely generated $\mathbb{Z}G$-modules, and has finite cohomological dimension if $\mathbb{Z}$ has finite projective dimension over $\mathbb{Z}G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.4 (R. Bieri).
