# Vanishing of `H^1` for `SL(2, F)` on trace-zero matrices in characteristic 3

Let $F$ be a finite field of characteristic $3$ and let $A$ be a representation of $\mathrm{SL}_2(F)$ over $F$. Suppose $e : A \to M_2(F)$ is an injective $F$-linear map whose image is the space of trace-zero $2 \times 2$ matrices and which intertwines the action with conjugation:

$$e(g \cdot a) = g \, e(a) \, g^{-1} \qquad (g \in \mathrm{SL}_2(F),\ a \in A).$$

Then $H^1(\mathrm{SL}_2(F), A)$ has at most one element, so it is zero.
