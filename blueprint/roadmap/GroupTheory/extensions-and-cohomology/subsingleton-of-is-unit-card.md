# H1 vanishing

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $G$ a finite group and $A$ a $k$-linear representation of $G$. If the order $|G|$ is a unit in $k$, then the first group cohomology $H^1(G, A)$ has at most one element, so $H^1(G, A) = 0$.

Averaging a $1$-cocycle $f$ over the group exhibits it as a coboundary: with $s = \sum_{h \in G} f(h)$, the cocycle relation gives $|G| \, f(g) = s - g \cdot s$, and dividing by $|G|$ produces the cobounding element.
