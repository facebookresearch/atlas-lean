# Xiao's theorem on complements of normal subgroups with Sylow direct factors

Let $H$ be a normal subgroup of a finite group $G$ such that for every prime $p$ every Sylow $p$-subgroup $P$ of $H$ is a direct factor of some Sylow $p$-subgroup $Q$ of $G$: there is a subgroup $K$ with $P \cap K = 1$, $\langle P, K \rangle = Q$ and $P$, $K$ commuting elementwise. Then $H$ has a complement in $G$, a subgroup $L$ with $H \cap L = 1$ and $HL = G$. This is Xiao's positive answer to Kourovka Problem 7.44, using the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.44 (V. I. Sergiyenko); resolution: W. Xiao, On a problem of Sergienko, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 87 (1993), 97-98, https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-4049(93)90072-2.
