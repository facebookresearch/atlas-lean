# Isomorphism of relation modules

For a group $G$ and two homomorphisms $\pi_1, \pi_2 \colon F_d \to G$ with kernels $R_1, R_2$, the relation modules $R_1/[R_1, R_1]$ and $R_2/[R_2, R_2]$ are isomorphic as $\mathbb ZG$-modules when some group isomorphism $f$ between them intertwines the conjugation actions: whenever $\pi_1(u_1) = \pi_2(u_2)$, conjugation by $u_1$ followed by $f$ equals $f$ followed by conjugation by $u_2$. For surjections these are the relation modules of two presentations of $G$.
