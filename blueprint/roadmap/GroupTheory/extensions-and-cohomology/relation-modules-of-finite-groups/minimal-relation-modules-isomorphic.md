# Kourovka Problem 4.54 (minimal relation modules)

The proposition that any two minimal relation modules of a finite group are isomorphic: for every finite group $G$ and any two surjections $F_{d(G)} \to G$ from the free group of rank $d(G)$, the two relation modules are isomorphic as $\mathbb ZG$-modules. For a presentation $\pi \colon F_d \to G$ with kernel $R$, the relation module is $R/[R, R]$ with $G$ acting by conjugation: $g = \pi(u)$ acts through conjugation by $u$. It is minimal when $d = d(G)$, the minimal number of generators of $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.54 (K. W. Roggenkamp).
