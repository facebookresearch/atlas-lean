# Finitely generated metabelian groups with finitely generated integral homology have finite rank

**Finitely generated metabelian groups with finitely generated integral homology have finite
rank.** Let $G$ be a finitely generated metabelian group, that is, its second derived subgroup
$G''$ is trivial, such that for every $n \ge 0$ the integral homology group $H_n(G, \mathbb{Z})$
with trivial coefficients is finitely generated. Then $G$ has finite Prüfer rank: there is a
natural number $r$ such that every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is generated by at most $r$
elements. This answers Kourovka Problem 10.37 positively.

The Kourovka Archive records this answer, citing Kochloukova (2003); Groves had proved it when $G$
splits over its derived subgroup.
