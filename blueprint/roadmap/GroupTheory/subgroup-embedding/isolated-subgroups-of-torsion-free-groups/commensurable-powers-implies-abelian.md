# Kourovka Problem 1.63(b) (torsion-free groups with commensurable powers)

The proposition that every torsion-free group $G$ in which any two non-identity elements $x, y$ satisfy $x^k = y^l$ for some nonzero integers $k, l$ is abelian.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.63 (P. G. Kontorovich).
