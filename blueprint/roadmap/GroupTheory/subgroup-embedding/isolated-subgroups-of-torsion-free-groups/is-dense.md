# Dense groups (no proper nontrivial isolated subgroups)

A group $G$ is dense if its only isolated subgroups are the trivial subgroup and $G$ itself, where a subgroup $H$ is isolated if $x^n \in H$ with $n \ge 1$ implies $x \in H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.63 (P. G. Kontorovich).
