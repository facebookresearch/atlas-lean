# Dense torsion-free groups that are not locally cyclic

There is a torsion-free group that is dense but not locally cyclic. A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is isolated if $x^n \in H$ with $n \ge 1$ implies $x \in H$; $G$ is dense if its only isolated subgroups are $1$ and $G$. $G$ is locally cyclic if every finitely generated subgroup is cyclic. This answers Kourovka Problem 1.63(a) positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.63 (P. G. Kontorovich); resolution: S. I. Adian, On some torsion-free groups, Math. USSR-Izv. 5 (1971), 475-484.
