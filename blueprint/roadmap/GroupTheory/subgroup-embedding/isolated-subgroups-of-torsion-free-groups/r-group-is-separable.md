# Kourovka Problem 1.64 (separability of R-groups)

The proposition that every nontrivial $R$-group is separable. An $R$-group is a group in which $x^n = y^n$ with $n \ge 1$ implies $x = y$ (roots are unique); a group is separable if it is the union of two proper subsemigroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.64 (P. G. Kontorovich).
