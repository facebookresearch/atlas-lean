# Non-abelian torsion-free groups with commensurable powers

There is a non-abelian torsion-free group $G$ in which any two non-identity elements $x, y$ satisfy $x^k = y^l$ for some nonzero integers $k, l$. This answers Kourovka Problem 1.63(b) negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 1.63(b) (torsion-free groups with commensurable powers)](commensurable-powers-implies-abelian.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.63 (P. G. Kontorovich); resolution: S. I. Adian, On some torsion-free groups, Math. USSR-Izv. 5 (1971), 475-484.
