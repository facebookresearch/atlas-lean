# Kourovka Problem 2.29 (abelian subgroups in normal subgroups of prime index)

The proposition that for every finite group $G$ the following two conditions are equivalent: every proper abelian subgroup of $G$ is contained in a proper normal subgroup of $G$; and every proper abelian subgroup of $G$ is contained in a normal subgroup of $G$ of prime index. A subgroup is abelian when any two of its elements commute. A normal subgroup of prime index is automatically proper, so the second condition always implies the first; for the trivial group both hold vacuously.

Kontorovich, V. T. Nagrebetskiĭ); resolution: G. M. Bergman and I. M. Isaacs, letter of 17 June 1974, recorded in the Archive under Problem 2.29.
