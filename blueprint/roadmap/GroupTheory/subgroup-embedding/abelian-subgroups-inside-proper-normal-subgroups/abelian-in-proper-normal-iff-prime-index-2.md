# Abelian subgroups need not lie in normal subgroups of prime index

There is a finite group $G$ in which every proper abelian subgroup of $G$ is contained in a proper normal subgroup of $G$, but some proper abelian subgroup of $G$ lies in no normal subgroup of $G$ of prime index; Kourovka Problem 2.29 has a negative answer. Bergman and Isaacs take a nontrivial finite perfect group $B$ of rank $r$ and $A = \bigoplus_{p \mid |B|} (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{r+1}$: in the wreath product $A \wr B$ every proper abelian subgroup lies in a proper normal subgroup, while the base group lies in no normal subgroup of prime index, because the quotient by the base group is $B = [B, B]$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.29 (abelian subgroups in normal subgroups of prime index)](abelian-in-proper-normal-iff-prime-index.md).

Kontorovich, V. T. Nagrebetskiĭ); resolution: G. M. Bergman and I. M. Isaacs, letter of 17 June 1974, recorded in the Archive under Problem 2.29.
