# Counterexamples to Wall's conjecture

There is a finite group $G$ with at least $|G|$ maximal subgroups; Kourovka Problem 7.17 (Wall's conjecture) has a negative answer. Lübeck found infinitely many such groups. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 7.17 (Wall's conjecture on the number of maximal subgroups)](maximal-subgroup-count-lt-order.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 7.17 (R. Griess); resolution: R. Guralnick, F. Lübeck, L. Scott and T. Sprowl, reported in C. P. Bendel et al., in: Developments and retrospectives in Lie theory, Springer, 2014, 51-69; F. Lübeck, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 373 (2020), 2331-2347.
