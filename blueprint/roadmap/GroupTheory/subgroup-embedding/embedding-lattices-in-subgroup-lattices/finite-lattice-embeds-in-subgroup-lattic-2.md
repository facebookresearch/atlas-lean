# Every finite lattice embeds in the subgroup lattice of a finite group

For every finite lattice $L$ there are a finite group $G$ and an injective map from $L$ to the subgroups of $G$ preserving joins and meets. This follows from the Pudlák–Tůma theorem that every finite lattice embeds in the partition lattice of a finite set.
