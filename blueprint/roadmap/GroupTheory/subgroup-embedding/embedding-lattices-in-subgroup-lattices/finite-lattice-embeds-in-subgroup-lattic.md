# Kourovka Problem 5.3 (finite lattices in subgroup lattices of finite groups)

The proposition that every finite lattice $L$ embeds in the subgroup lattice of some finite group $G$: there is an injective lattice homomorphism from $L$ to the lattice of subgroups of $G$ (preserving binary joins and meets, not necessarily the bounds). This is the sublattice question, not the question of representing $L$ as an interval.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.3 (G. M. Bergman).
