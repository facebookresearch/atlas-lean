# Good subnormal subgroups

A good subnormal subgroup of a group $G$ is a subnormal subgroup $H$ of $G$ such that the join $\langle H, J \rangle$ is subnormal in $G$ for every subnormal subgroup $J$ of $G$, where subnormal means reachable from $G$ by a finite chain of subgroups each normal in the next. In a finite group every subnormal subgroup is good, by Wielandt's join theorem.

Thompson; marked solved in the main list); resolution: K.
