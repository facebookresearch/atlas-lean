# Kourovka Problem 5.69 (lattice isomorphisms of torsion-free groups)

The proposition that for torsion-free groups $G$, $H$ with no non-trivial cyclic normal subgroups, every order isomorphism $\varphi$ between their subgroup lattices is induced by a group isomorphism $f \colon G \to H$: $\varphi(K) = f(K)$ for every subgroup $K$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.69 (B. V. Yakovlev).
