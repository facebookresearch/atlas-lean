# A lattice isomorphism of torsion-free groups not induced by a group isomorphism

There are torsion-free groups without non-trivial cyclic normal subgroups and an isomorphism of their subgroup lattices induced by no group isomorphism. Kourovka Problem 5.69 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.69 (lattice isomorphisms of torsion-free groups)](lattice-isomorphism-induced.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.69 (B. V. Yakovlev); resolution: A. Yu. Olshanskii, Groups with cyclic subgroups, C. R. Acad. Bulgare Sci. 32 (1979), 1165-1166.
