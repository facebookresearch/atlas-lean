# A Γ-quasi-nilpotent group that is not quasi-nilpotent

There is a finite group in which any two maximal subgroups satisfy the Berkovich–Kravchuk condition but some two subgroups do not: Mazurov's group $\langle x, y, z, t \mid x^4 = y^4 = z^2 = t^3 = 1, [x, y] = z, [x, z] = [y, z] = 1, x^t = y, y^t = x^{-1}y^{-1} \rangle$ of order $96$, with $G/\Phi(G) \cong A_4$, in which $A_1 = \langle zx^2, zy^2, t \rangle$ and $B_1 = \langle z, t \rangle$ violate the condition. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 2.3 (quasi-nilpotent and Γ-quasi-nilpotent groups)](quasi-nilpotent-iff-gamma-quasi-nilpoten.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.3 (Ya. G. Berkovich, M. I. Kravchuk); resolution: V. D. Mazurov (1973).
