# Kourovka Problem 2.3 (quasi-nilpotent and Γ-quasi-nilpotent groups)

The proposition that a finite group is quasi-nilpotent in the sense of Berkovich and Kravchuk (any two subgroups satisfy their condition) if and only if it is $\Gamma$-quasi-nilpotent (any two maximal subgroups satisfy it). This is not the quasinilpotence of the generalized Fitting subgroup.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.3 (Ya. G. Berkovich, M. I. Kravchuk).
