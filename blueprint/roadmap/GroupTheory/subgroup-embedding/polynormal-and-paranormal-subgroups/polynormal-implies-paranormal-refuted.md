# A polynormal subgroup that is not paranormal (Kourovka 14.50 refuted)

There is a finite
group $G$ with a subgroup $A$ that is polynormal but not paranormal. So the conjecture of
Kourovka Problem 14.50 is false.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 14.50 (Z. I. Borevich; A. S. Kondratiev); resolution: V. I. Mysovskikh, Dokl. Math. 60 (1999), 71-72, Kourovka Archive, Problem 14.50 (answer: Not always).
