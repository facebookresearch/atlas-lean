# R̃N-groups

A group $G$ is an $\tilde R N$-group when every quotient $G/N$ by a normal subgroup is an $RN$-group, that is, has a normal system all of whose jump factors are abelian. A normal system of a group is a set of subgroups that is a chain, contains the join and intersection of each of its subsets, and in which for every jump $A < B$ (no member strictly between) $A$ is normal in $B$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.17 (Sh. S. Kemkhadze); resolution: J. S. Wilson, On normal subgroups of SI-bar-groups, Arch. Math. 25 (1974), 574-577, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01238728.
