# Ñ-groups

A group $G$ is an $\tilde N$-group when every subgroup of $G$ is a member of some normal system of $G$. A normal system of a group $G$ is a set of subgroups of $G$ that is a chain under inclusion, contains the join and the intersection of each of its subsets (in particular $1$ and $G$), and in which, for every jump $A < B$ of members with no member strictly between, $A$ is normal in $B$. The definition follows Kargapolov and Merzljakov, Fundamentals of the Theory of Groups, §22.1.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.38 (Sh. S. Kemkhadze); resolution: Yu. I. Merzlyakov (1973), recorded in the Archive under Problem 1.38.
