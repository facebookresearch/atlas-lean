# Kourovka Problem 9.62 (embedding finite lattices in block lattices)

The proposition that every finite lattice $L$ embeds in the block lattice $C(G)$ of some finite group $G$: there is an injective map $f$ from $L$ to the blocks of $G$ with $f(x \wedge y) = f(x) \cap f(y)$ and $f(x \vee y)$ equal to the block join of $f(x)$ and $f(y)$. The blocks of a group $G$ are the empty set and the cosets $gN$ of normal subgroups $N$; ordered by inclusion they form the block lattice $C(G)$, with meet the intersection and join of two blocks the intersection of all blocks containing their union.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.62 (D. M. Smirnov).
