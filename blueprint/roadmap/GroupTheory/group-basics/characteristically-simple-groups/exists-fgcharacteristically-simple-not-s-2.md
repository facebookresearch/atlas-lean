# Wilson's finitely generated characteristically simple groups

There is an infinite finitely generated characteristically simple group $G$ that is not isomorphic to a direct power $S^I = \prod_{i \in I} S$ (the full direct product) of a simple group $S$ over any index set $I$; Wilson's construction carries out the task of Kourovka Problem 4.68. A characteristically simple group is a nontrivial group with no characteristic subgroups other than $1$ and itself.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.68 (J. Wiegold); resolution: J. S. Wilson, On characteristically simple groups, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 80 (1976), 19-35, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100052622.
