# Normal subgroup

Let $G$ be a group. A subgroup $N$ of $G$ is *normal* if $gng^{-1} \in N$ for every $n \in N$ and every $g \in G$, that is, if $gNg^{-1} \subseteq N$ for every $g \in G$, where $gNg^{-1} = \{gng^{-1} : n \in N\}$. Applying this condition to $g^{-1}$ gives $N \subseteq gNg^{-1}$, so a normal subgroup satisfies $gNg^{-1} = N$ for every $g \in G$.
