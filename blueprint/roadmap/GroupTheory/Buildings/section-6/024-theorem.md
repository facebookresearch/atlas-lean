# Theorem (Section 6.3)

The generalized Iwahori--Hecke algebra $H$ is $H \cong k[\Omega] \otimes' H_o$ with isomorphism given by the map $\operatorname{ch}_{B\sigma wB} \to [\sigma] \otimes \operatorname{ch}_{BwB}$.
