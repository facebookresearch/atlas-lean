# Proposition (Section 6.3)

The subalgebra $H_\Omega$ of $H$ consisting of functions supported on cosets of the form $B_o \sigma$ for $\sigma \in \Omega$ is isomorphic to the group algebra $k[\Omega]$, by the map $\operatorname{ch}_{B_o \sigma B_o} = \operatorname{ch}_{B_o \sigma} \to [\sigma] \in k[\Omega]$.
