# Lemma (Section 3.3)

Let $X$ be a thin chamber complex. Fix a chamber $C_o$ in $X$. Let $f \colon X \to X$ be a chamber complex map so that $f$ fixes $C_o$ pointwise. Let $\gamma$ be a non-stuttering gallery $C_o, C_1, \ldots, C_n$ starting at $C$. Then either $f\gamma = fC_o, \ldots, fC_n$ stutters, or else $f$ fixes every chamber $C_i$ pointwise.
