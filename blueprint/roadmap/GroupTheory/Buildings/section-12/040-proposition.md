# Proposition (Section 12.5)

The collection $H$ of affine hyperplanes $\eta_{\alpha,k}$ is locally finite and is stable under $W_a$. The affine Weyl group $W_a$ is the semi-direct product $W_a = W \ltimes \Lambda(\check{\Phi})$ of the group $W$ and the coroot lattice $\Lambda(\check{\Phi})$. The group generated by reflections in the hyperplanes in $H$ is just $W_a$.
