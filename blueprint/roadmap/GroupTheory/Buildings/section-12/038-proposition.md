# Proposition (Section 12.4)

Suppose that $\bar{W}$ is essential and that $W$ is indecomposable. Then the normal subgroup $W_1$ of translations in $W$ is a discrete subgroup of the group $T \cong \mathbb{R}^n$ of all translations of $X \cong \mathbb{R}^n$. Further, the quotient $X / W_1$ of $X$ by $W_1$ is compact.
