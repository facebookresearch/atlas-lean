# Theorem (Iwahori decomposition)

Given an element $b$ of the Iwahori subgroup $B$ of $\operatorname{GL}(n, k)$, there are uniquely-determined $u' \in N^{\mathrm{opp}} \cap B$, $m \in M \cap B$, and $u \in N \cap B$ so that $b = u' \cdot m \cdot u$. That is, $B$ decomposes as $B = (N^{\mathrm{opp}} \cap B) \cdot (M \cap B) \cdot (N \cap B)$ and uniquely so.
