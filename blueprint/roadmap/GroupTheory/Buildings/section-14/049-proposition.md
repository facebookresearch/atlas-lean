# Proposition (Section 14.7)

The following three conditions on a subset $E$ of $\tilde{G}$ are equivalent: (1) $E$ is contained in a finite union of double cosets $B\sigma wB$ with $w \in W$ and $\sigma \in \Omega$. (2) There is a point $x \in |X|$ so that $Ex = \{gx : g \in E\}$ is a bounded subset of the metric space $|X|$. (3) For every bounded subset $Y$ of the metric space $|X|$, the set $EY = \{gy : g \in E\}$ is bounded in $|X|$.
