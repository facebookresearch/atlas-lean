# Corollary (Section 1.7)

Given $w \in W$ and $s, t \in S$ with $\ell(sw) = \ell(w) + 1$ and $\ell(wt) = \ell(w) + 1$, either $\ell(swt) = \ell(w) + 2$ or $swt = w$.
