# Proposition (Section 15.5)

For a chamber $C$ in an apartment $A$, and for another chamber $D$, there is a unique label-preserving retraction $\rho = \rho_{D;\, C,A} \colon X \to A$ which sends $D$ to $C$, and so that the restriction of $\rho$ to any apartment $B$ containing $D$ is an isomorphism to $A$.
