# Lemma (Section 15.5)

Let $f \colon Y \to A$ be a strong isometry to an apartment $A$ in $X$. For any chamber $C'$ not in the image $f(Y)$ of $f$ but adjacent to a chamber in the image, there is a strong isometry $\tilde{g} \colon f(Y) \cup \{C'\} \to X$ extending the inverse $f^{-1} \colon f(Y) \to Y$.
