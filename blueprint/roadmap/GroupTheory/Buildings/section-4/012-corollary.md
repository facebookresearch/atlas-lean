# Corollary (Section 4.4)

For apartments $A$, $A'$ in a given apartment system with a chamber in common, there is a label-preserving chamber-complex isomorphism $f \colon A \to A'$ fixing $A \cap A'$ pointwise, and any isomorphism $f \colon A \to A'$ fixing $A \cap A'$ pointwise is label-preserving.
