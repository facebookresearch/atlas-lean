# Lemma (Section 20.3)

Let $V^+$ be an anisotropic quadratic space over $k$. Suppose that $k$ is a complete discretely-valued field. Suppose that $2$ is a unit in the valuation ring $o$. Then there is a unique maximal lattice on which $\langle\,,\rangle$ has values in the valuation ring $o$.
