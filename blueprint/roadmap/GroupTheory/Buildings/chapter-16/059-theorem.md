# Theorem (Chapter 16, Section 16.5)

Given a chamber $C$ in $X$ and a sector $\mathcal{C}$ in $X$ there is an apartment $B \in \mathcal{A}$ and a subsector $\mathcal{C}'$ of $\mathcal{C}$ so that both $C$ and $\mathcal{C}'$ are contained in $B$.
