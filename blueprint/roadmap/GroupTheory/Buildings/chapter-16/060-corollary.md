# Corollary (Chapter 16, Section 16.6)

Let $\eta$ be a wall in an apartment $A'$ containing a sector $\mathcal{C}'$. Then in one of the half-apartments cut out by $\eta$ there is a bound for the maximum distance of any point of $\mathcal{C}'$ from $\eta$, while in the other half-apartment there is no such bound.
