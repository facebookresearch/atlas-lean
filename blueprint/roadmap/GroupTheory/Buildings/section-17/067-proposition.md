# Proposition (Section 17.7)

Let $Y$ be a subset of $X$ which is contained in the union of finitely-many simplices in $X$. Suppose that $Y$ contains at least one chamber. Then the pointwise fixer $G_Y = \{g \in G : gy = y\ \forall\, y \in Y\}$ of $Y$ is open and compact in $G$.
