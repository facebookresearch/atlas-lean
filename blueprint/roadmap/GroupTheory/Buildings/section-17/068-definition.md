# Definition (Section 17.7)

A group $G$ is a topological group if it has a topology in which the multiplication and inverse operations are continuous. That is, the maps $G \times G \to G$ by $g \times h \to gh$ and $G \to G$ by $g \to g^{-1}$ are both continuous. Most often a topological group is also required to be Hausdorff and locally compact, as well.
