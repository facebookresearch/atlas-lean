# Theorem (Section 13.1)

The Tits cone $U$ is a convex cone in $V^*$, and every closed line segment in $U$ meets only finitely-many sets of the form $wF_I$. If $wF_I \cap F_J \neq \emptyset$, then $I = J$ and $w \in I$ (so actually $wF_J = F_I$ and $J = I$ and $w \in W_I$). The set $\overline{C}$ is a fundamental domain for $W$ acting on $U$. That is, given a point $u \in U$, the $W$-orbit $Wu$ of $u$ meets $\overline{C}$ in exactly one point.
