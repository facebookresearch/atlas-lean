# Theorem (Section 13.7)

Let $(W, S)$, $(W'', S'')$ be affine Coxeter systems with metrics as described. Let $\phi \colon \Sigma(W,S) \to \Sigma(W'',S'')$ be an isomorphism of simplicial complexes. Then the geometric realization $|\phi|$ is a similitude $|\phi| \colon |\Sigma(W,S)| \to |\Sigma(W'',S'')|$.
