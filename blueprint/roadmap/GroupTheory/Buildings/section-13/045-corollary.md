# Corollary (Section 13.7)

Let $(W, S)$, $(W'', S'')$ be affine Coxeter systems. Let $\phi \colon \Sigma(W,S) \to \Sigma(W'',S'')$ be an isomorphism of simplicial complexes. Normalize the metrics on the geometric realizations $|\Sigma(W,S)|$, $|\Sigma(W'',S'')|$ by dilating so that the diameter of a chamber is 1 in both cases. Then $|\phi| \colon |\Sigma(W,S)| \to |\Sigma(W'',S'')|$ is an isometry of the geometric realizations.
