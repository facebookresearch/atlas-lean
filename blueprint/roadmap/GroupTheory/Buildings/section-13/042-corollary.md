# Corollary (Section 13.3)

Let $M$ be an indecomposable real symmetric $n \times n$ matrix which is positive semi-definite. Assume further that $M_{ij} \leq 0$ for $i \neq j$. Let $N$ be the $(n{-}1) \times (n{-}1)$ matrix obtained by deleting the $i_o^{\text{th}}$ row and column from $M$. Then $N$ is positive-definite.
