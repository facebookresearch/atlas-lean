# Lemma (Section 13.4)

If $W_I$ is finite for subsets $I$ of $S$ strictly smaller than $S$, then the Tits cone $U$ associated to $(W, S)$ is $U = \{0\} \cup \{x \in V^* : \nu(x) < \infty\}$.
