# Theorem (Section 7.3, Extending isometries)

Let $U$, $W$ be subspaces of a non-degenerate space $V$. Every isometry $\varphi \colon U \to W$ extends to an isometry $\Phi \colon V \to V$. (That is, $\Phi$ restricted to $U$ is $\varphi$.) If $U$, $V$, $W$ are spaces so that $U \oplus V \cong U \oplus W$, then $V \cong W$.
