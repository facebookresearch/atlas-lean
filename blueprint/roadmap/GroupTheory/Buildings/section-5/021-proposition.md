# Proposition (Section 5.7)

The poset $Y$ of all special subsets of $G$ (with inclusion reversed) is isomorphic (as poset) to the poset $X$ of all proper parabolic subsets (with inclusion reversed), by the map $f \colon gP \to gPg^{-1}$. Further, this map respects the action of $G$ upon $X$ and $Y$.
