# Corollary (Section 5.3)

The parabolic subgroups of $G$ are exactly those subgroups containing $B$. Every parabolic subgroup is its own normalizer in $G$, and no two are conjugate in $G$. For a subgroup $P$ of $G$ containing $B$, let $W_P = (P \cap N)T / T$. Then we have $P = BW_PB$.
