# Proposition (Section 5.3)

Let $w = s_1 \cdots s_n$ be a reduced expression. Then the smallest subgroup of $G$ containing $BwB$ contains $s_i$ for all $i$. It is also generated by $B$ and $w^{-1}Bw$.
