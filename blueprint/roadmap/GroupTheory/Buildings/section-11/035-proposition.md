# Proposition (Section 11.3)

Let $Y$ be a totally isotropic $(n{-}1)$-dimensional subspace of $V$. Let $V_1$, $V_2$ be the two totally isotropic $n$-dimensional subspaces contained in $Y^{\perp}$. Then these two spaces $V_1$, $V_2$ are in distinct $\operatorname{SO}(n, n)$-orbits.
