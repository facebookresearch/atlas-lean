# Proposition (Section 16.10)

If $\varphi$ is an isometry of the geometric realization $|X|$ of the thick affine building $X$, then $\varphi$ preserves parallelism classes of geodesic rays, so it gives a well-defined map $\varphi_\infty$ on the geometric realization $|X_\infty|$ of the building at infinity, by $\varphi([x, \xi)) = [\varphi x, \varphi_\infty \xi)$ where $\xi$ is a point at infinity and $x$ is any point in $|X|$.
