# Definition (Signed permutation group)

Let $S_n$ be the permutation group on $n$ things, and let $H$ be the direct sum of $n$ copies of the group $\{\pm 1\}$. Let $\pi \in S_n$ act on $H$ by $(\varepsilon_1, \ldots, \varepsilon_n)^\pi = (\varepsilon_{\pi(1)}, \ldots, \varepsilon_{\pi(n)})$. Then the semi-direct product $S_n^{\pm} = H \rtimes S_n$ is the signed permutation group on $n$ things.
