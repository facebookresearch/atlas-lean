# Kourovka Problem 21.18 (exact factorizations and the generated subgroup)

This is the proposition that for every finite group $G$ and all subsets $A_1, A_2, A_3 \subseteq G$ such that the map $A_1 \times A_2 \times A_3 \to G$, $(a_1, a_2, a_3) \mapsto a_1 a_2 a_3$, is bijective, the size $|A_2|$ divides the order of the subgroup $\langle A_2 \rangle$ generated by $A_2$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.18.
