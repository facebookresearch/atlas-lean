# In an exact factorization the middle factor need not divide its generated subgroup

It is not the case that for every finite group $G$ and all subsets $A_1, A_2, A_3 \subseteq G$ such that the map $A_1 \times A_2 \times A_3 \to G$, $(a_1, a_2, a_3) \mapsto a_1 a_2 a_3$, is bijective, the size $|A_2|$ divides the order of the subgroup $\langle A_2 \rangle$ generated by $A_2$. Kourovka Problem 21.18 has a negative answer: Kabenyuk gives an exact factorization $A_4 = A \cdot B \cdot C$ with $|B| = 2$ and $|\langle B \rangle| = 3$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 21.18 (exact factorizations and the generated subgroup)](conjecture.md).
