# Exact factorization of a group by three subsets

For a group $G$ and subsets $A_1, A_2, A_3 \subseteq G$, the proposition that $G = A_1 A_2 A_3$ exactly: the map $A_1 \times A_2 \times A_3 \to G$, $(a_1, a_2, a_3) \mapsto a_1 a_2 a_3$, is a bijection.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.18.
