# Products of two locally cyclic torsion-free groups have a normal factor subgroup

Let a nontrivial group $G$ be the product $G = AB$ of two subgroups $A$ and $B$, each locally cyclic (any two elements lie in a common cyclic subgroup) and torsion-free. Then $A$ or $B$ contains a nontrivial subgroup that is normal in $G$. This proves Kourovka Problem 2.70(a).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.70 (N. F. Sesekin); resolution: D. I. Zaitsev, Algebra and Logic 19 (1980), 94-106.
