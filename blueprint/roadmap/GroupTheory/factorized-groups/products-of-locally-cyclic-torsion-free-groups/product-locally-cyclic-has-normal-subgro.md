# Kourovka Problem 2.70(a) (products of two locally cyclic torsion-free subgroups)

The proposition that whenever a nontrivial group $G$ is the product $G = AB$ (every $g$ is $ab$ with $a \in A$, $b \in B$) of two locally cyclic torsion-free subgroups $A$ and $B$, there is a nontrivial normal subgroup of $G$ contained in $A$ or in $B$. A subgroup is locally cyclic if any two of its elements lie in a common cyclic subgroup of it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.70 (N. F. Sesekin).
