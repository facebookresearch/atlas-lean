# Torsion-free subgroups

A subgroup $A$ of a group $G$ is torsion-free if no element of $A$ other than $1$ has finite order.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.70 (N. F. Sesekin).
