# Locally cyclic subgroups

A subgroup $A$ of a group $G$ is locally cyclic if any two elements $x, y \in A$ lie in a common cyclic subgroup $\langle z \rangle$ with $z \in A$; then every finitely generated subgroup of $A$ is cyclic.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.70 (N. F. Sesekin).
