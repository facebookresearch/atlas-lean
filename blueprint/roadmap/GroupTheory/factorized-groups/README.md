# Factorized groups

Groups that are products G = AB of two or more subgroups, finite or infinite: solubility, nilpotency and derived length of products of abelian, nilpotent, minimax or soluble factors (Itô and Kegel–Wielandt type theorems), triple factorizations, subnormal subgroups of factorized groups, and classes of groups closed under such products; supersoluble factorizations and products of maximal subgroups stay in Soluble and supersoluble finite groups, formations and Fitting classes in general in Formations and Fitting classes, and products of periodic subgroups in Periodic and locally finite groups.
