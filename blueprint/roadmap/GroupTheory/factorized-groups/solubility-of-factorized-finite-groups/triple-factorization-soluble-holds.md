# Finite groups with a triple soluble factorization are soluble

A finite group $G$ with soluble subgroups $A, B, C$ satisfying $G = AB = AC = BC$ is soluble. The proof uses the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.37 (V. S. Monakhov; question of H. Wielandt and O. H. Kegel); resolution: L. S. Kazarin, Factorizations of finite groups by solvable subgroups, Ukrainian Math. J. 43 (1991), 883-886, Theorem 3, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01058687.
