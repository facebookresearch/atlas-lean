# Kourovka Problem 8.48 (products of two soluble subgroups of odd index)

The proposition that a finite group $G = AB$ (every element a product $ab$) of two soluble subgroups $A$, $B$ of odd index is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.48 (V. S. Monakhov).
