# Kourovka Problem 6.37 (triple soluble factorizations)

The proposition that a finite group $G$ with soluble subgroups $A, B, C$ such that $G = AB = AC = BC$ (set products) is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.37 (V. S. Monakhov; question of H. Wielandt and O. H. Kegel).
