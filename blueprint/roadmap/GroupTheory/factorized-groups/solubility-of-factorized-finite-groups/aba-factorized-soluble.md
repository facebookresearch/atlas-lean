# Kourovka Problem 9.63 (finite groups of the form ABA)

The proposition that a finite group $G = ABA$ (every element is $aba'$ with $a, a' \in A$, $b \in B$), where $A$ is an abelian subgroup and $B$ a cyclic subgroup, is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.63 (Ya. P. Sysak).
