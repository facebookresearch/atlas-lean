# Kazarin's theorem on products of nearly nilpotent groups

A finite group $G = AB$ in which each factor $A$, $B$ contains a nilpotent subgroup of index at most $2$ is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.64 (L. A. Shemetkov); resolution: L. S. Kazarin, On the product of two groups that are close to being nilpotent, Math. USSR-Sb. 38 (1981), 47-59, https://doi.org/10.1070/SM1981v038n01ABEH001216.
