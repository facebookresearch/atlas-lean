# Finite products of two soluble subgroups of odd index are soluble

If a finite group $G = AB$ is the product of two soluble subgroups of odd index, then $G$ is soluble. The proof uses the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.48 (V. S. Monakhov); resolution: L. S. Kazarin, Factorizations of finite groups by solvable subgroups, Ukrainian Math. J. 43 (1991), 883-886, Theorem 3, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01058687.
