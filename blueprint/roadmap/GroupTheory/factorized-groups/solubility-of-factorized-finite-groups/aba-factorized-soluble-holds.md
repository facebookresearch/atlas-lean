# Finite groups ABA with A abelian and B cyclic are soluble

A finite group $G = ABA$ with $A$ an abelian subgroup and $B$ a cyclic subgroup is soluble. The proof uses the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.63 (Ya. P. Sysak); resolution: D. L. Zagorin and L. S. Kazarin, Abelian ABA-factorizations of finite groups, Dokl. Math. 53 (1996), no. 2, 237-239, Theorem 2.
