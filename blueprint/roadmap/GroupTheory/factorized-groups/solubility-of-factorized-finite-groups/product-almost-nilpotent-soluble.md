# Kourovka Problem 5.64 (products of two groups with nilpotent subgroups of index two)

The proposition that a finite group $G = AB$ (the set product of subgroups $A$ and $B$ is $G$), where each of $A$ and $B$ contains a nilpotent subgroup of index at most $2$, is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.64 (L. A. Shemetkov).
