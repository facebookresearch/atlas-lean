# Kourovka Problem 4.57(a) (cores in products of abelian minimax groups)

The proposition that if a non-trivial group $G = AB$ is the product of two abelian minimax subgroups, then $A_0B_0 \ne 1$, where $A_0 = \bigcap_{x \in G} A^x$ and $B_0$ are the normal cores (here $A_0B_0$ is the join $A_0 \vee B_0$). A subgroup is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = H_0 \le H_1 \le \cdots \le H_n$ ending in it, each term normal in the next, whose factors satisfy the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.57 (N. F. Sesekin).
