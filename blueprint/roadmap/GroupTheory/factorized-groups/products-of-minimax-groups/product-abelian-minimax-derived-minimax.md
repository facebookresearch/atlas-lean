# Kourovka Problem 4.57(b) (derived subgroups of products of abelian minimax groups)

The proposition that if a group $G = AB$ is the product of two abelian minimax subgroups, then the derived subgroup of $G$ is minimax. A subgroup is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = H_0 \le H_1 \le \cdots \le H_n$ ending in it, each term normal in the next, whose factors satisfy the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.57 (N. F. Sesekin).
