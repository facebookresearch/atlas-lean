# Kourovka Problem 9.54 (soluble products of minimax groups)

The proposition that a soluble group $G = HK$ (every element a product $hk$) of two minimax subgroups $H, K$ is minimax. A group is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = G_0 \trianglelefteq G_1 \trianglelefteq \cdots \trianglelefteq G_n = G$ each of whose factors satisfies the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.54 (D. J. S. Robinson).
