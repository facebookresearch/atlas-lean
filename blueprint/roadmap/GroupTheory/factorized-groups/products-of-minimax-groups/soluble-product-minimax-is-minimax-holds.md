# Soluble products of two minimax groups are minimax

If a soluble group $G = HK$ is the product of two minimax subgroups, then $G$ is minimax. A group is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = G_0 \trianglelefteq G_1 \trianglelefteq \cdots \trianglelefteq G_n = G$ each of whose factors satisfies the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.54 (D. J. S. Robinson); resolution: J. S. Wilson, Soluble products of minimax groups, and nearly surjective derivations, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 53 (1988), no. 3, 297-318, https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-4049(88)90129-6; Ya. P. Sysak, Radical modules over groups of finite rank, Inst. of Math. Acad. Sci. Ukrain. SSR, Kiev, 1989.
