# Minimax subgroups

A subgroup $K$ of a group $G$ is minimax when it has a finite series
$1 = H_0 \le H_1 \le \dots \le H_n = K$ of subgroups, each normal in the next, such that every factor
$H_{i+1}/H_i$ satisfies the maximal condition or the minimal condition on subgroups; that is, the
interval $[H_i, H_{i+1}]$ of the subgroup lattice has no infinite strictly ascending chain, or no
infinite strictly descending chain. This is the notion of [minimax groups](../../profinite-and-topological-groups/finite-images-of-minimax-groups/is-minimax.md)
for a subgroup of a group.
