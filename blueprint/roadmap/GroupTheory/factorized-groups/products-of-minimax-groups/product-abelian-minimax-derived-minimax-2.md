# The derived subgroup of a product of two abelian minimax groups is minimax

If a group $G = AB$ is the product of two abelian minimax subgroups, then the derived subgroup $G'$ is minimax. A subgroup is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = H_0 \le H_1 \le \cdots \le H_n$ ending in it, each term normal in the next, whose factors satisfy the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.

Sesekin); resolution: D. I. Zaitsev, Products of abelian groups, Algebra and Logic 19 (1980), 94-106, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01669835.
