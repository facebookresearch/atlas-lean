# Products of two abelian minimax groups have a non-trivial normal core

If a non-trivial group $G = AB$ is the product of two abelian minimax subgroups $A$ and $B$, then the normal cores $A_0 = \bigcap_{x} A^x$ and $B_0 = \bigcap_x B^x$ are not both trivial: $A_0B_0 \ne 1$. A subgroup is minimax when it has a finite series $1 = H_0 \le H_1 \le \cdots \le H_n$ ending in it, each term normal in the next, whose factors satisfy the maximal or the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.57 (N. F. Sesekin); resolution: D. I. Zaitsev, Products of abelian groups, Algebra and Logic 19 (1980), 94-106, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01669835.
