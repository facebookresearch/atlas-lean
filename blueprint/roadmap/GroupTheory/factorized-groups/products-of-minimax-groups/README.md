# Products of minimax groups

Groups $G = AB$ that are products of two abelian minimax subgroups: if $G$ is nontrivial, the normal
cores of $A$ and $B$ are not both trivial, and the derived subgroup of $G$ is minimax (Kourovka
Notebook, Problem 4.57, proved by Zaitsev).
