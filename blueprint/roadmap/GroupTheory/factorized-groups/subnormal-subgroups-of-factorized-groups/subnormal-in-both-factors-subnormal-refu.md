# Subnormal in both factors but not in the product

There are $G$, $A$, $B$ and $N$ such that $G = AB$ is a group that is the product of subgroups $A$ and $B$ (every element is a product $ab$), and $N \le A \cap B$ is subnormal in $A$ and in $B$ (reachable by a finite chain of subgroups, each normal in the next), but $N$ is not subnormal in $G$; Kourovka Problem 9.64 has a negative answer. Casolo and Dardano's example has $N$ cyclic and subnormal of defect $2$ in both factors, but not even serial in $G$; such a group is infinite. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 9.64 (subnormal subgroups of both factors)](subnormal-in-both-factors-subnormal.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.64 (Ya. P. Sysak); resolution: C. Casolo and U. Dardano, Subnormality in the join of two subgroups, J. Group Theory 7 (2004), 507-520, https://doi.org/10.1515/jgth.2004.7.4.507.
