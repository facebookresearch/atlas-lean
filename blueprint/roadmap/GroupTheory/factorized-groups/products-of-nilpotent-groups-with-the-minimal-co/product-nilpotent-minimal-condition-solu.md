# Kourovka Problem 2.69(a) (products of nilpotent groups with the minimal condition)

The proposition that a group $G = AB$, product of two nilpotent subgroups $A, B$ each satisfying the minimal condition on subgroups, is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.69 (N. F. Sesekin).
