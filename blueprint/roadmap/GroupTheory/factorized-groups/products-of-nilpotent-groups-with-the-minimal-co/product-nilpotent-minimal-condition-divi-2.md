# Divisible parts of nilpotent factors with the minimal condition commute

If $G = AB$ with $A$ and $B$ nilpotent subgroups satisfying the minimal condition on subgroups, then every element of the divisible part of $A$ commutes with every element of the divisible part of $B$. The hypotheses: $G = AB$ (every element a product $ab$) with $A$, $B$ nilpotent subgroups each satisfying the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.69 (N. F. Sesekin); resolution: N. S. Chernikov, Soviet Math. Dokl. 21 (1980), 701-703.
