# The divisible part of a subgroup

For a subgroup $A$ of a group $G$, the subgroup generated by all divisible subgroups $S \le A$, where $S$ is divisible when every $s \in S$ has an $n$-th root in $S$ for every $n \ge 1$. For a nilpotent group with the minimal condition this is its largest divisible subgroup.
