# Kourovka Problem 2.69(b) (divisible parts of the factors commute)

The proposition that under the same hypotheses the divisible parts of $A$ and $B$ commute elementwise: $xy = yx$ for $x$ in the divisible part of $A$ and $y$ in that of $B$. The hypotheses: $G = AB$ (every element a product $ab$) with $A$, $B$ nilpotent subgroups each satisfying the minimal condition on subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.69 (N. F. Sesekin).
