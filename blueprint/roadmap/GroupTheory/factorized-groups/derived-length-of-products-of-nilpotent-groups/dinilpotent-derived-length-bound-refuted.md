# Dinilpotent groups of large derived length

There are a finite group $G$ and natural numbers $\alpha$, $\beta$ such that $G$ is a finite group with $G = AB$ (every element is a product $ab$ with $a \in A$, $b \in B$) for subgroups $A$ and $B$ of nilpotency class at most $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively, that is, $\gamma_{\alpha+1}(A) = 1$ and $\gamma_{\beta+1}(B) = 1$, but $G^{(\alpha+\beta)} \ne 1$; Kourovka Problem 5.17 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.17 (derived length of dinilpotent groups)](dinilpotent-derived-length-bound.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.17 (O. H. Kegel); resolution: J. Cossey and S. E. Stonehewer, On the derived length of finite dinilpotent groups, Bull. London Math. Soc. 30 (1998), 247-250, https://doi.org/10.1112/S0024609397004050.
