# Group Theory

Group theory studies symmetry through groups, actions, subgroups, quotients, and structural decompositions.

No single field dominates the subject; the largest,
[characters of finite groups](characters-of-finite-groups/README.md), has about an eighth of its articles. Its largest
topic leads up to [Burnside's $p^a q^b$ theorem](characters-of-finite-groups/burnside-s-p-a-q-b-theorem/README.md),
that a group of order $p^a q^b$ is solvable, and the field also has the
[Frobenius determinant theorem](characters-of-finite-groups/frobenius-group-determinant/README.md) on the
factorization of the group determinant and the
[McKay conjecture](characters-of-finite-groups/mckay-conjecture/README.md), the Cabanes–Späth theorem that a finite
group and the normalizer of a Sylow $p$-subgroup have equally many irreducible characters of degree prime to $p$. The
theory of [finite simple groups](finite-simple-groups/README.md) includes the Gorenstein–Walter theorem on simple
groups with dihedral Sylow $2$-subgroups, and [soluble finite groups](soluble-finite-groups/README.md), the second
largest field, are studied through [formations and Fitting classes](soluble-finite-groups/formations/README.md).

On the infinite side, [geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory/README.md) covers growth, amenability,
hyperbolicity, Thompson's group $F$ and
[property (T)](geometric-group-theory/property-t-for-automorphism-groups-of-free-group/README.md) for
$\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ with $n \ge 6$. [Free groups](free-groups/README.md) appear with their subgroups and
automorphisms, and [presentations and decision problems](presentations-and-decision-problems/README.md) include
[braid and Artin groups](presentations-and-decision-problems/braid-and-artin/README.md), a finitely presented group
with unsolvable [word problem](presentations-and-decision-problems/word-problem-for-groups/README.md) (Novikov and
Boone) and finitely presented infinite simple groups. [Permutation groups](permutation-groups/README.md) run from
Frobenius groups to highly homogeneous and cofinitary groups on infinite sets, and in
[group rings](group-rings/README.md) Gardam's unit in $\mathbb{F}_2[P]$, for a torsion-free group $P$, disproves
[Kaplansky's unit conjecture](group-rings/kaplansky-s-unit-conjecture/README.md).
