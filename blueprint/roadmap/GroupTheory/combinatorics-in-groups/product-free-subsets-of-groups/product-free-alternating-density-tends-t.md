# AIM additive combinatorics Problem 4.3 (product-free subsets of alternating groups)

The proposition that product-free subsets of the alternating groups have density tending to $0$: for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N$ such that for every $n \ge N$ and every product-free subset $P$ of the alternating group $A_n$ on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, $|P| \le \varepsilon\,|A_n|$. A subset $P$ of a group is product-free if $xy \notin P$ for all $x, y \in P$ (with $x = y$ allowed).

Source: AIM workshop Recent trends in additive combinatorics (2004), problem list collected by E. Croot and V. F. Lev, https://aimath.org/WWN/additivecomb/additivecomb.pdf, Problem 4.3 (V. Sós; conjecture of B. Green).
