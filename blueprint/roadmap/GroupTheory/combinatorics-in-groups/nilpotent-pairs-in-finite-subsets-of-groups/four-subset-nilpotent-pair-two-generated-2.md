# Kourovka Problem 17.3 has a negative answer

There is a group $G$ in which every $4$-element
subset contains two distinct elements generating a nilpotent subgroup, but some subgroup of $G$
generated by two elements is not nilpotent.

The Kourovka Archive answers 17.3 "No, not always", citing Sozutov (2019).

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 17.3 (A. Abdollahi); resolution: A. I. Sozutov, Siberian Math. J. 60 (2019), 1099-1100, Kourovka Archive, Problem 17.3 (No, not always).
