# Sambale's bound on complements of unions of cosets

Let $G$ be a finite group, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and $C_1, \dots, C_n$ subsets of $G$ each of which is a left coset $gH$ or a right coset $Hg$ of some subgroup $H$. If $C_1 \cup \cdots \cup C_n \ne G$, then the complement $A = G \setminus (C_1 \cup \cdots \cup C_n)$ satisfies $|G| \le 2^n |A|$. For $n = 0$ the complement is $G$. Sambale proves more: $G$ is a union of at most $2^n$ left translates of $A$. This answers Kourovka Problem 21.115 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.115, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49; resolution: B. Sambale, On the complement of a union of cosets, arXiv:2609.09052 (2026), Theorem 1.
