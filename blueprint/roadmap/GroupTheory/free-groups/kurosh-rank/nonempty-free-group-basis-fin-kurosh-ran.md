# Kurosh rank formula

Let $G_0$ and $G_1$ be finite groups and $G = G_0 * G_1$ their free product. Let $H \le G$ be a subgroup of finite index $m = [G : H]$ that meets every conjugate of each factor trivially: for $i \in \{0, 1\}$, $g \in G$ and $x \in G_i$, $g^{-1} x g \in H$ implies $x = 1$. Then $H$ is a free group with a basis of cardinality

$$\left(1 + m - \left\lfloor \frac{m}{|G_0|} \right\rfloor\right) - \left\lfloor \frac{m}{|G_1|} \right\rfloor,$$

computed in $\mathbb{N}$ with truncated subtraction.
