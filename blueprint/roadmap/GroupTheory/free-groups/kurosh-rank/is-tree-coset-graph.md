# Is Tree coset Graph

Let $G_0$ and $G_1$ be groups and $G = G_0 * G_1$ their free product. Form the graph whose vertices are the left cosets $g G_0$ and $g G_1$ ($g \in G$), the two coset spaces taken disjointly, and in which $g G_0$ and $g G_1$ are joined by an edge for every $g \in G$; that is, two vertices are adjacent when they are cosets of different factors and share a representative $g$. This graph is a tree: it is connected and has no cycles.
