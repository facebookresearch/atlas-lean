# Automorphisms of free soluble groups need not lift

For some natural numbers $m$ and $n$ there is an automorphism of $F_m/F_m^{(n)}$ that is induced by no automorphism of $F_m/F_m^{(n+1)}$, where an automorphism $\varphi$ of $F_m/F_m^{(n)}$ is induced by an automorphism $\psi$ of $F_m/F_m^{(n+1)}$ if for all $u, v \in F_m$, $\psi(uF_m^{(n+1)}) = vF_m^{(n+1)}$ implies $\varphi(uF_m^{(n)}) = vF_m^{(n)}$. The free soluble group $F_m/F_m^{(n)}$ is the quotient of the free group $F_m$ of rank $m$ by the $n$-th term of its derived series. This answers Kourovka Problem 5.49(b) negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.49(b) (lifting automorphisms of free soluble groups)](free-soluble-automorphisms-lift.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.49 (V. N. Remeslennikov); resolution: V. Shpilrain, Automorphisms of F/R' groups, Internat. J. Algebra Comput. 1 (1991), 177-184.
