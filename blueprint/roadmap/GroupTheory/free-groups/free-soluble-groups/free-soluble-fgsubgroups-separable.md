# Kourovka Problem 2.19 (finitely separable subgroups of free soluble groups)

The proposition that for all natural numbers $r$ and $l$, every finitely generated subgroup of the free soluble group $F_r/F_r^{(l)}$ is finitely separable. The free soluble group $F_r/F_r^{(l)}$ is the quotient of the free group $F_r$ of rank $r$ by the $l$-th term of its derived series. A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is finitely separable if every element $g \notin H$ lies outside some subgroup of finite index containing $H$; equivalently, $H$ is the intersection of the subgroups of finite index that contain it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.19 (M. I. Kargapolov).
