# Free soluble groups

For natural numbers $r$ and $l$, the free soluble group of rank $r$ and derived length at most $l$ is the quotient $F_r/F_r^{(l)}$ of the free group $F_r$ on $r$ generators by the $l$-th term $F_r^{(l)}$ of its derived series ($F_r^{(0)} = F_r$, $F_r^{(i+1)} = [F_r^{(i)}, F_r^{(i)}]$). For $l = 0$ it is trivial and for $l = 1$ it is free abelian of rank $r$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.89 (A. L. Shmel'kin).
