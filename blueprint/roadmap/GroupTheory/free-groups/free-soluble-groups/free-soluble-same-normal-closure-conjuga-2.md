# Equal normal closures do not force conjugacy in free soluble groups

There are natural numbers $r$ and $l$ and elements $a, b$ of the free soluble group $F_r/F_r^{(l)}$ with the same normal closure such that neither $b$ nor $b^{-1}$ is conjugate to $a$. This answers Kourovka Problem 1.89 negatively; the analogous statement for free groups holds (Magnus). It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 1.89 (elements with equal normal closures in free soluble groups)](free-soluble-same-normal-closure-conjuga.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.89 (A. L. Shmel'kin); resolution: A. L. Shmel'kin, Algebra i Logika 6, no. 2 (1967), 95-109 (Russian).
