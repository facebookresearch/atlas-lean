# Finitely separable subgroups

A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is finitely separable if for every $g \in G$ with $g \notin H$ there is a subgroup $K$ of finite index in $G$ with $H \le K$ and $g \notin K$. Equivalently, $H$ is the intersection of the subgroups of finite index containing it.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.19 (M. I. Kargapolov).
