# Kourovka Problem 5.49(a) (finite generation of automorphism groups of free soluble groups)

The proposition that for all natural numbers $m$ and $n$ the automorphism group of the free soluble group $F_m/F_m^{(n)}$ is finitely generated. The free soluble group $F_m/F_m^{(n)}$ is the quotient of the free group $F_m$ of rank $m$ by the $n$-th term of its derived series.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.49 (V. N. Remeslennikov).
