# Kourovka Problem 1.89 (elements with equal normal closures in free soluble groups)

The proposition that for all natural numbers $r$ and $l$ and all elements $a, b$ of the free soluble group $F_r/F_r^{(l)}$ whose normal closures coincide, there is $x$ with $b = x^{-1}ax$ or $b^{-1} = x^{-1}ax$. The free soluble group $F_r/F_r^{(l)}$ is the quotient of the free group $F_r$ of rank $r$ by the $l$-th term of its derived series.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.89 (A. L. Shmel'kin).
