# Relative orders in free groups

The order of an element relative to a subgroup is the least positive exponent whose power of the element lies in the subgroup, or zero if there is none, and the relative spectrum is the set of these orders; here for free groups and direct products of free groups.

- [Relative spectrum membership is undecidable in a direct product of two free groups](spectrum-membership-undecidable-free-times-free.md)
