# The Hanna Neumann conjecture

The proposition that for all nontrivial finitely generated subgroups $A$ and $B$ of a free group $F(\alpha)$ on an arbitrary basis,

$$\operatorname{rk}(A \cap B) - 1 \le (\operatorname{rk} A - 1)(\operatorname{rk} B - 1),$$

where $\operatorname{rk}$ is the least number of generators and the inequality is in $\mathbb{Z}$ (so a trivial intersection gives $-1$ on the left); $A \cap B$ is finitely generated by Howson's theorem.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 3.1 (Hanna Neumann Conjecture), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
