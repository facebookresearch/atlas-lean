# Hanna Neumann inequality for intersections of subgroups of free groups

For all nontrivial finitely generated subgroups $A$ and $B$ of a free group,

$$\operatorname{rk}(A \cap B) - 1 \le (\operatorname{rk} A - 1)(\operatorname{rk} B - 1),$$

where $\operatorname{rk}$ is the least number of generators and the inequality is in $\mathbb{Z}$. This is the Hanna Neumann conjecture, proved by Friedman and independently by Mineyev (both even prove the strengthened form).

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 3.1 (Hanna Neumann Conjecture), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf; resolution: J. Friedman, Sheaves on graphs, their homological invariants, and a proof of the Hanna Neumann conjecture, Mem. Amer. Math. Soc. 233 (2015), no. 1100, arXiv:1105.0129, Theorem 2.1; I. Mineyev, Submultiplicativity and the Hanna Neumann conjecture, Ann. of Math. 175 (2012), 393-414.
