# Timoshenko's question on equally distributed elements (Kourovka 19.94)

The statement that for every natural number $n$ and all words $w_1, w_2$ in the free group $F_n$ of
rank $n$, the words $w_1$ and $w_2$ have the same distribution on every finite metabelian group $G$,
that is, for every $g \in G$ the equations $w_1(a_1, \dots, a_n) = g$ and $w_2(a_1, \dots, a_n) = g$
have equally many solutions $(a_1, \dots, a_n) \in G^n$, if and only if some automorphism of the
free metabelian group $M_n = F_n / F_n''$ maps the image of $w_1$ in $M_n$ to the image of $w_2$.
