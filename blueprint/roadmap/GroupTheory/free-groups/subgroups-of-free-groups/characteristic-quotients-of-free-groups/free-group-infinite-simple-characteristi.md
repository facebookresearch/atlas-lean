# Kourovka Problem 6.45 (infinite simple characteristic quotients of free groups)

The proposition that for every natural number $n \ge 2$ the free group $F_n$ of rank $n$ has a characteristic subgroup $N$ (normal, and mapped onto itself by every automorphism of $F_n$) such that $F_n/N$ is infinite and simple.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 6.45 (J. Wiegold).
