# Infinite simple characteristic quotients of free groups

For every natural number $n \ge 2$, the free group $F_n$ of rank $n$ has a characteristic subgroup $N$ such that the quotient $F_n/N$ is infinite and simple. Coulon and Fournier-Facio show that there are even continuum many pairwise non-isomorphic such quotients, each generated by two elements. This answers Kourovka Problem 6.45.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 6.45 (J. Wiegold); resolution: R. Coulon and F. Fournier-Facio, Infinite simple characteristic quotients, arXiv:2312.11684, Theorem 1.1 (Corollary 4.2).
