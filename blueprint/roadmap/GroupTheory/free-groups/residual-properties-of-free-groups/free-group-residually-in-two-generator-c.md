# Kourovka Problem 6.18 (free groups residually in a class of 2-generator groups)

The proposition that for every family $(K_i)_{i \in I}$ of groups, each generated by two elements, that generates the variety of all groups (for every nontrivial element $w$ of the free group $F_{\mathbb{N}}$ on countably many generators there are $i$ and a homomorphism $F_{\mathbb{N}} \to K_i$ not sending $w$ to $1$), the free group $F_2$ of rank $2$ is residually in the family in the sense of quotients: for every $w \ne 1$ in $F_2$ there are $i$ and a surjective homomorphism $F_2 \to K_i$ that does not send $w$ to $1$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.18 (V. M. Levchuk).
