# Domination relation between Dehn functions

For functions $f, g : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, $f \preceq g$ means that there is a natural number $C > 0$ with $f(m) \le C\,g(Cm + C) + Cm + C$ for every $m \in \mathbb{N}$. Two functions are equivalent when each dominates the other; Dehn functions of different finite presentations of the same group are equivalent.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 20 (M. Bridson and K. Vogtmann), Question 7.1.
