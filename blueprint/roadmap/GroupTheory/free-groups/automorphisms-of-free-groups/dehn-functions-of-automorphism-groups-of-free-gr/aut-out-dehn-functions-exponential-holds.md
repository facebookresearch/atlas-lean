# Exponential Dehn functions of Aut(F_n) and Out(F_n)

For every $n > 3$, the groups $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n)$ and $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ are finitely presented, and every finite presentation of either group has Dehn function $\delta$ with $\delta \preceq 2^m$ and $2^m \preceq \delta$. Here $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n)$ by the (normal) subgroup of inner automorphisms, the area of a relation $w$ is the least number of factors $u r^{\pm 1} u^{-1}$ ($r \in R$, $u$ in the free group) whose product is $w$, and $\delta(m)$ is the largest area of an element of the normal closure of $R$ of reduced length at most $m$, and $f \preceq g$ means that some integer $C > 0$ satisfies $f(m) \le C\,g(Cm + C) + Cm + C$ for all $m \in \mathbb{N}$. Bridson and Vogtmann prove this for all $n \ge 3$.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 20 (M. Bridson and K. Vogtmann), Question 7.1; resolution: M. R. Bridson and K. Vogtmann, The Dehn functions of Out(F_n) and Aut(F_n), Ann. Inst. Fourier 62 (2012), 1811-1817, arXiv:1011.1506, Theorem 1.
