# Area of a relation in a group presentation

For a set $R$ of relators in the free group $F(\alpha)$ and $w \in F(\alpha)$, the area of $w$ is the least natural number $N$ such that $w$ is a product of $N$ conjugates $u r^{\pm 1} u^{-1}$ with $r \in R$ and $u \in F(\alpha)$. If $w$ is not such a product (it does not lie in the normal closure of $R$), the area is $0$ by convention.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 20 (M. Bridson and K. Vogtmann), Question 7.1.
