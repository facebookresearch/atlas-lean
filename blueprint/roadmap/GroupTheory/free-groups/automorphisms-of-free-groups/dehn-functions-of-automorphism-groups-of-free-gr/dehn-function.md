# Dehn function of a group presentation

For a set $R$ of relators in the free group $F(\alpha)$, the Dehn function $\delta : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ of the presentation $\langle \alpha \mid R \rangle$ sends $m$ to the largest area of an element $w$ of the normal closure of $R$ whose reduced word has length at most $m$, where the area of $w$ is the least number of conjugates $u r^{\pm 1} u^{-1}$ ($r \in R$) with product $w$. For finite $\alpha$ the set of such $w$ is finite; in general the value is the supremum in $\mathbb{N}$, which is $0$ for an unbounded set.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 20 (M. Bridson and K. Vogtmann), Question 7.1.
