# Goldman's conjecture (ergodicity of Aut(F_n) on Hom(F_n, G))

The proposition that for every $n \ge 3$ and every a compact connected Lie group (a smooth Lie group modelled on a finite-dimensional real normed space, compact and connected) with a Haar measure $\mu$, and $G^n$ carries the product measure $\mu^n$, every measurable subset $A \subseteq G^n$ with $\varphi^{-1}(A) = A$ for every automorphism $\varphi$ of $F_n$ (acting as below) satisfies $\mu^n(A) = 0$ or $\mu^n(G^n \setminus A) = 0$. An automorphism $\varphi$ of the free group $F_n$ with basis $e_1, \dots, e_n$ acts on $G^n = \mathrm{Hom}(F_n, G)$ on the right by precomposition: $(g_i)_i \mapsto (\rho_g(\varphi(e_i)))_i$, where $\rho_g : F_n \to G$ is the homomorphism with $\rho_g(e_i) = g_i$. This is equivalent to ergodicity of $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ on $\mathrm{Hom}(F_n, G)/G$.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 13 (W. Goldman), Conjecture 2.3.
