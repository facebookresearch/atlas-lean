# Right action of Aut(F_n) on Hom(F_n, G)

For a group $G$, an automorphism $\varphi$ of the free group $F_n$ on the basis $e_1, \dots, e_n$ and a tuple $g = (g_1, \dots, g_n) \in G^n$, the tuple $g \cdot \varphi \in G^n$ has $i$-th entry $\rho_g(\varphi(e_i))$, where $\rho_g : F_n \to G$ is the homomorphism sending $e_j$ to $g_j$. Identifying $G^n$ with $\mathrm{Hom}(F_n, G)$, this is precomposition $\rho \mapsto \rho \circ \varphi$, a right action: $g \cdot (\varphi\psi) = (g \cdot \varphi) \cdot \psi$.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 13 (W. Goldman), Conjecture 2.3.
