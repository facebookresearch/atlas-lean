# Vanishing of the stable rational homology of Out(F_n)

For every $k \ge 1$ there is $N$ such that the group homology $H_k(\mathrm{Out}(F_n); \mathbb{Q})$ with trivial rational coefficients vanishes for all $n \ge N$; in fact $n > 2k + 3$ suffices. $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the automorphism group of the free group $F_n$ by the subgroup of inner automorphisms. Galatius proves $H_k(\mathrm{Aut}(F_n); \mathbb{Q}) = 0$ for $n > 2k + 1$, and by Hatcher, Vogtmann and Wahl the map $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n) \to \mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is an isomorphism on $H_k$ for $n > 2k + 3$.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 21, Conjecture 4.9, p. 366; resolution: S. Galatius, Stable homology of automorphism groups of free groups, Ann. of Math. 173 (2011), 705-768, arXiv:math/0610216, Corollary 1.2 and Theorem 1.4.
