# Stable rational homology of Out(F_n) (Farb Ch. 21, Conjecture 4.9)

The proposition that the stable rational homology of $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ vanishes: for every $k \ge 1$ there is $N$ such that $H_k(\mathrm{Out}(F_n); \mathbb{Q}) = 0$ for all $n \ge N$, with trivial coefficients $\mathbb{Q}$. $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the automorphism group of the free group $F_n$ by the subgroup of inner automorphisms.

Source: B. Farb (ed.), Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics (2006), Ch. 21, Conjecture 4.9, p. 366.
