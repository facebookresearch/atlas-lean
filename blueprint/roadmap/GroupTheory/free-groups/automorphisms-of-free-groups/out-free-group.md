# Outer automorphism group of a free group

For a natural number $n$, let $F_n$ be the free
group on $n$ generators. Its outer automorphism group $\mathrm{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient of the
automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n)$ by the normal subgroup generated by the inner
automorphisms. Since the inner automorphisms already form a normal subgroup $\mathrm{Inn}(F_n)$,
this is $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n) / \mathrm{Inn}(F_n)$.
