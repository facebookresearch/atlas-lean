# IA-automorphism group of a free group is not linear

For every $n \ge 3$, the group $\mathrm{IA}(F_n)$ of automorphisms of the free group $F_n$ of rank
$n$ that act trivially on its abelianization is not linear: there are no field $K$, natural number
$m$ and injective homomorphism $\mathrm{IA}(F_n) \to \mathrm{GL}_m(K)$.

This answers Kourovka Problem 15.10 negatively: the Kourovka Archive answers "No";
$\mathrm{IA}(F_n)$ is not linear for $n \ge 5$ (Pettet) and for every $n \ge 3$ (Bardakov and
Mikhailov).
