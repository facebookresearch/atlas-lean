# Farb's question on Property FA for finite-index subgroups of $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$

The proposition that for every natural number $n \ge 4$, every subgroup of finite index in the
automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ of the free group $F_n$ on $n$ generators has Serre's
Property FA.

## Sources

- M. R. Bridson and K. Vogtmann, *Automorphism groups of free groups, surface groups and free abelian groups*, in B. Farb (ed.), *Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics*, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 74, Amer. Math. Soc., 2006, Question 2.8, https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~farb/papers/mcgbook.pdf.
