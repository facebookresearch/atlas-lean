# Farb's positive first Betti number question for $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n)$

The proposition that
for every natural number $n > 3$, the automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(F_n)$ of the free group
$F_n$ on $n$ generators has a finite-index subgroup $H$ whose first Betti number, the
$\mathbb{Z}$-rank of the abelianization of $H$, is positive.

## Sources

- M. R. Bridson and K. Vogtmann, *Automorphism groups of free groups, surface groups and free abelian groups*, in B. Farb (ed.), *Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics*, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 74, Amer. Math. Soc., 2006, Question 2.5, https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~farb/papers/mcgbook.pdf.
