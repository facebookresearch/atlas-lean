# Khukhro's counterexample to Kourovka Problem 8.81(a)

It is not the case that there is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for every prime $p$, every finite $p$-group $G$, every automorphism $\alpha$ of $G$ of order $p$ and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|C_G(\alpha)| \le n$, the group $G$ has a subgroup of nilpotency class at most $2$ and index at most $f(n)$. Here $C_G(\alpha)$ is the set of fixed points of $\alpha$, and class at most $c$ means that the $c$-th term of the upper central series of the subgroup is the whole subgroup. Khukhro showed that no such bound exists even for metabelian $p$-groups, so Kourovka Problem 8.81(a) has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 8.81(a) (class-two subgroups of bounded index)](class-two-subgroup-bounded-index.md).
