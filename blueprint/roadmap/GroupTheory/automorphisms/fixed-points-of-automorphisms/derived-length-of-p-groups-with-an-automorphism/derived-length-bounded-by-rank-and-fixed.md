# Shalev and Khukhro's question on the derived length of $p$-groups with an automorphism

The
statement that there is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for
every prime $p$, every finite $p$-group $G$, all natural numbers $r$ and $m$ such that $G$ has
sectional rank at most $r$ (every subgroup of $G$ is generated by at most $r$ elements), and every
automorphism $\varphi$ of $G$ with exactly $m$ fixed points, the derived length of $G$ is at most
$f(r, m)$, that is, the term $G^{(f(r, m))}$ of the derived series of $G$ is trivial, where
$G^{(0)} = G$ and $G^{(i+1)} = [G^{(i)}, G^{(i)}]$.

This is Kourovka Notebook Problem 13.56 (A. Shalev; E. I. Khukhro).
