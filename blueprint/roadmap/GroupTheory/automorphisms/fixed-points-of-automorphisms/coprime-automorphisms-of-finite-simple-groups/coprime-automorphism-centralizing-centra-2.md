# A coprime automorphism of Sz(8) centralizing a centralizer

There are a finite simple group $G$, an element $\tau \in G$ of prime order and an automorphism $\alpha \ne 1$ of $G$ of order coprime to $|G|$ that fixes every element of $C_G(\tau)$; Kourovka Problem 4.23 has a negative answer. Podufalov's example is the Suzuki group $G = \mathrm{Sz}(8)$ of order $29120$, with $\tau$ of order $5$ and $\alpha$ of order $3$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 4.23 (coprime automorphisms centralizing a centralizer)](coprime-automorphism-centralizing-centra.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.23 (G. Glauberman); resolution: N. D. Podufalov, letter of 3 September 1975, recorded in the Archive under Problem 4.23.
