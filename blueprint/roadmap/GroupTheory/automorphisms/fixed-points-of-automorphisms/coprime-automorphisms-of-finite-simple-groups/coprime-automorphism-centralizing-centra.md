# Kourovka Problem 4.23 (coprime automorphisms centralizing a centralizer)

The proposition that whenever $G$ is a finite simple group, $\tau \in G$ has prime order, and $\alpha$ is an automorphism of $G$ whose order is coprime to $|G|$ and which fixes every element of $C_G(\tau)$, then $\alpha = 1$. Simple groups include the cyclic groups of prime order, where the conclusion holds trivially.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.23 (G. Glauberman); resolution: N. D. Podufalov, letter of 3 September 1975, recorded in the Archive under Problem 4.23.
