# Coprime automorphism groups have a faithfully acted-on soluble invariant subgroup

If $A$ is a group of automorphisms of a finite group $G$ with $|A|$ and $|G|$ coprime, then $G$ has an $A$-invariant soluble subgroup $H$ on which $A$ acts faithfully ($C_A(H) = 1$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.22 (G. Glauberman; J. G. Thompson); resolution: S. A. Syskin, Automorphisms of finite groups, Siberian Math. J. 32 (1991), no. 6, 1034-1037, Theorem 1, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00971211.
