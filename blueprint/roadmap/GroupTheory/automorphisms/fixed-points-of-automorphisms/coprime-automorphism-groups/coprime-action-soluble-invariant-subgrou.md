# Kourovka Problem 4.22 (soluble invariant subgroups under coprime action)

The proposition that for every finite group $G$ and every group $A$ of automorphisms of $G$ with $\gcd(|A|, |G|) = 1$, there is an $A$-invariant soluble subgroup $H$ of $G$ with $C_A(H) = 1$: only the identity of $A$ fixes $H$ elementwise.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.22 (G. Glauberman; J. G. Thompson).
