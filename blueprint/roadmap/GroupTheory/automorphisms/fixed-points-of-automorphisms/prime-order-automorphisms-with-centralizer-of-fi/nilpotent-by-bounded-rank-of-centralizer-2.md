# Khukhro's theorem on automorphisms of prime order with centralizer of finite rank

There are
functions $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and $g \colon \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N} \to
\mathbb{N}$ with the following property. Let $G$ be a nilpotent group in which every element has
finite order, let $\varphi$ be an automorphism of $G$ of prime order $p$, and let $r$ be a natural
number such that the fixed-point subgroup $C_G(\varphi)$ has rank at most $r$, that is, every
finitely generated subgroup of $C_G(\varphi)$ is generated by at most $r$ elements. Then $G$ has a
normal subgroup $N$, nilpotent of class at most $f(p)$, such that the quotient $G/N$ has rank at
most $g(r, p)$. This is the positive answer to Kourovka Problem 13.58; the case $p = 2$ was proved
earlier by Shumyatsky.
