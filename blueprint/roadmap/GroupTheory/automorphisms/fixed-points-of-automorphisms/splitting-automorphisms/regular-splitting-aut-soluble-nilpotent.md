# Kourovka Problem 1.10 (soluble groups with a regular splitting automorphism)

The proposition that a soluble group $G$ admitting a fixed-point-free (only $1$ is fixed) splitting automorphism $\varphi$ of prime order $p$ is nilpotent. $G$ may be infinite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.10 (Yu. M. Gorchakov).
