# Kourovka Problem 1.10 without regularity

The proposition that a soluble group $G$ admitting a splitting automorphism of prime order $p$ ($g\varphi(g)\cdots\varphi^{p-1}(g) = 1$ for all $g$) is nilpotent, with no fixed-point condition.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.10 (Yu. M. Gorchakov).
