# Soluble groups with a regular splitting automorphism of prime order are nilpotent

A soluble group with a fixed-point-free splitting automorphism of prime order is nilpotent.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.10 (Yu. M. Gorchakov); resolution: E. I. Khukhro, Algebra and Logic 17 (1978), 402-406; E. I. Khukhro, Algebra and Logic 19 (1980), 77-84.
