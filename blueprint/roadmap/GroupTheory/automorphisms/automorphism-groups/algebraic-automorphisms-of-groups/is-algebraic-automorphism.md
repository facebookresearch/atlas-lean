# Algebraic automorphisms of groups

An automorphism $\sigma$ of a group $G$ is algebraic when for every $g \in G$ the subgroup generated by $g, \sigma(g), \sigma^2(g), \dots$, the smallest subgroup containing $g$ and mapped into itself by $\sigma$, is finitely generated.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.7 (V. G. Vilyatser); resolution: A. V. Yagzhev, A sufficient condition for a group automorphism to be algebraic, Algebra and Logic 28 (1989), 83-85, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01980611.
