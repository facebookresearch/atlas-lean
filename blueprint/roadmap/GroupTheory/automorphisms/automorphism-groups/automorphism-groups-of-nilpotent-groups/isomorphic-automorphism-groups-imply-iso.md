# Kourovka Problem 4.49 (nilpotent groups with isomorphic automorphism groups)

The proposition that finitely generated torsion-free nilpotent groups $G$, $H$ with $\mathrm{Aut}\,G \cong \mathrm{Aut}\,H$ are isomorphic.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.49 (V. N. Remeslennikov).
