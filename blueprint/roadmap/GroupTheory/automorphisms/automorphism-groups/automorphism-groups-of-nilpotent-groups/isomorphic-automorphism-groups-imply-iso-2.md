# Non-isomorphic nilpotent groups with isomorphic automorphism groups

There are non-isomorphic finitely generated torsion-free nilpotent groups with isomorphic automorphism groups. Kourovka Problem 4.49 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 4.49 (nilpotent groups with isomorphic automorphism groups)](isomorphic-automorphism-groups-imply-iso.md).
