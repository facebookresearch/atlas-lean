# Automorphism groups transitive on the involutions of a non-abelian 2-group are soluble

If a group $A$ of automorphisms of a finite non-abelian $2$-group acts transitively on its involutions, then $A$ is soluble. By Li and Zhu's theorem a finite $p$-group whose automorphism group is transitive on elements of order $p$ is homocyclic, generalized quaternion, or a Suzuki $2$-group with soluble automorphism group; in both non-abelian cases the full automorphism group is soluble. The Notebook marks Problem 7.31 solved ("Yes, it is").
