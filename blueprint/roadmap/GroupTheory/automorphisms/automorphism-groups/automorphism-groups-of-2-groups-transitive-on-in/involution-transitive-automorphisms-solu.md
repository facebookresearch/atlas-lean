# Kourovka Problem 7.31 (automorphism groups transitive on involutions)

The proposition that if $G$ is a finite non-abelian $2$-group and $A$ a group of automorphisms of $G$ acting transitively on the involutions of $G$ (any involution is mapped to any other by some element of $A$), then $A$ is soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 7.31 (V. D. Mazurov).
