# Kourovka Problem 6.20 (supersoluble groups of odd order with only inner automorphisms)

The proposition that there is a nontrivial finite supersoluble group $G$ of odd order all of whose automorphisms are inner (every automorphism is conjugation by some element of $G$). Supersoluble means having a series $1 = S_0 \le \cdots \le S_k = G$ of normal subgroups of $G$ with cyclic factors $S_{i+1}/S_i$. The trivial group is excluded, since its only automorphism is inner.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.20 (V. D. Mazurov); resolution: B. Hartley and D. J. S. Robinson, On finite complete groups, Arch. Math. 35 (1980), 67-74, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01235320.
