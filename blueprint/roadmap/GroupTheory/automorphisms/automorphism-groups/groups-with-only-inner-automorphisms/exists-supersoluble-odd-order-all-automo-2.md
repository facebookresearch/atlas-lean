# Supersoluble groups of odd order with only inner automorphisms exist

There is a nontrivial finite supersoluble group $G$ of odd order all of whose automorphisms are inner (every automorphism is conjugation by some element of $G$). Supersoluble means having a series $1 = S_0 \le \cdots \le S_k = G$ of normal subgroups of $G$ with cyclic factors $S_{i+1}/S_i$. This is Hartley and Robinson's positive answer to Kourovka Problem 6.20.
