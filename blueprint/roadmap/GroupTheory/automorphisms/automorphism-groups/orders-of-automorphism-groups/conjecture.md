# Kourovka Problem 21.97 (ratios of automorphism group orders)

The proposition that for every positive rational number $r$ there is a finite group $G$ with $|\mathrm{Aut}(G)| / |G| = r$, the quotient taken in $\mathbb{Q}$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.97, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49; resolution: S. Sureaux, Automorphism ratios of finite groups (preprint, 2026), Theorem 1.1(a), https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/21.97-solution.pdf.
