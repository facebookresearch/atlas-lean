# Finitely many finite simple groups with an involution commuting with few involutions

For
every natural number $n$ there is a bound $N$ such that every finite simple group $G$ containing
an involution $t$ that commutes with at most $n$ involutions of $G$ (counting $t$ itself) has
order at most $N$.

This answers Kourovka Problem 11.96(a) in the affirmative; the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue)
marks it solved, "Yes", modulo the classification of finite simple groups.

## Sources

- E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 11.96 (S. P. Strunkov); resolution: S. V. Skresanov, J. Group Theory 27, no. 6 (2024), 1197-1202, the Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) marks 11.96 solved: (a) yes (mod CFSG), and a locally finite group with such an involution is finite of `n`-bounded order; (b) no, `PSL_2(ℝ)` is an infinite simple group with an involution and no Klein four-subgroup, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
