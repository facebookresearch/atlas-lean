# Bender–Suzuki theorem

Let $X$ be a finite simple group that has a strongly embedded
subgroup $M$. Then $X$ is a simple Bender group: $X$ is isomorphic to $\mathrm{PSL}(2, 2^n)$ for
some $n \ge 2$, to the Suzuki group $\mathrm{Sz}(2^{2n+1})$ for some $n \ge 1$, or to
$\mathrm{PSU}(3, 2^n)$ for some $n \ge 2$.

## Sources

- D. Gorenstein, R. Lyons, and R. Solomon, The classification of the finite simple groups, Number 2. Problem `bender_suzuki` of the lean-eval benchmark.
