# Baumann's theorem on simple groups with a nilpotent maximal subgroup

A finite non-abelian simple group with a nilpotent maximal subgroup is isomorphic to $\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)$ for some prime power $q$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.27 (J. G. Thompson; V. D. Mazurov); resolution: B. Baumann, J. Algebra 38 (1976), 119-135.
