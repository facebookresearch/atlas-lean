# No simple group has a Sylow 2-subgroup that is a power of Q₈

No finite simple group has a Sylow $2$-subgroup isomorphic to $Q_8^k$ for some $k \ge 1$; Kourovka Problem 1.80 has a negative answer, which follows from Glauberman's $Z^*$-theorem. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 1.80 (simple groups with Sylow 2-subgroup a product of quaternion groups)](simple-group-with-quaternion-product-syl.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.80 (A. I. Starostin); resolution: G. Glauberman, Central elements in core-free groups, J. Algebra 4 (1966), 403-420, Section 6, https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(66)90030-5.
