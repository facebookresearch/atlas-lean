# Kourovka Problem 1.80 (simple groups with Sylow 2-subgroup a product of quaternion groups)

The proposition that some finite simple group has a Sylow $2$-subgroup isomorphic to a direct product $Q_8^k$ of $k \ge 1$ copies of the quaternion group $Q_8$ of order $8$. Products of generalized quaternion groups of larger order are not included.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.80 (A. I. Starostin); resolution: G. Glauberman, Central elements in core-free groups, J. Algebra 4 (1966), 403-420, Section 6, https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(66)90030-5.
