# Kourovka Problem 5.13 (commutators of two classes of involutions)

The proposition that whenever $G$ is a finite group and $K$, $L$ are distinct conjugacy classes of involutions of $G$ such that $\langle x, y \rangle$ is a $2$-group for all $x \in K$ and $y \in L$, the subgroup $[K, L]$ is a proper subgroup of $G$. Here $[K, L]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ with $x \in K$, $y \in L$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.13 (V. V. Kabanov); resolution: A. A. Makhnëv, letter of 10 October 1981, recorded in the Archive under Problem 5.13.
