# Two classes of involutions whose commutators generate the group

There are a finite group $G$ and classes $K$, $L$ such that $G$ is a finite group and $K$, $L$ are distinct conjugacy classes of involutions of $G$ such that $\langle x, y \rangle$ is a $2$-group for all $x \in K$ and $y \in L$, and $[K, L] = G$, where $[K, L]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ with $x \in K$, $y \in L$; Kourovka Problem 5.13 has a negative answer. Makhnëv's example is $G = \mathrm{Sp}_4(2^n)$, $n \ge 2$, with $K$ and $L$ the classes of involutions meeting the centres of $N_G(M)'$ and $N_G(N)'$ for distinct elementary abelian subgroups $M$, $N$ of order $8$ in a Sylow $2$-subgroup. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.13 (commutators of two classes of involutions)](involution-classes-commutator-proper.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.13 (V. V. Kabanov); resolution: A. A. Makhnëv, letter of 10 October 1981, recorded in the Archive under Problem 5.13.
