# Kourovka Problem 5.63 (Chunikhin's coprime centralizer indices)

The proposition that the following holds. In a finite non-abelian simple group $G$, for any two nonidentity elements $x$ and $y$ (not necessarily distinct) the indices $|G : C_G(x)|$ and $|G : C_G(y)|$ are not coprime. Non-abelian simple groups are meant: in a cyclic group of prime order every centralizer has index $1$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.63 (S. A. Chunikhin); resolution: L. S. Kazarin, On a problem of S. A. Chunikhin, in: Investigations in Group Theory, Sverdlovsk, 1984, 81-99 (Russian), https://zbmath.org/?q=an:0591.20018.
