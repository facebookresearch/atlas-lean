# Kazarin's theorem on coprime centralizer indices

In a finite non-abelian simple group $G$, for any two nonidentity elements $x$ and $y$ (not necessarily distinct) the indices $|G : C_G(x)|$ and $|G : C_G(y)|$ are not coprime. Equivalently, a finite group with two nonidentity elements whose centralizers have coprime indices is not non-abelian simple. Kazarin proved this for the known finite simple groups, which answers Kourovka Problem 5.63 using the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.63 (S. A. Chunikhin); resolution: L. S. Kazarin, On a problem of S. A. Chunikhin, in: Investigations in Group Theory, Sverdlovsk, 1984, 81-99 (Russian), https://zbmath.org/?q=an:0591.20018.
