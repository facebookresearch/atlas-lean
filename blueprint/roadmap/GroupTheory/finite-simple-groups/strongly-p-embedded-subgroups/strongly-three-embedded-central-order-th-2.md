# Xiao's theorem on strongly 3-embedded subgroups

Let $G$ be a finite group and $H$ a strongly $3$-embedded subgroup ($H \ne G$, $3$ divides $|H|$, and $3$ does not divide $|H \cap gHg^{-1}|$ for any $g \notin H$). If the centre of $H/O_{3'}(H)$ contains an element of order $3$, then so does the centre of $G/O_{3'}(G)$. Here $O_{3'}(K)$ is the largest normal subgroup of $K$ of order prime to $3$. This is Xiao's positive answer to Kourovka Problem 6.42, using the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.42 (N. D. Podufalov); resolution: W. Xiao, Sci. China (A) 33 (1990), 1172-1181.
