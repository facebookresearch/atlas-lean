# Strongly p-embedded subgroups

For a natural number $p$, a subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is strongly $p$-embedded when $H \ne G$, $p$ divides $|H|$, and $p$ does not divide $|H \cap gHg^{-1}|$ for any $g \notin H$ (an infinite subgroup counts as having order $0$). For $p = 2$ these are Bender's strongly embedded subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.42 (N. D. Podufalov); resolution: W. Xiao, Sci. China (A) 33 (1990), 1172-1181.
