# Finitely many finite simple groups of a given exponent

For every $n \ge 1$ there is a bound $N$ such that every finite simple group $G$ with $g^n = 1$ for all $g \in G$ has order at most $N$; there are only finitely many finite simple groups of a given exponent. This is Jones's positive answer to Kourovka Problem 1.21, using the classification of finite simple groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.21 (M. I. Kargapolov); resolution: G. A. Jones, J. Austral. Math. Soc. 17 (1974), 162-173.
