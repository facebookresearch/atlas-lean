# Kourovka Problem 9.12 (faithful irreducible representations of soluble groups of finite rank)

The proposition that there is a soluble, torsion-free group $G$ of finite rank that is not finitely generated and has a faithful irreducible representation on a vector space (of any dimension) over some finite field. A group has finite (Prüfer) rank if there is $r$ such that every finitely generated subgroup can be generated by at most $r$ elements; a representation $\rho$ of $G$ on a vector space $V$ over a field $k$ is faithful if $\rho$ is injective and irreducible if $V \ne 0$ and the only $G$-invariant subspaces are $0$ and $V$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.12 (D. I. Zaitsev).
