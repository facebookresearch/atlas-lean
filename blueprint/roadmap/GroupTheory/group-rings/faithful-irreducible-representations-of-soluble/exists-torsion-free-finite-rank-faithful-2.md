# Torsion-free soluble groups of finite rank with faithful irreducible representations over finite fields

There is a soluble torsion-free group of finite rank, not finitely generated, with a faithful irreducible representation over a finite field. A group has finite (Prüfer) rank if there is $r$ such that every finitely generated subgroup can be generated by at most $r$ elements; a representation $\rho$ of $G$ on a vector space $V$ over a field $k$ is faithful if $\rho$ is injective and irreducible if $V \ne 0$ and the only $G$-invariant subspaces are $0$ and $V$. This answers Kourovka Problem 9.12 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 9.12 (D. I. Zaitsev); resolution: A. V. Tushev, Irreducible representations of locally polycyclic groups over an absolute field, Ukrainian Math. J. 42 (1990), 1233-1238.
