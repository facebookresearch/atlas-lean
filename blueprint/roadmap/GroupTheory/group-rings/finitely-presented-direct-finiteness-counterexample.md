# Failure of direct finiteness with finite presentation and odd torsion

There exist a finite field $K$ of characteristic two and a finitely presented group $G$ with some element of order an odd prime $\ell$, and $a,b\in K[G]$ satisfying $ab=1$ and $ba\ne1$. The torsion witness and one-sided inverse pair are in the same group, and the odd prime is existentially quantified, not prescribed.

## Depends on

- Finitely presented groups, order of a group element, and finite group-ring support.

## Proof depends on

- Verification of the finite presentation, survival of the odd-order subgroup, and the two prescribed product identities in the source's explicit construction.

## Expansion

Track which generators and relations are retained in the finite presentation and how it contains the torsion witness. Verify the one-sided identities there, rather than inferring finite presentation from finite generation.
