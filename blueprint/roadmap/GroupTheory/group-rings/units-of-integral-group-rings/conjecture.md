# Nontrivial unit conjecture for $C_2 \times C_2 \times C_2 \times C_6$

Let $G = C_2 \times C_2 \times C_2 \times C_6$. The statement that the integral group ring
$\mathbb{Z}G$ contains a nontrivial unit: a unit $u$ with $u \ne g$ and $u \ne -g$ for every
$g \in G$.

## Sources

- *Computational Group Theory*, Oberwolfach Rep. 8 (2011), Report 37/2011, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2011/37. The report (p. 2147) asks for which groups $G$ the ring $\mathbb{Z}G$ has exotic units, meaning units outside Hoechsmann's subgroup of constructible units; this page uses the different notion of a nontrivial unit, one not of the form $\pm g$.
