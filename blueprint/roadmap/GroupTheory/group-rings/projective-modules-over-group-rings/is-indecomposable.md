# Indecomposable modules

For a ring $R$, an $R$-module $M$ is indecomposable when it is nonzero and not the internal direct sum of two nonzero submodules (for every pair of complementary submodules $P$, $Q$, one of them is $0$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 4.55 (K. W. Roggenkamp; marked solved in the main list); resolution: A. Jayadevan, Nonunique indecomposable projective decompositions over the localized group ring of PSL2(F11), preprint (2026), https://zenodo.org/records/22750727.
