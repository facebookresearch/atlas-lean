# Unique decomposition into indecomposable projectives

For a ring $R$, an $R$-module $M$ has a unique decomposition into indecomposable projectives when such a decomposition of $M$ exists (indexed by some type), and any two such decompositions $(N_i)_{i \in I}$ and $(N'_j)_{j \in J}$ are matched by a bijection $e : I \to J$ with $N_i \cong N'_{e(i)}$ for every $i$. A decomposition here is a family $(N_i)_{i \in I}$ of submodules of $M$ such that $M$ is the internal direct sum of the $N_i$ and each $N_i$ is an indecomposable projective $R$-module.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 4.55 (K. W. Roggenkamp; marked solved in the main list); resolution: A. Jayadevan, Nonunique indecomposable projective decompositions over the localized group ring of PSL2(F11), preprint (2026), https://zenodo.org/records/22750727.
