# Decompositions into indecomposable projective summands

For a ring $R$ and an $R$-module $M$, a decomposition of $M$ into indecomposable projective summands is a family $(N_i)_{i \in I}$ of submodules of $M$ such that $M$ is the internal direct sum of the $N_i$ and each $N_i$ is an indecomposable projective $R$-module. An indecomposable module is one that is nonzero and not the internal direct sum of two nonzero submodules (for every pair of complementary submodules $P$, $Q$, one of them is $0$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 4.55 (K. W. Roggenkamp; marked solved in the main list); resolution: A. Jayadevan, Nonunique indecomposable projective decompositions over the localized group ring of PSL2(F11), preprint (2026), https://zenodo.org/records/22750727.
