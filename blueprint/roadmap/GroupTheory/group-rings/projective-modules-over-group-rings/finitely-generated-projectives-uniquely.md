# Kourovka Problem 4.55 (unique decomposition of projective modules over localized group rings)

The proposition that for every finite group $G$ and every prime $p$, every finitely generated projective module $M$ over the group ring $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}G$, where $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ is the localization of $\mathbb{Z}$ at the prime ideal $(p)$, is uniquely, up to permutation and isomorphism of the summands, an internal direct sum of indecomposable projective submodules. The problem is stated here for finitely generated modules.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 4.55 (K. W. Roggenkamp; marked solved in the main list); resolution: A. Jayadevan, Nonunique indecomposable projective decompositions over the localized group ring of PSL2(F11), preprint (2026), https://zenodo.org/records/22750727.
