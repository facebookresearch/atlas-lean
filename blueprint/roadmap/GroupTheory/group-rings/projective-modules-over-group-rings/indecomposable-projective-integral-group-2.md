# Indecomposable projective modules over the integral group ring of a finite group are finitely generated

For every finite group $G$, every indecomposable projective left module over the integral group ring $\mathbb{Z}G$ is finitely generated. Here a module is indecomposable when it is nonzero and is not the internal direct sum of two nonzero submodules. Kourovka Problem 8.34 has a positive answer.
