# Kourovka Problem 8.34 (indecomposable projective modules over integral group rings)

This is the proposition that for every finite group $G$, every indecomposable projective left module over the integral group ring $\mathbb{Z}G$ is finitely generated. Here a module is indecomposable when it is nonzero and is not the internal direct sum of two nonzero submodules.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.34 (P. A. Linnell).
