# Endotrivial modules

For a field $k$ and a group $G$, a representation $M$ of $G$ over $k$ is endotrivial when $M$ is finite-dimensional over $k$ and the $kG$-module $\mathrm{End}_k(M) = \mathrm{Hom}_k(M, M)$, with $G$ acting by conjugation, is isomorphic to the direct sum of the trivial module $k$ and a projective representation of $G$. For finite-dimensional $M$, $\mathrm{End}_k(M) \cong M \otimes M^*$.

Source: J. Thévenaz (joint work with J. F. Carlson), Torsion endo-trivial modules, in Cohomology of Finite Groups: Interactions and Applications, Oberwolfach Reports 12 (2015), no. 24, p. 1365 ff., Conjecture, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2015/24; resolution: J. Grodal, Endotrivial modules for finite groups via homotopy theory, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 36 (2023), no. 1, 177-250, Theorem F, arXiv:1608.00499.
