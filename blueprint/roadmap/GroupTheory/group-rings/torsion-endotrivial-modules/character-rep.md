# The one-dimensional representation of a character

For a field $k$, a group $N$ and a homomorphism $\chi : N \to k^\times$, $L_\chi$ is the representation of $N$ on the one-dimensional space $k$ in which $g \in N$ acts as multiplication by $\chi(g)$. Every one-dimensional representation of $N$ over $k$ is isomorphic to one of these, and the kernel of $L_\chi$ is $\ker \chi$.

Source: J. Thévenaz (joint work with J. F. Carlson), Torsion endo-trivial modules, in Cohomology of Finite Groups: Interactions and Applications, Oberwolfach Reports 12 (2015), no. 24, p. 1365 ff., Conjecture, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2015/24; resolution: J. Grodal, Endotrivial modules for finite groups via homotopy theory, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 36 (2023), no. 1, 177-250, Theorem F, arXiv:1608.00499.
