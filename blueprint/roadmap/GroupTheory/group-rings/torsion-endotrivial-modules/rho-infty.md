# The subgroup ρ^∞(S) of Carlson and Thévenaz

For a group $G$, a natural number $p$ and a subgroup $S \le G$, $\rho^\infty(S)$ is the subgroup generated by all the Carlson–Thévenaz subgroups $\rho_i(S)$, $i \ge 1$, where $\rho_1(Q) = A_{p'}(N_G(Q))$ and $\rho_{i+1}(Q) = \langle N_G(Q) \cap \rho_i(R) : R \le S,\ R \ne 1 \rangle$, $N_G(Q)$ is the normalizer of $Q$ and $A_{p'}(N)$ is the [abelian $p'$-residual](abelian-pprime-residual.md), generated by the commutators $[a, b]$ with $a, b \in N$ and by the elements $g \in N$ with $g^{p^n} = 1$ for some natural number $n$ (for prime $p$, the elements of $p$-power order). When $S \ne 1$, the $\rho_i(S)$ form an increasing chain, so $\rho^\infty(S)$ is their union.

Carlson), Torsion endo-trivial modules, in Cohomology of Finite Groups: Interactions and Applications, Oberwolfach Reports 12 (2015), no. 24, p. 1365 ff., Conjecture, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2015/24; resolution: J. Grodal, Endotrivial modules for finite groups via homotopy theory, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 36 (2023), no. 1, 177-250, Theorem F, arXiv:1608.00499.
