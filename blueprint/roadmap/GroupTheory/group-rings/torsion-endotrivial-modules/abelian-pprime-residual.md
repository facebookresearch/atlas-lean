# The abelian p'-residual of a subgroup

For a group $G$, a natural number $p$ and a subgroup $N \le G$, the abelian $p'$-residual of $N$ is the subgroup $A_{p'}(N)$ generated by the commutators $[a, b]$ with $a, b \in N$ and by the elements of $N$ of $p$-power order, that is, $[N, N] \, O^{p'}(N)$. For finite $N$ and prime $p$ it is the smallest normal subgroup of $N$ with abelian quotient of order prime to $p$, and it equals the product of $[N, N]$ with any Sylow $p$-subgroup of $N$; Carlson and Thévenaz write it $N'$.

Carlson), Torsion endo-trivial modules, in Cohomology of Finite Groups: Interactions and Applications, Oberwolfach Reports 12 (2015), no. 24, p. 1365 ff., Conjecture, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2015/24; resolution: J.
