# The abelian p'-residual of a subgroup

For a group $G$, a natural number $p$ and a subgroup $N \le G$, the abelian $p'$-residual of $N$ is the subgroup $A_{p'}(N)$ generated by the commutators $[a, b]$ with $a, b \in N$ and by the elements $g \in N$ with $g^{p^n} = 1$ for some natural number $n$. For prime $p$ these are the elements of $p$-power order, and $A_{p'}(N) = [N, N] \, O^{p'}(N)$; for $p = 0$, taking $n = 1$ gives $g^0 = 1$, so every element of $N$ is among the generators and $A_{p'}(N) = N$. For finite $N$ and prime $p$ it is the smallest normal subgroup of $N$ with abelian quotient of order prime to $p$, and it equals the product of $[N, N]$ with any Sylow $p$-subgroup of $N$; Carlson and Thévenaz write it $N'$.
