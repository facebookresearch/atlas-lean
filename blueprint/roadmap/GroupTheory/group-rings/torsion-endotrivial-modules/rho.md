# The Carlson–Thévenaz subgroups

For a group $G$, a natural number $p$ and a subgroup $S \le G$, the Carlson–Thévenaz subgroups $\rho_i(Q)$ of $G$, for $i \ge 1$ and subgroups $Q \le G$, are defined by $\rho_1(Q) = A_{p'}(N_G(Q))$ and $\rho_{i+1}(Q) = \langle N_G(Q) \cap \rho_i(R) : R \le S,\ R \ne 1 \rangle$, where $N_G(Q)$ is the normalizer of $Q$ and $A_{p'}(N)$ is the [abelian $p'$-residual](abelian-pprime-residual.md), generated by the commutators $[a, b]$ with $a, b \in N$ and by the elements $g \in N$ with $g^{p^n} = 1$ for some natural number $n$ (for prime $p$, the elements of $p$-power order). In the indexing used here the index $0$ stands for $\rho_1$. They are used with $S$ a Sylow $p$-subgroup and $Q$ a nontrivial subgroup of $S$.
