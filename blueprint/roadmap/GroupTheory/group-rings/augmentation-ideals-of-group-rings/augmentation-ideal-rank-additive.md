# Kourovka Problem 5.10 (generators of the augmentation ideal of a free product)

The proposition that for all finitely generated groups $H$ and $K$, with free product $E = H * K$, the minimum numbers of generators of right ideals of the integral group ring $\mathbb{Z}E$ satisfy

$$d_E(\mathfrak{e}) = d_E(\mathfrak{h}E) + d_E(\mathfrak{k}E).$$

Here $\mathfrak{e}$ is the augmentation ideal of $\mathbb{Z}E$ (the right ideal generated by all $g - 1$, $g \in E$), $\mathfrak{h}E$ and $\mathfrak{k}E$ are the right ideals of $\mathbb{Z}E$ generated by the elements $h - 1$ ($h \in H$) and $k - 1$ ($k \in K$), and $d_E(I)$ is the least number of generators of a right ideal $I$. All three right ideals are finitely generated.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.10 (K. W. Gruenberg).
