# Augmentation ideal summands give free factors

Let $H$ be a torsion-free subgroup of a group $G$, and suppose that the augmentation ideal $I_G$ of $\mathbb{Z}G$ is an internal direct sum $I_G = I_H\mathbb{Z}G \oplus M$, where $I_H\mathbb{Z}G$ is the right ideal generated by the elements $h - 1$ ($h \in H$) and $M$ is a right $\mathbb{Z}G$-submodule. Then $G = H * K$ for some subgroup $K$: the homomorphism $H * K \to G$ induced by the inclusions is bijective. This answers Kourovka Problem 5.28.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.28 (D. E. Cohen); resolution: W. Dicks and M. J. Dunwoody, Groups acting on graphs, Cambridge Univ. Press, 1989.
