# Generator numbers of augmentation ideals of free products are not additive

There are finitely generated groups $H$ and $K$ such that, for $E = H * K$, the augmentation ideal $\mathfrak{e}$ of $\mathbb{Z}E$ satisfies $d_E(\mathfrak{e}) \ne d_E(\mathfrak{h}E) + d_E(\mathfrak{k}E)$, where $\mathfrak{h}E$ and $\mathfrak{k}E$ are the right ideals generated by the $h - 1$ ($h \in H$) and the $k - 1$ ($k \in K$), and $d_E$ is the least number of generators of a right ideal of $\mathbb{Z}E$. For $H$ elementary abelian of order $4$ and $K$ elementary abelian of order $9$ one has $d_E(\mathfrak{e}) = 3$ while $d_E(\mathfrak{h}E) = d_E(\mathfrak{k}E) = 2$. This answers Kourovka Problem 5.10 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 5.10 (generators of the augmentation ideal of a free product)](augmentation-ideal-rank-additive.md).

Gruenberg); resolution: P. A. Linnell, unpublished; example from K. W. Gruenberg, letter of 27 July 1995, as recorded in the Kourovka Archive.
