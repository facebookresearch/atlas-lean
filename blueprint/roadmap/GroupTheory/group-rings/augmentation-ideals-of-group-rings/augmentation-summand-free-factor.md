# Kourovka Problem 5.28 (augmentation ideal summands and free factors)

The proposition that whenever $H$ is a torsion-free subgroup of a group $G$ and the augmentation ideal $I_G$ of $\mathbb{Z}G$ (the right ideal generated by all $g - 1$) is an internal direct sum $I_G = I_H\mathbb{Z}G \oplus M$ of the right ideal $I_H\mathbb{Z}G$ generated by the $h - 1$ ($h \in H$) and some right $\mathbb{Z}G$-submodule $M$ of $\mathbb{Z}G$ (the two meet in $0$ and together span $I_G$), there is a subgroup $K$ of $G$ with $G = H * K$: the homomorphism from the free product $H * K$ to $G$ induced by the inclusions is bijective.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.28 (D. E. Cohen).
