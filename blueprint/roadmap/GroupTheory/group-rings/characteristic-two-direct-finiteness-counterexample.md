# A characteristic-two group algebra that is not directly finite

There exist a finite field $K$ of characteristic two and a finitely generated group $G$ with elements $a,b\in K[G]$ such that $ab=1$ but $ba\ne1$. The group is not assumed torsion-free; finite generation is the assertion here, not finite presentation.

## Depends on

- Finite fields of characteristic two and finitely supported group algebras.

## Proof depends on

- [The finitely presented characteristic-two counterexample](finitely-presented-direct-finiteness-counterexample.md).

## Proof outline

Reuse the same field, group and witnesses from the finitely presented result. A group with a finite presentation is generated by its finitely many generators. Keep both product clauses and forget the additional odd-order torsion witness. The two endpoints are not equivalent.
