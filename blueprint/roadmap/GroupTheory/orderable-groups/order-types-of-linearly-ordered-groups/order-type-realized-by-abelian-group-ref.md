# Order types of ordered groups not realized by abelian groups

There is a linearly ordered group, a group with a linear order invariant under multiplication on both sides, whose order type is the order type of no linearly ordered abelian group; Kourovka Problem 3.19 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.19 (order types of linearly ordered groups)](order-type-realized-by-abelian-group.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.19 (A. I. Mal'cev; A. I. Kokorin); resolution: W. C. Holland, A. H. Mekler and S. Shelah, Order 1 (1985), 383-397.
