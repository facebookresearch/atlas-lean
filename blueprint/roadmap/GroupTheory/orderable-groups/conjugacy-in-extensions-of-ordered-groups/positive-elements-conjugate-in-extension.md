# Kourovka Problem 5.4 (conjugating positive elements in ordered extensions)

The proposition that for every linearly ordered group $G$ (a group with a linear order invariant under multiplication on both sides) and all $g, h > 1$ in $G$, there are a linearly ordered group $H$ and an injective order-preserving homomorphism $f : G \to H$ (strictly monotone) with $f(g)$ conjugate to $f(h)$ in $H$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.4 (V. V. Bludov); resolution: V. V. Bludov, Completion of linearly ordered groups, Algebra and Logic 44 (2005), 370-380, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10469-005-0036-6.
