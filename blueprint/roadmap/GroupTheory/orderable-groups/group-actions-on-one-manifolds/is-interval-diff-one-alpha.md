# C^(1+α) diffeomorphisms of the interval

For a real $\alpha$, a homeomorphism $f$ of $[0, 1]$ is an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphism if $f$ is non-decreasing and there is a function $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f(x) = F(x)$ for $x \in [0, 1]$ such that $F$ is $C^1$ on $[0, 1]$ (derivatives within $[0, 1]$, one-sided at the endpoints), its derivative within $[0, 1]$ is positive at every point of $[0, 1]$, and for some constant $C$, $|F'(x) - F'(y)| \le C\,|x - y|^{\alpha}$ for all $x, y \in [0, 1]$.

Source: A. Navas, Group actions on 1-manifolds: a list of very concrete open questions, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018, Vol. 2, World Scientific (2018), 2029-2056, arXiv:1712.06462, Question 13.
