# Orientation-preserving circle homeomorphisms

A homeomorphism $f$ of the unit circle $S^1 \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ is orientation-preserving if there is a strictly increasing map $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f(e^{it}) = e^{iF(t)}$ for every real $t$.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 4.1 (de la Harpe), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
