# Navas's Question 13 for the circle (critical regularity)

The proposition that for all reals $0 < \alpha < \beta < 1$ there is a finitely generated group $G$ of homeomorphisms of the circle $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$, all of whose elements are orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphisms, that does not embed into the group of orientation-preserving $C^{1+\beta}$ diffeomorphisms of the circle. Not embedding means that every injective homomorphism from $G$ into the homeomorphism group has some image element that is not an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\beta}$ diffeomorphism. A homeomorphism $f$ of the circle $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ is an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphism if it lifts to a $C^1$ map $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (so $f(x + \mathbb{Z}) = F(x) + \mathbb{Z}$) with $F(x + 1) = F(x) + 1$, $F' > 0$ everywhere and $|F'(x) - F'(y)| \le C|x - y|^{\alpha}$ for some constant $C$ and all real $x, y$.

Source: A. Navas, Group actions on 1-manifolds: a list of very concrete open questions, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018, Vol. 2, World Scientific (2018), 2029-2056, arXiv:1712.06462, Question 13.
