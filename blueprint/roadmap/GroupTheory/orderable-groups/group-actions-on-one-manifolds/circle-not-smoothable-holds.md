# Finitely generated groups of C^(1+α) circle diffeomorphisms of critical regularity

For all reals $0 < \alpha < \beta < 1$ there is a finitely generated group of orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphisms of the circle $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ such that every injective homomorphism from it into the homeomorphism group of the circle has an image element that is not an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\beta}$ diffeomorphism. A homeomorphism $f$ of the circle $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ is an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphism if it lifts to a $C^1$ map $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (so $f(x + \mathbb{Z}) = F(x) + \mathbb{Z}$) with $F(x + 1) = F(x) + 1$, $F' > 0$ everywhere and $|F'(x) - F'(y)| \le C|x - y|^{\alpha}$ for some constant $C$ and all real $x, y$. This answers Navas's Question 13 for the circle; it follows from Kim and Koberda's theorem applied with the exponents $1 + \alpha < 1 + \beta$.

Source: A. Navas, Group actions on 1-manifolds: a list of very concrete open questions, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018, Vol. 2, World Scientific (2018), 2029-2056, arXiv:1712.06462, Question 13; resolution: S.-h. Kim and T. Koberda, Diffeomorphism groups of critical regularity, Invent. Math. 221 (2020), 421-501, arXiv:1711.05589, Main Theorem.
