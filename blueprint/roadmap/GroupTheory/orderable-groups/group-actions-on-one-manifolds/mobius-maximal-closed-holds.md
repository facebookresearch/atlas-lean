# PSL₂(ℝ) is a maximal closed subgroup of Homeo₊(S¹)

Let $G$ be a group of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of the circle that is closed under uniform limits (of sequences, within orientation-preserving homeomorphisms) and contains every Möbius transformation. Then either every element of $G$ is a Möbius transformation, or $G$ contains every orientation-preserving homeomorphism. A Möbius transformation of the unit circle is a map $z \mapsto (\alpha z + \beta)/(\bar\beta z + \bar\alpha)$ with $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$ and $|\alpha|^2 - |\beta|^2 = 1$ (the action of $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{R})$, conjugated to the circle by the Cayley transform); a homeomorphism $f$ of the circle is orientation-preserving if some strictly increasing $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $f(e^{it}) = e^{iF(t)}$ for all real $t$. This confirms de la Harpe's conjecture (Bestvina Q 4.1); Giblin and Markovic deduce it from their classification of closed continuously transitive circle groups.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 4.1 (de la Harpe), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf; resolution: J. Giblin and V. Markovic, Classification of continuously transitive circle groups, Geom. Topol. 10 (2006), 1319-1346, arXiv:0903.0180, Theorem 1.3.
