# Finitely generated groups of C^(1+α) interval diffeomorphisms of critical regularity

For all reals $0 < \alpha < \beta < 1$ there is a finitely generated group of orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphisms of $[0, 1]$ such that every injective homomorphism from it into the homeomorphism group of $[0, 1]$ has an image element that is not an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\beta}$ diffeomorphism. A homeomorphism $f$ of $[0, 1]$ is an orientation-preserving $C^{1+\alpha}$ diffeomorphism if it is non-decreasing and agrees on $[0, 1]$ with a function $F$ that is $C^1$ on $[0, 1]$ (one-sided derivatives at the endpoints) with positive derivative there and with $|F'(x) - F'(y)| \le C|x - y|^{\alpha}$ for some constant $C$ and all $x, y \in [0, 1]$. This answers Navas's Question 13 for the interval; it follows from Kim and Koberda's theorem applied with the exponents $1 + \alpha < 1 + \beta$.

Source: A. Navas, Group actions on 1-manifolds: a list of very concrete open questions, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018, Vol. 2, World Scientific (2018), 2029-2056, arXiv:1712.06462, Question 13; resolution: S.-h. Kim and T. Koberda, Diffeomorphism groups of critical regularity, Invent. Math. 221 (2020), 421-501, arXiv:1711.05589, Main Theorem.
