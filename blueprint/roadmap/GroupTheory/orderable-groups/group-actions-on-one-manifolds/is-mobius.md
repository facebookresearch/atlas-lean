# Möbius transformations of the circle

A homeomorphism $f$ of the unit circle $S^1 \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ is a Möbius transformation if there are $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|\alpha|^2 - |\beta|^2 = 1$ and $f(z) = (\alpha z + \beta)/(\bar\beta z + \bar\alpha)$ for every $z \in S^1$. These form the action of $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ on the circle, conjugated by the Cayley transform.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 4.1 (de la Harpe), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
