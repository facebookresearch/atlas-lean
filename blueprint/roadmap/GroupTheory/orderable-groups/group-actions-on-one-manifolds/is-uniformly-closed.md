# Uniformly closed groups of circle homeomorphisms

A subgroup $G$ of the homeomorphism group of the unit circle is uniformly closed (closed in $\mathrm{Homeo}_+(S^1)$ for the uniform topology) if for every sequence $(f_k)$ in $G$ and every orientation-preserving homeomorphism $g$ such that $f_k \to g$ uniformly, $g \in G$. Here $f$ is orientation-preserving if $f(e^{it}) = e^{iF(t)}$ for some strictly increasing $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$.

Source: M. Bestvina, Questions in Geometric Group Theory (updated July 2004), Q 4.1 (de la Harpe), https://www.math.utah.edu/~bestvina/eprints/questions-updated.pdf.
