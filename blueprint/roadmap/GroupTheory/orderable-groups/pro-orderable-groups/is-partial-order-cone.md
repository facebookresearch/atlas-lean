# Positive cones of bi-invariant partial orders

A subset $P$ of a group $G$ is the positive cone of a partial order invariant under multiplication on both sides if $1 \in P$, $ab \in P$ for all $a, b \in P$, $a \in P$ and $a^{-1} \in P$ imply $a = 1$, and $gag^{-1} \in P$ for all $g \in G$ and $a \in P$. The order is $a \le b \iff a^{-1}b \in P$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.56 (A. I. Kokorin).
