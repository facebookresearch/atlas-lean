# Kourovka Problem 1.56 (pro-orderability modulo the centre)

The proposition that every torsion-free group $G$ whose quotient $G/Z(G)$ by its centre is pro-orderable is itself pro-orderable. $G$ is pro-orderable if every positive cone of a bi-invariant partial order on $G$ is contained in a linear one. A positive cone of a bi-invariant partial order on a group $G$ is a subset $P$ with $1 \in P$, $PP \subseteq P$, $a, a^{-1} \in P \Rightarrow a = 1$, and $gPg^{-1} \subseteq P$ for all $g$; it is linear if moreover $a \in P$ or $a^{-1} \in P$ for every $a$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.56 (A. I. Kokorin).
