# Kourovka Problem 2.71 (finitely generated perfect right-orderable groups)

The proposition that there is a nontrivial finitely generated group $G$ that coincides with its derived subgroup ($[G, G] = G$) and is right-orderable: some linear order $\le$ on $G$ satisfies $a \le b \Rightarrow ac \le bc$ for all $a, b, c$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.71 (D. M. Smirnov).
