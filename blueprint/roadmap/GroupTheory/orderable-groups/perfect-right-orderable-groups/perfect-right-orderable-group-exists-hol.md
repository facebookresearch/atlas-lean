# Finitely generated perfect right-orderable groups

There is a nontrivial finitely generated perfect group ($[G, G] = G$) that is right-orderable (some linear order satisfies $a \le b \Rightarrow ac \le bc$). Bergman shows that the universal covering group of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ is right-orderable and contains nontrivial finitely generated perfect subgroups. Such a group is not locally indicable. This answers Kourovka Problem 2.71 positively.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.71 (D. M. Smirnov); resolution: G. M. Bergman, Right orderable groups that are not locally indicable, Pacific J. Math. 147 (1991), no. 2, 243-248.
