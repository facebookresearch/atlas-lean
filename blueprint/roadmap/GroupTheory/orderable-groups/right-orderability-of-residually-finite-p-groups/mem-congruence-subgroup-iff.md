# Membership in a principal congruence subgroup

An element $g$ of
$\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Z})$ lies in the principal congruence subgroup of level $m$ if and only if
every entry of $g - 1$ is divisible by $m$.

## Sources

- P. A. Linnell, Right orderable residually finite p-groups and a Kourovka notebook problem, J. Algebra 248 (2002), 605-607, arXiv:math/0107094, proof of Theorem 1.
