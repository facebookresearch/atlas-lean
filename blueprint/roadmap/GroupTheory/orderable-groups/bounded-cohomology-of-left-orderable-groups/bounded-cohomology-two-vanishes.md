# Vanishing of the second bounded cohomology

The second bounded cohomology $H^2_b(G; \mathbb{R})$ of a group $G$ with trivial real coefficients vanishes if every bounded $2$-cocycle $c$ (a bounded $c : G \times G \to \mathbb{R}$ with $c(h, k) - c(gh, k) + c(g, hk) - c(g, h) = 0$) is the coboundary of a bounded function: $c(g, h) = f(h) - f(gh) + f(g)$ for all $g, h$, for some bounded $f : G \to \mathbb{R}$.

Source: A. Navas, Group actions on 1-manifolds: a list of very concrete open questions, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018, Vol. 2, World Scientific (2018), 2029-2056, arXiv:1712.06462, Question 8.
