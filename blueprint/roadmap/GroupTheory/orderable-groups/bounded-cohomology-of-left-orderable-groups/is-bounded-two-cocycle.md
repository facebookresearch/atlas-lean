# Bounded real 2-cocycles

For a group $G$, a function $c : G \times G \to \mathbb{R}$ is a bounded $2$-cocycle (with trivial real coefficients, in inhomogeneous form) if there is a constant $C$ with $|c(g, h)| \le C$ for all $g, h$, and $c(h, k) - c(gh, k) + c(g, hk) - c(g, h) = 0$ for all $g, h, k \in G$.

Source: A. Navas, Group actions on 1-manifolds: a list of very concrete open questions, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2018, Vol. 2, World Scientific (2018), 2029-2056, arXiv:1712.06462, Question 8.
