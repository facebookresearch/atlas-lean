# Quantifier-free formulas of the language of lattices

A quantifier-free formula of the language of lattices with the order $\le$ (joins and meets being definable), coded in conjunctive normal form: a finite list of clauses, each a finite list of literals $(\mathrm{true}, (i, j))$, meaning $x_i \le x_j$, or $(\mathrm{false}, (i, j))$, meaning $\neg\, x_i \le x_j$.
