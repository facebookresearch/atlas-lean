# Sentences of the language of lattices in prenex form

A first-order sentence of the language of lattices, coded in prenex form: a finite list of quantifiers binding $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in order, together with a matrix in conjunctive normal form over literals $x_i \le x_j$ and $\neg\, x_i \le x_j$. Variables not bound by the prefix are read as the least $l$-ideal $\{1\}$.
