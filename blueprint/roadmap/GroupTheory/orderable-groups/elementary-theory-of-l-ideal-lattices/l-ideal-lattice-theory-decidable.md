# Kourovka Problem 5.20 (elementary theory of l-ideal lattices)

The proposition that the elementary theory of the class of lattices of $l$-ideals of lattice-ordered abelian groups is decidable: the set of coded lattice sentences true in the $l$-ideal lattice of every lattice-ordered abelian group is computable. A lattice-ordered abelian group is a commutative group with a lattice order invariant under multiplication; its $l$-ideals are the subgroups $H$ that are solid: $a \in H$ and $|b| \le |a|$ imply $b \in H$, where $|a| = a \vee a^{-1}$. Decidability means computability of the predicate on the natural coding of these finite objects (lists of letters), so it does not depend on the coding.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.20 (A. I. Kokorin).
