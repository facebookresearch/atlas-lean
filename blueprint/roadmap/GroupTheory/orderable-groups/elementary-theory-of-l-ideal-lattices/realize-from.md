# Truth of prenex formulas in the lattice of l-ideals

For a commutative group $G$ with a lattice order, a matrix $M$ in conjunctive normal form, a list of quantifiers ($\forall$ or $\exists$), a starting index $k$ and an assignment $x$ of subgroups of $G$ to the variables: the truth of the formula whose $j$-th quantifier binds $x_{k+j}$, ranging over the $l$-ideals of $G$ ordered by inclusion, followed by $M$. With no quantifiers left it says that every clause of $M$ has a true literal, $x_i \le x_j$ being inclusion.
