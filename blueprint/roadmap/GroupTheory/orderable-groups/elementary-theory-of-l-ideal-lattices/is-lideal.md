# l-ideals of a lattice-ordered abelian group

For a commutative group $G$ with a lattice order, a subgroup $H$ is an $l$-ideal when it is solid: whenever $a \in H$ and $|b| \le |a|$ for $b \in G$, then $b \in H$, where $|a| = a \vee a^{-1}$ is the absolute value. In a lattice-ordered abelian group these are exactly the convex $l$-subgroups.
