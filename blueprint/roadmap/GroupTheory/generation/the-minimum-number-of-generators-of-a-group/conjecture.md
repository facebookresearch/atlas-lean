# Kourovka Problem 21.87 (generation from subgroups of coprime index)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $G$ be a finite group and $d \in \mathbb{N}$. If $G$ has a family of subgroups, each generated by at most $d$ elements, whose indices in $G$ have greatest common divisor $1$ (for every $n > 1$ some member has index not divisible by $n$), then $G$ is generated by at most $d + 1$ elements.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Sater, Generation of finite groups from subgroups of coprime index, arXiv:2608.12432 (2026).
