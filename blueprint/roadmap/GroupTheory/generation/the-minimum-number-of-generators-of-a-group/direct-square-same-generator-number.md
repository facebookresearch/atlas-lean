# Kourovka Problem 6.46 (generators of direct squares)

The proposition that whenever a group $G$ is generated by at most $d$ elements, for some $d \ge 2$, and has no nontrivial finite homomorphic images (every normal subgroup of finite index is $G$), the direct square $G \times G$ is also generated by at most $d$ elements.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.46 (John S. Wilson); resolution: V. N. Obraztsov, Growth sequences of 2-generator simple groups, Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 123A (1993), 839-855, https://doi.org/10.1017/S030821050002953X.
