# d-generated subgroups

For a group $G$, a subgroup $H$ and $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $H$ is $d$-generated when $H$ is generated by the entries of some $d$-tuple $(g_1, \dots, g_d)$ of elements of $G$, that is, by at most $d$ elements. For $d = 0$ only the trivial subgroup is $0$-generated.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 21.87, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49; resolution: R. Sater, Generation of finite groups from subgroups of coprime index, arXiv:2608.12432 (2026).
