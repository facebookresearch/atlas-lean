# Direct squares needing more generators

There are $d \ge 2$ and a group $G$ generated by at most $d$ elements, with no nontrivial finite homomorphic images (every normal subgroup of finite index is $G$), such that $G \times G$ is not generated by at most $d$ elements; Kourovka Problem 6.46 has a negative answer. Obraztsov's $2$-generator group $H$ with no nontrivial finite images has $d(H^n) = 3$ for every $n \ge 2$. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 6.46 (generators of direct squares)](direct-square-same-generator-number.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.46 (John S. Wilson); resolution: V. N. Obraztsov, Growth sequences of 2-generator simple groups, Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 123A (1993), 839-855, https://doi.org/10.1017/S030821050002953X.
