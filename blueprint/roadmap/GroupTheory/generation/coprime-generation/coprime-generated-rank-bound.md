# Lucchini's coprime generation bound (Kourovka 16.53)

The statement that for every finite
group $G$, all subgroups $A$ and $B$ of $G$ of coprime orders that together generate $G$, and every
natural number $d$, if each of $A$ and $B$ is generated by at most $d$ elements, then $G$ is
generated by at most $d + 1$ elements.
