# Kourovka Problem 4.12 (stability groups of series down to the Frattini subgroup)

The proposition that the following holds. Let $G$ be a finite group and $A$ a group of automorphisms of $G$ that stabilizes a series $G = G_0 \ge G_1 \ge \cdots \ge G_n = \Phi(G)$ of normal subgroups of $G$ ending at the Frattini subgroup: $g^{-1}\alpha(g) \in G_{i+1}$ for all $\alpha \in A$, $i < n$ and $g \in G_i$. Then $A$ is nilpotent. Nothing is assumed about the action of $A$ on $\Phi(G)$. The terms of the series are required to be normal in $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.12 (Ya. G. Berkovich); resolution: P. Schmid, Nilpotente Gruppen und Stabilitätsgruppen, Math. Ann. 202 (1973), 57-69, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01351205.
