# Maróti–Tamburini bound for two-generated direct powers

For every non-abelian finite simple
group $G$ there is a natural number $n$ with $n^2 > 4|G|$, that is, $n > 2\sqrt{|G|}$, such that
the direct power $G^n$ is generated by two elements. This answers Kourovka Problem 17.116
positively.

Maróti and Tamburini (Theorem 1.1) prove $2\sqrt{|G|} < h(G)$ for every non-abelian finite simple
group $G$, where $h(G)$ is the largest $n$ such that $G^n$ is 2-generated; writing $n(G)$ for this
same $h(G)$, the Kourovka Archive answers 17.116 "Yes, it is, even with $n(G) > 2\sqrt{|G|}$".
