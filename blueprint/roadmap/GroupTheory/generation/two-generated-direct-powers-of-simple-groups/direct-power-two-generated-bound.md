# Kourovka Problem 17.116 (two-generated direct powers of simple groups)

The statement, in
the form of its resolution, that for every non-abelian finite simple group $G$ there is a natural
number $n$ with $n^2 > 4|G|$, that is, $n > 2\sqrt{|G|}$, such that the direct power $G^n$ is
generated by two elements. The problem is due to A. Erfanian and J. Wiegold.

Since $G^n$ maps onto $G^m$ for $m \le n$, this says that the largest $n(G)$ such that $G^{n(G)}$ is
2-generated satisfies $n(G) > 2\sqrt{|G|}$.
