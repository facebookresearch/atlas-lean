# There are continuum many simple (2,3)-generated groups

The isomorphism classes of simple groups generated by an element of order $2$ and an element of order $3$ have the cardinality of the continuum: there is a family $(S_t)_{t \subseteq \mathbb{N}}$, indexed by the subsets of $\mathbb{N}$, of such groups that are pairwise non-isomorphic and such that every such group is isomorphic to some $S_t$. This answers Kourovka Problem 4.60: every $2$-generated group embeds in such a simple group (Schupp), each such group has only countably many $2$-generator subgroups, and there are continuum many $2$-generated groups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.60 (D. M. Smirnov; question of P. Hall); resolution: P. E. Schupp, Embeddings into simple groups, J. London Math. Soc. (2) 13 (1976), no. 1, 90-94, https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-13.1.90; Yu. I. Merzlyakov (1976), counting argument recorded in the Archive.
