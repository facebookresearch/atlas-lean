# Kourovka Problem 18.52 (generation by two elements of prime-power order)

The statement
that every finite simple group $G$ is generated by two elements $x, y \in G$ whose orders are prime
powers, that is, each of the form $p^k$ with $p$ prime and $k \ge 1$ (the primes and exponents may
differ between $x$ and $y$ and depend on the group). The problem is due to J. Krempa.
