# Kourovka Problem 6.44 (infinite simple groups needing three generators)

The proposition that there is a finitely generated infinite simple group that is not generated by any two of its elements.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.44 (J. Wiegold); resolution: V. S. Guba, A finitely generated simple group with free 2-generated subgroups, Siberian Math. J. 27 (1986), 670-684, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00969195.
