# Generator number of almost simple groups (Kourovka 11.27)

Let $S$
be a finite non-abelian simple group, identified with its inner automorphism group
$\mathrm{Inn}(S) \le \mathrm{Aut}(S)$. For every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $S$,
$d(G) = \max(2, d(G / S))$, where $d(G)$ is the minimal number of generators of $G$ and
$d(G / S)$, the minimal number of generators of $G / S$, is taken to be the least number of
elements of $G$ that together with $S$ generate $G$.

Dalla Volta and Lucchini proved this using the classification of finite simple groups.

Gruenberg); resolution: F. Dalla Volta and A. Lucchini, J. Algebra 178, no. 1 (1995), 194-223, Kourovka Archive, Problem 11.27: "The number `d(G)` is found (mod CFSG) for every such a group `G`. In particular, `d(G) = max {2, d(G/S)}` and `d(G) ≤ 3`", https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0300v49.
