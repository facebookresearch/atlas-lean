# The generator-number formula for almost simple groups (Kourovka 11.27)

The proposition
that for every finite non-abelian simple group $S$, identified with its inner automorphism group
$\mathrm{Inn}(S) \le \mathrm{Aut}(S)$, and every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $S$,
$d(G) = \max(2, d(G / S))$. Here $d(G)$ is the minimal number of generators of $G$, and
$d(G / S)$, the minimal number of generators of $G / S$, is taken to be the least number of
elements of $G$ that together with $S$ generate $G$.
