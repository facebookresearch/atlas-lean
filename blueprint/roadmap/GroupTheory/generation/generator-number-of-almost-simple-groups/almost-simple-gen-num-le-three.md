# The 3-generator bound for almost simple groups (Kourovka 11.27)

The proposition that for
every finite non-abelian simple group $S$, identified with its inner automorphism group
$\mathrm{Inn}(S) \le \mathrm{Aut}(S)$, every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $S$ is
generated by at most $3$ elements.
