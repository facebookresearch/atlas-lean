# Sambale's order bound for $2$-groups of bounded abelian rank fails (Kourovka 19.75)

There
exist natural numbers $e$ and $r$ and a finite $2$-group $P$ of exponent $2^e$ in which every
abelian subgroup is generated by at most $r$ elements, such that $|P| > 2^{r(e+1)}$.

The Kourovka Archive answers 19.75 "No, it is not true", as follows from Olshanskii's examples of
finite $p$-groups whose abelian subgroups are small compared to the group (pointed out by Mann).

Sambale); resolution: A. Yu. Olshanskii, The number of generators and orders of abelian subgroups of finite p-groups, Math. Notes 23 (1978), no. 3, 183-185; A. Mann, letter of 23 April 2018, abstract: the least f(n) such that every finite p-group whose abelian subgroups are generated by at most n elements has at most f(n) generators grows quadratically in n; Kourovka Archive, Problem 19.75: No, it is not true, as follows from the examples constructed by Olshanskii (Mann), https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01651428.
