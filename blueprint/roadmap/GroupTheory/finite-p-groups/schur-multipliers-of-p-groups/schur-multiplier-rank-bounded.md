# Kourovka Problem 4.67 (rank of the Schur multiplier of a p-group)

The proposition that there is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for every prime $p$, every finite $p$-group $G$ and every $r$ such that every subgroup of $G$ is generated by at most $r$ elements, the Schur multiplier $M(G) = H_2(G, \mathbb{Z})$ is generated as an abelian group by at most $f(r)$ elements. The bound depends on $r$ only, not on $p$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.67 (J. Wiegold); resolution: A. Lubotzky and A. Mann, Powerful p-groups. I. Finite groups, J. Algebra 105 (1987), 484-505, https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(87)90211-0.
