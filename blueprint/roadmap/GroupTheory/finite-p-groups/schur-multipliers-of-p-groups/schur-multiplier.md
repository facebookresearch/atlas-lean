# The Schur multiplier of a group

For a group $G$, the Schur multiplier is $M(G) = H_2(G, \mathbb{Z})$, the second homology group of $G$ with coefficients in the trivial $G$-module $\mathbb{Z}$, regarded as a $\mathbb{Z}$-module.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.67 (J. Wiegold); resolution: A. Lubotzky and A. Mann, Powerful p-groups. I. Finite groups, J. Algebra 105 (1987), 484-505, https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(87)90211-0.
