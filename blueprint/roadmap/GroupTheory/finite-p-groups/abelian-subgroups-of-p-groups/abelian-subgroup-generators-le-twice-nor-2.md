# Kourovka Problem 20.75 has a negative answer

The statement of Kourovka Problem 20.75 is
false: there are a prime $p$, a natural number $k$, a finite $p$-group $G$ all of whose abelian
normal subgroups can be generated by at most $k$ elements, and an abelian subgroup of $G$ that
cannot be generated by at most $2k$ elements.

Muliarchyk's counterexample (Theorem 1.1) is a finite $2$-group of order $2^{110N+1}$, with
$N = 2124210350$, whose abelian normal subgroups can be generated by $32N + 1$ elements while an
elementary abelian subgroup needs $65N > 2(32N + 1)$ generators; the 21st issue of the Kourovka
Notebook marks 20.75 solved ("No, it is not").
