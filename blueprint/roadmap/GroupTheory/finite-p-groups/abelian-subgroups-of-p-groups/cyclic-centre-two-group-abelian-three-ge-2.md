# A 2-group with cyclic centre and a non-3-generated abelian subgroup

There is a finite group $G$ such that $G$ is a finite $2$-group with cyclic centre in which every abelian normal subgroup is generated by at most two elements, but some abelian subgroup of $G$ cannot be generated by three elements; Kourovka Problem 3.28 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.28 (abelian subgroups of 2-groups with cyclic centre)](cyclic-centre-two-group-abelian-three-ge.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.28 (V. D. Mazurov); resolution: Ya. G. Berkovich, Algebra and Logic 9 (1970), 75.
