# No linear bound on generators of 2-groups from ranks of abelian subgroups

For all natural numbers $a, b$ there are $n$ and a finite $2$-group $G$ whose abelian subgroups can all be generated by $n$ elements but which cannot be generated by $an + b$ elements. Thus the number of generators of a finite $2$-group is not bounded by a linear function of the maximal number of generators of its abelian subgroups. This answers Kourovka Problem 4.61 negatively. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 4.61 (generators of 2-groups and ranks of abelian subgroups)](exists-linear-generator-bound-two-groups.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.61 (S. A. Syskin); resolution: A. Yu. Ol'shanskiĭ, The number of generators and orders of abelian subgroups of finite p-groups, Math. Notes 23 (1978), 183-185.
