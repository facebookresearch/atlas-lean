# Kourovka Problem 4.61 (generators of 2-groups and ranks of abelian subgroups)

The proposition that there are natural numbers $a, b$ such that for every $n$ and every finite $2$-group $G$ all of whose abelian subgroups can be generated by $n$ elements, $G$ can be generated by $an + b$ elements. The number of generators is the least size of a generating set. Natural coefficients suffice, since a real linear bound is dominated by one with integer coefficients.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.61 (S. A. Syskin).
