# Free products of equationally Noetherian groups are equationally Noetherian

If $G$ and $H$ are equationally Noetherian groups, then so is their free product $G * H$. A group
$G$ is equationally Noetherian when every system of equations over $G$, with coefficients in $G$ and
finitely many unknowns, has the same solutions in $G$ as some finite subsystem.

The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers Kourovka Problem 14.20 "Yes, it does", citing Sela's
preprint (Theorem 9.1): the free product of two equationally Noetherian groups is equationally
Noetherian.
