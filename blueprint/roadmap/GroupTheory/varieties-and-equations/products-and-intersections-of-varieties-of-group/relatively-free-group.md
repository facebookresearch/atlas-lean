# Relatively free group of a variety

Let $L$ be a set of words in the free group on $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$, viewed as laws, and let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The free group of rank $n$ of the variety defined by $L$ is

$$F_n / V_L(F_n),$$

where $F_n$ is the free group on $n$ generators and the verbal subgroup $V_L(F_n)$ is the normal subgroup of $F_n$ generated by all values $w(g_0, g_1, \dots)$ with $w \in L$ and $g_0, g_1, \dots \in F_n$.
