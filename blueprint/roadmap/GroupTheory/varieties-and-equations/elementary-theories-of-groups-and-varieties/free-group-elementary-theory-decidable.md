# Kourovka Problem 1.29 (decidability of the elementary theory of a free group)

The proposition that for every $r \ge 2$ the elementary theory of the free group $F_r$ of rank $r$ is decidable: the set of codes of first-order sentences of the language of groups true in $F_r$ is computable. Sentences are coded in prenex form: a list of quantifiers binding $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in order, followed by a matrix in conjunctive normal form over literals $w = 1$, $w \ne 1$; variables not bound by the prefix are read as $1$. Every first-order sentence of the language of groups is effectively equivalent to such a code.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.29 (A. Tarski; M. I. Kargapolov).
