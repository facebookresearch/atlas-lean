# Every non-abelian variety of groups has undecidable elementary theory

For every variety of groups containing a non-abelian group, the set of first-order sentences true in all its groups is undecidable. In particular no variety containing all abelian groups and a non-abelian group has decidable elementary theory. Kourovka Problem 4.63 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 4.63 (non-abelian varieties with decidable elementary theory)](exists-non-abelian-variety-with-decidabl.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.63 (A. Tarski); resolution: A. P. Zamyatin, A non-Abelian variety of groups has an undecidable elementary theory, Algebra and Logic 17 (1978), 13-17, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01670119.
