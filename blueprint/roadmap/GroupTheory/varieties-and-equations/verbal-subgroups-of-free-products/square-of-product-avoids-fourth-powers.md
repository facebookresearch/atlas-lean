# Kourovka Problem 3.8 (squares of products modulo fourth powers)

The proposition that for all free groups $A$ and $B$ (on arbitrary sets of generators), in the free product $G = A * B$, with $V$ is the subgroup of $G$ generated by all fourth powers $g^4$, the verbal subgroup for the law $x^4 = 1$, if $a \in A$ and $b \in B$ do not lie in $V$ then $(ab)^2 \notin V$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.8 (V. G. Vilyatser).
