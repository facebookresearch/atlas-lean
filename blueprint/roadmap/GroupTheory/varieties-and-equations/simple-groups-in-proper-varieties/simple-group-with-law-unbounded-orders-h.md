# A simple group with a non-trivial identity and unbounded element orders

There is a simple group $G$ satisfying a non-trivial identity $w = 1$ such that no exponent $N \ge 1$ has $g^N = 1$ for all $g \in G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.83 (A. L. Shmel'kin); resolution: V. S. Atabekyan, Dep. no. 5381-V86, VINITI, Moscow, 1986.
