# A proper variety contains finitely many finite non-abelian simple groups

For every non-trivial element $w$ of a free group $F_n$ of finite rank $n$, the orders of the finite non-abelian simple groups satisfying the law $w = 1$ are bounded. Hence a proper variety of groups contains only finitely many finite non-abelian simple groups up to isomorphism. The proof uses the classification of finite simple groups.
