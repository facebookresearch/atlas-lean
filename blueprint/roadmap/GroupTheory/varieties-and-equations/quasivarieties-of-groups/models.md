# The quasivariety defined by a set of quasi-identities

For a set $Q$ of quasi-identities,
a group $H$ belongs to the quasivariety defined by $Q$ when every quasi-identity in $Q$ holds in
$H$. Every quasivariety of groups has this form.

Only groups in the lowest universe are considered; a quasivariety is determined by its countable
members.
