# A group satisfying a law

A group $H$ satisfies the identity (law) $w$, an element of the free group on $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$, if

$$w(h_0, h_1, h_2, \dots) = 1 \quad\text{for every sequence } h_0, h_1, h_2, \dots \in H,$$

where $w(h_0, h_1, \dots)$ is the image of $w$ under the homomorphism sending $x_i$ to $h_i$.

This is the case of a single law of [groups satisfying a set of laws](../test-ranks-of-relatively-free-groups/satisfies-laws.md): $H$ satisfies $w$ exactly when it satisfies the set $\{w\}$.
