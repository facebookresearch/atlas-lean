# Words in the free group of rank $k$

For $k \in \mathbb{N}$, a word in the free group $F_k$ on generators $a_0, \dots, a_{k-1}$ is a finite list of letters $(i, b)$ with $0 \le i < k$ and $b \in \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$. The letter $(i, \mathrm{true})$ stands for $a_i$ and $(i, \mathrm{false})$ for $a_i^{-1}$, so the list $(i_1, b_1), \dots, (i_m, b_m)$ represents the element

$$a_{i_1}^{\varepsilon_1} \cdots a_{i_m}^{\varepsilon_m} \in F_k, \qquad \varepsilon_j = \pm 1 \text{ according as } b_j = \mathrm{true} \text{ or } \mathrm{false}.$$
