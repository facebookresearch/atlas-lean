# Kourovka Problem 8.18 without solubility (countable abelian verbal subgroups)

The proposition that for every countably infinite abelian group $A$ there are $m \in \mathbb{N}$, a fully invariant subgroup $N$ of the free group $F_m$ of rank $m$ (a normal subgroup mapped into itself by every endomorphism of $F_m$), and a set $W$ of words in countably many variables, such that $A$ is isomorphic to the verbal subgroup $W(F_m/N)$ of the relatively free group $F_m/N$, the subgroup generated by all values of the words of $W$ in $F_m/N$. This is Kleiman's question without its parenthetical requirement that the relatively free group be soluble.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.18 (Yu. G. Kleiman); resolution: A. Storozhev, On abelian subgroups of relatively free groups, Comm. Algebra 22 (1994), 2677-2701, https://doi.org/10.1080/00927879408824986.
