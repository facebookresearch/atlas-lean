# Equational quasigroups

A quasigroup structure on a set $Q$ in the equational sense: three binary operations, multiplication $x * y$, left division $x \backslash y$ and right division $x / y$, satisfying $x \backslash (x * y) = y$, $x * (x \backslash y) = y$, $(x * y) / y = x$ and $(x / y) * y = x$ for all $x, y \in Q$. The structure is data (the three operations together with the four laws).
