# Kourovka Problem 9.4 (isotope varieties of finitely generated group varieties)

The proposition that for every finite group $G_0$ there is a finite non-empty quasigroup $(Q, *, \backslash, /)$ generating the class $I\mathfrak M$ of quasigroups isotopic to groups in the variety $\mathfrak M$ generated by $G_0$: a non-empty quasigroup is isotopic to some group in $\mathfrak M$ if and only if it satisfies every identity of $Q$. Quasigroups are taken in the equational signature $(*, \backslash, /)$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 9.4 (A. A. Gvaramiya).
