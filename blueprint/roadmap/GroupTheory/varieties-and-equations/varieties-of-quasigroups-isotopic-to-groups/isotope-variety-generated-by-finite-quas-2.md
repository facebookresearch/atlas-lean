# No finite quasigroup generates the isotope variety of a non-trivial finite group

There is a finite group $G_0$ such that no finite quasigroup generates the class of quasigroups isotopic to groups in the variety generated by $G_0$. For a non-trivial finite group containing a cyclic subgroup $C_p$, the quasigroup $a * b = a + T(b)$ on $\bigoplus_{i \in \mathbb Z} \mathbb F_p$, with $T$ the shift, is isotopic to a group in the variety, infinite and one-generated, while the variety generated by a finite algebra is locally finite. The Notebook marks Problem 9.4 solved ("No, it is not true"). It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 9.4 (isotope varieties of finitely generated group varieties)](isotope-variety-generated-by-finite-quas.md).
