# Kourovka Problem 4.64 (a variety without an independent basis of identities)

The proposition that some set $W$ of laws has no independent basis: there is no set $B$ of laws with the same consequences as $W$ such that no $b \in B$ is a consequence of $B \setminus \{b\}$. A law $w$ is a consequence of a set of laws when it holds in every group satisfying that set.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.64 (A. Tarski).
