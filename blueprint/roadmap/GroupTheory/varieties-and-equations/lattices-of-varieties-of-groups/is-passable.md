# Passable varieties of groups

For a subgroup $V$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators (the laws of a variety), the variety is passable when there is a chain $C$ of fully invariant subgroups $U \ge V$ of $F$ (the laws of subvarieties) that is maximal among such chains and in which every nonempty subset has a largest member. Since a smaller variety has more laws, this says that some unrefinable chain of subvarieties is well-ordered by inclusion of varieties.
