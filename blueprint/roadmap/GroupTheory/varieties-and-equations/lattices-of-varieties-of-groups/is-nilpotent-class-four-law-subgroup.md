# Law subgroups of subvarieties of the class-four nilpotent variety

A subgroup $H$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators is the subgroup of laws of a subvariety of $\mathfrak N_4$, the variety of nilpotent groups of class at most $4$, when $H$ is fully invariant in $F$ and contains $\gamma_5(F)$, the fifth term of the lower central series ($\gamma_1(F) = F$).
