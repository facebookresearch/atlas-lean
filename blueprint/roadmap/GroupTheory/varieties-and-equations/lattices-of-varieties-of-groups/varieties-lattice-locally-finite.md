# Kourovka Problem 6.14 for all varieties

The proposition that the lattice of all varieties of groups is locally finite: for every finite set $S$ of fully invariant subgroups of $F$, the sublattice of the subgroup lattice of $F$ generated by $S$ is finite. Varieties are represented by their laws, fully invariant subgroups of the free group $F$ on countably many generators (join and meet of varieties are meet and join of laws), and a lattice is locally finite when every finitely generated sublattice is finite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.14 (A. V. Kuznetsov).
