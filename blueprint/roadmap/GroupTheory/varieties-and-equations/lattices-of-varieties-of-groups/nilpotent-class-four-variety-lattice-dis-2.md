# The lattice of subvarieties of the class-four nilpotent variety is not distributive

The lattice of subvarieties of the variety $\mathfrak N_4$ of nilpotent groups of class at most $4$ is not distributive: there are fully invariant subgroups $U, V, W$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators, each containing $\gamma_5(F)$, with $U \cap (VW) \ne (U \cap V)(U \cap W)$. Kourovka Problem 3.53 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.53 (distributivity of the lattice of subvarieties of the class-four nilpotent variety)](nilpotent-class-four-variety-lattice-dis.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.53 (D. M. Smirnov); resolution: Yu. A. Belov, The lattice of nilpotent varieties of class four groups, Algebra and Logic 9 (1970), 371-374, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02219039.
