# There are continuum many sparse varieties

There are exactly continuum many varieties of groups with at most countably many subvarieties, counted through their laws (fully invariant subgroups $N$ of the free group on countably many generators such that only countably many fully invariant subgroups contain $N$).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.16 (A. V. Kuznetsov); resolution: P. A. Kozhevnikov, On varieties of groups of large odd exponent, Dep. 1612-V00, VINITI, Moscow, 2000; S. V. Ivanov and A. M. Storozhev, On varieties of groups in which all periodic groups are abelian, Contemp. Math. 360 (2004), 55-62.
