# Kourovka Problem 6.16 (the number of sparse varieties)

The proposition that the set of sparse varieties of groups, those with at most countably many subvarieties, has the cardinality of the continuum. A variety is represented by its laws, a fully invariant subgroup $N$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators; its groups are those satisfying every element of $N$, and inclusion of varieties reverses inclusion of these subgroups. The subvarieties of the variety with laws $N$ correspond to the fully invariant $N' \ge N$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.16 (A. V. Kuznetsov).
