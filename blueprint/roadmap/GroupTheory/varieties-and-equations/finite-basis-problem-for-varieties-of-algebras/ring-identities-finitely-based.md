# Finitely based identities of an associative ring

For a (not necessarily unital) associative ring $A$, the proposition that the identities of $A$ are finitely based among associative rings: there is a finite set $B$ of identities of $A$ such that every (not necessarily unital) associative ring $C$ satisfying all members of $B$ satisfies every identity of $A$. Equivalently, the variety generated by $A$ is finitely based.
