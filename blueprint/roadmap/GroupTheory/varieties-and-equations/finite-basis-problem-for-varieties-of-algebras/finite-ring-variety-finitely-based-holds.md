# Finite associative rings generate finitely based varieties

For every finite (not necessarily unital) associative ring $A$, the variety generated by $A$ is finitely based: some finite set of identities of $A$ implies all identities of $A$ in every associative ring. Kourovka Problem 2.41(a) has a positive answer.
