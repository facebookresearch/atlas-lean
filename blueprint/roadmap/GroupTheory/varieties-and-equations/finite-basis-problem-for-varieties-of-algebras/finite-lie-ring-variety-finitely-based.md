# Kourovka Problem 2.41(b) (finite Lie rings)

This is the proposition that for every finite Lie ring $L$ the variety generated by $L$ is finitely based (finitely many identities of it imply all of its identities in every Lie ring).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.41 (A. I. Mal'cev).
