# Evaluation of quasigroup terms

For an algebra $(Q; \cdot, \backslash, /)$ of the quasigroup signature and an assignment $x \colon \mathbb{N} \to Q$, the value of a term: $x_i \mapsto x(i)$, and the constructors $s \cdot t$, $s \backslash t$, $s / t$ are interpreted by the corresponding operations of $Q$ applied to the values of $s$ and $t$.
