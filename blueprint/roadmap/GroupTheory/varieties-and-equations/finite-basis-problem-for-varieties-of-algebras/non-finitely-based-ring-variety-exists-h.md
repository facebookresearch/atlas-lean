# Some variety of associative rings is not finitely based

There is a (not necessarily unital) associative ring whose identities are not finitely based among associative rings. Kourovka Problem 2.39(b), the Specht problem for associative rings, has a positive answer: over fields of positive characteristic there are varieties of associative algebras without a finite basis of identities.
