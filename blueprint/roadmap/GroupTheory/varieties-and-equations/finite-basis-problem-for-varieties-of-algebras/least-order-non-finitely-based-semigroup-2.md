# The least non-finitely based finite semigroup has six elements

The least cardinality of a finite semigroup whose variety is not finitely based is $6$: there is a semigroup with $6$ elements whose identities (equations between words in $x_0, x_1, \dots$) are not finitely based, and every finite semigroup with fewer elements (the empty semigroup included) is finitely based. Kourovka Problem 2.41(d) has the answer $n = 6$ (Perkins's six-element Brandt monoid of $2 \times 2$ matrix units).
