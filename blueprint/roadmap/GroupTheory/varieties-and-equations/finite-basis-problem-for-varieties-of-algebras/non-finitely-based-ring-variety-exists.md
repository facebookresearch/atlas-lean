# Kourovka Problem 2.39(b) (the Specht problem for associative rings)

This is the proposition that some variety of (not necessarily unital) associative rings is not finitely based: there is an associative ring $A$ whose identities are not implied by any finite subset of them in every associative ring.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.39 (A. I. Mal'cev).
