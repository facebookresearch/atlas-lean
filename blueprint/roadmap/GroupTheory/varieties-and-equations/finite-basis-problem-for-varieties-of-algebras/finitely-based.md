# Finitely based variety of an algebra of the quasigroup signature

For an algebra $(Q; \cdot, \backslash, /)$ of the quasigroup signature, the proposition that the variety it generates is finitely based: there is a finite set $B$ of identities of $Q$ such that every algebra of the signature (with arbitrary operations) satisfying all members of $B$ satisfies every identity of $Q$.
