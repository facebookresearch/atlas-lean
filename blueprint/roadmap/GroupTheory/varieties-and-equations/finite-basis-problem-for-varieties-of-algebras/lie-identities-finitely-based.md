# Finitely based identities of a Lie ring

For a Lie ring $L$, the proposition that the identities of $L$ are finitely based among Lie rings: there is a finite set $B$ of identities of $L$ such that every Lie ring $M$ satisfying all members of $B$ satisfies every identity of $L$. Equivalently, the variety generated by $L$ is finitely based.
