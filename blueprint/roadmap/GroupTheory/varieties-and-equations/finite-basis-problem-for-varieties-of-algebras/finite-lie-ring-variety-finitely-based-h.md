# Finite Lie rings generate finitely based varieties

For every finite Lie ring $L$, the variety generated by $L$ is finitely based: some finite set of identities of $L$ implies all identities of $L$ in every Lie ring. Kourovka Problem 2.41(b) has a positive answer.
