# Kourovka Problem 2.41(d) (least non-finitely based semigroup)

This is the proposition that the least cardinality of a finite semigroup whose variety is not finitely based is $6$: there is a semigroup with $6$ elements whose identities (equations between words in $x_0, x_1, \dots$) are not finitely based, and every finite semigroup with fewer elements (the empty semigroup included) is finitely based.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.41 (A. I. Mal'cev).
