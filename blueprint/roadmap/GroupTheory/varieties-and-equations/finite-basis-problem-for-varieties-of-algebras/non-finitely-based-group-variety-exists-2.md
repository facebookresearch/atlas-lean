# Some variety of groups is not finitely based

There is a group $G$ whose laws are not finitely based: no finite set of laws of $G$ implies all laws of $G$ in every group. Equivalently, some set of group laws is not equivalent to any finite set. Kourovka Problems 2.39(a) and 1.82 have positive answers (Ol'shanskii: there are continuum many varieties of groups; Adian: an independent infinite system of laws).
