# Kourovka Problem 2.41(a) (finite associative rings)

This is the proposition that for every finite (not necessarily unital) associative ring $A$ the variety generated by $A$ is finitely based (finitely many identities of it imply all of its identities in every not necessarily unital associative ring).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.41 (A. I. Mal'cev).
