# Words for identities of associative rings

The free non-unital associative ring on the variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$: integer linear combinations of nonempty words in the noncommuting variables, i.e. noncommutative polynomials with integer coefficients and zero constant term, realized as the semigroup ring $\mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{FreeSemigroup}(\mathbb{N})]$.
