# Kourovka Problem 5.41 (abelian normal subgroups of finite relatively free groups)

The proposition that every nontrivial finite group that is free in some variety has a nontrivial abelian normal subgroup. A group $G$ is free in some variety (relatively free) if it has a generating family $(x_i)_{i \in I}$ such that every assignment $x_i \mapsto y_i \in G$ extends to an endomorphism of $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.41 (A. Yu. Olshanskii).
