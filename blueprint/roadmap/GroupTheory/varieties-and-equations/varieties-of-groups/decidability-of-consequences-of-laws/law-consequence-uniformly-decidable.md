# Kourovka Problem 3.4(a) (uniform decidability of consequences of laws)

The proposition that there is an algorithm deciding, for any words $f_1, \dots, f_m$ and $f$, whether the law $f = 1$ is a consequence of $f_1 = 1, \dots, f_m = 1$ (holds in every group satisfying them). Decidability means computability of the predicate on the natural coding of these finite objects (lists of letters), so it does not depend on the coding.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.4 (L. A. Bokut').
