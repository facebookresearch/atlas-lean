# Consequences of finitely many group laws

For a finite list of words $f_1, \dots, f_m$ and a word $f$ in the variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$, the law $f = 1$ is a consequence of the laws $f_1 = 1, \dots, f_m = 1$ when every group in which each $f_i$ is sent to $1$ by every homomorphism from the free group on countably many generators also has $f$ sent to $1$ by every such homomorphism. Words in the variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$ are finite lists of letters $(i, b)$, read as the product of $x_i$ (if $b$ is true) or $x_i^{-1}$ (if $b$ is false).
