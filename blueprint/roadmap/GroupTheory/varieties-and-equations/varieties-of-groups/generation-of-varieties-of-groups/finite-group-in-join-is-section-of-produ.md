# Kourovka Problem 8.22 (finite groups in a join of two varieties)

The proposition that for all varieties $\mathfrak A$, $\mathfrak B$ of groups, with laws the fully invariant subgroups $V_A$, $V_B$ of the free group on countably many generators, every finite non-abelian group in the join $\mathfrak A \vee \mathfrak B$ (whose laws are $V_A \cap V_B$) is a section of $A \times B$ for some finite groups $A \in \mathfrak A$ and $B \in \mathfrak B$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.22 (L. G. Kovács).
