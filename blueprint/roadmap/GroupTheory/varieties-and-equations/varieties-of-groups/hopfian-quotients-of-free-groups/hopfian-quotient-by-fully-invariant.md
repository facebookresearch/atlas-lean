# Kourovka Problem 2.72 (Hopfian quotients by fully invariant subgroups)

The proposition that for every free group $F$ of finite rank $n$, every normal subgroup $N$ of $F$ and every normal subgroup $V \le N$ of $F$ such that $V$ is fully invariant in $N$: every endomorphism of $N$ maps $V$ into $V$, if $F/N$ is Hopfian then $F/V$ is Hopfian. A group is Hopfian if every surjective endomorphism of it is injective. (A subgroup fully invariant in a normal subgroup is automatically normal in $F$.)

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.72 (D. M. Smirnov; question of G. Baumslag).
