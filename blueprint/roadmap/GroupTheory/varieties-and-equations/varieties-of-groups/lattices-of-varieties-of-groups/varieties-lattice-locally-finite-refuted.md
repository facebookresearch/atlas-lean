# The lattice of varieties of groups is not locally finite

Some finite set of varieties of groups generates an infinite sublattice of the lattice of varieties: there is a finite set $S$ of fully invariant subgroups of the free group $F$ on countably many generators such that the sublattice of the subgroup lattice of $F$ generated by $S$ is infinite. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 6.14 for all varieties](varieties-lattice-locally-finite.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.14 (A. V. Kuznetsov); resolution: M. I. Anokhin, Embedding lattices in lattices of varieties of groups, Izv. Math. 63, no. 4 (1999), 649-665, Corollary 1.2, https://doi.org/10.1070/IM1999v063n04ABEH000250.
