# Kourovka Problem 3.53 (distributivity of the lattice of subvarieties of the class-four nilpotent variety)

The proposition that the lattice of subvarieties of $\mathfrak N_4$ is distributive, stated on the dually isomorphic lattice of their laws: for all fully invariant subgroups $U, V, W$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators containing $\gamma_5(F)$, $U \cap (VW) = (U \cap V)(U \cap W)$, where the product of normal subgroups is their join in the subgroup lattice.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.53 (D. M. Smirnov).
