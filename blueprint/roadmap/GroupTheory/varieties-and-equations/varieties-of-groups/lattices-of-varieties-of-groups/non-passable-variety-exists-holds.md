# Non-passable varieties of groups exist

There is a variety of groups no unrefinable chain of whose subvarieties is well-ordered by inclusion: a fully invariant subgroup $V$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators such that no maximal chain of fully invariant subgroups $U \ge V$ of $F$ has a largest member in every nonempty subset. Every variety generated by its finite groups is passable. Kourovka Problem 8.26 has a positive answer.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.26 (A. V. Kuznetsov); resolution: M. I. Anokhin, A remark on lattices and the existence of impassable varieties of groups, Moscow Univ. Math. Bull. 51, no. 1 (1996), 48-49.
