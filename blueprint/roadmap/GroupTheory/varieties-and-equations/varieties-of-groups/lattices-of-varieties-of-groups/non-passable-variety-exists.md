# Kourovka Problem 8.26 (non-passable varieties)

The proposition that there is a fully invariant subgroup $V$ of the free group on countably many generators whose variety is not passable: no unrefinable chain of its subvarieties is well-ordered by inclusion. A variety is represented by its laws, a fully invariant subgroup $N$ of the free group $F$ on countably many generators; its groups are those satisfying every element of $N$, and inclusion of varieties reverses inclusion of these subgroups.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.26 (A. V. Kuznetsov).
