# Kourovka Problem 1.83 (simple groups with an identity and unbounded element orders)

The proposition that there is a simple group $G$ whose element orders are unbounded (for no $N \ge 1$ is $g^N = 1$ for all $g \in G$, which covers elements of infinite order) and which satisfies a non-trivial identity: some $w \ne 1$ in the free group on countably many generators evaluates to $1$ under every assignment of elements of $G$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.83 (A. L. Shmel'kin).
