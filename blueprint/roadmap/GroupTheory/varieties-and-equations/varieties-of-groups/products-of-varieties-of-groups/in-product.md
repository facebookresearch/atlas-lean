# Membership in a product of varieties

For sets $L_A, L_B$ of laws (elements of the free group $F$ on countably many generators) and a group $G$, $G$ lies in the product $\mathfrak A\mathfrak B$ of the varieties $\mathfrak A$ and $\mathfrak B$ defined by $L_A$ and $L_B$ when $G$ has a normal subgroup $N$ satisfying every law in $L_A$ whose quotient $G/N$ satisfies every law in $L_B$. A group satisfies a law $w$ when $w$ evaluates to $1$ under every assignment of elements of the group to the generators of $F$.
