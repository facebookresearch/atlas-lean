# A non-abelian variety with commutative relative product

There is a non-abelian variety $\mathfrak M$ of groups with infinitely many subvarieties in which $(\mathfrak A\mathfrak B) \cap \mathfrak M = (\mathfrak B\mathfrak A) \cap \mathfrak M$ for all subvarieties $\mathfrak A, \mathfrak B$: for every $n$ it has $n$ pairwise distinct subvarieties, and a group of $\mathfrak M$ has a normal subgroup in $\mathfrak A$ with quotient in $\mathfrak B$ exactly when it has a normal subgroup in $\mathfrak B$ with quotient in $\mathfrak A$. An example is $\mathfrak M = \mathfrak A_p\mathfrak A_p$ for a prime $p$, where $\mathfrak A_p$ is the variety of abelian groups of exponent $p$. Kourovka Problem 2.44 has a positive answer.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.44 (A. I. Mal'cev); resolution: Yu. M. Gorchakov, talk of 21 June 1967, Krasnoyarsk.
