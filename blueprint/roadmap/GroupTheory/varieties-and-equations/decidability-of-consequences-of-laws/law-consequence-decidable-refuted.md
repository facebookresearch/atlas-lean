# A finitely based variety with undecidable laws

There are words $f_1, \dots, f_m$ such that the set of words $f$ for which $f = 1$ is a consequence of $f_1 = 1, \dots, f_m = 1$ is undecidable: some finitely based variety of groups has an undecidable set of laws. Kourovka Problem 3.4(b) has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.4(b) (decidability of consequences of fixed laws)](law-consequence-decidable.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 3.4 (L. A. Bokut'); resolution: Yu. G. Kleiman, Soviet Math. Dokl. 20 (1979), 115-119.
