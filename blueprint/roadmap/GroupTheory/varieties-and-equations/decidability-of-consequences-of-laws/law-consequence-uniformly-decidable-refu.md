# No algorithm decides consequence of finitely many group laws

There is no algorithm deciding, for arbitrary words $f_1, \dots, f_m$ and $f$, whether the law $f = 1$ holds in every group satisfying $f_1 = 1, \dots, f_m = 1$. Kourovka Problem 3.4(a) has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.4(a) (uniform decidability of consequences of laws)](law-consequence-uniformly-decidable.md).
