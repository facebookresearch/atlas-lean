# Kourovka Problem 1.82 (an infinite set of identities with no finite equivalent)

The proposition that there is a set $E$ of identities (elements of the free group on countably many generators) not equivalent to any finite set: for every finite set $B$ of identities some group satisfies all identities of one of $E$, $B$ but not all identities of the other. Such an $E$ is necessarily infinite.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.82 (A. L. Shmel'kin).
