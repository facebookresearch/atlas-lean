# Kourovka Problem 5.51 (independent bases of quasi-identities of free groups)

The proposition that for every set $\alpha$ with at least two elements, the quasi-identities of the free group $F(\alpha)$ have an independent basis. A quasi-identity in $n$ unknowns is an implication $(w_1 = 1 \wedge \dots \wedge w_k = 1) \Rightarrow w = 1$ with $w_1, \dots, w_k, w$ in the free group on $n$ letters; it holds in a group when every assignment of the unknowns satisfying all premises satisfies the conclusion. A set $B$ of quasi-identities is an independent basis for the quasi-identities of $G$ if a quasi-identity follows from $B$ (holds in every group satisfying $B$) exactly when it holds in $G$, and no member of $B$ follows from the others.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 5.51 (D. M. Smirnov).
