# A set of group identities with no finite equivalent

There is a set $E$ of group identities such that for every finite set $B$ of identities, $E$ and $B$ define different varieties: some group satisfies every identity of one set but not every identity of the other.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.82 (A. L. Shmel'kin); resolution: S. I. Adian, Math. USSR-Izv. 4 (1970), 721-739.
