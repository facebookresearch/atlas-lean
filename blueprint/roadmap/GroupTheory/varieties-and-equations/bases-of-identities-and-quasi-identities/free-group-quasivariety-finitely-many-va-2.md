# The quasivariety of the free group of rank two has no basis in finitely many variables

For no $n \in \mathbb N$ is there a set of quasi-identities, each in at most $n$ unknowns, whose models are exactly the groups satisfying every quasi-identity of the free group $F_2$ of rank $2$. Kourovka Problem 3.52 has a negative answer. A quasi-identity is a sentence $\forall x\,(w_1 = 1 \wedge \dots \wedge w_k = 1 \to w = 1)$ with finitely many premises, all words in finitely many unknowns. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 3.52 (quasi-identities of the free group of rank two)](free-group-quasivariety-finitely-many-va.md).
