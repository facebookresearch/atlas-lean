# Homomorphic images of an axiomatizable class need not be axiomatizable

There is a first-order axiomatizable class $\mathfrak K$ of groups such that the class of homomorphic images of groups in $\mathfrak K$ is not axiomatizable. Kourovka Problem 1.68 has a negative answer. It is the negation of the proposition on [Kourovka Problem 1.68 (homomorphic images of axiomatizable classes)](homomorphic-images-of-axiomatizable-clas.md).

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.68 (A. Tarski; Yu. I. Merzlyakov); resolution: F. Clare, Algebra Universalis 5 (1975), 120-124.
