# Kourovka Problem 1.68 (homomorphic images of axiomatizable classes)

The proposition that for every axiomatizable class $\mathfrak K$ of groups (the models of some first-order theory in the language of groups), the class $Q\mathfrak K$ of homomorphic images of groups in $\mathfrak K$ is axiomatizable.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.68 (A. Tarski; Yu. I. Merzlyakov).
