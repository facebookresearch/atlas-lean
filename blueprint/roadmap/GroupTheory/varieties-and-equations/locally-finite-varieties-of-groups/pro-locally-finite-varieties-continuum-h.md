# There are continuum many pro-locally-finite varieties

There are exactly continuum many varieties of groups that are not locally finite but all of whose proper subvarieties are locally finite (every finitely generated group in them is finite). Counted through their laws, fully invariant subgroups of the countable free group on countably many generators, the upper bound is automatic. The variety of abelian groups is an example.
