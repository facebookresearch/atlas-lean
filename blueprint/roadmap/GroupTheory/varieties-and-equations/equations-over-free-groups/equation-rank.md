# The rank of a coefficient-free equation in a free group

For a coefficient-free equation $w = 1$, with $w$ an element of the free group on unknowns $x_i$ ($i \in \mathbb N$), its rank is the largest $r$ such that some solution $x \colon \mathbb N \to F$ in the free group $F$ on countably many generators ($w$ evaluates to $1$) makes the subgroup generated by the values of the unknowns occurring in the reduced word of $w$ isomorphic to the free group of rank $r$. The value is the supremum in $\mathbb N$ of these $r$; the set is non-empty (the trivial solution gives $r = 0$) and bounded by the number of unknowns occurring in $w$.
