# Kourovka Problem 6.25 (computing the rank of equations)

The proposition that the rank of coefficient-free equations in a free group is computable: the map sending a word (a list of letters in the unknowns) to the rank of the equation it defines is a computable function.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.25 (G. S. Makanin).
