# Kourovka Problem 1.18(a) (decidability of equations in free groups)

The proposition that for every finite rank $k$, solvability of equations with constants over the free group $F_k$ is decidable, uniformly in the number of unknowns: the set of coded equations having a solution in $F_k$ is computable. Decidability means computability of the predicate on the natural coding of these finite objects (lists of letters), so it does not depend on the coding.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.18 (A. Tarski; Yu. L. Ershov).
