# The rank of a coefficient-free equation is computable

There is an algorithm computing, from a coefficient-free equation $w = 1$ over a free group, the maximal rank of a free subgroup generated by the values (in a solution) of the unknowns occurring in $w$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 6.25 (G. S. Makanin); resolution: A. A. Razborov, On systems of equations in a free group, Math. USSR-Izv. 25 (1985), 115-162, https://doi.org/10.1070/IM1985v025n01ABEH001272.
