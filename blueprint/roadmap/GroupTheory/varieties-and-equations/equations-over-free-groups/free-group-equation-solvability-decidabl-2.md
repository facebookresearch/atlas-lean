# Makanin's theorem on equations in free groups

For every finite rank $k$ there is an algorithm deciding whether an equation with constants over the free group $F_k$ (in any number of unknowns) has a solution. Kourovka Problem 1.18(a) has a positive answer; part (b), a description of all solutions, asks for a description rather than a statement.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.18 (A. Tarski; Yu. L. Ershov); resolution: G. S. Makanin, Equations in a free group, Math. USSR-Izv. 21 (1983), 483-546; G. S. Makanin, Decidability of the universal and positive theories of a free group, Math. USSR-Izv. 25 (1985), 75-88.
