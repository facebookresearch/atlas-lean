# Equations with constants over a free group of finite rank

An equation over the free group $F_k$ of rank $k$ in unknowns $x_0, x_1, \dots$: a finite list of letters, each a generator $a$ of $F_k$ or an unknown $x_i$, together with a sign, read as the product of these letters or their inverses. The equation is $w = 1$ for this word $w$.
