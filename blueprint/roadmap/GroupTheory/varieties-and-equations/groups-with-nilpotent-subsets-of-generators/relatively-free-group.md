# The relatively free group G_f with nilpotent subsets

For a set $X$ and $f$ from the subsets of $X$ to the natural numbers, $G_f$ is the quotient of the free group $F(X)$ by the normal closure of the terms $\gamma_{f(Y)+1}\langle Y \rangle$ of the lower central series of the subgroups $\langle Y \rangle$, $Y \subseteq X$; it is the universal group generated by $X$ in which each $\langle Y \rangle$ is nilpotent of class at most $f(Y)$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.56 (A. Yu. Olshanskii).
