# Nilpotency relations on subsets of generators

For a set $X$ and a function $f$ from the subsets of $X$ to $\mathbb{N}$, the normal subgroup of the free group $F(X)$ generated by the terms $\gamma_{f(Y)+1}\langle Y \rangle$, $Y \subseteq X$, where $\langle Y \rangle$ is the subgroup generated by $Y$ and $\gamma_1 H = H$, $\gamma_{i+1}H = [\gamma_i H, H]$ is its lower central series.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.56 (A. Yu. Olshanskii).
