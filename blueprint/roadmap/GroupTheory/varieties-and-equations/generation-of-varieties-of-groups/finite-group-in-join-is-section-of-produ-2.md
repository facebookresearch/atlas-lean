# A finite group in a join of varieties that is no section of a product

There are varieties $\mathfrak A$, $\mathfrak B$ of groups and a finite non-abelian group in $\mathfrak A \vee \mathfrak B$ that is not a section of $A \times B$ for any finite $A \in \mathfrak A$ and $B \in \mathfrak B$. Storozhev's varieties are pseudoabelian (non-abelian, each defined by one law in two variables, with only abelian finite groups), so every such $A \times B$ is abelian. Kourovka Problem 8.22 has a negative answer.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.22 (L. G. Kovács); resolution: A. Storozhev, On the join of varieties of groups, Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 51 (1995), 287-290, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972700014118.
