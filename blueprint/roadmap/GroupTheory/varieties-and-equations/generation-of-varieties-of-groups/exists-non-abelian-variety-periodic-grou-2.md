# A non-abelian variety in which all periodic groups are abelian

There is a variety of groups that contains a non-abelian group but in which every periodic group (every element of finite order) is abelian. Kourovka Problem 4.73(b) has a positive answer.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 4.73 (A. L. Ivanov and A. M. Storozhev, On varieties of groups in which all periodic groups are abelian, Contemp. Math. 360 (2004), 55-62, https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/360/06570, and P. A.
