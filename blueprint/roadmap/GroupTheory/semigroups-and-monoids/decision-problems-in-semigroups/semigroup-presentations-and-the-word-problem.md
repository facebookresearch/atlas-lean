# Semigroup presentations and the word problem

A semigroup presentation lists generators and defining relations between words; the semigroup it presents is the free semigroup modulo the congruence the relations generate, and its word problem asks whether two words represent the same element.

A *semigroup* is a set with an associative binary operation. For a set $A$, the *free semigroup* $A^+$ consists of the nonempty words over $A$ under concatenation. For pairs of words $u_i, v_i \in A^+$ ($i \in I$), the semigroup

$$
\mathrm{Sgp}\langle A \mid u_i = v_i \ (i \in I) \rangle = A^+ / \sigma
$$

is the quotient of $A^+$ by the smallest congruence $\sigma$ containing all pairs $(u_i, v_i)$; equivalently, two words are equal in it iff one can be transformed into the other by finitely many replacements of a factor $u_i$ by $v_i$ or of $v_i$ by $u_i$. The presentation is *finite* if $A$ and $I$ are finite, and a semigroup is *finitely presented* if it has a finite presentation. The *word problem* of a finitely presented semigroup $S = \mathrm{Sgp}\langle A \mid R \rangle$ asks, given two words $w, w' \in A^+$, whether $w = w'$ in $S$. Monoid presentations, over the free monoid $A^*$, are defined in the same way, and the word problem of a finite monoid presentation is the word problem of a Thue system.

A *quasi-identity* is a universal formula $L \to U = V$, where $L$ is a finite conjunction of equalities $u = v$ between words over an alphabet $X$ and $U, V$ are words over $X$; it holds in a semigroup if it holds for every assignment of elements to the letters. The *uniform word problem* for a class $\mathcal K$ of semigroups asks, given a quasi-identity, whether it holds in every semigroup of $\mathcal K$; equivalently, given a finite presentation $\mathrm{Sgp}\langle X \mid L \rangle$ and two words $U, V$, whether $U = V$ under every assignment of elements of a member of $\mathcal K$ to the letters of $X$ that satisfies $L$, that is, under every homomorphism from $\mathrm{Sgp}\langle X \mid L \rangle$ into a member of $\mathcal K$. When $\mathcal K$ is closed under taking subsemigroups, as the class of finite semigroups and the class of finite members of a variety are, it suffices to consider members of $\mathcal K$ generated by elements satisfying $L$; for other classes the two questions can differ.

## Expansion

Finitely presented semigroups and monoids are also called associative calculi or Thue systems; the *uniform* word problem takes the finite presentation as part of the input as well.
