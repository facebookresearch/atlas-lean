# Monoid of queue actions

The monoid of queue actions is the monoid of transformations of the contents of a first-in first-out queue induced by finite sequences of the basic actions "write $a$" and "read $a$", where reading fails unless $a$ is the first entry.

Let $A$ be a finite alphabet with $|A| \geq 2$, let $\bar A = \{\bar a : a \in A\}$ be a disjoint copy of $A$, and let $\Sigma = A \cup \bar A$. The letter $a$ stands for writing $a$ at the end of the queue and $\bar a$ for reading $a$ from its front. Queue contents are words in $A^*$, and $\bot$ denotes an error. The free monoid $\Sigma^*$ acts on $A^* \cup \{\bot\}$ by

$$
q.\varepsilon = q, \qquad q.au = qa.u, \qquad q.\bar a u = \begin{cases} q'.u & \text{if } q = a q', \\ \bot & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \qquad \bot.u = \bot
$$

for $q \in A^*$, $a \in A$ and $u \in \Sigma^*$. Two words $u, v \in \Sigma^*$ are *equivalent*, $u \equiv v$, if $q.u = q.v$ for every queue content $q \in A^*$. This is a congruence on $\Sigma^*$, and the quotient $\mathcal{Q} = \Sigma^* / {\equiv}$ is the *monoid of queue actions*, with the natural epimorphism $u \mapsto [u]$.

For instance $\bar a c \equiv c \bar a$ for distinct letters $a \neq c$, while $a \bar a \not\equiv \bar a a$, since $\varepsilon.\bar a a = \bot$ but $\varepsilon.a \bar a = \varepsilon$.

## Expansion

If $A$ is a singleton the queue degenerates into a partially blind counter, which is why $|A| \geq 2$ is assumed. The submonoid of $\mathcal{Q}$ generated by $[a]$, $[ab]$, $[\bar b]$ and $[\overline{abb}]$ is isomorphic to the direct product $\{a, b\}^* \times \{c, d\}^*$ of two free monoids.
