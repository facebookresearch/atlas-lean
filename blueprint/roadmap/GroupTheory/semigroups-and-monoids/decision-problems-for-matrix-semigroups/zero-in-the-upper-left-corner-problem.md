# Zero-in-the-upper-left-corner problem for matrix semigroups

The zero-in-the-upper-left-corner problem asks, given finitely many square matrices, whether some nonempty product of them has upper left entry zero.

**Zero in the upper left corner.** Input: $n \times n$ integer matrices $M_1, \dots, M_k$. Question: is there $N \in \langle M_1, \dots, M_k \rangle$ with $N_{11} = 0$?

## Depends on

- [Semigroup generated by finitely many matrices](decision-problems-for-matrix-semigroups.md)

## Proof depends on

None.

## Expansion

The problem is the special case $u = v = (1, 0, \dots, 0)$, $a = 0$ of scalar reachability; a variant asks for a zero in the upper right corner.
