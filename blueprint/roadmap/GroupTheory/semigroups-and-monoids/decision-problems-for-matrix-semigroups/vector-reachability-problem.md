# Vector reachability problem for matrix semigroups

The vector reachability problem asks, given finitely many square matrices and two vectors, whether some nonempty product of the matrices maps the first vector to the second.

**Vector reachability.** Input: $n \times n$ integer matrices $M_1, \dots, M_k$ and row vectors $u, v \in \mathbb{Z}^n$. Question: is there $X \in \langle M_1, \dots, M_k \rangle$ with $u X = v$? The case $v = 0$ is the *vector mortality problem*.

## Depends on

- [Semigroup generated by finitely many matrices](semigroup-generated-by-finitely-many-matrices.md)

## Proof depends on

None.

## Expansion

Vector reachability is the orbit question for the action of the matrix semigroup on row vectors.
