# Scalar reachability problem for matrix semigroups

The scalar reachability problem asks, given finitely many square matrices, two vectors and a scalar, whether some nonempty product of the matrices, read through the two vectors, gives that scalar.

**Scalar reachability.** Input: $n \times n$ integer matrices $M_1, \dots, M_k$, vectors $u, v \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ and $a \in \mathbb{Z}$. Question: is there $N \in \langle M_1, \dots, M_k \rangle$ with $v N u^{\mathsf{T}} = a$?

## Depends on

- [Semigroup generated by finitely many matrices](semigroup-generated-by-finitely-many-matrices.md)

## Proof depends on

None.

## Expansion

With $u = v = (1, 0, \dots, 0)$ and $a = 0$ the question becomes whether some product has upper left entry $0$, the zero-in-the-upper-left-corner problem.
