# Notice

This entry formalizes the probability chapters of CDIS, *Calcul Différentiel, Intégral et
Stochastique*, the Mines Paris course by Sébastien Boisgérault, Thomas Romary, Emilie Chautru
and Pauline Bernard, at commit `afa15a2` of https://github.com/boisgera/CDIS. The course is
licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## What comes from the course

- The selection and order of the 102 tagged statements, which give the statement ids, and the
  mathematical content of each statement, which is standard probability theory.
- The headings of the roadmap articles of chapters I to IV, which are the course's statement
  titles (for example "Theorem (Inégalité de Markov)"), and the section names used as locators
  in `roadmap/` and `sources/`.

No course prose or proof is reproduced: the article bodies restate each result in English, and
the Lean code in `Code/` and `Verify/` is original work.
