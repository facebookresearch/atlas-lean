# Theorem (Mesure produit sur un espace de dimension infinie)

CDIS id 64. Given probability spaces (Ω_i, A_i, P_i), i ∈ ℕ*, there is a unique probability P on the product Ω = Π Ω_i with the product σ-algebra such that P(A_1 × ... × A_k × Ω_{k+1} × ...) = Π_{i≤k} P_i(A_i) for all k and A_i ∈ A_i.
