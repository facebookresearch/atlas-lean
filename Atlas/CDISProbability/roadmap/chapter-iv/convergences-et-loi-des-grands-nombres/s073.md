# Theorem (Loi des grands nombres cas $\L^2$)

CDIS id 73. Law of large numbers, L² case: if (X_n) are i.i.d., square integrable, with mean m, then M_n = (X_1 + ... + X_n) / n converges to m almost surely, in L¹, in probability, and in L² (E((M_n - m)²) → 0).

Note: Paul proves the weak law; L2 rate via IndepFun.variance_sum; deps 63, 74.
