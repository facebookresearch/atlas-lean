# Corollary (Cas où $X$ admet une densité)

CDIS id 78. If X_n converges in law to X and X has a density, then P(X_n ∈ ]a, b]) → P(X ∈ ]a, b]) for all a < b.

Note: frontier of ]a,b] is null for a law with density; deps 77.
