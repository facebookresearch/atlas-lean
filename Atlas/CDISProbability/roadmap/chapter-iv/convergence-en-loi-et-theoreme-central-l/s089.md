# Theorem (Cas gaussien)

CDIS id 89. X is a Gaussian vector iff φ_X(u) = exp(i ⟨u, m⟩ - ⟨u, C u⟩ / 2), where m = E(X) and C is the covariance matrix of X, which is positive semidefinite.

Note: Mathlib already gives the characterization through a continuous bilinear form (isGaussian_iff_gaussian_charFun) and identifies the mean and that form with the measure's mean and covarianceBilin (gaussian_charFun_congr). This article's part is the translation to the covariance matrix of id 39: the quadratic form ⟨u, C u⟩ with C positive semidefinite, and m the mean vector. It is stated for the law of X on ℝⁿ. No moment hypothesis is assumed: when φ_X has the Gaussian form for some m and some positive semidefinite C, the law is Mathlib's multivariateGaussian m C, which makes m the mean and C the covariance matrix.
