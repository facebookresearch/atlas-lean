# Theorem (Cas gaussien)

CDIS id 89. X is a Gaussian vector iff φ_X(u) = exp(i ⟨u, m⟩ - ⟨u, C u⟩ / 2), where m = E(X) and C is the covariance matrix of X, which is positive semidefinite.

Note: stated for the law of X on ℝⁿ, with the mean vector and covariance matrix of id 39. No moment hypothesis is assumed: when φ_X has the Gaussian form for some m and some positive semidefinite C, the law is Mathlib's multivariateGaussian m C, which makes m the mean and C the covariance matrix.
