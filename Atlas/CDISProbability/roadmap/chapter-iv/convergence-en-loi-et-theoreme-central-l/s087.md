# Proposition (Fonction caractéristique et moments)

CDIS id 87. If |X|^m is integrable for an integer m, then φ_X is m times continuously differentiable and ∂^m φ_X / ∂u_{i_1} ... ∂u_{i_m} (u) = i^m E(e^{i⟨u, X⟩} X_{i_1} ... X_{i_m}).
