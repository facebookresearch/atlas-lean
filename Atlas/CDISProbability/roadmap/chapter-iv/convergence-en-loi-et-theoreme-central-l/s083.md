# Proposition (Transformation linéaire)

CDIS id 83. For X in ℝⁿ, a ∈ ℝᵐ and a real m × n matrix A, φ_{a + AX}(u) = exp(i ⟨u, a⟩) φ_X(Aᵀ u) for u ∈ ℝᵐ.

Note: charFun of an affine image.
