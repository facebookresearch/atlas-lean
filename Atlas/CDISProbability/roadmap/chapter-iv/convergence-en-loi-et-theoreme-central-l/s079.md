# Theorem (central limite (TCL))

CDIS id 79. Central limit theorem: if the X_n are i.i.d., square integrable, with mean m and variance σ² > 0, then (S_n - n m) / (σ √n) converges in law to N(0, 1), where S_n = X_1 + ... + X_n.

Note: 20-line bridge: rescaling by σ and N(0,σ²) → N(0,1).
