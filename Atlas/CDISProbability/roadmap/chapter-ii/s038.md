# Proposition (Densités marginales)

CDIS id 38. If the pair Z = (X, Y) has density f on ℝ², then X and Y have densities f_X(x) = ∫ f(x, y) dy and f_Y(y) = ∫ f(x, y) dx.

Note: densities in ENNReal; the course's real densities are ENNReal.ofReal of them.
