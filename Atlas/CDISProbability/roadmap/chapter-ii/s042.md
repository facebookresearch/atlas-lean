# Proposition (Caractérisation de l'indépendance)

CDIS id 42. Real random variables X and Y with densities f_X and f_Y are independent iff the pair (X, Y) has density f_X(x) f_Y(y) on ℝ².

Note: Mathlib's pdf.indepFun_iff_pdf_prod_eq_pdf_mul_pdf assumes a joint density; the course's statement does not, and neither does the version proved here.
