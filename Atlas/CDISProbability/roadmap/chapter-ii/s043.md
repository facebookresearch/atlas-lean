# Proposition (Fonctions de vecteurs aléatoires indépendants)

CDIS id 43. If X and Y are independent with densities f_X, f_Y and g, h are measurable, then g(X) and h(Y) are independent; if moreover they are integrable, g(X) h(Y) is integrable and E(g(X) h(Y)) = E(g(X)) E(h(Y)).
