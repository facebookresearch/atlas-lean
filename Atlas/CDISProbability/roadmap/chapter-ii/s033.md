# Definition (Covariance)

CDIS id 33. For X, Y ∈ L², (X - E X)(Y - E Y) is integrable; its expectation is the covariance Cov(X, Y), and the correlation coefficient is ρ(X, Y) = Cov(X, Y) / √(Var X Var Y).
