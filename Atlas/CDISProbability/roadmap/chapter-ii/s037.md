# Proposition (Fonction d'un vecteur aléatoire)

CDIS id 37. If X has density f on ℝⁿ and g is measurable from ℝⁿ to ℝ, then g(X) ∈ L¹ iff ∫ |g| f < ∞, and then E(g(X)) = ∫ g(x) f(x) dx.
