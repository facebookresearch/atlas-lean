# Proposition (Formule de Bayes)

CDIS id 11. Under the hypotheses of the total probability formula and if P(A) > 0, for every i, P(B_i | A) = P(A | B_i) P(B_i) / Σ_n P(A | B_n) P(B_n).

Note: countable Bayes; deps 10.
