# Theorem (Caractérisation de la fonction de répartition)

CDIS id 25. A function F from ℝ to ℝ is the distribution function of a (necessarily unique) probability on ℝ iff it is nondecreasing, right-continuous, tends to 0 at -∞ and to 1 at +∞.

Note: proved from Mathlib's StieltjesFunction.measure and Measure.eq_of_cdf.
