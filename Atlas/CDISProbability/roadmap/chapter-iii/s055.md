# Proposition (Critère d'indépendance)

CDIS id 55. X and Y are independent iff P_{Y|X=x} does not depend on x for P_X-almost every x, and then P_{Y|X=x} = P_Y; when (X, Y) has a density, X and Y are independent iff f_{Y|X=x} does not depend on x.
