# Definition (Espérance conditionelle d'une fonction de variables aléatoires)

CDIS id 57. For g Borel and nonnegative or P_{X,Y}-integrable, E(g(X, Y) | X = x) = ∫ g(x, y) P_{Y|X=x}(dy), and E(g(X, Y) | X) = ψ(X) where ψ(x) = E(g(X, Y) | X = x).
