# Theorem (Théorème de Fubini (pour les probabilités))

CDIS id 49. Fubini for probabilities: given probabilities P_1 on ℝⁿ and P_2 on ℝᵐ, there is a unique probability P on ℝⁿ⁺ᵐ with P(A × B) = P_1(A) P_2(B), and for integrable g, ∫ g dP = ∫ (∫ g(x, y) P_2(dy)) P_1(dx) = ∫ (∫ g(x, y) P_1(dx)) P_2(dy).
