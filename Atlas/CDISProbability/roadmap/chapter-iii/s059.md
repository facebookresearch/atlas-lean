# Proposition (Transfert conditionnel)

CDIS id 59. For g Borel and nonnegative or P_{X,Y}-integrable, for P_X-almost every x, E(g(X, Y) | X = x) = E(g(x, Y) | X = x) = ∫ g(x, y) P_{Y|X=x}(dy); if moreover X and Y are independent, this equals ∫ g(x, y) P_Y(dy).

Note: no proof in the course; proved here via a transfer kernel and uniqueness of condDistrib.
